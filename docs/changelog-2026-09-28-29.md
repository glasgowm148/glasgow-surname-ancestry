## 14. Durable project rules

transcriptions, Y-DNA detail, shared styling/navigation and catalogue ordering.

not itself authorize withdrawal of established research or an unrelated redesign;

unless already authorized. An approved publication failing a check should stop
the release, not be silently replaced with a reduced page. Restoration reports
must identify remaining omissions.

change the already packaged public site. They reduce repeat workflow mistakes;
they do not constitute a guarantee that an entire site is free of every possible
regression.
