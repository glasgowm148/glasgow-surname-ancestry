# Privacy and publication policy

## Public site behaviour

The published site loads the original project Google Analytics tag
(`G-JB207MGBMX`), restored at the project owner's explicit request on 29 September
2026. Google Analytics measures visits and page use and may set analytics cookies
or collect usage information under Google's privacy terms.
Some pages may use browser local storage only for a presentation preference,
such as the selected Y-DNA colour theme; that preference stays in the browser.

Visitors who follow a link to another service are subject to that service's
privacy information. Normal web hosting can keep access logs, including
technical request information, under the host's own retention arrangements.

## Research publication

The public catalogue is a filtered research publication. People assessed as
likely living are omitted from public catalogue views, exports and the sitemap.
Historical claims remain qualified where the evidence is incomplete. A person
who supplies a correction or an enquiry chooses what information to include;
the project uses it to assess that request.

Raw WikiTree narratives and restricted captures stay in local research storage.
Public citations and historical fields are filtered independently of the raw
captures; potentially living relatives are withheld in HTML, JSON and exports.
The previously public curated Y-DNA study was explicitly approved for full
restoration by the project owner on 29 September 2026. Its historical narrative,
kit comparisons, STR matrices, SNP call summaries, research tools and exports
are published with their original provenance and uncertainty. The exact
retained study and documentary-line inputs are recorded by hash in
`research/ydna-publication-approval.json`; new tester records require their own
publication permission. The owner also explicitly restored Alexander Glasgow's
full DNA dossier and matrix exports, the Atlas tester annotations and controls,
and the supplied reference pages. The preserved original publication sources
and their hashes are recorded in `research/publication-restoration-approval.json`.
Raw sequence files and unrelated private DNA material
remain private. Historical transcription images and the linked source PDF
explicitly approved by the project owner on 29 September 2026 are published in
full. Their exact paths and hashes are maintained in
`research/transcriptions/public-preview-approvals.json`; generation and release
checks preserve these assets and the detailed illustrated readings. Other
licensed scan images and unapproved record excerpts remain private. The six
previously public Alexander Glasgow record excerpts are explicitly approved
and included in the same exact asset manifest.

## Superseded supplied pages

The former `original-upload.html` and `supplied-timeline.html` Y-DNA reference
pages are retained in `data/private/publication-source-archives/` as source
records. Their public URLs again serve the supplied research pages, restored at
the owner's request. They are dated reference material; the maintained `/ydna`
and `/timeline` pages provide the current account.
