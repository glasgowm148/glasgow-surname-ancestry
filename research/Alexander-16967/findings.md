# Findings: [Rose Alexander (Alexander-16967)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Alexander-16967)

## Current conclusion

The detailed 1851 return adds several facts absent from Rose's sparse biography:
she was John Glasgow's wife, aged 80, born in County Antrim, able to read, and
reported 1791 as her first-marriage year. It does not identify her parents or
state that she was mother of John's adult children.

== Biography ==
'''Rose Alexander''' was born about 1770 or 1771 in County Antrim, Ireland. In the detailed 1851 census return she was recorded at Glenleslie, Dunaghy parish, as the 80-year-old wife of [[Glasgow-1022|John Glasgow]]. She could read, and both spouses reported 1791 as the year of their first marriage.<ref name="Glenleslie1851">[https://durrushistory.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/1851irishcensusreturnscommadelimitedtextfilejul24_071.pdf ''1851 Irish Census Returns: detailed comma-delimited transcription''], Glenleslie household 21, pp. 103–104.</ref><ref name="Kilconway1851">[https://www.billmacafee.com/census/1851censuskilconway.pdf William Macafee and W. Scott, ''1851 Census for some Parishes and Townlands in the Barony of Kilconway, Co. Antrim''], Glenleslie household 21.</ref>

William Alexander was recorded in the household as John's brother-in-law. This supports an Alexander connection but does not identify Rose's parents or the precise kinship route.<ref name="Kilconway1851" />

== Research Notes ==
The return identifies John as the father of Nancy, Andrew and Ellen Glasgow but does not identify their mother. Rose's maternity of each attached child should therefore remain uncertain. The suggested descent from [[Alexander-2237|John Alexander]] through William King Alexander is not supported by the inspected census and should be presented only as an unproved research hypothesis.
