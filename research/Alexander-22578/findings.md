# Findings: [Eunice Nancy (Alexander) Field (Alexander-22578)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Alexander-22578)

## Current conclusion

Alexander-22578 is **Eunice Nancy (Alexander) Field**, not the William Alexander
in the Glenleslie 1851 mortality schedule. Eunice was born on 1 February 1783 at
Northfield, Massachusetts, and died there on 14 February 1821. The Irish
record-defined man was a labourer aged 60 who reportedly died of rheumatism in
1846 and was called the household head's brother-in-law.

The former free-space link is a same-name misidentification. No change to this
Massachusetts profile is required; it must simply be excluded from the Irish
household.
