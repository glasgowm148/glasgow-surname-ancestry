# Findings: [William Alexander (Alexander-22578)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Alexander-22578)

## Current conclusion

This profile is **not** the William Alexander in the Glenleslie 1851 mortality
schedule. Alexander-22578 was born on 1 February 1783 at Northfield,
Massachusetts, and died there on 14 February 1821. The Irish record-defined man
was a labourer aged 60 who reportedly died of rheumatism in 1846 and was called
the household head's brother-in-law.

The former free-space link is a same-name misidentification. No change to this
Massachusetts profile is required; it must simply be excluded from the Irish
household.
