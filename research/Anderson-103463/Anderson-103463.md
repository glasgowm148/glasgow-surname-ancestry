== Biography ==

No historical Jonet Anderson corresponding to this profile has been identified.
The sole cited record concerns [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow of uncertain
Corsoun]], whose wife is written '''Elizat Stevinsoun'''. The occurrence of
'''Jonet''' is among Andrew's daughters,
not in the name of a wife surnamed Anderson.<ref name="Andrew1598">National
Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124,
testament testamentar and inventory of Andro Glasgw, made 6 February 1597/8,
death stated as 7 February, confirmed 10 July 1598; corrected working
transcription of the original register images. Printed index: Scottish Record
Society, ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments, 1514–1600'',
[https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso01scotuoft/page/n117/mode/1up
p. 108].</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The profile was created from the superseded reading of Andrew's testament. No
independent evidence has been found for this profile's name, estimated birth
date, marriage or children. The Andrew relationship is contradicted by the
corrected reading. It remains populated only as a temporary placeholder until
an evidenced Elizabeth Stevenson profile has passed duplicate review, avoiding
an unsourced gap during a coordinated correction. The attachment does not
constitute evidence that Jonet was Andrew's wife.

This profile is not an appropriate substitute for Elizabeth Stevenson. A
compatible [[Stevinsoune-1|Bessie Stevinsoune]] profile remains under duplicate
review, and silent repurposing would conceal how the error arose.
