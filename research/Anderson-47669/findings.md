## Source findings — Second Portglenone marriage list, 28 September 1893

A reproduced Second Portglenone Presbyterian marriage list ([RootsChat, 'Elder, Murdoch & McMeekin Families'](https://www.rootschat.com/forum/index.php?topic=214109.0); a derivative transcription) records Neal Anderson, full-age bachelor farmer of Gortreaghy, marrying Sarah Elder, full-age spinster of Gortreaghy, on 28 September 1893. It gives Sarah's father as **Robert Elder, farmer**, and the witnesses as **Robert Anderson and Nancy Elder**. This is consistent with a 1908 entry naming Alexander Elder as a son of a Robert Elder, but whether Sarah's father is the Robert Elder who married Mary Glasgow (Elder-7144 is a possible match) is unproved until the original register is inspected.
