## Peoria source findings — 23 October 2026

**Original family account, 23 October 2026:** The 1880 Peoria history, pp. 791–792, explicitly names Elizabeth Askren as Stewart Glasgow’s first wife and mother of Huston and an infant Elizabeth. It supports [Robert Houston Glasgow-1923](/people/glasgow-1923.html) as her son at **96%**, a first individual rating of the existing attachment. The sketch says Huston died aged 24; a 1925 recollection says 26, while his retained dates imply 23. No vital field or infant identity is altered. The child’s father [Stewart-17](/people/glasgow-17.html) is separately assessed 96% for that pair.

The 1880 sketch prints Stewart’s birth as 23 April 1823, versus the retained 13 April field. It says Huston died aged 24; the 1925 recollection says 26, while the retained 23 August 1848–15 October 1871 dates imply 23. The 1925 account’s death “2 or 3 years ago” also conflicts with Stewart’s retained 1904 death, and its “second wife” Edith omits Hadassah. The matching Peoria residence, first wife and son identify the family despite these specific errors; none supplies authority to overwrite the dates. Inspect Faris p. 63 and original death/marriage records to resolve them.
