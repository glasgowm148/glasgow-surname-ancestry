# Elizabeth Averns (Averns-99)

## Current conclusion

Elizabeth Averns married William Prime at Bickenhill in 1705 and was buried
there in 1736 as William's wife. She is the probable mother of Samuel Prime,
whose 1708 baptism names William but omits the mother. Her link to Samuel is
therefore uncertain.

- [Burial, 17 July 1736](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XXGX-MLH), Bickenhill: Elizabeth, wife of William Prime.
