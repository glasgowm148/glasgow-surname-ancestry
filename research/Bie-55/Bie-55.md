== Research Notes ==
=== Fisherrow family review — 17 October 2026 ===

Prefer the existing [[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]] and [[Bie-55|Christina Bie]] family account for the Fisherrow James and [[Bie-55|Christina Bye]]. The printed 1624–1625 index explicitly groups James, tailor in Fisherrow and burgess of Musselburgh, his son Alexander and wife [[Bie-55|Christina Bye]]; it separately names Alexander’s wife [[Clerk-296|Janet Clerk]]. Those are the same named family roles already represented by these profiles. The local James account also includes the Inveresk father sequence of 1609–1618, which can continue into the Fisherrow household. The ordinary Bye/Bie spelling and the full family fit make the identification probable, rather than a match resting only on James’s forename. This is an assessment of the preferred existing family account, not independent confirmation from its copied tree fields.

The Mid Calder and Inveresk baptism sequences may still represent one mobile father or two contemporaries; no occupation or property recital joins them. The profile’s birth and death places remain inherited claims. The signed-in FamilySearch record VQ4V-K87 was inspected on 17 October 2026: it names William, father James and the 10 July 1614 Inveresk event, but no mother. It therefore supplies no independent evidence for Christina Bie’s maternity, consistent with the FMP and supplied ScotlandsPeople entries. The earlier citation-based claim that this record named her is withdrawn; the underlying parish image remains uninspected. Christina’s 1589 New Aberdour birth is tree-derived. Naming her as James’s wife in 1624 does not establish her as Alexander’s or William’s mother. The [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow]] and Murieston-George ancestry alternatives retain their grades.
