Reviewed: 2026-10-15

Christina is a compatible candidate for **Christina Bye**, named as spouse of James Glasgow in the visually checked printed Fisherrow sasine index of 1624–1625. ## Source findings — Fisherrow household, 15 October 2026

The earlier sweep reports SRO *Indexes to the Particular Register of Sasines, Edinburgh etc.*, no.38 (1624–1630), pp.187–188: James Glasgow, tailor at Fisherrow and burgess of Musselburgh, spouse Christina Bye, son Alexander; Alexander’s spouse is Janet Clerk. Locators: 8 July 1624, vol.9 fol.135, and 11 July 1625, vol.10 fol.171 ([printed index p.187](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=mdp.39015072481057&seq=193)). The printed pages and column alignment were visually checked on 15 October 2026: Christina Bye is James’s spouse; Janet Clerk is Alexander’s. The column heading gives dates of recording, not verified execution dates. Original sasine clauses remain to inspect.

A cited 10 July 1614 Inveresk baptism for [William Glasgow (Glasgow-3517)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3517) names James Glasgow and Christina Bie ([FamilySearch record VQ4V-K87](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4V-K87)). The live profile’s citation was read on 15 October 2026; neither the cited provider record nor the parish image was inspected. A separately rechecked Findmypast transcript, R_686177762, names the 1614 child and father James but omits the mother and exact day. This makes the existing couple [James Glasgow (Glasgow-991)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-991) and Christina Bie a useful comparison for the sasines, without proving that all Mid Calder and Inveresk entries belong to one household.

The [consolidated household account](/research/subjects/early-scotland-1650/#fisherrow-household) and [Alexander Glasgow’s findings](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1148) retain the source locators, separate spouse clauses and identity limits.
