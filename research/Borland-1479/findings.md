# Findings: [Mary Borland (Borland-1479)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Borland-1479)

Last updated: 2026-09-27

## Current conclusion

Mary Borland is named with Robert Glasgow as parent in Irvine baptism transcripts for a John in 1736 and a John in 1738. Robert's separate adult profile Glasgow-3479 was merged on 27 September 2026 into [Glasgow-2474](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2474), the Robert baptised at Irvine in 1716. The same town and compatible chronology make that identity reasonable, though the later baptisms do not name Robert's parents. Mary's live biography now links the surviving father profile and notes the unresolved two-John question.
