# Bowen-14056 research findings

## Current conclusion

[Jonas Bowen (Bowen-14056)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Bowen-14056) is the
son of [Thomas Bowen (Bowen-15419)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Bowen-15419)
and [Sarah Gardner (Gardner-29435)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Gardner-29435).
The relationships are directly supported by Jonas's 1786 baptism. Thomas and
Sarah's 1764 marriage supplies Sarah's birth surname and the exact marriage
date and place. Both parent profiles, the spouse relationship, and the marriage
were created and verified live on 2 September 2026.
Their previously blank birth and death locations were set to Birmingham,
Warwickshire, England, and marked uncertain from the marriage and child
baptism records.

## Parentage assessment

The baptism explicitly identifies both parents. The marriage links the same
couple in the same parish and establishes Sarah as Gardner. No compatible prior
WikiTree profile was found for either parent during the live duplicate reviews.
