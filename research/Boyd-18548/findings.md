# Findings: [Mary Jane (Boyd) Glasgow (Boyd-18548)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Boyd-18548)

Last updated: 2026-09-12

## Current conclusion

Mary Jane Boyd of Tamlaght, daughter of Hugh Boyd, married Daniel Neely
Glasgow on 28 June 1888. The original birth returns name her as the mother of
Sarah Taylor Glasgow, born 8 September 1889, and Elizabeth Boyd Glasgow, born
9 September 1889. The original 1901 Tamlaght household calls both girls nieces
of Hugh S. Boyd. The Drimbolg memorial then names Mary Jane Glasgow as the
beloved sister of Hugh Scott and Joseph A. Boyd and Elizabeth Boyd Glasgow as
their niece. This continuous, conflict-free chain identifies the memorial woman
with Daniel's wife and Elizabeth with their daughter at high confidence.

The memorial records Mary Jane's death as 17 September 1958 aged 93, implying
birth about 1864–65. The current profile's c.1868 birth is therefore too late;
about 1865 is the better estimate. The memorial is a transcription rather than
an inspected stone image or civil death entry, so its age remains approximate.

## Remaining limits

The original stone image and civil death entry have not been inspected. Those
records may refine Mary Jane's age, but they are not required to identify her
with the documented Tamlaght family. Daniel's later New York identity remains a
separate research question.
