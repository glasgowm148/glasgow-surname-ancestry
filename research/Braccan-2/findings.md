## Current conclusion — newspaper identity review, 6 October 2026

- [The Public Register, or, Freeman's Journal, 1765-09-28, p.3](https://www.newspapers.com/image/1050029120/): Mrs. Glasco (forename and maiden name not printed) (deceased wife of Philip Glasco junior) — **probable** match. Braccan-2 ('Mary Braccan / Glasco', b. before 1747, d. 1765, spouse of Glasco-147) is the WikiTree profile created for this woman. Her only cited source is the Faulkner's Dublin Journal transcription of this same death. The notice identifies her only as the wife of Philip Glasco junior, so matching her to this profile is secure as a role match. However, the given name and maiden name on the profile are not supported. The RC baptism of Phil Glascoe at SS Michael & John, Dublin, in 1762 (DU-RC-BA-46402, quoted on Braccan-1) names the father Phil Glascoe and the mother only as 'Mary [nr]', and lists Mary Braccan as SPONSOR 2. 'Braccan' therefore seems to have been taken from the sponsor, not the mother. The forename Mary is compatible with the 1762 mother if the father there is the junior Philip.
