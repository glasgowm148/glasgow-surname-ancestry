== Biography ==

'''William Bryse''' was christened at Linlithgow on 14 November 1613. The indexed record names his father as John Bryse.<ref name="Baptism">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", FamilySearch ([https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RN-H3Y record X1RN-H3Y] : accessed 26 August 2026), William Bryse, christened 14 November 1613 at Linlithgow; father John Bryse.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

[[Bryse-15|John Bryse]] represents the same-name father recorded in William's baptism. His identity and estimated dates remain uncertain. [[Bryse-10|John Bryse]], baptised 10 March 1605, was previously attached but has been removed as William's father because he was only eight years old in 1613.
