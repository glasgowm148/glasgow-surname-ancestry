### Newspaper identity review — 6 October 2026

- Testament confirmed 27 June 1768 (Commissariot of Glasgow) as 'Margaret, wife of George Glasgow, of Nethermains, daughter of William B., of Drumakill' - so she died by 1768. The recorded death date 9 Mar 1796 matches a different entry ('Margaret, daughter of the deceased William B., of Carbeth') and should be reviewed. — Commissariot Record of Glasgow, Register of Testaments 1547-1800 (SRS 7), Buchanan list (archive.org OCR) ([The Caledonian Mercury, 1768-06-04, p.3](https://www.newspapers.com/image/395710454/))
