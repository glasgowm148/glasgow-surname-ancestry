# Findings: [Elizabeth Burr (Burr-5654)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Burr-5654)

## Current conclusion

Elizabeth Burr was baptised in 1751 at Bickenhill, Warwickshire, as a daughter
of John and Sarah Burr. She married Samuel Prime at Bickenhill on 13 November
1770. Their identification as the parents of Esther Prime is supported by
Esther's 1784 Coleshill baptism, which names Samuel and Elizabeth, together
with the marriage record that supplies Elizabeth's birth surname Burr.

Elizabeth's parents are [John Burr (Burr-5655)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Burr-5655)
and [Sarah (Unknown) Burr (Unknown-765121)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Unknown-765121).
No cited record establishes Sarah's maiden surname.
