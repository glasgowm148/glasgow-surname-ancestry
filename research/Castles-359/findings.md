# Findings: [Jean Castles (Castles-359)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Castles-359)

## Current conclusion

Jean Castles was christened on 24 June 1722 at Lesmahagow, daughter of
**William Castles** and **Agnes Weir**. The baptism directly names both parents.
Her marriage to James Guil and the Douglas child records give moderate-high,
but not fully direct, continuity from the baptism to the adult.

## WikiTree duplicate audit

- [William Cassels (Cassels-181)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Cassels-181), born in 1716, is chronologically impossible as the father of the 1722 child. Searches of Castles, Cassels and Castle found no compatible William.
- [Agnes Weir (Weir-3392)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Weir-3392), born 1721 and married Andrew Fairservice, is too young and is a different woman.
- [Agnes Weir (Weir-1915)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Weir-1915) is attached to Archbald Gallaway; `Weir-1136`, `Weir-1138`, and `Weir-4625` have incompatible localities, dates, or family contexts. None is a proved match.

Lanarkshire, Scotland as an uncertain location inferred from their daughter's
