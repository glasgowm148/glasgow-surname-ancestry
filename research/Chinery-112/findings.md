# Findings: [Thomas Chinery (Chinery-112)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Chinery-112)

## Current conclusion

[Thomas Chinery (Chinery-112)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Chinery-112), born
1 April 1782 in Birmingham, has one attached parent: father [Thomas Chinery
(Chinery-113)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Chinery-113). The recursive paternal
branch continues to [Thomas Chinery (Chinery-114)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Chinery-114),
born about 1725. On 2 September 2026, [Elizabeth Key (Key-6784)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Key-6784)
was created and attached as Chinery-113's mother, and [Mathew Chinnerey
(Chinnerey-1)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Chinnerey-1) was created and
attached as Chinery-114's explicitly uncertain father. Chinery-112's mother
remains unattached.

The concrete parents for Chinery-112 are **Thomas Chinery and Mary Wright**.
This is supported by the reported St Martin's, Birmingham marriage of Thomas
Chinery and Mary Wright on 11 March 1776 and the baptism list that records their
son Thomas on 1 April 1782. Both are transcriptions reported in Birmingham
local-history discussions, not inspected register images. The existing spouse
relationship links Chinery-112 to [Jane Swift
(Swift-7403)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Swift-7403). Their FamilySearch
marriage record places the couple at Birmingham in July 1803. Jane's live
profile was given record-level citations and verified on 2 September 2026.

The 28 October 1753 Uttoxeter baptism directly names Thomas and Elizabeth as
parents of the younger Thomas represented by Chinery-113. Their 3 February 1741
Uttoxeter marriage identifies the mother as Elizabeth Key. No compatible prior
WikiTree profile was found, so Key-6784 was created and linked.

The best-supported father for the true root, Chinery-114, is Mathew Chinnerey.
A 6 September 1718 Uttoxeter baptism directly names Mathew as father of Thomas.
The child is the right age to marry Elizabeth Key there in 1741, their first
recorded son was named Mathew, and their children were baptised in the same
parish. This is strong identity continuity but not a single record spanning
baptism and marriage, so Chinnerey-1 is attached as an **uncertain** father.

## Parentage assessment

### Mary Wright: Chinery-112 mother

Mary Wright is the supported mother for Chinery-112. The Birmingham baptism
sequence explicitly groups the 1 April 1782 baptism with children of Thomas and
Mary, and the profile's 1803 Birmingham marriage is consistent with that child
remaining in the same locality. The mother's given name is therefore concrete;
the surname Wright comes from the reported 11 March 1776 St Martin's marriage.
No compatible WikiTree profile for Mary Wright was established by an exact-name
search tied to Thomas Chinery; no FamilySearch person profile was found that
can safely be identified as this Mary. Retain the relationship as
evidence-backed, but do not upgrade beyond the quality of the online reports
until the register image or an independent marriage record is inspected.

### Mother of Chinery-113

The 1753 baptism names Elizabeth as mother and the 1741 marriage names the
couple as Thomas Chinnery and Elizabeth Key. Elizabeth Key is therefore the
supported mother. A WikiTree search for Elizabeth Key in Staffordshire found no
compatible eighteenth-century profile.

### Parents of Chinery-114

Mathew Chinnerey is directly named as father in the 1718 Uttoxeter baptism. The
same Thomas is strongly indicated by his age, the rare surname, continuous
parish, 1741 marriage, and first son Mathew. Because the identification is
assembled across records, attach Mathew as uncertain. The baptism names no
mother, so leave that field blank.

## Duplicate and identity cautions

The FamilySearch profile `MD8T-1N9` is a matching profile for Chinery-114. Its
Uttoxeter marriage and child cluster show that WikiTree's estimated Birmingham
birthplace is stale family-context inference. No Mathew Chinnerey/Chinnery or
compatible Elizabeth Key profile was found in WikiTree's surname searches.
