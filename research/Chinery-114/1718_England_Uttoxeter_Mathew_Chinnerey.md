== Biography ==

Mathew Chinnerey was the father named when Thomas Chinnerey was baptised at
Uttoxeter, Staffordshire, on 6 September 1718.<ref name="Thomas1718">FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:NXJG-9LR "England, Births and Christenings, 1538-1975"], Thomas Chinnerey, baptised 6 September 1718 at Uttoxeter, father Mathew Chinnerey.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The baptised child is the strong candidate for [[Chinery-114|Thomas Chinery]],
who married Elizabeth Key at Uttoxeter in 1741 and named his first recorded son
Mathew. The profile relationship should remain uncertain because no single
record explicitly links the 1718 child to the 1741 groom.
