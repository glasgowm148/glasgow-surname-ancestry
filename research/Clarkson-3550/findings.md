# Findings: Clarkson-3550

## Current conclusion

David Clarkson, born 6 February 1810 and baptised 11 February 1810 at Douglas,
Lanarkshire, is an ancestor of [M Glasgow (Glasgow-933)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-933).
His attached parents are [John Clarkson (Clarkson-4004)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Clarkson-4004)
and [Elizabeth Gold (Gold-2337)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Gold-2337).

The earlier statement that David had no parents was incorrect. "Tree
continues" means attached ancestry exists beyond the displayed generation; it
does not mean David's parents are absent. David's baptism names John Clarkson
and Elizabeth Gold, so these are concrete parents at record level.

David married Janet/Jessie Ritchie at Old Monkland on 24 August 1834 and a
matching proclamation was recorded at Larbert on 7 September 1834. His profile
gives a death date of 6 December 1876, but no death-registration citation is
currently attached; that date should remain qualified.
