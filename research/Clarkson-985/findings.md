# Findings: [William Clakson (Clarkson-985)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Clarkson-985)

## Current conclusion

William's 14 October 1716 Dalkeith baptism explicitly names James Clerksone
and Jean Threepland as parents. Compatible existing profiles are [James
Clerksone (Clerksone-2)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Clerksone-2) and [Jean
Threepland (Threepland-1)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Threepland-1). This is
the safer of the two parent leads audited on 2 September 2026, but it should
remain uncertain until evidence connects the Dalkeith child to the William who
married Agnes Nimmo and raised children at Wiston and Roberton.

Image unavailable; digital folder `007908836`, microfilm `1066679`, batch `C11683-5`.
- FamilySearch PID [`KCRK-KQ5`](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/KCRK-KQ5) combines that baptism with Agnes Nimmo, their 1734 Wiston and Roberton marriage, and their children. The combined tree supplies continuity as a lead, not as an independent record.
- WikiTree `Clerksone-2` is the only exact James Clerksone result near 1698 and cites the 1716 child record. `Threepland-1` is the only compatible Jean Threepland result and is attached as James's wife. Its displayed 1698 birth conflicts with biography text transcribing a 1658 christening and needs correction separately.

## Parentage assessment

**Safer immediate link, but uncertain:** attach the existing James and Jean
profiles only if retaining an uncertain relationship status. The source proves
the parents of the Dalkeith child; it does not by itself prove that this child
was the later Wiston husband and father.

Jean's profile also needs a separate identity correction: the chronologically
compatible woman was baptised in 1693 at Lasswade, not 1658 at South Leith.
See `research/Threepland-1/findings.md` before changing this family.
