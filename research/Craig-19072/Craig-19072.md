== Biography ==
'''Elizabeth (Elspeth) Craig''' was born around 1560 and lived in Glasgow, Scotland, during the late 16th and early 17th centuries. She is known for her connection to [[Hutcheson-1648|George Hutcheson (1554–1639)]], the Glasgow notary, banker, and philanthropist who co-founded Hutchesons’ Hospital and School.

In 1588, the Glasgow kirk session records show that George Hutcheson fathered a child out of wedlock with Elspeth Craig. The session required George to publicly repent and marry Elspeth within the month, which he did. Their daughter, [[Hutcheson-1650|Janet Hutcheson (1587–1684)]], remained a natural (illegitimate) child because there is no record of a formal legitimation. No other children are documented from this marriage.

Elspeth is believed to have been related to John Craig, a writer and notary public in Glasgow — likely his sister or daughter. She lived with George in their house on the north side of the Trongate, beside the tolbooth, where he ran his legal practice and early banking business.

She remained George’s wife for forty-four years until his death in 1639. Little else is known about her later life.

== Family ==
* Possible father or brother: John Craig, notary public in Glasgow
* Spouse: [[Hutcheson-1648|George Hutcheson (1554–1639)]]
* Children:
** [[Hutcheson-1650|Janet Hutcheson (1587–1684)]], natural daughter

== Legacy ==
Through her marriage, Elspeth Craig’s family line became connected to the Hutchesons, whose charitable bequests — Hutchesons’ Hospital and Hutchesons’ School — continue to impact Glasgow today.
