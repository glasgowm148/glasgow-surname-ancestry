== Biography ==
'''Elizabeth (Elspeth) Craig''' was born around 1560 and lived in Glasgow, Scotland, during the late 16th and early 17th centuries. She is known for her connection to [[Hutcheson-1648|George Hutcheson (1554–1639)]], the Glasgow notary, banker, and philanthropist who co-founded Hutchesons’ Hospital and School.

The published history reports that in 1588 the Glasgow kirk session required George Hutcheson to publicly repent and marry Elspeth within the month after the birth of their unnamed daughter. It does not identify this girl as [[Hutcheson-1650|Janet Hutcheson]], later named in the council accounts in Holland in 1679, 1680 and 1684. Those accounts do not establish Janet's identity as Elspeth's child.<ref name="GlasgowHistory">''The History of Glasgow'', volume 2, chapter XX, [https://www.electricscotland.com/history/glasgow/glasgow2_20.htm “George Hutcheson, Notary, Banker, and Philanthropist”]. Published account of the 1588 kirk session proceedings and Janet's later support; original kirk minute not inspected.</ref>

Elspeth is believed to have been related to [[Craig-19073|John Craig]], a writer and notary public in Glasgow, as his sister or daughter; fatherhood remains uncertain.<ref name="GlasgowHistory" /> She lived with George in their house on the north side of the Trongate, beside the tolbooth, where he ran his legal practice and early banking business.

The history describes their marriage as lasting forty-four years.<ref name="GlasgowHistory" /> Little else is known about her later life.

== Legacy ==
Through her marriage, Elspeth Craig’s family line became connected to the Hutchesons, whose charitable bequests — Hutchesons’ Hospital and Hutchesons’ School — continue to impact Glasgow today.
