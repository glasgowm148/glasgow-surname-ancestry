## Source findings — 1588 kirk session account, 7 October 2026

The reviewed draft biography reports that in 1588 the Glasgow kirk session found that [George Hutcheson (Hutcheson-1648)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hutcheson-1648), notary, had fathered a child by Elspeth Craig, and required him to make public repentance and marry her within the month. The child is an unnamed girl in the cited account. The draft's identification of her as [Janet Hutcheson (Hutcheson-1650)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hutcheson-1650) is superseded: the council accounts name Janet in Holland in 1679, 1680 and 1684, but do not establish that she was Elspeth's 1588 child. The cited history treats Janet separately. Elspeth's supposed relationship to John Craig, notary in Glasgow, remains the basis for attaching [John Craig (Craig-19073)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Craig-19073) as an uncertain father, with the sister alternative retained.

The draft lists *The History of Glasgow*, vol. 2, chapter XX ([electricscotland text](https://www.electricscotland.com/history/glasgow/glasgow2_20.htm)) and "Glasgow Kirk Session Records, 1588" as sources. The history's HTML text was checked on 15 November 2026 and preserves the daughter-or-sister qualification and the distinction between the unnamed girl and Janet.
