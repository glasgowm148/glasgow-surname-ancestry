# Findings: [Mary (Cranmer) Glasgow (Cranmer-656)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Cranmer-656)

## Current conclusion

[Find a Grave memorial 93259804](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/93259804/john-glasgow)
is for Mary's husband John Glasgow, not Mary. Its memorial narrative explicitly
states that Mary Cranmer Glasgow was buried beside John and their son in the old
Nottingham Presbyterian churchyard before the identified graves were reinterred
at West Nottingham Cemetery in 1960. The memorial is therefore a derivative
source about Mary despite not being her own memorial. WT+ hint 572 is a false
positive and the link should be retained with its scope stated clearly.
