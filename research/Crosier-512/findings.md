# Findings: Mary, wife of James Glasgow (Crosier-512)

Last updated: 2026-10-06

## Current conclusion

James Glasgow's 1775 will directly names his wife **Mary**. The 1817-1818 Wilson
inheritance pleadings say Mary was a daughter of one of Archibald Wilson's six
sisters. Because James's mother Rachel was another sister, the pleaded
relationships make James and Mary first cousins.

Neither the will nor the equity papers gives Mary the birth surname
Crozier/Crosier or identifies her mother. Preserve Mary as James's wife and as a
daughter of an unidentified Wilson sister, but treat the `Crosier-512` surname
and exact parentage as unproved.
