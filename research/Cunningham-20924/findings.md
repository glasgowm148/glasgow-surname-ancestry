# Findings: [Jean Cunningham (Cunningham-20924)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Cunningham-20924)

## Current conclusion

Jean's marriage to [John Glasgow (Glasgow-1073)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1073) is supported by a Kilbirnie parish marriage entry dated 14 January 1690. A published Ayrshire history describes her as a daughter of John Cuninghame of Wattieston, but that parentage remains derivative until the relevant original record is identified. An older project page also mis-associated this same profile with Robert Glasgow, M.D.; do not conflate the marriages.
