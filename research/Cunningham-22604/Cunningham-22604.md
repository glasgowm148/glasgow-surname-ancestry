== Biography ==
'''Jonet Cunningham'''—also indexed as '''Jonet Cunnyngham'''—was probably born in Scotland during the early seventeenth century. Her current estimated birth year of about 1624 is uncertain.

The original Glasgow parish register records [[Glasgow-3971|James Glasgow]] on 25 November 1645 as the son of Robert Glasgow and Jonet Cunynghame, with James Cunynghame and Johne Muir named as godfathers.<ref name="James1645">Scotland's People, Church of Scotland Old Parish Registers, births and baptisms, Glasgow parish 644/1, reference 40/56, James Glasgow, male, 25 November 1645, parents indexed as “Rot. Glasgow/Jonet Cunnyngham”. Original register image inspected 31 August 2026; it names James Cunynghame and Johne Muir as godfathers. See also [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQHK-VMK FamilySearch record VQHK-VMK], a derivative index to the same event.</ref> This directly establishes Jonet as the mother named in that record.

The attached husband, [[Glasgow-1078|Robert Glasgow]], baptised at Glasgow in 1618, is chronologically compatible but is not identified by the index. Their relationship remains uncertain.

No reliable record has yet been identified for Jonet’s parents, marriage, later residence or death.

=== The 1645 child ===

The Scottish child is [[Glasgow-3971|James Glasgow]], whose profile now carries the exact indexed date and register reference.<ref name="James1645" />

He has been proposed as identical with [[Glasgow-1081|James Glasgow of Kilwaughter]], County Antrim, first securely recorded there in 1669. No inspected record supplies migration, residence, occupation or family evidence joining them. They remain separate profiles; Jonet should not be attached to Glasgow-1081 on the present evidence.

=== Proposed husband ===

[[Glasgow-1078|Robert Glasgow]], baptised on 17 November 1618, remains a possible father of the 1645 child. The register gives the father only as Robert Glasgow and supplies no age, occupation, residence, birthplace or parentage, so it cannot identify him as the 1618 child. The relationship should remain uncertain.

An exact-surname Scotland-wide search of the indexed Church of Scotland marriage registers for Robert Glasgow from 1630 through 1650 returned no result.<ref>Scotland's People, [https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/search-records/church-registers/church-banns-marriages/opr-marriages Old Parish Register marriages search], exact surname Glasgow and forename beginning Robert, 1630–1650, searched 31 August 2026.</ref> This limited negative does not cover spelling variants, register gaps or unindexed entries and does not show that the couple never married.

=== Not the older Jonet Conynghame ===

This Jonet must not be confused with [[Cunningham-20923|Jonet Conynghame]], wife of [[Glasgow-1027|Robert Glasgow]] and mother of the Robert baptised in 1618. They belong to different proposed generations.

=== Cunningham–Muir associates ===

The obvious James Cunningham, merchant and burgess admitted in 1629, had a testament confirmed on 31 January 1642 and therefore cannot have been the living godfather in 1645. Other men of the name—including James Cunningham of Easter Garden and later entrants associated with Dunoon and Cobblehaugh—remain possible, but none has been connected to Jonet or this baptism.<ref name="Indexes">James R. Anderson, ed., [https://www.electricscotland.com/history/records/scottishrecordso43scotuoft.pdf ''Burgesses & Guild Brethren of Glasgow, 1573–1750'']; Scottish Record Society, [https://archive.org/stream/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/scottishrecordso07scotuoft_djvu.txt ''The Commissariot Record of Glasgow: Register of Testaments, 1547–1800''], surname entries for Cunningham and Muir.</ref>

The testament index excludes a Johne Muir described as a Glasgow tailor whose testament was confirmed in 1640 and a mariner whose testament was confirmed in 1642. Later entries for men described as a maltman, weaver, doctor and of Langside leave several possible living candidates. Neither godfather has yet been identified, so their presence cannot establish Jonet's parentage or kin group.

=== Further research ===

The most useful next sources are records identifying the godfathers James Cunynghame and Johne Muir; other baptisms to a Robert Glasgow and Jonet Cunningham; a marriage record or contract under Cunningham spelling variants; and craft, property or testamentary records identifying the parents.
