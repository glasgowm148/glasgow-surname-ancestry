# Findings: [Jonet Cunningham (Cunningham-22604), born about 1624, proposed Glasgow-to-Kilwaughter family](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Cunningham-22604)

## Current conclusion

This profile should represent the **younger Jonet Cunningham** reportedly named
as mother of James Glasgow in a November 1645 Glasgow parish entry. She is
distinct from [Jonet Cunningham (Cunningham-20923)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Cunningham-20923),
who was mother of Robert baptised in 1618.

The Scotland's People register records the event on 25 November 1645 in parish
Glasgow, OPR `644/1`, reference `40/56`. The purchased original image names
James's godfathers as **James Cunynghame and Johne Muir**. It directly supports
Jonet as mother of the **Scottish child** and provides the first concrete FAN
lead for her family. Her
identification as wife of [Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-1078)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1078)
and the separate identification of her Scottish son with the later
Kilwaughter James are **possible but unproved**, because neither identity link
is stated in the register.

Live-profile audit **31 August 2026**: the structured son and biography now
correctly point to [James Glasgow (Glasgow-3971)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3971),
while the distinct Kilwaughter elder
[Glasgow-1081](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1081) remains separate.

## Godfather candidate audit

James Cunningham, merchant and burgess admitted in Glasgow in 1629, died before
his testament was confirmed on 31 January 1642 and cannot be the 1645
godfather. Several other contemporary James Cunninghams remain possible, but no
record links one to Jonet. For Johne Muir, testament evidence excludes a tailor
dead by 1640 and a mariner dead by 1642, while several other John Muirs survived
past 1645. The witnesses are useful FAN evidence, but neither has yet been
identified and neither supplies Jonet's parentage.
