# Findings: [Anne (Cushnehan) Glasgow (Cushnehan-1)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Cushnehan-1)

Last updated: 2026-08-25

## Current conclusion

Anne Cushnehan was born about 1827 in County Antrim. She was a daughter of
[[Cushnehan-2|Hugh Cushnehan]], weaver, and had previously married a man
surnamed Wilson. The original marriage register directly states her father,
so the relationship is marked **confident** on WikiTree.

As the 25-year-old widow Anne Wilson, she married [[Glasgow-4048|Hugh Glasgow]],
a weaver of Liminary, on 24 December 1852. The marriage record directly names
her father and establishes her birth surname.

Anne died at Liminary on 14 March 1877. She was described as married, aged 54
and a weaver's wife. Nathaniel Glasgow was present at death, but no relationship
is stated.
