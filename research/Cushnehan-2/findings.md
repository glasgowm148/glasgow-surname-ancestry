# Findings: [Hugh Cushnehan (Cushnehan-2)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Cushnehan-2)

Last updated: 2026-08-25

## Current conclusion

Hugh Cushnehan was a weaver in County Antrim. The original 1852 marriage
register for [[Cushnehan-1|Anne Cushnehan]], widow Wilson, directly identifies
her father as Hugh Cushnehan, weaver. Their father-daughter relationship is
therefore marked **confident** on WikiTree.

Hugh's birth is conservatively entered as before 1812 because Anne was about
25 in 1852. This is only an estimate; the record gives no age or birthplace
for Hugh. It also does not expressly state whether he was living or deceased.

No wife or additional child has yet been identified.

## Primary source

- [Marriage of Hugh Glasgow and Anne Wilson, entry 49, 24 December 1852](https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1852/09422/5413278.pdf).
