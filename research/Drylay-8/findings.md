# Johne Drylay (Drylay-8)

## Current conclusion

Johne Drylay, later John Drylie/Dryelly, was baptised at Dunfermline on 28
December 1673 as the son of William Drylay and Bessie Stevinson. `Dryelly-2`
represented the same record bearer and was merged into `Drylay-8` on 2
September 2026, preserving the register spelling as the birth surname and the
later profile's spouse, descendants, and sourced biography.
