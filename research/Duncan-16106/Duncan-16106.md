== Research Notes ==
=== Edinburgh apprentice family ===
The Greyfriars register names Agnes as wife of James Glasgow, weaver, at her burial on 21 January 1674. Its entries under Duncan (printed page196) and Glasgow (page252) cross-index the same burial and are not independent witnesses. The date identifies her interment rather than her exact death.

This fits [[Glasgow-1150|James Glasgow’s]] wife Agnes Duncan. Her burial precedes the children of [[Glasgow-2682|James Glasgow]] and [[Duffe-18|Isobell Duffe]], recorded from 1677. Successive marriages are therefore compatible, although the two James accounts have not been identified as one adult. The late apprentices’ mothers are unstated; Agnes is not established as mother of [[Glasgow-4114|James]] or [[Glasgow-3285|Thomas]].<ref name="EdinburghBrothersReview" />
