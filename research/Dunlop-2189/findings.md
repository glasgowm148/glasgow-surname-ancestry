# Findings: Dunlop-2189

Last updated: 2026-10-03

## Current conclusion

A contemporary marriage notice expressly identifies Rachel as daughter of the late John Dunlop of Dunlop. [John Dunlop-2144](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Dunlop-2144), 1707–1785, is a strong compatible father profile: its inspected public biography lists Rachel’s baptism on 17 May 1769 to Mr Dunlop of Dunlop. This record does not establish Rachel’s mother. No live parent attachment is made here.

## Source findings

[Caledonian Mercury, 1 March 1802, p.3](https://www.newspapers.com/image/393025725/): at Dunlop-house on the **22d ult.**, Robert Glasgow of Montgreenan married Miss Rachel Dunlop, daughter of the late John Dunlop of Dunlop. Original passage inspected 3 October 2026. The newspaper gives **22 February 1802**; the maintained Dunlop OPR citation (Ref.20/211) gives **23 February**. Preserve both until the original register is checked.

## Unresolved questions

Compare the original marriage register and Rachel’s baptism. Check the compatible father profile against the parish family; do not infer a mother from his spouse alone.
