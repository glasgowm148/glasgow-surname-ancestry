# Findings: [Grizel Dunlop](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Dunlop-3562)

## Current conclusion

Reviewed: 2026-10-15

Grizel is named as mother of [Alexander Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3101), with [George Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3100) as father, in the 1659 Campbeltown index accounts. The mother link was already marked confident; this review does not count it as newly proved. Her own circa-1640 birth, origin and parents are unproved.

## Source findings

The saved [Findmypast transcript](https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3017984&tab=this), OPR 507/1, page 1, item 3, names Alexander, father George Glasgow, mother Grizel Dunlop and residence Auchinhork. It gives **13 April 1659 in the birth-date field**, with no baptism date. The supplied Scotland’s People index gives **12 April 1659**, Campbeltown, 507, reference 11/6, FR9, with the same parents. These are representations of one parish event, not independent events. The original image has not been inspected; retain both literal dates and reconcile the volume/page conventions.

The entry does not establish a marriage date, Grizel’s own parents, or her maternity of George’s other attached or suggested children. See [George’s household and origin comparison](/people/glasgow-3100.html#archive-tools).
