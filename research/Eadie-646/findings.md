# Findings: Eadie-646

Last updated: 2026-09-02

## Current conclusion

[Ann Eadie (Eadie-646)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Eadie-646) has no attached
parents. The evidence strongly identifies her parents as **John Eadie** and
**Helen Crawford**, but new WikiTree profiles should remain **HOLD** until two
plausible existing-profile candidates are resolved.

The live profile now records Ann's exact birth on **5 December 1794**, retains
her 7 December baptism, cites the stable FamilySearch baptism, and records the
FamilySearch parent PIDs with duplicate cautions. No structural parent links
were added: [John Adie (Adie-192)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Adie-192) and
[Helen Crawford (Crawford-30067)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Crawford-30067)
remain unresolved candidates.

The parent relationship itself is well supported by three mutually consistent
records:

1. Ann's indexed Dunblane baptism names her father as John Eadie.
2. John Eadie married Helen Crawford at Dunblane in 1787, seven years before
   Ann's birth.
3. Ann's 1866 statutory death registration names both parents, John Eadie and
   Helen Crawford.

This is enough to treat the names as Ann's record-supported parents. It is not
enough to accept the unsupported FamilySearch birth estimates for the parents
or to bypass the WikiTree duplicate candidates.

## WikiTree duplicate audit

### John Eadie

Searches covered `Eadie`, `Edie` and `Adie`, about 1758, Dunblane and wider
Scotland.

- [John Eadie (Eadie-583)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Eadie-583), born 7
  December 1766 at Falkirk and died 7 February 1839 at Dysart, is a different
  indexed identity. His profile has no spouse or children but the exact
  Falkirk birth and Fife death do not match FamilySearch's unsupported 1758
  Dunblane reconstruction.
- [John Edie (Edie-396)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Edie-396), born before
  14 January 1768 at Logie, Fife, and died in 1800, is geographically distinct
  and has a separate documented identity.
- [John Adie (Adie-192)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Adie-192), born 24
  October 1765 at Muthill, Perthshire, is a **plausible candidate**, not a
  rejected duplicate. He would have been 21 at the 1787 marriage, Muthill is
  within the regional Perthshire network, and his profile has no spouse,
  children or death. Its baptism names Andrew Adie and Elizabeth Cairns
  ([FamilySearch VQWQ-W76](https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQWQ-W76)).
  No record yet connects or excludes him from the Dunblane marriage.

No existing WikiTree profile was found for a John Eadie born 1758 at Dunblane
and married to Helen Crawford. `Adie-192` must be resolved before creating a
new John.

### Helen Crawford

Searches covered Helen, Ellen and Hellen Crawford, about 1763, Dunblane and
wider Scotland.

- [Helen Crawford (Crawford-30067)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Crawford-30067),

No exact WikiTree profile was found for Helen Crawford, wife of John Eadie at
Dunblane. `Crawford-30067` should be compared against the marriage and later
family records before creating a new Helen.
