## Record findings and identity limits — 17 October 2026

A possible identification concerns Robert Elder. The reproduced Third Portglenone Presbyterian marriage list records Robert Elder, full-age bachelor labourer of Ballymoney, marrying Jane Adams McCaughey, full-age spinster of Garvaghey, on 13 July 1887. Robert's father is recorded as James Elder, labourer; Jane's father as William McCaughey, labourer. (RootsChat, Elder, Murdoch & McMeekin Families; [record](https://www.rootschat.com/forum/index.php?topic=214109.0).) The witnesses were Archie McCaughey and Lizzie Connaghy. This Robert is distinct from Mary Glasgow’s 1871 husband, Robert Elder, whose father was Nathaniel Elder and whose occupation was farmer. The source is a secondary transcription and the original register remains to be inspected. The identification remains uncertain.
