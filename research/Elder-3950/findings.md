## Record findings and identity limits — 17 October 2026

A possible identification concerns Elizabeth Elder. The same 1911 Gortereghy browse lists two further Elder households: Joseph Elder, aged 79, with Ellen Elder, aged 38, recorded as his niece, and William Elder, aged 40, with Elizabeth Elder, aged 50, recorded as his sister. This demonstrates that Gortereghy contained several distinct Elder households at the same time. (1911 Gortereghy, Joseph Elder household; [record](https://api-census.nationalarchives.ie/census/image/nai001390154.pdf); 1911 Gortereghy, William Elder household; [record](https://api-census.nationalarchives.ie/census/image/nai001390136.pdf).) The identification remains uncertain.

A possible identification concerns Elizabeth Elder. The original 1901 return names James Elder 80, wife Mary 73, daughter Lizzie 25 and son William 23. William and Elizabeth are a proved sibling pair in 1911, aged 40 and 50. These are substantial original-record age conflicts, not index errors. [Elder-3951](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Elder-3951) and [Elder-3950](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Elder-3950) remain compatible together, while identifying the 1901 children with the 1911 pair and attaching the latter to [Elder-3946](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Elder-3946) remains uncertain. (1901 Gortereghy, James Elder household; [record](https://api-census.nationalarchives.ie/census/image/nai000298444.pdf); 1911 Gortereghy, William Elder household; [record](https://api-census.nationalarchives.ie/census/image/nai001390136.pdf).) The identification remains uncertain.
