## Record findings and identity limits — 17 October 2026

A possible identification concerns Annie Elder. The reproduced marriage list records Alex Hillis, full-age bachelor merchant of Ballymena, marrying Annie Elder, full-age spinster of Gortereghy, on 7 July 1857. Annie's father is recorded as James Elder, farmer; witnesses were James McChesney and Robert Bell. (RootsChat, Elder, Murdoch & McMeekin Families; [record](https://www.rootschat.com/forum/index.php?topic=214109.0).) It adds another distinct Elder household in the Gortereghy area and names two witnesses for future tracing, but it does not connect Annie or James Elder to Robert Elder, whose 1871 marriage names Nathaniel Elder as father. The source is a secondary transcription and the original register remains to be inspected. The identification remains uncertain.
