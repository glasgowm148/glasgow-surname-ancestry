# John Elder of Rasharkin (Elder-5907)

Last updated: 2026-10-07

## Current conclusion

The 12 May 1886 marriage in the reviewed Second Portglenone account matches this existing profile: John is a Rasharkin grocer, his father is John Elder, farmer; his bride is Annie Patterson, aged 20, daughter of David Patterson, farmer. The public biography cites the same civil marriage and the 1901 Carnfinton household. The 1912 Rasharkin grocer/postmaster is compatible with this man; the 1842 John Elder–Peggy Stuart groom is a separate older subject, with Elder-7230 and Elder-4387 retained as candidates. No father–son bridge between the 1842 and 1886 men is established.

## Original civil entry inspected, 30 September 2026

The [Second Portglenone Presbyterian civil marriage register, printed p. 53, PDF p. 1, entry 86](https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1886/10833/5951328.pdf) records John Elder, a full-age bachelor and grocer of Rasharkin, marrying Annie Patterson, 20, spinster of Rasharkin, on 12 May 1886. It names John Elder, farmer, and David Patterson, farmer, as their respective fathers. Jane M’Caughern is a witness; the other witness’s M’Cahon surname is readable, but the forename was not confidently read in this inspection. The original confirms the record identification already matched to Elder-5907. It does not identify the 1842 namesake as his father or resolve the 1912 continuity. No new father-confidence upgrade is claimed.

## Source findings — 1912 Belfast and Ulster Directory, Rasharkin, 17 September 2026

The [1912 Belfast and Ulster Directory, Rasharkin](https://lennonwylie.co.uk/PT191214.htm) (online transcription) lists **John Elder, junior, grocer and postmaster**, among the inhabitants of Rasharkin, and in the neighbourhood section separately lists James Elder and James Elder, junior, at Ballydonnelly. The grocer fits this John, the Rasharkin grocer of the 1886 marriage; reading 'junior' as pointing to his father John is an inference. A directory is not a parentage record: it does not make either James a son or brother of Robert Elder, and the identification remains compatible, not proved.
