## Record findings and identity limits — 17 October 2026

A possible identification concerns Robert Elder. The published headstone transcription for Rasharkin Second Presbyterian Church records a memorial erected by Alexander Elder of Gortrickey to his father Robert Elder, his mother Jane, and his sisters and brothers. It also records Alexander Elder's death on 5 January 1943. (Rasharkin Second Presbyterian Church headstones; [record](https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/RasharkinP/graveyard.html).) This is a new direct memorial transcription for the catalogue's parentage study, not a Glasgow record and not proof that the Elder family was related to Alexander Glasgow. Its value is locality context: Gortrickey/Gortereghy is the townland where the Glasgow family appears in the 1837–1838 Rasharkin Presbyterian baptism entries. The memorial supplies a named Elder household for future comparison with the later Robert Elder who occupied Alexander Glasgow's former Lisnagaver lot; identity between those Elder references remains unproved. The identification remains uncertain.

A possible identification concerns Robert Elder. The same reproduced Second Portglenone Presbyterian marriage list records Neal Anderson, full-age bachelor farmer of Gortreaghy, marrying Sarah Elder, full-age spinster of Gortreaghy, on 28 September 1893. Sarah's father is recorded as Robert Elder, farmer; witnesses were Robert Anderson and Nancy Elder. (RootsChat, Elder, Murdoch & McMeekin Families; [record](https://www.rootschat.com/forum/index.php?topic=214109.0).) This is a new parent-bearing record for the Gortreaghy Elder household and is consistent with the 1908 entry naming Alexander Elder as a son of a Robert Elder, but the shared father's identity and any connection to Robert Elder, husband of Mary Glasgow, remain unproved until the original register is inspected. The identification remains uncertain.

A possible identification concerns Robert Elder. The reproduced Second Portglenone Presbyterian marriage list records Isaac Elder, full-age bachelor farmer of Gohorney, marrying Jane Elliott on 19 February 1903. Isaac's father is recorded as Robert Elder, farmer. (RootsChat, Elder, Murdoch & McMeekin Families; [record](https://www.rootschat.com/forum/index.php?topic=214109.0).) This is another catalogue-absent parent-bearing record for a Robert Elder household in the Rasharkin area. It supports the existence of a substantial local Elder family network, but does not by itself identify Isaac's Robert with Robert Elder, husband of Mary Glasgow, or establish any Glasgow relationship. The identification remains uncertain.

A possible identification concerns Robert Elder. A 2007 RootsChat research post reproduces a 18 June 1908 Second Portglenone Presbyterian marriage entry for Alexander Elder, full-age bachelor farmer of Gortreaghy, whose father is recorded as Robert Elder, farmer. Alexander married Mary Anderson, full-age spinster of Gortreaghy, daughter of Neil Anderson; witnesses were Robert Anderson and Margaret Elder. (RootsChat, Elder, Murdoch & McMeekin Families; [record](https://www.rootschat.com/forum/index.php?topic=214109.0).) The entry is not yet an inspected original, so it is a secondary-source lead rather than final evidence. It is nevertheless a new, specific parent-bearing record for the Gortreaghy Elder household and gives a testable route for determining whether the Alexander Elder on the Rasharkin Second Presbyterian memorial was a son of the Robert Elder who later occupied Alexander Glasgow's Lisnagaver lot. That identity chain remains unproved. The identification remains uncertain.

A possible identification concerns Robert Elder. The original 1901 Gortereghy return records Robert Elder, 65, widowed farmer, with Alex 27 and Robert 20 as sons and Eliza 22 and Margaretta 14 as daughters. The 1911 Alexander/Mary household explicitly records Robert as Alexander’s brother and Margaret as his sister. The combined names and locality strongly support continuity, with age differences retained. Robert [Elder-7144](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Elder-7144) remains a qualified father identity; Robert/Mary Glasgow’s Gortahar household is separate. (1901 Gortereghy, Robert Elder household; [record](https://api-census.nationalarchives.ie/census/image/nai000298460.pdf); 1911 Gortereghy, Alexander Elder household; [record](https://api-census.nationalarchives.ie/census/image/nai001390156.pdf).) The identification remains uncertain.

A possible identification concerns Robert Elder. The original birth entry names Jane Elder, born 27 April 1881 at Gortreighy, daughter of farmer Robert Elder and Jane formerly Calderwood. (Ballymoney civil birth register 1881; [record](https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/birth_returns/births_1881/02832/2038201.pdf).) The identification remains uncertain.
