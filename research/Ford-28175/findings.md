# Findings: Ford-28175

## Current conclusion

Anne Ford is expressly named as Thomas’s mother in the original 1777 Rathdowney baptism. This assessment identifies the register child represented by Glasgow-3592 and his expressly named parents. It does not prove that this child became the Tipperary husband of Johanna Ryan or father of Michael Glasgow, and does not identify David with the Scottish David Glasgow-3456. No new parent is attached and no paternal family is joined. Margaret’s separate baptism and the 1773 marriage have not been inspected in this batch. The household townland and sponsors are not securely read from the available low-resolution scan and are not asserted here.

Read the 14 September 1773 Rathdowney marriage and Margaret’s July 1774 entry for the household place and witnesses, then test the later Thomas–Johanna–Michael identification against original Tipperary records. David’s own parents remain unknown; his before-1755 birth is an adult-marriage estimate, not a record of his birthplace or age.
