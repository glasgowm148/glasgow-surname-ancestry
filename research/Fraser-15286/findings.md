## Current conclusion

Reviewed: 2026-10-15

The existing Leith couple is compatible with the 10 July 1610 marriage indexed as Catherine Fraith or Cathrene Fraser and William Glascoe/Glascow. The two transcripts represent one event; the original surname reading remains unverified.

The proposed 1610 bride identification therefore retains an explicit chronology question; no birth date or couple identity is proved by this review.
