# Findings: [Thomas Glasco (Glasco-143)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasco-143)

Last updated: 2026-09-08

## Current conclusion

This profile is not proved to be the Dublin watchmaker recorded at
Christchurch Lane in 1779 and 20 Ormond Quay in 1783. Its uncertain birth about
1766 is an estimate based on a reported 1785 marriage to Mary King; neither the
marriage nor Mary's dates has a substantive citation beyond a generic record
index link. The estimate conflicts with adult status in 1779 but is too weak to
exclude identity.

The profile's attached parents, [[Glasco-144]] and [[Duffy-6026]], are likewise
not established by its cited evidence. Do not add Miss Hynes, the watchmaker
events, or another marriage, and do not use the 1779–1783 cluster to validate
the attached parent-child chain.

## Identity comparison and relationship caution

- [[Glasco-144]] is directly supported as Margaret Duffy's 1773 groom, but no
  cited record proves he was this profile's father.
- [[Glasco-173]] is directly supported as Sarah's father with wife Catherine in
  1760, but no cited record proves he was [[Glasco-144]]'s father.
- No inspected source proves that any of these three Thomases had serial
  marriages to Catherine, Margaret Duffy, Miss Hynes and Mary King.

Keep the watchmaker as a free-space documentary cluster unless an
address-bearing marriage, baptism, burial, probate or trade record links him to
this profile.
