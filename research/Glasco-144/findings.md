# Findings: [Thomas Glasco (Glasco-144)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasco-144)

Last updated: 2026-09-08

## Current conclusion

This profile is securely associated with Margaret Duffy's 12 December 1773
marriage at St Audoen, Dublin, but is not proved to be the Thomas Glasco who
married Miss Hynes in 1779 or the Ormond Quay watchmaker of 1783. A remarriage
is chronologically possible, but no burial, licence, parish entry, probate or
address-bearing record proves that Margaret died or that the two grooms were
the same man.

The structured spouse is labelled Ann Duffy with a 1776 marriage, while the
biography's marriage index and the Duffy pedigree identify Margaret Duffy and
date the marriage to 1773. This internal conflict requires source resolution.
The attached parents, [[Glasco-173]] and [[Mangan-824|Catherine Mangan]], and
attached son, [[Glasco-143]], are not established by the cited marriage or
pedigree.

## Identity and relationship action

- Preserve the 1773 Margaret Duffy evidence and resolve the Ann/Margaret
  structured-spouse conflict from the underlying record before changing it.
- Do not add Miss Hynes or the watchmaker occurrence without evidence joining
  the two marriages.
- Do not treat [[Glasco-173]] as a proved father or [[Glasco-143]] as a proved
  son. The cited records do not establish either edge.

Keep the 1779–1783 watchmaker on the Dublin Glasco watchmaker free-space cluster
until a direct identity bridge is found.
