== Biography ==
Philip Glasco is retained here as a provisional account of the elder Dublin watchmaker. The 1752 Christ Church Lane trade association and the existing family structure favour that working use, while the old circa 1715 birth is an unsupported estimate. Previous research distinguishes the elder, established by 1750 and reported dead in 1772, from Philip junior, whose 1765 wife notice,1768 Teresa Plunkett marriage and reported 1770 death belong to the younger record group. The old after 1772 date does not follow from a 1777 probate occurrence. The local 1772 death year depends on selecting the elder identity; no original source newly inspected establishes this profile’s birth or Scottish origins. The old mother phrase for Catherine Mangan conflicts with the profile’s spouse field and is not adopted as maternity evidence.
