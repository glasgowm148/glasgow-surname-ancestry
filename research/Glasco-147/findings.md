### Findmypast sweep — 7 October 2026

- **Adds detail (index only):** Findmypast indexes **Philip Glasco Jun** and **Mary Glasco** on the same page of the Chancery Bill Books, 11 February 1765 (vol. 38, p.53; `IRE/COURT/CHANCERY/0000834604`, `IRE/COURT/CHANCERY/0000834605`). This fits his first wife Mary (Braccan-2), whose death as wife of Philip Glasco junior was reported in September 1765. The index gives no role or relationship, so the pairing is probable, not stated.
- **Context:** *Hibernian Journal*, 11 January 1773, p.2 (BNA `BL/0000817/17730111/005`), repeated in the same paper on 8, 10 and 22 February, 3, 5 and 22 March and 7 and 9 April 1773, and in *Saunders's News-Letter*, 12 March 1773, p.3 (`BL/0001057/17730312/016`). Full-page OCR of the 5 March issue (`BL/0000817/17730305/010`) reads, with long-s normalised: "THOMAS GLASCO, who served Apprenticeship to his Father, Philip Glasco, late [of] Christ-Church-lane, Watch-maker, and since had several Years Practice in London, takes this Method of informing his Friends and the Public, that he carries on the usual [business] at his said Father's House". The father there is very probably the elder Philip (died 1772), not this Philip, because a son of Philip junior (born about 1740) could not have finished an apprenticeship and several years' London practice by 1773.


## Selected uncertain family — 16 October 2026

The Glasco-146 parent profile is used provisionally for the elder watchmaker and is now selected under Thomas-1242, uncertain. This younger inventory-based account remains a strong same-person match to Glasgow-3932, not a proved second younger Philip. The 1772 citation has not established its asserted 1740 birth, exact grant role or literal sonship. Existing father and mother fields remain unupgraded. The Dublin records name no Scottish parents, birthplace or apprenticeship, so the Scottish child and elder watchmaker may be different men. The fresh Glasco-146 biography itself mixes the 1752 watchmaker with junior notices of 1765,1768 and 1770. Its c.1715 birth and the c.1740 birth of Glasco-147 are tree estimates, not evidence selecting a generation. If Glasco-146 instead represents the junior, the elder Philip is an alternative father-generation placement and the Thomas link would need revision; no competing father is attached alongside it. The elder placement is a preferred working interpretation of the profile’s trade/address and family structure, not a claim that every event in that biography belongs to him. The junior’s 1765 unnamed-wife death,1768 Teresa Plunkett marriage and reported 1770 death remain with the younger documentary account Glasgow-3932. Glasco-147 is a strongly supported duplicate account of that younger man, but its quoted 1772 younger/inventory wording was not independently rechecked here and establishes neither the estimated birth nor a newly verified father clause; a grant date is not a death date. No merge, spouse transfer or new child attachment follows.
