# Findings: [Thomas Glasco (Glasco-173)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasco-173)

Last updated: 2026-09-08

## Current conclusion

This profile represents Thomas Glasco, father with Catherine of Sarah Glasco,
baptised at St Michan Roman Catholic Church, Dublin, on 10 August 1760. That
record supports Thomas, Catherine and Sarah as a nuclear family but gives no
surname for Catherine, trade or address, and does not identify Thomas with the
1779–1783 watchmaker. It therefore does not prove that Catherine was
[[Mangan-824|Catherine Mangan]].

The attached son [[Glasco-144]] is not established by the 1760 baptism. No
cited record connects Sarah's father to the man who married Margaret Duffy in
1773, and chronology alone cannot supply that parent-child relationship.

## Identity and relationship action

- Do not assign the watchmaker or Miss Hynes to this profile without a direct
  later-life bridge.
- Do not use the 1760 baptism to attach [[Glasco-144]] as a son; it names only
  Sarah as the child.
- Compare [[Glasco-143]] and [[Glasco-144]], but do not infer a three-generation
  chain or serial marriages from compatible dates.

Preserve the 1779–1783 watchmaker as a free-space documentary cluster until a
parish, probate, burial, address or trade record identifies him.

## Current conclusion — newspaper review, 5 October 2026

This is a chronologically compatible Thomas Glasco namesake for the 1799 Tinicum Island subscriber; no profile identification is proved.

## Source findings — newspaper review

The [Aurora, 9 April 1799, p.3](https://www.newspapers.com/image/586542475/) and [11 April, p.1](https://www.newspapers.com/image/586542512/) print one STOP THIEF notice signed Thomas Glascoe: $115 reportedly stolen from his Tinicum Island house on 31 March. The reward is twenty dollars for recovery of the money, or fifteen for the thief alone.

## Identity and relationship assessments — newspaper review

The notice supplies residence but no age, relatives or migration link to this Dublin profile. Thomas is the reported victim; William Steel is the alleged thief. Check Tinicum tax, land, church and probate records before assignment. No family amendment is supported.

Full-page OCR of the 5 March issue (`BL/0000817/17730305/010`) reads, with long-s normalised: "THOMAS GLASCO, who served Apprenticeship to his Father, Philip Glasco, late [of] Christ-Church-lane, Watch-maker, and since had several Years Practice in London, takes this Method of informing his Friends and the Public, that he carries on the usual [business] at his said Father's House". The watchmaker Thomas named his father as Philip Glasco. If [[Glasco-144]] is the watchmaker, this profile cannot be his father. If he is not, the edge is still unsourced.
- **Candidate, unproved:** Dublin diocesan wills and grants index, **Catherine Glasco**, 1760 (`IRE/IET0090/0424/212`). This could be this Thomas's wife or the elder Philip's wife (died early 1760); the index gives no residence or husband.
- **Searches that found nothing:** no Thomas-and-Catherine Glasco baptism other than Sarah's was found in the Findmypast Irish Catholic parish index for Dublin 1720–1820.
