# Findings: [George Glascoe (Glascoe-36)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glascoe-36)

## Current conclusion

George is presently defined only by a reported burial at Berwick-upon-Tweed on
3 July 1589. The live biography cites the FamilySearch record for Margaret
Glasko of Norwich rather than a record for George, so its source does not
support this profile.

## Placement assessment

Berwick geography makes Scottish origin possible, but not proved. Treat George
as an **unplaced Borders/English control** until his own parish entry is found.
He cannot sensibly be attached as a son of Alan Glasgw, who was killed before
26 February 1495; the events are 94 years apart. A multi-generation descent
from Alan or another late-medieval bearer is possible in the abstract but has
no identified bridge.

## Recommended correction

Replace the incorrect Margaret-Glasko citation only after George's actual
Berwick register/index entry is recovered. Do not add Scottish parents from
the surname spelling alone.
