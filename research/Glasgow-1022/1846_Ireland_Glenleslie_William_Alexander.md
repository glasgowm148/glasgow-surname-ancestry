# William Alexander of Glenleslie, died 1846

## Documentary identity

- **Name:** William Alexander
- **Sex:** male
- **Born:** about 1785/86 from reported age 60 at death, uncertain; place unknown
- **Occupation:** labourer
- **Died:** 1846 of rheumatism, exact date and place unknown
- **Relationship:** described as brother-in-law of John Glasgow in Glenleslie household 21
- **Parents, spouse and children:** unknown

## Evidence

The surviving detailed 1851 mortality schedule records William Alexander with
John Glasgow's Glenleslie household. William was a labourer, aged 60, described
as John's brother-in-law, and had died of rheumatism in 1846.<ref>[https://www.billmacafee.com/census/1851censuskilconway.pdf William Macafee and W. Scott, ''1851 Census for some Parishes and Townlands in the Barony of Kilconway, Co. Antrim''], Dunaghy parish, Glenleslie household 21.</ref>

`Brother-in-law` does not identify the precise route. William could have been a
brother of John's wife Rose, the husband of John's sister or related through
another marital route. The record does not justify calling him Rose's brother
or using him alone to prove Rose's birth surname.
