# Sarah Alexander, visitor at Glenleslie in 1851

## Documentary identity

- **Name:** Sarah Alexander
- **Sex:** female
- **Born:** about 1816/17 from reported age 34, County Antrim, Ireland
- **Residence:** visitor in John and Rose Glasgow's Glenleslie household 21 in 1851
- **Literacy:** could read
- **Parents, spouse, children and relationship to the household:** unknown

## Evidence

The surviving detailed 1851 return records Sarah Alexander as a 34-year-old
visitor in the Glenleslie household of John Glasgow and Rose Alexander. She was
born in County Antrim and could read.<ref>[https://durrushistory.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/1851irishcensusreturnscommadelimitedtextfilejul24_071.pdf ''1851 Irish Census Returns: detailed comma-delimited transcription''], Glenleslie household 21, pp. 103–104; see also [https://www.billmacafee.com/census/1851censuskilconway.pdf William Macafee and W. `Visitor` and the shared Alexander surname do not establish kinship to Rose,
marriage to a Glasgow or any other family connection.
