== Biography ==

Thomas Glasgow was born on 5 November 1725. He was baptized two days later at Kilwinning, Ayrshire, Scotland, and the register recorded his family's residence as Byres, Kilwinning. He was the son of [[Glasgow-1056|Hugh Glasgow]] and [[Kilpatrick-1635|Janet Kilpatrick]].<ref name="fmp-baptism">Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929'', transcript “Thomas Glasgow in 1725,” birth 5 November 1725, baptism 7 November 1725, Kilwinning, Ayrshire, residence Byres, father Hugh Glasgow, mother Janet Kilpatrick, archive reference OPR 599/1, item 1, page 197; record ID SCOT/OPR/BAP/3626888; [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3626888&tab=this transcript], accessed 4 September 2026.</ref>

Thomas married [[Mill-357|Janet Isobel Mill]] in 1742.<ref>“Scotland Marriages, 1561-1910,” database, FamilySearch ([https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XY96-5ZV record for Thomas Glasgow], accessed 22 December 2023), marriage in 1742.</ref> They had seven known children between 1743 and 1757, principally recorded at Livingston. Their son [[Glasgow-2980|John Glasgow]] was born at Mid Calder in 1750, with Janet Mill named as his mother.<ref>“Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950,” database, FamilySearch ([https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BL-JX1 entry for John Glasgow], accessed 12 February 2020).</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The transcript's exact date, locality and two named parents establish the record-to-profile match. The existing ScotlandsPeople citation and the Findmypast transcript use different finding-aid locators for the Kilwinning entry; both are retained pending comparison with the register image.
