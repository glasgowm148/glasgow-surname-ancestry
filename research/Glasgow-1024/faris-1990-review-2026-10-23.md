# Faris book review — 23 October 2026

The actual Faris book has now been inspected, rather than only its catalogue abstract. Printed p.11 uses a dotted possible connection from James of Moneymore to the American brothers; p.12 explicitly reports that evidence for Robert and his brothers’ parentage had not been found. James was the author’s identifiable County Londonderry candidate, not an evidenced father. The retrospective Irish pedigree on pp.8–9 and Samuel’s 1824 Pennsylvania will discussed on p.12 identify an Irish sibling group, including William, James and Robert remaining in Ireland, without joining it to the emigrant brothers. County setting, a compatible generation and the existing family tradition keep James a plausible candidate, but cannot sustain the earlier probable father assessment.

Existing father-profile propositions only: Robert-12 85% → 35%; Arthur-649 and Joseph-1543 each 82% → 35%; John-4027 76% → 30%; Mary-4030 64% → 22%. All five were probable; the first four are now possible and Mary uncertain. James-1024 remains the selected named candidate; no attachment is removed. James-1024’s own older father stays 18%, and Samuel-1492’s father stays 23%.

Mary’s case additionally depends on identifying the 1779 will witness Mary Arter as the emigrant sister. Faris p.13 transmits an unnamed sister styled Mrs Arthur; p.15 identifies Mary Arter with that sister through the tax/probate cluster, rather than an explicit sibling or birth statement. The association remains positive but does not establish her father.

Robert, Arthur and Joseph’s established brotherhood remains separately supported by the maintained 1806 letter transcription and Joseph’s 1831 will. John of Peters Township remains a probable brother at 83%; Faris p.15 also reconstructs the four brothers from the 1779–1780 tax/probate cluster. These sibling arguments do not identify their father. The 1824 Samuel will has been read only through Faris’s excerpt, not its manuscript. The Irish Robert in that will/pedigree is not identified as the emigrant Robert born in 1749. All mothers, upstream ancestry, life dates and current attachments are preserved; no live WikiTree save or identity merge follows. Exact percentages are subjective pair-specific judgments, not measured frequencies or multiplied probabilities.

Faris p.12 abstracts Samuel Glasgow of West Fallowfield, Chester County, Pennsylvania: will dated 17 May 1824 and proved 31 May 1824, daughter Margaret Ellen Glasgow, with the residue to sons of his brothers William, James and Robert in Ireland. This is a useful compatible identity lead for the already existing Samuel-1492 account, whose contributed Irish family has the same brother pattern, not a proved identification. The manuscript, daughter’s records and migration/estate continuity remain to be checked. No independent birth age is supplied. Keep the Irish brother Robert distinct from Robert-12; do not infer the American emigrants’ father from this will. Samuel-1492’s father probability remains 23%.

The book’s p.8 says Drumearn for the 1759 probate entry. The already reviewed PRONI index says Drumcarn; retain that discrepancy and do not overwrite the corrected place or treat the entry as an identified father.
