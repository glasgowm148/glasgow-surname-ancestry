The letter-defined father is the family role represented by James-1025. James-1024 is reviewed as the letter-defined son who settled near Moneymore. The father James and son James occupy explicitly distinct generations in that account; the earlier possibility that these two family roles were one duplicated adult no longer deserves the same weight. The 78% assessment does not identify the son with the Corvanaghan householder or make him father of the American emigrant Robert.

Samuel-1492 is reviewed as the letter-defined emigrant son. This supports the family reconstruction while the testator’s profile identity remains open. The 76% is the complete father proposition for the letter-defined Samuel, not proof of his Gorteen identity or a new assessment of Samuel-2872.

Robert-2425 is the inherited James/Margaret Rutherford son account, matching the letter’s named Robert, himself described as a William Glasgow’s father. This specific position in the named sibling family supports 77%, while his adult life remains little anchored. His imported United States death location is not established by the letter or by the will’s Ireland designation.

William-2426 already represents the letter’s maternal-grandfather family role and is assessed as Margaret, wife of the Coleraine minister’s father at 84%.
