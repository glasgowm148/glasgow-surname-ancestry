== Biography ==
'''William Glasgow''' was married to [[McCook-217|Ann McCook]]. They were living in or associated with Kirknewton parish, Midlothian, Scotland, by 1682.

Their daughter [[Glasgow-1545|Bessie Glasgow]] was recorded there on 21 April 1682, daughter of William Glasgow and Ann McCook.<ref name="Bessie1682">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950," database, FamilySearch, Bessie Glasgow, 1682; [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-ZQN FamilySearch record].</ref>

William and Ann were also recorded as the parents of [[Glasgow-1318|William Glasgow]], baptised on 22 April 1705.<ref name="William1705">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950," database, FamilySearch, William Glasgow, 1705; [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-FY9 FamilySearch record].</ref>

William's own birth, parentage and death have not yet been established from contemporary evidence.

=== Identity ===

This William was previously conflated with [[Glasgow-3974|William Glasgow]], who was baptised on 24 September 1671 as a son of Andro Glasgow.<ref name="William1671">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950," database, FamilySearch, William Glasgow, baptised 24 September 1671, son of Andro Glasgow; [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-F2B FamilySearch record].</ref>

The two men cannot reasonably be the same person. [[Glasgow-1545|Bessie Glasgow]] was recorded as the daughter of William Glasgow and Ann McCook in April 1682,<ref name="Bessie1682" /> when the William baptised in September 1671 was only ten years old.

The 1671 baptism and the parentage by Andro Glasgow therefore belong to [[Glasgow-3974|William Glasgow]], not this profile.

No contemporary evidence has yet been found establishing this William's date or place of birth or identifying his parents. The former structured father [[Glasgow-1554|Walter Glasgow]] was disconnected on 26 September 2026. Walter was christened in 1649, only about eleven years before this William's estimated birth, and no cited record names Walter as his father.<ref name="Walter1649">FamilySearch, “Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” Walter Glasgow, christened 23 September 1649 in the Kirknewton/East Calder index, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-FWL record VQ4K-FWL].</ref>

=== Marriage ===

William was married to [[McCook-217|Ann McCook]].

No contemporary marriage entry for the couple has yet been identified. Their relationship is nevertheless supported by parish records naming them together as the parents of children in 1682 and 1705.<ref name="Bessie1682" /><ref name="William1705" />

Ann's currently estimated birth date should be checked separately against the 1682 record, since she was already Bessie's mother by that date.

=== Drummore and "Walter" ===

Later online genealogies describe this man as '''William Walter Glasgow''' and associate him with Drummore. No contemporary source has yet been identified establishing either the middle name "Walter" or a Drummore birthplace or residence.

These claims should remain research leads rather than established facts.

=== Ireland ===

Later pedigrees connect this family with County Tyrone, Ireland. The presently identified records do not establish when or whether William himself migrated to Ireland.

The 1759 probate-index entry for [[Glasgow-1025|James Glasgow]] places James at Drumearn, County Tyrone, but does not by itself establish James as William's son or prove a Scottish-to-Irish migration by this William.
