# Findings: [William Glasgow (Glasgow-1026), born about 1660](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1026)

## Current conclusion

The profile has now been split from the William baptised in 1671. This William
is the older Kirknewton householder who could chronologically have fathered
Bessie in 1682 and the later children recorded through 1705. His birth is
estimated as **about 1660**, uncertain, and his parentage remains unproved;
[Walter Glasgow (Glasgow-1554)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1554)
is attached only uncertainly.

The former structured death before 1784 had no supporting death record and
produced an implausible 120-year lifespan. It is replaced by **after 1705,
place unknown, uncertain**, reflecting the last baptismal household record
currently assigned to this same-name Kirknewton father. The identity across
the 1682-1705 entries remains a working reconstruction.

[John Glasgow (Glasgow-3610)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3610) is
now the best automated alternative: John was born in Kirknewton in 1626 and
had children recorded there in 1646, 1649 and about 1650. Another son before
1664 is chronologically and geographically possible. The unusual-name pattern
across the descendant branches adds support, but no baptism, testament or
other record yet names William as John's son. This is a genuine research lead,
not a relationship ready to attach.

## Recommended WikiTree changes

1. Retain [Andrew-1319](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1319) as the
   baptism-proved father.
2. Remove `Walter` from the middle-name field unless a contemporary source is
   found; explain it as a later tree name if retained in research notes.
3. Remove County Tyrone as a proved death place and remove statements that this
   William migrated to or lived at Moneymore unless a source is added.
4. Detach Bessie-1545 as a child: the 1671 father was ten at her baptism.
5. Detach or mark Andrew-1546's parentage contradicted/highly uncertain; first
   investigate whether Bessie and Andrew belong to an older William and Ann.
6. Mark Robert-2867 and James-1025 uncertain or detach them; neither profile
   supplies a parentage record.
7. Keep John-1544 and William-1318 only as uncertain candidate children pending
   inspection of the original parish entries and separation of same-name
   fathers.
8. Mark Ann McCook as an uncertain spouse; do not use the unsourced marriage to
   validate the children.

## Do not infer

- Ireland from the later Drumearn James or the 1740 Derryloran households.
- The middle name Walter from descendant naming patterns.
- Identity of every Kirknewton father named William Glasgow with the child
  baptised there in 1671.
