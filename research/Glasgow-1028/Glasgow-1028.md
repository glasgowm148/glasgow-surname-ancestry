== Biography ==
'''David Glasgow''' was the father named at two baptisms in Mid Calder:

* [[Glasgow-1054|Elspeth Glasgow]], baptised 2 March 1606.<ref name="ElspethC1606">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BJ-22S : 12 February 2020), David Glasgow in entry for Elspeth Glasgow, christened 2 Mar 1606, Mid Calder, Midlothian, Scotland. Father's Name: David Glasgow.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-1085|Issobell Glasgow]], baptised 3 May 1612.<ref name="IssobellC1612">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BJ-LR9 : 12 February 2020), David Glasgow in entry for Issobell Glasgow, christened 3 May 1612, Mid Calder, Midlothian, Scotland. Father's Name: David Glasgow.</ref>

The indexes establish a father called David in the parish but name no mother or grandparent and give no occupation, death date or death place. The common father identity is a working household reconstruction. His birth about 1586 is an estimate derived from his children’s dates, not a recorded event. Mid Calder as a death place is an uncertain locality lead, not evidence of a death there.

== Research Notes ==
No parents were attached to the live profile when checked on 15 October 2026. [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow]] and [[Anderson-103463|Jonet Anderson]] were earlier uncertain parent hypotheses. The corrected reading of Andrew’s 1597/8 testament names wife Elizat Stevinsoun, their son Robert, and daughters Marion, Janet and Isobel. It does not name David or Jonet Anderson.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124, testament testamentar and inventory of Andro Glasgw, made 6 February 1597/8, died 7 February 1597/8, confirmed 10 July 1598; corrected working reading of the original register images.</ref> The retained printed index citation identifies Andrew’s testament, rather than the children alleged in the superseded reading.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, Andrew Glasgow testament, CC8/8/32 (1598); printed testament index in Francis J. Grant, ed., ''Commissariot Record of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments'', p. 108, [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso01scotuoft/page/n117/mode/1up index entry].</ref>

David’s omission from the dispositive clauses does not by itself prove that Andrew could not be his father. The Mid Calder and Calder geography keeps a wider family connection plausible, but no record calls David Andrew’s son. A one-generation-lower placement through supposed sons James, Andrew or William is unsupported because the corrected testament proves none of those sons. An indirect or collateral connection remains possible, with no exact relationship selected.

Inspect the original Mid Calder entries, including witnesses and any residence or occupation, then follow rentals and named heirs. The two baptism indexes alone do not identify David’s parents or wife.

=== Selected uncertain working father,15 October2026 ===
[[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow of Calder]] is selected as the working father in the local research model. David’s1606 and1612 Mid Calder baptisms place his household in the same small Calder area as the earlier Andrew household. His approximate generation permits Andrew to be his father. The first recorded daughter Elspeth is consistent with the Elizat name in Andrew’s household, a minor naming clue rather than a mother identification. Select Andrew as the tentative shared father with James and John; exact birth years remain estimates.

This is a hypothesis from existing material, not a parent named in a newly found record. John of Hermand-3181 or another older local Glasgow could be the father. If David’s approximate1586 birth were established, he would have been young when omitted from Andrew’s1598 testament, making that omission more troublesome. It is not an established birth date. Elizat Stevinsoun and the discredited Jonet Anderson attribution are not automatically assigned as mothers. No mother or live WikiTree parent-field change is implied. The earlier proof-based rationale remains superseded where corrected.
