== Biography ==

'''David Glasgow''' was the father named at the baptisms of two children in Mid
Calder:

* '''Elspeth Glasgow''', christened 2 March 1606.<ref name="Elspeth1606">“Scotland,
  Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch,
  [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BJ-22S Elspeth Glasgow, 1606],
  father David Glasgow, Mid Calder, Midlothian.</ref>
* '''Issobell Glasgow''', christened 3 May 1612.<ref name="Issobell1612">“Scotland,
  Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch,
  [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BJ-LR9 Issobell Glasgow, 1612],
  father David Glasgow, Mid Calder, Midlothian.</ref>

These records establish David's activity at Mid Calder but do not name his
wife, parents, occupation, death date or death place. His birth about 1586 is an
estimate based on being a father by 1606.

== Research Notes ==

[[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow of [Corsoun?]]] and
[[Anderson-103463|Jonet Anderson]] are presently attached as uncertain parents.
That placement was partly justified by an incorrect transcription of Andrew's
1597/8 testament. The corrected reading names Elizat Stevinsoun as Andrew's
wife, Robert as their son, and Marion, Janet and Isobel as Andrew's daughters;
it does not name David or Jonet Anderson.<ref>National Records of Scotland,
Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124, testament testamentar and
inventory of Andro Glasgw, made 6 February 1597/8 and confirmed 10 July 1598;
corrected working transcription of the original register images.</ref>

David's Mid Calder locality keeps Andrew as a possible cluster hypothesis, but
no source calls him Andrew's son. Omission from a dispositive clause does not by
itself prove that he was not a son, so the correct status is unsupported rather
than directly disproved. Do not place David through supposed sons James, Andrew
or William: the corrected testament does not establish those sons either.
