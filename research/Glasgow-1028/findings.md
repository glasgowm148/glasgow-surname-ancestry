# Findings: [David Glasgow of Mid Calder (Glasgow-1028)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1028)

## Current conclusion

David was the father named in Mid Calder baptism indexes for Elspeth Glasgow in
1606 and Issobell Glasgow in 1612. Neither record identifies his parents.

The corrected reading of Andrew Glasgow's 1597/8 testament does not support the
current parents. It names Elizat Stevinsoun as Andrew's wife, Robert as their
son, and Marion, Janet and Isobel as Andrew's daughters. It does not name David
or Jonet Anderson. Because the surviving dispositive clauses need not enumerate
every child, David's omission is not by itself proof that Andrew could not be
his father; however, the former claim that the testament positively placed him
in an Andrew/Jonet household is invalid.

## Relationship assessment

- Andrew-3163 as father: **possible locality hypothesis, unsupported**. Mid
  Calder and Corsoun/Calder are suggestive, but no record calls David his son.
- Anderson-103463 as mother: **unsupported and based on a testament misreading**.
- The profile's circa-1586 birth and Mid Calder death place are estimates, not
  events established by the two baptism indexes.

Do not invent a one-generation-lower placement through Andrew's supposed sons
James, Andrew or William; the corrected testament proves none of those sons.
Search original baptisms, Calder estate rentals and later testaments for a wife,
occupation, residence or kinship bridge.
