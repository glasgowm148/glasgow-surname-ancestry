## Next records — Glasgow University and Dowhill rental leads, 6 October 2026

The OCR of *Munimenta Alme Universitatis Glasguensis*, vols I–III, records a **Stephanus Glasgw** incorporated at the University of Glasgow on 24 October 1556, and a **Stevin Glasgw** in a Dowhill rental. Neither has been checked against this Stephen, and the register gives no page reference. Chronology needs care (an inference, not stated in the register): a student incorporated in 1556 would usually have been born about 1540–1543, earlier than the estimated 1550 birth shown for this profile. A match would move that estimate; a mismatch could point to a second Stephen. Read the printed pages, and the Dowhill rental's date and context, before attaching either occurrence.

## Source findings — later Glasgow protocols, 1585–1599

The owner's earlier notes preserve OCR text of further entries in Robert Renwick, ed., *Abstracts of Protocols of the Town Clerks of Glasgow*. Protocol **2838**, 2 April 1586, records John Grahame, bailie, resigning to **Stephen Glasgu** a great tenement, fore and back, with yard, on the west side of the street from the metropolitan church to the market cross, with the chaplainry of St Kentigern (then William Porterfield of that Ilk's) on the north; Robert Rowet, bailie, gave sasine, and William Spreull, citizen, witnessed. Protocol **3533**, 2 March 1598/9, records John Andersone, sailor, as procurator for **Stephen Glasgw**, resigning a great tenement on the same side of that street, with William Porterfeld of that Ilk's lands on the north, to Archibald Hegate, notary and citizen. Stephen also witnesses several sasines in 1585–86.

Seen as: pasted OCR transcription of the printed abstracts; the protocol books were not inspected, and the notes record no volume or page. The live biography cites these entries as "vol. 6"; confirm volume and page before citing. That the 1598/9 tenement is the one acquired in 1586 is an inference from the matching description. An act by a procurator does not show that Stephen was alive or resident in Glasgow in 1599.
