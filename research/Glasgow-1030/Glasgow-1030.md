## Suggested corrections

* The 1619 baptism calls George Glasgow, Mathew Will and Robert Glasgow
  witnesses. It does not call George or Robert uncles or brothers.
* Remove the statement that John, George and Robert were all brothers. Direct
  records prove George was a son of [[Glasgow-3522|William Glasgow]], whereas
  Robert was a son of [[Glasgow-3188|Ninian Glasgow]].
* Treat [[Glasgow-3522|William Glasgow]] and [[Herbison-439|Margaret
  Herbesoun]] as uncertain parents of John. The witness group does not prove
  this attachment.
* Correct the biography's stale parent links from `Glasgow-1098` and
  `Herbesoun-1` to the profiles actually attached, `Glasgow-3522` and
  `Herbison-439`.
* Ninian's 1613 testament names a brother John. Ninian's proved father
  [[Glasgow-1096|Robert Glasgow, fisher]] is only a possible father for John,
  because the testament does not distinguish a full from a half-brother. This
  man is the leading identity candidate for John-1030 because Ninian's son
  Robert witnessed John-1030's child's baptism, but neither the identity nor
  the father link is proved.

## Do not add as fact

* Do not call either 1619 witness John's brother or his son's uncle.
* Do not merge John-1030 with Ninian's record-defined brother or attach Robert
  as his father without an occupation, property, spouse or explicit kinship
  bridge.

## Saltcoats identity HOLD

NRS CC9/7/27, images 478–480, is the testament dative of John Glasgow, sailor
in Saltcoats, whose widow was Isobel Young and whose lawful children were
Katherine and Marie. He died in December, provisionally 1636; confirmation was
11 April 1637. John-1030 is chronologically compatible, but no record joins
his Jonet Mathie household to Saltcoats. Do not create or merge the Saltcoats
John until that bridge is found. See the consolidated HOLD draft at
`../../surname-research/new-people/1637_Scotland_Saltcoats_John_Glasgow.md`.
