# Rose Ann Glasgow Redpath (Glasgow-1034) — findings

## Current conclusion

Rose Ann's birth remains **about 1841, uncertain**. Her biography now explains
that later death and cemetery records imply about 1843–1844 but are less
contemporaneous than the surviving census.

## Source findings

The current National Archives of Ireland index records Rose Ann Glasgow as age
10 in Lisnagaver household 37 in 1851: [migrated census record ID 43310](https://nationalarchives.ie/collections/search-the-census/census-record/#census_year=1851&surname__icontains=Glasgow&firstname__icontains=Rose&county=Antrim&townland__icontains=Lisnagaver&limit=30&id=43310).
[Find a Grave memorial 157357358](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/157357358)
gives 1843; the 1921 death age gives about 1843 and another cemetery record
gives about 1844. WT+ Hint 575 was marked false because the earlier census is
the stronger evidence for the estimated birth year.
