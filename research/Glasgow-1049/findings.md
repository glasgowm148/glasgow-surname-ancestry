## Original Kendall directory checked — 12 November 2026

George Fisher & Co., *Biographical Directory of the Voters and Tax-Payers of Kendall County, Illinois* (Chicago, 1876), [printed pp. 97 and 102, PDF pages 103 and 108](https://libsysdigi.library.uiuc.edu/oca/Books2007-09/biographicaldire00fish/biographicaldire00fish.pdf), read from the original page images.

On p. 97, R. Glasgow senior is from North Ireland, Presbyterian, and lives on a son's farm in Section 36, Plainfield post office. The adjacent Robert Glasgow entry concerns a man born in County Antrim on 17 June 1848, arriving in the United States on 8 September 1866 and Kendall County on 1 March 1871. He rents 80 acres in Section 30 from H. A. Gardner, has four living children, and married Margaret J., daughter of Robert Aiken of County Antrim. **The junior entry does not name his own parents.** Senior's Section 36 residence must not be silently equated with junior's Section 30 farm.

On p. 102, the entry for James Wylie identifies his wife Margaret Ann as daughter of Robert Glasgow senior of County Antrim. This is a contemporary explicit father–daughter statement for the woman in that entry. [Margret Glasgow-1399](/people/glasgow-1399.html), the thirteen-year-old Donbought daughter in 1851, remains a compatible candidate; the directory supplies no age, mother or Irish townland for Wylie's wife, so that adult identification remains separate. Wylie was born in County Antrim on 12 July 1836, reached the United States on 17 April 1863 and Kendall County on 18 May 1863.

These pages confirm the held directory dates and provide a concrete Wylie family lead. They do not identify [Robert Glasgow-1049's](/people/glasgow-1049.html) father or name his wife. ### Wylie marriage resolves the father's reading

The [original civil marriage return](https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1859/09563/5467079.pdf), Ballymena district, register p. 134, entry 98, PDF p. 1, records **11 November 1859**, James Wylie, full age, bachelor, farmer, of Glenleslie, Dunaghy, son of Archibald Wylie, farmer; and **Margaret Anne Glasgow**, full age, spinster, of **Donbought, Dunaghy**, daughter of **Robert Glasgow, farmer**. They married at the Presbyterian church in Newtowncrommelin. The bride's father's forename is Robert, not Andrew as transcribed in the older Glasgow-951 survey table. That table is corrected, with this dated reading retaining the change.

The matching couple, the bride's exact Donbought residence, her father Robert, and the Illinois directory's explicit father–daughter statement strongly support identifying the Wylie wife with [Margret Glasgow-1399](/people/glasgow-1399.html), aged thirteen in the 1851 Donbought household of Robert and Margaret. The groom's Glenleslie residence and father Archibald provide additional distinctive anchors for the Illinois family, where Archibald Wylie is also listed on p. 102. The marriage names no mother; the father's description as farmer differs from the 1851 linen-weaver occupation but can coexist with farming and is not by itself a different-person test. No new father is attached and the existing child–father confidence is unchanged. Neither original identifies Robert-1049's father, so his upstream **54% possible** assessment is unchanged.
