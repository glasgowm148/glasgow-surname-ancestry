== Biography ==

'''Andrew Glasgow''' was born about 1818 in County Antrim, Ireland. The detailed
1851 census return directly calls him a son of [[Glasgow-1022|John Glasgow]] and
records that Andrew was a thirty-year-old linen weaver who died of consumption
in 1848.<ref name="Census1851">[https://durrushistory.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/1851irishcensusreturnscommadelimitedtextfilejul24_071.pdf ''1851 Irish Census Returns: detailed comma-delimited transcription''], Glenleslie household 21.</ref>
The household also appears in the National Archives of Ireland's earlier
online census presentation.<ref name="Census1851NAI">[http://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Dunaghy/Glenleslie/21/ National Archives of Ireland, 1851 census], Glenleslie household 21.</ref>

The return does not identify Andrew's mother. [[Alexander-16967|Rose Alexander]]
was John's wife in 1851, but her reported age would make her about forty-seven
when Andrew was born. She may have been his mother, but that relationship should
remain uncertain.

== Research Notes ==

[[Glasgow-1419|Samuel John Glasgow]] is a strong probable son. Samuel's 1866
marriage names his father as Andrew Glasgow, labourer.<ref name="SamuelMarriage">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1866/11540/8244496.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Samuel John Glasgow and Margaret Calagher, 21 August 1866.</ref> Samuel was born in County Antrim by about 1845, when Andrew was in his twenties. The certificate does not give Andrew's residence and no baptism has been found, so the attachment remains uncertain.
