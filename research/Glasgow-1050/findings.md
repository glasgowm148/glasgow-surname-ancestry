# Findings: Glasgow-1050

Last updated: 2026-08-13

## Current conclusion

The detailed 1851 Glenleslie return directly identifies Andrew as a son of John
Glasgow. It records Andrew as a thirty-year-old linen weaver who died of
consumption in 1848. The return does not identify his mother.

Samuel John `Glasgow-1419` is a strong probable son. Samuel's original 1866
marriage names his father as Andrew Glasgow, labourer. His age, County Antrim
origin and the rarity of an adult Andrew in this locality fit this Andrew, but
the certificate does not give the father's residence and therefore falls short
of a unique identification.
