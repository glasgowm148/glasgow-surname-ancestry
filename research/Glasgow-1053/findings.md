# Findings: Margaret Glasgow (Glasgow-1053), Glenleslie

Last updated: 2026-08-11

## Current conclusion

The surviving 1851 census places Margaret Glasgow, aged 50 and explicitly
**unmarried**, in the Glenleslie household of Robert `Glasgow-1367` and
Catherine. This directly supports her identity as their daughter.

The same WikiTree profile is currently attached as wife of Samuel Winters and
has been used for a family with children beginning before 1851. That identity
is incompatible with Margaret remaining unmarried in her parents' Irish
household in 1851. Pending identification of a materially better profile for
the Canadian woman, the marriage and all four mother-child links are retained
but explicitly marked uncertain. This is a substantive contradiction, not
merely a less favoured hypothesis.
