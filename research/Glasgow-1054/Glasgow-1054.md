== Biography ==

'''Elspeth Glasgow''' was baptised on 2 March 1606 at Mid Calder, Midlothian,
Scotland. The parish index names her father as [[Glasgow-1028|David Glasgow]];
one parallel transcript also records her residence as Mid Calder.<ref
name="ElspethBaptism">Findmypast,
[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=R_686313316&tab=this “Scotland,
Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929,” Elspeth Glasgow], baptised 2 March 1606,
Mid Calder, Midlothian, daughter of David Glasgow, record R_686313316; and
[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=R_694107799&tab=this parallel
transcript], record R_694107799 (accessed 3 September 2026).</ref>

She is attached as a sister of [[Glasgow-1085|Issobell Glasgow]].

== Research Notes ==

The Findmypast transcripts do not name Elspeth's mother and do not themselves
establish the attached sibling relationship. Those attachments have been
preserved, with their evidence kept separate from this baptism.

An existing source note records a '''Bessie Glasgow''' with '''James Justice'''
at Mid Calder on 23 September 1627: parish 694, reference 10/55, frame FR60.
Elspeth and Bessie can be forms of the same name, and
[[Justice-3095|James Justice]] is attached as this profile's spouse, but no
reviewed record yet proves that the 1606 child and the 1627 woman were the same
person. The 1627 entry is not evidence that Elspeth was born in 1610.
