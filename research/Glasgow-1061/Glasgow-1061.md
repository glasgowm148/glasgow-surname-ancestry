== Biography ==

William Glasgow was born on 25 June 1715. He was baptized the following day at Kilwinning, Ayrshire, Scotland, and the register gave his family's residence as Byres, Kilwinning. He was the son of [[Glasgow-1056|Hugh Glasgow]] and [[Kilpatrick-1635|Janet Kilpatrick]].<ref name="fmp-baptism">Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929'', transcript “William Glasgow in 1715,” birth 25 June 1715, baptism 26 June 1715, Kilwinning, Ayrshire, residence Byres, father Hugh Glasgow, mother Janet Kilpatrick, archive reference OPR 599/1, item 1, page 108; record ID SCOT/OPR/BAP/3626228; [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3626228&tab=this transcript], accessed 4 September 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The transcript's exact baptism date, locality and two named parents identify this record with William rather than another man of the same name. “North Ayrshire” in the current structured birthplace is the modern administrative form of the transcript's Ayrshire locality; it is not evidence of a different place.
