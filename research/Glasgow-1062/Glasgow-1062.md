== Biography ==

John Glasgow was born on 1 July 1721 and baptized the following day at Kilwinning, Ayrshire, Scotland. The register gave the family residence as Byres. He was the son of [[Glasgow-1056|Hugh Glasgow]] and [[Kilpatrick-1635|Janet Kilpatrick]].<ref name="FMP">Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929'', John Glasgow, birth 1 July 1721, baptism 2 July 1721, Kilwinning, residence Byres, parents Hugh Glasgow and Janet Kilpatrick, OPR 599/1, item 1, page 158; record SCOT/OPR/BAP/3626585; [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3626585&tab=this transcript], accessed 4 September 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The transcript explicitly separates the birth and baptism dates. The exact baptism date, locality and both named parents establish the record-to-profile match.
