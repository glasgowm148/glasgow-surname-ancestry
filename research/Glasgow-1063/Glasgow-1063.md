== Biography ==

Margaret Glasgow was born on 14 March 1723 and baptized on 17 March at Kilwinning, Ayrshire, Scotland. The register gave the family residence as Byres. She was the daughter of [[Glasgow-1056|Hugh Glasgow]] and [[Kilpatrick-1635|Janet Kilpatrick]].<ref name="fmp-baptism">Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929'', transcript “Margaret Glasgow in 1723,” birth 14 March 1723, baptism 17 March 1723, Kilwinning, Ayrshire, residence Byres, father Hugh Glasgow, mother Janet Kilpatrick, archive reference OPR 599/1, item 1, page 171; record ID SCOT/OPR/BAP/3626677; [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3626677&tab=this transcript], accessed 4 September 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The transcript distinguishes Margaret's birth on 14 March from her baptism on 17 March. Its exact baptism date, locality and named parents distinguish her from the other Margaret Glasgows born in 1721 and November 1723.
