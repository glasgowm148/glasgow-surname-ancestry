John was born 16 Sep 1683. He is the son of [[Glasgow-1058|George Glasgow]] and [[Young-40954|Jonet Young]].

=== Selected uncertain family — 16 October 2026 ===

[[Glasgow-1065|John Glasgow, Jean Wyllie household]] is selected as the uncertain working father of [[Glasgow-2467|John Glasgow, Mary Auld household]], the John recorded with Mary Auld at Kilwinning for Andrew’s baptism on 26 October 1746 and James’s on 19 August 1750. The later John’s existing biography already suggests descent from [[Glasgow-2463|Glasgow-2463]], an old parent reference with a 16 September 1683 Kilwinning date. The current captured family fields of the older household’s children point to [[Glasgow-1065|John Glasgow, Jean Wyllie household]], whose profile carries that same 1683 date and the Jean Wyllie household. This resolves the intended candidate account; it is not a new historical father statement. A specific route is the recorded [[Glasgow-2464|John Glasgow, Kilwinning child of 1703]], baptised at Kilwinning on 19 December 1703 to John Glasgow and Jean Wylie, residence Chappell. Treat that child provisionally as the later Mary Auld husband. If baptised in infancy, he would have been about 42 and 46 at the two known children’s baptisms, compatible ages for a father. The later profile’s before-1728 estimate permits this earlier birth. Exact name, parish, an already recorded descent suggestion and a documented child with named parents give a testable family model. No record yet establishes that the child and adult were the same man.

The older John’s profile also represents a Kilwinning baptism on 16 September 1683. If those child and later-father identities are correct, he was about twenty at the 1703 baptism. This is compatible, but not independent proof that the 1683 child became Jean Wyllie’s husband. The competing Jean Gardiner and Jonet Young mother names in the 1683 indexes remain unresolved, as do the older account’s existing ancestral qualifications. A later son of the Wyllie household, a grandson through a different child, or another local Glasgow father remains possible for [[Glasgow-2467|John Glasgow, Mary Auld household]]. The already recorded descent suggestion does not specify a generation. The 1703-child route is selected because it provides a named child in the right parish with feasible later fatherhood, without inventing an intermediate ancestor; it is preferred only weakly.

The existing child-record citations are Kilwinning OPR 599, 10/131 for John in 1703, 20/93 for Andrew in 1746, and 20/106 for James in 1750. The retained Findmypast transcript SCOT/OPR/BAP/3625663 names the 1703 parents and residence Chappell. No original register image has been newly inspected. The baptism does not establish an exact birthday. [[Glasgow-2468|Glasgow-2468]] and [[Glasgow-2469|Glasgow-2469]] retain their existing father and Mary Auld retains her existing spouse and maternal links; no new certainty is assigned to them. The 1703 child and later father remain separate profile accounts, possibly duplicates rather than two proved brothers. No merge is performed, and no vital field changes to 1703 follow from this model.

* John Glasgow was born in 1683 and baptized on September 16, 1683, in Kilwinning, Ayrshire, Scotland. He was the son of Gorg Glasgow and Jonet "JEAN" Glasgow formerly Young aka Gardiner . The baptism occurred within the context of the Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929 collection, which falls under the Birth, Marriage, Death & Parish Records category, specifically within the subcategory of Parish Baptisms. The record provides details about John's birth, baptism, and his parents' names. The family's residence in Kilwinning, Ayrshire, Scotland, adds to the understanding of their historical origins.

Born in Kilwinning, Ayrshire. He likely fits into the Ayrshire branch, possibly as a cousin or uncle of  [[Glasgow-1058|George Glasgow (bef.1687-)]]
