=== Current research ===

The 24 May 1676 Kilwinning baptism directly proves Hew as son of Hew Glasgow and Margaret Lachland. Later records place a Hugh with Mary Dean at Kilmarnock in 1699 and with further children in Ayrshire.

[[Glasgow-3109|Glasgow-3109]], father in a 1696 Irvine family with Jean Service, is a strong possible earlier-marriage segment. The chronology and Ayrshire geography fit, but no record proves Jean's death, either marriage, or that both fathers were the 1676 child.
