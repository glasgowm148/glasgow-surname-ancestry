# Findings: [Hew Glasgow (Glasgow-1067), baptised 1676](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1067)

## Conclusion

The 24 May 1676 Kilwinning baptism directly proves Hew as son of Hew Glasgow
and Margaret Lachland. Later records place a Hugh with Mary Dean at Kilmarnock
in 1699 and with further children in Ayrshire.

[Glasgow-3109](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3109), father in a 1696
Irvine family with Jean Service, is a strong possible earlier-marriage segment.
The chronology and Ayrshire geography fit, but no record proves Jean's death,
either marriage, or that both fathers were the 1676 child.

## Recommended update

- Retain the baptism-proved parents.
- Treat Mary Dean and the later children as a probable rather than birth-linked family.
- Add Glasgow-3109 as a possible duplicate; do not merge before original OPR review.
- Target Irvine/Kilwinning/Kilmarnock marriages and Jean Service's death, 1695-99.
