== Research Notes ==
=== Parish parent review — 17 October 2026 ===

The accepted Kilwinning parish transcripts name Robert Glasgow and Mary Simson as the parents of Hugh in 1716, George in 1719 and Jean in 1722. The existing 1715 Kilwinning marriage index names Robert Glasgow and Mary Simpson; ordinary Simson/Simpson spelling variation, the repeated parental pair and the ordered 1715–1722 family sequence identify one record-defined household. George and Jean also share Byrehill residence. These records document both parents for each indexed child, not merely a spouse inferred as mother. The three child profiles already represent those checked entries, so their father and mother assessments can be marked documented.

These are previously accepted derivative register transcripts and indexes; no fresh original image reading or new record discovery is claimed. The three transcripts derive from one parish register and are not independent sources. Hugh’s transcript has no residence field; Byrehill is not newly assigned to him. The FMP page references and the separately retained ScotlandsPeople references remain literal provider locators. This household proof does not newly identify the adult Robert with the 1678 child, prove ancestry above either parent, or identify any child with a later adult. George’s merchant, Manchester inventor and artillery comparisons remain separate; [[Glasgow-1120]] and the later Covington family do not receive a new parentage grade. No date, birthplace, spouse, occupation, live parent field or saved certainty flag changes.
