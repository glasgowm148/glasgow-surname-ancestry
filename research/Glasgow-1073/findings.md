# Findings: [Rev. John Glasgow M.A. (Glasgow-1073), about 1653-1721, Kilbirnie, Ayrshire](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1073)

## Current conclusion

John should be placed in the wider [William Glasgow (Glasgow-1098)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1098)
descendant project, most probably in the **Corsankell-Stevenston-Kilwinning**
branch. Calling him the root of a separate Ayrshire family was too strong.

The best current route is:

```text
William Glasgow (Glasgow-1098)
  ? James Glasgow elder (Glasgow-1168), Corsankell
    -> John Glasgow (Glasgow-3193), record-proved son
      ? Hugh Glasgow (Glasgow-1603), Ayrshire placeholder
        ? John Glasgow (Glasgow-1604), Kilwinning placeholder
          ? Rev. John Glasgow M.A. (Glasgow-1073)
```

Only James-1168 to his named son John is directly proved within this displayed
route. Glasgow-1603 and Glasgow-1604 have only tree-derived dates and parentage.
Glasgow-1604 is nevertheless the best *existing-profile candidate* for John's
father by generation and place. Do not replace one unsupported certain father
with another certain father.

[John Glasgow (Glasgow-3375), about 1650, Kilwinning](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3375)
is a serious duplicate candidate for the minister, not merely another son to add
under Glasgow-1604. His John-and-Barbara household has indexed Kilwinning events
in 1679 and January 1689, immediately before the minister's documented January
1690 marriage. The complete OPR pages must be checked for `Mr`, occupation or
residence before merging.
