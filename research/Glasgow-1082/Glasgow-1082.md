== Biography ==
'''George Glasgow''' was baptised on 17 February 1605 at Mid Calder,
Midlothian, Scotland. The parish index names his father as
[[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]]; one parallel transcript also records George's
residence as Mid Calder.<ref name="GeorgeBaptismFMP">Findmypast,
[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=R_686312170&tab=this “Scotland,
Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929,” George Glasgow], baptised 17 February
1605, Mid Calder, Midlothian, son of James Glasgow, record R_686312170; and
[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=R_694110723&tab=this parallel
transcript], record R_694110723 (accessed 3 September 2026).</ref><ref name="GeorgeBaptismFS">“Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950,” database,
FamilySearch ([https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BK-1BW George
Glasgow, 1605], accessed 12 February 2020).</ref>

George is attached as a son of [[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]] and
[[Adamson-3318|Christina Adamson]]. His attached siblings are
[[Glasgow-1603|Hugh Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-1148|Alexander Glasgow]],
[[Glasgow-994|Eduard Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-1083|Beatrix Glasgow]],
[[Glasgow-3321|Cathrin Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-3517|William Glasgow]],
[[Glasgow-1084|John Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-993|Agnes Glasgow]],
[[Glasgow-3144|Elizabeth Ann Glasgow]] and [[Glasgow-3145|John Glasgow]].

== Research Notes ==
=== Current evidence assessment ===

The reviewed baptism transcripts establish Mid Calder as the baptism place
and, in one index, George's residence. They do not support a birth at
Musselburgh. Because the record documents baptism rather than birth, it does
not prove that George was born on the same day.

The mother field is blank in the Findmypast transcripts. That absence neither
proves nor disproves the attached Christina Adamson relationship, which has
therefore been preserved.

=== Murieston and the adult witness ===
The original Mid Calder register,17 February1605, names James Glasgow in provisionally read Muirestoun, probably Murieston, as father of George. Adult witnesses include George Glasgow and John Smith; the last name is unresolved. No mother, actual birth date or kinship to the witnesses is given.<ref>NRS, Mid Calder OPR694/1, volume10,p.7, baptism17 February1605; https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/; original owner-held page reread15 October2026.</ref>

The adult George may be the George Glasgw named in Mureistoun by the19 April1586 barony act. That act orders a4 August muster; it does not prove attendance.<ref>James Maidment, ed., ''Analecta Scotica'' I(1834), art.CXVI,pp.395–396,399; https://archive.org/details/analectascotica00maidgoog; printed pages visually checked15 October2026.</ref> He could be James’s father, another relative or a neighbour. No identity with [[Glasgow-1099|the Glasgow merchant]], [[Glasgow-3171|the undated Dublin man]] or [[Glascoe-36|the unplaced Berwick man]] is selected. The child [[Glasgow-1082|George]] is distinct from the adult witness.

Compare the other Mid Calder baptism pages for the same holding and witnesses, then inspect NLS MS.3724 and Beveridge’s transcript MS.3725 for George, James, heirs, leases and succession at Mureistoun. The catalogue places the 1586 land-title courts at original pp.1–15 and the chronological court sequence at pp.23–189; it records a gap between 2 May 1592 and 26 November 1594. These manuscript pages have not been read.<ref>NLS catalogue: MS.3724 https://manuscripts.nls.uk/repositories/2/archival_objects/37880; MS.3725 https://manuscripts.nls.uk/repositories/2/archival_objects/37881; manuscript pages uninspected.</ref>
