== Biography ==

'''George Glasgow''' was baptised on 17 February 1605 at Mid Calder,
Midlothian, Scotland. The parish index names his father as
[[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]]; one parallel transcript also records George's
residence as Mid Calder.<ref name="GeorgeBaptismFMP">Findmypast,
[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=R_686312170&tab=this “Scotland,
Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929,” George Glasgow], baptised 17 February
1605, Mid Calder, Midlothian, son of James Glasgow, record R_686312170; and
[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=R_694110723&tab=this parallel
transcript], record R_694110723 (accessed 3 September 2026).</ref><ref
name="GeorgeBaptismFS">“Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950,” database,
FamilySearch ([https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BK-1BW George
Glasgow, 1605], accessed 12 February 2020).</ref>

George is attached as a son of [[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]] and
[[Adamson-3318|Christina Adamson]]. His attached siblings are
[[Glasgow-1603|Hugh Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-1148|Alexander Glasgow]],
[[Glasgow-994|Eduard Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-1083|Beatrix Glasgow]],
[[Glasgow-3321|Cathrin Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-3517|William Glasgow]],
[[Glasgow-1084|John Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-993|Agnes Glasgow]],
[[Glasgow-3144|Elizabeth Ann Glasgow]] and [[Glasgow-3145|John Glasgow]].

== Research Notes ==

The reviewed baptism transcripts establish Mid Calder as the baptism place
and, in one index, George's residence. They do not support a birth at
Musselburgh. Because the record documents baptism rather than birth, it does
not prove that George was born on the same day.

The mother field is blank in the Findmypast transcripts. That absence neither
proves nor disproves the attached Christina Adamson relationship, which has
therefore been preserved.
