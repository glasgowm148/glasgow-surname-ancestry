== Biography ==

'''Jonet''', whose surname is provisionally read '''Kinnie''', was the widow
of [[Glasgow-1086|John Glasgow]] of Quothquan in Libberton parish. She was
appointed executrix dative surrogate in the proceeding dated 25 March
1685.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Lanark Commissary Court, testament
dative of John Glasgow in Quothquan, Libberton parish, NRS CC14/5/12, images
67–68; proceeding dated 25 March 1685. Working transcription of the original
images supplied 6 September 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The manuscript clearly gives the widow relationship and first name. The
surname is not secure enough to normalise as Kinnear, Kinney or another modern
form; retain the uncertain-status marker. The testament names no child or
parent for Jonet.
