# Findings: Jhone Glasgow (Glasgow-1086), indexed at Mid Calder in 1608

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

A derivative 1608 Mid Calder index records Jhone Glasgow and supplies a possible
sibling lead for William-1087, christened there in 1609 to a father also indexed
as Jhone Glasgow. The original 1608 and 1609 entries must be compared before
asserting that both children had the same father. Retain the sibling relationship
as uncertain and do not infer their mother's identity.
