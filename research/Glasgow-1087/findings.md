# Findings: William Glasgow (Glasgow-1087), christened at Mid Calder in 1609

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

A derivative baptism index records William Glasgow, son of **Jhone Glasgow**,
christened at Mid Calder on 24 December 1609. The date is a christening date,
not a proved birth date. The original register has not been inspected, and the
index should not be expected to name William's mother because Mid Calder
baptisms did not record mothers at this period.

William is the strongest same-parish chronological candidate for the adult
William who fathered Johne and Issobel at Mid Calder in 1648. He is also a
possible father behind the 1643-1655 entries catalogued under Kirknewton and
East Calder. No located record identifies any of those adult fathers as the
child christened in 1609. No spouse or child is therefore proved for
Glasgow-1087.

Disconnect reconstructed adult-father profiles Glasgow-3515 and Glasgow-3610
as sons of Glasgow-1087. Do not transfer their children to Glasgow-1087 merely
because his age and locality fit.
