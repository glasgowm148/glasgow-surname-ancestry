== Biography ==
George was born 2 Feb 1627 in High Church, Glasgow, Lanark, Scotland. He is the son of [[Glasgow-1030|Jhone Glasgow]] and [[Mathie-159|Jonat Mathei]]<ref>"Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQHK-3WY : 16 September 2021), George Glasgow, 1627.</ref>. His guardians are listed as George Glasgow, Alexander Simsoun. 

Not to be confused with a [[Glasgow-1560|George Glasgow (bef.1622-)]]  christened 20 Jan 1622 in Linlithgow, Linlithgowshire, Scotland to a John 'Glesgow' <ref> https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RN-XVQ</ref>
