# George Glasgow (Glasgow-1090) research findings

## Current conclusion

Two 1724 Court of Session file titles do not identify
[[Glasgow-1090|George Glasgow]]. He would have been about ninety-seven, and no
documentary bridge connects him to either process.

## Identity assessment — 1724 Court of Session files

`CS143/24` names George Glasgow and Robert Crawford in title deeds;
`CS228/G/1/105` is George Glasgow v Stevenson and others. Neither public NRS
record gives George's residence, occupation, kin, property or age. They are
preserved as separate [title-deeds](../hold-resolution/record-dossiers/cs143-24-george-glasgow-robert-crawford.md)
and [George v Stevenson](../hold-resolution/record-dossiers/cs228-g-1-105-george-glasgow-v-stevenson.md)
legal-file dossiers rather than person records.

## Recommended action

Do not amend this profile from either file title. Reassess only if original
process papers produce an independent identifier.
