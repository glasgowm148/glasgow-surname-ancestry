## Suggested corrections

- Treat the current `before 1516` as a boundary, not an exact birth. **About
  1510** is the better working estimate, with roughly 1505-1516 a reasonable
  range, because John was already a married property recipient in 1534.

## Do not add as fact

- Do not state 1510 as recorded. Protocol 1196 gives John's marriage and adult
  property context but no age.
- Do not state that the older John was William's father. Eufame Craig may be
  described as the older John's likely widow when the property reasoning is
  included; do not present the marriage as directly recorded.
- Do not describe Isobella as John's proved sister. Her husband's role in the
  disposal of John's paternal inheritance makes the relationship probable, not
  explicit.
