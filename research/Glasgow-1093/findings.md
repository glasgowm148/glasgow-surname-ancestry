## Current conclusion

Father assessment: [William Glasgow (1500)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1098) is **confirmed by record**.

Birth-date assessment: John's WikiTree value is **before 1516**, not an exact
1516 birth. He was already married to Jonet Spreull and receiving property with
her on 31 December 1534. The record gives no age. The legal minimum marriage
age supplies only a remote outer bound of birth before about 1520; treating him
as an adult householder makes **about 1510** the better working estimate, with
roughly **1505-1516** a reasonable genealogical range. The existing `before
1516` value is therefore defensible as a conservative boundary, but 1516 is not
a recorded year.

## Rear-tenement title note - 30 August 2026

Protocol 748's Rankin–Young–St Mary boundaries identify the tenement conveyed
to John junior as the rear component of the same family block whose fore
tenement John senior inherited in protocol 805. Protocol 809 shows that Eufame
Craig's liferent still burdened that rear fee. John senior could therefore
convey the fee in 1563 and buy the outstanding liferent in 1564. Eufame is most
likely the older John's widow: her interest can have been reserved before the
fee passed rapidly to William, while the same-day Craig transactions and Craig
witnesses fit a coordinated settlement. William as her husband is less likely
because it requires Esbell Brade's death and William's remarriage within about
fourteen months. The original is Glasgow City Archives
[B/10/1/3](https://cityarchives.glasgowlife.org.uk/Record.aspx?src=CalmView.Catalog&id=B%2f10%2f1%2f3),
especially protocol 748 near fol. 31 and protocols 805/809–810 through fol. 54.

## Earlier title continuity - 30 August 2026

The institutional title now reaches William earlier and more exactly. A
*Liber Collegii* instrument dated 20 February 1528/29 places William
Glasgow immediately north of the Henryson/Houston parcel and Andrew Craig
immediately south; this is the parcel later held by Rankin. The St Andrew
prebend rental, confirmed 3 June 1542 and transumed 8 January 1548/49, charges
four shillings on the tenement of the late William Glasgow between late John
Crownar north and John Rankin south. That is the same sum and institutional
title attached to the older John Glasgow's Rankin–Young tenement in 1527.
Together with protocol 805, the evidence is direct for John's inheritance from
William and strong for William's rapid succession to the older John's parcel;
it does not identify the older John as William's father.
