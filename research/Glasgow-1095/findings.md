# Findings: [John Glasgow (Glasgow-1095)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1095)

## Current conclusion

This pre-1500 profile is not managed by the requesting account and is not the
project's maintained research destination. The current sourced biography is
[John Glasgow of Saltmarket, estimated born about 1480 and dead before 21
October 1553](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Space:John_de_Glasgow_alias_Smith_/_John_Glasgow_of_Saltmarket%27).
Managed relatives and project pages should point prominently to that
free-space biography while preserving this existing attachment.

The Saltmarket biography now links prominently to [John Glasgw of
Stirling](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Space:John_Glasgw_of_Stirling), active
in 1475–1479/80, as a plausible father of the Saltmarket John estimated born
about 1480. This is a chronological and surname-based reconstruction, not a
recorded relationship.

The profile is an unsourced early root estimated born in 1475 at Glasgow. Two
record-defined men are compatible with parts of that description, but neither
is proved to be Glasgow-1095:

- John Glasgw witnessed Stirling acts in 1475 and 1479/80; as a pre-1500
  subject he is maintained on a free-space page, not as a new person profile.
- John Glasgow held a Saltmarket tenement in 1527; the same property description
  recurs in 1548 and an adjoining boundary calls him dead by 21 October 1553.

No inspected record calls either man father of William Glasgow-1098. A new
property reconstruction does, however, show William occupying the older
John's exact Rankin–Young parcel in 1534/35 and William's proved son inheriting
it in 1564. This raises the older John to a serious possible relative while
remaining insufficient to prove fatherhood. Preserve the current attachment as
uncertain; do not present it as proved.

## Identity and duplicate assessment

Do not create a post-1500 Saltmarket profile until Glasgow-1095's intended
identity is resolved. Do not repurpose Glasgow-1095 as the Stirling witness or
Saltmarket owner merely because its unsupported estimate fits both. A document
linking a relative, property or civic role is required.

## Recommended profile treatment

- Keep the attached son William-1098 uncertain; add no claim of proved descent.
- Do not replace the populated Glasgow birth estimate with an empty location.
- If a sourced identity is eventually established, replace the estimated vital
  data with that evidence in the same edit.
