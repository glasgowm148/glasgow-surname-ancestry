== Biography ==
'''Preferred working family, 17 October 2026:''' Andrew Glasgow-3163 and Elizat Stevinsoun-2 are now the selected '''uncertain local parents''' of Robert Glasgow-1156 through his possible identity with their testament-proved son Robert Glasgow-4006. Archibald Glasgow-1097 and Isobell Newlands-647 remain unselected uncertain/possible alternatives. The two Robert profiles remain separate; the recorded son’s documented parentage does not make the adult identification certain.<ref name="CorsounFamilyReview" />
Archibald Glasgow was married and acquiring Glasgow burgh property by 21 May 1551. His earlier birth estimate of about 1533 would make him about eighteen at that transaction and is not record-derived. The structured estimate is therefore broadened to '''before about 1530'''; Glasgow remains an uncertain birthplace.

=== Marriage and property ===
On 21 May 1551 Elizabeth Beith sold a fore-and-back tenement on the north side of the street running from the South Port to the Clyde bridge to Archibald Glasgow and [[Newlands-647|Isobell Newlands]], explicitly described as spouses. Robert Glasgow was one of six witnesses.<ref>Robert Renwick, ed., ''Abstracts of Protocols of the Town Clerks of Glasgow'', vol. 1, protocol 84, printed p. 31, 21 May 1551, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc07robegoog/page/n59/mode/2up scan].</ref> The transaction directly proves the couple, their property acquisition, and Archibald's survival to that date.

The witness may have been [[Glasgow-1096|Robert Glasgow]] of the same burgh, but the protocol gives no occupation, spouse or patronymic that makes the identification certain. His presence supports a family or civic association, not a stated brotherhood.

=== Property succession and probable parentage ===

[[Glasgow-1098|William Glasgow]] is the leading and '''probable''' father of Archibald, but the relationship remains marked uncertain because no record explicitly calls Archibald his son. The conclusion is based on a short, unusually specific property sequence rather than chronology alone.

In 1551 Archibald and Isobell acquired the fore-and-back parcel immediately east of Thomas Bargill; [[Glasgow-1096|Robert Glasgow]] witnessed the purchase. By 31 January 1554/55 the property immediately east of Bargill's back tenement belonged to [[Glasgow-1093|John Glasgow]], who is independently documented as William's son. John's east boundary remained unchanged in 1556 and 1557.<ref name="BargillSequence">Renwick, ''Abstracts of Protocols'', vol. 1, protocol 205, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc07robegoog/page/n97/mode/2up 31 January 1554/55], printed p. 69; vol. 2, protocol 307, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc08scogoog/page/n29/mode/2up 5 August 1556], and protocol 402, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc08scogoog/page/n57/mode/2up 22 November 1557].</ref> A 1575 boundary then calls John deceased and carries the same rear title forward.<ref name="P2241">Renwick, ''Abstracts of Protocols'', vol. 7, protocol 2241, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc00robegoog/page/n145/mode/2up 23 December 1575].</ref>

The most economical genealogy is that Archibald belonged to William's household and that the parcel passed to his elder brother John by sale, settlement or succession. Robert's presence at the acquisition, followed by Robert's own repeated property and fisher association with John, reinforces a close three-man family group. A transfer to a cousin or unrelated namesake remains possible because the 1551-54 conveyance has not survived in the published abstracts. William should therefore remain an uncertain rather than confident father. Esbell Brade is William's documented wife and mother of John, but no record identifies her as Archibald's mother; her attachment also remains uncertain.

No reviewed record identifies a child of Archibald and Isobell. The attached [[Glasgow-3026|Isabella Glasgow]] and [[Glasgow-1156|Robert Glasgow I]] therefore remain uncertain children. Earlier compilations also proposed sons Andrew and John, but no primary record currently supports those placements.

[[Glasgow-1093|John Glasgow]] and Robert Glasgow occupied the same South Port and Bridgegait network. Their association supports the probable household reconstruction above but does not by itself establish exact kinship.

Archibald's date and proved place of death are unknown. The retained structured death estimate and Bridgegate location remain uncertain; protocol 84 proves only that he was alive on 21 May 1551.

=== Validator chronology note ===
WikiTree's warning that Archibald died before [[Glasgow-1156|Robert Glasgow I]] was born is not a valid reading of the structured data. Archibald's death is recorded as '''after 1560''', an open lower boundary rather than a death in 1560. Robert's 1582 birth is an uncertain estimate, and their father-son relationship is itself marked uncertain. No source currently proves when Archibald died or that Robert was his son.
