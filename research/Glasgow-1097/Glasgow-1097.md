## Suggested corrections

- Replace the unspecific 1551 citation with volume 1, protocol 84, printed page
  31 and the direct scan link above.
- Change the birth from `about 1533` to `before about 1530`, uncertain, or omit
  the year. He was already married and acquiring burgage property in May 1551;
  1533 is possible but has no record basis.
- Describe Robert Glasgow as a witness or possible relative, not as Archibald's
  likely brother. No reviewed source states their relationship.

## Do not add as fact

- William Glasgow and Esbell Brade as Archibald's proved parents. Their son
  John is proved by a separate 1534 protocol; Archibald is not named there.
- Isabella Glasgow, wife of John Howe, or Robert Glasgow of Duddingston as
  proved children. No reviewed record connects either person to Archibald.
- Isobella Glasgow, wife of Archibald Herbisoune, as Archibald's daughter. She
  was already a married property owner in January 1563 and is a separate woman.
