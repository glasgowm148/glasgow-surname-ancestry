# Findings: [Isobel Glasgow Rankine (Glasgow-1127)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1127)

## Current conclusion

The saved Irvine descendants page preserves an exact, potentially valuable
collateral relationship: Isobel Rankine, wife of John Rankine, was reportedly
served heir-general to her uncle James Glasgow, shipmaster in Irvine, with the
service registered 9 May 1766. The citation points to the printed *Decennial
Indexes to the Services of Heirs in Scotland, 1700–1859*, but no official image
or full printed entry was preserved with the page.
