# Findings: [Samuel Glasgow (Glasgow-1134), about 1749–about 1834](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1134)

## Current conclusion

`Glasgow-2869` was merged into this profile on 26 August 2026. Both profiles
represented the same Samuel Glasgow: they gave the same 1749 birth, the same
1810 and 1820 Pennsylvania residences, the same Millersburg burial, the same
FamilySearch PID, and the same Find a Grave memorial. The surviving profile
retains the spouse and child from `Glasgow-1134` and the parents and siblings
from `Glasgow-2869`.

Birth and death dates and places remain uncertain. The attached parents are
also uncertain because the only cited parentage source is a collaborative
FamilySearch tree.

August 2026. The merged biography consolidates all cited sources and records
the conflict between the memorial's 1834 death year and the contributed
FamilySearch tree's 1843 year. WT+ suggestions 585 and 578 were marked
Corrected after the live merge was verified.
