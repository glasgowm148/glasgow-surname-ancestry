== Biography ==

{{One Name Study|name=Glasgow}}

'''John Glasgow''' was born about 1756. His exact birthplace has not been proved. The estimate comes from his Gilders Creek gravestone, which records that he died on 11 December 1826 aged 70.<ref name="Gilders">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/gilders_creek_cem.htm "Gilders Creek Cemetery"], Newberry County, South Carolina, transcription for John Glasgow (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

John was a son of [[Glasgow-1144|James Glasgow]] and [[Crosier-512|Mary Crosier]]. James's will, dated 17 October 1775, gave his “well beloved son, John Glasgow” fifty acres. James also directed Mary, Robert and John to support his younger children from the profits of the land.<ref name="JamesWill">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124215937id_/http://lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_JGlasgowWill.html "Will of James Glasgow"], transcription of Newberry Will Book A, p. 194; will dated 17 October 1775 and proved 13 July 1793 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

Mary's will, dated 5 April 1822, independently called John “my son” and entrusted him with the management of her property during her lifetime.<ref name="MaryWill">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101104164512id_/http://lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_MaryWill.html "Will of Mary Glasgow"], transcription of Newberry Will Book H, pp. 489–490; will dated 5 April 1822 and proved 14 October 1822 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

John headed a three-person household in Newberry District in 1820. Its age distribution is compatible with John, a wife and [[Glasgow-1145|Absolom Glasgow]], but the schedule does not name anyone except the household head.<ref name="Census1820">[https://archive.org/details/populationsc18200120unit/page/n146/mode/1up 1820 United States census], Newberry District, South Carolina, p. 135, John Glasgow household; NARA microfilm publication M33, roll 120 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

John died on 11 December 1826 and was buried at Gilders Creek.<ref name="Gilders" />

== Research Notes ==

No inspected record names John's wife.

[[Glasgow-1145|Absolom Glasgow]], born about 1799, is attached as John's son. The 1820 household is compatible with John, a wife and Absolom, but it does not name the younger people and therefore does not itself prove the relationship. No additional son of John has been identified.

Hiram Glasgow should not be added as another son. The will of Mary, widow of [[Glasgow-1982|Wilson Glasgow]], directly calls Hiram her son.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124215757id_/http://lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_WilsonGlasgowWife.html "Will of Mary, wife of Wilson Glasgow"], transcription of Newberry Will Book N, p. 1; will dated 28 November 1837 and proved 22 October 1838 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

Hiram administered Absolom's estate in 1834 and became guardian of “J L & others” in 1837.<ref>[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/estates1785-1949/22.htm "Probate court general index to estates, 1785–1949"], entries for Absolom Glasgow, estate 1069, and J. L. Glasgow and others, estate 1901 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref> These fiduciary roles show a close family connection but do not make Hiram John's son.

[[Glasgow-2291|John Glasgow]] represents the same man. That profile repeats the approximate 1756 birth, exact 11 December 1826 death and Newberry identity, and cites the Gilders Creek memorial for this John. It has no attached relatives that belong to a separate family.
