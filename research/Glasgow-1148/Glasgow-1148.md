== Biography ==
'''Family review, 17 October 2026:''' [[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]] is now Alexander’s '''probable father''' in the preferred Fisherrow family account. [[Clerk-296|Janet Clerk]] is his '''documented wife'''. The earlier uncertain father assessment is retained below as history.<ref name="FisherrowFamilyReview" />
Alexander Glasgow was born about 1600, likely in or near Inveresk and Musselburgh, Midlothian, Scotland. He is documented in parish records as the father of at least six children between 1626 and 1637.

Parish and sasine records confirm that Alexander was the son of James Glasgow, tailor and burgess of Musselburgh, and his wife Janet Clerk. Property transactions recorded in 1624 and 1625 further support this identification.

== Children ==

All children were baptized in Inveresk and Musselburgh Parish (Parish #689):

* [[Glasgow-1150|James Glasgow]] — baptized 19 March 1626, father: Alexander Glasgow
* [[Glasgow-1149|Robert Glasgow]] — baptized 30 December 1627, father: Allexander Glasgow
* [[Glasgow-1429|Marioune Glasgow]] — baptized 17 March 1630, father: Allexander Glasgow
* [[Glasgow-1151|John Glasgow]] — baptized 25 November 1632, father: Alexander Glasgow
* [[Glasgow-1155|Bessie Glasgow]] — baptized 22 March 1635, father: Allexander Glasgow
* [[Glasgow-1152|Allexander Glasgow]] — baptized 3 September 1637, father: Allexander Glasgow, mother: Jannet Clerk (FR260)

== Property Records ==

Sasine entries confirm family relationships:

<blockquote>
"Alexander, son of James Glasgow, tailor, burgess of Musselburgh, and his spouse Janet Clerk" — 8 July 1624, 11 July 1625.
</blockquote>

These transactions name all three generations and place them in Musselburgh’s trades community.

== Research Notes ==
=== Fisherrow family review — 17 October 2026 ===

Prefer the existing [[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]] and [[Bie-55|Christina Bie]] family account for the Fisherrow James and [[Bie-55|Christina Bye]]. The printed 1624–1625 index explicitly groups James, tailor in Fisherrow and burgess of Musselburgh, his son Alexander and wife [[Bie-55|Christina Bye]]; it separately names Alexander’s wife [[Clerk-296|Janet Clerk]]. Those are the same named family roles already represented by these profiles. The local James account also includes the Inveresk father sequence of 1609–1618, which can continue into the Fisherrow household. The ordinary Bye/Bie spelling and the full family fit make the identification probable, rather than a match resting only on James’s forename. This is an assessment of the preferred existing family account, not independent confirmation from its copied tree fields.

The printed sasine index places [[Clerk-296|Janet Clerk]] with Alexander, while [[Bie-55|Christina Bye]] belongs with his father James. The 1637 Inveresk entry explicitly names Alexander Glasgow and [[Clerk-296|Jannet Clerk]] as parents of their indexed son. The matching named couple and compatible 1624–1637 parish/property sequence document Alexander and Janet’s spouse relationship; no marriage ceremony date is supplied.

The Inveresk entries name Alexander Glasgow as father of Marioune in 1630, Johnne in 1632 and Bessie in 1635. They fall within the 1626–1637 series already represented by the Fisherrow Alexander’s family. The 1624–1625 sasines identify his wife [[Clerk-296|Janet Clerk]], and the 1637 child entry repeats that parental pair. The repeated father, parish, ordered child series and independently named couple strongly support this Alexander as the father in all three entries. This is confident family reconstruction from the combined records, not an assertion that each earlier entry names his occupation or wife.

The Mid Calder and Inveresk baptism sequences may still represent one mobile father or two contemporaries; no occupation or property recital joins them. The profile’s birth and death places remain inherited claims. The 1614 FamilySearch citation’s claimed mother has not been verified: the provider record requires sign-in, while the inspected FMP and supplied ScotlandsPeople entries name James but no mother. That maternal claim is not used as independent support. Christina’s 1589 New Aberdour birth is tree-derived. Naming her as James’s wife in 1624 does not establish her as Alexander’s or William’s mother. The [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow]] and Murieston-George ancestry alternatives retain their grades.

No mother is named in the three reviewed child entries, so their maternal grades stay unchanged. Bessie’s 22 March index date and existing 20 March birth field remain unreconciled; no date is changed. The later identities of these children, [[Glasgow-3923|James Glasgow]] versus the Edinburgh weaver [[Glasgow-1150|James Glasgow]], and the Edward property associations remain separate. Existing confident father settings for [[Glasgow-3923|James Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-1149|Robert Glasgow]] and [[Glasgow-1152|Allexander Glasgow]] are retained, not counted as new upgrades. Original sasines and parish images were not newly inspected.
