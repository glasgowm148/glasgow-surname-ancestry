== Biography ==  
{{One Name Study|name=Glasgow}}

Alexander Glasgow was born about 1600, likely in or near Inveresk and Musselburgh, Midlothian, Scotland. He is documented in parish records as the father of at least six children between 1626 and 1637.

Parish and sasine records confirm that Alexander was the son of James Glasgow, tailor and burgess of Musselburgh, and his wife Janet Clerk. Property transactions recorded in 1624 and 1625 further support this identification.

== Children ==  

All children were baptized in Inveresk and Musselburgh Parish (Parish #689):

* [[Glasgow-1150|James Glasgow]] — baptized 19 March 1626, father: Alexander Glasgow
* [[Glasgow-1149|Robert Glasgow]] — baptized 30 December 1627, father: Allexander Glasgow
* [[Glasgow-1429|Marioune Glasgow]] — baptized 17 March 1630, father: Allexander Glasgow
* [[Glasgow-1151|John Glasgow]] — baptized 25 November 1632, father: Alexander Glasgow
* [[Glasgow-1155|Bessie Glasgow]] — baptized 22 March 1635, father: Allexander Glasgow
* [[Glasgow-1152|Allexander Glasgow]] — baptized 3 September 1637, father: Allexander Glasgow, mother: Jannet Clerk (FR260)

== Property Records ==  

Sasine entries confirm family relationships:

<blockquote>
"Alexander, son of James Glasgow, tailor, burgess of Musselburgh, and his spouse Janet Clerk" — 8 July 1624, 11 July 1625.
</blockquote>

These transactions name all three generations and place them in Musselburgh’s trades community.

== Research Notes ==

The currently attached father [[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]] is marked uncertain. The cited Musselburgh record identifies Alexander’s father as a James Glasgow, but does not establish that he was the attached Mid Calder James Glasgow-991; the profile itself distinguishes the local candidate.
  

* Identification of Alexander’s father as James Glasgow may overlap with other men of the same name. [[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow (abt.1575–)]] from Mid Calder may be less likely than a locally residing James Glasgow.
