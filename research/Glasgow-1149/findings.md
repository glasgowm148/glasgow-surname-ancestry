# Findings: Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-1149), baptised 1627, Inveresk

Last updated: 2026-08-31

## Current conclusion

The Scotland's People index records Robert Glasgow on 30 December 1627 at
Inveresk and Musselburgh, OPR parish 689, reference `10/171`, with father
Allexander Glasgow. The free index does not name his mother.

WikiTree assigns this Robert to the Edinburgh household of Robert Glasgow and
Euphame Lorimer. The placement is plausible by name, chronology and Midlothian
location, but no inspected record yet carries the 1627 child into that adult
household. Keep the relationship assessment cautious.

The Edinburgh burgess roll then records Robert Glasgow, cordiner, on 4 July
1666 by right of his wife Euphame, daughter of the deceased Andrew Lorimer,
beltmaker and burgess. Name, wife, place and chronology make this very probably
the father in the four baptisms. It is not evidence that he was the Robert
baptised in 1618 at Glasgow.

The 1 January 1657 St Cuthbert's index instead gives Jonet Glasgow's parents as
Robert Glasgow and Eupham Tailyour (`685/2 30/15`). Do not silently identify
Tailyour with Lorimer or combine the mothers without the original image.

## North Leith namesake, 1694

The original North Leith poll-tax page dated 3 October 1694 lists `Robt
Glasgow` among four servants in the household of Alexander Ainslie, clerk to
the glass manufactory. A later history dates the entry to 1695 and calls Robert
a glassmaker, but the original calls him only a servant. On 16 July 1694 a
Robert Glasgow gave a receipt in Ainslie's name for bottles supplied to the
Earl of Breadalbane (`GD112/15/59`, item 24). The shared employer and year make
these almost certainly the same man and prove a functional glassworks-business
role, but not that he was a skilled glassmaker. This profile's Robert would
have been about 66. Neither record supplies a link to Euphame Lorimer or the
cordiner trade, so the identity remains unresolved.

[Glasgow-3011](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3011), baptised at
Glencorse in 1676, is a stronger younger candidate for the 1694 servant. Exact
Leith OPR searches found no Glasgow/Glasgo/Glascow/Glaskow/Glassgow baptism in
1694–1730 and no exact Robert Glasgow marriage in 1694–1710 or burial in
1694–1730; register survival and spelling limit the negative evidence.
