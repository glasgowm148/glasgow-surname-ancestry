## Former new-profile draft: James Glasgow, Edinburgh weaver and burgess

This profile is required because [[Glasgow-3098]] was merged into

== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' was born before 16 June 1641, probably in Scotland. His
precise birth date and place have not been found.

On 16 June 1641 James, explicitly described as the son of '''Robert Glasgow,
servant to William Crichtoun in Wester Duddingston''', was apprenticed to
William Sclaitter, weaver.<ref name="Apprentice1641">Francis J. Grant, ed.,
''Register of Apprentices of the City of Edinburgh, 1583-1666'' (Scottish
Record Society, 1906), printed p. 71,
[https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso28scotuoft/page/71/mode/2up scan]:
"Glasgow, James, son to Robert G., servitor to William Crichtoun, in Wester
Duddingston, with William Sclaitter, weaver 16 June 1641."</ref>

James was admitted a burgess of Edinburgh on 20 June 1649. The entry identifies
him as a weaver, states that he had been apprenticed to the deceased William
Sklatter, and repeats the exact apprenticeship date of 16 June 1641. This proves
that the 1641 apprentice was the later Edinburgh weaver and burgess.<ref name="Burgess1649">Charles B. Boog Watson, ed.,
[https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/roll_of_edinburgh_burgesses_1406_to_1700.pdf#page=218
''Roll of Edinburgh Burgesses and Guild-Brethren, 1406-1700''] (Scottish Record
Society, 1929), printed p. 208.</ref>

James married first [[Whyte-2772|Bessie Whyte]] at Edinburgh on 6 July 1649.
He married second [[Duncan-16106|Agnes Duncan]] there on 11 November 1653.<ref name="Marriages">Henry Paton, ed.,
[https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/register_of_marriages_for_the_parish_of_edinburgh_1595-1700_~_1898.pdf#page=277
''Register of Marriages for the Parish of Edinburgh, 1595-1700''] (Scottish
Record Society, 1905), printed p. 267.</ref>

His known children were:

* [[Glasgow-3097|Helen Glasgow]], daughter of Bessie Whyte;
* [[Glasgow-3099|Jonet Glasgow]], daughter of Bessie Whyte;
* [[Glasgow-1374|Robert Glasgow]], son of Agnes Duncan;<ref>"Scotland, Births
and Baptisms, 1564-1950," FamilySearch,
[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VRSV-T1T Robert Glasgow,
5 May 1655].</ref>
* [[Glasgow-3143|Marion Glasgow]], daughter of Agnes Duncan;
* [[Glasgow-1373|Bethia Glasgow]], daughter of Agnes Duncan; and
* [[Glasgow-1057|John Glasgow]], son of Agnes Duncan.

On 30 June 1686 William Calder was admitted a burgess by right of his wife
Marion, explicitly described as a daughter of James Glasgow, weaver and
burgess.<ref name="Calder1686">Watson, ''Roll of Edinburgh Burgesses'', printed
p. 91,
[https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/roll_of_edinburgh_burgesses_1406_to_1700.pdf#page=101 PDF p. 101].</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Parent identity ===

The 1641 record directly proves that James's father was '''Robert Glasgow,
servant to William Crichtoun in Wester Duddingston'''. It does not provide
Robert's age, wife, parentage or occupation beyond his service to Crichtoun.

[[Glasgow-1156|Robert Glasgow]], buried at Duddingston on 17 January 1647, is
the leading existing-profile match because of locality and chronology, but the
identity is '''not confirmed'''. If he is attached, the relationship must be
marked uncertain and the distinction between the proved father-description and
the unproved profile match must remain explicit. Do not create a second Robert
merely to turn a record-defined name into a nominally certain WikiTree link.

=== Distinct Inveresk James ===

This man was not [[Glasgow-1150|James Glasgow]], baptised at Inveresk and
Musselburgh on 19 March 1626 to [[Glasgow-1148|Alexander Glasgow]] and
[[Clerk-296|Jannet Clerk]].<ref>"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950,"
FamilySearch,
[https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4V-GQW James Glasgow,
19 March 1626].</ref> The apprenticeship names Robert, not Alexander, as the
Edinburgh weaver's father. The two profiles were merged before that conflicting
record was found and now require separation.

[[Glasgow-1376|James Glasgow]] is not a supported child. Its cited 29 January
1657 record belongs to Marion Glasgow and names James as her father.
