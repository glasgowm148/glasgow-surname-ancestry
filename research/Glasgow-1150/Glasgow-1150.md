== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' was born before 16 June 1641, probably in Scotland. His
precise date and place of birth have not been identified.

=== Apprenticeship, trade and burgess status ===

On 16 June 1641 James, explicitly described as a son of Robert Glasgow, servant
to William Crichtoun in Wester Duddingston, was apprenticed to William
Sclaitter, weaver.<ref name="Apprentice1641">Francis J. Grant, ed.,
[https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso28scotuoft/page/71/mode/2up
''Register of Apprentices of the City of Edinburgh, 1583–1666''] (Scottish
Record Society, 1906), printed p. 71: “Glasgow, James, son to Robert G.,
servitor to William Crichtoun, in Wester Duddingston, with William Sclaitter,
weaver 16 June 1641.”</ref>

James was admitted as a burgess of Edinburgh on 20 June 1649. The entry calls
him a weaver, says that he had been apprenticed to the deceased William
Sklatter, and repeats the apprenticeship date of 16 June 1641.<ref
name="Burgess1649">Charles B. Boog Watson, ed.,
[https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/roll_of_edinburgh_burgesses_1406_to_1700.pdf#page=218
''Roll of Edinburgh Burgesses and Guild-Brethren, 1406–1700''] (Scottish Record
Society, 1929), printed p. 208.</ref> The matching master and exact date prove
that the 1641 apprentice became the Edinburgh weaver and burgess.

These entries trace a long, consistent career as an Edinburgh weaver,
burgess and master.

=== Marriages ===

James married first '''[[Whyte-2772|Bessie Whyte]]''' at Edinburgh on 6 July
1649. He married second '''[[Duncan-16106|Agnes Duncan]]''' there on 11 November
1653.<ref name="EdinburghMarriages">Henry Paton, ed.,
[https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/register_of_marriages_for_the_parish_of_edinburgh_1595-1700_~_1898.pdf#page=277
''Register of Marriages for the Parish of Edinburgh, 1595–1700''] (Scottish
Record Society, 1905), printed p. 267.</ref> The matching name, occupation and
parish, together with the sequential dates, identify the bridegroom in both
entries as the same man.

=== Children ===

James's record-supported children include:

* [[Glasgow-3097|Helen Glasgow]], daughter of Bessie Whyte;
* [[Glasgow-3099|Jonet Glasgow]], daughter of Bessie Whyte;
* [[Glasgow-1374|Robert Glasgow]], son of Agnes Duncan, baptised at Edinburgh
  on 5 May 1655;<ref name="RobertBaptism">“Scotland, Births and Baptisms,
  1564–1950,” database, FamilySearch,
  [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VRSV-T1T Robert Glasgow],
  baptised 5 May 1655, Edinburgh, son of James Glasgow and Agnes Duncan.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-3143|Marion Glasgow]], daughter of Agnes Duncan;
* [[Glasgow-1373|Bethia Glasgow]], daughter of Agnes Duncan; and
* [[Glasgow-1057|John Glasgow]], son of Agnes Duncan.

The individual baptism entries should be consulted for the evidence naming
each child's mother.

Marion married William Calder at Edinburgh on 29 June 1686.<ref
name="MarionMarriage">Paton, ''Register of Marriages for the Parish of
Edinburgh'', [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/register_of_marriages_for_the_parish_of_edinburgh_1595-1700_~_1898.pdf#page=278
printed p. 268].</ref> On the following day William was admitted as a burgess
through his wife Marion, explicitly described as a daughter of James Glasgow,
weaver and burgess.<ref name="CalderBurgess">Watson, ''Roll of Edinburgh
Burgesses'', [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/roll_of_edinburgh_burgesses_1406_to_1700.pdf#page=101
printed p. 91].</ref>

James's date and place of death have not been identified. He was alive on 18
October 1693.<ref name="Master1693" />

== Research Notes ==

=== Birth and parentage ===

The former birth date of 19 March 1626 and birthplace of Inveresk belong to
[[Glasgow-3923|James Glasgow]], son of [[Glasgow-1148|Alexander Glasgow]] and
[[Clerk-296|Jannet Clerk]], not to this man.<ref name="InvereskBaptism">“Scotland,
Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” database, FamilySearch,
[https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4V-GQW James Glasgow], baptised 19
March 1626, Inveresk and Musselburgh.</ref>

The apprenticeship directly names this James's father as Robert Glasgow,
servant to William Crichtoun in Wester Duddingston.<ref
name="Apprentice1641" /> [[Glasgow-1156|Robert Glasgow]] is a plausible
profile match, but the record does not prove that identity; preserve the
relationship as uncertain. The record does not name James's mother.
[[Ivey-2264|Deborah Ivey]] is an uncertain working attachment only.

=== Incorrect child profile ===

[[Glasgow-1376|James Glasgow]] is not supported as a son born on 29 January
1657. The cited event is the baptism of Marion Glasgow and names James as her
father; it is not a birth record for a son James.

=== Probable later sons ===

On 22 April 1696 a James Glasgow, son of James Glasgow, weaver and burgess, was
apprenticed to William Adam, apothecary and burgess. On 11 October 1699 Thomas,
youngest son of James Glasgow, weaver and burgess, was apprenticed to James
Affleck, baxter and burgess.<ref name="LaterSons">Henry Paton, ed.,
[https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/edinburgh_apprentices_~_1666_to_1700_~_1929.pdf
''Register of Apprentices of the City of Edinburgh, 1666–1700''] (Scottish
Record Society, 1929), printed p. 37.</ref>

The exact name, trade, burgess status and city, together with James's 1693
master entry, make this profile the probable identity of their father. Neither
entry names a mother, however, and the late chronology warrants uncertain
attachments until a baptism, testament or incorporation record adds a second
identifier.

The 7 May 1690 apprentice James was a different person: his father was already
deceased and was a weaver at Lindsayland.<ref name="LaterSons" />

=== Further research ===

Priority records are James's burial or testament; baptisms or incorporation
papers for the 1696 James and 1699 Thomas; and evidence that identifies Robert
Glasgow of Wester Duddingston. [[Glasgow-3077|James Glasgow]], husband of
[[Clerk-460|Helen Clerk]], is a separate same-name man.
