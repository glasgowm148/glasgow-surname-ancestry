== Biography ==

'''Robert Glasgow''' was buried at Duddingston on 17 January 1647.<ref
name="Burial1647">“Church of Scotland: Old Parish Registers—Deaths and
Burials,” National Records of Scotland,
[https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/ ScotlandsPeople], Robert Glasgow, 17
January 1647, Duddingston; parish 684, reference 60/47.</ref> The record cited on
this profile does not supply his age, parents, wife or occupation, so his birth
about 1582 remains an estimate.

On 16 June 1641, '''James Glasgow''', son of a Robert Glasgow who was servant to
William Crichtoun at Wester Duddingston, was apprenticed to Edinburgh weaver
William Sclaitter.<ref name="James1641">Francis J. Grant, ed., ''Register of
Apprentices of the City of Edinburgh, 1583–1666'' (Scottish Record Society,
1906), [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso28scotuoft/page/71/mode/2up
p. 71], 16 June 1641.</ref> The matching Duddingston locality makes this profile
the leading identity candidate for that father, but the record gives no age,
spouse or occupation that makes the identification unique. [[Glasgow-1150|James
Glasgow]] should therefore remain only an uncertain son.

== Research Notes ==

=== Possible parents ===

No record found names [[Glasgow-1097|Archibald Glasgow]] as Robert's father;
that attachment is based on chronology and should remain uncertain. Archibald
was recorded alive in 1560, but any later survival estimate used to accommodate
this relationship is not itself a record.

Robert was compared with the '''Robert Glasgow''' whom
[[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow of uncertain Corsoun]] directly called his son by Elizat
Stevinsoun in 1597/8. Andrew directed that Elizat should enjoy his land (one
preceding word is unclear) until Robert came of age.<ref name="Andrew1598">National Records
of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124, testament
testamentar and inventory of Andro Glasgw, made 6 February 1597/8 and confirmed
10 July 1598; corrected working transcription of the original register
images.</ref> The estimated birth and Lothian geography fit, but no record links
the Duddingston man to Corsoun, Andrew, Elizat or the land. This profile is not
the present match for Andrew's son; do not attach Andrew without a direct
bridge. A later property or succession record could justify merging the
separate documentary profile.

[[Glasgow-1027]] is separately proved as the son of Ninian Glasgow and Jonet
Meik. The similar estimated dates require comparison but do not justify a merge.
[[Glasgow-3923]], baptised in 1626 to Alexander Glasgow and Jannet Clerk, is a
different person from the 1641 Edinburgh apprentice [[Glasgow-1150|James
Glasgow]] and should not be attached here.
The presently attached wife [[Ivey-2264|Deborah Ivey]] and son
[[Glasgow-1157]] are not established by either the burial or the apprenticeship
and require independent evidence.

The highest-value test is an occupier succession in NRS GD150/696–757,
GD150/2027–2060 or GD150/320–493 connecting Andrew's land and son Robert to the
later Duddingston household.
