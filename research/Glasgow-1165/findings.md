# Findings: [James Glasgow (Glasgow-1165)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1165)

## Probate identity exclusion

Glasgow-1165 is not a defensible match for the James Glasgow of Killycurragh in the 1836 probate index. Although the structured profile reports death on 17 August 1836 in Ireland, its biography quotes a family Bible entry for a person born 9 November 1808 who died 20 April 1823, and the opening sentence calls him John rather than James. The profile is internally conflated and supplies no Killycurragh connection.

Do not attach the 1836 Killycurragh occurrence until this profile's name and vital dates are reconstructed from the cited family Bible.
## Tyrone probate exclusions

The family Bible death of 20 April 1823 excludes this James from the 1826
administration bond for James Glasgow, gentleman of Oritor, and the 21 March
1836 will of James Glasgow of Killycarragh. Those records must not be attached
to this profile. Preserve them as separate candidate estates until another
existing profile is identified.
