# Findings: [Euphame Glasgow/Glasfoord (Glasgow-1167), 1634](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1167)

## Relationship correction - 20 August 2026

Euphame's former attachment to [Rev. John Glasford of Kilwinning (Glassford-289)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glassford-289) was caused by a conflation of two men.

Edinburgh's register records John Glassford, slater, marrying Janet or Jonet Murriell on 15 July 1619. The minister's wife is identified by Fasti as Jean Blair. Euphame and the other Edinburgh Glasford/Murriell children have therefore been moved to [Glassfuird-2](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glassfuird-2), with [Morriel-1](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Morriel-1) retained as mother.

This assignment is well supported, but the original baptism image should still be inspected for the father's occupation, residence and witnesses.

---

## Conclusion

This is a genuine two-family conflation. FamilySearch X1Y9-V87 indexes Euphame
in 1634 with father `Johnne Glasgow`. The same event is claimed in
[Glasgow-1030](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1030)'s biography as a
child of John Glasgow and Jonet Mathie, while Euphame is structurally attached
to Rev. [John Glassford](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glassford-289) and Jonet
Morriel within an otherwise coherent Edinburgh Glassford sibling sequence.

The event's Edinburgh placement favours the Glassford household over the
Glasgow High Kirk family, but the index surname favours Glasgow. The original
parish image is required. It must not be attached to both families meanwhile.

The 1850-52 Euphemia Glasgow occurrence in the biography is more than two
centuries later and unrelated.

## Recommended update

1. Remove Jonet Mathie from the prose; structured mother Morriel-1 is the wife
   associated with the Glassford family.
2. Remove the 1850-52 occurrence.
3. Retain `Glasfoord` as an alternate surname; do not change LNAB until the
   original 1634 image is inspected.
4. Remove Euphame from Glasgow-1030's child list unless the image instead proves
   that Glasgow/Mathie family.
5. Keep the Glassford parents qualified as probable pending that review.
