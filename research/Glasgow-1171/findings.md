# Robert Glasgow (`Glasgow-1171`)

## Conclusion

Robert Glasgow (1809-1900) is a strong candidate member of the County Tyrone sibling group represented by [[Glasgow-1969|William Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-1967|John Glasgow]], and [[Glasgow-4025|Eliza Glasgow]], but no located record directly names their shared parents or calls Robert their brother. No relationship should yet be attached.

## Evidence assessment

| Claim | Assessment | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Born 25 May 1809 in County Tyrone | Secondary, plausible | Cayuga County biographical account used by the live profile |
| Settled in Auburn, New York | Supported | County biography and later census sequence |

| Emigrated aged nineteen | Conflicted | Existing Cayuga biography implies about 1828; modern reconstruction reports 1835 |

## Candidate family connection

A modern reconstruction identifies Robert (born 1809), William (born 1811), Eliza (born 1814), and John (born 1814) within the same County Tyrone-to-Cayuga migration network and treats Robert and Eliza as probable siblings. The nineteenth-century Hillsdale biographies independently prove William and John were brothers and state that Eliza came to Auburn with two brothers, but do not name those brothers. The overlap is significant but not proof.

The unidentified brother Samuel named in Robert's biography must also be reconciled before attaching parents or siblings. He may be one of Eliza's two emigrating brothers, another sibling, or a biographical error.
