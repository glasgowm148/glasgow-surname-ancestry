# Sarah Glasgow Kermode (Glasgow-1180) — findings

## Current conclusion

Sarah's WikiTree birth estimate of **about 1824** remains preferable to Find a
Grave's 1826 estimate. It remains uncertain.

## Source findings

The 1841/1842 assisted-immigrant passenger records give Sarah's age as 18,
supporting birth about 1823–1824: [passenger record 60382](https://www.ancestry.com/discoveryui-content/view/60382:1204)
and [passenger record 85718](https://www.ancestry.com/discoveryui-content/view/85718:1204).
[Find a Grave memorial 178441932](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/178441932)
gives 1826 but is later derivative evidence. WT+ Hint 575 was therefore marked
false rather than changing the earlier record-based estimate.
