# Suggested WikiTree additions for [Philip Glasgow (Glasgow-1241), baptised 1711, Uphall/Linlithgowshire, Scotland](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1241)

## Research Notes

The Dublin material contains two watchmakers named Philip Glasco. Philip
senior was of Christ Church Lane by 1750, when a newspaper reported his
marriage to the widow Mangan of County Kildare. His wife died in 1760. Philip
junior of Christchurch Yard married Teresa Plunkett in 1768 and died in Christ
Church Lane in 1770. A Dublin Gazette death notice and will index place the
elder watchmaker's death in 1772.

These records establish the Dublin trade family but do not yet prove that
Philip senior was the child baptised in Scotland in 1711. The Dublin identity
and migration should remain uncertain until the 1772 will or another record
names a Scottish relative.
