# Findings: [Philip Glasgow (Glasgow-1241), baptised 1711, Uphall/Linlithgowshire, Scotland](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1241)

## Current conclusion

The Irish records define **two Dublin watchmakers named Philip Glasco**. The
elder was established in Christ Church Lane by 1750 and died in 1772; Philip
junior married Teresa Plunkett in 1768 and died in 1770. The live profile's
Scottish baptism and the Dublin elder are chronologically compatible, but no
reviewed record yet proves that the baptised child migrated to Dublin.

## Identity treatment

- Keep the Scottish baptism as proved for this profile.
- Describe the Dublin elder as a **possible later identity**, not a proved
  migration, until his will or another Dublin record names Scottish kin.
- Create or locate a separate profile for Philip junior before attaching
  Teresa Plunkett or the 1770 death.
