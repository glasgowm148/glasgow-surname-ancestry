== Biography ==
Thomas Glasgow was christened on 21 February 1675 in Linlithgow, Linlithgowshire, Scotland, the son of [[Glasgow-1105|John Glasgow]] and [[Eastone-3|Agnes Eastone]].<ref name="Baptism">"Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, ''FamilySearch'' (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4C-P4L : 16 September 2021), Thomas Glesgow, 1675.</ref>

He married [[Duncan-16665|Jean Duncan]] on 4 May 1701 in Linlithgow.<ref name="Marriage">Marriage date from family records; primary source from the Linlithgow Old Parish Registers needs to be located and attached.</ref> Thomas and Jean had at least two sons confirmed in the Linlithgowshire area:

* [[Glasgow-1244|A son]], whose name is not given in the indexed record, was christened on 2 July 1704 in the neighboring parish of Uphall, Linlithgowshire.<ref name="Son1704">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, ''FamilySearch'' (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ48-MZ7 : 16 September 2021), Thomas Glasghow in entry for Glasghow, 1704.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-1241|Philip Glasgow]] was christened on 21 October 1711, back in the parish of Linlithgow.<ref name="Philip1711">"Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, ''FamilySearch'' (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ48-S78 : 16 September 2021), Thomas Glesgow in entry for Philips Glesgow, 1711.</ref>

Although the 1711 christening record for Philip omits his mother's name, the established marriage of his father to Jean Duncan in the same parish makes her the certain mother. The omission was likely a simple oversight by the parish clerk.

The preceding claim of certain maternity is not adopted by this review. An omitted mother and a proposed household identification do not establish Jean Duncan as this child’s certain mother; retain the attribution as uncertain until an identifying record resolves it.

The date and place of Thomas's death are not yet known, but he was alive as of October 1711.
