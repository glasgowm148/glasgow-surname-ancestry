== Biography ==

Thomas Glasgow was born before 15 June 1715, when he was baptized at Edinburgh, Midlothian, Scotland. The parish entry names his father as [[Glasgow-3285|Thomas Glasgow]], a baxter and indweller.<ref name="FMP">Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929'', Thomas Glasgow, baptism 15 June 1715, Edinburgh, father Thomas Glasgow, “Baxter Indweller,” OPR 685_1/16, item 3, page 53; record SCOT/OPR/BAP/0606238; [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F0606238&tab=this transcript], accessed 4 September 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The baptism names only Thomas's father. It supports a confident father relationship to Thomas Glasgow (Glasgow-3285), whose own biography identifies this son from the same baptism. It does not identify a mother. [[Glasgow-1242|Thomas Glasgow]] and [[Duncan-16665|Jean Duncan]] appeared as parents in the captured narrative, but that claim conflicts with the structured father and is not supported by this record.
