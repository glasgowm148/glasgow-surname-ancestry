# Agnes Glasgow (Glasgow-1312) research findings

## Current conclusion

The documented 1677 Kirknewton and East Calder birth remains the
identity-defining event for [[Glasgow-1312|Agnes Glasgow]]. A malformed
Findmypast burial row does not establish her death.

## Identity assessment — malformed burial index

Findmypast record `SCOT/OPR/BUR/1625561` gives Kirknewton and East Calder and an
unyear'd 7 April burial, but cites `OPR 689/20` and range 1642–1737. Parish 689
is Inveresk and volume 20 is deaths for 1820–1854; Kirknewton and East Calder
is parish 690. The displayed 1642 cannot concern a woman born in 1677, while
the row supplies no later event year. See the [source-anomaly dossier](../hold-resolution/record-dossiers/fmp-glasgow-11443ace3ea2-agnes-glasgow-burial-index-anomaly.md).

## Recommended action

Do not add the malformed burial row or a death date from it.
