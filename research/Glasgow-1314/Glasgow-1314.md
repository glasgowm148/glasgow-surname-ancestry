== Biography ==
'''Father review, 17 October 2026:''' Alexander [[Glasgow-3118]] is now the '''probable father''' of the 1673 Kirknewton Alexander. Margaret Smith’s maternity and the proposed later children remain separately unproved.<ref name="ParishParentReview" />
Alexander Glasgow was born on 7 July 1673 and christened on 20 July 1673 at Kirknewton and East Calder, Midlothian, Scotland.<ref name="Alexander1673">''Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950'', database, FamilySearch ([https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-X9V VQ4K-X9V]), Alexander Glasgow, birth 7 July 1673, christening 20 July 1673, Kirknewton and East Calder, Midlothian, Scotland; father Alexander Glasgow.</ref> His father was [[Glasgow-3118|Alexander Glasgow]]. The indexed christening record names no mother.

Alexander is currently linked as a son of [[Smith-230509|Margaret (Smith) Glasgow]], although the cited baptism record does not identify his mother. His known sibling is [[Glasgow-1312|Agnes Glasgow]].

=== Marriages ===

Alexander married [[Aikman-803|Margaret Aikman]] on 7 November 1701 at Kirknewton, Linlithgowshire, Scotland.<ref name="AikmanMarriage">''Scotland, Marriages, 1561-1910'', database, FamilySearch ([https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XY9W-T57 XY9W-T57]), Alexander Glasgou and Margrat Aikman, marriage 7 November 1701, Kirknewton, Linlithgowshire, Scotland.</ref>

They were the parents of at least [[Glasgow-1313|Andrew Glasgow]]. Andrew's christening record explicitly names his parents as Alexander Glasgow and Margaret Aikman.<ref name="Andrew1703">''Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950'', database, FamilySearch ([https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-NJB VQ4K-NJB]), Andrew Glasgow, christening 21 November 1703, Kirknewton and East Calder, Midlothian, Scotland; parents Alexander Glasgow and Margaret Aikman.</ref>

Alexander apparently married secondly [[Graham-23064|Janet Graham]] on 16 November 1705 at Kirknewton, Linlithgowshire, Scotland.<ref name="GrahamMarriage">''Scotland, Marriages, 1561-1910'', database, FamilySearch ([https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XY9W-TYZ XY9W-TYZ]), Alexander Glasgow and Janet Graham, marriage 16 November 1705, Kirknewton, Linlithgowshire, Scotland.</ref>

The two marriages, four years apart in the same parish, together with Alexander's children recorded there, make it plausible that the 1701 and 1705 marriage records refer to the same man. The evidence presently available does not establish what happened to Margaret Aikman between Andrew's baptism in 1703 and Alexander's marriage to Janet Graham in 1705.

=== Children ===

Children currently linked to Alexander are:

* [[Glasgow-1141|Alexander Glasgow]]
* [[Glasgow-1313|Andrew Glasgow]], christened 21 November 1703.<ref name="Andrew1703" />
* [[Glasgow-1316|James Glasgow]], christened 8 September 1707.<ref name="James1707">''Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950'', database, FamilySearch ([https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-6MS VQ4K-6MS]), James Glasgou, christening 8 September 1707, Kirknewton and East Calder, Midlothian, Scotland; father Alexander Glasgou.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-1315|Marion Glasgow]], christened 18 May 1711.<ref name="Marion1711">''Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950'', database, FamilySearch ([https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-H54 VQ4K-H54]), Marion Glasgou, christening 18 May 1711, Kirknewton and East Calder, Midlothian, Scotland; father Alexander Glasgou.</ref>

[[Glasgow-1313|Andrew Glasgow]] can be confidently assigned to Alexander and [[Aikman-803|Margaret Aikman]], since both parents are named in his christening record.<ref name="Andrew1703" />

[[Glasgow-1316|James Glasgow]] and [[Glasgow-1315|Marion Glasgow]] were christened after Alexander's 1705 marriage to [[Graham-23064|Janet Graham]]. Their christening records name Alexander as father but do not name a mother.<ref name="James1707" /><ref name="Marion1711" /> Janet is therefore a plausible mother of both children, but the relationship should not be treated as proven solely from these records.

No source for [[Glasgow-1141|Alexander Glasgow]] as a child has yet been included in this profile.

== Research Notes ==
=== Parish parent review — 17 October 2026 ===

Alexander’s 1673 Kirknewton birth/baptism index explicitly names father Alexander Glasgow. The existing [[Glasgow-3118]] account is the same-parish man indexed marrying Margaret Smith in 1667, six years earlier, with a compatible father generation and a later Agnes child association in 1677. The exact father name, parish and ordered household chronology together favour that existing adult as the named father. Assess the attachment as probable rather than leaving it ungraded. This is an inference identifying the indexed father with the 1667 adult; the child entry alone does not identify that adult by wife, trade or property.

Another Alexander could have fathered the 1673 child. The marriage and baptism supply no inspected cross-reference, and the father’s circa-1650 birth is an estimate, not an age statement. Margaret Smith is not named as mother in the reviewed 1673 entry and receives no maternity upgrade. [[Glasgow-1546]] remains an uncertain proposed son of the older Alexander. [[Glasgow-1141]] remains an unproved child of the 1673 Alexander; the latter himself remains a possible older father behind the 1721–1732 child series. The 1673 birth/baptism dates, the 1701 and 1705 marriages and the later children’s maternity questions are retained. The older [[Glasgow-3610]] father route is not strengthened.

This profile incorporates information previously represented by two profiles for an Alexander Glasgow.

One profile gave Alexander's birth year as 1685. However, the indexed parish record identifies an Alexander Glasgow born on 7 July 1673 and christened on 20 July 1673 at Kirknewton and East Calder, with father Alexander Glasgow.<ref name="Alexander1673" /> The 1673 date is therefore used here. The origin of the 1685 date remains unclear.

The family chronology is:

* 7 July 1673 — birth of Alexander Glasgow.<ref name="Alexander1673" />
* 7 November 1701 — marriage to [[Aikman-803|Margaret Aikman]].<ref name="AikmanMarriage" />
* 21 November 1703 — christening of their son [[Glasgow-1313|Andrew Glasgow]], with both parents named.<ref name="Andrew1703" />
* 16 November 1705 — marriage of Alexander Glasgow to [[Graham-23064|Janet Graham]].<ref name="GrahamMarriage" />
* 8 September 1707 — christening of [[Glasgow-1316|James Glasgow]], father Alexander named but no mother recorded.<ref name="James1707" />
* 18 May 1711 — christening of [[Glasgow-1315|Marion Glasgow]], father Alexander named but no mother recorded.<ref name="Marion1711" />

This sequence is consistent with one Alexander Glasgow marrying Margaret Aikman in 1701 and Janet Graham in 1705. It also explains why Andrew can be assigned to Margaret while the maternity of James and Marion remains uncertain.

=== Current research ===

FamilySearch VQ4K-X9V supports Alexander's 1673 Kirknewton event and names his father Alexander. His 7 November 1701 marriage to Margaret Aikman and their children are separately indexed.

[[Glasgow-3119|Glasgow-3119]] may duplicate him, but that profile supplies no birth source. Its structured 1673 date and same-father attachment conflict with its biography's 1685 estimate. Its 1705 marriage to Janet Graham could be a second marriage or could identify another Alexander.
