# Findings: [Andrew Glasgow (Glasgow-1319), Kirknewton father](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1319)

Checked against the live profile and linked child profiles on **26 July 2026**.

## Current conclusion

Andrew is a legitimate record-defined father: Kirknewton baptism indexes name
Andrew/Andro Glasgow as father of Bessie in 1666 and William in 1671. The latter
is [Glasgow-1026](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1026), so that
father-child link is direct rather than a naming-pattern reconstruction.

No inspected record identifies Andrew's wife, parents, birth or death. His
`about 1645` birth is a demographic estimate from the 1666 child, and the
structured link to [William-3515](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3515)
rests only on locality and naming. It should not be treated as proved.

## Recommended WikiTree changes

1. Retain Bessie-1493 and William-1026 as confirmed children and cite both
   baptism indexes on Andrew's profile.
2. Retain `about 1645` and Kirknewton as explicitly uncertain estimates from
   the 1666 child. Kirknewton is Andrew's documented child-baptism location,
   not his proved birthplace, but remains a useful estimate until better
   evidence supplies a replacement.
3. Retain William-3515 as an uncertain father; no record names Andrew as
   William's son and the link should not be treated as proved.
4. Leave Andrew's mother and spouse blank. Do not manufacture a wife from the
   children's entries.
5. Add no death date or place without a separate record.

## Do not infer

- Parentage from the name William used by Andrew's son.
- That Andrew was born at the parish where his children were baptised.
- Any Scotland-to-Ireland connection from the later claims on William-1026.
