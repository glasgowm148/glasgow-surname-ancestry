== Biography ==

'''Robert Glasgow''' was born about 1779 in County Antrim, Ireland. The detailed
1851 census records him as seventy-two years old and County Antrim-born.<ref name="Robert1851Detailed">[https://durrushistory.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/1851irishcensusreturnscommadelimitedtextfilejul24_071.pdf ''1851 Irish Census Returns: detailed comma-delimited transcription''], Glenleslie household 23.</ref>

Robert married Catherine about 1803. In 1851 he was a farmer at Glenleslie and
Catherine, aged sixty-eight, was spinning flax. The household included their
unmarried daughter Margaret, aged fifty; grandchildren Mary and James Young,
both fifteen; and Mary Glasgow, an unmarried thirty-four-year-old niece and
linen weaver. Sons John, forty, and Samuel, thirty, were recorded as being in
America. An infant granddaughter, Catherine Hume, had died in 1850.<ref name="Robert1851Macafee">[https://www.billmacafee.com/census/1851censuskilconway.pdf Bill MacAfee, ''1851 Census, Barony of Kilconway''], Glenleslie household 23.</ref>
The household also survives in the National Archives of Ireland's earlier
online census presentation.<ref name="Robert1851NAI">[http://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Dunaghy/Glenleslie/23/ National Archives of Ireland, 1851 census], Glenleslie household 23.</ref>

Robert may be the Robert Glasco recorded at Doonbought in the 1825 tithe
applotment. He was about forty-six and living in the correct parish, but the
entry gives no age or family relationship.<ref name="Doonbought1825">[https://www.billmacafee.com/182030stithe/tithenorthantrimrevisionorder.pdf Bill MacAfee, ''North Antrim Tithe Applotment Books arranged in document order''], record 5804, Robert Glasco, Doonbought, 1825.</ref>

His death has not been proved. PRONI's pre-1858 name index records a Robert
Glasgow of Glenleslie in 1854, but the original will was destroyed and no text
naming heirs survives.<ref name="RobertWillIndex">[https://apps.proni.gov.uk/ProniNames_IE/SearchPage.aspx PRONI Name Search], pre-1858 wills and administrations, Robert Glasgow of Glenleslie, 1854.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Parents and siblings ===

No inspected record identifies Robert's parents. [[Glasgow-3902|Adam Glasgow]]
and Rose were once proposed, but Robert was born when Rose was about twelve and
the 1851 census places his birth in County Antrim, not their County Londonderry
cluster. [[Glasgow-1022|John Glasgow]] lived nearby at Glenleslie, but Robert was
born about twelve years before John and Rose Alexander's reported 1791 marriage.
Household proximity does not establish a father-son relationship, and Robert's
parents remain blank.

The niece in Robert's household is probably [[Glasgow-3957|Mary (Glasgow)
Montgomery]]. Mary's 1862 marriage names her father as '''Hugh Glasgow,
weaver''', and her 1898 death age gives a birth about 1818, closely matching the
1851 niece.<ref name="MaryMarriage1862">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1862/09623/5489617.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Thomas Montgomery and Mary Glasgow, 13 September 1862.</ref><ref name="MaryDeath1898">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/deaths_returns/deaths_1898/05842/4654852.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil death register], Mary Montgomery, 22 March 1898.</ref> If the identity is correct, Hugh was probably Robert's brother. The link remains probable because neither record identifies Mary as the Glenleslie niece.

Another Hugh Glasgow, weaver, was established at Liminary in Ballyclug by the
marriages of his children Hugh and Eliza. Hugh junior married Ann Wilson in
1852 and Eliza married William Montgomery in 1854; both certificates name Hugh
Glasgow, weaver, as father.<ref name="HughMarriage1852">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1852/09422/5413278.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Hugh Glasgow and Ann Wilson, 24 December 1852.</ref><ref name="ElizaMarriage1854">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1854/09463/5428881.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], William Montgomery and Eliza Glasgow, 21 August 1854.</ref> Mary may have been their older sister, but the common name and occupation do not yet prove that the Liminary Hugh was Robert's brother.

=== Children ===

The 1851 return directly supports Margaret, John and Samuel as Robert's
children. It also establishes grandchildren in Young and Hume families, though
their parents are not specified. [[Glasgow-1053|Margaret Glasgow]] was unmarried
and living with Robert in 1851; she should not be merged with Margaret Glasgow
Winters, who reportedly emigrated in 1845 and had children from 1829.

The younger [[Glasgow-1049|Robert Glasgow]] at Doonbought in 1851 is a plausible
son or close relative and may represent succession to the 1825 holding. The two
census households do not state a relationship, so that attachment remains
uncertain.

=== Identity caution ===

Robert was not the later Robert at Gortereghy. Revision books carry that holding
after 1864, when this Robert would have been about eighty-five. [[Glasgow-938|Robert
Glasgow]], whose 1866 marriage names father Alexander and connects Lisnagaver
with Gortereghy, is the materially better identification.<ref name="Robert938Marriage">[https://civilrecords.irishgenealogy.ie/churchrecords/images/marriage_returns/marriages_1866/11531/8240963.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Robert Glasgow and Jane Graham, 1866.</ref>

Earlier local searches also consulted published marriage notices, Belfast-area
newspaper extracts and Tamlaght graveyard material.<ref name="LocalSearches">[https://cotyrone.com/~inthenews/marriages-notices-1840-42/ County Tyrone marriage notices, 1840–1842]; [https://www.kinsleuth.com/belnews/1770-1779.htm Belfast newspaper extracts, 1770–1779]; [https://www.oneirishrover.com/tamlaght-graveyard/ Tamlaght graveyard material]. None names Robert's parents or overrides the Glenleslie evidence above.</ref>
