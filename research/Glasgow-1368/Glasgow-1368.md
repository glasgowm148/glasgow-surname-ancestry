== Biography ==

'''Ellen Glasgow''' was born about 1825 in County Antrim, Ireland. The more specific Dunaghy birthplace in the structured field is an uncertain local estimate rather than a detail stated by the census. The surviving 1851 census records her at Glenleslie, Dunaghy, as the twenty-six-year-old married daughter of [[Glasgow-1022|John Glasgow]].<ref name="Census1851">[https://www.billmacafee.com/census/1851censuskilconway.pdf William Macafee and W. Scott, "1851 Census for some Parishes and Townlands in the Barony of Kilconway, Co. Antrim"], Dunaghy, Glenleslie, household 21, entries 8292–8301 (accessed 13 August 2026).</ref>

On 13 May 1847 Ellen entered Ballymena Workhouse. The register describes her as 22, married and deserted, Presbyterian, and resident at Glenleslie. She was discharged alive on 13 July 1847.<ref name="Workhouse1847">[https://www.billmacafee.com/otherrecords/ballymenaworkhouse.pdf William Macafee, "Admissions to Ballymena Workhouse: 13th May to 8th July 1847"], registration 4130, Ellen Glasgow of Glenleslie (accessed 13 August 2026).</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Family relationships ===

The agreement in name, age, marital status and townland makes the 1847 workhouse entrant a strong match for this Ellen. Neither record names her husband.

The 1851 household also contains Jane and James Grimes, each described as a grandchild of John Glasgow. It does not state that they were Ellen's children. [[Grimes-6878|Unknown Grimes]], [[Grimes-7900|Jane Grimes]] and [[Grimes-7901|James Grimes]] therefore remain uncertain working relationships rather than proved members of Ellen's nuclear family.

The census directly proves [[Glasgow-1022|John Glasgow]] as Ellen's father. It does not state that John's wife [[Alexander-16967|Rose Alexander]] was Ellen's mother, so Rose's maternity remains uncertain.
