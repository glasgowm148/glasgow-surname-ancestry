# Findings: Glasgow-1368

Last updated: 2026-08-19

## Current conclusion

The 1851 Glenleslie census directly calls [[Glasgow-1368|Ellen Glasgow]], age
26, a married daughter of [[Glasgow-1022|John Glasgow]]. An independent 1847
Ballymena Workhouse entry records Ellen Glasgow, age 22, married and deserted,
Presbyterian, of Glenleslie. The agreement of name, age, status and townland
strongly identifies the same woman.

Neither record names Ellen's husband. The 1851 return calls Jane and James
Grimes grandchildren of John, but does not call them Ellen's children. Keep
[[Grimes-6878|Unknown Grimes]] and the two Grimes children uncertain; the
records do not prove that nuclear family. The census proves John as Ellen's
father but does not independently prove Rose as her mother.

## Source findings

- [Ballymena Workhouse admissions](https://www.billmacafee.com/otherrecords/ballymenaworkhouse.pdf), registration 4130, 13 May 1847: Ellen Glasgow, 22, married and deserted, Presbyterian, Clogh electoral division, Glenleslie; discharged alive 13 July 1847.
- [1851 Kilconway census transcription](https://www.billmacafee.com/census/1851censuskilconway.pdf), Dunaghy, Glenleslie, household 21: Ellen, 26, married daughter; Jane and James Grimes are separately described only as grandchildren of John.
