# Findings: [Nancy Glasgow (Glasgow-1369)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1369)

## Current conclusion

The 1851 mortality schedule directly proves that Nancy was a daughter of John
Glasgow and died of dysentery in 1847 aged 36. It does not name her mother.

## Recommended correction

Age 36 at death in 1847 implies birth about 1810/11, not 1815. Apply that
sourced replacement rather than blanking the existing date. The record does not
supply a birthplace or exact death date.
