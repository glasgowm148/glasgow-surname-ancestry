== Biography ==

'''Mary Jane Glasgow''' was born about 1839 or 1840 in County Antrim, Ireland. An 1847 Ballymena Workhouse return records a nine-year-old Mary Jane Glasgow of Glenleslie in a family group with Mary, Boyd, William James and Margaret Glasgow.<ref name="Workhouse1847">[https://www.billmacafee.com/otherrecords/ballymenaworkhouse.pdf William Macafee, "Admissions to Ballymena Workhouse: 13th May to 8th July 1847"], registrations 4406–4410 (accessed 13 August 2026).</ref>

The surviving 1851 Ballycregagh census directly calls an eleven-year-old Mary Jain Glasgow a daughter of [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]]. She was a weaver of yarn.<ref name="Census1851">[https://durrushistory.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/1851irishcensusreturnscommadelimitedtextfilejul24_071.pdf "1851 Irish Census Returns: comma-delimited transcription"], Dunaghy, Ballycregagh, household 29 (accessed 13 August 2026).</ref>

== Research Notes ==

[[Hamilton-25516|Peggy Hamilton]] married James in 1848, years after Mary Jane's birth, and cannot be her mother. Mary in the 1847 workhouse group is the probable mother.

The workhouse transcription records its Mary Jane as dying on 27 November 1847, but the 1851 return records a same-named daughter alive at approximately the same age. This may be a transcription or return error; death remains unknown until the original records resolve it.

The [http://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Dunaghy/Ballycregagh/29/ National Archives of Ireland household link] cited on the profile is preserved.
