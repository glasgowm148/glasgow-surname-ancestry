# Findings: Glasgow-1378

Last updated: 2026-08-19

## Current conclusion

The 1851 Ballycregagh census directly calls [[Glasgow-1378|Mary Jane Glasgow]],
age 11, a daughter of [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]]. Her biography currently
links the father as [[Glasgow-1377]], which is the wrong profile ID. Peggy
Hamilton married James in 1848, making a stepmother relationship plausible,
but the later marriage does not concretely exclude premarital maternity. Peggy
is therefore retained as an uncertain mother; Mary of the 1847 workhouse group
remains another probable mother candidate.

The workhouse transcription says its nine-year-old Mary Jane died on 27
November 1847, while the 1851 census records an eleven-year-old Mary Jane
living. This conflict must remain explicit; do not add the 1847 death to this
profile without checking the originals.

## Suggested correction

Hamilton]] as uncertain mother; estimate birth about 1839/40; leave death
