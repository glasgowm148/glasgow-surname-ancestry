== Biography ==

'''Robert Glasgow''' was born about 1850 in County Antrim, Ireland. The surviving 1851 census records him at Ballycregagh, Dunaghy, aged eleven months and directly calls him a son of [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]].<ref name="Census1851">[https://durrushistory.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/1851irishcensusreturnscommadelimitedtextfilejul24_071.pdf "1851 Irish Census Returns: comma-delimited transcription"], Dunaghy, Ballycregagh, household 29, Robert Glasgo (accessed 13 August 2026).</ref>

James and [[Hamilton-25516|Peggy Hamilton]] were recorded as having married in 1848. Robert's birth after that marriage makes Peggy his probable mother, although the census relationship is formally stated only to the household head.<ref name="Census1851" />

== Research Notes ==

The earlier estimated birth about 1840 is contradicted by the census age of eleven months.

The [http://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Dunaghy/Ballycregagh/29/ National Archives of Ireland household link] cited on the profile is preserved.
