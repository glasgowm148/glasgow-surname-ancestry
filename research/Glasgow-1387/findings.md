# Findings: William Glasgow (Glasgow-1387), Cloughgaldenagh

Last updated: 2026-08-11

## Current conclusion

William `Glasgow-1387` is the William Glasgow, aged 40, recorded at
Cloughgaldenagh house 1 in the surviving 1851 Dunaghy census. His wife was
Ellin/Ellen, also 40, and the return explicitly records eight children. This is
an existing profiled family, not a new unidentified branch.

The census does not name William's father. His current uncertain placement
under Robert `Glasgow-1367` may remain as a working attachment, but the record
does not prove it and does not connect William to Robert, James or James junior
in the 1825 Dunaghy tithe cluster.
