# Findings: George Glasgow (Glasgow-1400), Glenleslie

Last updated: 2026-08-11

## Current conclusion

George's 1851 household was Glenleslie house 20; John `Glasgow-1022` and Rose
were at house 21. George's wife Sarah Alexander and the Alexander relatives in
John's household place both families in the same Glasgow-Alexander network.

John is therefore the leading candidate for George's father, but the relationship
is not proved. Keep it uncertain or leave George parentless. Do not move George
under Adam `Glasgow-3902` because his fifth child/fourth son was named Adam;
household placement is materially better evidence than that naming pattern.
