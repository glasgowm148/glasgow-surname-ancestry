== Biography ==
Michael Glasgow was named as a skinner at Irvine in a September 1679 list of persons summoned in Kyle and Cunninghame. The list gives his trade and town but no age, relatives or reason for the summons.<ref name="Skinner1679">Findmypast, Scottish Covenanters 1679–1688, [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=GBPR%2FCOVENANT%2F028557&tab=this Michael Glasgow transcript], archival reference JC10/19, September 1679 summons list, describing him as "Skinner yr [Irving]"; captured 3 September 2026. The collection title is a date range; this entry is dated September 1679.</ref> The same uncommon name, Irvine locality and leather trade identify him as the later saddler represented here, although the summons does not itself name his later household.

Michael Glasgow was a saddler in Irvine, Ayrshire, by 1 March 1699. On that date he obtained the grass of the Green and the lime holes or pits for 1699-1700 at £36 Scots. On 5 April 1701 he obtained the grass of the Green and the lime or tanning pits for 1701-1702 at £43 Scots.<ref>Ayrshire Archives, ''Irvine Burgh Catalogue'' (2017), [https://web.archive.org/web/20190919232657/https://ayrshirearchives.files.wordpress.com/2019/04/irvine-burgh-catalogue-2017-watermarked.pdf archived PDF], refs. IB2/14/19 and IB2/14/22, p. 142.</ref>

Michael and [[Garven-52|Elizabeth Garven]] had a daughter, [[Glasgow-1418|Elizabeth Glasgow]], who was baptised at Irvine on 1 January 1700.<ref>FamilySearch, "Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950," database, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XBLZ-SQS Elizabeth Glasgow, 1700], daughter of Michael Glasgow and Elizabeth Garven; citing the Irvine parish register.</ref>

Michael and [[Baird-5776|Catharine Baird]] had at least four children baptised at Irvine: [[Glasgow-1408|Agnes]] on 13 January 1706, [[Glasgow-1409|Elizabeth]] on 12 November 1710, [[Glasgow-1410|Michael]] on 14 October 1712, and [[Glasgow-1407|Janet]] on 30 January 1715.<ref>FamilySearch, "Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950," database: [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XBLZ-S35 Agnes Glasgow, 1706]; [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XBLZ-SQ7 Elizabeth Glasgow, 1710]; [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XTV2-BQB Michael Glasgow, 1712]; and [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XBLH-YPJ Janet Glasgow, 1715]; each citing the Irvine parish register.</ref>

== Research Notes ==
=== Selected uncertain family — 16 October 2026 ===

Select Robert [[Glasgow-1512]] as Michael [[Glasgow-1406]]’s uncertain working father. This activates a parent comparison already recorded in Michael’s profile. Robert is a record-defined Ayr father with Margaret Donald in 1670 and 1671; Michael is recorded as an Irvine skinner in 1679 and is already treated locally as the later saddler/councillor. Robert’s before 1652 birth is an upper-bound estimate from his later children, not evidence that he was born in 1652. Michael’s before 1679 boundary likewise does not date his birth. An older Robert and a Michael born well before the summons could fit a father/son generation; their ages are not recorded. The regional family setting and existing candidate justify a very low-confidence working choice. No record names Michael’s father, and no shared occupation, holding, witness or household identifies this Robert as his parent.

John [[Glasgow-1604]] is another viable older father generation, rather than a disproved alternative. Robert’s own attachment to that John is unproved, and taking John’s approximate 1626 date narrowly could press the generation spacing if Robert had Michael early. Those older dates and attachments remain hypotheses; no date is adjusted to repair the pedigree. Another Ayrshire father, or a sibling/collateral relationship to Robert, remains possible. Margaret [[Donald-1722]] is named in Robert’s 1670–1671 children’s records, not in a record naming Michael’s mother. She is not selected as Michael’s mother. The existing 1679 skinner/later saddler/councillor identification is inherited from the completed merge, not newly proved; keep the 1679 summons,1686/1702 civic records and 1699/1701 leather-work references distinct in their evidence roles. Elizabeth Garven and Catharine Baird remain the recorded mothers in Michael’s later child group. The 1700 Elizabeth entry has an assumed year and no proved 1 January birthday. No mother, marriage or death date is newly inferred for Michael, and the 1700–1715 household chronology does not itself identify his parents. The earlier proof-level hold on Robert is retained as history, not treated as a disproof of this qualified local model. The published Irvine burgh muniments name a Michael Glasgow among the town councillors on 16 April 1686.<ref>''Muniments of the Royal Burgh of Irvine'', vol. II (Edinburgh: David Douglas for the Ayrshire and Galloway Archaeological Association, 1891), [https://electricscotland.com/history/gazetteer/MunimentsoftheroyalburghofIrvine02.pdf#page=130 p. 103], act no. 60, 16 April 1686.</ref> A man of the same name was still a councillor in September 1702 and was chosen to the new council "for Trades."<ref>''Muniments of the Royal Burgh of Irvine'', vol. II (1891), [https://electricscotland.com/history/gazetteer/MunimentsoftheroyalburghofIrvine02.pdf#page=143 p. 116] and [https://electricscotland.com/history/gazetteer/MunimentsoftheroyalburghofIrvine02.pdf#page=150 p. 123], 17 and 25 September 1702.</ref>

The uncommon name, shared Irvine location and 1702 trades classification make the councillor a strong candidate to be the saddler, but the council entries do not state his occupation. The 1679 skinner is treated as the same adult on the combined locality and trade evidence. His birth is placed before September 1679 from the adult trade entry; no exact birth or baptism has been established.

The former skinner profile Glasgow-4141 was merged into this profile on 27 September 2026. Its 1679 source gives neither age nor family; the same-person identification rests on the uncommon name, continuous Irvine setting and related leather trades. The burgh catalogue and councillor records provide the later adult sequence; inspect the original summons and guild records for a direct family or occupation bridge.

No reviewed source identifies Michael's parents. The suggestion that he was a son of [[Glasgow-1512|Robert Glasgow]] rests on repeated given names and estimated dates, not a relational record or a conflict-free proof argument. Robert should not be attached as Michael's father without further evidence.
