## Prior assessment — superseded by merge

No reviewed source identifies either parent of Michael Glasgow. Robert Glasgow
(Glasgow-1512) is an unsupported WikiTree suggestion and should not be attached
as his father.

An Irvine councillor named Michael Glasgow is documented on 16 April 1686 and
again in September 1702. The later entry places him among the trades councillors.
The uncommon name, shared place, occupation class, and continuity make the
councillor a strong candidate to be the saddler, but the civic records do not
state his occupation. The profile's "before 1680" date is compatible with this
evidence. This is strong indirect identity evidence, not a parent record.

The highest-yield unreviewed originals are Irvine's Dean of Guild register
(Gr1/7/1), council indexes and minutes (Gr1/5/1, Gr1/1/5-6; NRS B37/12/4-5),
and Incorporated Trades minute book (Gr1/23/3). A burgess, apprentice, or craft
admission could name Michael's father or master.

[Glasgow-4141](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4141) represents a Michael Glasgow described as a skinner at Irvine in a September 1679 summons list. The uncommon name, location and related leather trade make him a plausible earlier identity for this saddler. The [Findmypast transcript](https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=GBPR%2FCOVENANT%2F028557&tab=this) gives no age, family or later identifier, so this possible match remains unmerged.

## Source findings — Irvine saddler roups, 1699 and 1701, 7 October 2026

The Irvine burgh catalogue lists two roups (auction lettings) to Michael Glasgow, saddler of Irvine, among the Town Treasurer's articles of roup: on 1 March 1699, the grass of the Green and the lime holes or pits for 1699–1700 at £36 Scots (IB2/14/19); and on 5 April 1701, the grass of the Green and the lime or "tann" pits for 1701–1702 at £43 Scots (IB2/14/22). Ayrshire Archives, *Irvine Burgh Catalogue* (2017), p. 142; the entries were read in the recovered local copy `sources/irvine_burgh_catalogue_2017_wayback_recovered.pdf` on 7 October 2026.

These are the records behind the statement that Michael was a saddler from 1699. Seen as catalogue descriptions; the original roup papers have not been inspected. They name no relatives. The tanning pits fit a leather trade, which is consistent with the 1679 Irvine skinner merged into this profile, but they do not prove that identity.

Recorded in `research/Glasgow-1406/Glasgow-1406.md` (20 August 2026); carried into this file on 7 October 2026.
