## Current conclusion

No reviewed source identifies either parent of Michael Glasgow. Robert Glasgow
(Glasgow-1512) is an unsupported WikiTree suggestion and should not be attached
as his father.

An Irvine councillor named Michael Glasgow is documented on 16 April 1686 and
again in September 1702. The later entry places him among the trades councillors.
The uncommon name, shared place, occupation class, and continuity make the
councillor a strong candidate to be the saddler, but the civic records do not
state his occupation. The profile's "before 1680" date is compatible with this
evidence. This is strong indirect identity evidence, not a parent record.

The highest-yield unreviewed originals are Irvine's Dean of Guild register
(Gr1/7/1), council indexes and minutes (Gr1/5/1, Gr1/1/5-6; NRS B37/12/4-5),
and Incorporated Trades minute book (Gr1/23/3). A burgess, apprentice, or craft
admission could name Michael's father or master.
