# Findings: [Michael Glasgow (Glasgow-1416)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1416)

Last updated: 2026-09-04

## Current conclusion

The Findmypast parish transcript records Michael Glasgow's birth at Irvine on
19 November 1741 and baptism there on 22 November. It names his father as
Michael Glasgow, matching the structured father
[Michael Glasgow (Glasgow-1410)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1410).
The transcript does not name a mother and therefore neither proves nor
disproves the existing attachment to
[Mary Crauford (Crauford-31)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Crauford-31).

The live structured birth date is the baptism date, and the biography links
the father to the different profile Glasgow-1412. Both are substantive errors:
the birth date should be 19 November and the biography should link the already
attached Glasgow-1410.

The 1789 Old Bailey testimony by a witness named Michael Glasgow remains a
possible later-life record. Nothing in the cited extract supplies enough
identity detail to prove that the witness was this Irvine-born Michael.
