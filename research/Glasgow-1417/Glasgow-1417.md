== Biography ==

'''Katharine Glasgow''', also indexed as Katherine or Catherine, was born before her baptism at Irvine, Ayrshire, Scotland, in 1749. Two derivative transcriptions disagree about the month. The FamilySearch record cited on this profile gives a christening date of 3 August,<ref name="FamilySearchEvent">"Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950," database, FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XBLC-SY9 Katharine Glasgow, 1749], citing the Irvine parish register.</ref> while a Findmypast transcript gives 3 September and names her father as Michael Glasgow.<ref name="FMP">"Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929," transcript, Findmypast ([https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3575702&tab=this record SCOT/OPR/BAP/3575702] : captured 3 September 2026), Katherine Glasgow, baptised 3 September 1749 at Irvine, father Michael Glasgow; citing OPR 595/1, item 1, p. 66.</ref>

Her attached parents are [[Glasgow-1410|Michael Glasgow]] and [[Crauford-31|Mary Crauford]]. The Findmypast transcript supports the father's given name but does not name the mother.<ref name="FMP" />

Katharine is reported to have died on 26 February 1825 and to have been buried at Irvine. She is attached as the wife of [[Findlay-2535|John Findlay]]. These later-life claims remain dependent on the contributed FamilySearch tree and require independent records.<ref name="FamilyTree">"Family Tree," database, FamilySearch, [https://beta.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/4:1:LVZJ-W92 entry for Katharine Glasgow], modified 20 April 2022; contributed by various users.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Baptism date ===

The 3 August and 3 September dates should be preserved as a transcription conflict until the original Irvine OPR image for page 66 is inspected. The same uncommon child's name, Irvine parish, year and father Michael make two separate daughters one month apart substantially less likely than a month-reading or indexing error. Do not create a second Katherine Glasgow profile from the Findmypast entry.

Because both dates are baptism or christening dates, neither should be presented as an exact birth date. Retain an uncertain birth before the baptism and do not choose between the two months without the register image.

=== Parents ===

The prose link to [[Glasgow-1412|Michael Glasgow]] does not match Katharine's structured father, [[Glasgow-1410|Michael Glasgow]]. The latter is therefore the father linked in this account. The new transcript identifies only "Michael Glasgow" and does not by itself distinguish his WikiTree profile or prove [[Crauford-31|Mary Crauford]] as the mother, so the existing structured relationships and their present confidence should be preserved pending inspection of the original entry.
