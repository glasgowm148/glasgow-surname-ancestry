== Biography ==

{{One Name Study|name=Glasgow}}

'''Samuel John Glasgow''' was born before about 1845 in County Antrim, Ireland.

He married Margaret Calagher on 21 August 1866 at Ballymoney. The original
register describes Samuel as a full-age bachelor and labourer and names his
father as '''Andrew Glasgow, labourer'''. Margaret was of full age and unmarried;
her father was James Calagher, labourer.<ref name="Marriage1866">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1866/11540/8244496.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Samuel John Glasgow and Margaret Calagher, 21 August 1866.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

[[Glasgow-1050|Andrew Glasgow]] is Samuel's strongest identified father
candidate. Andrew was a Glenleslie linen weaver born about 1818 and died in
1848.<ref name="Census1851">[https://durrushistory.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/1851irishcensusreturnscommadelimitedtextfilejul24_071.pdf ''1851 Irish Census Returns: detailed comma-delimited transcription''], Glenleslie household 21.</ref> The chronology and locality fit, but Samuel's marriage does not state Andrew's residence and no baptism has been found. Their relationship therefore remains uncertain.

The previously cited 1870 civil birth is not Samuel's marriage record and should
not be used as proof of his parentage. It is retained among the profile's earlier
research sources as the FamilySearch record [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:FG82-Q36 FG82-Q36] and its [https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/view/?record_id=f66be225e5-6432791 IrishGenealogy.ie result page], while the original marriage above provides the controlling evidence.
