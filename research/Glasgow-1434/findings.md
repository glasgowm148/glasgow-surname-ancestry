# Findings: [John Glasgow (Glasgow-1434), Kilwinning/Stevenston](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1434)

## Conclusion

John's 1679 Kilwinning baptism to John Glasgow and Barbara Alason is direct.
His later identity as Margaret Cowan's husband and father at Stevenston is
plausible but already acknowledged as unproved.

[Glasgow-3018](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3018) should not be
dismissed solely because its reconstructed family contains another son John.
One man could theoretically have had a 1693 child with Agnes Martine and later
children with Margaret Cowan, although the 1679 child would have been only
about fourteen in 1693. The later skipper/bailie identity on Glasgow-3018 is
also reconstructed.

## Recommended update

- Keep Glasgow-3018 separate but list it as an open identity comparison.
- Search Irvine burgh records for age, kin, residence or property continuity
  with the Stevenston/Pipers-Heugh John.
- Do not merge without resolving Agnes Martine, the 1693 child and the
  skipper/bailie material.
