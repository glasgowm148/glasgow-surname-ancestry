# Findings: [Isobell Glasgow (Glasgow-1443)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1443)

## Current conclusion

The live profile represents an Isobell Glasgow born before 10 August 1716 at
Stitchel, Roxburghshire, with parents Patrick Glasgow and Helen Fairburn. A
separate Scotland's People index entry records Isobell Glasgow baptised at
Glencorse on 26 December 1714 to Robert Glasgow and Margaret Davidson.
FamilySearch `FMMM-S4T` and `FMMM-S4Y` were inspected on 2 September 2026 and
confirm the Stitchel event and Patrick/Helen parentage. The two records identify
separate people; no merge or correction is justified.
