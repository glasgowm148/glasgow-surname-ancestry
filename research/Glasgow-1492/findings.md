# Findings: [Samuel Glasgow (Glasgow-1492), born about 1726, Moneymore-labelled](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1492)

## Current conclusion

The original 1766 Derryloran return proves that a **Samuel Glasco was a
householder at Gorteen**. It does not give his age or parents, so it cannot by
itself identify him as this profile or as a son of either currently proposed
James Glasgow. The Samuel at Whitehill belongs to the separate adjacent parish
of Kildress and must not be used as a second Derryloran occurrence.

| [Virtual Record Treasury `Glasco` result](../../surname-research/sources/virtual_treasury_glasco_exact_2026-07-17.json) | Preserves the original Derryloran townland sequence and image metadata. | Resolves the exact locality of the Samuel cited on the live profile. |

## Profile consequence

Retain the Gorteen occurrence as a possible match, explicitly labelled
unidentified. Do not use it to make the current father attachment certain and
remove any wording that combines Gorteen and Whitehill as one man's movements.
