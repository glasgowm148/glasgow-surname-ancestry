# Findings: [Samuel Glasgow (Glasgow-1492), born about 1726, Moneymore-labelled](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1492)

## Current conclusion

The original 1766 Derryloran return proves that a **Samuel Glasco was a
householder at Gorteen**. It does not give his age or parents, so it cannot by
itself identify him as this profile or as a son of either currently proposed
James Glasgow. The Samuel at Whitehill belongs to the separate adjacent parish
of Kildress and must not be used as a second Derryloran occurrence.
