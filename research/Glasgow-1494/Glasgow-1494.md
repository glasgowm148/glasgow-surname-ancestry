== Biography ==
'''Jonet Glasgow''' was born before 1610, likely around 1600, in Ayrshire, Scotland. She lived in the parish of Stevenston, where she was married to George Logane.

The primary record of her life is her testament, which confirms she died in or before September 1628. Her Testament Dative and Inventory was recorded in the Glasgow Commissary Court on 2 September 1628, just six months after that of her sister, Agnes.<ref name="Testament1628">''The Commissariot Record of Glasgow, Register of Testaments, 1547-1800'', Vol. 7, ed. by Francis J. Grant (Edinburgh: Scottish Record Society, 1901), citing the testament of Jonet Glasgw, spouse to George Logane in Steinstoun. National Records of Scotland, Reference CC9/7/22.</ref>

The term "Testament Dative" signifies that Jonet died without a formal will, and the court appointed an executor to settle her estate.

=== A Tale of Two Sisters ===
The testament records from 1628 paint a poignant picture of two sisters, Jonet and [[Glasgow-3178|Agnes Glasgow]]. Both were married to Logan men in the small parish of Stevenston, and tragically, both died in the same year.

Agnes's testament, as the spouse of Johnne Logane, was recorded in March 1628. Jonet's testament followed in September. This remarkable coincidence strongly suggests the two sisters married two brothers, George and Johnne Logane, and may have succumbed to the same illness or event.

== Research Notes ==

The currently attached father [[Glasgow-1557|Edward Glasgow]] is marked uncertain. The cited 1628 testament index identifies Jonet’s spouse but supplies no father; Edward’s attachment is a working hypothesis.

* The central fact of Jonet's life is her death in 1628. The birth year of 1610 found on some trees is an estimate and is likely incorrect, as she was married and established enough to have an estate by this time. A birth year closer to 1600 is more probable.
* The primary research goal is to find the parents of Jonet and Agnes Glasgow. A search of baptismal records in the Stevenston parish registers (or nearby parishes like Kilwinning or Irvine) from roughly 1590-1610 is the most promising next step.
* Locating the marriage records for both couples (Jonet to George Logan, and Agnes to Johnne Logan) prior to 1628 would further solidify the family structure.
