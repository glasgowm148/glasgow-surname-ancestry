== Biography ==

'''Jonet Weir''', whose forename is read provisionally and may be modernised as
Janet, was one of three Weirs appointed executors-dative in the testament of
[[Glasgow-1495|Katherine Glasgow]], wife of [[Weir-4172|Thomas Weir]] in
Stevenston parish. Katherine died in January 1603. When the Glasgow
Commissary Court confirmed the estate in May 1605, it described the executors
collectively as minors and Thomas acted in their names.<ref name="KatherineTestament">National Records of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary
Court, testament dative and inventory of Katherine Glasgow, CC9/7/4, images
6–7; original register inspected through the
[https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/search-records/legal-records/wills
Scotland's People wills search]; working transcription prepared September
2026.</ref>

Jonet's surname, minority, appointment in Katherine's estate and Thomas's
representation of her make her very probably the couple's child. The
surviving wording does not expressly call her their daughter, so both parent
links should initially be marked uncertain.<ref name="KatherineTestament" />

== Research Notes ==

The third executor's forename is less secure than William and James. Retain
'''Jonet''' as a provisional manuscript reading and '''Janet''' only as its
modern form until the original clause is independently checked.

The record gives no exact age or birthplace. Stevenston is an uncertain
birthplace inferred from Katherine and Thomas's documented household there,
not a stated birth location. The suggested birth range combines the ordinary
legal implication of minority in May 1605 with Katherine's January 1603 death;
it should not be treated as a recorded date.

The manuscript confirmation day is not fully secure: it appears to read
'''[26?] May 1605''', whereas the printed testament calendar and Findmypast
index give '''9 May 1605'''. Only May 1605 should be treated as settled. Neither
date is a birth, death or marriage date for Jonet.<ref name="Grant">Francis J.
Grant, ed., ''The Commissariot Record of Glasgow: Register of Testaments,
1547–1800'' (Edinburgh, 1901),
[https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/page/196/mode/1up
p. 196], Katherine Glasgow entry.</ref><ref name="FMP">Findmypast,
[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FWILLS%2F057453&tab=this
Scotland, Will and Testament Index 1481–1807], Katherine Glasgow,
SCOT/WILLS/057453.</ref>
