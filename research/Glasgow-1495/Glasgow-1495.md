== Biography ==

'''Katherine Glasgow''' was the wife of [[Weir-4172|Thomas Weir]] in the
parish of Stevenston, Ayrshire. The original Glasgow Commissary Court testament
states that she died in January 1603.<ref name="KatherineWill">National Records
of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary Court, testament dative and inventory of
Katherine Glasgow, CC9/7/4, images 6–7; original register inspected through
[https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/search-records/legal-records/wills Scotland's
People] and working transcription supplied 8 September 2026.</ref>

Her testament appointed William, James and probably Jonet Weir as three minor
executors, with Thomas acting in their names.<ref name="KatherineWill" /> Their
shared surname, minority and Thomas's representation of them make them very
probably Katherine and Thomas's children, although the surviving wording does
not explicitly call them son or daughter.

The date of confirmation is not completely settled. The manuscript appears to
read '''[26?] May 1605''', while both Grant's printed calendar and the
Findmypast transcript give '''9 May 1605'''.<ref name="KatherineWill" /><ref name="Grant">Francis J. Grant, ed., ''The Commissariot Record of Glasgow:
Register of Testaments, 1547–1800'' (Edinburgh, 1901), [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/page/196/mode/1up
p. 196], entry for Katherine Glasgow.</ref><ref name="FMP">Findmypast,
[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FWILLS%2F057453&tab=this
Scotland, Will and Testament Index 1481–1807], Katherine Glasgow,
SCOT/WILLS/057453, citing the Glasgow Commissary Court register.</ref> May 1605
is therefore secure, but the day should remain unresolved.

== Research Notes ==

Katherine's birth date is estimated. Stevenston is her documented parish, not
an explicitly stated place of birth or death.

The attached [[Glasgow-3921|James Glasgow younger of Corsankell]] remains an
uncertain father. He is a chronologically and geographically plausible
candidate, but Katherine's testament names no parent. The attachment should
not be removed without a positively evidenced replacement.

[[Glasgow-3179|Geillis Glasgow]] died in the same month in neighbouring
Ardrossan parish, and her estate owed James Glasgow in Stevenston. This is
useful evidence of a local Glasgow network, but it does not prove that Geillis
and Katherine were sisters or identify James's relationship to either woman.

The testament begins at the bottom of image 6; most of that image belongs to
another estate. It names James, Earl of Glencairn, and Mr Alexander Campbell,
minister at Stevenston. Other qualified readings include [Bryce?] Blair of
[Lochwood?], Archibald [Muir?], John Weir in an unresolved ''Nether …''
locality, servant John [Wicht/Wight?], and [Jean Doun?], another servant.<ref
name="KatherineWill" />

A full working transcription, modern-English rendering and record-boundary
analysis is maintained at [[Space:Kathereine Glasgow: Will and testament
translation]].

The May 1605 date is the testament-confirmation date, not a marriage date. The
three minor Weir executors were each checked against the four local One Tree
exports and fully paged live WikiTree searches on 8 September 2026. No
compatible profile was found. Separate profiles may therefore be created for
William, James and provisionally named Jonet, with both parent links and their
inferred Stevenston birthplaces marked uncertain.
