== Biography ==

'''Katherine Glasgow''' was the wife of [[Weir-4172|Thomas Weir]] in the
parish of Stevenston, Ayrshire. Her testament states that she died in January
1603; it was confirmed on 9 May 1605.<ref name="KatherineWill">National Records
of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary Court, testament of Katherine Glasgow,
CC9/7/4, images 6–7; working transcription supplied 6 September 2026.</ref>

Thomas acted in the names of William, James and Jonet Weir in the executor
appointment.<ref name="KatherineWill" />

== Research Notes ==

The executor passage does not securely call William, James and Jonet the
couple's lawful children. Treat them as possible children until another source
states the relationship.

The attached [[Glasgow-3921|James Glasgow younger of Corsankell]] is only an
uncertain father candidate; the testament names no parent. [[Glasgow-3179|Geillis
Glasgow]] died in the same month in neighbouring Ardrossan parish, and her
estate owed James Glasgow in Stevenston. This supports a local kin-network
hypothesis, not sisterhood or fatherhood.

The testament begins at the bottom of image 6; most of that image belongs to
another estate. It names James, Earl of Glencairn, and provisionally Mr
Alexander Cunynghame, minister at Stevenston. Other readings include Hew
Blair; Archibald, surname unresolved (`Moir` only a candidate); John, possibly
Weir; John Wright; and Jonet, possibly Doun. John Wright and the latter Jonet
were servants owed wages. `Archibald Wood` and a specific territorial identity
for Hew Blair are not established.<ref name="KatherineWill" />

The printed calendar gives the confirmation date and spouse/locality.<ref>Francis
J. Grant, ed., ''The Commissariot Record of Glasgow: Register of Testaments,
1547–1800'' (1901),
[https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/page/196/mode/1up p.
196].</ref>
