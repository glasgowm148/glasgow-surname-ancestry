# Findings: [Katherine Glasgow (Glasgow-1495), born about 1565, Scotland](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1495)

## Current conclusion

The Glasgow Commissary testament calendar directly identifies Katherine as the
spouse of **Thomas Weir of the parish of Stevenston** and dates her testament to
9 May 1605. She therefore died before that date. The existing
[Thomas Weir profile (Weir-4172)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Weir-4172)
has now been repurposed for this record-defined Stevenston husband: its former
1590 Aberdeen birth was removed and the live page now gives birth before 1580.
Retain that profile as Katherine's spouse and do not restore the unrelated
Aberdeen identity. Its live family header currently misreads 9 May 1605 as the
marriage date and `Stevenson, Lanarkshire` as the place; the source instead
records Katherine's testament at Stevenston parish, Ayrshire.

The attached uncertain father,
[James Glasgow younger (Glasgow-3921), born before 1542, Corsankell](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3921),
is chronologically and geographically plausible but remains unproved. The
testament calendar names no parent.

The family naming pattern does not strengthen that attachment. Katherine's
lawful children were William, Thomas and Jonet Weir. None repeats James, and no
known child repeats Margaret, the name of the wife currently associated with
the proposed father. Scottish naming patterns were never mandatory and the
children's order is not fully known, so this is not disproof. It is negative
weight against upgrading James younger from possible to probable on locality
alone.

## Paste-ready biography

Katherine Glasgow lived in the parish of Stevenston, Ayrshire, and was married
to Thomas Weir. Her testament was confirmed in the Glasgow Commissary Court on
9 May 1605, so she died before that date.<ref>Francis J. Grant, ed., ''The
Commissariot Record of Glasgow: Register of Testaments, 1547-1800'' (Edinburgh,
1901), [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/page/196/mode/1up
p. 196], entry for Katherine Glasgow; underlying register NRS CC9/7/4.</ref>

The testament calendar does not name Katherine's parents or children. James
Glasgow younger of Corsankell is retained only as an uncertain father because
the generation and Stevenston locality fit; no reviewed record states that
relationship. Weir-4172 now represents this Stevenston husband. Its former
identification as a Thomas born at Aberdeen in 1590 was incompatible with the
record and should not be restored.
