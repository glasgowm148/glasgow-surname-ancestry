```text
== Genealogical Notes ==

=== Identity and parentage ===

The prisoner list names John Glasgow and William Glasgow under Cavers parish and marks both as survivors of the December 1679 wreck.<ref name="CloudCavers">John H. Thomson, ed., ''A Cloud of Witnesses'', appendix, prisoner list under “Parish of Cavers”; the editor explains that survivors are marked with a star, [https://media.sabda.org/alkitab-8/LIBRARY/THO_CLWT.PDF digital copy].</ref> It does not give John's age, birthplace, parents, exact residence, relationship to William, or later destination.

No reviewed record identifies [[Glasgow-3331|John Glasgow of Quothquan]] as this John's father. The Quothquan man belongs to a documented Lanarkshire group, while this profile is known only from a Cavers-parish prisoner entry; no relational or two-location record joins them. The father relationship should remain unconnected and John's parents remain unknown.

The same prisoner list does not identify the other prisoner William Glasgow as [[Glasgow-1605]], the child baptised at Polwarth in 1658. That link should remain an unproved Research Note rather than a biography fact.
```
