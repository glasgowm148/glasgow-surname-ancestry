== Biography ==

'''Hugh Glasgow''' has been represented with estimated dates of about 1700 and
1726, but no identified record supports him as a separate historical person.
The only cited note says that a source would later be added.<ref name="OriginalNote">WikiTree source note: “Source will be added by [[Glasgow-933|M Glasgow]] by 6 Jan 2021.” The note identifies no record.</ref>

Research has established that the 1726 date belongs to [[Glasgow-1081|James
Glasgow]], not to Hugh.<ref name="PorterSession" />

== Research Notes ==

=== The 1726 date ===

Classon Porter's published transcription of the Larne and Kilwaughter session
list records:

* James Glasgow — deceased in 1726; and
* Hugh Glasgow — dead on 8 July 1767.<ref name="PorterSession">Classon Porter,
“Congregational Memoirs—Larne and Kilwaughter,” ''The Christian Unitarian'',
vol. 3 (1864), p. 197, [https://archive.org/stream/christianunitari0003unse/christianunitari0003unse_djvu.txt Internet Archive transcription].</ref>

The estimated death in 1726 has therefore been removed. No independent
source has been found for its estimated birth about 1700.

=== Probable duplicate ===

[[Glasgow-1503|Hugh Glasgow]] is independently documented at Larne by a 1736
lease, the congregational session list and a family monument. The session source
gives his death as 8 July 1767.<ref name="PorterSession" /> No separate record
distinguishes the Hugh represented here from that documented man.

The evidence therefore indicates that this was the same man as
[[Glasgow-1503|Hugh Glasgow]]. The two WikiTree profiles should be merged, using
the sourced profile for the surviving identity. The merge does not establish
Hugh's father: [[Glasgow-1081|James Glasgow]] and
[[Glasgow-1502|Robert Glasgow]] remain unproved parentage hypotheses.
