## Current conclusion

Ann was born 15 November 1733 to Robert Glasgow and Rachel Wilson according to
the congregation-register chronology. She was sister of James Glasgow-1144 and
wife of Hugh Shannon. The 1817 complaint says Ann predeceased Archibald Wilson, but
Robert Glasgow-1983's sworn answer expressly corrects this and says she survived
Archibald. The sworn answer is preferable for that chronology.

Her relationship to mother Rachel and marriage to Hugh Shannon are stated
directly in the equity litigation. Robert's paternity is strongly supported by
the reported baptism, but the original register has not yet been inspected.
