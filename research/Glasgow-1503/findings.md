# Findings: Hugh Glasgow (Glasgow-1503), Larne

Last updated: 2026-08-13

## Current conclusion

Hugh died **8 July 1767 aged 62**, placing his birth between 9 July 1704 and
8 July 1705. His wife was **Ann**, who died 8 June 1786 aged 75. The Larne
memorial does not support Jane as a second wife: one Jane was a deceased child,
and the later Jane was wife of Hugh and Ann's son James.

The memorial commemorates eleven children of Hugh and separately adds
[James Glasgow (Glasgow-3991)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3991) as
`their son`, making at least twelve children the most natural reading. Only Jane,
Hugh and the adult James are named in the exposed inscription. The derivative
register chronology adds Jean, Rose, Mary and a James in 1731-1737, but must be
reconciled with the monument son James, born about 1747/48. The two James
clusters must remain separate: the monument son was only twelve or thirteen in
1761 and cannot have been the ropemaker who had already established a ropewalk.

Hugh was definitely a Presbyterian ruling elder, although no inspected record
supports the exact claim that he was admitted in 1741. No record names his
parents. His placement under Robert-1502 remains unproved; James, John and Robert
of the older Kilwaughter generation are all possible.

[Hugh Glasgow (Glasgow-1499)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1499) is
a probable duplicate rather than a separately documented man. That profile's
only distinguishing event is a supposed death in 1726, but the session-list
transcription assigns 1726 to James Glasgow and records this Hugh's death as
8 July 1767. No independent source has been found for another Hugh born about
1700. A merge should preserve this profile's sourced identity and keep its
parentage uncertain; the unsupported James-1081 father should not displace the
current uncertain Robert-1502 hypothesis during the merge.

There is now one exact-age parentage candidate which must be kept separate from
that reconstruction. Dublin's official church-record database transcribes the
baptism of **Hugh Glasroe**, son of **Wm Glasroe** and **Jone**, at St Catherine's
Church of Ireland on 23 October 1704. That date falls exactly within Hugh's
memorial-derived birth range. The record supplies no address or sponsors, and no
record yet carries this child from Dublin to Larne. It is therefore a serious
candidate, not an identification. If the original surname proves to be Glasgow
and the child proves to be this Hugh, it would point to William rather than the
currently attached Robert as his father.

A newly identified PRONI title chain is the best prospect for extending this
household another generation. A 1736 lease put Larne tenements jointly in the
names of William and Hugh Glasgow; endorsements inserted new lives in 1751 and
1767; and the same 1736 lease was renewed to Elizabeth Glasgow, widow of Larne,
in 1824. If she is Elizabeth, widow of Hugh's grandson William Glasgow-3324, the
original renewal may recite the missing succession. The catalogue descriptions
do not identify her husband, so that link is not yet proved.

## Best next proof targets

- PRONI `MIC/1B/6`, exact entries for all five children and any marriages.
- Inspect Herbert Wood's 1908 St Catherine register, Book 1 p. 139 entry 2577,
  for the exact handwritten/printed surname; then search the same household and
  any occupation, residence or migration clue before considering a merge.
- Inspect the 1736/1757 leases, especially the named lives in the 1751 and 1767
  endorsements, and the 1824 renewal's recital of title.
- Compare the 1824 widow Elizabeth with Elizabeth, wife of William Glasgow-3324,
  using William's surviving 1815 will (`D300/1/5/49`).
- St Cedma inscriptions for Hugh and his son Hugh.
- Use exact birth range **9 July 1704-8 July 1705** and wife Ann's range
  **9 June 1710-8 June 1711** only as calculations from age at death.
- Search `MIC/1B/6` for Hugh's marriage to Ann, all baptisms, his admission to
  session and mortcloth/poor-account entries for the eleven deceased children.
- Locate Donna Jean Glasgow or the custodian of the original 2006 Webshots album
  `558514063awmMGT`; request the full-resolution church-register photographs and
  their captions/file metadata.
