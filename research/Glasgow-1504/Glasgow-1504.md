== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' was probably born about 1707 in County Antrim, Ireland. His exact birth date and parents have not been proved.

James married '''[[Murray-20395|Sarah or Sara Murray]]''' in the Larne and Kilwaughter Presbyterian congregation in August 1727. A derivative chronology gives 5 August, while a related narrative gives 3 August.<ref name="Chronology">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184247/http://lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Kilwaughter/Timeline.html "Chronology of the Glasgow Family in Northern Ireland in Historical Context"], entries for 1727–1740.</ref><ref name="Narrative">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124183914id_/http://lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Kilwaughter/TheirStory.html "Glasgows from Kilwaughter Parish and Larne"], marriage discussion.</ref> The year 1727 is consistent with the first recorded child in 1728; the original register should be consulted for the exact day.

The same register chronology places four children in their household:<ref name="Chronology" />

* '''[[Glasgow-3325|Jean Glasgow]]''', recorded in 1728;
* '''[[Glasgow-3326|William Glasgow]]''', recorded in 1730;
* '''[[Glasgow-3327|Helen or Hellen Glasgow]]''', recorded in 1732; and
* '''[[Glasgow-3328|Christian Glasgow]]''', recorded in 1740.

The public Emerald Ancestors index independently lists the 1728 Jean, 1730 William and 1732 Helen entries in County Antrim, although its public results do not expose the parents or full register details.<ref>[https://www.emeraldancestors.com/northern-ireland-ancestry-search-birth-list?County=Antrim&Forenames=&Surname=Gillis&brideforenames=&bridesurname=&civildistrict=&searchtype= Emerald Ancestors, County Antrim birth index], entries for Jean, William and Helen Glasgow.</ref>

James may have been the James Glasgowe shown at Altilevelly in Raloo parish in 1747. Presbyterians from Raloo attended the Larne congregation, but the map supplies no age or family details and the identification remains uncertain.<ref name="Chronology" />

One of the two men named James Glasgow among the Larne-area petitioners published on 7 April 1772 may also have been this James. The newspaper extract does not distinguish the same-name men.<ref>[https://www.kinsleuth.com/belnews/1770-1779.htm Blair extracts from the ''Belfast News-Letter'', 1770–1779], 7 April 1772, p. 3, Larne, Kilwaughter and Raloo petition.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

No inspected record identifies James's parents. His attachment to [[Glasgow-1502|Robert Glasgow]] remains uncertain.

The source promised by [[Glasgow-933|M Glasgow]] on 6 January 2021 was not subsequently identified; the register-derived sources above now provide the evidence for James's known household.

No later spouse or child has been proved for Jean, Helen or Christian. William is the only child with a credible later Larne trail, but his possible 1772 petition and 1783 probate occurrences remain same-name identifications rather than relationship records.

The original Larne and Kilwaughter congregation collection survives at PRONI under reference `MIC/1B/6`. It should be checked for the exact marriage, the four child entries and later marriages before married surnames or descendants are assigned.
