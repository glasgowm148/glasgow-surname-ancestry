## Current conclusion

Current father assessment (22 October 2026): **34% (possible)** for [Glasgow-1502](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1502). Possible 34%; a compatible local older generation, without a named-son or specific succession clause. This scoped assessment supersedes earlier grades for this parent pair; other identity questions remain as qualified below.

James and Sarah/Sara Murray had four children recorded in the derivative
Larne/Kilwaughter register chronology: Jean (1728), William (1730),
Helen/Hellen (1732) and Christian (1740). No public record inspected in this
pass identifies a spouse or child of Jean, Helen or Christian.

William is the only child with a credible later trail. The chronology identifies
him as the probable William Glasgow who signed the Larne petition in 1772. A
probate index then names William Glasgow of Larne in 1783, but it does not give
an age or relatives and an older William was also living locally. The chronology
also reports that James Glasgow, born about 1775, shared a St Cedma's stone with
William born 1730. That is a genuine descendant lead, but the same page elsewhere
assigns the stone to William born about 1708. Until the inscription is recovered,
it does not prove whether James was William's son, grandson or another relative.

The four child profiles are therefore not empty because the relationships are
weak; they are dead ends because the surviving public transcription stops at
their births. The original register, the full 1783 probate material and the
unrecovered memorial are the records capable of extending the line.

## Identity assessment — "James Glasgow junior" in the 1738/9 lease, 6 October 2026

"Jas Glasgow Junr" is one of the three lives in William Agnew's lease of Ballykeel to Robert Glasgow, dated 13 February 1738 (1738/9 by modern reckoning; Registry of Deeds memorial 74015, re-read on the saved register images). "Junior" most naturally distinguishes him from another James Glasgow known to the parties, usually a living one, but it does not prove one and it states no relationship. The memorial names no other James.

The officer James Glasgow of Kilwaughter was dead by then ("deceased 1726"; will indexed 1727). The attested candidates for an older James alive in 1738-44 are James Glasgow-1504 (married 1727, children 1728-1740 in the derivative register chronology, residence unrecorded) and James Glasgow of Larne, who took a townparks lease on 30 August 1744 (PRONI D2977/3A/4/81/116, known from a catalogue line only); they may be one man. The word is not evidence of kinship between James-1504 and Robert Glasgow-1143.
