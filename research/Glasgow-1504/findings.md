# Findings: [James Glasgow (Glasgow-1504), Kilwaughter/Larne](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1504)

Last updated: 2026-08-12

## Current conclusion

James and Sarah/Sara Murray had four children recorded in the derivative
Larne/Kilwaughter register chronology: Jean (1728), William (1730),
Helen/Hellen (1732) and Christian (1740). No public record inspected in this
pass identifies a spouse or child of Jean, Helen or Christian.

William is the only child with a credible later trail. The chronology identifies
him as the probable William Glasgow who signed the Larne petition in 1772. A
probate index then names William Glasgow of Larne in 1783, but it does not give
an age or relatives and an older William was also living locally. The chronology
also reports that James Glasgow, born about 1775, shared a St Cedma's stone with
William born 1730. That is a genuine descendant lead, but the same page elsewhere
assigns the stone to William born about 1708. Until the inscription is recovered,
it does not prove whether James was William's son, grandson or another relative.

The four child profiles are therefore not empty because the relationships are
weak; they are dead ends because the surviving public transcription stops at
their births. The original register, the full 1783 probate material and the
unrecovered memorial are the records capable of extending the line.

## Duplicate and descendant audit

- Jean-3325 is distinct from Jean-3489 of Kirknewton and Jean-810 of Falkirk.
- William-3326 is distinct from William-3962 of County Tyrone and William-2426
  of the Moneymore/United States line.
- Helen-3327 is distinct from Helen-3527 of Kirknewton.
- Christian-3328 is distinct from Christian-3492 of Glencorse.
- The incomplete Registry of Deeds name index produced no later deed naming
  Jean, Helen or Christian and no identifying later William deed. This is not a
  negative finding because married daughters would usually be indexed under
  their husbands' surnames and the volunteer index is incomplete.

## Best next proof targets

1. PRONI `MIC/1B/6`: inspect every marriage from about 1745 to 1769 for Jean,
   Helen/Hellen and Christian, and every child entry naming William as father.
2. Obtain the complete St Cedma's inscription or photograph said to contain
   William born 1730 and James born about 1775.
3. Check the Connor 1783 probate entry and any surviving bond, grant or abstract
   for relatives, occupation and residence.
4. Inspect the 1744 lease and later assignments or endorsements for named lives,
   heirs or successors.
