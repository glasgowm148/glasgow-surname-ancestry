## Suggested corrections

The 1744/1775 memorial directly establishes that an adult William Glasgow of
Larne survived into January 1775. It is much more consistent with this older
William than with the younger William reportedly born about 1730, who would
have been only about fourteen when the deed was witnessed. The record does not
name William's parents, wife, or children.

## Do not add as fact

* That Hugh Glasgow was William's brother or son; the 1736 lease supplies no
  kinship term.
* That William was a son of Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-1502).
* That the 1783 probate entry necessarily belongs to this William rather than a
  younger namesake.
