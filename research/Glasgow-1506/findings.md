## Current conclusion

The derivative register chronology's Jean, daughter of Hugh, baptised in 1731,
may be the Jane named first among Hugh and Ann's eleven deceased children and
recorded as dying 8 January 1733. Jean/Jane interchange and chronology support
the hypothesis, but the original baptism and full monument must be compared.
