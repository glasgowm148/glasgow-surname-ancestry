=== Ropemaking and Larne property ===

James was established as a ropemaker at Larne by 1760. A lease dated 10 September 1760, registered on 24 November 1763, describes him as a ropemaker of Belfast and leases him a marked-out ropewalk at Gardenmore in Larne for thirty-one years from 1 November 1760. He was also to build a house at the south end of the ropewalk.<ref name="Ropewalk1760">Registry of Deeds transcript, lease dated 10 September 1760 and registered 24 November 1763, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSH3-S7XZ-8 FamilySearch image].</ref>

On 28 March 1768 James, then described as a ropemaker of the Old Town of Larne, assigned to John Hill for £50 the plot, workhouse and remainder of that same thirty-one-year lease. The matching commencement date and annual rent identify the assigned premises as the 1760 ropewalk.<ref name="Ropewalk1768">Registry of Deeds transcript, assignment dated 28 March 1768, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSJW-DDXW FamilySearch image].</ref>

The assignment was witnessed by another James Glasgow who was expressly described as a tanner. The tanner should not be conflated with the ropemaker; the tanner records are a better fit for [[Glasgow-3991|James Glasgow (about 1747–1812)]].

In 1773 Mr James Glasgow of Larne, gentleman, swore that he was aged upwards of thirty, placing his birth before 27 October 1743.<ref name="Larne1773">Registry of Deeds memorial and affidavit, sworn 27 October 1773, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSH3-M72W-M FamilySearch image].</ref>

=== Identity distinction ===

The 1778 record calling a Larne witness a tanner and the 1786 Blackcave lease to a Larne tanner belong to a separate occupational sequence, probably [[Glasgow-3991]]. They should not be used to claim that this ropemaker was also a tanner.
