== Biography ==
'''Robert Glasgow''' was likely born in Ayrshire, Scotland, before 1652. This birth estimate is based on his marriage and the birth of his known children in the early 1670s.

He married '''Margaret Donald'''. Together, they had at least two sons who were baptised in the parish of Ayr, Ayrshire:
* '''[[Glasgow-1513|Robert Glasgow]]''', born on 6 June 1670 and christened on 9 June 1670.<ref>"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XY8Z-L28 : 11 February 2020), Robert Glasgow in entry for Robert Glasgow, 1670.</ref>
* '''[[Glasgow-1514|Ralph Glasgow (bef.1671-)]]''', born on 3 December 1671.<ref>"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XY8Z-22X : 11 February 2020), Robert Glasgow in entry for Ralph Glasgow, 1671.</ref>

Both birth records name Robert Glasgow as the father and Margaret Donald as the mother. The family was living in Ayr, Ayrshire, at the time of these events.

Further details regarding his life, including his parentage and death, are not yet known.
