The 1648 Glasgow child could have been this Quothquan man; the index does not give his age, parents, spouse or occupation. This is a working cross-record identification. The original testament, NRS CC14/5/9 images 24–26 (Lanark Commissary Court, in the [ScotlandsPeople wills collection](https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/search-records/legal-records/wills)), was reviewed on 10 September 2026. Its selective reading and partly obscured opening yield no secure kin bridge to the 1648 child; the [printed Lanark index](https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/the_commissariot_record_of_lanark__register_of_testaments__1595-1800_~_1903.pdf#page=50) supplies the Quothquan spelling. It gives no evidence for the separate Tyrone migration story.

Scotland's People separately indexes a **Williame Glasgow** on 28 September 1648, Glasgow parish OPR 644/1, reference 40/156, with parents Williame Glasgow and Bessie Howie. The index is evidence for this child's event and recorded parent names, but its combined birth/baptism date does not establish an exact birthday. The original page has not been inspected. A different William was indexed to parents of the same names on 24 October 1641, reference 30/298, and belongs to [Glasgow-1497](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1497). These are separate indexed events, not duplicate profiles for one event; the older child's death estimate still requires evidence.

The live [Glasgow-1521](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1521) biography was corrected on 26 September 2026: it no longer uses the 1641 FamilySearch citation to support the 1648 child and now gives the separate 1648 OPR locator. The structured birth date and attached parent identities remain uncertain. No record connects this child to [Glasgow-591](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-591) or the Tyrone family.
