## Identity assessment — not Margaret, daughter of Archibald of Rathmalton, 30 September 2026

Betham's manuscript pedigree abstract of the will of Archibald Glasgow, clerk of Rathmalton, County Donegal (will dated April 1673; [NLI catalogue](https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000540633)), names Margaret as his only daughter, married to an unnamed Cairns, with a son Thomas Cairns. This Margaret is **excluded** as that daughter: her own cited baptism, 15 February 1638 at Glasgow (Scotland's People OPR 644/1, 30/198; index only, original uninspected), names her father as William Glasgow, with Bessie Howie, a different father from Archibald. The profile's estimated 1642 death is unsupported and played no part in the exclusion. Glasgow-3186 (wife of George Lyell in South Leith in 1633) remains a candidate for Archibald's daughter, but no later Cairns marriage or household link is proved.
