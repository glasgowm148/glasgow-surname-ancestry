# Findings: [Joseph Glasgow (Glasgow-1543), Wythe County, Virginia](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1543)

Last updated: 2026-08-12

## Current conclusion

Joseph is proved to be a brother of
[Arthur Glasgow (Glasgow-649)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-649) and
[Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-12)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-12).
His current parents remain unproved.

Faris prints a letter dated 6 November 1806 from Joseph and Betty Glasgow to
Arthur, using explicit brother language for Arthur and Robert. Joseph's own
1831 will independently calls Arthur his deceased brother.
