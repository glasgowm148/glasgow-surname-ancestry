== Biography ==
Andrew Glasgow’s birth is estimated at about 1685; no birth or baptism record has been identified for that estimate.

Andreu Glasgou and [[Cook-36136|Christian Cook]] are indexed as marrying at Kirknewton and East Calder on 14 June 1700.<ref name="AndrewCook1700">"Scotland, Marriages, 1561–1910", FamilySearch ([https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XY9W-VZX XY9W-VZX]), Andreu Glasgou and Christian Cook, 14 June 1700, Kirknewton and East Calder. == Research Notes ==
=== Checked Kirknewton record roles ===

The checked 1702 christening index explicitly names Andreu Glasgou as father of James Glasgou at Kirknewton and East Calder on 20 September 1702. Andrew-1546’s original cited source is this same named-father entry, and James-1547 is the child account. Their record-defined father–son relationship is assessed as documented at 99% subjective confidence. This does not identify the child with a later adult or strengthen Andrew’s own possible father. The checked marriage index names Andreu Glasgou and Christian Cook at Kirknewton and East Calder on 14 June 1700. The existing Andrew-1546 and [[Cook-36136]] accounts carry this exact couple, parish and date. Their record-defined spouse relationship is assessed as documented at 99% subjective confidence. The 1702 child index does not name a mother; no maternal relationship is upgraded. These are newly graded existing relationships, previously unmarked, rather than new parent attachments or new source discoveries. FamilySearch transcripts were inspected 18 October 2026; original images are unavailable there. The 1702 displayed birth field contains only “9”, which does not independently establish the full 9 September date retained on James’s profile. James’s possible later-life identities and his descendants’ parent questions remain separate.<ref name="KirknewtonNamedRoles18Oct2026">[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-6MQ FamilySearch, Andreu Glasgou in the James Glasgou christening entry, 20 September 1702]; [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XY9W-VZX FamilySearch, Andreu Glasgou and Christian Cook, marriage 14 June 1700]. Both index transcripts inspected 18 October 2026; original images unavailable.</ref>

=== Andrew’s Kirknewton father ===

Prefer Alexander [[Glasgow-3118]] as Andrew [[Glasgow-1546]]’s father at 58% subjective confidence, possible. The particular Alexander household is established at Kirknewton by the 1667 Margaret Smith marriage and the father-named Alexander and Agnes entries in 1673 and 1677. Andrew’s 1700 Christian Cook marriage and fatherhood of James in 1702 place his own adult household in the same parish, one generation later. The sustained parish setting and feasible household succession together favour this existing family. Andrew, Alexander and Agnes recur in the descendant branches, providing modest supporting context rather than an independent naming rule. This weighs a specific family against alternatives, rather than inferring fatherhood from surname and estimated ages alone.

The 1685 birth is an unsourced estimate. Taken literally it makes Andrew about fifteen at the indexed 1700 marriage and seventeen at the 1702 christening. Those ages are possible, but they cannot independently support the pedigree. A birth earlier in the 1670s or early 1680s would also fit the adult events and Alexander’s family chronology; no replacement birth year is adopted. Alexander’s own circa-1650 birth is likewise an estimate. The argument rests chiefly on the recorded adult households, not on moving either birth date to fit.

No checked source names Andrew’s father. Another Kirknewton father remains possible: the older Andro account [[Glasgow-1319]], or one of the local John or William households, cannot be excluded merely because no Andrew baptism has been identified. John [[Glasgow-1057]] of Kilwinning/Stevenston remains a weaker comparison because his documented household is elsewhere. Common forenames and the inherited father attachment must not be counted as independent confirmation. The complete sibling and child sequences are not established. Margaret Smith is not assigned as Andrew’s mother.

Check the Kirknewton register around the 1667 marriage and the known 1673/1677 children for an earlier Andrew, and compare residences, witnesses and occupations with the 1700 Cook household. Inspect the original marriage and 1702 child entry when available.
