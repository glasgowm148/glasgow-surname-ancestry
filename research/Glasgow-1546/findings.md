# Findings: [Andrew Glasgow (Glasgow-1546)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1546)

## Current conclusion

[John Glasgow (Glasgow-1057)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1057) is
not a strong father candidate. Andrew's approximately 1685 birth is not a
recorded baptism, and his attached father remains uncertain. John's documented
childbearing household was at Kilwinning and later Stevenston: George was
baptised at Kilwinning in 1687, only about two years from Andrew's estimated
birth, while Andrew's family is placed at Kirknewton.

## Recommended action

Keep John only as a weak comparison lead unless an original Kirknewton record
connects the households. Descendant name repetition does not bridge the parish
gap.

## Stronger candidate

[Alexander Glasgow (Glasgow-3118)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3118)
is the more coherent alternative. He was of the right generation, was married
at Kirknewton in 1667, and had children recorded there in 1673 and 1677. An
additional child about 1685 is chronologically possible, and Andrew/Alexander/
Agnes recur in the two descendant branches. This is useful converging evidence,
but no baptism or testament yet names Andrew as Alexander's son. Search the
Kirknewton register after the 1677 Agnes entry and before Andrew's marriage.
