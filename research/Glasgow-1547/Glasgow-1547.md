== Research Notes ==
=== Checked Kirknewton record roles ===

The checked 1702 christening index explicitly names Andreu Glasgou as father of James Glasgou at Kirknewton and East Calder on 20 September 1702. Andrew-1546’s original cited source is this same named-father entry, and James-1547 is the child account. Their record-defined father–son relationship is assessed as documented at 99% subjective confidence. This does not identify the child with a later adult or strengthen Andrew’s own possible father. The checked marriage index names Andreu Glasgou and Christian Cook at Kirknewton and East Calder on 14 June 1700. The existing Andrew-1546 and [[Cook-36136]] accounts carry this exact couple, parish and date. Their record-defined spouse relationship is assessed as documented at 99% subjective confidence. The 1702 child index does not name a mother; no maternal relationship is upgraded. These are newly graded existing relationships, previously unmarked, rather than new parent attachments or new source discoveries. FamilySearch transcripts were inspected 18 October 2026; original images are unavailable there. The 1702 displayed birth field contains only “9”, which does not independently establish the full 9 September date retained on James’s profile. James’s possible later-life identities and his descendants’ parent questions remain separate.<ref name="KirknewtonNamedRoles18Oct2026">[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-6MQ FamilySearch, Andreu Glasgou in the James Glasgou christening entry, 20 September 1702]; [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XY9W-VZX FamilySearch, Andreu Glasgou and Christian Cook, marriage 14 June 1700]. Both index transcripts inspected 18 October 2026; original images unavailable.</ref>

=== Selected uncertain family — 16 October 2026 ===

[[Glasgow-3526|James Glasgow, Kirknewton father account]] is the maintained account for the James named in the 1717 record. Its own circa-1688 birth and selected uncertain William [[Glasgow-1026|Glasgow-1026]] father remain prior hypotheses. The child’s earlier biography instead names [[Glasgow-1547|James Glasgow, alternative 1702 childhood account]]. That 1702 Kirknewton childhood, son of [[Glasgow-1546|Glasgow-1546]], remains an alternative identity for the adult James. If baptised near birth in September 1702, he would have been about fourteen at the March 1717 baptism, making that route less straightforward than the older James account. An older-child baptism or an incorrect age estimate remains possible; neither is asserted as fact. The named father James is distinct from the question of which modern James account represents him. The earlier child-parent grade is not upgraded, and neither James’s ancestry receives new certainty.

[[Glasgow-3526|James Glasgow, Kirknewton father account]] is selected as the uncertain working father of [[Glasgow-2847|John Glasgow, West Calder Anderson household]], husband of Margaret Anderson and father of the West Calder children recorded from 1745 to 1755. The route provisionally identifies that adult with [[Glasgow-3525|John Glasgow, Kirknewton child of 1717]], indexed at neighbouring Kirknewton and East Calder on 31 March 1717 with father James. The parish setting, exact forename and surname, and feasible generation favour this concrete local childhood over an unidentified origin: if baptised near birth in 1717, John would have been about 28 in 1745 and 38 in 1755. The later family also contains a James, although a common repeated forename does not independently establish a grandfather. No register entry yet joins the child to Margaret Anderson’s husband. This is a low-confidence identity-based parent model, not a proved father clause for the adult.

A competing childhood is [[Glasgow-1544|Glasgow-1544]], recorded at Kirknewton in 1702 with father William. That child has already been used as a possible identity for [[Glasgow-3481|Glasgow-3481]] of the Christian Meek household. The Meek and Anderson households have children in 1755 under different mothers, so the new model keeps those adult accounts distinct; no second household, remarriage or polygamy is invented to equate them. That does not prove the 1702 child could not instead be Margaret Anderson’s husband. The two proposed adult identities for that childhood remain alternatives. Another local John and another James father remain possible; the current shortlist is not exhaustive. The 1717 route is selected weakly because it fits an ordinary adult generation and a specific nearby record without requiring the Meek household identity.

No mother is selected for the West Calder John. The retained 1717 citation names James but supplies no mother in the existing account. Neither Ann McCook from the older William household nor Christian Cook from [[Glasgow-1547|James Glasgow, alternative 1702 childhood account]]’s childhood is transferred into this generation. John’s new proposed father does not identify the later life of his son [[Glasgow-2846|Glasgow-2846]]: that child remains separate from the Larne Rose Allan husband [[Glasgow-4179|Glasgow-4179]] unless their own identity bridge is established. [[Glasgow-1922|Glasgow-1922]]’s Kirk marriage and all existing child biographies remain outside the new parent inference.

One uncertain father is newly selected through a possible child-to-adult identity. No mother, original inspection, merge, date change, live WikiTree edit or confidence upgrade is claimed. The 1717 child and later father remain separate profile accounts and may be duplicates rather than two distinct brothers. Working connectivity counts retained accounts and inherits the uncertainty in their older ancestry; the family-size gain is not identity evidence.
