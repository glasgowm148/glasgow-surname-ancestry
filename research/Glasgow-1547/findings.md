The earlier child-parent grade is not upgraded, and neither James’s ancestry receives new certainty.

This is a low-confidence identity-based parent model, not a proved father clause for the adult.

No mother is selected for the West Calder John. The retained 1717 citation names James but supplies no mother in the existing account.
