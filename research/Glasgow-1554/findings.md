# Findings: Walter Glasgow (Glasgow-1554), recorded in 1649

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

FamilySearch `VQ4K-FWL` indexes Walter Glasgow, 23 September 1649, under the
combined `Kirknewton and East Calder` label and names father William. The exact
historical parish remains unresolved.

Walter's date falls within the documented July 1649-August 1651 gap in the Mid
Calder baptism register. His entry therefore cannot demonstrate that the family
was registering children at Mid Calder in September 1649. Inspect the original
Kirknewton/East Calder source before assigning a parish or merging adult fathers.

## Potential-father review — 10 August 2026

[James Glasgow (Glasgow-1150)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1150) is
not a viable replacement for Walter's recorded father. FamilySearch
`VQ4K-FWL` names the father William, and the live profile now attaches
[William Glasgow (Glasgow-2453)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2453).
The precise parish and the identity of that William still require the original
record, but the father's recorded given name rules out James.
