== Biography ==

'''John Glasgow''' was father of Jean Glasgow, christened at Linlithgow on 17
July 1613; John Glasgow, christened there on 14 September 1617; and George
Glasgow, christened there on 20 January 1622.<ref>“Scotland, Births and
Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch,
[https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RX-HPB Jean, 1613];
[https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RV-T8R John, 1617]; and
[https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RN-XV7 George, 1622], each naming
father John Glasgow at Linlithgow.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

No cited record names John's parents. The current Andrew Glasgow and Jonet
Anderson attachment rests on an incorrect reading of Andrew's 1597/8
testament. The corrected reading identifies Andrew's wife as Elizat Stevinsoun,
their son as Robert, and daughters Marion, Janet and Isobel.<ref>National
Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124,
testament of Andro Glasgw, made 6 February 1597/8 and confirmed 10 July 1598;
corrected working transcription.</ref> Andrew remains only an unsupported
Lothian-cluster candidate for this John.
