== Biography ==

'''John Glasgow''' was the father named at the baptisms of three children at
Linlithgow:

* [[Glasgow-3055|Jean Glasgow]], christened 17 July 1613.<ref
  name="Jean1613">“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch,
  [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RX-HPB Jean Glasgow, 1613], father
  John Glasgow, Linlithgow.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-3056|John Glasgow]], christened 14 September 1617.<ref
  name="John1617">“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch,
  [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RV-T8R John Glasgow, 1617], father
  John Glasgow, Linlithgow.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-1560|George Glasgow]], christened 20 January 1622.<ref
  name="George1622">“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch,
  [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RN-XV7 George Glasgow, 1622], father
  John Glasgow, Linlithgow.</ref>

The indexes establish John's activity at Linlithgow but do not name his wife,
parents, occupation or death details. His birth date and birthplace are
estimates derived from being a father by 1613.

== Research Notes ==

[[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow of [Corsoun?]]] and
[[Anderson-103463|Jonet Anderson]] are presently attached as uncertain parents.
That placement was partly justified by an incorrect transcription of Andrew's
1597/8 testament. The corrected reading names Elizat Stevinsoun as Andrew's
wife, Robert as their son, and Marion, Janet and Isobel as Andrew's daughters;
it does not name this John or Jonet Anderson.<ref>National Records of Scotland,
Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124, testament testamentar and
inventory of Andro Glasgw, made 6 February 1597/8 and confirmed 10 July 1598;
corrected working transcription of the original register images.</ref>

John's Lothian setting keeps Andrew as a possible cluster hypothesis, but no
record calls him Andrew's son. Omission from a dispositive clause does not by
itself prove that he was not a son, so the correct status is unsupported rather
than directly disproved. Do not place John through supposed sons James, Andrew
or William: the corrected testament does not establish those sons.

A possible duplicate with Glasgow-3608 requires a separate same-person audit.
Naming patterns or broad Lothian geography alone cannot resolve it.
