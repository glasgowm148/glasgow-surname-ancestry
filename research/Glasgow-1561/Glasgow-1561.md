== Biography ==
'''John Glasgow''' was the father named at three baptisms in Linlithgow:

* [[Glasgow-3055|Jean Glasgow]], baptised 17 July 1613.<ref>https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RX-HPB</ref>
* [[Glasgow-3056|John Glasgow]], baptised 14 September 1617.<ref>https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RV-T8R</ref>
* [[Glasgow-1560|George Glasgow]], baptised 20 January 1622.<ref>https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RN-XV7</ref>

These indexes document a father called John in the parish over nine years. They do not name his wife, parents or occupation, establish his birthplace, or record his death. The common father identity is a working household reconstruction. Birth estimates of 1579 and 1590 have both been used; neither is a recorded birth date.

=== Possible parents and the corrected testament ===
No parents were attached to the live profile when checked on 15 October 2026. [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow]] and [[Anderson-103463|Jonet Anderson]] were earlier uncertain parent hypotheses. The corrected reading of Andrew’s 1597/8 testament names wife Elizat Stevinsoun, their son Robert, and daughters Marion, Janet and Isobel. It does not name this John or Jonet Anderson.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124, testament testamentar and inventory of Andro Glasgw, made 6 February 1597/8, died 7 February 1597/8, confirmed 10 July 1598; corrected working reading of the original register images.</ref> The printed testament index identifies Andrew’s testament; it does not supply the children’s names.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, testament of Andrew Glasgow, CC8/8/32; printed index: [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso01scotuoft/page/n117/mode/1up ''Scottish Record Society'', p. 108].</ref>

The testament is in Secretary Hand, and the corrected working reading remains open to refinement. John’s omission from its dispositive clauses is not itself proof that Andrew could not be his father. Andrew remains an unsupported locality-based possibility; there is no basis for a lower-generation placement through supposed sons James, Andrew or William, because the corrected testament proves none of those sons.

[[Glasgow-1099|George Glasgow]] and [[Glasgow-1168|James Glasgow]] have also been proposed as possible ancestors. Broad regional geography and naming patterns do not identify either as John’s father. The suggestion that [[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]] was John’s son is likewise unproved; the estimated birth years must not be treated as fixed facts to accept or reject it.
