== Biography ==
'''John Glasgow''' was the father named at three baptisms in Linlithgow:

* [[Glasgow-3055|Jean Glasgow]], baptised 17 July 1613.<ref>https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RX-HPB</ref>
* [[Glasgow-3056|John Glasgow]], baptised 14 September 1617.<ref>https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RV-T8R</ref>
* [[Glasgow-1560|George Glasgow]], baptised 20 January 1622.<ref>https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1RN-XV7</ref>

These indexes document a father called John in the parish over nine years. They do not name his wife, parents or occupation, establish his birthplace, or record his death. The common father identity is a working household reconstruction. Birth estimates of 1579 and 1590 have both been used; neither is a recorded birth date.

== Research Notes ==
=== Possible parents and the corrected testament ===
No parents were attached to the live profile when checked on 15 October 2026. [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow]] and [[Anderson-103463|Jonet Anderson]] were earlier uncertain parent hypotheses. The corrected reading of Andrew’s 1597/8 testament names wife Elizat Stevinsoun, their son Robert, and daughters Marion, Janet and Isobel. It does not name this John or Jonet Anderson.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124, testament testamentar and inventory of Andro Glasgw, made 6 February 1597/8, died 7 February 1597/8, confirmed 10 July 1598; corrected working reading of the original register images.</ref> The printed testament index identifies Andrew’s testament; it does not supply the children’s names.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, testament of Andrew Glasgow, CC8/8/32; printed index: [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso01scotuoft/page/n117/mode/1up ''Scottish Record Society'', p. 108].</ref>

The testament is in Secretary Hand, and the corrected working reading remains open to refinement. John’s omission from its dispositive clauses is not itself proof that Andrew could not be his father. Andrew remains an unsupported locality-based possibility; there is no basis for a lower-generation placement through supposed sons James, Andrew or William, because the corrected testament proves none of those sons.

[[Glasgow-1099|George Glasgow]] and [[Glasgow-1168|James Glasgow]] have also been proposed as possible ancestors. Broad regional geography and naming patterns do not identify either as John’s father. The suggestion that [[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]] was John’s son is likewise unproved; the estimated birth years must not be treated as fixed facts to accept or reject it.

=== Thomas is a different record-defined child ===
A suggestion that [[Glasgow-1103|Thomas Glasgow, indexed in Glasgow on 15 March 1612]], was John’s child conflicts with that entry’s named father George Glasgow and mother Margaret Steuart, represented by the compatible existing [[Stewart-65226|Margaret Stewart]] profile.<ref>Scotland’s People, owner-supplied Old Parish Registers births/baptisms index, reviewed 10 September 2026, Thomas Glasgow, indexed 15 March 1612, Glasgow parish 644/1, reference 10/81; parents GEORGE GLASGOW / MARGARET STEUART. Index only; original register image not inspected. https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/.</ref> This excludes John as father of that record-defined child. Identifying the child with later adult Thomas households at Linlithgow or Inveresk is a separate question; this comparison does not settle those adult identities.

=== Possible same-person identification ===
The former [[Glasgow-3608|John Glasgow]] profile now redirects to [[Glasgow-3296|John Glasgow]]. The latter account includes the father named at Mid Calder baptisms of Jhone on 26 June 1608 and William on 24 December 1609, with no mother named in the inspected provider transcripts.<ref>Findmypast, Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564–1929, Jhone Glasgow, Midcalder, 26 June 1608, father Jhone Glasgow, OPR 694/1 item 3 p. 5; https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F4585634&tab=this (father field rechecked 15 October 2026). William Glasgow, Midcalder, 24 December 1609, father Jhone Glasgow, OPR 694/1 item 3; https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F4585702 (provider transcript captured 3 September 2026). Original registers uninspected.</ref>

The Mid Calder and Linlithgow fathers are compatible in chronology, but the indexes give no shared wife, occupation, holding or explicit cross-parish identity. The John baptisms of 1608 and 1617 require comparison for survival, name reuse and household continuity. Keep this as an open identity comparison and do not merge or select a parent from names and estimated dates alone. The Montfoid and Quothquan identities already associated with Glasgow-3296 remain qualified in that account.

The spelling ''Glesgow'' has been reported in the parish material; original pages are needed to verify the exact spellings and any omitted household detail. A related FamilySearch source collection is also cited, without treating it as independent parentage evidence.<ref>https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/sources/MG7J-6GM</ref>

=== Selected uncertain working father,15 October2026 ===
[[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow of Calder]] is selected as the working father in the local research model. Select the same older Calder household as the working origin for this neighbouring Lothian family. John’s estimated1579 generation and children recorded1613–1622 fit an older son of Andrew, whose own father is tentatively John-1093. The proximity and compatible generations make this a useful shared-family model alongside James and David, rather than an assertion that the three are proved brothers.

This is a hypothesis from existing material, not a parent named in a newly found record. John of Hermand-3181 is a compatible alternative if John1561’s estimated birth is earlier than1577. A separate unnamed local father remains possible. Andrew’s1598 testament does not name John; do not restore the obsolete James/Andrew/William child list or attach Jonet Anderson as mother. No mother or live WikiTree parent-field change is implied. The earlier proof-based rationale remains superseded where corrected.
