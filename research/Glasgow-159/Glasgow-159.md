== Biography ==

John was an agricultural labourer.

John married Agnas Gray on 19 September 1785 in Kirkliston, West Lothian, Scotland.<ref>"Scotland, Select Marriages, 1561-1910".  {{Ancestry Record|60144|2049453}} (accessed 13 October 2021).  John Glassgow marriage to Agnas Gray on 19 Sep 1785 in Kirkliston, West Lothian, Scotland.</ref>

In July 1798, John Glasgow, labourer at Millrig in Kirkliston parish, subscribed to a voluntary contribution. The name, parish, occupation and date favour identifying him with this John.<ref>The Edinburgh Advertiser, 20 July 1798, p.6, Kirkliston voluntary subscriptions, [https://www.newspapers.com/image/601860386/ image 601860386]. Original-image review recorded 6 October 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Possible Mid Calder family ===

John may have been the son of [[Glasgow-1023|Thomas Glasgow]] and [[Mill-357|Janet Mill]] represented by [[Glasgow-2980|John Glasgow]], recorded at Mid Calder in 1750.<ref name="MidCalder1750">“Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch, John Glasgow, Mid Calder, 1750, cited in [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BL-JFM the child index] and [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BL-JX1 the Janet Mill parent entry]. These are citations to the same event; the original register has not been inspected.</ref> His approximately 1761 birth is an estimate without a cited age. A 1750 child could have married in 1785 at about 35. The nearby Lothian setting and his children Thomas and Janet favour this couple as a working comparison. The present assessment is possible, 42% for each parent, rather than proved. Both relationships depend on one unresolved child-to-adult identification.

The local Johns [[Glasgow-3581|born at Kirknewton in 1755]], [[Glasgow-3480|at Kirknewton/East Calder in 1751]], and [[Glasgow-2846|at West Calder in 1747]] remain alternatives. No record yet identifies the Kirkliston husband’s parents. John-2980 and John-159 may represent one man; the two accounts are not evidence of brothers with the same name. Thomas’s earlier Kilwinning identification is a separate question.
