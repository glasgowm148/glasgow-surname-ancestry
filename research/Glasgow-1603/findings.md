# Findings: [Hugh Glasgow (Glasgow-1603), about 1600, Ayrshire](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1603)

## Current conclusion

Hugh occupies a plausible generation in the route from the Corsankell family to
the later Kilwinning Glasgows, but no reviewed record yet proves Hugh's existence,
birth, father or son. Every structured fact is derived from one Ancestry tree.

His current father [James Glasgow (Glasgow-991), about 1575, Midlothian](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-991)
belongs to a record-led Calder/Inveresk household. The profile's own biography
instead calls Hugh an Ayrshire progenitor. The attachment is therefore poorly
supported and geographically inferior to the working Ayrshire route:

```text
Glasgow-1098 ? Glasgow-1168 -> Glasgow-3193
  ? missing Ayrshire generation / Glasgow-1603
    ? Glasgow-1604
      ? Rev. Glasgow-1073
```

Hugh could represent the son or grandson of [John Glasgow (Glasgow-3193), born
before 1542, Corsankell/Kilwinning](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3193),
but that exact edge is not proved. John at Montfoid in 1610, James at Brekpleughe
in 1632 and Ninian at Brigend of Eglinton in 1634 are documented people in the
same missing-generation corridor and may expose Hugh as a conflated placeholder.
