# New information for Glasgow-1604

- Birth about 1626, death about 1664 at Kilwinning, father Glasgow-1603 and all
  three attached sons are not supported by a record on the profile.
- He is the best existing-profile candidate father for Rev. John Glasgow M.A.
  (Glasgow-1073) by generation and Kilwinning-Kilbirnie geography, but only as
  an uncertain working relationship.
- Glasgow-3375 must first be tested as a duplicate of Rev. Glasgow-1073; otherwise
  the proposed family would again contain two contemporary sons named John.
- Hew Glasgow-1066 was an adult husband and father in 1676, contradicting the
  attached birth estimate of 1658.
- Glasgow-3145 is an empty, unsourced John with the exact same 1626 estimate and
  is probably a duplicate or another artifact of the imported tree.
