== Biography ==
John Glasgow is retained as an uncertain Ayrshire-generation profile. His approximate 1626 birth in Ayrshire and approximate 1664 death at Kilwinning come from online family trees, not an identified contemporary record. No independent original record currently establishes those dates or identifies his parents.

=== Attached relationships ===

The attached father [[Glasgow-1603|Hugh Glasgow]] and three sons are uncertain working relationships. Records for the sons' own adult households do not name this John as their father:

* [[Glasgow-3375|John Glasgow]] and Barbara Alason were parents at Kilwinning in 1679 and 1689. Whether this John was the father of that man remains unproved. Glasgow-3375 is also a possible identity match for [[Glasgow-1073|Rev. John Glasgow M.A.]].
* [[Glasgow-1512|Robert Glasgow]] and Margaret Donald had sons at Ayr in 1670–71; the baptism records do not name Robert's father.
* [[Glasgow-1066|Hew Glasgow]] married Margaret Lachland in 1676 and had children at Kilwinning; those records do not name Hew's father.

=== Former duplicate and parentage routes ===

Glasgow-3145 also represented a John Glasgow with an estimated birth about 1626 in Scotland and no independent life event. The profiles were merged into this ID on 27 September 2026 as duplicate imported-tree scaffolds, with no record identifying two different Johns. The former profile was attached, uncertainly, to [[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]] and [[Bie-55|Christina Bie]], while this profile's uncertain route runs through Hugh Glasgow. Neither route is proved. The former parentage is preserved here as a research hypothesis; these alternatives must not be combined into a single asserted couple.

The source of the supposed 1664 Kilwinning death has not been identified. Its date and place remain uncertain research leads.
