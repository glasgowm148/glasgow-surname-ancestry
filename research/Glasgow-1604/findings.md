# Findings: [John Glasgow (Glasgow-1604), about 1626, Kilwinning/Ayrshire](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1604)

## Current conclusion

John is the best existing-profile candidate for the father of [Rev. John Glasgow
M.A. (Glasgow-1073), about 1653, Kilbirnie](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1073),
but the profile is only a scaffold. Birth about 1626, death about 1664 at
Kilwinning, father Glasgow-1603 and the three attached sons all ultimately rest
on an Ancestry tree or on records that name only the later fathers.

[John Glasgow (Glasgow-3145), about 1626, Scotland/Ayrshire](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3145)
is empty and unsourced, has the exact same estimated birth year, and is attached
directly to Midlothian James-991. It is probably a duplicate of Glasgow-1604 or a
second artifact of the same imported tree, not independent evidence for another
John generation.

The strongest working placement is:

```text
Glasgow-1098 ? Glasgow-1168 -> Glasgow-3193
  ? Glasgow-1603 / omitted Ayrshire generation
    ? Glasgow-1604
      ? Glasgow-1073 (possibly the same man as Glasgow-3375)
```

Do not add Rev. John as a second contemporary son named John until
[Glasgow-3375](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3375) has been tested as his
duplicate. The 1679 and 1689 Kilwinning OPR entries could refer to the minister
before his 1690 marriage to Jean Cunningham.
