== Biography ==

'''Margaret Glasgow''' was born in 1740 in Edrom parish, Berwickshire,
Scotland. The parish index names her father as James Glasgow.<ref
name="Edrom1740">Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564–1929'',
Margaret Glasgow, birth 1740, Edrom, Berwickshire, father James Glasgow, OPR
738/4, item 5, p. 24;
[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F4839749&tab=this
transcript SCOT/OPR/BAP/4839749], captured 3 September 2026.</ref>

Her attached father, '''[[Glasgow-1238|James Glasgow]]''', married
'''[[Tait-2506|Elizabeth Tait]]''' at Stitchel, Roxburghshire, on 25 January
1740.<ref name="ParentsMarriage">“Scotland Marriages, 1561–1910,” database,
FamilySearch,
[https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYQ1-SPB James Glasgow and Elizabath
Tait], 25 January 1740, Stitchel, Roxburghshire.</ref> Children of that couple
were recorded at Stitchel from 1742 onward. The marriage, chronology and nearby
Borders parishes make them the probable identities of Margaret's parents, but
the Edrom entry itself names only her father as James and does not name her
mother.

Margaret's later life and death have not been identified.

== Research Notes ==

=== Birthplace ===

The parish index supports Edrom, Berwickshire, in 1740. The former uncertain
Kelso birthplace was inferred from relatives and should be replaced by the
more specific record evidence.

=== Parent identities ===

The record directly establishes that Margaret's father was named James
Glasgow. Identifying him as [[Glasgow-1238|James Glasgow]] is strongly supported
by that man's January 1740 marriage and subsequent Borders family, but is not
stated by a unique occupation or residence in Margaret's entry. Elizabeth Tait
is the probable mother; she is not named in this birth index.

An online Ancestry tree also identifies this family.<ref>[https://www.ancestry.co.uk/family-tree/person/tree/66808278/person/46161259840/facts
Ancestry family tree 66808278, person 46161259840], accessed 2026. This is a
derivative tree and should be evaluated against the parish records.</ref>
