## Suggested corrections

The structured birth year and place should not be presented as sourced. The
original marriage of Adam's son establishes neither Adam's age nor birthplace.
If retained as working estimates, both should remain uncertain and the biography
should say explicitly that `about 1780, County Antrim` is a guess.

```text
'''Adam Glasgow''' is known from the original 1851 marriage register of his son [[Glasgow-1623|James Glasgow]]. James, a 38-year-old farmer of Inishrush, named his father as Adam Glasgow, farmer. The record does not state Adam's age, residence, birthplace, wife, parents, or whether he was living in 1851.<ref name="JamesBarkleyMarriage1851">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/view/?record_id=cima-3208068 IrishGenealogy.ie], civil marriage register, Portglenone, 17 March 1851, James Glasgow and Rosanna Barkley; original register image.</ref>

Adam was a different person from [[Glasgow-3902|Adam Glasgow (abt.1758-1844)]] unless the original marriage record is wrong. The older Adam already had a gravestone-proved son [[Glasgow-3903|James Glasgow]], born about 1794 and living until 1878, while the 1851 groom was born about 1812/13. The current attachment of this Adam as the older Adam's son is chronologically possible, but no record yet proves that relationship.
```
