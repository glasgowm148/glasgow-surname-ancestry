## Current conclusion

The original civil register proves that James was a 38-year-old bachelor and
farmer living at Inishrush in March 1851, son of farmer Adam Glasgow. It also
identifies his wife as Rosanna Barkley of Tyanee, daughter of Henry Barkley.
This exact combination makes James, rather than the older James `Glasgow-3903`,
the compelling identity for the James described in the 1847 family letter as
having a child by Henry Barkly's servant-maid daughter. The child is still not
named by either record.
