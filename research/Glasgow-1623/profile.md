== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' was born about 1812 or 1813, calculated from his reported
age at marriage. On 17 March 1851 he was recorded as a 38-year-old bachelor and
farmer of Inishrush, son of [[Glasgow-1622|Adam Glasgow]], farmer. He married
[[Barkley-2097|Rosanna Barkley]], about 25, a farm servant of Tyanee and daughter
of Henry Barkley.<ref name="Marriage1851">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/view/?record_id=cima-3208068 IrishGenealogy.ie], marriage of James Glasgow and Rosanna Barkley, 17 March 1851; original civil register.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Identity conflict ===

The marriage directly supplies James's father's name but no age, residence,
wife or parents for Adam. The record-defined Adam must not be identified with
or attached beneath [[Glasgow-3902|Adam Glasgow of Inishrush]] without another
record.

The Glasgow family monument identifies a son James of Adam and Rose who died in
1878 aged eighty-four; the corresponding civil return records an 84-year-old
married farmer at Inishrush.<ref name="Grave">[https://www.oneirishrover.com/tamlaght-graveyard/ ''Tamlaght O'Crilly Lower Graveyard''], Glasgow family monument transcription.</ref><ref name="Death1878">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/deaths_returns/deaths_1878/020497/7192875.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil death register], James Glasgow, Inishrush, 12 December 1878.</ref> The age differs by about nineteen years from the 1851 groom's age. Keep
[[Glasgow-3903|the monument-defined James]] and this profile distinct pending a
record that resolves whether the ages are wrong or two men are represented.

=== The 1847 report ===

A 20 March 1847 family letter reported that a James Glasgow had a child by a
servant who was a daughter of Henry Barkly.<ref name="Letter1847">[https://www.hiltner.com/mckeown/documents/John%20Glasgow%20letter%20to%20Jane%20Glasgow%20Gray%203-20-1847.pdf John Glasgow to Jane Glasgow Gray], 20 March 1847.</ref> The later marriage's exact combination of James, Henry Barkley's daughter,
her servant status and the Inishrush–Tyanee setting makes this couple the
strongest match. The letter is hearsay and does not name or sex the child; no
child should be created or attached from it.

Nothing in these records connects James or his father to [[Glasgow-951|Alexander
Glasgow of Lisnagaver]].
