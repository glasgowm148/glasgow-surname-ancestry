## Identity effect

Robert belongs to the Ballymena sibling group of James McClure Glasgow and Adam
Dickey Glasgow. He is not Robert `Glasgow-1367`, who was already aged 72 in 1851,
and supplies no evidence that the Glenleslie man was Adam of Inishrush's son.
