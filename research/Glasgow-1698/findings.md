# Findings: [Glasgow-1698](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1698)

## Current conclusion

A private MyHeritage tree and autosomal-match label propose descent through Adelaide Glasgow, daughter of Samuel Glasgow and Nancy Johnstone. Neither the online tree nor a predicted autosomal relationship proves the displayed historical pedigree.

PRONI T1959/3 provides a new but still derivative family-placement lead. Its
undated and unsigned family letter says that the writer's paternal grandfather
William Glasgow of Killycurragh and Miss Cunningham of Omagh had sons including
Samuel, who remained in the house where his parents lived and died. This
profile's reported circa-1800 birth and 1857 death at Killycurragh make him the
strongest live candidate for that Samuel, but the online transcription does
not by itself prove the profile identity or the proposed parents.
