# Findings: [James Glasgow (Glasgow-1702), born about 1799, County Tyrone; later Ballynasollus](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1702)

## Current conclusion

James is a strong candidate for the child baptised at St Luran, Derryloran,
on **21 August 1799** to **William and Mary Glasgow**. The year, parish, child
name and father all agree with James's later records. The baptism supplies no
abode, so identity is probable rather than conclusive.

James is **chronologically excluded as the father of Alexander `Glasgow-951`**.
James was born in 1799 and Alexander about 1811, leaving only about twelve
years. James's first known children from his earlier marriage begin about 1822,
which is consistent with the normal chronology and supplies no reason to move
his birth back a generation.

Live-profile audit **19 July 2026**: the baptism, marriage, death and parentage

- On 29 November 1851, James, a widower aged 50 and resident at Ballynasollus, married Nancy McCord, aged 26, of Ballynasollus. She was the daughter of James McCord, farmer. The witnesses were George Glasgow and Samuel Allen.[3]
- James died on 15 March 1882 at Ballynasollus, reportedly aged 83. His son Thomas Glasgow was present at the death.[1]
- James's 1851 marriage record confirms that his father was named William Glasgow and was a farmer.[3]
