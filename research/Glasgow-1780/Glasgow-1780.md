# WikiTree update notes: Glasgow-1780

Facts found after the current WikiTree profile capture, for manual review.

## Suggested additions

- James's surviving missionary journal directly calls Adam Dickey Glasgow his
  brother and identifies Adam's later move to New Zealand.
- The same journal directly calls Robert Glasgow in Victoria his brother.
- In the entry for 30 June 1858, James says he had just learned that his mother
  died on 21 May 1858. The entry does not state her name.

Source: [public journal transcription](https://revjamesmcclureglasgow.blogspot.com/).
The local capture is for internal preservation only and is not a WikiTree citation.

## Do not add as fact

- Do not connect these brothers to Adam of Inishrush or Robert of Glenleslie.
  Their already documented Ballymena generation and chronology identify a
  different family.
