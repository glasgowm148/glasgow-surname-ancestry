### Belfast College award and India ministry notices, 1829–1842

“Belfast College,” dated 1 May 1829, reports that premiums were distributed at the close of the session. Under “For Essays proposed to the Logic Class of last year,” it lists “On the Application of Testimony to Miraculous Events, James Glasgow, Belfast College.” James is listed as the essay award recipient, not as the proposer of the topic. The same page also has a separate geographic “near Glasgow” and a separate James M’Clatchey. The named Belfast College student is a possible match to Glasgow-1780 by the broader education-to-ministry sequence only; this notice alone does not identify him as the later Castledawson minister. [Belfast News-Letter, 5 May 1829, p. 3](https://www.newspapers.com/image/390581832/).

Under ‘ORDINATION AT BELLAGHY,’ the report says the Magherafelt Presbytery ordained Rev. Gilbert Kirker Wilson to Bellaghy on ‘the 15th ult.’ Rev. James Glasgow, of Castledawson, commenced the service with a sermon. After the report of the service, the same account describes the toasts: the chairman gave ‘The Gentlemen who officiated this day,’ and Rev. James Glasgow returned thanks. With the 5 April issue date, ‘15th ult.’ means 15 March 1836. A contemporaneous Castledawson ministerial occurrence, compatible with Glasgow-1780 in the established career sequence; the source does not state his middle name or family. [Belfast News-Letter, 5 April 1836, p. 4](https://www.newspapers.com/image/390544149/).
