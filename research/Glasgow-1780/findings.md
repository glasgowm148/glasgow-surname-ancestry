## Source findings

| Source | Finding | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [James McClure Glasgow missionary journal, 1857-1862](https://revjamesmcclureglasgow.blogspot.com/) | James repeatedly writes of `my brother Adam`, identifies Adam's moves from Belfast to New Zealand, and reports an encouraging letter from `my brother Robert in Victoria`. | First-person direct evidence that James, Adam Dickey Glasgow, and Robert Glasgow of Victoria were brothers. | Same journal, entry dated 30 June 1858 | James says he learned that day that his mother had died on 21 May 1858. | First-person direct evidence for his mother's exact death date, though the entry does not state her name. This matches the attached Jane McClure's 1858 death chronology but should be combined with an independent identity source before adding the date to her profile. |

## Identity effect

This is the Clough/Ballymena family already attached on WikiTree to John Glasgow
and Jane McClure. It is a separate collateral family from Adam Glasgow of Inishrush
and Robert Glasgow of Glenleslie. The journal must not be used to bridge those
families merely because one brother was named Adam and another Robert.
