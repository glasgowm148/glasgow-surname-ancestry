## Identity effect

This is the Clough/Ballymena family already attached on WikiTree to John Glasgow
and Jane McClure. It is a separate collateral family from Adam Glasgow of Inishrush
and Robert Glasgow of Glenleslie. The journal must not be used to bridge those
families merely because one brother was named Adam and another Robert.



## Source findings: Belfast College mathematics notice, 4 October 2026

Adam D. Glasgow of Broughshane appears in the Royal Belfast Academical Institution school prizes in June 1830 and Belfast College examinations reported in May 1831. The 1831 report also names James Glasgow, Institution, in voluntary advanced mathematical examinations. Sources: [Belfast News-Letter, 29 June 1830, p.3, Royal Belfast Academical Institution School Department](https://www.newspapers.com/image/390569859/), [Belfast News-Letter, 6 May 1831, p.3, Belfast College report dated 30 April](https://www.newspapers.com/image/390545285/).

## Identity and relationship assessment: October newspaper review

James McClure Glasgow-1780 (1805–1890) is a strong compatible candidate: his live biography expressly names the Royal Belfast Academical Institution and mathematical distinction. That current-profile statement is a comparison, not a newly inspected primary education source. The newspaper’s James has no age or relatives. Co-occurrence with Adam does not independently prove siblinghood; other namesakes are not excluded merely for missing fields.

No existing parent or sibling certainty changes from these notices. See the [complete qualified newspaper account](/research/subjects/newspapers-irish-1800-1850/#james-belfast-college) and maintained review `research/newspapers-1800-1850-continuation-2026-10-04.md`.
