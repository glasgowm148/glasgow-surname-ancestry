# Findings: [John B. Glasgow Jr. (Glasgow-1967)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1967)

## Current conclusion

John was born 15 April 1814 in County Tyrone, emigrated in 1839, lived first at Auburn, New York, and moved to Fayette Township, Hillsdale County, in August 1843. His parents were [John (Glasgow-1968)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1968) and [Jane (Unknown-764117)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Unknown-764117).

## Source findings

The [1888 Hillsdale biography, p. 688](https://archive.org/details/portraitbiograph00chpm/page/688/mode/1up) identifies three wives: [Elizabeth Eddy (Eddy-4623)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Eddy-4623), [Mary Sinclair (Sinclair-10598)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Sinclair-10598), and [Mary Ann Babbitt (Babbitt-880)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Babbitt-880). It names surviving daughter Mary J. Gould by the first marriage and Walter and Rose B. Clark by the second.
