== Biography ==

John Glasgow was identified in the ''History of Hillsdale County, Michigan'' as a son of [[Glasgow-1970|James Glasgow]] and James's wife, [[Mitchell-34480|Unknown Mitchell]]. That account says the elder couple emigrated from Scotland to northern Ireland about 1750 and settled in County Tyrone near Cookstown.<ref name="Hillsdale1879">''History of Hillsdale County, Michigan'' (Philadelphia: Everts & Abbott, 1879), [https://archive.org/details/bad0928.0001.001.umich.edu/page/263/mode/1up p. 263].</ref>

John married [[Unknown-764117|Jane]], whose maiden surname is unknown. Two 1888 biographies identify John and Jane as the parents of seven children. The sketch of [[Glasgow-1967|John B. Glasgow Jr.]] says the parents were born in Scotland and moved to County Tyrone after marriage.<ref name="HillsdaleJohn">''Portrait and Biographical Album of Hillsdale County, Michigan'' (Chicago: Chapman Brothers, 1888), [https://archive.org/details/portraitbiograph00chpm/page/688/mode/1up p. 688]; [https://www.migenweb.org/hillsdale/bios/bios-g.html transcription].</ref> The sketch of [[Glasgow-1969|William Glasgow]] instead says John and Jane were born in County Tyrone to Scottish parents.<ref name="HillsdaleWilliam">''Portrait and Biographical Album of Hillsdale County, Michigan'' (Chicago: Chapman Brothers, 1888), [https://archive.org/details/portraitbiograph00chpm/page/395/mode/1up p. 395]; [https://www.migenweb.org/hillsdale/bios/bios-g.html transcription].</ref>

The seven children were Robert, Mary A., [[Glasgow-1969|William]], [[Glasgow-1967|John B. Jr.]], Bessie, James and Margaret. William's sketch says John and Jane left Ireland in 1844 and came directly to Fayette Township near Jonesville; John Jr.'s sketch dates their arrival to 1848. John's death date has not been established.

== Research Notes ==

John's birth year of about 1778 comes from John Jr.'s 1888 sketch. His birthplace remains uncertain because the same volume gives conflicting accounts. The 1844 and 1848 immigration dates also remain competing published accounts.

John may be confused with [[Glasgow-2539|John Glasgow]] or [[Glasgow-2058|John Glasgow of Killycurragh]]. A John Glasgow recorded at Drummeen in the 1827 Lissan tithe applotment book is compatible with this man before migration, but the entry gives no relatives.

No compatible WikiTree profiles have been established for children Robert, Mary A., Bessie, James or Margaret.
