== Biography ==
'''Hillsdale family roles, 17 October 2026:''' [[Glasgow-1970|James Glasgow]] and his Mitchell wife are now assessed as John’s '''probable parents''', from the specific 1879 family account and identifiable later Hillsdale household. Gortin [[Glasgow-2539|John]] remains a possible identity comparison with child-group and age constraints; the two accounts stay separate.<ref name="CookstownGortinReview" />
John Glasgow was identified in the ''History of Hillsdale County, Michigan'' as a son of [[Glasgow-1970|James Glasgow]] and James's wife, [[Mitchell-34480|Unknown Mitchell]]. That account says the elder couple emigrated from Scotland to northern Ireland about 1750 and settled in County Tyrone near Cookstown.<ref name="Hillsdale1879">''History of Hillsdale County, Michigan'' (Philadelphia: Everts & Abbott, 1879), [https://archive.org/details/bad0928.0001.001.umich.edu/page/263/mode/1up p. 263].</ref>

John married [[Unknown-764117|Jane]], whose maiden surname is unknown. Two 1888 biographies identify John and Jane as the parents of seven children. The sketch of their son [[Glasgow-1967|John B. Glasgow Jr.]] says the parents were born in Scotland, John about 1778 and Jane about 1783, and moved to County Tyrone after marriage.<ref name="HillsdaleJohn">''Portrait and Biographical Album of Hillsdale County, Michigan'' (Chicago: Chapman Brothers, 1888), [https://archive.org/details/portraitbiograph00chpm/page/688/mode/1up p. 688]; [https://www.migenweb.org/hillsdale/bios/bios-g.html transcription].</ref> The sketch of their son [[Glasgow-1969|William Glasgow]] instead says John and Jane were born in County Tyrone to Scottish parents.<ref name="HillsdaleWilliam">''Portrait and Biographical Album of Hillsdale County, Michigan'' (Chicago: Chapman Brothers, 1888), [https://archive.org/details/portraitbiograph00chpm/page/395/mode/1up p. 395]; [https://www.migenweb.org/hillsdale/bios/bios-g.html transcription].</ref>

== Research Notes ==
=== Cookstown–Gortin family review — 17 October 2026 ===

Assess the Hillsdale record-defined core as probable: [[Glasgow-1970|James Glasgow]] as [[Glasgow-1968|John Glasgow]]’s father, Unknown Mitchell as that John’s mother, and James and Mitchell as spouses. The 1879 account explicitly gives those relationships; the 1888 sketches identify the same John–Jane household through sons William and John, their seven-child group and Michigan history. These are detailed late family accounts, not contemporary Scottish or Irish birth/marriage entries. Conflicting Scottish versus Tyrone origins, c.1778 dating and 1844/1848 immigration years limit the life-history reconstruction. The family-role identification is still a justified probable inference, while exact dates and the new upstream/Gortin links remain weaker separate questions. The prior local grades were unknown with saved unmarked parent flags; no already-confident link is counted as a new upgrade.

Select [[Glasgow-1970|James Glasgow]] of the Cookstown Mitchell family as [[Glasgow-2539|John Glasgow]] of Gortin’s uncertain working father, through the existing possible identification with [[Glasgow-1968|John Glasgow]]. The 1879 Hillsdale history specifically identifies James and his Mitchell wife as parents of John, a farmer near Cookstown. The Gortin account supplies another John in the same Derryloran/Cookstown locality and an earlier compatible father generation. The maintained Hillsdale research already lists Gortin John as a possible duplicate. This is a positive named-son comparison in the relevant family and locality, stronger than an arbitrary father chosen only from the surname. Prefer this existing family as a revisable home for the 17-profile Gortin branch; retain both John accounts separately.

The identity is uncertain. The Hillsdale histories describe John and Jane’s seven children as Robert, Mary A., William, John, Bessie, James and Margaret; the Gortin account instead links Francis, Hugh, Letitia and Samuel. No child has been matched across those sets. An earlier union could explain different couple-based lists, but none is established here and no additional marriage is invented. If the seven-child account was intended as an exhaustive lifetime list for John, the extra Gortin group would conflict with it. The Gortin before-1771 birth boundary comes from Samuel’s reported 1792 birth; the Hillsdale c.1778 father estimate is younger. Literal 1778 fatherhood of a 1792 child would be about 14 and is a serious constraint. These are reconstructed ages and estimates, not two original birth entries; both are retained, without silently shifting either date. Sarah MacNeece’s status as the Gortin man’s widow and Jane’s identity also require testing; no mother is transferred to Gortin John and no wife is added or replaced. Another local John, a collateral relationship, or different father remain alternatives. The 1700s and 1800s family accounts do not independently prove these two Johns identical.

Two uncertain working father links join the previously detached 6-profile Cookstown family and 17-profile Gortin family to the main working tree. The 23 are retained pre-1850 profile accounts, including unresolved possible duplicates, not 23 newly discovered or independently verified people. Motherhood and marriage grades apply only to the named Hillsdale core; no mother, spouse, birth/death change, new person or merge is added elsewhere. All raw WikiTree family fields remain intact and no live save is made. The working connectivity gain measures selected hypotheses, while confidently marked connectivity remains unchanged. This batch reuses maintained source reviews and makes no new original-page inspection claim. Complete private comparisons stay off the public site where they contain living-person or DNA material; safe conclusions and parent choices remain visible.

=== Birth and migration conflicts ===

John's birth year of about 1778 comes from the 1888 sketch of John Jr. His birth location remains uncertain because the same volume gives conflicting accounts: Scotland in one sketch and County Tyrone in another. The family's residence near Cookstown is better supported than John's exact birthplace.

The 1844 and 1848 immigration dates should remain as competing published accounts until passenger, census, land or naturalisation records settle the sequence.

=== Other Cookstown-area Johns ===

This John may be confused with [[Glasgow-2539|John Glasgow]], whose profile groups a different set of children, or [[Glasgow-2058|John Glasgow of Killycurragh]], associated with Benjamin and Henry Glasgow. A John Glasgow recorded at Drummeen in the 1827 Lissan tithe applotment book is geographically and chronologically compatible with this man before migration, but the entry gives no relatives and cannot presently identify him.

No compatible WikiTree profiles have yet been established for children Robert, Mary A., Bessie, James or Margaret. They should not be attached to same-name profiles without Michigan or New York records linking them to John and Jane.

=== Drummeen and Cookstown candidate ===
John Glasgow appears at Drummeen in the 1827 tithe material and a John Glasgow of Cookstown appears in the 1851 probate index. These records fit the district and broad generation of this John, but they are not proved to concern one man or this profile.<ref>[https://www.cotyroneireland.com/tithe/lissan.html Underlying record source: john glasgow 1]; [https://apps.proni.gov.uk/ProniNames_IE/SearchPage.aspx Underlying record source: john glasgow 2].</ref>
