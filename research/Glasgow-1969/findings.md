# Findings: [William Glasgow (Glasgow-1969)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1969)

## Current conclusion

William was born 11 February 1811 in County Tyrone, a son of [John (Glasgow-1968)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1968) and [Jane (Unknown-764117)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Unknown-764117). He married [Eliza Glasgow (Glasgow-4025)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4025) at Auburn on 4 August 1836 and moved to Fayette Township, Hillsdale County, in 1842.

## Source findings

The [1888 Hillsdale biography, p. 395](https://archive.org/details/portraitbiograph00chpm/page/395/mode/1up) identifies William's seven-sibling family, Eliza's Glasgow birth surname, the marriage, migration, and their four children. It explicitly describes William's and Eliza's families as distinct despite the shared surname.

## Relationship assessment

The parent, spouse and four child relationships now attached on WikiTree agree with the 1888 account. Eliza's two immigrant brothers remain unidentified; [Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-1171)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1171) is a plausible candidate for one brother but is not proved.
