The ''History of Hillsdale County, Michigan'' identifies James Glasgow as a Scotsman whose wife “was a Mitchell.” The couple emigrated from Scotland to northern Ireland about 1750 and settled in County Tyrone near Cookstown.<ref name="Hillsdale1879">''History of Hillsdale County, Michigan'' (Philadelphia: Everts & Abbott, 1879), [https://archive.org/details/bad0928.0001.001.umich.edu/page/263/mode/1up p. 263].</ref> Her given name and the couple's exact birth, marriage and death dates are not stated in the cited account.

Their son John is represented by [[Glasgow-1968|John Glasgow]]. James's wife is represented by [[Mitchell-34480|Unknown Mitchell]].

A Protestant householder named James Glasgow was recorded at Corvanaghan in Kildress parish in 1766.<ref name="Kildress1766">National Archives of Ireland, Parliamentary Return 667, 1766 religious census for Kildress, IHP/1/667, [https://virtualtreasury.ie/item/NAI-IHP-1-667 Virtual Treasury catalogue and original return].</ref> That man is represented by [[Glasgow-2988|Glasgow-3137]]. The shared name, approximate period and County Tyrone setting make him a candidate, but neither the 1766 return nor the Hillsdale account identifies the two men as the same person. The profiles should remain separate unless a record connects the Mitchell family or John to Corvanaghan.


=== Descendant reconstruction ===

The Hillsdale County account continues the family beyond James and his Mitchell wife. Their son [[Glasgow-1968|John Glasgow]] was a farmer near Cookstown who raised seven children, four sons and three daughters. John's second son [[Glasgow-1969|William Glasgow]] was about twenty-one when he went to America in 1833. William married [[Glasgow-4025|Eliza Glasgow]] on 4 August 1836. William and his brother [[Glasgow-1967|John B. Glasgow Jr.]], who was born 15 April 1814, moved to Hillsdale County, Michigan, in 1842. Their father later joined the family there and died at an advanced age.<ref name="Hillsdale1879" />

This produces a coherent documented lineage from James and his Mitchell wife through their son John to the Michigan descendants. The account does not say that John was James's only child.

=== Comparison with other Cookstown-area James Glasgows ===

James is of the right generation and locality to be compared with [[Glasgow-2988|the 1766 Corvanaghan householder]] and [[Glasgow-1024|the reported James Glasgow of Moneymore]]. No record presently identifies any two of those profiles as the same person.

A merger with Glasgow-1024 would require an explanation for the differing migration chronology. Traditions concerning that family place Arthur's mother and elder brothers in America by 1766 or Robert there by 1769, while this family account preserves a son and later descendants near Cookstown. Return migration, remarriage or an error in one of the traditions is possible but has not been demonstrated.

The Corvanaghan return does not name a wife or children. It therefore cannot presently establish that Glasgow-2988 was this Mitchell-family progenitor.
