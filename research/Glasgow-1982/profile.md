== Biography ==

'''Wilson Crossier Glasgow''' was born about 1772, calculated from his gravestone age. His present Irish birthplace has not been verified by an inspected birth or baptism record.

Wilson was a son of [[Glasgow-1144|James Glasgow]] and [[Crosier-512|Mary Crosier]]. James's 1775 will named Wilson among his children.<ref name="JamesWill">Newberry District, South Carolina, will of James Glasgow, dated 4 June 1775; [https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184247id_/http://www.lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_JGlasgowWill.html transcription].</ref>

In 1800 Wilson headed a household in Newberry District, South Carolina.<ref name="Census1800">"United States Census, 1800," FamilySearch ([https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XHRC-7BB record]), Wilson Glasgow, Newberry District, South Carolina; citing NARA microfilm publication M32, roll 50, p. 75.</ref>

Wilson married [[McCracken-3711|Mary McCracken]]. Their reciprocal gravestone inscriptions identify them as husband and wife.<ref name="GildersCreek">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/gilders_creek_cem.htm Gilders Creek Cemetery survey], Newberry County, South Carolina, entries for Wilson and Mary Glasgow.</ref>

Wilson died on 11 September 1817, aged about 45, and was buried at Gilders Creek Cemetery in Newberry County.<ref name="GildersCreek" /><ref name="WilsonFindAGrave">{{FindAGrave|33531795}}, memorial for Wilson Crossier Glasgow.</ref> John Glasgow administered his estate.<ref name="EstateIndex">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/estates1785-1949/22.htm Newberry County estate index], Wilson Glasgow, 1817, estate 651, box 28, package 58.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Children ===

Mary's 1837 will gave their daughter Ruth one-seventh of her property, daughter Mariah one-sixth of the remainder, and the residue to her “other five children.” Their son Hiram was appointed trustee for Mariah. The wording establishes seven children in total.<ref name="MaryWill">Newberry District, South Carolina, Will Book N, p. 1, will of Mary Glasgow, widow of Wilson Glasgow, dated 28 November 1837 and proved 22 October 1838; [https://web.archive.org/web/20101124215757id_/http://lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_WilsonGlasgowWife.html archived transcription]. The displayed recording date of July 1837 precedes execution and is evidently a transcription error.</ref>

The index to Wilson's real-estate packet lists Mary with Robert, Ruth, Naomi, Matilda, Hiram, Almina and Maria. This is the widow plus seven apparent children and matches the will's child count, although the original packet is still needed for explicit kin terms.<ref name="RealEstate">Newberry County, South Carolina, real-estate packet 1-13, estate of Wilson Glasgow; [https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/realestate.html index], entries for Wilson Glasgow and Mary, Robert, Ruth, Naomi, Matilda, Hiram, Almina and Maria.</ref>

An archived Glasgow family reconstruction gives further background on the Kilwaughter and Newberry branches.<ref name="FamilyReconstruction">Donna Jean Glasgow, [https://web.archive.org/web/20101124183914/http:/lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Kilwaughter/TheirStory.html “GLASGOWS from Kilwaughter Parish and Larne”], archived family reconstruction. Its family claims should be checked against the records cited on the individual profiles.</ref>

Ruth, Mariah and Hiram are directly identified as Mary's children. Robert, Naomi, Matilda and Almina complete the seven-name group in Wilson's estate, exactly matching Mary's stated child count. The original real-estate packet should still be inspected before describing its wording as direct proof of each relationship.

[[Glasgow-2738|James J Glasgow]] was proposed elsewhere as another son. He is not included in the matching seven-child estate group and was not attached here. The same FamilySearch family also includes Fleming Bates Glasgow, reportedly born in 1823, six years after Wilson's death. That online child list combines more than one family.
