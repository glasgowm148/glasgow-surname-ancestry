== Biography ==

{{One Name Study|name=Glasgow}}

'''Robert Glasgow''' was born about 1762 or 1763 in Ireland. His Gilders Creek gravestone records that he died on 17 January 1835 aged 72 years and three months.<ref name="Gilders">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/gilders_creek_cem.htm "Gilders Creek Cemetery"], Newberry County, South Carolina, transcriptions for Robert, Elizabeth and their son Robert (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

Robert was the oldest son of [[Glasgow-1144|James Glasgow]] and [[Crosier-512|Mary Crosier]]. James's will gave Robert 100 acres including the house in which James lived.<ref name="JamesWill">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124215937id_/http://lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_JGlasgowWill.html "Will of James Glasgow"], transcription of Newberry Will Book A, p. 194; will dated 17 October 1775 and proved 13 July 1793 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

In a sworn answer dated 2 June 1817, Robert called James his father, Mary his mother, Rachel Wilson his paternal grandmother and Ann Glasgow his father's sister. He also described himself as James's oldest son.<ref name="Reply">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184247id_/http://www.lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_Reply.html "Reply of Robert Glasgow"], transcription of Robert Glasgow's sworn answer, 2 June 1817 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref> His amended answer explained that Rachel was one of six sisters of Archibald Wilson.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20101104165918id_/http://lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_Answer.html "Amended answer of Robert Glasgow"], transcription dated 18 June 1818 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

Robert married [[Kinard-572|Elizabeth Kinard]]. Their shared gravestone calls Elizabeth Robert's wife and records “their son” Robert, who died on 12 December 1813 aged five.<ref name="Gilders" />

In 1800 Robert headed a Newberry household containing one boy aged 10–15, two girls aged 10–15, one girl under ten, an adult woman aged 26–44 and a woman aged 45 or older.<ref name="Census1800">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/1800nbycensusG.htm "1800 Newberry District census"], Robert Glasgow household (accessed 12 August 2026); citing NARA microfilm publication M32, roll 50.</ref>

The same return is indexed by FamilySearch.<ref name="FamilySearch1800">"United States Census, 1800," database with images, FamilySearch ([https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XHRC-7BF record XHRC-7BF]), Robert Glasgow, Newberry District, South Carolina; citing p. 75, NARA M32, roll 50 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

Elizabeth died on 10 November 1823 aged 60. Robert died on 17 January 1835. Zaccheus Wright administered Robert's estate.<ref name="Gilders" /><ref name="Estate">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/estates1785-1949/22.htm "Probate court general index to estates, 1785–1949"], Robert Glasgow, estate 1055, box 43, package 98 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Children ===

The 1800 household shows that Robert and Elizabeth's family was larger than the presently documented son Robert.

[[Glasgow-2439|James Glasgow]], born about 1787, is a probable son. His age fits the only boy aged 10–15 in Robert's 1800 household, but the census does not name him and no direct relationship record has yet been inspected.

[[Glasgow-3226|Mary Caroline Glasgow]], born 17 September 1785 and wife of [[Wright-73441|Zaccheus Wright]], is a probable daughter. She fits one of the two girls aged 10–15 in 1800. A Wright family account identifies her as a daughter of Robert Glasgow and Elizabeth Kinard, and her husband administered Robert's estate.<ref>[https://www.geocities.ws/richmond01_2000/wright.html "Family: Wright"], account of Zaccheus Wright and Mary Glasgow (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref><ref name="Estate" /> The relationship should remain uncertain until Robert's estate packet or another direct record names her.

The census requires two further girls in Robert's household: another aged 10–15 and one under ten. They may have been daughters, but their identities have not been established.

[[Glasgow-3982|Robert Glasgow]], born about 1808 and died in 1813, is directly proved as a son by the shared gravestone.<ref name="Gilders" /> [[Glasgow-2769|Robert Glasgow]] of Tennessee was previously attached as another son, but he was already alive while the gravestone-proved child lived and no direct record supports that former attachment.

No other son has been identified. Robert's estate packet, estate 1055, box 43, package 98, is the most likely source for a complete list of heirs.

An older compiled family page stated that Robert was born in Ireland.<ref>[http://web.bledsoe.net/~danshell/fam00485.htm Glasgow family page], formerly cited on this profile; link retained although the page is no longer consistently available.</ref> This agrees with the profile's broad birthplace but is not a contemporary birth record.
