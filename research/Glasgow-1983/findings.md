# Findings: [Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-1983)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1983)

## Current conclusion

Robert's parentage is unusually well supported. James Glasgow's will calls him
a son, and Robert's 1817 sworn answer calls James his father, Mary his mother,
Rachel Wilson his paternal grandmother, Ann Glasgow his father's sister, and
Robert himself James's oldest son.

Elizabeth was Robert's wife. Their shared Gilders Creek stone directly identifies
their son Robert, born about 1808 and dead in 1813. That child is
[Glasgow-3982](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3982), not the Tennessee
Robert [Glasgow-2769](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2769).

Robert and Elizabeth had a larger family than WikiTree currently shows. The
1800 census records one boy aged 10–15, two girls aged 10–15 and one girl under
10 in their household. The boy fits James (about 1787–1831). Mary Caroline
Glasgow (1785–1841), wife of Zaccheus Wright, is a strong daughter candidate:
she fits one of the two older girls, a Wright family account calls her the
daughter of Robert and Elizabeth Kinard, and Zaccheus administered Robert's
estate in 1835. The other two daughters remain unidentified.

No further son is presently indicated. In 1800 the only boy in Robert's
household was the age-compatible James; Robert (1808–1813) was born later.
The 1835 estate packet is the decisive uninspected source for a complete heir
list.
