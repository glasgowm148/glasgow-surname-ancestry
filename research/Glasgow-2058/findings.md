### Military surgeons J. and John Glasco, 1816–1839

An appointment dated 8 February 1816 names Assistant Surgeon J. Glasco of the 59th Foot as surgeon. An October 1822 notice places Surgeon John Glasco, from half-pay of the 59th, in the 60th Foot. A June 1825 Hospital Staff notice names Staff-Surgeon John Glasco for brevet deputy inspector; in 1826 a replacement succeeded the promoted 60th Foot surgeon Glasco, and a separate August notice names John Glasco, senior, retiring upon half-pay. In December 1836 Staff-Surgeon John Glasco, recently employed at Grenada, was appointed surgeon of the Liverpool Recruiting District. A Liverpool subscription in 1838 names Staff-Surgeon Glasco, and a September 1839 notice names John Glasco retiring upon half-pay. The senior retiree and later staff surgeon may be different men.
