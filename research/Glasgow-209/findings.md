## Source findings — father's will, 29 March 1827

Hugh Glasgo of Deer Township, Allegheny County, Pennsylvania (`Glasgow-540`), signed his will on 29 March 1827; it was proved on 25 April 1831 ([Allegheny County Will Book 3, no. 252, pp. 492–493](https://www.ancestry.com/imageviewer/collections/8802/images/005537856_00276?pid=1733435); original loose packet FamilySearch DGS 5538049, no. 252). The will says "I leave to my son John Glasgo one cow", allows "John to get his at my decease", and appoints "my sons John and Robert Glasgo executors". Seen as the recorded will-book image (the clerk's copy); the transcription at `../Glasgow-540/sources/hugh_glasgow_will_1827_transcription.md` was made from that image and checked against thumbnails of the original loose packet.

The will proves that Hugh called a John his son and made him an executor in 1827. It gives John no age or residence. That this profile (born 28 April 1780) is the will's John is inference: it rests on the WikiTree family placement and on W. M. Glasgow's late *History of the Glasgow Family*, p. 3, which calls Hugh's eldest son John, born 28 April 1780. No record in this repository yet ties the will's John to this profile's dates.
