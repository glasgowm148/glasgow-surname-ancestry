# Findings: William George Glasgow (Glasgow-2135)

## Current conclusion

The Belfast News-Letter of 30 January 1896 records W. G. Glasgow, B.A., a student of Tyrone Presbytery, accepting the unanimous call from First Dromara. The meeting was held at Cookstown on 28 January. The live profile, checked 4 October 2026, identifies Rev. William George Glasgow as the Dromara minister marrying in December 1896. Initials, qualification, church and chronology strongly identify the newspaper subject with this profile. This adds a career event, not a parentage finding.
