# Findings: [John Glasgow (Glasgow-2291)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2291)

## Current conclusion

This profile is a duplicate of
[John Glasgow (Glasgow-1146)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1146).
Both represent the John Glasgow born about 1756 who died in Newberry on
11 December 1826. Glasgow-2291 cites the Gilders Creek memorial for that exact
identity and has no parents, spouse or children attached. It should be merged
into the better-developed Glasgow-1146 profile.

The merge does not reveal another son. Absolom remains John's only plausible
attached son, and that relationship is still awaiting direct evidence.
