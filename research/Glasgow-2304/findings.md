# Findings: Archibald Glasgow (Glasgow-2304)

Last updated: 2026-08-12

## Current conclusion

James Glasgow's will and the 1817 equity case strongly support Archibald as a son
of James and Mary. Gilders Creek gravestones name three children of Archibald and
Mary: James `Glasgow-3981` (1806-1824), Neomi `Glasgow-3979` (1797-1831), and
Rachel `Glasgow-3980` (1801-1855), wife of Isaac Keller. Their parentage is direct
gravestone evidence rather than merely inherited tree structure.

The 1818 estate index names Mary Glasgow as administrator of Archibald's estate,
which strongly corroborates the household but does not itself print “wife.”
Sarah Glasgow-3042's current father link is tree-only; her stone names her spouse,
not her parents.

Robert `Glasgow-2769`, later of Tennessee, is now the leading named candidate for
an additional son. Archibald's 1800 Newberry household contained exactly one male
under ten. None of the three gravestone-proved children fills that slot: Neomi was
female, Rachel was born in 1801 and James in 1806. Robert's combined census
evidence places his birth about 1790, within the 1800 category if he was born after
the census date or if his later reported age was approximate. This is a genuine
household fit, not proof of parentage.

The fit has one important weakness. The only adult female in Archibald's 1800
household was aged 16–25. If she was Mary and Robert was born close to 1790, she
would have been at the extreme young end—or too young—to be his mother. A slightly
later birth for Robert, a previous wife, a step-relationship, an age error, or a
different identity for the boy could explain the return. Archibald should therefore
be treated as a possible father, not attached as certain.

## Possible son: Robert Glasgow-2769

```text
James Glasgow-1144 and Mary Crosier-512
└── Archibald Glasgow-2304
    ├── ? Sarah Glasgow-3042, born about 1793
    ├── Neomi Glasgow-3979, born about 1797
    ├── Rachel Glasgow-3980, born 1801
    ├── James Glasgow-3981, born 1806
    └── ? Robert Glasgow-2769, born about 1790, later Tennessee
        └── ? James J Glasgow-2738, born about 1809
            └── tested direct-male descendant, kit 1002232 (R-FT20271)
```

James `Glasgow-3981` is a proved son, but he was born in 1806 and therefore
cannot be the male under ten in Archibald's 1800 household. Neomi and possibly
Sarah can account for two of the four girls; Rachel and James were not yet born.
The census therefore indicates that the currently identified child group is
incomplete even without accepting Robert as the missing boy.

Alexander `Glasgow-951`'s tested line is also basal R-FT20271 and is the closest
reported Y-111 match to kit 1002232 at genetic distance 3. That supports a paternal
relationship between the two tested lines, but it cannot choose Archibald as
Robert's father. If the proposed chain is correct, Alexander's Antrim line and
Archibald's South Carolina line must share an ancestor above Archibald, probably in
an earlier Irish generation. The repeated McCracken marriages in the South Carolina
family and Thomas `Glasgow-987`'s Antrim family are a useful network clue, not
evidence of paternal descent.

Priority: inspect estate 647 and related guardianship 646 before creating the
mother profile or altering Sarah's parentage. The original 1810 census image should
also be read for a male aged 10–15 or 16–25. Archibald's estate packet is the best
chance of naming Robert directly or excluding him from a complete heir group.
