== Biography ==

'''Alexander Glasgow''' was born about 1816 in County Antrim, Ireland. The 1851
census gave his birthplace as Runflea, County Antrim, and his age as thirty-
five.<ref name="Glenleslie1851">[https://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Dunaghy/Glenleslie/35/ 1851 Census of Ireland], Glenleslie house 35, Dunaghy, County Antrim.</ref>

Alexander married [[Martin-93219|Mary Anne Martin]] on 6 April 1847 at Clogh.
The original register describes him as a full-age bachelor and labourer living
at Glenleslie. His father was '''John Glasgow, labourer'''. Mary Anne was a
full-age spinster and daughter of John Martin, labourer. Peter Richmond and John
Montgomery witnessed the marriage.<ref name="Marriage1847">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1847/09322/5375567.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Alexander Glasgow and Mary Anne Martin, 6 April 1847, Clogh, entry 31; derivative [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QGVD-P9CS FamilySearch index entry].</ref>

In 1851 Alexander was an agricultural labourer at Glenleslie. Mary Anne, aged
twenty-seven and born in County Armagh, worked as a linen weaver. Their son
[[Glasgow-2826|John Glasgow]] was two years old and born in County Antrim.<ref name="Glenleslie1851" />

== Research Notes ==

=== Father ===

Alexander's marriage proves that his father was named '''John Glasgow''' and
worked as a labourer. No inspected record gives the older John's age, wife or
residence.

[[Glasgow-867|John Glasgow of Lisnagaver]] is currently attached as Alexander's
father. However, that John's family gravestone places his birth about 1816 or
1817, while the conflicting 1851 return places it about 1801. Neither date
permits him to be the father of Alexander, born about 1816. The relationship is
retained only as uncertain until the older John can be identified.<ref name="RasharkinStone">[https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/RasharkinP/graveyard.html Pauline O'Keeffe and Helen Evans, ''Rasharkin Second Presbyterian Church headstones''], transcription made August 2004, Glasgo memorial erected by James Garvin Glasgo.</ref>

[[Glasgow-1022|John Glasgow of Glenleslie]] is old enough to be the father named
in 1847, but he was recorded as a farmer rather than a labourer and no record
calls Alexander his son. He remains a possible local candidate rather than an
identified parent.

The [https://www.myheritage.com/research/collection-20882/ireland-marriages-1845-1958 MyHeritage Ireland marriage index] and its [https://www.myheritage.com/research/record-20882-2493853/alexander-glasgow-in-ireland-marriages Alexander Glasgow entry] are retained as derivative finding aids. The original register, rather than the index's list of possible spouses, identifies Mary Anne Martin as Alexander's wife.
