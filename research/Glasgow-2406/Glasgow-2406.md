== Biography ==

'''James Garvin Glasgow''' was born about 1851 at Lisnagaver, County Antrim,
Ireland. His family gravestone records that he died in 1918 aged sixty-seven,
and the 1901 census reported his age as forty-nine.<ref name="RasharkinStone">[https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/RasharkinP/graveyard.html Pauline O'Keeffe and Helen Evans, ''Rasharkin Second Presbyterian Church headstones''], transcription made August 2004, Glasgo memorial erected by James Garvin Glasgo.</ref><ref name="Census1901">[http://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1901/Antrim/Killoquin_Lower/Lisnagaver/946378/ 1901 Census of Ireland], Lisnagaver house 33, Killoquin Lower, County Antrim.</ref>

James was the son of [[Glasgow-867|John Glasgow]], a farmer, and
[[Garvin-1831|Margaret Garvin]]. His 1886 marriage names his father, while the
memorial erected by James names both parents.<ref name="Marriage1886">[https://civilrecords.irishgenealogy.ie/churchrecords/images/marriage_returns/marriages_1886/10840/5957283.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], James Garvin Glasgow and Eliza Ann Aiken, 1 July 1886, Clough, entry 62.</ref><ref name="RasharkinStone" />

James married [[Aiken-2081|Eliza Ann Aiken]] on 1 July 1886 at Clough. He was a
full-age bachelor farmer of Lisnagaver; she was a full-age spinster and daughter
of William Aiken, farmer. Robert Aiken and Matilda Bell witnessed the
marriage.<ref name="Marriage1886" />

The family gravestone records their infant son '''John Garvin'''. James and Eliza
were the only people in their household in 1901, so the child had died before
that census.<ref name="RasharkinStone" /><ref name="Census1901" />

James and Eliza Ann were again recorded at Lisnagaver in the 1911 census.<ref name="Census1911">[http://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1911/Antrim/Killoquin/Lisnagaver/130063/ 1911 Census of Ireland], Lisnagaver house 22, Killoquin, County Antrim.</ref>

James died at Lisnagaver on 14 February 1918 aged sixty-seven. He was a married
farmer and had suffered acute pneumonia for six days. Eliza Ann Glasgow, widow,
was present at the death.<ref name="Death1918">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/deaths_returns/deaths_1918/05197/4435754.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil death register], James Garvin Glasgow, 14 February 1918, Dervock registrar's district, entry 165.</ref>

The gravestone records Eliza Ann's death on 16 June 1933 aged eighty-four.<ref name="RasharkinStone" />

== Research Notes ==

The family memorial uses the spelling '''Glasgo''' and calls James's wife
'''Elizabeth Ann'''. The civil marriage, censuses and death registration use
'''Glasgow''' and the shorter form '''Eliza Ann'''. The matching residence,
husband and dates identify the same couple.
