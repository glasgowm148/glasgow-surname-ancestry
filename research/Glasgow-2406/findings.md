# Findings: Glasgow-2406

Last updated: 2026-08-13

## Current conclusion

James Garvin Glasgow's parentage is now proved. His 1886 marriage names his
father as John Glasgow, farmer, while the family gravestone erected by James
names both his father John Glasgo and mother Margaret Garvin. The same memorial
records an infant son, John Garvin. James died at Lisnagaver on 14 February
1918; his widow Eliza Ann registered the death.
