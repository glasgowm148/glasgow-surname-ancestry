# Findings: Glasgow-2406

Last updated: 2026-09-17

## Current conclusion

**17 September 2026 — Craigavole deed.** A 14 June 1895 Registry of Deeds
assignment (registered 19 June 1895) records **Fanny Garvan, spinster of
Craigavole, parish Desertoghill, County Londonderry**, assigning her yearly
tenancy there (13a 2r 33p, held from the Mercers' Company at £6 15s) to **James
Garvan Glasgow, farmer of Lisnagaver, County Antrim**, "in consideration of the
natural love and affection of the said Fanny Garvan to the said James Garvan
Glasgow" and his covenants; witnesses Robert Liddle, solicitor of Kilrea, and
Hugh Scott, farmer of Craigavole. A deed made for natural love and affection
normally records a close family bond, but the memorial does not name the
relationship, so Fanny's kinship to James (sister? aunt? cousin? via his mother
Margaret Garvin?) remains unproved. Craigavole sits in Desertoghill parish,
about 15 km from Lisnagaver across the Bann; this is the first documentary link
between James Garvan Glasgow and a Desertoghill Garvan holding. The reading
rests on a saved FamilySearch full-text machine transcription, not yet a
verified image reading, and contains obvious OCR errors ("Lisnagarver",
signature-block "Gavan", a clause reading "the said Jane Garvan" where Fanny is
expected). Source: [FamilySearch Registry of Deeds film 008138538, image 325
of 653](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSK3-34C9-D) (saved
transcription: `sources/deeds-fulltext-2026-09-17-continuation.json`). Next
records: a visual reading of the memorial at that image; Garvan entries in the
Mercers' Company estate records for Craigavole; any Fanny Garvan birth, census
or death record tying her to James Garvin, father of Margaret Garvin of
Lisnagaver.

James Garvin Glasgow's parentage is now proved. His 1886 marriage names his
father as John Glasgow, farmer, while the family gravestone erected by James
names both his father John Glasgo and mother Margaret Garvin. The same memorial
records an infant son, John Garvin. James died at Lisnagaver on 14 February
1918; his widow Eliza Ann registered the death.
