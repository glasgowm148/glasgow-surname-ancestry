# Findings: Glasgow-2407

Last updated: 2026-10-07

## Current conclusion

John was a bachelor farmer at Cross, Ballyclug, when he married Rachel Grey in
1865. The original certificate names his father as John Glasgow, farmer. His
1874 death return gives age sixty-five, occupation farmer and residence Cross;
Michael Glasgow of Cross was present at death.

He is not the forty-year-old nephew in John `Glasgow-1022`'s 1851 household,
because that man was recorded as married since 1839. John `Glasgow-1022` remains
one possible father, but John `Glasgow-1657`, who was explicitly of Cross and
died in 1836, is geographically stronger. Leave the parent field blank until a
record distinguishes them.
