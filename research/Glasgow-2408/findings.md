# James Glasgow (Glasgow-2408): Ballymena birth, 1871

Last updated: 2026-09-23

## Current conclusion

[James Glasgow (Glasgow-2408)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2408) was born on **16 February 1871** in Ballymena. His profile cites the separate birth registration `U/1871/24/1004/3/478` and the original civil return. He is distinct from the James born on **20 August 1869**, `U/1869/20/1004/5/338`, now [Glasgow-4342](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4342).
