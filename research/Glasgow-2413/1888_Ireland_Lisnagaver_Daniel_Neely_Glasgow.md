## Paste-ready biography

Daniel Neely Glasgow was a farmer of Lisnagaver when he married Mary Jane Boyd at Boveedy First Presbyterian Church on 28 June 1888. The original civil marriage return names his father as Thomas Glasgow, farmer.<ref>[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/view/?record_id=cima-2455742 IrishGenealogy.ie index] and [https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1888/10763/5925382.pdf original marriage return], 28 June 1888.</ref>

His uncle [[Glasgow-3896|Daniel Glasgow]] had previously named “my nephew Daniel Neely Glasgow” and “my late brother Thomas Glasgow” in his 1871 will. Together the will and marriage prove the uncle-nephew and father-son links.

No birth or death record has been identified. Do not use the marriage date as a birth estimate more precise than “before 1870.”
