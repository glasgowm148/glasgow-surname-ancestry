## Current conclusion

The 5 September master review confirms that Daniel Neely's father link to
Thomas `Glasgow-987` is proved by his original 1888 marriage. Daniel
`Glasgow-3896`'s will independently makes him Thomas's son by calling Daniel
Neely a nephew and Thomas the testator's late brother. This does not identify

The existing Glasgow-2413 profile represents Daniel Neely Glasgow. His original
28 June 1888 marriage return names Thomas Glasgow, farmer, as his father and
Lisnagaver as Daniel's residence. Daniel Glasgow's 1871 will independently
calls Daniel Neely his nephew and Thomas his late brother. The structured
marriage date shown as 22 June conflicts with the original return and should be
corrected to 28 June 1888.

The original birth returns for Daniel's twin daughters add a significant
emigration clue. Both say that Daniel was a **labourer residing in New York**
when Sarah Taylor Glasgow and Elizabeth Boyd Glasgow were born at Tamlaght in
September 1889. An Irish-born cemetery labourer named Daniel Glascow who died
at Troy, New York, on 12 February 1931 is therefore a strong candidate for the
same man. The identity is not yet proved. A bounded free search found no usable
census schedule, obituary or newspaper result supplying his wife, parents,
precise Irish origin or other identifying details. The cemetery transcription
also conflicts with the state index over whether he died in 1913 or 1931. The
death certificate has not been purchased or inspected.
