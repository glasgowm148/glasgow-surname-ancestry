# Findings: [Sarah Taylor Glasgow (Glasgow-2414)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2414)

Last updated: 2026-09-12

## Current conclusion

The original birth return records Sarah Taylor Glasgow on **8 September 1889**
at Tamlaght, daughter of Daniel Glasgow and Mary Jane Glasgow formerly Boyd.
The imported 9 September date belongs to the next entry for Elizabeth Boyd
Glasgow. The 1901 Tamlaght household identifies Sarah and Elizabeth as nieces
of Hugh S. Boyd, supporting the maternal family link.
