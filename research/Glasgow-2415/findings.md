# Findings: [Elizabeth Boyd Glasgow (Glasgow-2415)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2415)

Last updated: 2026-09-12

## Current conclusion

The original birth return records Elizabeth Boyd Glasgow on **9 September
1889** at Tamlaght, daughter of Daniel Glasgow and Mary Jane Glasgow formerly
Boyd. The 1901 census records Lizzie B. Glasgow as a niece of Hugh S. Boyd, and
the Drimbolg memorial names Elizabeth Boyd Glasgow as niece of Hugh Scott and
Joseph A. Boyd and records her death on **4 January 1933**, aged 43. The combined
records strongly identify the memorial woman with the registered child.
