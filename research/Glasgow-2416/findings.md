## Current conclusion

This Irish-born Daniel Glasgow/Glascow is a **strong but unproved candidate**
for Daniel Neely Glasgow `Glasgow-2413`. The original September 1889 birth
returns for Daniel Neely's twins describe their father Daniel as a labourer
residing in New York. The Troy man was Irish-born, worked for Albany Rural
Cemetery for over forty years, and the profile's cited 1920 census reports
immigration in 1889. The chronology, place, and occupation align unusually
well, but no inspected US record yet names his Irish wife or parents.

The official New York State index confirms the death as **12 February 1931**
at Troy, under the spelling `Glascow`, certificate **9941**. An Albany Rural
Cemetery transcription gives 1913; this conflicts with the state index and is
best treated as a transposition pending inspection of the original burial card.

## Identification test

The next record should be Troy death certificate **9941**. The official form
and a field-by-field order guide are preserved locally. A parent entry for
Thomas Glasgow and Mary Ann McCracken, or spouse entry for Mary Jane Boyd,
would prove the identification. A conflicting spouse, parents, or detailed
Irish birthplace would exclude it. Also inspect the 1910 and 1920 original
census schedules and seek naturalisation or an obituary.
