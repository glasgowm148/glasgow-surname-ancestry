## Identity assessment — later life as a Belfast teacher, recorded 16 July 2026

- The 1901 Inishrush census lists Henry, 12, born in America, son of Adam and Eliza (`../Glasgow-3905/sources/census_1901_glasgow_derry.json`).
- A nationwide [1911 census](https://nationalarchives.ie/collections/search-the-census/) search for Henry Glasgow returned three results; only one fits the age, American birth and occupation: Henry Glasgow, 22, Church of Ireland, single, national-school teacher, boarding at 44 York Road, Belfast (Form A saved as `../Glasgow-3905/sources/census_1911_henry_glasgow_form_a.pdf`, seen as the original form). The form gives no middle name or parent. The other two were older Irish-born farmers or labourers.
- Belfast street directories for 1932, 1939, 1943, 1947 and 1951 list "Glasgow, H. D., teacher" at 62 Deerpark Road. The [PRONI will calendar](https://apps.proni.gov.uk/willscalendar_ie/willssearch.aspx) records Henry Downing Glasgow of 62 Deerpark Road, retired teacher, who died on 20 June 1959.

The source grades the identification of the 1911 teacher with this Henry as high-confidence (probable), on age, American birth and occupation. The link from the 1911 teacher to H. D. Glasgow of Deerpark Road is inference from name and occupation; the 1911 and 1932 addresses differ. A [Tamlaght vestry account of 2 August 1893](https://www.oneirishrover.com/tamlaght-vestry/) lists "Henry Adam Glasgow" in Sunday-school class 5, probably this child, but the middle name conflicts with Downing and the two must not be harmonised. No child of Henry has been identified.
