# Findings: [Adam Glasgow (Glasgow-2419)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2419)

Last updated: 2026-10-07

## Current conclusion

The project tree defines this later Adam, about 1853–1916, with wife Eliza and
American-born sons [Adam D. Glasgow (Glasgow-2420)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2420)
and [Henry Glasgow (Glasgow-2418)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2418).
No inspected record connects this household to either James of Inishrush or to
elder [Adam Glasgow (Glasgow-3902)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3902).

## Identity assessment — the plot 29 successor of 1893, recorded 16 July 2026

The Inishrush valuation revision books (PRONI [Val12B](https://apps.proni.gov.uk/Val12B/Search.aspx); original images saved under `../Glasgow-3905/sources/`) show plot 29 (19a 2r 10p) passing from James Glasgow to Rose Anne (annotation `84`) and then to Adam (annotation `93`) in `VAL/12/B/34/10D`; Adam as sole occupier in the 1899–1910 book `10E`; and Adam still occupier in the 1911–29 book `10F` (20a 1r 15p) under the Earl of Strafford, Lord Deramore and Lady Louisa Trench, with no `in fee` note. The 1901 and 1911 censuses place this Adam, a farmer, at Inishrush, so the plot-29 Adam of 1893–1910 is probably this man (same townland, occupation and period; the books give no age). After his death in December 1916 the `10F` entry may refer to his son Adam D. (`Glasgow-2420`), since revision books lag.

This identifies the occupier, not his parents: no record yet calls this Adam a son of James or of Rose Anne, and descent must not be inferred from the succession.

Recorded in `research/Glasgow-3905/agent_land_civil.md` (16 July 2026); carried into this file on 7 October 2026.
