## Source findings — parentage and death, recorded 16 July 2026

- The [1901 and 1911 Irish censuses](https://nationalarchives.ie/collections/search-the-census/) (saved data `../Glasgow-3905/sources/census_1901_glasgow_derry.json` and `census_1911_glasgow_inishrush.json`) list Adam, 22, in 1901, and Adam D., 28, unmarried, born in Philadelphia, in 1911, as a son in the Inishrush household of Adam Glasgow (`Glasgow-2419`) and Eliza. The civil death return of the elder Adam in 1916 (`../Glasgow-3905/sources/adam_glasgow_death_1916.pdf`) names Adam D. Glasgow of Inishrush, the deceased's son, as present. Together these prove Adam D.'s parents. Seen as census transcriptions and a civil register image.
- The [PRONI will calendar](https://apps.proni.gov.uk/willscalendar_ie/willssearch.aspx) records Adam Glasgow of Innisrush, Portglenone, farmer, who died on 15 June 1957 (probate Belfast, 30 September). That this is Adam D. is inference from name, place and generation; the abstract does not say so. The grantee's relationship is not stated, so no son or later male-line descendant is proved.
