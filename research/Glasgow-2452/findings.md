# Findings: Johne Glasgow (Glasgow-2452), baptised at Mid Calder in 1648

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

The reported Mid Calder OPR `694/10/94` entry records Johne and Issobel Glasgow
baptised on 3 February 1648 to a father William Glasgow. It proves the father's
name but does not distinguish adult profiles Glasgow-2453, Glasgow-3515 or
Glasgow-1087. Baptism on the same date does not by itself prove that Johne and
Issobel were twins. Inspect whether the original contains one or two entries,
residence, occupation or witnesses.
