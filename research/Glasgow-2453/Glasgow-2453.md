== Biography ==

'''William Glasgow''' represents the adult father named in the Mid Calder baptism of Johne and Issobel Glasgow on 3 February 1648. The parish record identifies William as their father but does not name his wife, parents, occupation or residence beyond the children's baptismal locality.<ref name="MidCalder1648">Old Parish Registers, Mid Calder, Linlithgowshire, Scotland, OPR 694/10/94, baptisms of Johne and Issobel Glasgow, children of William Glasgow, 3 February 1648; indexed source cited on the children's profiles.</ref>

William may also have been the father of Margaret Glassgow, recorded under the combined Kirknewton and East Calder label on 22 December 1643.<ref name="Margaret1643">“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch ([https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-8Q5 record VQ4K-8Q5]), Margreat Glassgow, 22 December 1643, father William.</ref> Walter in 1649, Hellein in 1652 and Marion in 1655 form a coherent possible later household under the same combined catalogue label, but the original entries must establish their exact parishes and whether they name the same adult.

== Research Notes ==

The former profile [[Glasgow-3515]] now redirects here. Its Kirknewton/East Calder child cluster and this profile's 1648 Mid Calder father are retained as one working identity after the merge, but the surviving derivative entries do not prove that every event belongs to one William.

The attached [[Glasgow-1084|John Glasgow]] was baptised in 1616. That chronology is strained for the father of an adult William who had a child by 1643, and no inspected record identifies John as William's father. The relationship must remain uncertain unless a relational record proves it; it should not be used to calculate William's birthplace or family.

[[Glasgow-1087|William Glasgow]], baptised at Mid Calder in 1609, is the strongest same-name candidate for the adult father because his age and locality fit, but no record yet bridges the child to the later household.<ref name="William1609">“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch ([https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1B2-3LV record X1B2-3LV]), William Glasgow, baptised 24 December 1609 at Mid Calder, son of Jhone Glasgow.</ref>

[[Glasgow-1319|Andrew Glasgow]] is a possible close relative rather than a proved son. Naming similarities among Andrew's children do not establish the relationship.

William's birth remains an uncertain estimate from adult parenthood. Mid Calder is supported as a child's baptismal place, not as William's proved birthplace. No death date or place has been established.
