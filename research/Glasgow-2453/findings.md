# Findings: William Glasgow (Glasgow-2453), reconstructed 1648 father

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

Glasgow-2453 is a reconstructed adult-father profile associated with Issobel's
3 February 1648 Mid Calder baptism. The baptism names a father William but does
not identify his parents, birth, spouse, occupation or death.

The current father Glasgow-1084 was himself christened in 1616. That chronology
is highly implausible for the father of an adult William who had a child in
1648, and no relational record supports the attachment. Detach Glasgow-2453
from Glasgow-1084.

This adult may be the same man as Glasgow-3515 or Glasgow-1087, but neither
identity is proved. Do not merge the adult profiles until the original 1609,
1643-1655 and 1648 entries can be compared by residence, occupation, witnesses
and manuscript wording.

Glasgow-2454 and Glasgow-3654 appear to duplicate the same Issobel baptism.
