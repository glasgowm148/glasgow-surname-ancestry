## Prior assessment — superseded by merge

Glasgow-2453 is a reconstructed adult-father profile associated with Issobel's
3 February 1648 Mid Calder baptism. The baptism names a father William but does
not identify his parents, birth, spouse, occupation or death.

His father is unknown. The live attachment to Glasgow-1087, christened in 1609,
was removed on 26 September 2026: this reconstructed William's circa-1620
birth estimate would have made the alleged father about eleven, and none of the
1643–55 child entries names this adult's own father. An older biography also
called Glasgow-1084, christened in 1616, the attached father; that description
was stale and has been corrected live. Neither man is a documented father.

Glasgow-3515 already redirects to this profile. This adult may be the same man
as Glasgow-1087, but that identity is unproved. Do not merge until the original 1609,
1643-1655 and 1648 entries can be compared by residence, occupation, witnesses
and manuscript wording.
