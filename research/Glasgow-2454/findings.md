# Findings: Issobel Glasgow (Glasgow-2454), baptised 1648

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

Glasgow-2454 and Glasgow-3654 appear to represent the same Issobel/Isobel
Glasgow baptised at Mid Calder on 3 February 1648 to a father William Glasgow.
Submit the pair for ordinary duplicate review.

Their different attached father profiles do not establish two children. The
record identifies an adult William but does not distinguish Glasgow-2453,
Glasgow-3515 or Glasgow-1087. Baptism on the same date as Johne Glasgow does not
by itself prove that the children were twins.
