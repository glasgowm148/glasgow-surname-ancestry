The transcript-validated 1634 Inveresk baptism remains the identity-defining

## Identity assessment — malformed burial index

Findmypast record `SCOT/OPR/BUR/1625561` gives an unyear'd 7 April burial with
incompatible metadata: Kirknewton and East Calder, `OPR 689/20`, and range
1642–1737. Parish 689 is Inveresk, but volume 20 is deaths for 1820–1854;
Kirknewton and East Calder is parish 690. It cannot be assigned to this Agnes,
[[Glasgow-993|Agnes Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-1312|Agnes Glasgow]] or a new person.
See the [source-anomaly dossier](../hold-resolution/record-dossiers/fmp-glasgow-11443ace3ea2-agnes-glasgow-burial-index-anomaly.md).

## Recommended action

Do not add the malformed burial row or a death date from it.
