== Biography ==

'''William Glasgow''' was born on 20 August 1639 and baptised the following
day at Inveresk, Midlothian, Scotland. He was the son of
[[Glasgow-1103|Thomas Glasgow]] and [[Thomsone-70|Agnes Thomson]].<ref
name="WilliamBaptism">Findmypast,
[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F4437421&tab=this
“Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929,” William Glasgow], born 20
August and baptised 21 August 1639 at Inveresk, son of Thomas Glasgow and Agnes
Thomson; archive reference OPR 689/1, item 1, record
SCOT/OPR/BAP/4437421 (accessed 3 September 2026).</ref>

The Inveresk and Musselburgh parish material records the following children
of Thomas Glasgow and Agnes Thomson:<ref name="InvereskFamily">Old Parish
Registers, Inveresk and Musselburgh, Midlothian, Scotland, OPR 689/1, frame
259, entries for children of Thomas Glasgow and Agnes Thomsone.</ref>

* [[Glasgow-2460|Agnes Glasgow]], baptised 27 July 1634;
* [[Glasgow-2461|Thomas Glasgow]], baptised 6 August 1637;
* William, born 20 August and baptised 21 August 1639;<ref name="WilliamBaptism" />
* [[Glasgow-3049|Marione Glasgow]], baptised 18 December 1642; and
* [[Glasgow-3050|Margaret Glasgow]], baptised 16 November 1643.

== Research Notes ==

resolves to the currently attached [[Glasgow-1103|Thomas Glasgow]]. This draft
uses the current profile ID and does not imply a second father.
