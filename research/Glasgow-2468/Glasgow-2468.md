== Biography ==

Andrew Glasgow was born on 24 October 1746 and was baptised two days later, on 26 October, in Kilwinning, Ayrshire, Scotland. The parish transcript records the family's residence as Town, Kilwinning.<ref name="FMP">"Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929," transcript, Findmypast ([https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3628386&tab=this record SCOT/OPR/BAP/3628386] : accessed 4 September 2026), Andrew Glasgow, born 24 Oct and baptised 26 Oct 1746, Kilwinning, son of John Glasgow and Mary Auld; residence Town, Kilwinning; citing OPR 599/2, item 2, p. 83.</ref> He was the son of [[Glasgow-2467|John Glasgow]] and [[Auld-1724|Mary Auld]].<ref name="FMP" />

The baptism is also indexed in the Kilwinning church registers held by ScotlandsPeople.<ref>Kilwinning parish baptism record for Andrew Glasgow, 26 Oct 1746, ScotlandsPeople, church registers, parish 599, reference 20/93.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The parish transcript gives separate birth and baptism dates. Andrew's birth
was 24 October 1746; 26 October 1746 is his baptism date.
