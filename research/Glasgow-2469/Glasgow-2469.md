== Biography ==

James Glasgow was born on 12 August 1750 and was baptised one week later, on 19 August, in Kilwinning, Ayrshire, Scotland. The parish transcript records the family's residence as Byres.<ref name="FMP">"Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929," transcript, Findmypast ([https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3628676&tab=this record SCOT/OPR/BAP/3628676] : accessed 4 September 2026), James Glasgow, born 12 Aug and baptised 19 Aug 1750, Kilwinning, son of John Glasgow and Mary Auld; residence Byres; citing OPR 599/2, item 2, p. 96.</ref> He was the son of [[Glasgow-2467|John Glasgow]] and [[Auld-1724|Mary Auld]].<ref name="FMP" />

The baptism is also indexed in the Kilwinning church registers held by ScotlandsPeople.<ref>Kilwinning parish baptism record for James Glasgow, 19 Aug 1750, ScotlandsPeople, church registers, parish 599, reference 20/106.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The parish transcript gives separate birth and baptism dates. James's birth
was 12 August 1750; 19 August 1750 is his baptism date.
