== Biography ==

Robert Glasgow was born before 7 January 1703, when he was baptized at Edinburgh, Midlothian, Scotland. The parish entry identifies his parents as [[Glasgow-3103|James Glasgow]], a shoemaker and indweller, and [[Dobie-449|Jean Dobie]].<ref name="FMP">Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929'', Robert Glasgow, baptism 7 January 1703, Edinburgh, parents James Glasgow and Jean Dobie, father's occupation “Shoemakers Indweller,” OPR 685_1/14, item 4, page 117; record SCOT/OPR/BAP/0597973; [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F0597973&tab=this transcript], accessed 4 September 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The exact baptism date, place and both named parents establish the record-to-profile match and support confident relationships to the attached parents. Earlier broad speculation that Robert might descend from Thomas Glasgow or another James Glasgow is unnecessary and is not a substitute for this direct record.
