## Current conclusion

The Findmypast parish transcript belongs to this profile. It records Robert
Glasgow's baptism at Irvine on 10 January 1716 and names his parents as John
Glasgow and Isabell Kyle. The exact date, place and attached parents are already
represented in the live structured profile, and its biography already cites
the corresponding FamilySearch index. This is supporting evidence only.

The adult formerly represented by [Glasgow-3479](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3479), named with Mary Borland in 1736 and 1738 Irvine baptism indexes, is treated as the later identity of this child. A 1738 Irvine archive catalogue names a Robert Glasgow as a ship's master there between those baptisms. The name, locality, compatible chronology and absence of a better candidate support the identification, although the later records do not directly name the adult's parents. The profiles were **merged into Glasgow-2474 on 27 September 2026**. The surviving profile retains John Glasgow and Isabell Kyle as parents, Mary Borland as spouse, John Glasgow as child, and the combined citations and research cautions.
