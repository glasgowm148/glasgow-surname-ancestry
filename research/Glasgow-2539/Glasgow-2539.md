== Biography ==
'''Cookstown working father, 17 October 2026:''' [[Glasgow-1970|James Glasgow]] is now the selected '''uncertain father''' of the Gortin John, conditional on the possible Hillsdale-John identification. The different child groups and 1771/1778 generation estimates remain explicit constraints; no mother or marriage is inferred.<ref name="CookstownGortinReview" />
John was the father of [[Glasgow-2054|Samuel Glasgow (abt.1792-1865)]] , and [[Glasgow-2540|Hugh Glasgow (abt.1805-abt.1900)]] of Gortin, Derryloran, County Tyrone.
<ref>Hugh's burial application names father as "John Glasgow"; and Samuel's son Thomas names Hugh as an uncle.</ref>

He was living in Gortin as of 1826. The only other tithe-payer of Gortin at this time was Martha Magowan, widow.
<ref>Derryloran title apploment book 1826 - index accessed via CoTyroneIreland.com although there are other sources, PRONI carries the full images for some of these books.</ref>

He was not in Griffith's Valuation suggesting he would have died before 1860 -- or perhaps emigrated to Canada?.

== Research Notes ==
=== Cookstown–Gortin family review — 17 October 2026 ===

Select [[Glasgow-1970|James Glasgow]] of the Cookstown Mitchell family as [[Glasgow-2539|John Glasgow]] of Gortin’s uncertain working father, through the existing possible identification with [[Glasgow-1968|John Glasgow]]. The 1879 Hillsdale history specifically identifies James and his Mitchell wife as parents of John, a farmer near Cookstown. The Gortin account supplies another John in the same Derryloran/Cookstown locality and an earlier compatible father generation. The maintained Hillsdale research already lists Gortin John as a possible duplicate. This is a positive named-son comparison in the relevant family and locality, stronger than an arbitrary father chosen only from the surname. Prefer this existing family as a revisable home for the 17-profile Gortin branch; retain both John accounts separately.

The identity is uncertain. The Hillsdale histories describe John and Jane’s seven children as Robert, Mary A., William, John, Bessie, James and Margaret; the Gortin account instead links Francis, Hugh, Letitia and Samuel. No child has been matched across those sets. An earlier union could explain different couple-based lists, but none is established here and no additional marriage is invented. If the seven-child account was intended as an exhaustive lifetime list for John, the extra Gortin group would conflict with it. The Gortin before-1771 birth boundary comes from Samuel’s reported 1792 birth; the Hillsdale c.1778 father estimate is younger. Literal 1778 fatherhood of a 1792 child would be about 14 and is a serious constraint. These are reconstructed ages and estimates, not two original birth entries; both are retained, without silently shifting either date. Sarah MacNeece’s status as the Gortin man’s widow and Jane’s identity also require testing; no mother is transferred to Gortin John and no wife is added or replaced. Another local John, a collateral relationship, or different father remain alternatives. The 1700s and 1800s family accounts do not independently prove these two Johns identical.

Two uncertain working father links join the previously detached 6-profile Cookstown family and 17-profile Gortin family to the main working tree. The 23 are retained pre-1850 profile accounts, including unresolved possible duplicates, not 23 newly discovered or independently verified people. Motherhood and marriage grades apply only to the named Hillsdale core; no mother, spouse, birth/death change, new person or merge is added elsewhere. All raw WikiTree family fields remain intact and no live save is made. The working connectivity gain measures selected hypotheses, while confidently marked connectivity remains unchanged. This batch reuses maintained source reviews and makes no new original-page inspection claim. Complete private comparisons stay off the public site where they contain living-person or DNA material; safe conclusions and parent choices remain visible.

He may or may not be the same person as other WikiTree profiles for John Glasgow of the Cookstown area, such as [[Glasgow-2058|John Glasgow (bef.1788-)]] --  we don't have enough information to say . At some point, Glasgows might have moved to Gortin from another townland.

There is known to be at least one Glasgow headstone at the Derryloran old cemetery; perhaps there are others that have not yet been placed on Findagrave.

=== Sarah Kermode ===
[[Glasgow-1180|Sarah (Glasgow) Kermode (abt.1826-1894)]] emigrated to Australia in 1842 "age 18" and her parents were Hugh and Margaret, with "Margaret still alive", and her origin "Gorten".

Not sure where to place her just yet! The wording of "Margaret still alive" perhaps implies Hugh was not; but [[Glasgow-2540|Hugh Glasgow (abt.1805-abt.1900)]] of Gortin was in fact still alive.

=== Sarah MacNeece Glasgow ===
Buried in the same plot in New Brunswick as his son Francis is "Sarah MacNeece Glasgow", who is "relict of John Glasgow" and born abt. 1780. {{FindAGrave|220753408|sameas=no}}.

John's son Samuel has a son Thomas McNeice Glasgow too.  The obvious answer to this would be that Sarah was this John's wife.

However that leads to a problem with ages: Samuel was born 1792 according to his age at death. So, maybe Samuel was younger, but then what about [[Glasgow-2537|John Glasgow (abt.1815-1896)]] of Gortin whose age we already fudged from his "88" at death?

Further clues will be needed to figure all of this out.

=== Hugo Glasgow ===
Based on a DNA match (27cM unweighted / 2seg), [[Glasgow-2599|Hugh Glasgow (abt.1831-)]] from Ireland to Nova Scotia could be a descendant.

=== Derryloran parish records - Glasgows of Gortin ===
Baptisms of children:
* bap. 28 May 1814 - Eliza HILL dau of John GLASGOW (Gurteen) + Sarah (Gortalowry)  -- illegitimate.  ''Gurteen'' might mean Gortin? ans so perhaps Eliza Hill is a daughter of this John?
* b. 12 Mar 1830 - Samuel CARROLL, illegitimate son of Hugh GLASGOW (late Gortin, now Cookstown) + Margaret (Gortin)

Confirmations at Derryloran parish church, 1843:
<ref>[[Space:The_Parish_Registers_of_Derryloran_parish_church|The Parish Registers of Derryloran parish church]] V2</ref>
* William Glasgow (age 21), Gortin
* [[Glasgow-212|James Glasgow]] (age 21), Gortin
* Sally Glasgow (age 17), Gortin
* [[Glasgow-2541|Letitia Glasgow]] (age 15), Gortin

Confirmations at Derryloran parish church, 1846:
* [[Glasgow-2538|Thomas Glasgow]] (age 14.5), Gortin
* Margaret Glasgow (age 15.5), Gortin.

Thomas, Letitia and James would be the children of John's son Samuel (although not sure why there are no baptism records for those same children).

We don't yet know if William, Sally and Margaret were also children of Samuel, or perhaps of another Glasgow family. The existence of [[Glasgow-1180|Sarah (Glasgow) Kermode (abt.1826-1894)]] seems to suggest there might have been a family Hugh + Margaret also.

=== Other records for Glasgows of Gortin ===
The 1740 Protestant Householders had William Glasgow of Derryloran but no further information. The 1766 Religious Census had James Glasgow of Kildress. There is a 1775 Dissenter Petition from James Glasgow of Cookstown. (PRONI Names Index)
