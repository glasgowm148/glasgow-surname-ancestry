- John Geddes McGowan: Second son of Samuel McGowan, a farmer near Cookstown. Born in 1792. Educated at Glasgow University, earning a Master of Arts degree in 1814. Licensed by the Tyrone Presbytery in 1816 and ordained at Orritor on April 26, 1825. Married in 1827 to the daughter of Reverend John Glasgow, from the Second Coleraine congregation. He served as the Clerk of the Tyrone Presbytery from 18XX to 1841 and retired in May 1855. He passed away on September 19, 1867.
- To estimate Reverend John Glasgow's birth year, we know he was the father-in-law of John Geddes McGowan, who married his daughter in 1827. Assuming John Glasgow was around 25-35 years old when his daughter was born, and his daughter married around the age of 20-30, a reasonable estimate would place Reverend John Glasgow's birth year between 1760 and 1775.
