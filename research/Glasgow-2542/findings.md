# Findings: [John George Glasgow (Glasgow-2542)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2542)

## Current conclusion

The original newspaper identifies John **George** Glasgow, infant son of Benjamin of Cookstown, who died on 4 August 1857 aged six months. This strongly matches the existing John profile on date, age, locality and father. The inspected gravestone photograph independently calls John the son of the Benjamin who died on 8 February 1870, identifying that father with Glasgow-2059 rather than the separate Drapersfield mill owner.
