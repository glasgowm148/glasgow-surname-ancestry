== Biography ==
The parentage and birth date for James Glasgow are not yet known. Based on the birth of his first known child in 1677, he was likely born by about 1657.

He was married to [[Duffe-18|Isobell Duffe]]. Her name is recorded as "Issobell Duff" or "Issobell Duffe" in the christening records of most of their children.

James and Issobell had the following children, all christened in Edinburgh Parish, Midlothian, Scotland:
* [[Glasgow-3088|Jean Glasgow]], christened 13 April 1677.<ref name="Jean1677">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XTPF-7YB : 11 February 2020), James Glasgow in entry for Jean Glasgow, 1677.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-3103|James Glasgow]], christened 16 March 1679.<ref name="James1679">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQC9-Q9J : 11 February 2020), James Glasgow in entry for James Glasgow, 1679.</ref> (See Research Notes regarding mother's name on this record).
* [[Glasgow-3368|James Glasgow]], christened 18 April 1680.<ref name="James1680">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQC9-8S4 : 11 February 2020), James Glasgow in entry for James Glasgow, 1680.</ref> The christening of a second son with the same name strongly suggests the first James died in infancy.
* [[Glasgow-3284|Mary Glasgow]], christened 23 April 1682.<ref name="Mary1682">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQCM-LLH : 11 February 2020), James Glasgow in entry for Mary Glasgow, 1682.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-3285|Thomas Glasgow]], christened 13 November 1684.<ref name="Thomas1684">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQC9-N9X : 11 February 2020), James Glascow in entry for Thomas Glascow, 1684.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-2681|Margaret Glasgow]], christened 20 March 1691.<ref name="Margaret1691">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:FM3W-XBN : 11 February 2020), James Glasgow in entry for Margarett Glasgow, 1691.</ref>

The date and place of James's death are unknown, but he was alive as of March 1691.
== Research Notes ==
=== Edinburgh apprentice family ===
The older Edinburgh weaver [[Glasgow-1150|James Glasgow]] remains a compatible possible identity for this James. His wife [[Duncan-16106|Agnes Duncan]] was buried on 21 January 1674, three years before the Duffe child series begins. Different wives therefore do not establish separate men. The circa-1655 birth assigned to the Duffe husband is estimated from his children and does not exclude an older father. Continuing fatherhood to 1691 would nevertheless be late for the craftsman apprenticed in 1641. No marriage, address or wife clause yet identifies their households.

The 1699 baxter apprentice [[Glasgow-3285|Thomas Glasgow]] is described as the youngest son of James Glasgow, weaver and burgess; his maintained identification with the 1684 child fits an entry age of almost fifteen. The 1696 apothecary apprentice [[Glasgow-4114|James Glasgow]] has the same paternal description. They are probable brothers, 79%, from their combined name, craft, civic status and chronology. Two matching fathers remain possible. The 1696 James might be one of the 1679 or 1680 children, but neither identity nor his mother is established.

The speculative [[Glasgow-1151|Johnne Glasgow]] father hypothesis, assessed at 26%, concerns a distinct younger James. It is conditional on the Duffe husband not being the older craftsman, whose apprenticeship instead names father Robert. Both adult accounts and their recorded families remain separate pending resolution of that identity.<ref name="EdinburghBrothersReview">[https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/edinburgh_apprentices_~_1666_to_1700_~_1929.pdf#page=47 Watson, Edinburgh Apprentices (1929), printed p.37, PDF p.47: James1696 and Thomas1699; printed page visually checked18 October2026]; [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/register_of_internments_~_greyfriars_churchyard_1658-1700_~_1898.pdf#page=268 Paton, Greyfriars interments1658–1700 (1902), printed p.252, PDF p.268: Agnes Duncan and James the weaver’s unnamed children; visually checked18 October2026]; [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/register_of_internments_~_greyfriars_churchyard_1658-1700_~_1898.pdf#page=212 Paton, same register, printed p.196, PDF p.212: Agnes Duncan, wife of James Glasgow,weaver,21 January1674; cross-index of same burial, not independent corroboration].</ref>
