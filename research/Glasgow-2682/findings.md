# Findings: [James Glasgow (Glasgow-2682), Edinburgh](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2682)

## Conclusion

Retain this as the record-defined husband of Isobell Duffe and father of the
Edinburgh children recorded from 1677 to 1691. His own parents and birth remain
unknown.

Do **not** merge with [Glasgow-1376](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1376).
That profile's apparent 1657 birth is a role error: the cited event belongs to
Marion Glasgow, with James named as her father. No record connects the
Whitecastle material on Glasgow-1376 to this Duffe household.

The two sons named James, baptised in 1679 and 1680, should remain separate
children. The unresolved question is which child, if either, survived to marry
Jean Dobie.

## Recommended update

- Keep Isobell Duffe and the six baptismal children.
- Leave James's parents blank and his birth as an estimate from the first child.
- Add a rejected-merge note for Glasgow-1376.
- Do not assume the 1679 son survived; see [Glasgow-3103 findings](../Glasgow-3103/findings.md).
