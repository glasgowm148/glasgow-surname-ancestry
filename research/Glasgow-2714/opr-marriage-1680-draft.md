== Biography ==
[[Glasgow-2714|George Glasgow (bef.1658-)]] was baptised 16 May 1658 in South Leith, Midlothian, Scotland to [[Glasgow-3078|Archibald Glasgow (1618-)]] and [[Watson-45727|Agnes Watson (abt.1640-)]].<ref>"Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", FamilySearch, 12 February 2020, Georg Glasgowe, 1658; [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:FQMH-CXH record].</ref>

George is attached to [[Gardiner-6839|Jean Gardiner]]; an OPR index records their entry at Kilwinning on 8 November 1678, parish 599, reference 10/312. A separate Kilwinning index entry records George Glasgow and Isobell Young on 5 August 1680, reference 10/313. The available index does not establish whether the two entries concern the same George or the type of event. Isobell Young is not automatically Jonet Young, the name in the baptism comparison discussed on Jean Gardiner’s profile. Neither a second wife nor identity between these women is established.<ref>National Records of Scotland, ''Old Parish Registers, Banns and Marriages'', Kilwinning, entries for George Glasgow and Jean Gardiner, 8 November 1678, reference 10/312, and George Glasgow and Isobell Young, 5 August 1680, reference 10/313; [https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/ Scotland's People], exact-surname Glasgow search through 1700, index extract supplied 10 September 2026; original images not inspected.</ref>
