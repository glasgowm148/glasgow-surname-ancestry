# Findings: [James J Glasgow (Glasgow-2738)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2738)

Last updated: 2026-08-12

## Current conclusion

The FamilySearch attachment to Wilson Crossier Glasgow and Mary McCracken is
contradicted by Wilson's estate records. Mary's 1837 will divides her property
among seven children. The matching real-estate index names Mary with Robert,
Ruth, Naomi, Matilda, Hiram, Almina and Maria in Wilson's estate; James is not
listed. The original packet is still needed for its exact kinship wording, but
the matching count makes James's omission material. The FamilySearch child list
is demonstrably conflated because it also calls Fleming Bates Glasgow, born in
1823, a son of Wilson, who died in 1817.

James remains a plausible but unproved son of [[Glasgow-2769|Robert Glasgow]].
Robert's 1820 Giles County household had one boy aged 10–15, compatible with a
James born about 1809/10, and James was later established in Giles County. No
record yet names Robert as his father. The tested Y-DNA line reaches James and
places his paternal line under R-FT20271, but it cannot identify his father
without a documented comparison line.

A new paper-tree hypothesis can now be stated without treating it as proved.
Robert's leading named father candidate is Archibald `Glasgow-2304`, whose 1800
Newberry household contained one otherwise-unidentified boy under ten. If both
uncertain links are correct, James's line would run through Archibald to James
`Glasgow-1144` and the older Kilwaughter family. That would give a coherent place
for the close Y-DNA relationship to Alexander `Glasgow-951`'s Antrim line, but no
record yet proves either James-to-Robert or Robert-to-Archibald.

## WikiTree changes

The FamilySearch claim that James was a son of [[Glasgow-1982|Wilson Crossier
Glasgow]] and [[McCracken-3711|Mary McCracken]] was rejected because their
seven-child estate group does not include him. James remains attached to
[[Glasgow-2769|Robert Glasgow]] as an uncertain son: the age and Giles County
evidence fit, but no parent-child record has been found.

## Best next records

1. Newberry guardianship 646, box 27, package 57, and real-estate packet 1-13,

2. Giles County deeds and court minutes around James's 1845 title bond, looking

3. Pontotoc County probate, deeds and court minutes after James's 1860 census
