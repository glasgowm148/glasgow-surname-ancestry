== Biography ==

'''Robert Glasgow''' was probably born about 1790 or 1791 in South Carolina. The original 1820 census placed him at 26–44 and the 1840 census at 40–49. Taken together, those brackets favour a birth between about 1790 and 1794.<ref name="Census1820">1820 United States census, Giles County, Tennessee, Robert Glasgow household; NARA microfilm publication M33, roll 124; [https://archive.org/details/populationsc18200124unit/page/n21/mode/1up original schedule, scan 21].</ref><ref name="Census1840">1840 United States census, Lauderdale County, Alabama, p. 48, Robert ''Glasgow'' household; NARA microfilm publication M704, roll 7; [https://archive.org/details/populationsc18400007unit/page/n245/mode/1up original schedule, scan 245].</ref> The 1850 census reported Robert as 60 and born in South Carolina.<ref name="Census1850">1850 United States census, Giles County, Tennessee, district 11, p. 446, dwelling and family 558, Robert ''Glaseoe'' household; NARA microfilm publication M432, roll 879; [https://archive.org/details/populationschedu0879unix/page/n278/mode/1up original schedule, scan 278].</ref> That age may have been estimated or rounded from 59; it is close enough to the earlier brackets to support an early-1790s birth, but should not be treated as an exact calculation.

Robert served as Robert Glasco in Captain William McKay's Tennessee militia company during the War of 1812. The surviving muster covers 20 June 1814 to 27 January 1815.<ref name="Muster1814">University of Tennessee Libraries, Betsey B. Creekmore Special Collections and University Archives, [https://scout.lib.utk.edu/repositories/2/resources/4000 ''Muster Roll and Pay Roll of Captain William McKay's Company of Tennessee Militia'', MS-3419], entry for Robert Glasco.</ref>

By September 1818, Robert Glascow was in Giles County, where the county court assigned him to work on a local road.<ref name="Road1818">Giles County, Tennessee, court minutes, September term 1818, p. 41, item 123, road order naming Robert Glascow; [https://tngenweb.org/giles/misc-court-records-1813-1818/ transcription], TNGenWeb.</ref> On 28 December 1818, Robert Glasco purchased fifty-nine acres on the headwaters of Bradshaw Creek from Josiah Stovall.<ref name="Deed1818">Giles County, Tennessee, deed dated 28 December 1818 and registered 30 June 1819, Josiah Stovall to Robert Glasco, fifty-nine acres on Bradshaw Creek; [https://tngenweb.org/giles/deed-josiah-stoval-to-robert-glasco-glasgow/ transcription], TNGenWeb.</ref> The original 1820 Giles County census schedule names Robert Glasgow as head of a six-person household: two males under 10, one male 10–15, one male 26–44, one female under 10 and one female 26–44.<ref name="Census1820" />

In 1840 Robert was enumerated in Lauderdale County, Alabama, with two younger males and three females. In 1850 he was back in Giles County, Tennessee, working as a laborer. Priscilla Glascoe, aged 28 and born in Tennessee, was living in his household.<ref name="Census1850" /> Robert's death date has not been established; 1850 is his last presently documented occurrence.

== Research Notes ==

=== Wife and children ===

Robert is attached to [[Unknown-658277|Nancy]] as an uncertain wife. No contemporary record inspected in this research names his wife. A female aged 50–59 in Robert's 1840 household is compatible with the reported Nancy, but the census gives neither her name nor her relationship.<ref name="Census1840" />

The Delaware marriage of Robert Glasgow and Nancy Black on 19 January 1814 belongs to another couple.<ref name="DelawareMarriage">[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QPQN-BH57 Delaware Vital Records, 1650–1974], Robert Glasgow and Nancy Black, 19 January 1814; FamilySearch.</ref> A Robert Glasgow also appears in contemporary Delaware militia material, while FamilySearch's sourced reconstruction gives the Delaware couple a son born there about 1825.<ref name="DelawareMilitia">Delaware Public Archives, [https://archivesfiles.delaware.gov/ebooks/Military_Records_Vol_5.pdf ''Military Records'', vol. 5], return naming Robert Glasgow in the 7th Company, 5th Regiment, Delaware Militia.</ref><ref name="DelawareFamily">FamilySearch, [https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/L7KB-QB9/robert-glasgow-1825-1911 Robert Glasgow (about 1825–1911)], parents shown as Robert Glasgow and Nancy Black; a derivative family reconstruction with linked record sources.</ref> The target Robert served in Tennessee in 1814–1815, was established in Giles County by 1818 and was enumerated there in 1820.<ref name="Census1820" /> Nancy Black was therefore not added as his wife.

=== Parentage ===

[[Glasgow-1983|Robert Glasgow]] was previously attached as Robert's father. However, the shared Glasgow memorial in Gilders Creek Cemetery directly identifies a different Robert—[[Glasgow-3982|Robert Glasgow]], who died on 12 December 1813 aged five—as the son of [[Glasgow-1983]] and Elizabeth.<ref name="GildersCreek">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/gilders_creek_cem.htm Gilders Creek Cemetery transcription and linked memorial photograph], Newberry County, South Carolina; inscription for Elizabeth, spouse of Robert Glasgow, and “their son” Robert.</ref>

The two sons cannot be the same person: this Robert was alive during the 1814–1815 muster and bought land in Tennessee in 1818. The couple cannot plausibly have had two living sons named Robert at the same time. With no direct evidence for the former attachment, [[Glasgow-1983]] was detached and Robert's parents were left blank.

[[Glasgow-2304|Archibald Glasgow]] is the leading possible father found so far. The 1800 Newberry census recorded Archibald with exactly one male under ten, one male aged 26–44, four females under ten, one female aged 16–25 and two enslaved people.<ref name="Archibald1800">"United States Census, 1800," Archibald Glasgow household, Newberry District, South Carolina, p. 75, NARA microfilm publication M32, roll 50; [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XHRC-7BG FamilySearch record XHRC-7BG] and [https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/1800nbycensusG.htm public household transcription].</ref> Archibald's gravestone-proved children [[Glasgow-3979|Neomi]], [[Glasgow-3980|Rachel]] and [[Glasgow-3981|James]] do not account for that boy.<ref name="ArchibaldChildren">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/gilders_creek_cem.htm Gilders Creek Cemetery survey and photographs], inscriptions naming Neomi and James as children of Archibald and Mary and Rachel as their daughter.</ref> Robert's census-derived birth about 1790 and South Carolina birthplace fit the broad age category.

The census does not name the boy. Its only adult female was aged 16–25, which makes maternity by Archibald's known wife Mary chronologically tight if Robert was born near 1790. The 1810 census again indexes Archibald in Newberry, but the household columns have not yet been inspected.<ref name="Archibald1810">1810 United States census, Newberry, South Carolina, entry for ''Archald Glasgow''; [https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/7613/records/585568 Ancestry record 585568], image 4433429_00206.</ref> Mary Glasgow administered Archibald's 1818 estate, estate 647, box 27, package 57; that packet may name a complete heir group.<ref name="ArchibaldEstate">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/estates1785-1949/22.htm Newberry County estate index], 1818 entry for Archibald Glasgow, Mary Glasgow administrator, estate 647, box 27, package 57.</ref> Until it or another record names Robert, Archibald remains a possible father and Robert's parent fields remain blank.

=== Other Newberry Robert Glasgow records ===

A Robert Glasgow headed a household in Newberry District in 1800.<ref name="OtherRobert1800">"United States Census, 1800," FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XHRC-7BF Robert Glasgow, Newberry District, South Carolina].</ref> The target Robert's revised birth estimate of about 1790 makes him too young to be that household head. The entry therefore documents a different local Robert.

A FamilySearch tree reconstruction also represents a different Robert Glasgow associated with Newberry.<ref name="OtherRobertTree">FamilySearch Family Tree, [https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/LHBL-QFT Robert Glasgow, LHBL-QFT].</ref> It is a derivative identity lead and does not establish that the two men were the same person.

An archived Glasgow family page assigns Robert a wife and children but does not cite the records underlying the reconstruction.<ref name="FamilyPage">[https://archive.ph/xIXp1 Archived Glasgow family research page].</ref> Its family list is a research lead; the census and land records above provide the present documentary basis.
