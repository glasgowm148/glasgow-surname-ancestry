== Biography ==

'''John Glasgow''' was reportedly born about 1800 in South Carolina. No contemporary record inspected in this research proves the date, place or parentage.

A John Glasgow was assessed in Giles County, Tennessee, district 10, in 1836 for one white poll and one enslaved person.<ref name="Tax1836">[https://tngenweb.org/gilestn/tax/1836/1836-10.html Giles County, Tennessee, 1836 tax list, district 10], entry for John Glasgow; TNGenWeb.</ref> The tax entry supplies no age, birthplace or relative, so it is only a possible occurrence of this John.

== Research Notes ==

=== Parentage ===

[[Glasgow-2769|Robert Glasgow]] and [[Unknown-658277|Nancy]] remain attached as uncertain working parents because no better replacement has been identified. However, Robert's 1840 census age was 40–49, placing his birth between 1790 and 1800.<ref name="Census1840">1840 United States census, Lauderdale County, Alabama, p. 48, Robert ''Glasgow'' household; NARA microfilm publication M704, roll 7; [https://archive.org/details/populationsc18400007unit/page/n245/mode/1up original schedule, scan 245].</ref> Robert would therefore have been no more than about ten years old when John was reportedly born.

No record names John as their son, so the links must not be treated as proved.
