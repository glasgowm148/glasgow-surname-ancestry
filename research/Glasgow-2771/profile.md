== Biography ==

'''Emma Glasgow''' was reportedly born about 1815 in South Carolina. Her exact birth date, later life and parentage have not been established by an inspected contemporary record.

== Research Notes ==

Emma remains attached as an uncertain daughter of [[Glasgow-2769|Robert Glasgow]] and [[Unknown-658277|Nancy Unknown]] because no better placement has been identified. A female under 10 in Robert's 1820 Giles County household and a female aged 20–29 in his 1840 Lauderdale County household are both compatible with Emma's reported birth year.<ref name="Census1820">1820 United States census, Giles County, Tennessee, Robert Glasgow household; NARA microfilm publication M33, roll 124; [https://archive.org/details/populationsc18200124unit/page/n21/mode/1up original schedule, scan 21].</ref><ref name="Census1840">1840 United States census, Lauderdale County, Alabama, p. 48, Robert ''Glasgow'' household; NARA microfilm publication M704, roll 7; [https://archive.org/details/populationsc18400007unit/page/n245/mode/1up original schedule, scan 245].</ref> Neither census names the woman, so the two-household fit supports the chronology but does not prove that she was Emma or Robert and Nancy's daughter.

An archived page formerly cited on this profile has been retained for reference, but it does not provide a contemporary record identifying Emma.<ref name="ProjectSource1">[https://archive.ph/xIXp1 Archived family-research page].</ref>
