# Findings: [Jesse Martin Glasgow (Glasgow-2772)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2772)

Last updated: 2026-08-19

## Current conclusion

The Texas State Historical Association documents Jesse Martin Glasco's birth
in Giles County, Tennessee, on 12 May 1818 and his later Texas life, but does not
name his parents. His age fits the male aged 20–29 in Robert Glasgow-2769's 1840
household. The son attachment remains plausible and uncertain, not proved.
