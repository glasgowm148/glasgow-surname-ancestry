== Biography ==

'''Jesse Martin Glasco''' was born on 12 May 1818 in Giles County, Tennessee.<ref name="TSHA">[https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/glasco-jesse-martin “Glasco, Jesse Martin”], ''Handbook of Texas Online'', Texas State Historical Association.</ref>

He married Louisa Earp on 16 December 1846. Jesse became a Methodist minister and moved to Upshur County, Texas, in 1850. He died there on 17 December 1886.<ref name="TSHA" />

== Research Notes ==

Jesse remains attached as an uncertain son of [[Glasgow-2769|Robert Glasgow]] and [[Unknown-658277|Nancy Unknown]] because no better placement has been identified. His age fits one of the two males under 10 in Robert's 1820 Giles County household and the male aged 20–29 in Robert's 1840 Lauderdale County household.<ref name="Census1820">1820 United States census, Giles County, Tennessee, Robert Glasgow household; NARA microfilm publication M33, roll 124; [https://archive.org/details/populationsc18200124unit/page/n21/mode/1up original schedule, scan 21].</ref><ref name="Census1840">1840 United States census, Lauderdale County, Alabama, p. 48, Robert ''Glasgow'' household; NARA microfilm publication M704, roll 7; [https://archive.org/details/populationsc18400007unit/page/n245/mode/1up original schedule, scan 245].</ref> Neither census names him, and Jesse's published biography does not identify his parents. The relationship is therefore plausible but unproved.

The published form of his given name is '''Jesse''', not Jessie, and the Texas biography uses the surname spelling '''Glasco'''. No compatible WikiTree profile for Louisa Earp was identified in the local One Tree catalogue.

An archived family tree formerly cited on this profile is retained for reference but is unsourced and does not prove the parentage.<ref name="ProjectSource1">[https://archive.ph/xIXp1 Archived descendants tree].</ref>
