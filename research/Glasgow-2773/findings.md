## Current conclusion

Priscilla was probably a daughter of Robert Glasgow-2769. In 1840 Robert's
household contained one female aged 15–19, exactly compatible with Priscilla's
estimated 1822 birth. In 1850 Priscilla Glascoe, aged 28 and born in Tennessee,
was named in Robert Glaseoe's Giles County household. The 1850 census had no
relationship column, so the daughter identification is a strong inference, not
an explicitly stated relationship.

No marriage or later occurrence has been identified. The available pre-1865
Giles County marriage compilation contains no Priscilla Glasgow/Glasco entry.
