== Biography ==

'''Priscilla Glasgow''' was born about 1822 in Tennessee. Her age and birthplace come from the 1850 United States census.<ref name="Census1850">1850 United States census, Giles County, Tennessee, district 11, p. 446, dwelling and family 558, Robert ''Glaseoe'' household; NARA microfilm publication M432, roll 879; [https://archive.org/details/populationschedu0879unix/page/n278/mode/1up original schedule, scan 278].</ref>

In 1850 Priscilla Glascoe, aged 28, was living with Robert Glaseoe, aged 60, a South Carolina-born laborer, in Giles County.<ref name="Census1850" />

== Research Notes ==

=== Parentage ===

Priscilla is attached as the probable daughter of [[Glasgow-2769|Robert Glasgow]]. The 1840 census recorded a female aged 15–19 in Robert's household, an exact fit for Priscilla's estimated birth year.<ref name="Census1840">1840 United States census, Lauderdale County, Alabama, p. 48, Robert ''Glasgow'' household; NARA microfilm publication M704, roll 7; [https://archive.org/details/populationsc18400007unit/page/n245/mode/1up original schedule, scan 245].</ref> Ten years later Priscilla was named in Robert's household with the same surname and a plausible 28-year age gap.<ref name="Census1850" />

The 1850 census did not include a relationship column, so it does not literally call her Robert's daughter. The continuity between the two households nevertheless makes the relationship probable. [[Unknown-658277|Nancy]] remains an uncertain mother because no inspected record identifies Priscilla's mother.

No marriage or later record for Priscilla has yet been identified. Her death date is unknown.
