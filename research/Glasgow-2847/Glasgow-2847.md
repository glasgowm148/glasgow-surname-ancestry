== Biography ==
'''John Glasgow''' was recorded with [[Anderson-83671|Margaret Anderson]] as parents of the West Calder family represented by [[Glasgow-2849|Margaret]] in 1745, [[Glasgow-2846|John]] in 1747, [[Glasgow-2848|James]] in 1750, and [[Glasgow-1922|Jean]] in 1755. His birth date and birthplace are not established by those children’s records.<ref>[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4T-H5Y Margaret Glasgow,1745]; [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4T-R5D John Glasgow,1747]; [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4T-R5Z James Glasgow,1750], FamilySearch parish indexes; existing profile citations. Jean’s citation is ScotlandsPeople,West Calder OPR 701,10/202,FR 142,30 November 1755. Originals not newly inspected.</ref>

He is provisionally identified with [[Glasgow-3525|John Glasgow]], indexed at Kirknewton and East Calder on 31 March 1717 with father James. Through that possible identity, [[Glasgow-3526|James Glasgow of the Kirknewton father account]] is selected as an uncertain father. [[Glasgow-1547|James Glasgow of the 1702 childhood account]] remains an alternative identification of that named father. No mother is supplied.<ref>[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-6JT FamilySearch,John Glasgow,1717], retained Kirknewton/East Calder index; no new original inspection.</ref>

If baptised near birth in 1717, he would have been about 28 in 1745 and 38 in 1755. The circa 1730 structured birth and earlier before 1739 biographical estimate are not recorded ages. They are retained as earlier estimates rather than proof against the 1717 identity. No exact birth, marriage or death date is established here. The son John’s possible later-life comparisons are separate questions.

=== Selected uncertain family — 16 October 2026 ===

[[Glasgow-3526|James Glasgow, Kirknewton father account]] is selected as the uncertain working father of [[Glasgow-2847|John Glasgow, West Calder Anderson household]], husband of Margaret Anderson and father of the West Calder children recorded from 1745 to 1755. The route provisionally identifies that adult with [[Glasgow-3525|John Glasgow, Kirknewton child of 1717]], indexed at neighbouring Kirknewton and East Calder on 31 March 1717 with father James. The parish setting, exact forename and surname, and feasible generation favour this concrete local childhood over an unidentified origin: if baptised near birth in 1717, John would have been about 28 in 1745 and 38 in 1755. The later family also contains a James, although a common repeated forename does not independently establish a grandfather. No register entry yet joins the child to Margaret Anderson’s husband. This is a low-confidence identity-based parent model, not a proved father clause for the adult.

The 1717 hypothesis requires an earlier birth than circa 1730 but does not contradict a measured date. Those structured fields remain unchanged; the hypothetical date must not be presented as a new birth fact. All four children, their mother, their dates and existing relationship grades are retained.

[[Glasgow-3526|James Glasgow, Kirknewton father account]] is the maintained account for the James named in the 1717 record. The earlier child-parent grade is not upgraded, and neither James’s ancestry receives new certainty.

A competing childhood is [[Glasgow-1544|Glasgow-1544]], recorded at Kirknewton in 1702 with father William. That child has already been used as a possible identity for [[Glasgow-3481|Glasgow-3481]] of the Christian Meek household.
