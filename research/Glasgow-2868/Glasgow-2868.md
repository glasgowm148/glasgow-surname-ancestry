== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' was recorded in the Glencorse baptism register on 14 February 1720, a son of Robert Glasgow and Margaret Davidsone.<ref name="James1720">Scotland's People, Church of Scotland old parish registers, James Glasgow, 14 February 1720, parents Robert Glasgow and Margaret Davidsone, Glencorse parish 687, reference 10/121.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The baptism proves the Scottish child and identifies his parents by name. It does not identify his father as [[Glasgow-2867|Robert Glasgow]], master of the ''John and Archibald'' in 1754; that shipmaster identification is unsupported and should remain uncertain.

[[Glasgow-3011|Robert Glasgow (1676-)]] is the probable candidate for the Glencorse father: he is the only indexed Robert Glasgow baptism in Glencorse from 1640 to 1690.<ref name="Robert1676">Scotland's People, Church of Scotland old parish registers, Robert Glasgow, 29 December 1676, parents John Glasgow and Margaret Currie, Glencorse parish 687, reference 10/8; exact-name search for local Robert Glasgow baptisms, 1640–1690.</ref> A same-name man married [[Davidson-20783|Margaret Davidson]] there on 8 June 1704.<ref name="GlencorseMarriage1704">Scotland's People, Church of Scotland old parish registers, Robert Glasgow and Margarett Davidson, 8 June 1704, Glencorse parish 687, reference 20/53.</ref> The marriage index supplies no age, parents, or occupation, so this is not proof of identity. Any future controlled replacement of Glasgow-2867 with Glasgow-3011 must retain an uncertain father relationship.

No cited contemporary record carries the 1720 Glencorse child to Cecil County, Maryland, identifies Ann Hartshorne as his wife, or identifies the attached colonial children as his. The existing colonial dates, location, and relationships should remain uncertain until a migration, marriage, land, probate, or other continuity record identifies this Scottish man.
