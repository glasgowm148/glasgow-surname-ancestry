# Findings: James Glasgow (Glasgow-2868), Glencorse, 1720

Last updated: 2026-08-31

Scotland's People indexes James Glasgow on 14 February 1720 at Glencorse,
parish 687, reference `10/121`, naming Robert Glasgow and Margaret Davidsone as
parents. This proves the Scottish child but does not identify the father as
Glasgow-2867, the 1754 shipmaster.

No inspected contemporary record carries this child to Cecil County, Maryland,
or proves Ann Hartshorne and the attached colonial children. The live profile
now marks the father relationship, 1784 death and Maryland death place
uncertain. A migration, marriage, land or probate record is required before the
Scottish and colonial identities can be treated as one person.
