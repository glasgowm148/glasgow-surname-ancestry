== Biography ==
John Glasgow was born in 1722 and died in 1769. He was originally buried in the Nottingham Presbyterian Old Church Yard across from what is now Rising Sun Middle School in Maryland.<ref name="glasgowbible">Glasgow Family Bible, York Historic Trust Museum and Genealogy Library, York, Pennsylvania.</ref> According to the Glasgow Family Bible, he was buried alongside his wife Mary (Cranmer) Glasgow and their son John Glasgow II.

In 1959, the West Nottingham Church Historian recorded that the congregation decided to relocate the old burial ground. Only 45 graves were found and 25 were identified. In 1960, the remains were reinterred at West Nottingham Church Cemetery. Today, John Glasgow Sr. and John Glasgow II are buried next to each other there.<ref name="glasgowbible" />

The Glasgow Family Bible, originally belonging to John Glasgow II, was later passed to his son Congressman [[Glasgow-2873|Hugh Glasgow (1769-1818)]], who is buried at Slate Ridge Cemetery, York County. The Bible remains documented at the York Historic Trust Museum and Genealogy Library.<ref name="glasgowbible" />

=== Possible Parentage and Family Connections ===


There is strong evidence connecting John Glasgow to [[Glasgo-52|John Glasgo (abt.1772-1851)]], who married [[Boydston-414|Amelia Boydston (1778-1857)]]. Jeremiah Glasgow, likely [[Glasgo-23|Jeremiah Glasgow (1713-1777)]], witnessed the will of David Boydston, Amelia’s father.<ref name="boydstonwill">Will of David Boydston, cited in Boydston Family Records. [https://www.genealogy.com/ftm/b/a/t/Margaret-R-Bates/GENE15-0008.html]</ref>

His sons [[Glasgow-2871|John Glasgow (1738-1792)]] and [[Glasgow-2872|Samuel Glasgow (1761-1824)]] were also buried in the Nottingham Old Presbyterian Burying Grounds at Rising Sun, Maryland.
