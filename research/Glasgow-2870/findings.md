## Identity assessment — newspaper candidates withdrawn, 6 October 2026

- [The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1758-01-12, p.1](https://www.newspapers.com/image/39389283/): no longer a candidate for this profile. John Glasgow, Nottingham, Philadelphia letter list January 1758. No John Glasgow is on the Chester County tax lists before 1760 (the Nottingham Glasgows taxed in the 1750s are Samuel and James), so this addressee is either the young John who first appears as a West Nottingham freeman in 1760 (Glasgow-2871) or an untaxed elder; both Nottingham profiles stay possible.
- [The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1760-08-14, p.3](https://www.newspapers.com/image/39404812/): no longer a candidate for this profile. John Glasgow signs a 1760 vendue notice for William Graham's creditors (200-acre Susquehanna tract near Shepard's Ferry, Cecil County) and announces a store at Nottingham. The Chester County tax lists show John Glasgow as a West Nottingham freeman in 1760 and as storekeeper there in 1765 and 1767, with the same holding continuing to 1791 and passing to Hugh Glasgow in 1793: this is probably Glasgow-2871 (d. 1792), not the elder Glasgow-2870.
- [The Pennsylvania Journal, or, Weekly Advertiser, 1765-10-17, p.1](https://www.newspapers.com/image/1033731085/): no longer a candidate for this profile. John Glasgow, Nottingham Township, Chester County, Chester post-office list October 1765. In 1765 the Chester County tax list has two Johns in West Nottingham: the storekeeper-landholder (probably Glasgow-2871) and an inmate; the merchant is the likelier addressee, but only possible.
- [The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1765-11-28, p.4](https://www.newspapers.com/image/39406491/): no longer a candidate for this profile. John Glasgow, Nottingham, Chester post-office list November 1765 (same period as the October list). Same assessment: storekeeper John of West Nottingham, probably Glasgow-2871, is the likelier addressee; only possible.
- [The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1766-05-29, p.4](https://www.newspapers.com/image/39391550/): no longer a candidate for this profile. John Glasgow 'near Oxford' is one of the agents taking subscriptions for De Jure Regni (May 1766). Both the West Nottingham storekeeper (probably Glasgow-2871) and an East Nottingham landholder John (taxed 1768-1775) lived near Oxford, so identity stays possible.
- [The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1769-10-26, p.1](https://www.newspapers.com/image/39396485/): no longer a candidate for this profile. John Glasgow, 'Kensing-/ton', Philadelphia letter list October 1769. Kensington (Northern Liberties, Philadelphia) has no link to the Nottingham Johns; unidentifiable.
- [Maryland Gazette, 1771-10-24, p.3](https://www.newspapers.com/image/41041559/): no longer a candidate for this profile. Charles-Town (Cecil County), Maryland letter list, 1 October 1771: John Glasgow in West Nottingham, Chester County, and a separate William Glasgow. Two Johns were taxed in West Nottingham in 1771 (landholder and inmate), so John is only possibly Glasgow-2871; Glasgow-2870 (d. 1769) is now unlikely. William has no WikiTree profile; the Cecil County William named among the children of James Glasgow Sr. (d. 1784) in Cecil probate is a plausible but unproved identity.
