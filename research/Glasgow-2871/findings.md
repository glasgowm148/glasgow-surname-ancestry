# Glasgow-2871 research findings

## Identity assessment — 4 October 2026 review

Glasgow-2871 remains a compatible candidate for the documentary John Glasgow who signed a 14 August 1760 Pennsylvania Gazette advertisement concerning a Cecil County vendue and a planned Nottingham store. The merchant context is a stronger occupational fit than Glasgow-2870, but the record does not identify him. No family or relationship change follows.

## Source findings

- The notice announces a public vendue on 2 September 1760 for creditors of deceased William Graham, including a 200-acre patent tract on the Susquehanna about two miles from Shepard's Ferry in Cecil County, Maryland; about 60 acres were cleared.
- John Glasgow signed the notice and stated that he intended to open a store in Nottingham by the end of September, selling for ready money or short credit.
- Source: [Pennsylvania Gazette, 14 August 1760, p.3, image 39404812](https://www.newspapers.com/image/39404812/), original inspected 4 October 2026.

## Identity and relationship assessment

Glasgow-2871 (born 1738, died 1792 in Chester County) and Glasgow-2870 (born 1722 at Libberton, died 1769 at Nottingham) remain compatible candidates after a bounded public catalogue search and live biography review. Glasgow-2871's merchant context is the stronger occupational fit. The notice names no kin or origin; do not infer Irish origin or attach family. The existing Glasgow-2871 biography's conflation of wife Mary Cranmer with mother is not resolved by this source.
