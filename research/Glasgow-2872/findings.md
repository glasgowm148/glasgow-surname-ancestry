## West Fallowfield will and Ireland relatives — 23 October 2026

**Historical identity assessment:** the original record readings remain valid. The provisional profile identity and retained 72% John father below are superseded by the 24 October review above.

**Original will, 23 October 2026:** Samuel Glasgow of West Fallowfield names wife **Tabitha**, infant daughter **Margaret Ellen**, and a contingent remainder to the **sons of his three brothers William, James and Robert Glasgow, of Ireland**. The three named men are the testator’s brothers; the beneficiaries are their sons. This is not a clause naming the testator’s own son James. The will is dated **17 May 1824**; the proving endorsement is **31 May 1824**. [County original](https://portal.laserfiche.com/Portal/DocView.aspx?id=773006&page=588&repo=r-24f48cc6), file 7520, scanned volume 07451–7600, portal pp. 588–589 (downloaded extract pp. 3–4). Cover is portal p. 586. Original pages were visually read; the brother clause was independently reread.

The will appoints Robert Futhey and Thomas Stewart executors; estate office does not establish kinship. It also names Samuel Glasgow Haslett, described as James Haslett’s infant twin son; no godparent relationship is stated. His Glasgow middle name does not make him a Glasgow son. Later accounts, portal pp. 594 and 596, name Benjamin Glasgow as a debtor and William Glasgow in transmitting another estate’s legacy, without stating their relationship to the testator.
