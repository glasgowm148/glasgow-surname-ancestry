```text
=== Death ===

Samuel died at Lisnagaver on 17 August 1875, aged 85. His civil death registration describes him as a bachelor and weaver.<ref name="SamuelDeath1875">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/view/?record_id=cide-7225943 IrishGenealogy.ie], civil death registration of Samuel Glasgow, 17 August 1875, Lisnagaver; aged 85, bachelor and weaver (accessed 17 July 2026).</ref>

His reported age gives an estimated birth in about 1789 or 1790.

=== Brother and father ===

The 1851 census explicitly calls Samuel the brother of John Glasgow, head of the Lisnagaver household.<ref name="Census1851Lisnagaver">[https://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Rasharkin/Lisnagaver/34/ 1851 Ireland census], Lisnagaver household 34, John Glasgow household (accessed 17 July 2026).</ref>

John's original 1846 marriage register names his father as '''John Glasgow, farmer'''.<ref name="JohnMarriage1846">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/view/?record_id=cima-3156063 IrishGenealogy.ie], civil marriage registration of John Glasgow and Margaret Garvin, 16 November 1846; John recorded as a bachelor farmer of Lisnagaver and son of John Glasgow, farmer (accessed 17 July 2026).</ref>

On the ordinary meaning of the census relationship, the same older John was Samuel's father. He is currently represented by [[Glasgow-1022|John Glasgow]] as a plausible working identification. Because neither record gives enough detail to distinguish the older farmer from every namesake, the relationship is marked uncertain.
```
