## Parentage conclusion

The proof chain is:

1. The census directly identifies Samuel as John Glasgow's brother.
2. John's marriage is uniquely matched by spouse, year, residence, parish, and
   occupation.
3. That original marriage record directly identifies John's father as John
   Glasgow.
4. Therefore John, rather than Adam, is Samuel's father on the ordinary meaning
   of `brother` and absent evidence that they were paternal half-brothers.

Confidence: **high** that the Adam attachment is wrong; **not absolute** because
neither record names Samuel's father in Samuel's own event.

John `Glasgow-1022` is the best current candidate for the father named through
this proof chain. He was an older Antrim farmer named John and his reported 1791
marriage is compatible with Samuel's approximate 1789-91 birth within normal age
error. No record directly connects him to Lisnagaver, so keep the link uncertain
and leave Samuel's mother blank.
