# Findings: [Glasgow-2916](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2916)

## Current conclusion

The man born about 1719 may duplicate or be a close collateral of the James Glasgow born about 1721, but no inspected record resolves the identity. The Cross/Derry reconstruction is not joined to Moneymore, Mary Harkness or the documented emigrant family. Charles Glasgow's alleged migration and James's parentage are unproved.

The local church-register gap explains why ordinary baptism searches cannot settle the claim: PRONI lists Derryloran Church of Ireland baptisms only from 1795/1797 and Cookstown Presbyterian baptisms from 1822 or later. No exact-name search completed on 30 August 2026 produced a primary record for James at Cross.

## Recommended next records

Compare original church, deed, probate and migration records for both James profiles. Keep both profiles and all attached locations/relationships until a positive identity or relationship replacement is established.
