== Biography ==

'''John Glasgow''' was born about 1832 or 1833 in County Antrim, Ireland. The surviving 1851 census calls him an eighteen-year-old son of [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]]. John was a linen weaver and was temporarily absent elsewhere in County Antrim when the Ballycraigargh household was enumerated.<ref name="Census1851">1851 Census of Ireland, County Antrim, Dunaghy parish, Ballycraigargh, household 29, James Glasgow household: [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QSQ-G94X-YD22 living household, image 251] and [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L94X-Y621 continuation, image 252].</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Parentage ===

The census directly proves James as John's father. [[Hamilton-25516|Peggy Hamilton]] remains attached as an uncertain working mother because no better-supported replacement profile has been identified. Peggy and James reported marrying in 1848, about fifteen years after John's birth, so Peggy should not be treated as his proved mother. The marriage year alone does not make earlier biological maternity impossible. [[Unknown-764460|Mary Glasgow]] of the closely matching 1847 Glenleslie workhouse family is the stronger candidate, but that record does not include John and no record inspected so far explicitly calls him her son.

The [http://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Dunaghy/Ballycregagh/29/ National Archives of Ireland household link] already cited on the profile is preserved.
