# Findings: Glasgow-2982

Last updated: 2026-08-19

## Current conclusion

The 1851 Ballycregagh census directly calls [[Glasgow-2982|John Glasgow]], age
18, an absent son of [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]]. John was a linen weaver,
born in County Antrim and temporarily elsewhere in that county. James and
[[Hamilton-25516|Peggy Hamilton]] reported marrying in 1848, about fifteen
years after John's birth, so her maternity is unproved but not impossible. Mary
Glasgow of the matching 1847 workhouse family is the stronger mother candidate,
but the record does not include John and no direct maternity record has been
found. Retain Peggy uncertain until a better replacement can be attached.
