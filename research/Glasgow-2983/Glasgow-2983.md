== Biography ==

'''William James Glasgow''' was born about 1836 in County Antrim, Ireland. On 3 June 1847 an eleven-year-old William James Glasgow of Glenleslie entered Ballymena Workhouse with Mary, Boyd, Mary Jane and Margaret Glasgow.<ref name="Workhouse1847">[https://www.billmacafee.com/otherrecords/ballymenaworkhouse.pdf William Macafee, "Admissions to Ballymena Workhouse: 13th May to 8th July 1847"], registrations 4406–4410 (accessed 13 August 2026).</ref>

The 1851 Ballycregagh return calls William James the fourteen-year-old son of [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]]. He was attending school and was temporarily absent elsewhere in County Antrim.<ref name="Census1851">[https://durrushistory.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/1851irishcensusreturnscommadelimitedtextfilejul24_071.pdf "1851 Irish Census Returns: comma-delimited transcription"], Dunaghy, Ballycregagh, household 29 (accessed 13 August 2026).</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Parentage ===

The census directly proves James as William James's father. [[Hamilton-25516|Peggy Hamilton]] remains attached as an uncertain working mother because no better-supported replacement profile has been identified. Peggy and James reported marrying in 1848, long after William James's birth, so Peggy should not be treated as his proved mother. The marriage year alone does not make earlier biological maternity impossible. Mary of the 1847 workhouse group is the stronger candidate, but no direct maternity record has been found.

The County Antrim birthplace is retained as an uncertain family-locality estimate. The records place William James at Glenleslie and Ballycregagh but do not directly state his birthplace.

The [http://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Dunaghy/Ballycregagh/29/ National Archives of Ireland household link] is preserved. [[Glasgow-1694|William Glasgow]], born about 1837, remains a similar-age profile; no record inspected here establishes a duplicate identity.
