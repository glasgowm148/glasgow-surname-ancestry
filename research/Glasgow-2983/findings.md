## Current conclusion

The 1847 workhouse return and 1851 census identify the same William James
Glasgow in the Glenleslie/Ballycregagh family. He was 11 in 1847 and 14 in
1851, making birth about 1836 more appropriate than an exact 1837. The census
directly calls him [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]]'s son. Peggy Hamilton's 1848
marriage means her maternity is unproved but not impossible; Mary of the 1847
family group is the stronger candidate. Retain Peggy uncertain pending a better
replacement.
