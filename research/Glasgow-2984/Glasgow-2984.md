== Biography ==

'''Boyd Glasgow''' was born about 1834 or 1835 in County Antrim, Ireland. He entered Ballymena Workhouse from Glenleslie on 3 June 1847, aged twelve, with Mary, William James, Mary Jane and Margaret Glasgow. He was discharged alive on 31 July.<ref name="Workhouse1847">[https://www.billmacafee.com/otherrecords/ballymenaworkhouse.pdf William Macafee, "Admissions to Ballymena Workhouse: 13th May to 8th July 1847"], registrations 4406–4410 (accessed 13 August 2026).</ref>

The mortality portion of the surviving 1851 Ballycregagh census calls Boyde a sixteen-year-old son of [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]] and states that he died in winter 1847.<ref name="Census1851">[https://durrushistory.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/1851irishcensusreturnscommadelimitedtextfilejul24_071.pdf "1851 Irish Census Returns: comma-delimited transcription"], Dunaghy, Ballycregagh, household 29 (accessed 13 August 2026).</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Parentage ===

The census directly proves James as Boyd's father. [[Hamilton-25516|Peggy Hamilton]] remains attached as an uncertain working mother because no better-supported replacement profile has been identified. Peggy's reported 1848 marriage to James occurred after Boyd's death, so she should not be treated as his proved mother; however, the marriage date does not make earlier biological maternity impossible. Mary in the 1847 workhouse family is the stronger candidate, although no marriage or baptism naming both parents has been found.

The County Antrim birth and death places in the structured fields are useful uncertain estimates based on the Glenleslie workhouse entry and Ballycregagh mortality return; neither record states a more exact place.

The [http://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Dunaghy/Ballycregagh/29/ National Archives of Ireland household link] cited on the profile is preserved.
