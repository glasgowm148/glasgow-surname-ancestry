# Findings: Glasgow-2984

Last updated: 2026-08-19

## Current conclusion

The 1847 workhouse return records Boyd Glasgow, 12, of Glenleslie. The 1851
Ballycregagh mortality return calls Boyde Glasgow a son of
[[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]], age 16, who died in winter 1847. Peggy
Hamilton's later marriage means her maternity is unproved but not impossible.
Mary in the workhouse group is the stronger candidate; retain Peggy uncertain
until a better replacement can be attached.
