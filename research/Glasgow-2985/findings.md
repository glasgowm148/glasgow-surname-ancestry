# Findings: Glasgow-2985

Last updated: 2026-08-19

## Current conclusion

The 1851 Ballycregagh mortality return calls Andy Glasgow a deceased five-year-
old son of [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]]. This implies birth about 1845/46,
not 1820. The return does not supply a death year, only that he had died by
1851. Peggy Hamilton married James in 1848, making a stepmother relationship
plausible, but the later marriage does not concretely exclude premarital
maternity. Peggy is therefore retained as an uncertain mother; Mary of the
1847 workhouse family remains another leading candidate.
