# Findings: Glasgow-2986

Last updated: 2026-10-07

## Current conclusion

The 1847 workhouse return records Margaret Glasgow, 16, of Glenleslie. The
1851 Ballycregagh mortality return calls Margaret a twenty-year-old daughter
of [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]] who died in winter 1850. Peggy Hamilton's
1848 marriage means her maternity is unproved, not impossible. Mary of the
matching workhouse group is the stronger candidate, but Peggy remains attached
uncertain until a better-supported replacement can be made.

## Source findings

- [Ballymena Workhouse admissions](https://www.billmacafee.com/otherrecords/ballymenaworkhouse.pdf), registration 4410, Margaret Glasgow, age 16.
- [1851 detailed census transcription](https://durrushistory.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/1851irishcensusreturnscommadelimitedtextfilejul24_071.pdf), Ballycregagh household 29 mortality schedule, Margaret, daughter, age 20, died winter 1850.

## Suggested correction

Retain James as a confident father and Peggy as an uncertain mother pending a
better replacement; record death in winter 1850. Retain County Antrim as an
uncertain estimated birth and death place.
