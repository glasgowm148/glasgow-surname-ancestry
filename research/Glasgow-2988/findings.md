### Record-bearing passages to audit

- James Glasgow was born about 1720. His birth date is an estimate.
- He was recorded in the 1766 religious census at Corvanaghan, Kildress parish, County Tyrone. His name appears in the record as James Glasgo.
- Corvanaghan in Kildress parish should not be confused with Gortnavreeghan, also recorded as Gortnafreechan, Gortnafrechan and similar spellings, in Templeport parish, County Cavan. The leases dated 1717 and 1774 concerning Gortnavreeghan in County Cavan relate to a different place and have therefore been removed from this biography.
- This James should not presently be merged with James Glasgow (abt.1700-abt.1759). That profile is associated with a will-index entry for James Glasgow of Drumearn, Derryloran parish, dated 17 January 1759.
- Drumearn and Corvanaghan are separate townlands in different County Tyrone parishes. The 1766 Derryloran return also contains two men named James Glasgo at Drumearn, so the existence of a James Glasgow in each location is not enough to establish that they were the same person.
