# WikiTree update notes: [John Glasgow (Glasgow-2990), born about 1750, Derryloran, County Tyrone, Ireland](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2990)

## Suggested additions

The original 1766 Derryloran religious return records John Glasco at
**Drumearn**, immediately after two separate entries for `Jams Glasco` in the
same townland.<ref>National Archives of Ireland, Parliamentary Return 665,
1766 religious census for Derryloran, `IHP/1/665`, [https://virtualtreasury.ie/item/NAI-IHP-1-665
original return and transcription], accessed 17 July 2026; independently
transcribed in PRONI `T808/15264`.</ref>

This confirms John's occurrence and local cluster, but the return gives no age
or relationship.
