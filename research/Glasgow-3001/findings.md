## Current conclusion

The cited parliamentary table proves a James Glasgow associated with a
Killybegs collector balance entered under 1728. It does not prove birth about
1650, Scottish origin or death in 1728. He belongs in an early-eighteenth-
century Donegal working cluster, not the seventeenth-century count.
