== Biography ==

John Glasgow was christened in 1649 in the parish of Kirknewton and East Calder, Midlothian, Scotland. He was the son of [[Glasgow-3610|John Glasgow]].<ref name="Birth1649">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-X6G : 16 September 2021), Johne Glasgow, 1649.</ref> His known siblings included [[Glasgow-3112|Margaret Glasgow]] and [[Glasgow-3118|Alexander Glasgow]].{{Citation needed }}

He married '''[[Currie-4998|Margaret Currie]]''' in 1673 in Kirknewton.<ref name="Marriage1673">"Scotland, Marriages, 1561-1910", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XY9W-J5Q : 11 February 2020), Johne Glasgow, 1673.</ref>

The cited entries place children of John Glasgow and Margaret Currie at Glencorse in1676,1680 and1684. Charles’s maintained account gives a later baptism at South Leith in1686; the conflicting date and parish in this father account require the original register to be checked.

John and Margaret had the following children:
* [[Glasgow-3011|Robert Glasgow]], christened 29 December 1676 in Glencorse.<ref name="Robert1676">Old Parish Registers, Baptisms, Glencorse, Midlothian. Ref: 687/ FR11.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-3013|William Glasgow]], christened 26 December 1680 in Glencorse.<ref name="William1680">Old Parish Registers, Baptisms, Glencorse, Midlothian. Ref: 687/ FR16.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-3085|Margaret Glasgow]], christened 13 April 1684 in Glencorse.<ref name="Margaret1684">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4J-FXD : 16 September 2021), John Glaskow in entry for Margarat Glaskow, 1684.</ref>
