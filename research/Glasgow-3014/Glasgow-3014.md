== Biography ==

That entry includes '''JOHNE GLASGOW''' in its Parents/Other details field, supporting the comparison without explicitly stating the relationship.<ref name="JonBurial1638">National Records of Scotland, Old Parish Registers, deaths and burials, Duddingston, parish 684, reference 60/16, 15 January 1638, JON GLASGOW; [https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/ Scotland's People], exact-surname Glasgow search to 1700, index extract supplied 10 September 2026. == Research Notes ==

The proposed infant death/burial identification rests on name, parish, associated Johne and the four-day interval. Check the original for a kin term, age or residence before treating this as a proved death or exact death date.

A second '''Jon Glasgow''' entry occurs on '''23 August 1641''', with '''JON GLASGOW''' in the other-details field.<ref name="JonBurial1641">National Records of Scotland, Old Parish Registers, deaths and burials, Duddingston, parish 684, reference 60/24, 23 August 1641, JON GLASGOW; [https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/ Scotland's People], same index extract supplied 10 September 2026. Original image not inspected.</ref> This John and [[Glasgow-1559|John Glasgow]], recorded at Duddingston in April 1639, are chronological candidates for the later entry if the 1638 burial does not belong to this child.
