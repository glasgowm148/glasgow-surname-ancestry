# Findings: [John Glasgow (Glasgow-3015), Duddingston father](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3015)

## Conclusion

The 11 January 1638 Duddingston entry directly identifies John Glasgow and
Helen Patoune as parents of John-3014. It does not identify the father's birth
or parents.

Use **born before about 1618, place unknown, uncertain**, estimated from
parenthood, rather than presenting Duddingston 1618 as a recorded birth.
[Glasgow-1084](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1084) is a possible
same-person lead only; do not merge without a parish or marriage bridge.
