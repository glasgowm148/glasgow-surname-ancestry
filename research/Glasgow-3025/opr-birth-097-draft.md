== Research Notes ==

An Old Parish Register index records Marion Glasgow and William Glasgow at Kirknewton and East Calder on 10 June 1681.<ref name="Marriage1681">Scotland's People, ''Old Parish Registers: banns and marriages'', index entry for William Glasgow and Marion Glasgow, 10 June 1681, Kirknewton and East Calder, parish 690, reference 10/82, [https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/ Scotland’s People] (original image not inspected; supplied reciprocal index entries, reviewed 10 September 2026).</ref> The entry is compatible with this Marion by name, district and age. It does not identify either person's parents or residence, and the combined banns/marriages index cannot prove that 10 June was the wedding date. The original register is needed before attaching the recorded William as a structured spouse.
