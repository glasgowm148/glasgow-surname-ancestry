## Current conclusion

Father assessment: **uncertain; no present leading candidate**. The attached
[Archibald Glasgow (Glasgow-1097)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1097)
is chronologically possible but has no identified property or personal link to
Isabella. [Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-1096)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1096)
was previously preferred because he twice appeared with David Howe. Protocol
2448 now shows, however, that the John Howe who later became a Glasgow dyer was
the son of **Peter Howie, dyer, and Isobella Maxwell**, not the documented
David Howe household. The Robert-David association therefore does not bridge
to Isabella's marriage and should carry no material weight in choosing her
father.

## Relationship reconstruction

- **Confirmed spouses:** [Isabella Glasgow (Glasgow-3026), adult by 1600,
  Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3026) and John Howe, dyer and
  citizen of Glasgow.
- **Probable identity:** Isabella's husband John Howe, dyer in 1600, was the
  John Howee whom Peter Howie, dyer, and Isobella Maxwell called their son in
  1582. Keep the identification qualified because no surviving act repeats
  both his parents and wife.
- **No leading father candidate for Isabella:** neither Archibald nor Robert
  has a demonstrated link to her.
- **Do not treat as proved:** Robert Glasgow as Isabella's father, or the
  1557/1560 Robert as certainly Glasgow-1096.
