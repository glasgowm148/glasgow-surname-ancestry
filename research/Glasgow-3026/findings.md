# Findings: [Isabella Glasgow (Glasgow-3026), born 1560, Glasgow, Lanarkshire, Scotland](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3026)

## Current conclusion

Father assessment: **uncertain**. [Robert Glasgow (about 1520), Glasgow-1096,
fisher and burgess of Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1096) is
now the leading candidate, but is not proved. Robert appeared with David Howe
in two Glasgow transactions in 1557 and 1560, including as a witness when David
Howe and Cristina Lyndesay acquired South-Port-to-Clyde-bridge property. By
1600 Isabella was married to John Howe, dyer, in the same southern burgh
corridor. This repeated cross-family association is better evidence than the
previously cited general proximity to Archibald Glasgow, but no record calls
Isabella Robert's daughter or John Howe David's son.

## Relationship reconstruction

  Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3026) and John Howe, dyer and
  citizen of Glasgow.
- **Leading father candidate only:** [Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-1096), active
  1557-1596, Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1096).
- **Do not treat as proved:** David Howe as John's father; Robert Glasgow as
