== Biography ==
Marione was born in 1642. She is the daughter of [[Glasgow-2459|Thomas Glasgow]] and [[Thomsone-70|Agnes Thomsone]].

== Research Notes ==
=== Inveresk father and combined-profile identity ===

The Inveresk father Thomas Glasgow is represented provisionally by [[Glasgow-1103|Thomas Glasgow]]. The Inveresk baptisms identify a Thomas Glasgow household. The 1637 and 1642 entries name Agnes Thomsone/Thomson with Thomas, and William’s 1639 transcript names the same pair. Agnes’s 1634 entry names Thomas only. These children are provisionally attributed to [[Glasgow-1103|Thomas Glasgow]], whose combined profile also contains the Glasgow baptism of 1612 and the Linlithgow family.

The record-defined Inveresk father is Thomas Glasgow, but his identification with the combined [[Glasgow-1103]] profile remains uncertain. That profile also represents the Glasgow child baptised in 1612 and the Linlithgow father associated with Hellin Bryse. A 1612 birth allows ages about 22, 25 and 30 at the Inveresk baptisms, but does not prove that adult identity. The profile’s estimated 1639 Bryse marriage overlaps the Agnes Thomsone household, which continues to December 1642. A later remarriage could fit the 1644 Bryse child, but is not established; a distinct Inveresk Thomas remains an alternative. Jannet’s 1640 Linlithgow entry names no mother. Keep these households distinguishable and do not infer a death or remarriage date.

Agnes’s mother is not named in her indexed entry; her Agnes Thomsone attribution rests on the family reconstruction. Marione’s recorded 13 December birth and 18 December christening are distinct dates. [[Glasgow-3077|James Glasgow]] is a circumstantial naming-pattern placement. [[Glasgow-3050|Margaret Glasgow]] has a conflicting William Glasgow/Bessie Howie index identification and is not corroboration of this sibling group.

The father’s combined identity remains a working reconstruction.
