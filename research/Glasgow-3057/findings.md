# Findings: [Christina Glasgow (Glasgow-3057)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3057)

## Current conclusion

Christina Martin's family monument is in a Glasgow cemetery and records her death in April 1902 aged 54. This supports **Glasgow, Lanarkshire, Scotland** as an uncertain death location; a burial monument does not by itself prove the place of death.

## Source findings

| Evidence | Finding |
|---|---|
| [Gravestone Photographic Resource, Necropolis iota Cemetery, monument 37, ref. 267589](https://www.gravestonephotos.com/public/cemeterylistimages.php?cemetery=2031&limit=26) | Identifies Christina Martin, 1848-1902, on the Martin/Glasgow family monument in Glasgow. |
| Monument inscription | Names her as Charles Martin's mother and records death in April 1902 aged 54; it also names Elizabeth Glasgow and other relatives. |
