## Current conclusion

Father assessment: [George Glasgow (estimated about 1569), merchant and burgess
of Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1099) is **confirmed by
record** as Jonet's father.

The 25 November 1624 burgess admission identifies the same couple represented by
the profiles: John Stewart, mason, was admitted as the eldest lawful son of the
deceased mason Walter Stewart and as the husband of Jonat, lawful daughter of
George Glasgow, merchant, burgess and guild brother. Jonet's attached husband is
[John Stewart (Stewart-62467)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Stewart-62467), and
their first known child was baptised in Glasgow in March 1625. Her attached
father George-1099 is independently documented in Glasgow, married by 1598, and
described in the profile and civic material as a merchant and burgess. The
combined spouse, occupation, place and chronology resolve the named daughter to
this profile rather than merely identifying an otherwise unknown Jonat Glasgow.

The admission does **not** name Jonet's mother. Margaret Steuart remains the
chronologically probable mother because she married George in 1598, but that
particular edge is not proved by this record.
