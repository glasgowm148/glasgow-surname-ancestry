# New-profile draft: Robert Fultoun, spouse of Marion Glasgow

== Biography ==

Robert Fultoun was the husband of [[Glasgow-3062|Marion Glasgow]]. Their lawful
son [[Fultoun-5|James Fultoun]] was baptised at Glasgow High Kirk on 7 August
1610. John Howie and James Barr witnessed the baptism.<ref>Glasgow & West of
Scotland Family History Society, ''Glasgow High Kirk Baptisms 1609-1777'', 7
August 1610, vol. 1/320, James Fultoun, lawful son of Robert Fultoun and Marion
Glasgow; witnesses John Howie and James Barr,
[https://www.gwsfhs.org.uk/records/james-fultoun/ public record index].</ref>

The record does not state Robert's age, parents, occupation, exact residence or
death details.

=== Research Notes ===

Suggested creation data:
* Birth: before about 1592; date uncertain and place unknown.
* Spouse: [[Glasgow-3062|Marion Glasgow]], certain from their son's baptism.
* Child: [[Fultoun-5|James Fultoun]], certain.

Do not merge this Robert into [[Fultoun-4|James Fultoun]]. Fultoun-4 was
created from the same baptism but bears the wrong forename; the record
explicitly identifies the father as Robert.
