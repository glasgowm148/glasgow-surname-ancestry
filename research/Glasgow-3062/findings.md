# Findings: [Marion Glasgow (Glasgow-3062), born 1592, Glasgow, Lanarkshire, Scotland](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3062)

## Current conclusion

The 7 August 1610 Glasgow High Kirk baptism directly establishes that Marion's
husband was **Robert Fultoun** and that their lawful son was
[James Fultoun (Fultoun-5), born 1610, Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Fultoun-5).
The currently attached [James Fultoun (Fultoun-4), born 1590](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Fultoun-4)
is not the father named in the record and should be removed from this family.
Do not merge the two men: create a distinct Robert Fultoun profile.

Marion's own father remains uncertain. [Ninian Glasgow (Glasgow-3188), born
about 1550, Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3188) is plausible,
not proved.

## Paste-ready biography addition

On 7 August 1610, James Fultoun was baptised at Glasgow High Kirk as the lawful
son of Robert Fultoun and Marion Glasgow. John Howie and James Barr witnessed
the baptism.<ref>Glasgow & West of Scotland Family History Society, ''Glasgow
High Kirk Baptisms 1609-1777'', 7 August 1610, vol. 1/320, James Fultoun,
lawful son of Robert Fultoun and Marion Glasgow; witnesses John Howie and James
Barr, [https://www.gwsfhs.org.uk/records/james-fultoun/ public record index].</ref>

== Research Notes ==

Marion was named as a legatee in the 1607 testament of Jonet Meik, wife of
Ninian Glasgow, but the testament does not describe Marion as their daughter.
Her current parentage therefore remains uncertain.
