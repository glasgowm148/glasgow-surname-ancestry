== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' was the father in a St Cuthbert's parish family by 1658. No record of his own birth has been identified. His birth before about 1640 is an adult-status estimate from his parenthood, and St Cuthbert's is retained only as an uncertain estimate from his daughter's parish record.

James and [[Clerk-6355|Helen Clerk]] were the parents of [[Glasgow-3092|Agnes Glasgow]], baptised at St Cuthbert's on 15 August 1658.<ref name="Agnes1658">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:F318-T2H James Glesgow in entry for Agnes Glesgow, 1658]; accessed 19 August 2026.</ref>

The date and place of James's death have not been identified.

== Research Notes ==

The attached [[Glasgow-1103|Thomas Glasgow]] and [[Thomsone-70|Agnes Thomsone]] remain uncertain parents. Agnes's baptism names this James as her father but does not identify his parents.

[[Glasgow-3337|James Glasgow]], whose children with Annapill or Anabell Glessie begin in 1661, is a strong same-person candidate. One widower with consecutive wives would fit the chronology, but no death, remarriage or other identifying record has yet bridged the two fathers. The 1661 FamilySearch record concerns [[Glasgow-3076|the younger James]] in that Annapill family; it is not a late christening for this profile.<ref name="James1661">"Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:F316-KWL James Glesgow, 1661]; accessed 19 August 2026.</ref> The profiles should not be merged without further evidence.
