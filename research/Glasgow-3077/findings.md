# Findings: [James Glasgow (Glasgow-3077), St Cuthbert's](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3077)

## Conclusion

The 15 August 1658 baptism of Agnes directly establishes a St Cuthbert's father
James Glasgow and mother Helen Clerk. The profile's own parentage remains
uncertain.

[Glasgow-3337](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3337) may be the same
father after remarriage: that household's children with Annapill/Anabell begin
in 1661 and continue in 1663 and 1665. The sequence is strong circumstantial
evidence, but no inspected death, remarriage or other record identifies the two
fathers as one man.

## Recommended update

- Retain Helen Clerk and Agnes-3092 as directly supported family.
- Keep the attached Inveresk parents uncertain; the baptism does not name them.
- Add Glasgow-3337 as a possible duplicate, **do not merge yet**.
- Search St Cuthbert's deaths and marriages between August 1658 and June 1661.
