## Current conclusion

Father assessment: [William Glasgow (Glasgow-3075), born about 1590, probably Scotland](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3075) is **confirmed by three records**.

The 1618 South Leith baptism names Archibald's father as William. More decisively for the adult identity, Archibald's 1647 service of heir calls him a shoemaker living in Leith and expressly calls William Glasgow, mealmaker in Leith, his father. The shared names, Leith residence and paternal relationship securely identify the heir with the baptized child and the deceased William with the Leith mealmaker whose testament was registered in 1646.

## WikiTree-ready reconstruction

Archibald Glasgow was baptized at South Leith on 20 March 1618 as a son of William Glasgow. On 1 May 1647 he was formally served heir to his father. The national abstract describes him as a shoemaker living in Leith and his father William as a mealmaker who had lived in the same town. This occupational description independently matches William Glasgow, mealmaker in Leith, who died in September 1645 and whose testament dative was registered on 22 July 1646. The father-son link can therefore be marked certain.
