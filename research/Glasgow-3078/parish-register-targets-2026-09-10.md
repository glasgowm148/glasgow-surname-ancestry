parish marriage citation, not CC8/8/70. The reference is copied from the public

## Capture checklist

1. Confirm parish, page, entry date and whether birth and baptism have separate dates.
2. Retain the whole entry and neighbouring context, not just the surname crop.
3. Transcribe both parents, occupation, abode, witnesses and marginal annotations.
4. Look for an express father William the mealmaker, a former trade, or a property link.
5. Treat repeated witnesses as supporting association, not proof of kinship or identity by themselves.
