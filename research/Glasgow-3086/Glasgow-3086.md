== Biography ==

Margaret Glasgow was born before 3 June 1688, when she was baptized at Kilwinning, Ayrshire, Scotland. She was the daughter of [[Glasgow-3087|John Glasgow]] and [[Smith-351282|Bessie Smith]].<ref name="fmp-baptism">Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564-1929'', transcript “Margaret Glasgow in 1688,” baptism 3 June 1688, Kilwinning, Ayrshire, father John Glasgow, mother Bessie Smith, archive reference OPR 599/1, item 1, page 28; record ID SCOT/OPR/BAP/3624944; [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3624944&tab=this transcript], accessed 4 September 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The parish transcript gives 3 June 1688 as Margaret's baptism date and does not state her date of birth. A structured birth date of 3 June should therefore be treated as an estimate or baptism-date proxy unless separate birth evidence is found. The exact date, place and parents distinguish her from [[Glasgow-3162|Margaret Glasgow]], baptized at Kilwinning later in 1688 to different parents.
