# Findings: [John Glasgow (Glasgow-3093)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3093)

## Current conclusion

[John Glasgow (Glasgow-1084)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1084) is
ruled out as the father named in the cited baptism. The 9 July 1648 Mid Calder
entry identifies the child's father as **Jamis Glasgow**, and the live tree now
attaches [James Glasgow (Glasgow-3332)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3332).
Whether Glasgow-3332 is the correct identity of that Jamis remains a separate
question; a man named John cannot replace the recorded father.
