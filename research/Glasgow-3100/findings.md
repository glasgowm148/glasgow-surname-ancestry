## Current conclusion

Reviewed: 2026-10-15

George is the record-defined father of [Alexander Glasgow (Glasgow-3101)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3101), with [Grizel Dunlop (Dunlop-3562)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Dunlop-3562) named as mother in the 1659 Campbeltown indexes. These are representations of one parish event, not independent events. The original image has not been inspected; retain both literal dates and reconcile the volume/page conventions.
