== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' was baptised at Edinburgh on 16 March 1679, a son of [[Glasgow-2682|James Glasgow]] and [[Duffe-18|Elizabeth or Isobell Duffe]].<ref>“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950,” FamilySearch database, [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQC9-Q9N James Glasgow, 1679].</ref>

The same parents had another son named James, baptised on 18 April 1680.<ref>“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950,” index entry for James Glasgow, baptised 18 April 1680 at Edinburgh, son of James Glasgow and Elizabeth/Isobell Duffe.</ref> These are consecutive child records, so [[Glasgow-3368|James Glasgow]] remains a distinct same-name sibling. Reuse of the name suggests that the elder child may have died before the later baptism, but no burial or other survival record proves that conclusion.

An Edinburgh banns-and-marriages index records James Glasgow and [[Dobie-449|Jean Dobie or Wood]] on 28 July 1698.<ref>Scotland's People, [https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/ Old Parish Registers], banns and marriages, Edinburgh parish 685/1, reference 450/47, 28 July 1698; index supplied 10 September 2026. The original entry has not been inspected.</ref> The index's “or Wood” is an alternative surname for Jean as indexed; it does not show whether it was an alias, prior surname or another convention.

This profile is attached to Jean, with sons [[Glasgow-3102|Peter Glasgow]] and [[Glasgow-2473|Robert Glasgow]], but those relationships remain uncertain. Peter's 1699 baptism proves an adult father James Glasgow at Edinburgh,<ref>“Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950,” FamilySearch database, [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:F3BL-B2K Peter Glasgow, 1699].</ref> not whether he was the child baptised in 1679 or his 1680 brother. No inspected record bridges either infant to the adult marriage or proves Robert's parentage.
