# Findings: [James Glasgow (Glasgow-3103), baptised 1679](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3103)

## Conclusion

James was baptised at Edinburgh on 16 March 1679 to James Glasgow and
Elizabeth/Isobell Duffe. Another son James was baptised to the same parents on
18 April 1680. These are consecutive child records, not demonstrated duplicate
profiles; the repeated name strongly suggests the elder child died young.

No inspected record identifies the 1679 child as the James who later married
Jean Dobie. The 1699 baptism of Peter proves an adult father James and may prove
Jean as mother when the original is checked, but it does not state which infant
James survived.

## Recommended update

- Retain [Glasgow-3368](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3368) as a distinct sibling.
- Mark Jean Dobie and children uncertain on this profile pending burial/marriage evidence.
- Search for the elder James's burial between March 1679 and April 1680.
- Do not merge the brothers.
