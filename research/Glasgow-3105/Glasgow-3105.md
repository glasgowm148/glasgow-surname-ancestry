== Biography ==
James Glasgow is represented in two 1689 Kilwinning baptism records as a father. [[Glasgow-4134|Anne Glasgow]] was baptised on 22 May 1689 with James named as father; the surviving index does not name her mother.<ref name="anne">Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564–1929'', Kilwinning Old Parish Register, Anne Glasgow, baptised 22 May 1689. The individual indexed entry is cited on Anne's profile.</ref> [[Glasgow-3104|James Glasgow]] is indexed on 14 July 1689 with parents James Glasgow and Margaret Park.<ref name="james">FamilySearch, “Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XTVB-3YQ James Glasgow, 1689]. The date is an index date and should be checked against the original register before calling it a birth date.</ref>

The two father profiles, [[Glasgow-4178]] and this one, were combined as the best current identification. Both describe an adult James Glasgow in Kilwinning in 1689; neither supplies distinct parents, occupation or death that separates them. This is an inferred cross-record identity rather than an explicit statement in either baptism. The May and July dates are baptism/index dates for two children, not proof of two births to one mother within those months. Margaret Park is recorded as mother of the July James; Anne's mother remains unidentified by her cited index. An original-page comparison may alter this assessment.

The older profile gave James a birth year of 1670 at Kilwinning. No baptism or birth record for this adult has been found. The year and place are uncertain estimates from his adult presence there by 1689, not an independently recorded birth. His death is unknown.

=== Selected uncertain Ayrshire family —16 October2026 ===

Select John [[Glasgow-3375]] as the uncertain working father of James [[Glasgow-3105]]. James is the combined working father account behind the two 1689 Kilwinning child entries, one naming Margaret Park. John is a record-defined father in that same parish in 1679 and 1689, associated with Barbara Alason/Allason. The rough 1650 and 1670 family generations would put John at about twenty when James was born, and both generations could have children recorded during 1689. Neither year is a recorded birth; John’s captured before-about 1661 boundary is an adult-status estimate and permits an earlier birth. No date is changed to fit this placement. Prefer this recorded local adult as a provisional parent-generation anchor over assigning James directly to the older John-1604 scaffold or to an otherwise unidentified father. The shared parish and compatible generational spacing supply the logic, at very low confidence; no source names James as John’s son.

John-1604 remains a possible older father rather than a rejected alternative. His approximate 1626 birth and 1664 death are unsourced; a literal 1670 James birth would conflict with that death estimate, requiring at least one estimate to be wrong. Neither is silently adjusted. Hew-1066 or another local Glasgow remains possible; the current shortlist is not exhaustive. John-3375 may be the same man as the minister John-1073. If so, the minister’s approximate 1653 generation would imply younger parenthood around 1670, still dependent on James’s unrecorded birth. No merge, proved brotherhood, ministerial occupation for John-3375 or new father for the minister is inferred. John’s own attachment to John-1604 and the older Hugh-1603 scaffold retain their existing uncertainty. Barbara Alason is the recorded/probable mother of John’s known children, not a newly selected mother of James. James’s mother stays unknown. Margaret Park is recorded for the July 1689 child; Anne’s May 1689 index omits a mother. The combined James father account and the two children’s sibling status remain provisional. Two index/baptism dates two months apart do not prove two births to one mother. No witness, occupation, property or inheritance bridge ties James to John.
