== Biography ==
'''James Glasgow''' married '''Agnes Watt''' (recorded as Agnes Waitt or Waitte) on 18 June 1663 in Polwarth, Berwickshire, Scotland.<ref name="marriageRecordScotlandsPeople">“James Glasgow and Agnes Waitte,” Old Parish Registers - Banns and Marriages, National Records of Scotland (ScotlandsPeople), Parish Number 754, Ref 10/62, Frame 37, Polwarth, Berwickshire; marriage date 18 June 1663.</ref><ref name="marriageRecordFS">"Scotland, Marriages, 1561-1910", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XTL8-X6D : 11 February 2020), James Glasgow, marriage to Agnes Waitte, 18 Jun 1663, Polwarth, Berwickshire, Scotland.</ref> His parentage remains unconfirmed. While some online trees suggest a birth year around 1640,<ref>See, for example, the FamilySearch profile GKWN-35S which associates this marriage with a 1640 birth year, though this profile demonstrably conflates individuals (see Possible Confusions section).</ref> this is an estimate likely based on his marriage date; he was probably born in the early-to-mid 17th century.

Polwarth is a parish located in the historic county of Berwickshire, near the town of Duns. Following their marriage, James and Agnes appear to have settled in the adjacent parish of Gordon, Berwickshire. A series of baptism records in Gordon list a James Glasgow (or Glascow) as the father of several children between 1666 and 1679. Given the proximity of Gordon to Polwarth, the consistent father's name, and the timing relative to the 1663 marriage, these children are very likely the issue of James and Agnes Watt:
*   '''[[Glasgow-3117|John Glasgow (1666-)]]''', christened 29 April 1666.<ref name="johnBaptismFS">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XTBS-Z9H : 16 September 2021), James Glascow in entry for John Glascow, christened 29 Apr 1666, Gordon, Berwickshire, Scotland.</ref>
*   '''[[Glasgow-1440|Patrick Glasgow (abt.1667-)]]''', christened 4 August 1667.<ref name="patrickBaptismFS">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XTBS-98Z : 16 September 2021), James Glasgow in entry for Patrike Glasgow, christened 4 Aug 1667, Gordon, Berwickshire, Scotland.</ref>
*   '''[[Glasgow-3364|Jean Glasgow (1671-)]]''', christened 16 April 1671.<ref name="jeanBaptismFS">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYLM-Y4Z : 16 September 2021), James Glasgow in entry for Jean Glasgow, christened 16 Apr 1671, Gordon, Berwickshire, Scotland.</ref>
*   '''[[Glasgow-3106|Robert Glasgow (1673-)]]''', christened 1 April 1673.<ref name="robertBaptismFS">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYHJ-3R9 : 16 September 2021), James Glasgow in entry for Robert Glasgow, christened 1 Apr 1673, Gordon, Berwickshire, Scotland.</ref>
*   '''[[Glasgow-3363|Agnes Glasgow (1676-)]]''', christened 29 October 1676.<ref name="agnesBaptismFS">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYLM-Y4H : 16 September 2021), James Glasgow in entry for Agnes Glasgow, christened 29 Oct 1676, Gordon, Berwickshire, Scotland.</ref>
*   '''[[Glasgow-3365|Margaret Glasgow (1679-)]]''', christened 24 May 1679.<ref name="margratBaptismFS">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYL9-Q32 : 16 September 2021), James Glasgow in entry for Margrat Glasgow, christened 24 May 1679, Gordon, Berwickshire, Scotland.</ref>

The christening of a daughter named Agnes in 1676 strongly supports the identification of this family unit with the 1663 marriage of James Glasgow and Agnes Watt, following common naming patterns. While the indexed baptism records from FamilySearch do not include the mother's name, the circumstantial evidence linking these Gordon children to James and Agnes (Watt) of Polwarth is compelling.

Further evidence of the family's presence in Gordon comes from the marriage record of their daughter, Jean. On 3 June 1702, '''John Smith''' married '''Jean Glasgow''' in Gordon, Berwickshire. The record explicitly names the bride's father as '''James Glasgow'''.<ref name="jeanMarriageFS">"Scotland, Marriages, 1561-1910", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XTLH-1MB : 11 February 2020), James Glasgow in entry for John Smith and Jean Glasgow, 3 Jun 1702, Gordon, Berwickshire, Scotland.</ref> This confirms Jean survived to adulthood and indicates the family's continued connection to the Gordon parish into the early 18th century. The record identifies James as Jean's father but does not necessarily confirm he was still living in 1702.

Beyond these records, specific details about James's occupation or death date remain unconfirmed in contemporary Scottish sources.

== Research Notes ==
=== Gordon household review — 17 October 2026 ===

'''Gordon household review, 17 October 2026:''' The six Gordon children form the probable household of James Glasgow and Agnes Watt; named father roles are documented, maternity is probable.

Prefer one household: James Glasgow, indexed with Agnes Waitte at Polwarth on 18 June 1663, is probably the James who fathered the Gordon children John (1666), Patrike (1667), Jean (1671), Robert (1673), Agnes (1676) and Margrat (1679). The father’s repeated full name, the neighbouring Berwickshire parishes, the first child three years after marriage and the orderly thirteen-year child sequence form a coherent family. The daughter Agnes supplies modest naming support; it is not the deciding evidence. The maintained 1702 Gordon marriage citation for Jean also names father James, adding a compatible adult continuation for her. Read together, these favour a single local family over six isolated fathers or an unrelated Polwarth couple. No concrete competing contemporary Gordon James household has been identified in the maintained material. That is the scope of this comparison, not a claim to have exhausted the population.

The child indexes explicitly name James as father but omit the mother. Their named-father roles are documented for the record-defined children; identifying that father with the 1663 Polwarth husband is probable. Agnes Watt is separately assessed as probable mother of all six children on the marriage, locality and sustained family sequence. This is an inference, not literal maternity in the indexes; a later marriage or another local James remains possible. Jean’s later marriage and the later adult identities of John and Patrick remain separately assessed. Patrick’s proposed Kilclone son and migration, James’s own John Glasgow-1084 ancestry and the unrelated Kilwaughter James are not strengthened. No original register image was newly inspected: this review combines the previously accepted index entries and maintained citations.

The cited Gordon index dates are retained separately from the profile date and place fields. John’s Duns attribution needs comparison with the Gordon baptism citation; Patrick’s 8 April date differs from the 4 August index, and Robert’s 4 January date differs from the 1 April index. These discrepancies require the original entries. The indexes do not by themselves establish exact birth dates. James’s own parents and the later adult identities and migrations of the children remain separate questions.

=== Possible Confusions with Ulster Glasgows ===
Genealogical research on this James Glasgow is complicated by the presence of other men with the same name in 17th-century records, particularly in Ulster, Ireland. Several online genealogies and collaborative trees (such as FamilySearch profile GKWN-35S, which attaches the Polwarth marriage, the Gordon baptisms, ''and'' a 1725 death date) have mistakenly connected James Glasgow of Polwarth and Gordon to the James Glasgow who settled in Kilwaughter Parish, County Antrim, Ireland. This Kilwaughter James appears in the 1669 Hearth Money Rolls for County Antrim and reportedly died in 1726.<ref name="kilwaughterInfo">This information regarding the Kilwaughter James is commonly cited in Glasgow family histories but requires primary source verification for the death date and details. Secondary sources often cite a James Glasgow in Kilwaughter, Co. Antrim, appearing in the 1669 Hearth Money Rolls and dying c. 1726. Primary source verification is recommended. This individual is considered distinct from James Glasgow of Polwarth/Gordon.</ref>

Crucially, there is currently no known documentary evidence directly linking the James Glasgow who married Agnes Watt in Polwarth (1663) and raised a family in Gordon, Berwickshire (c. 1666-1679+), to the James Glasgow recorded in Kilwaughter, County Antrim. The established family life in Berwickshire during the period the Kilwaughter James was documented in Ireland strongly suggests these were two distinct individuals. Researchers should treat them separately. The 1725/1726 death date often associated with James Glasgow belongs to the Kilwaughter individual, not the James of Polwarth and Gordon.
