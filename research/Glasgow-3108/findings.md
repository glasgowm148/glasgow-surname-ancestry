# Hugh Glasgow (Glasgow-3108) research findings

## Current conclusion

The 1696 Irvine baptism and confident parents define
[[Glasgow-3108|Hugh Glasgow]]. He is a strong candidate for a later
Irvine/London sailor, but identity is not proved.

## Identity assessment — Irvine/London sailor

The printed Edinburgh testament index names Hugh Glasgow, sometime sailor in
Irvine and thereafter in London, on 12 March 1751. Correct Findmypast mariner
and testament rows confirm that date. The apparent 3 February 1733 row is a
parser conflation with the following James Glasgow entry. See the
[source-correction dossier](../hold-resolution/record-dossiers/fmp-glasgow-a0b809464cc9-hugh-glasgow-index-conflation.md).

This profile fits the name, Irvine and chronology, but the testament index
does not name Hugh's age, parents, spouse, vessel, executor or London address.
Several other Ayrshire Hugh profiles remain viable.

## Recommended action

Do not amend this profile from the 1751 record without a second identifier.
