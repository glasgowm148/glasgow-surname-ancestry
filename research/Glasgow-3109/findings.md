# Findings: [Hugh Glasgow (Glasgow-3109), Irvine family](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3109)

## Conclusion

The 1696 records define a father Hugh Glasgow associated with Jean Service and
children at Irvine. They do not record his birth or parents, so `about 1676` is
an estimate rather than an identity statement.

[Glasgow-1067](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1067), baptised at nearby
Kilwinning in 1676 and later associated with Mary Dean from 1699, is a strong
duplicate candidate. A Jean-Service first marriage followed by Mary Dean is
possible, not yet proved.

## Recommended update

- Retain born before about 1676 and Irvine as explicit adult-status and
  child-parish estimates until better evidence supplies replacements.
- Retain Jean Service and the 1696 children as a record-defined family.
- Add Glasgow-1067 as a hold-merge candidate.
- Do not merge without a death/remarriage or another continuous identifier.

Applied live on 19 August 2026. The two record-defined child-parent links and
the co-parent relationship with Jean Service are confident; the identification
with Glasgow-1067 remains uncertain.

## Identity assessment — Irvine/London sailor

The apparent 1733 sailor entry is malformed. The printed Edinburgh testament
index places Hugh Glasgow, sometime sailor in Irvine and thereafter in London,
on 12 March 1751; the next James Glasgow entry owns the 3 February 1733 date.
Correct Findmypast mariner and testament rows corroborate 1751. See the
[source-correction dossier](../hold-resolution/record-dossiers/fmp-glasgow-a0b809464cc9-hugh-glasgow-index-conflation.md).

[[Glasgow-3109|Hugh Glasgow]] remains chronologically possible but would be an
older candidate. The index supplies no age, parent, spouse, vessel, executor
or address, and therefore does not establish this identity.

## Recommended action — Irvine/London sailor

Make no profile amendment without an independent sailor/London identifier.

## Identity assessment — Hugh Glasgow, preacher of the gospel, 1677

A printed Register of Deeds index (*Register of Deeds, 1677. Name and Designation*, vol. XVII, p. 80; FamilySearch item #007830797, Item 3, image 48, [Scotland. Land Records 1677](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CS39-S9V2-4)) names Hugh Glasgow, preacher of the gospel, as grantee in a deed recorded on 8 March 1677 (Durie Office, vol. 42, p. 148; National Records of Scotland RD3/42). Seen as an index image; the deed itself has not been read. The full-text scan of 22–23 September 2026 lists this profile as one of several unresolved candidates, with Glasgow-1603 (where the entry is attached as a possible match) and Hew-1066.

The entry gives no residence or family. It fits this profile only if the Irvine father of 1696 was already an adult in 1677. Inference: if this Hugh is the same man as Glasgow-1067, baptised in 1676, he cannot be the 1677 preacher. Grade: possible at most; nothing is attached here.
