== Research Notes ==

The baptism readings conflict. A Findmypast/Scottish Genealogy Society transcript gives 26 November 1649, daughter of John Glasgow, Kirknewton and East Calder, OPR 689/20 p.5.<ref>Findmypast, ''Scotland Parish Births & Baptisms'', [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F4473472 transcript SCOT/OPR/BAP/4473472], accessed 15 October 2026; original image not inspected.</ref> The maintained FamilySearch account gives 11 November 1646. Neither reading should supersede the other, or establish a second child, before the original pages are compared. The 1649 reading is Findmypast/SGS, not a verified ScotlandsPeople reading.

An Old Parish Register index records Margaret Glasgow and William Thomsone at Kirknewton and East Calder on 2 September 1674.<ref name="Marriage1674">Scotland's People, ''Old Parish Registers: banns and marriages'', index entry for Margaret Glasgow and William Thomsone, 2 September 1674, Kirknewton and East Calder, parish 690, reference 10/73, [https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/ Scotland’s People] (original image not inspected; supplied index, reviewed 10 September 2026).</ref> The entry is compatible with this profile by name, district and age, but it supplies no parent or residence. It is a combined banns/marriages index, so the original register must be checked before treating the date as a proved wedding date or adding a structured spouse.
