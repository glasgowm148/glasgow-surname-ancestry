== Research Notes ==
=== Andrew’s Kirknewton father ===

Prefer Alexander [[Glasgow-3118]] as Andrew [[Glasgow-1546]]’s father at 58% subjective confidence, possible. The particular Alexander household is established at Kirknewton by the 1667 Margaret Smith marriage and the father-named Alexander and Agnes entries in 1673 and 1677. Andrew’s 1700 Christian Cook marriage and fatherhood of James in 1702 place his own adult household in the same parish, one generation later. The sustained parish setting and feasible household succession together favour this existing family. Andrew, Alexander and Agnes recur in the descendant branches, providing modest supporting context rather than an independent naming rule. This weighs a specific family against alternatives, rather than inferring fatherhood from surname and estimated ages alone.

The 1685 birth is an unsourced estimate. Taken literally it makes Andrew about fifteen at the indexed 1700 marriage and seventeen at the 1702 christening. Those ages are possible, but they cannot independently support the pedigree. A birth earlier in the 1670s or early 1680s would also fit the adult events and Alexander’s family chronology; no replacement birth year is adopted. Alexander’s own circa-1650 birth is likewise an estimate. The argument rests chiefly on the recorded adult households, not on moving either birth date to fit.

No checked source names Andrew’s father. Another Kirknewton father remains possible: the older Andro account [[Glasgow-1319]], or one of the local John or William households, cannot be excluded merely because no Andrew baptism has been identified. John [[Glasgow-1057]] of Kilwinning/Stevenston remains a weaker comparison because his documented household is elsewhere. Common forenames and the inherited father attachment must not be counted as independent confirmation. The complete sibling and child sequences are not established. Margaret Smith is not assigned as Andrew’s mother.

Check the Kirknewton register around the 1667 marriage and the known 1673/1677 children for an earlier Andrew, and compare residences, witnesses and occupations with the 1700 Cook household. Inspect the original marriage and 1702 child entry when available. === Parish parent review — 17 October 2026 ===

Alexander’s 1673 Kirknewton birth/baptism index explicitly names father Alexander Glasgow. The existing [[Glasgow-3118]] account is the same-parish man indexed marrying Margaret Smith in 1667, six years earlier, with a compatible father generation and a later Agnes child association in 1677. The exact father name, parish and ordered household chronology together favour that existing adult as the named father. Assess the attachment as probable rather than leaving it ungraded. This is an inference identifying the indexed father with the 1667 adult; the child entry alone does not identify that adult by wife, trade or property.

Another Alexander could have fathered the 1673 child. The marriage and baptism supply no inspected cross-reference, and the father’s circa-1650 birth is an estimate, not an age statement. Margaret Smith is not named as mother in the reviewed 1673 entry and receives no maternity upgrade. [[Glasgow-1546]] remains an uncertain proposed son of the older Alexander. [[Glasgow-1141]] remains an unproved child of the 1673 Alexander; the latter himself remains a possible older father behind the 1721–1732 child series. The 1673 birth/baptism dates, the 1701 and 1705 marriages and the later children’s maternity questions are retained. The older [[Glasgow-3610]] father route is not strengthened.
