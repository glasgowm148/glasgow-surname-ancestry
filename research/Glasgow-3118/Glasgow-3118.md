== Research Notes ==
=== Parish parent review — 17 October 2026 ===

Alexander’s 1673 Kirknewton birth/baptism index explicitly names father Alexander Glasgow. The existing [[Glasgow-3118]] account is the same-parish man indexed marrying Margaret Smith in 1667, six years earlier, with a compatible father generation and a later Agnes child association in 1677. The exact father name, parish and ordered household chronology together favour that existing adult as the named father. Assess the attachment as probable rather than leaving it ungraded. This is an inference identifying the indexed father with the 1667 adult; the child entry alone does not identify that adult by wife, trade or property.

Another Alexander could have fathered the 1673 child. The marriage and baptism supply no inspected cross-reference, and the father’s circa-1650 birth is an estimate, not an age statement. Margaret Smith is not named as mother in the reviewed 1673 entry and receives no maternity upgrade. [[Glasgow-1546]] remains an uncertain proposed son of the older Alexander. [[Glasgow-1141]] remains an unproved child of the 1673 Alexander; the latter himself remains a possible older father behind the 1721–1732 child series. The 1673 birth/baptism dates, the 1701 and 1705 marriages and the later children’s maternity questions are retained. The older [[Glasgow-3610]] father route is not strengthened.
