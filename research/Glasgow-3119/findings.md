# Findings: [Alexander Glasgow (Glasgow-3119), Kirknewton](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3119)

## Conclusion

The only exposed direct source is Alexander Glasgow's marriage to Janet Graham
on 16 November 1705. The profile's structured 1673 birth, attached father and
biography statement `born in 1685` are mutually inconsistent and unsourced.

The attached son [Glasgow-1141](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1141)
was not documented as a 1703 child of this couple; his profile estimates birth
from his own later children and assigns the father by name and locality.

[Glasgow-1314](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1314) is a high-priority
duplicate candidate, but a merge would combine two wives and potentially
overlapping childbearing.

## Recommended update

- Retain the 1705 marriage.
- Clear or mark the 1673/1685 birth and father uncertain until the original is checked.
- Mark Glasgow-1141 uncertain or detach him; the current evidence does not prove the edge.
- Hold the Glasgow-1314 merge pending the 1673 OPR image and wife/child audit.
