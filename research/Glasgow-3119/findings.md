# Findings: [Alexander Glasgow (Glasgow-3119), Kirknewton](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3119)

## Conclusion

WikiTree now redirects Glasgow-3119 to
[[Glasgow-1314|Alexander Glasgow]]. Do not maintain a separate person under the
former ID. The canonical live profile preserves the 16 November 1705 marriage
to Janet Graham together with the sourced 1673 birth and baptism and the 1701
marriage to Margaret Aikman.

The merge did not prove every imported relationship. [[Glasgow-1141]] remains
an uncertain child because no child-parent record has been found, and the 1707
James and 1711 Marion baptisms name only father Alexander. The former 1685
birth estimate remains unsupported.

## Recommended update

- Use the [canonical Glasgow-1314 findings](../Glasgow-1314/findings.md).
- Retain the sourced 1673 event and both indexed marriages.
- Keep Glasgow-1141 uncertain pending positive relationship evidence.
- Do not reopen or recreate Glasgow-3119 as a separate profile.
