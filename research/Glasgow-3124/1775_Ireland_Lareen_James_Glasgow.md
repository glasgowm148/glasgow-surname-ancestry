== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''', gentleman of “Larine,” was trustee to the Parke-Clarke settlement in Registry of Deeds memorial 209151, dated 27 May 1775. Historical “Laryn” and “Larin” forms identify the place with modern Lareen in Rossinver parish, County Leitrim.<ref>Registry of Deeds memorial 209151, Parke-Clarke settlement, 27 May 1775.</ref>

An unnamed Mr Glasgow had been the previous adjoining occupier of Samuel Parke's Ballyshannon tenement in memorial 206114 on 30 April 1765.<ref>Registry of Deeds memorial 206114, Samuel Parke tenancy, 30 April 1765.</ref> The same Parke family and the short distance between the places make James the probable earlier occupier, but the omitted forename prevents conclusive identification.

No record inspected links him to [[Glasgow-3960|John Glasgow of Gortaggle]] or another Irish cluster.
