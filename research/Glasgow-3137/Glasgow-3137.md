== Biography ==

James Glasgow was a Protestant householder at Corvanaghan in Kildress parish, County Tyrone, in 1766.<ref name="Kildress1766">National Archives of Ireland, Parliamentary Return 667, 1766 religious census for Kildress, IHP/1/667, [https://virtualtreasury.ie/item/NAI-IHP-1-667 Virtual Treasury catalogue and original return]; [https://by2022-prod.adaptcentre.ie/loris/RCBL_23_176.JPG/full/full/0/default.jpg RCB Library transcript, p. 176].</ref> The return gives no age, spouse, children, occupation or later history. His birth is known only to have occurred before the return was compiled.

== Research Notes ==

=== Possible duplicates ===

James is the strongest currently documented local candidate for [[Glasgow-1024|James Glasgow of Moneymore]], the reported father of Robert Glasgow. Corvanaghan is about nine miles west of Moneymore, and no later Irish record has yet been identified for this householder. No source connects him to Mary Harkness, Robert, Arthur, Joseph, emigration about 1766–70 or the 1804 Pennsylvania naturalisation entry.

[[Glasgow-2988|Glasgow-2988]] may represent this same householder, but its estimated birth and attached family relationships are not established by the 1766 return. [[Glasgow-1970|Glasgow-1970]] represents James and his Mitchell wife, who were described in an 1879 county history as migrating from Scotland to northern Ireland about 1750 and settling near Cookstown. The Corvanaghan return does not establish that they were the same family.

The 1826 Kildress tithe list names David and John Glasgow at Muntober, John at Whitehill and William at Oritor, but no Glasgow occupier at Corvanaghan.<ref name="Kildress1826">[https://www.irishgenealogyhub.com/tyrone/tithe-applotment-books/parish-of-kildress.php Kildress Parish Tithe Applotment transcription, 1826].</ref> This later absence does not prove that the 1766 householder emigrated.
