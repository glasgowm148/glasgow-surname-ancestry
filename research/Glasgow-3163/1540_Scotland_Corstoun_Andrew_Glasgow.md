== Biography ==

'''Andrew Glasgow''' (''Andro Glasgw'') lived at a place read uncertainly as
“Corsoun,” in the parish read as “Calder Clair” or “Calder Cleir.” He made his testament on 6 February 1597/8,
died on 7 February, and the testament was confirmed on 10 July 1598.<ref
name="Andrew1598">National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court,
CC8/8/32, images 123–124, testament testamentar and inventory of Andro Glasgw;
working transcription of the original register images. Printed index: Scottish
Record Society, ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments,
1514–1600'', [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso01scotuoft/page/n117/mode/1up
p. 108].</ref>

His wife was Elizat Stevinsoun. The will directly calls Robert Glasgow Andrew's
son by Elizat and names daughters Marion, Janet and Isobel. Elizat was to enjoy
Andrew's land (one preceding word is unclear) until Robert came of age. Provision for a child
with whom she might be pregnant was conditional and does not prove a further
birth.<ref name="Andrew1598" />

== Research Notes ==

The former reading of Jonet Anderson and children Marion, James, Andrew and
William is not supported by images 123–124. The exact identities of Andrew's
proved wife and four children remain under duplicate review. No source in the
testament identifies Andrew's parents.
