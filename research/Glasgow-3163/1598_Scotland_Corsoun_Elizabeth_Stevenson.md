# DOCUMENTARY ASSOCIATE — NO SEPARATE PROFILE CREATION

Elizat Stevinsoun is directly proved as Andrew Glasgow's wife and Robert's
mother. Publish those facts in the Glasgow profiles, but do not create or attach
a separate Elizat profile: [[Stevinsoune-1]] remains a compatible possible
remarried identity that the surviving records cannot resolve.

== Biography ==

'''Elizat Stevinsoun''' was the wife of [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow of
uncertain Corsoun]]. Andrew's testament expressly calls Robert Glasgow his son by
Elizat:

<blockquote>“Robart Glasgw his sone begottin be him vpoun the said Elizat his
wyff”</blockquote>

Andrew directed that Elizat should “bruik his [word unclear] land quhill his
said Robart cum to aige,” giving her possession or enjoyment of the land until
Robert came of age. He also made a conditional provision “incais his said spous
be with bairne to him” and provided that if “Elizat his spous do not hir pairt
to his said bairnis,” she was to be removed from administration.<ref
name="Andrew1598">National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court,
CC8/8/32, images 123–124, testament testamentar and inventory of Andro Glasgw,
made 6 February 1597/8, death stated as 7 February, confirmed 10 July 1598;
corrected working transcription of the original register images. Printed
index: Scottish Record Society, ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register of
Testaments, 1514–1600'', [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso01scotuoft/page/n117/mode/1up
p. 108].</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Suggested profile data ===

* Gender: female.
* Last name at birth/current surname: Stevinsoun; retain Stevenson as the
  modern spelling or other-surname form rather than silently replacing the
  manuscript spelling.
* Birth: before the birth of son Robert and before 6 February 1597/8; exact
  date unknown.
* Birth place: [Corsoun?], Calder Cleir parish, Scotland, uncertain. This is
  inferred from Andrew's household and is not stated as Elizat's birthplace.
* Death: after 6 February 1597/8; exact date unknown.
* Death place: [Corsoun?], Calder Cleir parish, Scotland, uncertain, if a
  location is required. This is derived from her own documented residence and
  administration of Andrew's household, not from a death record.
* Spouse: [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow]], certain.
* Child: Robert Glasgow, certain. Andrew's daughters Marion, Janet and Isobel
  are not expressly called Elizat's children in the secure clauses.

The possible unborn child is conditional only and must not receive a profile.

=== Duplicate audit ===

[[Stevinsoune-1|Bessie Stevinsoune]], estimated born about 1580, married George
Lugtoun at Dunfermline on 25 February 1606. The register then records children
John on 3 February 1607 and Annas on 27 June 1609. This evidence makes George's
surname clearer, but does not resolve Bessie's earlier identity: Andrew died in
1597/8, so a 1606 remarriage is possible, and the register gives no prior
marital status, parents or residence for Bessie. Conversely, nothing connects
her to Corsoun, Andrew, Robert or the land Elizat held until Robert came of age.
[[Stevenson-7398|Elizabeth Stevenson]], estimated born about 1570 and married in
Yorkshire in 1606, is excluded as the present match: her profile consistently
represents Robert Blaikstone's Yorkshire wife and supplies no Scottish link.

This is a terminal no-action disposition for the present publication batch,
not a person-creation task. A later Dunfermline record, Morton estate paper or
other source may identify or exclude Bessie as Andrew's widow and permit a new
identity decision.

The Dunfermline evidence was checked in the Findmypast transcript
`R_695585805/2` and Henry Paton's Scottish Record Society edition
[https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/parish_register_of_dunfermline_1561-1700.pdf
''Parish Registers of Dunfermline, 1561–1700''] (1911), pp.
125, 129 and 138 (register years 1606, 1607 and 1609), accessed 8 September
2026.
