# Findings: Mary Glasgow (Glasgow-3167), probable wife of Robert Elder

Last updated: 2026-08-18

## Current conclusion

The original 1871 civil marriage directly identifies Mary Glasgow, aged **28**, of
Lisnagaver as a daughter of **Alexander Glasgow, farmer**, and wife of Robert
Elder of Gortereghy, son of Nathaniel Elder. Her residence and Robert's later
succession to Alexander's valuation lot 25 make identification with
Alexander-951's family strong.

Her age excludes identification with Maryan Glasgow, 17, recorded in John
`Glasgow-1022`'s household in 1851: Maryan would have been about 37 in 1871.
The marriage and lot succession therefore do not connect Alexander to John.

The 1901-1911 Gortahar household is the strongest located continuation of this
couple, but not yet a proved match. It contained an older Mary Elder, children
Mary, Robert and Jane, and in 1911 Robert J. Elder's two-year-old nephew
**Samuel Glasgow**. Samuel's parents remain unidentified.

The Mary Jane Elder who died at Lisnagaver in 1897 should not be merged with
Mary-3167 as established fact. If the Gortahar woman is the 1871 bride, Mary was
alive in 1911. The competing identity must be resolved through children's civil
births, the original census forms and Samuel's birth.

## Next proof targets

- Original 1901 and 1911 household forms.
- Civil births for Mary, Robert J. and Jane Elder, beginning in Ballymoney district.
- Samuel Glasgow's birth and any guardianship or school record naming his parents.
- Death or probate evidence distinguishing the Gortahar Mary from Mary Jane Elder who died in 1897.
