## Current conclusion

PRONI references support a Belfast-associated shipmaster active from at least
1667 and a Belfast will in 1686. The profile's birth about 1600 is unsupported.
He is a genuine Belfast maritime root witness, but no reviewed source identifies
him with the Kilwaughter James or supplies parents or children.

## Branch placement - 31 August 2026

James falls outside the user's pre-1600 birth filter, but his supplied island
was also reviewed. His best current placement is as a **possible son or younger
relative** of [Robert Glaskow of Larne (Glasgow-3970)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3970)
and possible collateral kin of the adjacent Kilwaughter householders
[John (Glasgow-3913)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3913) and
[James (Glasgow-1081)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1081).

Belfast, Larne and Kilwaughter form a credible north-east Antrim maritime and
estate corridor, and a shipmaster active by 1667 fits the next generation after
Robert's 1653 occurrence. This is geographical/chronological evidence only.
The former categorical exclusion of identity with the Kilwaughter James is
superseded by the dated audit below. The 1686 will/estate reference has not been
confirmed as a death or probate date, so it cannot alone exclude later survival.

## Identity and civic-status audit — 21 September 2026

Retain separate Belfast and Kilwaughter record groups: neither identity nor
kinship is proved. The earlier August exclusion relied on 1686 versus 1726,
but a will date alone does not prove death. A confirmed 1686 probate or death
would exclude identity with the later Kilwaughter officer, while leaving open
whether the shipmaster was the 1669 householder. The householder's identification
with the officer of 1700–1726 is itself a same-name continuity hypothesis.

Young's printed p.271 supplies **free commoner**, not a demonstrated governing
burgess office. Its mariner entry supplies no father, spouse, child or
apprenticeship master. The detailed local brief preserves the 21 January
1669/70 date and ten-shilling fee; this audit recovered the entry in independent
OCR as `James Glosco`, but did not newly verify its date visually.

The full [NLI-catalogued 1686 extract](https://sources.nli.ie/Record/MS_UR_012684),
PRONI T/828/15, was not recovered: the catalogue returned HTTP 403. That is an
access limit, not evidence that the surviving extract lacks kin. No new relative
is established. [Search scope and handoff](../larne-proof-2026-09-21/early-james.md).
