# Findings: [Agnes (Glasgow) Logan (Glasgow-3178)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3178)

## Current conclusion

Agnes was the wife of John Logan, a trumpmaker at Stevenston, and died before
her testament dative was recorded on 4 March 1628. The testament-calendar entry
does not name either parent.

Her former profile attached [James Glasgow elder
(Glasgow-1168)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1168) as father and
[Margaret Smith (Smith-374110)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Smith-374110) as
mother. Margaret is attached as wife of James elder's son
[James Glasgow younger (Glasgow-3921)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3921),
not of James elder. No inspected record calls Agnes a daughter of any of these
people. Both parents have therefore been detached and left blank rather than
converting the cross-generation placement into another unproved relationship.
