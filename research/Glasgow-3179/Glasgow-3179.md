== Biography ==

'''Geillis Glasgow''' was the spouse of [[Wilson-127012|Thomas Wilson]] at
Chaplehill in Ardrossan parish. Her testament states that she died in January
1603 and was confirmed on 11 May 1603.<ref name="GeillisWill">[https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/search-records/legal-records/wills National Records
of Scotland, wills and testaments], Glasgow Commissary Court, testament dative
and inventory of Geillis Glasgow, CC9/7/3, images 113–114; working transcription
reinspection supplied 6 September 2026.</ref>

James, Isobel and Bessie Wilson are identifiable in the executor clause. The
words between James and Isobel remain unresolved, so [[Wilson-142005|James
Wilson]] should be treated only as a possible son, not as a directly proved
lawful son.<ref name="GeillisWill" />

Geillis's estate owed money to a James Glasgow in Stevenston.<ref
name="GeillisWill" />

== Research Notes ==

The testament does not identify Geillis's parents or state James Glasgow's
relationship to her. [[Glasgow-3921|James Glasgow younger of Corsankell]]
remains an uncertain father candidate only.

The debts also name the lord of Eglinton and servants Robert Boyd and a Bessie
whose surname is unresolved; Robert and Bessie were owed wages. The servant
Bessie is not thereby identified with Bessie Wilson in the executor clause.<ref
name="GeillisWill" />

[[Glasgow-1495|Katherine Glasgow]] also died in January 1603 in neighbouring
Stevenston parish. The common date, adjoining parishes and Stevenston creditor
justify researching them as a kin network; they do not prove sisterhood.

The published testament index and Findmypast transcript are finding aids for
the original.<ref>Findmypast, Scotland Will and Testament Index,
[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FWILLS%2F057431 transcript],
Geillis Glasgow, 11 May 1603.</ref>
