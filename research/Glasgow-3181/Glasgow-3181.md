## Suggested corrections

1. Remove [Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-1096), born about 1520, Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1096) as father. The evidence used for that attachment identifies Robert's son John as alive in 1613, but this John died in 1576.
2. Attach [John Glasgow (Glasgow-1093), born about 1510, Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1093) only as an uncertain father. Glasgow burgh protocols prove that he had a son John, but no record identifies that son as the Hirmanscheillis testator.
3. Retain [Katherine Glasgow (Glasgow-3590), born before 1576, Hirmanscheillis](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3590) as a confirmed daughter.
4. Retain [Marjorie Glasgow (Glasgow-3589), born before 1576, Hirmanscheillis](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3589) as a confirmed daughter, but change that profile's FirstName from `Margaret` to `Marjorie`.
5. The attached wife [Margaret Hutcheson (Hutcheson-1656), Scotland](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hutcheson-1656) should be corrected to **Margaret Hoghame** if, as the current attachment intends, she represents the testament widow. Do not create a second wife merely to correct the surname.

## Do not add as fact

- Do not identify John with the Glasgow-burgh son of John Glasgow-1093 as proved.
- Do not retain or restore [Ninian Glasgow (Glasgow-3188), born about 1550, Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3188) as a sibling. Ninian's testament names a brother John alive in 1613, whereas this John died in 1576; Ninian was also directly recorded as a son of Robert Glasgow.
- Do not treat `Hutcheson` as a spelling variant of `Hoghame` without a separate record.
