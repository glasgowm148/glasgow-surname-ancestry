== Biography ==

'''John Glasgow''' was the man indexed in the sixteenth century as being “in Hirmenschelis.” His surviving testament is a testament dative—an estate administration prepared after his death—not a will dictated by him. The entry was recorded on 4 May 1577. Its death clause says that John died in '''March''', but the year-numerals have not been read securely.<ref name="JohnTestament">National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/5, images 276–277, testament dative and inventory of Johne Glasgw. The published index is ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments, 1514–1600'', [https://archive.org/details/commissariotreco16scot/page/n117/mode/2up?q=Glasgow p. 109], entry dated 4 May 1577.</ref><ref name="FMPIndex">[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FWILLS%2F057444&tab=this Findmypast, Scotland, Will and Testament Index 1481–1807, record SCOT/WILLS/057444], John Glasgow in Hirmenschelis, 4 May 1577. This is a derivative index entry.</ref>

=== Family ===

Margaret, John's widow, submitted the inventory on behalf of the children. Her surname is written in a Cochrane-like form and is probably '''Cochrane''', although the exact reading remains uncertain. The record names two lawful children, Katherine and a second child whose forename is provisionally read Androw or Andrew. The confirmation later repeats that they were executors-dative to John, “thair father.” The pronoun before “lawful bairns” is unresolved, so the wording does not independently prove that Margaret was their mother.<ref name="JohnTestament" />

A later debt entry says that '''Katherin Glasgow, servant''' was owed wages. She may have been John's daughter Katherine, but that clause does not identify the servant as his child.<ref name="JohnTestament" />

=== Estate ===

The inventory describes a rural, livestock-based household. Secure or reasonably clear items include two cows, two two-year-old cattle, a mare, sheep of mixed ages, oats and barley, household furnishings and John's clothing. The debts include rent and servants' wages. Individual quantities, names and valuations should not be extended beyond what can be read from the manuscript.<ref name="JohnTestament" />

The clearer inventory wording gives two cows at £5 each, two cattle aged two
years at four merks each, two bolls of barley together valued at £5, and what
appears to be ten bolls of oats at two merks per boll. The number and value of
the sheep, the mare's valuation and the total remain unresolved. These were
historical Scots amounts, not modern pounds sterling.<ref name="JohnTestament" />

=== Debts and associates ===

The section headed ''Followis the dettis awand to the deid'' records sums owed
to John. Recoverable but variably secure names include Alexander Paterson,
William [Flint?], William Douglas, John Wilson, John [Millar?], John Mathie,
William [Weir?], Peter [Baillie?], James [Mathie?], and a person surnamed
Murray. Some entries concern grain. None of these financial entries states a
family relationship.<ref name="JohnTestament" />

Under ''Followis the dettis awand be the deid'', John apparently owed the
remainder of his rent to James [Stevinsoun?] of an unresolved place, wages to
Thomas [surname unclear], servant, and wages to Katherin Glasgow, servant. The
outgoing debts totalled £6. The free estate was then divided into three parts,
but the remaining figures have not been read securely.<ref name="JohnTestament" />

=== Confirmation and record boundaries ===

The useful confirmation wording repeats:

<blockquote>“the saidis Katharine & [Androw?] Glasgwis in executouris dativis
to the said umquhile Johne Glasgw thair father”</blockquote>

This is independent confirmation that John was the children's father. John's
entry occupies image 276 and the upper part of image 277. The later testament
on image 277 and the page labelled as a continuation of Andrew Hutchesoun's
testament are separate records and supply no evidence about John's family.<ref
name="JohnTestament" />

== Research Notes ==

=== Dates and place ===

The 4 May 1577 date belongs to the court register, not to John's death. March is clear in the death clause, but the following year is unresolved. The safest structured death statement is therefore before 4 May 1577, uncertain.

Hirmenschelis is supplied by the published and Findmypast indexes. The corresponding word in the manuscript remains difficult. Published Calder court extracts and a place-name study associate historical Hirdmanschiels forms with Hermand in West Calder, but that modern identification should remain qualified.<ref name="Hermand">Hardy Bertram McCall, ''The History and Antiquities of the Parish of Mid-Calder'' (1894), [https://www.electricscotland.com/history/westcalder/mid_calder.pdf Calder court extracts]; ''The Germanic Toponymicon of Southern Scotland'', University of Glasgow thesis, [https://theses.gla.ac.uk/1343/1/2003scott1phd.pdf place-name study].</ref>

=== Parentage ===

The attached [[Glasgow-1093|John Glasgow]] is a plausible but uncertain father. A Glasgow burgh protocol dated 13 May 1563 records John Glasgow senior resigning property to “John Glasgow junior, his son,” and another dated 8 January 1570/71 names “John Glasgw, younger son of John Glasgw.”<ref name="FatherProtocols">Robert Renwick, ed., ''Abstracts of Protocols of the Town Clerks of Glasgow'', vol. 3, protocol 748, 13 May 1563, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc06scogoog/page/n52/mode/2up scan]; vol. 6, protocol 1722, 8 January 1570/71, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc05scogoog/page/n58/mode/2up scan].</ref> Those records prove that Glasgow-1093 had a son John of the right generation, but no record yet identifies that burgh son with the Hirmenschelis testator.

This John cannot be the brother named in [[Glasgow-3188|Ninian Glasgow's]] 1613 testament. Ninian's burgess admission identifies him as the second son of [[Glasgow-1096|Robert Glasgow]], fisher and burgess, while Ninian's testament names a brother John who was alive on 10 January 1613.<ref name="Ninian">James R. Anderson, ed., ''Burgesses & Guild Brethren of Glasgow, 1573–1750'', [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso43scotuoft/page/24/mode/2up p. 24], 23 October 1596; National Records of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary Court, CC9/7/9, testament of Ninian Glasgow, made 10 January 1613.</ref> The man represented here was dead by May 1577.

=== Wife and children ===

[[Hutcheson-1656|Margaret Hutcheson]] remains attached as John's wife, but the manuscript does not securely read Hutcheson, Hoghame or Hochaine. She is only a possible profile identity for the widow Margaret and should be treated as uncertain. Margaret's presentation of the inventory for John's children is consistent with her being their mother, but it is not proof of maternity.

[[Glasgow-3589|Marjorie Glasgow]] remains attached pending further evidence, but the high-resolution reading does not name Marjorie. Her parent links should be uncertain, and this testament should not be cited as proof that she existed.

[[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow]] of the Calder Cleir area is the leading possible identity for the record's Androw/Andrew because his name, generation and nearby Calder geography fit. His 1597/8 testament establishes a separate adult household but does not identify his father.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124, testament of Andrew Glasgow, 1597/8.</ref> The two men should not be connected without an additional record.
