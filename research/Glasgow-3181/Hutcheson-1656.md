== Biography ==

The testament dative of [[Glasgow-3181|John Glasgow]], indexed in Hirmenschelis and registered on 4 May 1577, names '''Margaret''' as his widow. The surname remains unresolved. Cochrane and Hochaine are suggested readings, and the identification of the widow with this Margaret Hutcheson is uncertain.<ref name="JohnTestament">National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/5, images 276–277, testament dative and inventory of Johne Glasgw; ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments, 1514–1600'', [https://archive.org/details/commissariotreco16scot/page/n117/mode/2up?q=Glasgow p. 109], entry dated 4 May 1577.</ref>

Margaret submitted John's inventory on behalf of [[Glasgow-3590|Katherine Glasgow]] and [[Glasgow-3589|Marjory Glasgow]], his lawful children and executors-dative. The confirmation calls John their father. The possessive before “lawful bairns” may read “his” or “thair.” Margaret's maternity remains possible.<ref name="JohnTestament" /><ref name="Reading">[https://glasgow.phenotype.dev/research/transcriptions/john-1577/#passage-john-widow-children Illustrated reading of CC8/8/5, images 276–277], including the accepted human reading m[ar]iory contributed by Double_Show_9316 and the unresolved possessive.</ref>

John died in March, but the year-numerals in the manuscript remain unresolved. He was dead by 4 May 1577.<ref name="JohnTestament" />

== Research Notes ==

The existing profile remains a possible identity for John's widow. The associated Hutcheson manuscript fragment leaves its connection to the testament unresolved. Kinship with the Lambhill and Gairbraid Hutchesons, including [[Hutcheson-1651]], has yet to be established. The existing spouse and mother attachments remain uncertain until another record identifies the widow or establishes maternity.<ref name="Reading" />

The accepted reading Marjory supersedes the earlier Androw/Andrew reading. The testament identifies the children as executors-dative for their father. That appointment alone leaves their inheritance rights and shares unresolved.<ref name="JohnTestament" /><ref name="Reading" />

No birth record survives for this Margaret. Scotland is retained as a broad uncertain location from the documented family context; no evidence establishes Hermiston or Glasgow burgh as her residence.
