== Biography ==

'''Margaret''' was the widow of [[Glasgow-3181|John Glasgow]], the man indexed in Hirmenschelis whose estate was recorded on 4 May 1577. Her surname in the high-resolution manuscript is Cochrane-like and is probably '''Cochrane''', but the reading is not sufficiently certain to replace the populated Hutcheson name on this profile.<ref name="JohnTestament">National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/5, images 276–277, testament dative and inventory of Johne Glasgw; ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments, 1514–1600'', [https://archive.org/details/commissariotreco16scot/page/n117/mode/2up?q=Glasgow p. 109], entry dated 4 May 1577.</ref>

Margaret submitted John's inventory for [[Glasgow-3590|Katherine Glasgow]] and a second lawful child whose forename is provisionally read Androw or Andrew. The record directly calls John their father. The pronoun before “lawful bairns” is unresolved, so Margaret's action is consistent with but does not independently prove that she was their mother.<ref name="JohnTestament" />

John died in March, but the year-numerals in the manuscript remain unresolved. He was dead by 4 May 1577.<ref name="JohnTestament" />

== Research Notes ==

This profile is an uncertain identity for John's widow. The readings Hoghame and Hochaine are not supported by the higher-resolution image, and no evidence has been found that the widow belonged to the Hutchesons of Lambhill and Gairbraid or was related to [[Hutcheson-1651]]. The attachment to John and the mother link to Katherine should remain uncertain until another record identifies Margaret by surname and relationship.

The high-resolution testament does not name [[Glasgow-3589|Marjorie Glasgow]] as a child. Marjorie's attachment should remain uncertain. [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow]] is a possible later match for the record's Andrew, but the identity is not proved.

No birth record survives for this Margaret. Scotland is retained as a broad uncertain location from the documented family context; no evidence establishes Hermiston or Glasgow burgh as her residence.
