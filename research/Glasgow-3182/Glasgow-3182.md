== Biography ==
Barbara Glasgow married, or had a marriage/proclamation entry with, [[Wilson-126696|Johne Wilsoune]] at South Leith in 1611. The South Leith OPR transcription gives 20 September 1611 (692/2 90/219, FR2993), while a FamilySearch marriage index gives 26 November 1611.<ref>South Leith OPR transcription, “GLASCOW BARBARA—JOHNE WILSOUNE,” 20 September 1611, parish 692/2, reference 90/219, FR2993.</ref><ref>“Scotland, Marriages, 1561–1910,” FamilySearch [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYML-ZBB XYML-ZBB], Barbara Glasgow and Johne Wilsoune, 26 November 1611.</ref>

The original register is needed to determine whether the dates represent proclamation and marriage or a transcription error. The spouse is supported, but the exact marriage date remains unresolved.

== Research Notes ==
No reviewed record identifies Barbara's parents. Her birth about 1590 is an uncertain estimate from the 1611 marriage; no birthplace is proved, and South Leith is not a documented death place.

Other adult Barbara Glasgows appear at Edinburgh in 1611 and 1616: [[Glasgow-567|Barbara Glasgow]], mother of Catharine Little, and [[Glasgow-3302|Barbara Glasgow]], wife of George Johnstoun. Either may be the same woman, but no record proves an identity. [[Glasgow-1102|Barbara Glasgow]], baptised in 1617, is a later child. Do not merge the adult profiles without an identity bridge.

=== Branch and identity assessment ===
Barbara's leading family placement is as a possible daughter or niece of [[Glasgow-3188|Ninian Glasgow]]. Ninian's probable son [[Glasgow-3075|William]] later lived at Leith, while Ninian's proved son Robert was a cordiner. Barbara's South Leith entry and the adjoining Edinburgh cluster of Glasgow women marrying cordiners and a tailor fit that household's geography and trade network, but no record names her father.

Barbara may also be the same woman as [[Glasgow-567|Barbara, mother of Catharine Little]], and [[Glasgow-3302|Barbara, wife of George Johnstoun]]. Catharine was born on 9 September 1611;<ref>FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4N-CDX baptism index for Catharine Little], Edinburgh, 29 September 1611.</ref> the Wilson entry is dated either 20 September or 26 November 1611;<ref>FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYML-ZBB Barbara Glasgow and Johne Wilsoune marriage index].</ref> and the Johnstoun marriage was in 1616. The sequence is possible because the Little baptism proves parenthood, not a marriage. This is a moderate identity hypothesis only; do not merge until the original 1611 entries and John Wilson's fate are resolved.

=== Selected local working father, 15 October 2026 ===
[[Glasgow-3188|Ninian Glasgow]] is the selected '''uncertain, low-confidence''' father in the local research tree. The existing branch assessment already favours Barbara as a daughter or niece of Ninian. Her 1611 South Leith marriage entry fits the Leith setting of Ninian’s possible son William and the adjoining Edinburgh craft households; Ninian’s documented son Robert was a cordiner. The estimated 1590 daughter generation fits Ninian’s estimated 1550 generation. Select daughter as the simpler working placement within this already identified household, while retaining niece as an alternative. This is a combined locality, generation and family-network inference.

Neither the marriage entry nor Ninian’s testament names Barbara as his daughter. The proposed Leith brother William is himself uncertain, and shared craft geography is suggestive rather than individual proof. Barbara may instead be Ninian’s niece or belong to another local family. If she is Barbara-567, the existing William-3522 father claim becomes a rival explanation. The adult Barbara identities remain separate, the 1611 date discrepancy stays open, and no mother is assigned.

This supersedes earlier advice to leave the comparison outside the local tree. It does not name a parent supplied by a new record or authorise a live parent attachment. No mother is inferred.
