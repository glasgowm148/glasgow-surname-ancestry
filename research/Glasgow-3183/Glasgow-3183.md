== Biography ==

'''Jonet Glasgow''' married '''Martin Ramsy''' at Mid Calder in 1624.<ref
name="Ramsy1624">“Scotland Marriages, 1561–1910,” database, FamilySearch,
[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XY9X-4PR Jonet Glasgow and Martin
Ramsy, 1624], Mid Calder, Midlothian, Scotland.</ref> The indexed marriage names
no parent and supplies no secure birth date.

== Research Notes ==

=== Parentage unresolved ===

[[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]] is presently attached as an uncertain father. He
is of a possible generation and men named James Glasgow fathered children at
Mid Calder in 1605, 1610 and 1616, but no record identifies this Jonet as his
daughter.<ref>FamilySearch indexes: [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BK-1BW
George Glasgow, 1605], [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BJ-L51
Beatrix Glasgow, 1610], and [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BL-JXY
John Glasgow, 1616]. These entries identify a father James, not the 1624 bride's
father and not necessarily one internally unique man.</ref>

The attachment was made after an earlier reading of [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew
Glasgow of uncertain Corsoun]]'s testament was thought to exclude a daughter Jonet.
The corrected reading instead names Andrew's daughters Marion, '''Jonet''' and
Isobel, as well as his son Robert by Elizat Stevinsoun.<ref name="Andrew1598">National
Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124,
testament of Andro Glasgw, made 6 February 1597/8 and confirmed 10 July 1598;
corrected working transcription of the original register images.</ref>

The forename and Mid Calder setting make comparison with Andrew's daughter
important, but exact parish geography does not identify one woman. This profile
has no Corsoun/Stevenson, sibling, property or estate link and is not the
present match. If later evidence showed they were the same woman, Jonet was
alive by February 1597/8, so the profile's circa-1600 birth estimate would be
too late and the separate documentary profiles should be merged.
