# Findings: [Jonet Glasgow, wife of Martin Ramsy (Glasgow-3183)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3183)

## Current conclusion

Jonet married Martin Ramsy at Mid Calder in 1624. Her parents are not named in
that record.

The corrected reading of Andrew Glasgow's 1597/8 testament materially changes
the parentage assessment. It **does name a daughter Jonet**; the former claim
that Jonet was cancelled and excluded arose from the same transcription error
that produced the false Andrew and William child profiles. The name, generation
and Mid Calder locality make comparison important, but there is no record
equating the bride of 1624 with the child of `[Corsoun?]`. Exact parish
geography alone is not an identity bridge. This profile is excluded as the
present match at moderate confidence, and a distinct creation draft now
represents Andrew's daughter. A later direct link could still justify a merge.

The 31 August 2026 attachment to [[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]] was made partly
to replace the supposedly contradicted Andrew parentage. That premise is now
invalid. James remains a possible Mid Calder father by locality and generation,
but the marriage record does not name him. The relationship must stay uncertain
and should be reconsidered against Andrew rather than presented as a
moderate-to-strong placement.
