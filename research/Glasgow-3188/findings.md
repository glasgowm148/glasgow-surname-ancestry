## Current conclusion

Father assessment: [Robert Glasgow (about 1520), fisher and burgess of Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1096) is **confirmed by record**. The attached [John Glasgow (1516)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1093) should be removed as father.

[Elizabeth Glasgow, widow of Alexander Kaine](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3915)
is a plausible **possible older sister**, but should not be attached. Ninian's
son [Robert](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1027) and possible son
[William](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3075) each have a daughter
recorded as Elizabeth/Elizabethe or Bessie, creating an independent two-branch
naming echo. No record calls Elizabeth Ninian's sister, the name was common,
and her current `before 1530` birth only fits their father
[Robert](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1096)
if Robert's approximate 1520 estimate is substantially too late.

## Testament reinspection — 6 September 2026

The supplied working transcription of NRS CC9/7/9, images 142–145, corrects
and extends the family reading:

- Ninian's testament begins partway down image 142 and ends in the upper part
  of image 145; entries outside those boundaries belong to other estates.
- Ninian was a **fisher and burgess of Glasgow**, not a flesher.
- [[Hutcheson-1774|Jonet Hutchesoun]] is expressly his spouse and executor.
- [[Glasgow-1027|Robert Glasgow]] is expressly his son.
- John Glasgow is expressly his brother; [[Glasgow-1030]] remains the leading
  existing-profile candidate, not a proved identity.
- [[Glasgow-3062|Marioun (Marion) Glasgow]] is expressly his sister, not his
  daughter.
- William Glasgow occurs in the debt/damaged opening material, but the claimed
  words `his sone` cannot be securely recovered. [[Glasgow-3075]] remains only
  a **possible** son and possible identity match.

Jonet Hutchesoun's status as Ninian's spouse does not make her Robert's mother.
Robert's maternity rests on the separate 1607 testament of Jonet Meik. The
exact description of the clothing left to brother John is unresolved and
should not be quoted as `a new gown` without a clearer image.

Elizabeth Hutchesoun received £5, but her relationship is not stated. George
Glasgow, Ninian Hill, David Stevin and notary Mr Jeronimus Hutchesoun occur as
witnesses or in the administration; George and Ninian Hill recur in the
caution arrangements. Other named associates include James Stewart, James
Wilson, Alexander Fischer, John Morrison younger and Malcolm Muir. None is
called a parent, sibling or child. The shared Hutchesoun surname does not
establish how Elizabeth, Jonet and Jeronimus were related.

Use the complete replacement draft in `Glasgow-3188.md`; preserve these as
working manuscript readings rather than a finished diplomatic transcription.

## Possible daughters and Edinburgh/Leith cluster - 31 August 2026

Four disconnected women now have a coherent branch placement in Ninian's
household or immediate collateral circle. [Jonat Glasgow
(Glasgow-3262)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3262), mother of a child
baptised at Mid Calder in 1609, repeats the name of Ninian's wife Jonet Meik.
[Barbara-3182](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3182) had a South Leith
entry in 1611; [Margaret-3598](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3598),
[Barbara-3302](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3302) and
[Isobel-3336](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3336) married two
cordiners and a tailor at Edinburgh in 1611-19.

This is family-specific evidence: Ninian's proved son Robert was a cordiner;
his possible son William moved into Leith; and William's son was apprenticed
to a cordiner. The repeated trade/locality make the women possible
daughters/nieces, with Jonat-3262 the strongest individual daughter placement.
Neither testament names them as daughters, so no exact edge is confident.
