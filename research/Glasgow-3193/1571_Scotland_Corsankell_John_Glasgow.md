# New-profile draft: John Glasgow, son of James elder of Corsankell

== Biography ==

John Glasgow was a son of [[Glasgow-1168|James Glasgow elder]], a tenant at Corsankell in the parish of Stevenston, Ayrshire. On 9 February 1571, letters of reversion concerning the 40-shilling land of Corsankell identified James Glasgow and John Glasgow as sons of James Glasgow elder.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Charter Chest of the Earls of Glencairn, [https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/details.aspx?reference=GD39%2F5%2F59 GD39/5/59], letters of reversion concerning the 40-shilling land of Corsankell, 9 February 1571. The catalogue describes the land as occupied by James Glasgow elder, his sons James and John Glasgow, and Christian Campbell.</ref>

The same record proves that John was a brother of James Glasgow younger, who had already been separately designated "younger" beside James elder in the Cunninghame teind material of 1559-60.<ref>Margaret H. B. Sanderson, ''The People of Sixteenth Century Ayrshire'', ''Ayrshire Collections'', vol. 14, no. 8 (Ayrshire Archaeological and Natural History Society, 1987), appendix "The Cunninghame Teind Lists of 1559-60," [https://aanhs.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/people-of-ayrshire.pdf#page=36 p. 331], Corsankell entry. Sanderson cites Scottish Record Office, Register of Acts and Decreets, vol. 22, fols. 102v-167v.</ref>

=== Research Notes ===

Suggested creation data:
* Birth: before 1553, Scotland, uncertain. This assumes he was at least 18 when named among the occupiers in 1571; use before 1571 if applying only the strict record boundary.
* Death: after 9 February 1571, place unknown, uncertain.
* Father: [[Glasgow-1168|James Glasgow elder]], confirmed.
* Mother: unknown.
* Brother: James Glasgow younger of Corsankell, confirmed.

The record gives no age, birth order, mother, spouse, children, precise birthplace or later residence. The before-1553 estimate is not a baptismal date. Corsankell is a documented residence in 1571 and should not be entered as John's birth or death place.

[[Glasgow-3193|John Glasgow of Kilwinning]] is a plausible identity candidate because he was documented nearby in the same general period. However, no reviewed record connects the Kilwinning occurrences at Burrowland or Dalgarven with Corsankell. Keep the profiles separate unless a later record proves that they were the same man.

A live WikiTree name-and-date search on 18 July 2026 found the Kilwinning John
and [[Glasgow-3181|John Glasgow of Hirdmanscheillis]], but no profile securely
representing this record-proved son of James elder.
