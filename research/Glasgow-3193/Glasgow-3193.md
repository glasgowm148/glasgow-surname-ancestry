== Biography ==
'''John Glasgow''' was a son of [[Glasgow-1168|James Glasgow elder]] of Corsinkell, as directly stated in a 9 February 1571 letter of reversion. The document names both John and [[Glasgow-3921|James Glasgow younger]] as James elder's sons and occupiers of the 40-shilling land of Corsinkell.<ref name="NRS1571">National Records of Scotland, Charter Chest of the Earls of Glencairn, [https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/details.aspx?reference=GD39%2F5%2F59 GD39/5/59], letters of reversion concerning the 40-shilling land of Corsinkell, 9 February 1571. Official catalogue entry inspected 30 August 2026; the original is open but held off site and no manuscript image is available online.</ref> The document does not name their mother. John was alive on that date; his death is not recorded here. The confident father status applies to the record-defined Corsinkell son in this merged profile; it does not resolve the separate attribution of the Kilwinning appearances below.

The Cunninghame teind list for 1559–60 names a John Glasgow at Burrowland and names John Glasgow jointly with Agnes Wilson at Dalgarven, both in the Kilwinning district.<ref name="Teinds1559">Margaret H. B. Sanderson, ''The People of Sixteenth Century Ayrshire'', ''Ayrshire Collections'', vol. 14, no. 8 (Ayrshire Archaeological and Natural History Society, 1987), appendix “The Cunninghame Teind Lists of 1559-60,” [https://aanhs.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/people-of-ayrshire.pdf#page=38 p. 333]. Sanderson cites Scottish Record Office, Register of Acts and Decreets, vol. 22, fols. 102v-167v. See also [https://www.kilwinningheritage.org.uk/office/resources/documents/a-short-history-of-kilwinning-parish-2nd-ed.pdf ''A Short History of Kilwinning Parish''].</ref> Sanderson notes that a tenant could hold more than one property. These appearances may describe this John, but the list does not state his father and the Corsinkell document does not state his Kilwinning holdings. The combined regional pattern, rare name, overlapping period, and lack of a better identified local John make the identification probable rather than certain.

[[WIlson-130143|Agnes Wilson]] remains an uncertain spouse candidate. The teind list proves joint occupancy but does not call her John's wife. His birth before about 1540 is an adult-status estimate, and the earlier profile's Glasgow, Lanarkshire association was not based on a birth record. Burrowland, Dalgarven and Corsinkell are documented or proposed residences, not proved birth or death places.

== Research Notes ==
=== Saltcoats family: selected uncertain earlier father ===

Select John [[Glasgow-3193]], the recorded son at Corsankell in 1571 and probable Kilwinning tenant of 1559–60, as the Saltcoats sailor John [[Glasgow-4152]]’s uncertain working father. Saltcoats in Ardrossan adjoins the Stevenston–Kilwinning coastal parish cluster where this earlier Glasgow family is documented. A later-sixteenth-century son of the earlier adult could have been a working sailor and father of two daughters by 1636. The choice gives the Saltcoats household a provisional place in an existing local John line, using geography and a feasible generation. It is a low-confidence selection, not a named parent clause or an identification of the two Johns.

No source names the sailor’s parents or age. His former c.1600 birth was an unsupported creation estimate; it is not replaced by an invented older birth to fit this hypothesis. If a birth near 1600 is eventually established, the earlier John would have been an older father, and a grandson or more distant collateral placement could be more natural. Same given name is weak support and does not distinguish this candidate securely from [[Glasgow-3921|James Glasgow younger]], another member of the local family, or an unrecorded father. The sailor could belong elsewhere in that family or to another branch. The 1559–60 Kilwinning attribution to the Corsankell John is itself probable, although the 1571 Corsankell family is directly recorded. The same-person alternative is less economical: an adult in 1559 would have been about 95 or older at the sailor’s death in 1636, assuming adulthood at eighteen. That is an illustrative age comparison, not a proved exclusion. The father-versus-grandfather degree remains open. No mother is assigned to the sailor or to his daughters; Agnes Wilson’s joint tenancy does not make her his mother, and Isobell Gray’s widowhood does not prove her maternity. The girls’ already-proved father links remain proved. No identity merge, new original inspection, live WikiTree save or confidence upgrade is made. <ref>National Records of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary Court, CC9/7/27, testament dative and inventory of John Glasgow, sailor in Saltcoats, register pp.478–480, April 1637; death December 1636; original reviewed September 2026; [https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/search-records/legal-records/wills record search]. NRS [https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/details.aspx?reference=GD39%2F5%2F59 GD39/5/59], 9 February 1571, James Glasgow elder and sons James and John at Corsankell. Margaret H. B. Sanderson, People of Sixteenth Century Ayrshire, [https://aanhs.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/people-of-ayrshire.pdf#page=38 p.333], Kilwinning teind list, 1559–60. No record names the sailor’s father.</ref>


The former Glasgow-4061 profile represented the record-defined Corsinkell son, and this older profile represented the Kilwinning tenant. They were merged as the leading identification; the source-specific distinction above is retained. An original deed joining the Kilwinning holdings to Corsinkell would strengthen this inference. The earlier profile's structured death boundary was “after 1559”; the 1571 document now makes “after 9 February 1571” the supported boundary.

The Glasgow-burgh protocol of 8 January 1570 naming “Johne Glasgw yonger,” son of “Johne Glasgw,” concerns a different locality cluster. No property sequence connects that burgh family or [[Glasgow-1096|Robert Glasgow]] the fisher to this Ayrshire tenant.<ref>''Protocol Books of the Town Clerks of Glasgow'', entry dated 8 January 1570, naming “Johne Glasgw yonger,” son of “Johne Glasgw”; volume and folio remain to be confirmed.</ref> [[Glasgow-1030|John Glasgow of the later Glasgow-burgh network]] is also a poor match: his approximate 1570 birth and later burgh context do not fit an adult Corsinkell occupier in 1571.
