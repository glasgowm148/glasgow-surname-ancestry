## Current conclusion

Father assessment: [Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-1096), born about 1520,
Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1096) is **unsupported**. No
record or property sequence connects Robert the Glasgow fisher to this
Kilwinning man. The geographically closer John, directly called son of James
Glasgow elder at Corsankell in 1571, is the stronger competing hypothesis.

Preferred working placement: [James Glasgow elder (Glasgow-1168), Corsankell,
Ayrshire](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1168) is a **plausible father
candidate**. Corsankell in Stevenston parish is substantially closer to
Kilwinning than Robert's Glasgow-burgh household, and the 1571 record expressly
gives James a son John. The remaining issue is proving that this son was the
same John recorded with Agnes Wilson at Burrowland and Dalgarven in 1559-60.
