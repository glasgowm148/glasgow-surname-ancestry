an uncertain father because the record-defined son has been kept separately as

Father assessment: [Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-1096), born about 1520,
Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1096) is **unsupported**. No
record or property sequence connects Robert the Glasgow fisher to this
Kilwinning man. The geographically closer John, directly called son of James
Glasgow elder at Corsankell in 1571, is the stronger competing hypothesis.

Preferred working placement: [James Glasgow elder (Glasgow-1168), Corsankell,
Ayrshire](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1168) is a **probable father
candidate**. This is not based on proximity alone. The same 1559-60 teind list
places the rare same-name bearer at Burrowland and Dalgarven in Kilwinning and
places James elder with James younger at Corsankell in neighbouring Stevenston.
Sanderson expressly warns that one tenant could occupy multiple holdings. In
1571 the Corsankell title then names one John Glasgow as James elder's son and
co-occupier. No competing Cunninghame John has been identified outside the two
Kilwinning entries, which may themselves be one multi-holding man. The compact
date, name and adjacent-parish pattern makes Glasgow-3193 the leading and
probable match, although it still falls short of a certain identity because no
record carries a residence or spouse between the holdings.
