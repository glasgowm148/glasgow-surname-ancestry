== Biography ==

{{One Name Study|name=Glasgow}}

'''Mary Caroline Glasgow''' was born on 17 September 1785 in South Carolina.<ref name="Memorial">[https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/144856288/mary_c-wright Find a Grave memorial 144856288], Mary C. Glasgow Wright, Old Tranquil Methodist Cemetery, Newberry County, South Carolina (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

Mary married [[Wright-73441|Zaccheus Wright]] on 7 December 1809 in Abbeville, South Carolina.<ref name="WrightAccount">[https://www.geocities.ws/richmond01_2000/wright.html "Family: Wright"], account of Zaccheus Wright and Mary Glasgow (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref> Their daughter [[Wright-73437|Elizabeth Wright]] was born in 1815.

Mary died on 6 September 1841 and was buried at Old Tranquil Methodist Cemetery in Newberry County.<ref name="Memorial" />

== Research Notes ==

Mary was probably a daughter of [[Glasgow-1983|Robert Glasgow]] and [[Kinard-572|Elizabeth Kinard]]. Robert's 1800 household contained two girls aged 10–15, and Mary's birth date fits one of those places.<ref>[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/1800nbycensusG.htm "1800 Newberry District census"], Robert Glasgow household (accessed 12 August 2026); citing NARA microfilm publication M32, roll 50.</ref> The Wright family account names Robert and Elizabeth as Mary's parents, and Zaccheus administered Robert's estate in 1835.<ref name="WrightAccount" /><ref>[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/estates1785-1949/22.htm "Probate court general index to estates, 1785–1949"], Robert Glasgow, estate 1055, box 43, package 98 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

Together these points support an uncertain parent attachment, but none is a direct statement from Robert's records. His estate packet may identify Mary or Zaccheus as an heir.
