# Findings: [Mary Caroline Glasgow Wright (Glasgow-3226)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3226)

## Current conclusion

Mary Caroline is a probable daughter of Robert Glasgow and Elizabeth Kinard,
but the relationship is not yet direct. Her 17 September 1785 birth fits one
of the two girls aged 10–15 in Robert's 1800 household. A Wright family account
names Robert and Elizabeth as her parents, and Mary's husband Zaccheus Wright
administered Robert's estate in 1835. Those three independent points make an
uncertain attachment reasonable.

Robert's estate packet remains the decisive source. Until it names Mary or
Zaccheus as an heir, the parent links should remain uncertain.

## Remaining work

Obtain Robert Glasgow's estate 1055, box 43, package 98. An administrator's
bond, sale receipt or distribution naming Zaccheus Wright or Mary would convert
the present probable relationship into direct evidence.
