== Biography ==
Thomas Glasgow was christened on 13 November 1684 in Edinburgh Parish, Midlothian, Scotland. His parents were recorded as [[Glasgow-2682|James Glasgow]] and [[Duffe-18|Isobell (Duffe) Glasgow]].<ref name="Baptism1684">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQC9-N9X : 11 February 2020), James Glascow in entry for Thomas Glascow, 1684.</ref>

He is the father of at least one son:
* [[Glasgow-1243|Thomas Glasgow]], christened on 15 June 1715 in Edinburgh Parish.<ref name="SonBaptism1715">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch ({{FamilySearch Record |   F3BT-24K }} : 11 February 2020), Thomas Glasgow in entry for Thomas Glasgow, 1715.</ref>

The name of his wife, the mother of his son, has not yet been found in the records.{{citation needed}}

His known siblings include:
* [[Glasgow-3088|Jean Glasgow]]
* [[Glasgow-3103|James Glasgow]]
* [[Glasgow-3368|James Glasgow]]
* [[Glasgow-3284|Mary Glasgow]]
* [[Glasgow-2681|Margaret Glasgow]]


== Research Notes ==
=== Edinburgh apprentice family ===
On 11 October 1699 Thomas Glasgow, youngest son of James Glasgow, weaver and burgess of Edinburgh, was entered as apprentice to James Affleck, baxter and burgess. The maintained identification with this Thomas fits his Edinburgh baptism on 13 November 1684 and entry at almost fifteen. The apprenticeship supplies no birth date or mother’s name, so that cross-record identity remains inferred.

[[Glasgow-4114|James Glasgow]], apprenticed to William Adam, apothecary, in 1696, was probably Thomas’s brother (79% subjective confidence). His father has the same name, weaving trade and burgess status. The two entries form a coherent late family, though two matching fathers remain possible. James could be one of the Duffe household’s 1679 or 1680 children; neither is selected as that adult.

The older weaver [[Glasgow-1150|James Glasgow]] may be the same man as Thomas’s father [[Glasgow-2682|James of the Duffe household]]. [[Duncan-16106|Agnes Duncan]] was buried in 1674, making a subsequent Duffe marriage compatible with the child series beginning in 1677. No marriage, address or wife clause yet identifies the two father accounts. Thomas’s recorded parents remain James and [[Duffe-18|Isobell Duffe]]; the inferred brotherhood does not establish James-4114’s mother.<ref name="EdinburghBrothersReview">[https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/edinburgh_apprentices_~_1666_to_1700_~_1929.pdf#page=47 Watson, Edinburgh Apprentices (1929), printed p.37, PDF p.47: James1696 and Thomas1699; printed page visually checked18 October2026]; [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/register_of_internments_~_greyfriars_churchyard_1658-1700_~_1898.pdf#page=268 Paton, Greyfriars interments1658–1700 (1902), printed p.252, PDF p.268: Agnes Duncan and James the weaver’s unnamed children; visually checked18 October2026]; [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/register_of_internments_~_greyfriars_churchyard_1658-1700_~_1898.pdf#page=212 Paton, same register, printed p.196, PDF p.212: Agnes Duncan, wife of James Glasgow,weaver,21 January1674; cross-index of same burial, not independent corroboration].</ref>


*  The primary research goal is to locate a marriage record for Thomas Glasgow in the Edinburgh parish registers, dated before June 1715. This would identify his wife.
*   A search for a death or burial record for Thomas should be conducted in the Edinburgh parish records for any time after June 1715.
*   Additional children for Thomas may be found by searching the Edinburgh parish registers between his likely marriage (c. 1705-1715) and his potential death.

Further research is needed to identify his wife and to find a death or burial record for him.
