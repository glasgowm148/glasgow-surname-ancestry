## Current conclusion — newspaper identity review, 6 October 2026

- [The Pennsylvania Journal, or, Weekly Advertiser, 1785-03-23, p.2](https://www.newspapers.com/image/1034008744/): James Glasgow (advertiser of tanyard, resident at Christiana Bridge) — **possible** match. The notice gives only the name, the residence 'living at Christiana Bridge' and a property in the borough of Wilmington, Delaware, held by Thomas Glenn. The same name and residence appear in the 1782 notice for the former Charles Whitlock tanyard in Wilmington (local findings for Glasgow-2868 and Glasgow-3290), so the two advertisements very probably refer to one Christiana Bridge man. Glasgow-3290 (James, b. 1758 Cecil County, d. 1789, son of Glasgow-2868 and Hartshorne-320) was alive in 1785 and belongs to a family recorded in neighbouring Cecil County, Maryland, about 10 miles from Christiana Bridge. However, his profile cites only Glasgow-2868 and has no Delaware, tanning or property record, so he is possible only.
