# John Glasgow in Montfoid

== Biography ==

John Glasgow lived in Montfoid and was the husband of Bessie Robertson by 17 August 1610. The Glasgow Commissary Court testament index records "Bessie, spouse to John Glasgow, in Montfoid" on that date.<ref>Francis J. Grant, ed., ''Commissariot Record of Glasgow: Register of Testaments, 1547-1800'' (Edinburgh: Scottish Record Society, 1901), [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/page/n435/mode/1up p. 426], Robertson, Bessie, 17 August 1610; cross-reference under Glasgow, John, [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/page/196/mode/1up p. 196].</ref>

=== Research Notes ===

Profile implications:
* Birth: before about 1592, Scotland, uncertain; estimated from adult marriage by 1610.
* Death: after 17 August 1610, place unknown, uncertain.
* Residence: Montfoid, probably Montfode in Ardrossan parish, Ayrshire; retain `Montfoid` as the record spelling.<ref>Historic Environment Scotland, [https://www.trove.scot/place/269034 Montfode], site record and map location.</ref>
* Spouse: Bessie Robertson.

[[Glasgow-3296]] has been identified as the existing profile for this John. The probable Montfode identification places the 1610 occurrence in the Ardrossan/Stevenston/Kilwinning corridor, whereas the same profile's 1650 record is at Quothquan in Lanarkshire. Retain the identity as a working match and state the geographic gap; the testament-calendar entry itself does not prove that the Montfoid husband and Quothquan decedent were the same man.
