== Biography ==
'''Barbara (Glasgow) Johnstoun''' was born before 1600, calculated from her 1616 marriage to [[Johnstoun-29|George Johnstoun (abt.1594–)]]. Their marriage is recorded in Scottish parish records.

=== Possible Connections ===
Early 17th-century Edinburgh-area records mention several women named Barbara Glasgow who may or may not be closely related:
* [[Glasgow-3182|Barbara (Glasgow) Wilson (abt.1590–)]] who married [[Wilson-126696|John Wilson (1590–)]] in 1611 at South Leith.
* [[Glasgow-567|Barbara (Glasgow) Little (abt.1586–)]] of Edinburgh, mother of Catharine Little (bapt. 1611).
* [[Glasgow-1102|Barbara Glasgow (b.1617)]], daughter of George Glasgow and Margaret Steuart, with older siblings Margrat (b.1610), Thomas (b.1612), and Archibald (b.1614).

Although no direct evidence ties these women together, their overlapping timelines and parishes suggest potential family connections.

=== Branch and identity assessment ===
Barbara's leading family placement is within the household or immediate collateral circle of [[Glasgow-3188|Ninian Glasgow]]. Her husband George Johnstoun was a cordiner,<ref>Henry Paton, ed., ''The Register of Marriages for the Parish of Edinburgh, 1595-1700'', [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/register_of_marriages_for_the_parish_of_edinburgh_1595-1700_~_1898.pdf#page=277 printed p. 267], 21 November 1616.</ref> the same trade as Ninian's proved son Robert. Ninian's probable son [[Glasgow-3075|William]] later lived in Leith and William's son entered a cordiner apprenticeship. This is concrete trade and locality evidence, but no record names Barbara's father.

Barbara may be a later appearance of [[Glasgow-3182|Barbara Wilson]] and [[Glasgow-567|Barbara, mother of Catharine Little]]. The 1611 Little birth, the disputed September or November Wilson entry, and the 1616 Johnstoun marriage form a possible successive life course. Assessment: moderate identity hypothesis. Do not merge until the original 1611 entries and John Wilson's death or disappearance are resolved.
