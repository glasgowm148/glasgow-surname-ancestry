# WikiTree update notes: [James Glasgow (Glasgow-3305), born 1740, Moneymore, County Londonderry, Ireland](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3305)

## Required corrections

- Remove the statement that Moneymore borders County Down. County Londonderry
  and County Down are not adjacent.
- Recast the 1766 Hawthorne will identification as an unverified possibility.
  The current profile provides neither the abstract nor an archive reference.
- State that the 1740 birth, Moneymore residence, parents and claim that James
  remained in Ireland have not been independently documented.
- Do not use this profile as proof that the emigrant sibling group left a
  brother James in Ireland.

## Paste-ready Research Notes

```text
No contemporary record currently cited identifies this James. His estimated 1740 birth, Moneymore residence, attached parents and identification as the sibling who remained in Ireland derive from the biography of [[Glasgow-12|Robert Glasgow]], where the sibling statement is itself uncited. David Faris's 1990 genealogy says in its public catalogue summary that Robert emigrated with three brothers and one sister, but that summary does not name the siblings or identify another James who remained in Ireland.<ref>David Faris, ''The Glasgow Family of Adams County, Ohio'' (Baltimore: Gateway Press, 1990), WorldCat catalogue record 22731567, https://search.worldcat.org/title/22731567 (accessed 21 July 2026).</ref>

This profile has also been proposed as the James Glasgow called a nephew in a 1766 Hawthorne will abstract. The complete abstract, archive reference and identifiers needed to distinguish that man from other contemporary James Glasgows have not been supplied. The identification remains unproved. Moneymore is in County Londonderry and does not border County Down.
```
