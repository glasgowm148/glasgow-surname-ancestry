# Findings: [James Glasgow (Glasgow-3305), born 1740, Moneymore, County Londonderry, Ireland](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3305)

Last updated: 2026-07-21

## Current conclusion

The live profile supplies no record uniquely identifying this James. His 1740
birth, Moneymore residence, attached parents and reported decision to remain in
Ireland are copied from the biography of Robert Glasgow `Glasgow-12`, not from
an Irish record. He cannot presently serve as the bridge between the
Virginia/Ohio emigrant siblings and the Inishrush family.

The proposed equation with a James Glasgow called nephew in a 1766 Hawthorne
will abstract is unproved. The profile does not cite or reproduce the abstract,
identify the exact testator residence reliably, or show how that nephew is
distinguished from other contemporary James Glasgows. Moneymore does not border
County Down; County Londonderry and County Down are non-adjacent.
