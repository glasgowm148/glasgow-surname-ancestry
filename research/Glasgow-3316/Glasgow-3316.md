== Biography ==

'''Elisabeth Glasgow''', recorded as Elizabeth in one modern transcript, was born on 23 July 1737 and baptised the following day at Irvine, Ayrshire, Scotland. The parish transcript names [[Glasgow-1058|George Glasgow]], shipmaster, and [[Martin-103040|Margaret Martin]] as her parents.<ref name="Irvine1737">[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3574665&tab=this Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564–1929''], Elizabeth Glasgow, born 23 July and baptised 24 July 1737 at Irvine; parents George Glasgow and Margt Martin; father's occupation shipmaster; archive reference OPR 595/1, item 1, record SCOT/OPR/BAP/3574665.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The profile previously used 24 July, the baptism date, as a birth-date boundary. The transcript distinguishes the two events, giving birth on 23 July and baptism on 24 July. The structured birth date should therefore be 23 July 1737. Irvine is the documented baptismal place and may be used as an uncertain birth location; the record does not explicitly say where the birth occurred.

The parent links already on the profile agree exactly with the record and should be retained. No inspected record in this update identifies a spouse, marriage, children or death. The current surname Gairdner is therefore preserved but is not independently proved by this baptism.

The existing FamilySearch tree source is retained as a finding aid.<ref>[https://beta.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/sources/K4K9-T1S FamilySearch tree sources for Elisabeth Glasgow, K4K9-T1S].</ref>
