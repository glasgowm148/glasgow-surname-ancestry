== Biography ==

'''Jean Glasgow''' was recorded in 1728 as a child of '''[[Glasgow-1504|James Glasgow]]''' and '''[[Murray-20395|Sarah or Sara Murray]]''' in a derivative chronology of the Larne and Kilwaughter Presbyterian register.<ref name="Chronology">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184247/http://lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Kilwaughter/Timeline.html "Chronology of the Glasgow Family in Northern Ireland in Historical Context"], 1728 entry for Jean in the James Glasgow–Sara Murray household.</ref>

The public Emerald Ancestors index independently lists a female Jean Glasgow in County Antrim in 1728.<ref>[https://www.emeraldancestors.com/northern-ireland-ancestry-search-birth-list?County=Antrim&Forenames=&Surname=Gillis&brideforenames=&bridesurname=&civildistrict=&searchtype= Emerald Ancestors, County Antrim birth index], Jean Glasgow, 1728.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

No inspected record identifies Jean's spouse, married surname, children, death or later residence. The original register survives at PRONI under reference `MIC/1B/6`; its marriage entries from 1721 to 1769 are the best prospect for extending her line.

Jean is distinct from [[Glasgow-3489|Jean Glasgow of Kirknewton]], born in 1730 to John Glasgow, and [[Glasgow-810|Jean Glasgow of Falkirk]], born about 1733 and married to John Renny.
