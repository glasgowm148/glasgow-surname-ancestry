== Biography ==

'''Helen Glasgow''', written '''Hellen''' in the derivative chronology, was recorded in 1732 as a child of '''[[Glasgow-1504|James Glasgow]]''' and '''[[Murray-20395|Sarah or Sara Murray]]''' in the Larne and Kilwaughter Presbyterian register.<ref name="Chronology">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184247/http://lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Kilwaughter/Timeline.html "Chronology of the Glasgow Family in Northern Ireland in Historical Context"], 1732 entry for Hellen in the James Glasgow–Sara Murray household.</ref>

The public Emerald Ancestors index independently lists a female Helen Glasgow in County Antrim in 1732.<ref>[https://www.emeraldancestors.com/northern-ireland-ancestry-search-birth-list?County=Antrim&Forenames=&Surname=Gillis&brideforenames=&bridesurname=&civildistrict=&searchtype= Emerald Ancestors, County Antrim birth index], Helen Glasgow, 1732.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

No inspected record identifies Helen's spouse, married surname, children, death or later residence. The original register survives at PRONI under reference `MIC/1B/6`; its marriage entries should be searched under Helen, Hellen, Ellen and Nellie.

She is distinct from [[Glasgow-3527|Helen Glasgow of Kirknewton]], who was born in 1714 to a different family.
