# Findings: [Helen Glasgow (Glasgow-3327), born 1732](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3327)

Last updated: 2026-08-12

## Current conclusion

Helen, written Hellen in the chronology, is strongly supported as a daughter of
James Glasgow and Sarah Murray. The public Emerald Ancestors index independently
confirms a Helen Glasgow entry in County Antrim in 1732. No spouse, married
surname, child, death or later residence was found.

Helen-3527 belongs to a different Kirknewton family and is not a duplicate.
