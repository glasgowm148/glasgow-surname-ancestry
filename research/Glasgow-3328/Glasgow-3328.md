== Biography ==

'''Christian Glasgow''' was recorded in 1740 as a child of '''[[Glasgow-1504|James Glasgow]]''' and '''[[Murray-20395|Sarah or Sara Murray]]''' in a derivative chronology of the Larne and Kilwaughter Presbyterian register.<ref name="Chronology">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184247/http://lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Kilwaughter/Timeline.html "Chronology of the Glasgow Family in Northern Ireland in Historical Context"], 1740 entry for Christian in the James Glasgow–Sara Murray household.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The derivative source does not quote the original entry, distinguish birth from baptism or state Christian's sex. The name Christian was historically used for women as well as men. The original register should therefore be inspected before gendered language or a later marriage is assigned.

No inspected record identifies Christian's spouse, children, death or later residence. The original register survives at PRONI under reference `MIC/1B/6`; its marriage entries continue to 1769.

Christian is distinct from [[Glasgow-3492|Christian Glasgow of Glencorse]], who was born in 1708 to Robert Glasgow and Margaret Davidson.
