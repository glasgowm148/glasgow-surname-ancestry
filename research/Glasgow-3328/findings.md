# Findings: [Christian Glasgow (Glasgow-3328), recorded 1740](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3328)

Last updated: 2026-08-12

## Current conclusion

Christian is strongly supported as a child of James Glasgow and Sarah Murray by
the derivative Larne/Kilwaughter register chronology. No later spouse, child,
death or residence was found.

The derivative source gives only the name and year in its household list. The
original entry must establish whether 1740 is a birth or baptism date and confirm
Christian's sex. Christian was historically used for women as well as men, so a
gendered descendant search is premature.

Christian-3492 of Glencorse belongs to a different Scottish family and is not a
duplicate.

1769 under Christian and its common variants. No spouse or child should be named
from the present evidence alone.
