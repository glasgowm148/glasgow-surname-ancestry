# Findings: Glasgow-3331

## Current conclusion

No reviewed record identifies Glasgow-1498 or Glasgow-2731 as this John's sons.
His own biography says no children are confirmed. The published Lanark index also
contains separate John Glasgow testament entries dated 1650, 1662 and 1685;
their identities must not be collapsed from the index alone. Glasgow-3019 now
redirects to attached father [[Glasgow-1086]]. Reinspection of CC14/5/12 shows
that the 1685 John had widow Jonet (surname provisionally Kinnie), but the
record names no child. It therefore does not prove this father-son edge.
