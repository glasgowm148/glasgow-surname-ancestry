== Biography ==
James Glasgow represents an uncertain Biggar/Quothquan family account. The 1630 and 1634 birth years in the earlier biography are unsupported estimates, not two birth records. His attached parents [[Glasgow-1086|John Glasgow]] and [[Unknown-717333]] remain uncertain; the earlier mother-name reading is not adopted as proved. Neither the 1624 nor the 1680 testament index identifies this modern profile or his parents.

The existing review of [[Glasgow-3093|Johne Glasgow]]’s Mid Calder baptism dated 9 July 1648 reports father Jamis Glasgow. That identifies a father-name account, not necessarily this James. Do not transfer the 1648 child attachment to the man dead by 1624; a literal 1648 birth would conflict with that earlier death, while the index review establishes a baptism rather than a separate birth date.<ref>Existing reviewed FamilySearch index, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BL-JXR Johne Glasgow, Mid Calder, 1648], names father Jamis; the modern adult-profile identification remains uncertain. No new original inspection.</ref>

== Research Notes ==
=== Surviving identity alternative — 16 October 2026 ===

James [[Glasgow-3332]] remains a surviving cross-profile identity alternative for the 1624 Quothquan weaver. Its circa-1634 birth is an unsupported estimate and cannot by itself exclude that earlier man. Identification with the distinct weaver indexed at Back of Lindsaylands in 1680 would exclude a death before 1624 if established, but that later identity is itself only possible. Retain [[Glasgow-3910]] as the record-defined 1624 account for the selected uncertain family; do not merge the profiles or transfer [[Glasgow-3332]]’s unproved parent and child attachments. Those existing family fields remain unchanged, and the proposed Stephen placement is an alternative to that account’s current pedigree. [https://glasgow.phenotype.dev/branches/early-scottish-records/#quothquan-working-family Full working-family comparison].

=== Superseded birth-based exclusion ===

The earlier profile assessment read as follows. Its exclusion based solely on an estimated birth year is withdrawn; the later-record identity tests remain open.

<blockquote>

The profile's estimated birth in 1634 means this James cannot be the man whose testament was recorded in 1624. A James Glasgow, weaver at Back of Lindsaylands, had a testament recorded in 1680; this profile is a chronological candidate for that later man, but no reviewed record joins the identities.<ref>Francis J. Grant, ed., ''The Commissariot Record of Lanark: Register of Testaments, 1595–1800'', pp. 38, 99, entries for James Glasgow.</ref>
</blockquote>

The attached parents, child, birth estimate and Biggar birthplace are retained as uncertain pending a fresh reading of the original 1662 estate material. The connected John Glasgow profiles have been conflated, so the existing family links should not be treated as proved.
