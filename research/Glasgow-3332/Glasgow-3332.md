```text
== Research Notes ==

The published Lanark Commissary index names James Glasgow, weaver in Quodquen, whose testament was recorded on 26 July 1624; a possibly related James Glesgow was the deceased husband of Margaret Threipland by 1626; and another James Glasgow, weaver at the Back of Lindsaylands in Biggar parish, had a testament recorded on 10 November 1680.<ref name="LanarkIndexJames">Francis J. Grant, ed., ''The Commissariot Record of Lanark: Register of Testaments, 1595–1800'' (Edinburgh: Scottish Record Society, 1903), printed pp. 38 and 99, [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/the_commissariot_record_of_lanark__register_of_testaments__1595-1800_~_1903.pdf digital copy].</ref>

This profile, estimated born in 1634, cannot be the James deceased by 1624. It is chronologically possible that he was the 1680 weaver, but no record presently connects him to Back of Lindsaylands. That identity should remain unproved pending the original 1662 and 1680 testaments.
```
