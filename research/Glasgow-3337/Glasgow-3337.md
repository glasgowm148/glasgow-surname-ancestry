== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' was an adult in the St Cuthbert's parish family by 1661. No record of his own birth has been identified. A birth before about 1641 is estimated from his parenthood, and Edinburgh is retained only as an uncertain estimate from the children's parish records.

James and [[Glessie-1|Annapill Glessie]], also indexed as Anabell Glass, were the parents of three children recorded at St Cuthbert's:

* [[Glasgow-3076|James Glasgow]], recorded in 1661;<ref name="James1661">"Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:F316-KWL James Glesgow, 1661]; accessed 19 August 2026.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-3091|Thomas Glasgow]], recorded in 1663;<ref name="Thomas1663">"Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:F316-L74 Anabell Glass in entry for Thomas Glassgow, 1663]; accessed 19 August 2026.</ref> and
* [[Glasgow-3338|Robert Glasgow]], recorded in 1665.<ref name="Robert1665">"Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950", database, FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:F316-F84 Robert Glasgow, 1665]; accessed 19 August 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The FamilySearch record F316-KWL is a 1661 event for James's son James. It is not this father's birth record.

[[Glasgow-3077|James Glasgow]], who had a daughter with Helen Clerk at St Cuthbert's in 1658, is a strong same-person candidate. One widower with consecutive wives would fit the chronology, but no death, second marriage or other identifying record has yet bridged the two fathers. The profiles should not be merged without that evidence.
