# Findings: [James Glasgow (Glasgow-3337), St Cuthbert's](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3337)

## Conclusion

This profile represents the father James in the St Cuthbert's family associated
with Annapill or Anabell Glessie/Glass and children in 1661, 1663 and 1665. Its
`about 1640` birth is only an estimate from parenthood; the 1661 index is a
child's event, not James's birth.

[Glasgow-3077](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3077), who had a daughter
with Helen Clerk at St Cuthbert's in 1658, is a strong same-person candidate.
One widower with consecutive wives is plausible, but not yet proved.

## Recommended update

- Change birth to before about 1641 and retain Edinburgh as an explicitly
  uncertain estimate from the children's parish records until better evidence
  supplies a replacement.
- Explain that FamilySearch F316-KWL concerns son James in 1661.
- Retain Annapill and the three children as the working record-defined family.
- Hold the Glasgow-3077 merge until Helen's death or a second marriage is found.
  (Superseded: the duplicate follow-up records the merge on 27 September 2026; see below.)

Applied live on 19 August 2026. The three baptism-recorded child-parent links
and the co-parent relationship with Annapill are confident; the possible
identity with Glasgow-3077 remains unresolved.
