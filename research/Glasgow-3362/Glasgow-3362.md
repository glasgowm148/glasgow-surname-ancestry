The 1656 index names Elizabeth's father only as William Glasgow. It does not identify him with [[Glasgow-2462|William Glasgow]], whose own record is at Inveresk and whose profile supplies no documented move to Polwarth. That father link remains uncertain pending the original parish entry or another identity bridge.

The 1657 entry is a probable match, not proof that the child died on that exact day.
