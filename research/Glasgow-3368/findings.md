# Findings: [James Glasgow (Glasgow-3368), baptised 1680](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3368)

## Conclusion

The 18 April 1680 baptism is a separate event to the same parents who had a son
James in March 1679. Name reuse makes the 1680 child the likelier survivor, but
does not by itself prove his later identity.

Do not merge with [Glasgow-3103](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3103).
Instead determine which child, if either, became Jean Dobie's husband and
father of the 1699 family.

## Recommended update

- Retain both baptismal profiles and note probable death of the elder as a hypothesis.
- Search Edinburgh burials in 1679-80 and the James Glasgow/Jean Dobie marriage.
