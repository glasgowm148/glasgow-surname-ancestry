== Biography ==
'''John Glasgow''' was born before about 1661, probably in Scotland. His exact date and place of birth are unknown.

The estimated birth date is based on John being old enough to have a child baptised in July 1679. He may have been born considerably earlier.

John was living in, or otherwise associated with, the parish of Kilwinning, Ayrshire, Scotland, between 1679 and 1689.

He had at least two known children:

* '''Jon Glasgow''', baptised on 6 July 1679 in Kilwinning, recorded as the son of Jon Glasgow and Barbara Alason.<ref name="Jon1679">Kilwinning Old Parish Register, parish 599, frame 44, baptism of Jon Glasgow, 6 July 1679, son of Jon Glasgow and Barbara Alason.</ref>
* '''Jannet Glasgow''', baptised on 1 February 1689 in Kilwinning, recorded as the daughter of John Glasgow and a woman recorded by the surname Allason.<ref name="Jannet1689">Kilwinning Old Parish Register, parish 599, frame 75, baptism of Jannet Glasgow, 1 February 1689, daughter of John Glasgow and Allason.</ref>

The woman surnamed Allason in Jannet's baptism was probably the same Barbara Alason named in Jon's baptism. The surviving indexed entry does not give her forename.

Her surname may appear in records under several spellings, including Alason, Allason, Alison and Allison.

No marriage or proclamation record for John Glasgow and Barbara Alason has yet been identified. Barbara should therefore be described as John's probable wife or partner rather than as a proven legal spouse.

John was alive, or was at least named as the father, when Jannet was baptised in February 1689.

His date and place of death are unknown.

=== Children ===

John Glasgow and probably Barbara Alason had the following known children:

# '''Jon Glasgow''', baptised 6 July 1679 at Kilwinning, Ayrshire.<ref name="Jon1679" />

# '''Jannet Glasgow''', baptised 1 February 1689 at Kilwinning, Ayrshire.<ref name="Jannet1689" />

There may have been other children whose baptisms were not recorded, whose records have not survived, or whose entries have not yet been identified.

== Possible Parentage ==

John's parents have not been established.

Five Scottish baptism records have been identified for men named John or Johne Glasgow who were born within, or close to, a plausible period for the father of a child baptised in 1679.

None of these records currently provides a direct connection to Kilwinning, Barbara Alason, or the children Jon and Jannet Glasgow.

=== Johne Glasgow, son of Archbald Glasgow and Agnes Watsone ===

A Johne Glasgow was baptised on 27 April 1656 in South Leith, Midlothian, Scotland. He was recorded as the son of Archbald Glasgow and Agnes Watsone.<ref name="John1656">FamilySearch, "Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950," Johne Glasgow, baptised 27 April 1656, South Leith, Midlothian, Scotland, son of Archbald Glasgow and Agnes Watsone.</ref>

This Johne would have been about 23 years old when Jon Glasgow was baptised in 1679 and about 32 when Jannet was baptised in 1689.

He is a strong chronological candidate among the currently identified records.

However, South Leith is in Midlothian, while John's known children were baptised in Kilwinning, Ayrshire. No evidence has yet been found showing that this Johne moved to Ayrshire or was associated with Barbara Alason.

Archbald Glasgow and Agnes Watsone should not presently be attached as John's parents.

=== Johne Glasgow, son of Johne Glasgow ===

A Johne Glasgow was baptised on 22 April 1649 in Kirknewton and East Calder, Midlothian, Scotland. He was recorded as the son of another Johne Glasgow.<ref name="John1649">FamilySearch, "Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950," Johne Glasgow, baptised 22 April 1649, Kirknewton and East Calder, Midlothian, Scotland, son of Johne Glasgow. The indexed record does not name his mother.</ref>

This Johne would have been about 30 years old in 1679 and about 40 in 1689.

He is chronologically plausible, but no evidence currently links him or his father with Kilwinning or Barbara Alason.

The elder Johne Glasgow should not presently be attached as John's father.

He is chronologically plausible, but no evidence has yet been found connecting him or his parents with Kilwinning or Barbara Alason.

Thomas Glasgow and Hellin Bryse should not presently be attached as John's parents.

=== Johne Glasgow, son of James Glasgow and Agnes Duncan ===

A Johne Glasgow was baptised on 30 August 1661 in Edinburgh, Midlothian, Scotland. He was recorded as the son of James Glasgow and Agnes Duncan.<ref name="John1661">FamilySearch, "Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950," Johne Glasgow, baptised 30 August 1661, Edinburgh, Midlothian, Scotland, son of James Glasgow and Agnes Duncan.</ref>

This Johne would have been approximately 17 years and 10 months old when Jon Glasgow was baptised on 6 July 1679. He would probably have been about 17 when the child was conceived.

He cannot be excluded entirely, but he is a less likely candidate because of his young age. His baptism also falls slightly later than John's estimated birth date of before about 1661.

No evidence has been found connecting him with Kilwinning or Barbara Alason.

James Glasgow and Agnes Duncan should not presently be attached as John's parents.

=== Parentage Assessment ===

The candidates may be ranked by chronological plausibility as follows:

# '''Johne Glasgow''', baptised in 1656 at South Leith — about 23 years old in 1679.

# '''Johne Glasgow''', baptised in 1649 at Kirknewton and East Calder — about 30 years old in 1679.

# '''John Glasgow''', baptised in 1644 at Linlithgow — about 35 years old in 1679.

# '''Johne Glasgow''', baptised in 1639 at Duddingston — about 40 years old in 1679.

# '''Johne Glasgow''', baptised in 1661 at Edinburgh — about 17 years old in 1679.

This ranking concerns age only. It is not evidence that the higher-ranked candidates were more closely connected to the Kilwinning family.

All five men were baptised in Midlothian or Linlithgowshire. The John Glasgow represented by this profile is currently known only from the baptism records of his children in Kilwinning, Ayrshire.

No candidate has yet been shown to have:

* lived in Kilwinning or elsewhere in Ayrshire;
* married or formed a household with Barbara Alason;
* fathered Jon Glasgow or Jannet Glasgow;
* used an occupation or territorial designation matching the Kilwinning records;
* shared relatives, witnesses, neighbours or associates with the Kilwinning family.

The absence of a matching Ayrshire baptism does not prove that John was born elsewhere. Scottish Old Parish Registers are incomplete, and some baptisms were never recorded or have not survived.

John's parents should remain unknown until additional evidence links one of these baptism records to the Kilwinning family.

== Identity and duplicate assessment ==
This Kilwinning John is a serious same-person candidate for [[Glasgow-1073|Rev. John Glasgow M.A.]]: the name and estimated generation agree, Kilwinning and Kilbirnie are neighbouring Ayrshire parishes, and the last indexed Barbara-family event in February 1689 preceded the minister's January 1690 marriage to Jean Cunningham. The chronology permits the identification but does not prove it.

Neither ''Fasti Ecclesiae Scoticanae'' nor Paterson's county history mentions Barbara Alason, and the available index extracts do not give this John a title, occupation or residence that would identify him as the minister.<ref>''Fasti Ecclesiae Scoticanae'', vol. 3, p. 103, [https://electricscotland.com/bible/fastiecclesiaesc03scot.pdf PDF]; James Paterson, ''History of the County of Ayr'', pp. 252-253, [https://archive.org/details/historyofcountyo02pateuoft/page/252/mode/2up scan].</ref> Glasgow-3375 and Glasgow-1073 must therefore remain separate unless the full Kilwinning OPR pages or another identifying record supplies a bridge.
