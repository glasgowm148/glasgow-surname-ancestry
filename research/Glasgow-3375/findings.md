This profile represents the John Glasgow associated with Barbara Alason/Allason
at Kilwinning in 1679 and 1689. It is not proved that his father was
[John Glasgow (Glasgow-1604)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1604).

He is a serious duplicate candidate for [Rev. John Glasgow M.A. (Glasgow-1073),
about 1653, Kilbirnie](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1073): same name,
same estimated generation, adjacent parishes, and a last Barbara-family event in
January 1689 before the minister married Jean Cunningham in January 1690. The
minister had been ordained at Kilbirnie in August 1688, which does not exclude a
Kilwinning family event five months later.

The identification remains unproved because neither *Fasti* nor Paterson mentions
Barbara, and the index extracts do not give John's title, trade or residence.
