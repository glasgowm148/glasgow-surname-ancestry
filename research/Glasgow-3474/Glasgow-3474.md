== Biography ==

'''John Glasgow''' was born about 1807, probably in County Antrim. No inspected
record establishes a more precise birthplace; retain the location as uncertain
pending a sourced replacement.

John was a married farmer at Lisnagaver, County Antrim. He died there on '''24
June 1885''', aged seventy-eight. His son James Glasgow of Lisnagaver reported
the death.<ref name="Death1885">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/deaths_returns/deaths_1885/06280/4799372.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil death register], John Glasgow, Lisnagaver, 24 June 1885.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

John is the strongest current later-life candidate for the married, Antrim-born
nephew aged forty in [[Glasgow-1022|John Glasgow's]] 1851 Glenleslie household.
The age and locality are compatible, but no record yet names his parents or
connects him to Glenleslie. The identity remains unproved.<ref name="Glenleslie1851">[https://durrushistory.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/1851irishcensusreturnscommadelimitedtextfilejul24_071.pdf ''1851 Irish Census Returns: detailed comma-delimited transcription''], Glenleslie household 21.</ref>

He was not [[Glasgow-867|John Glasgow]], husband of Margaret Garvin, whose family
memorial records death in 1895.
