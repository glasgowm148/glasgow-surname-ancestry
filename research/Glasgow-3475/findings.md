# Daniel Glasgow (Glasgow-3475): death-index match

Last updated: 2026-09-26

## Current conclusion

The [GRONI civil-death register](https://geni.nidirect.gov.uk/) records Daniel Glasgow, a married weaver aged 50, dying **14 December 1867** at Culmore, Rasharkin, County Antrim (`D/1868/31/1005/1/451`). The image was inspected on 23 September 2026. Martha Glasgow was present at his death and signed by mark; the entry does not state her relationship, although she was probably his wife. Birth about 1817 remains an estimate from age. The 1868 index year is the registration year, and Ballymoney is the district, not the death address. [Glasgow-4091](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4091), created from this same register entry, was merged into this older profile on 26 September 2026. The resulting profile retains the full-return details, distinct derivative indexes and the existing uncertain Martha attachment.

## Source findings

- GRONI death register, Ballymoney superintendent registrar's district, Dirraw entry 451, Daniel Glasgow, 14 December 1867, Culmore, married weaver, 50, peritonitis; informant Martha Glasgow. Image inspected 23 September 2026.
- [FamilySearch death index QP3S-P6DL](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QP3S-P6DL) and [MyHeritage derivative index](https://www.myheritage.com/research/record-20866-6724983/daniel-glasgow-in-ireland-deaths) refer to the same death; neither is a separate death event.

## Unresolved questions
