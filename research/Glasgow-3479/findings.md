# Robert Glasgow of Irvine (Glasgow-3479)

Last updated: 2026-09-27

## Current conclusion

This former profile represented the adult Robert Glasgow named with Mary Borland in Irvine baptism indexes for children called John in 1736 and 1738. It was **merged into [Glasgow-2474](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2474) on 27 September 2026**. The surviving profile joins the 1716 Irvine baptism and parents to the later household, with the identity stated as a reasonable inference rather than a direct parent-identifying record.

An Irvine archive catalogue also names a Robert Glasgow as master of the ship *Betty and Grizell* on 4 August 1738, between those baptisms. The same name, town and adult chronology support the identification, but the ship entry states no spouse or child. The former “before 1719” birth estimate was an adult-age inference, not a conflicting birth record.
