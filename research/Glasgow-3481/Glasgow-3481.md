== Biography ==
'''John Glasgow''' was named as the father of [[Glasgow-3489|Jean Glasgow]], recorded on 11 November 1730 at Kirknewton and East Calder, Midlothian. The cited index does not name a mother in the existing profile.<ref>“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-H5X Jean Glasgow, 1730], citation from former Glasgow-3490; compare the original parish entry for the mother's name and the birth/baptism distinction.</ref>

A John Glasgow and [[Meek-3817|Christian Meek]] were named as parents of [[Glasgow-3480|John Glasgow]], christened 18 August 1751 at Kirknewton and East Calder.<ref>“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” MyHeritage, [https://www.myheritage.com/research/record-30226-356058/john-glasgow-in-scotland-births-baptisms John Glasgow, 1751], citing film 1067762; the indexed entry names John Glasgow and Christian Meek.</ref> [[Glasgow-3482|Isabel Glasgow]] was recorded on 16 May 1755 at Leith with parents John Glasgow and Christian Meek.<ref>“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch, [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQCR-935 Isabel Glasgow, 1755].</ref>

== Research Notes ==
=== Selected uncertain family — 16 October 2026 ===

Select William [[Glasgow-1026]] as the uncertain working father of John [[Glasgow-3481]], the combined Calder father account recorded in 1730 and with Christian Meek in 1751–1755. This adopts possible identity with John [[Glasgow-1544]], the child indexed at Kirknewton in 1702 with father William. The same name and parish and a normal adult generation favour this concrete child account over an unidentified origin: if baptised near birth in 1702, he would be about 28 in 1730 and 53 at the 1755 child. Those ages are conditional examples, not ages stated in the registers. The old circa 1710 birth was inferred from assumed age at the 1730 fatherhood and cannot exclude the 1702 child. The child’s existing father identity as William-1026 is itself qualified; no record explicitly identifies the later Meek husband as his son.

John-1544 and John-3481 remain separate profile accounts of a possible single man; the selection does not establish two distinct John sons. The 1702 date remains unreconciled: the child’s field uses a before 11 November boundary while its biography cites a 29 November baptism. Neither date is newly corrected. The later account combines the 1730 father of Jean, whose mother is omitted, with the 1751 and 1755 Meek household. That earlier adult identification remains a preferred working combination, not a continuous household proved by occupation, land or witnesses. John-81, estimated born 1709, remains a specific competing identity for the 1730 father. His Guthrie household is indexed with children in West Calder in 1751 and 1753 while the Meek household appears in Kirknewton in 1751 and Leith in 1755. That concrete overlap makes a simple succession of wives less persuasive; do not combine the full Guthrie and Meek families without resolving the entries. No birth estimate alone excludes John-81. Other local John origins remain possible. Ann McCook is not selected as John-3481’s mother: the reviewed 1702 child citation names William, and the attached Ann field does not supply an independently verified maternal clause. William’s existing uncertain John-3610 father and older family alternatives retain their grades; no date or family field is changed to force the new route. The father profiles formerly Glasgow-3490 (1730 Kirknewton child) and Glasgow-3481 (1751 Kirknewton and 1755 Leith children) were combined as the leading identification. They share a name and parish, and no distinct age, occupation, parentage or death separates them. The 1730 register index does not name Jean's mother, so it cannot establish whether Christian Meek was her mother or whether John had an earlier union. An original-register comparison may refine this assessment.

The earlier profile estimated John's birth about 1730 from the 1751 child. Fatherhood in 1730 makes that estimate untenable for the combined profile. About 1710 is only a generation estimate, not an independently recorded birth date. His death is unknown.
