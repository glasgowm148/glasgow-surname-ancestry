# Findings: John Glasgow (Glasgow-3491), Glencorse, 1710

Last updated: 2026-08-31

Scotland's People indexes John Glasgow on 1 January 1710 at Glencorse, parish
687, reference `10/93`, naming Robert Glasgow and Margaret Davidson as parents.
The record does not identify the father as Glasgow-2867, master of the *John and
Archibald* in 1754. The live father relationship is now uncertain; Margaret's
maternity remains directly supported.
