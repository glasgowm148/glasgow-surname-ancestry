# Findings: John Glasgow (Glasgow-3491), Glencorse, 1710

Last updated: 2026-09-10

## Current conclusion

The 1 January 1710 Glencorse baptism directly names John's parents as Robert Glasgow and Margaret Davidson. It does not identify that Robert as `Glasgow-2867`, the 1754 master of the *John and Archibald*; retain that shipmaster identification as uncertain. `Glasgow-3011` is the probable Glencorse-father candidate, not a proved identification.

## Recommended next actions

- Do not detach or blank the present father relationship from this evidence alone.
- If a controlled replacement from `Glasgow-2867` to `Glasgow-3011` is later made, mark it uncertain and make no other relationship change in that operation.
- Seek an original parish, testament, land, or occupational record connecting the 1704 groom to the 1676 child.
