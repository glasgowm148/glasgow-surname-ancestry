# Findings: Christian Glasgow (Glasgow-3492), Glencorse, 1708

Last updated: 2026-08-31

FamilySearch indexes Christian Glasgow at Glencorse on 15 February 1708 as a
daughter of Robert Glasgow and Margaret Davidson (`VQ4J-CB3`). The baptism
supports the named parents but does not identify the father as Glasgow-2867,
master of the *John and Archibald* in 1754. The live father relationship is now
uncertain; Margaret's maternity remains directly supported.
