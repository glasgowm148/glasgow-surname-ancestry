## Record findings and identity limits — 17 October 2026

### Edward George Glasgow, army officer, 1825–1826

An April 1825 notice names Lieutenant Edward George Glasgow, formerly on half-pay of the 49th Regiment, among appointments to lieutenants in the 84th Regiment. In June 1826 John James Peck was appointed from the 89th Foot in his place; Edward George retired upon half-pay of the 18th Light Dragoons. ([Freeman’s Journal, 1825-04-20, p.3](https://www.newspapers.com/image/1050085864/).) ([Freeman’s Journal and Daily Commercial Advertiser, 20 June 1826, p. 1, 84th Regiment retirement notice](https://www.newspapers.com/image/1050134797/).)

The rare full name distinguishes the officer from George Mark Glasgow, but the officer is not yet joined to the passenger arriving in New York in 1850. A reported Emily Caroline widow notice of December 1849 would be a material conflict if its identity is verified; that notice remains a lead rather than a newly inspected source.
