## Current conclusion

The attached father [William Glasgow (Glasgow-1098), born about 1500,
Stirlingshire](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1098) is no longer the best
placement. A direct 1565 record proves that John Glasgow senior and Jonet
Spreull had a son William; the identity of that son with this profile is highly
probable but not absolutely resolved.

Birth-date assessment: William's `1525` is explicitly marked as a **guess** in
the live profile data. The biography explains it as a back-calculation from his
son George's minority; no record supplies 1525. William was an instrumentary
witness on 9 June 1564. The Scots-law minimum age of 14 for such a witness gives
a defensible outer bound of **born by 9 June 1550**. He received property as
John's son in 1565 and fathered George, who was aged about 14-20 in 1586 and was
therefore born between late 1565 and September 1572. Taken together, **about
1540**, with a reasonable range of roughly **1535-1550**, is a better working
estimate than 1525. It fits an adult witness/property recipient in 1564-65 and
fatherhood in the following decade without pretending that an exact birth year
has been found.
