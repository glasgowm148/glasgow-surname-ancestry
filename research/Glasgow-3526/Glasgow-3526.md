== Biography ==
'''James Glasgow''' is represented here by the father named in John Glasgow’s 1717 Kirknewton and East Calder index. The child is [[Glasgow-3525|John Glasgow]]. James’s own circa 1688 birth is an unsupported estimate; the parish index does not establish his age or parents.<ref>[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4K-6JT FamilySearch,John Glasgow,1717], previously reviewed index.</ref>

[[Glasgow-1026|William Glasgow]] remains his selected uncertain working father from the earlier family review. [[Glasgow-1547|James Glasgow of the 1702 childhood account]] remains an alternative identity for the 1717 father. Neither identification is newly proved. No wife or mother of the 1717 child is supplied here.

[[Glasgow-2847|John Glasgow,husband of Margaret Anderson]], is selected as an uncertain possible son through possible identity with the 1717 child. If that identity holds, the two John accounts may be duplicates rather than distinct sons. The later West Calder household does not independently prove James’s own parentage. The existing Helen Glasgow-3527 association and earlier life reconstruction are preserved below.

== Research Notes ==
=== Selected uncertain family — 16 October 2026 ===

[[Glasgow-3526|James Glasgow, Kirknewton father account]] is selected as the uncertain working father of [[Glasgow-2847|John Glasgow, West Calder Anderson household]], husband of Margaret Anderson and father of the West Calder children recorded from 1745 to 1755. The route provisionally identifies that adult with [[Glasgow-3525|John Glasgow, Kirknewton child of 1717]], indexed at neighbouring Kirknewton and East Calder on 31 March 1717 with father James. The parish setting, exact forename and surname, and feasible generation favour this concrete local childhood over an unidentified origin: if baptised near birth in 1717, John would have been about 28 in 1745 and 38 in 1755. The later family also contains a James, although a common repeated forename does not independently establish a grandfather. No register entry yet joins the child to Margaret Anderson’s husband. This is a low-confidence identity-based parent model, not a proved father clause for the adult.

[[Glasgow-3526|James Glasgow, Kirknewton father account]] is the maintained account for the James named in the 1717 record. Its own circa-1688 birth and selected uncertain William [[Glasgow-1026|Glasgow-1026]] father remain prior hypotheses. The child’s earlier biography instead names [[Glasgow-1547|James Glasgow, alternative 1702 childhood account]]. That 1702 Kirknewton childhood, son of [[Glasgow-1546|Glasgow-1546]], remains an alternative identity for the adult James. If baptised near birth in September 1702, he would have been about fourteen at the March 1717 baptism, making that route less straightforward than the older James account. An older-child baptism or an incorrect age estimate remains possible; neither is asserted as fact. The named father James is distinct from the question of which modern James account represents him. The earlier child-parent grade is not upgraded, and neither James’s ancestry receives new certainty.

No mother is selected for the West Calder John. The retained 1717 citation names James but supplies no mother in the existing account. Neither Ann McCook from the older William household nor Christian Cook from [[Glasgow-1547|James Glasgow, alternative 1702 childhood account]]’s childhood is transferred into this generation. John’s new proposed father does not identify the later life of his son [[Glasgow-2846|Glasgow-2846]]: that child remains separate from the Larne Rose Allan husband [[Glasgow-4179|Glasgow-4179]] unless their own identity bridge is established. [[Glasgow-1922|Glasgow-1922]]’s Kirk marriage and all existing child biographies remain outside the new parent inference.

The 1755 citation is West Calder OPR 701,10/202, FR 142; the other retained citations are parish indexes. No original register image has been newly inspected. The present circa-1730 birth and the former biography’s before-1739 wording are unsupported estimates from a child’s event, not recorded ages or a childhood identification. If the rough 1660/1688 generations are close, William would have been about 28 at his birth, and the proposed son falls between William’s already recorded children. This same-area, compatible household generation is preferred provisionally, at low confidence. The combined Kirknewton/East Calder label does not establish the exact historic parish or continuous residence, and no record names William as James’s father.

John-3525’s earlier biography links its father to James [[Glasgow-1547]], whereas the maintained local family uses James-3526. The 1717 index names James, not either modern profile ID. James-1547, associated with Andrew [[Glasgow-1546]] and a 1702 entry, remains a surviving alternative childhood identity for the adult father. If baptised as an infant in 1702 he would be about 15 in 1717 and about 12 at Helen’s tree-held 1714 date. That makes the ordinary older-William model more straightforward, but neither a later childhood baptism nor an erroneous Helen association is excluded. No merge or categorical biological exclusion is selected. Andrew-1546 therefore remains an alternative father through that identity, not a newly attached parent.
