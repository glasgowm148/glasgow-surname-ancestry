== Biography ==
Matthew Glasgow's birth date and place have not been established by a source. The profile's before-1650 estimate and Stevenson, Scotland birthplace are retained as uncertain research leads.

The 24 April 1665 testament-index entry for his spouse, Jonet Cowan, identifies her as the spouse of ''Mathew Glasgow in Pyperheugh'', parish of Stevinstoune (Stevenston).<ref>Francis J. Grant, ed., ''Commissariot Record of Glasgow: Register of Testaments, 1547–1800'' (Edinburgh, 1901), p. 110, [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/page/110/mode/2up?q=Cowan Jonet Cowan entry].</ref> This is indexed evidence for the couple and for Matthew's locality in 1665. The date belongs to Jonet's testament entry; it is not Matthew's birth, marriage or death date.

== Research Notes ==
=== Selected uncertain family — 16 October 2026 ===

Select John [[Glasgow-1030]] as the very low-confidence working father of Matthew [[Glasgow-3538]] of Pyperheugh, Stevenston. John and Jonet Mathie married in Glasgow on 25 January 1618 and have recorded children in the following decade. A son of that household, born in the 1620s, could be an adult spouse in the 1665 testament index that names Matthew. This is a compatible generation, not a new birth estimate. The existing Mathie–Glasgow network supplies a positive social route between Glasgow and Stevenston: David Mathy and Robert Glasgow were co-witnesses in 1554, while Matthew Glasgow and John Mathie were together at Ardeer Nether in 1559–60. The later marriage joins those surname networks. The recurrence of Matthew in the same parish makes this existing household a useful selected descent hypothesis; it does not identify a father in a record.

No inspected record names Matthew’s parents or connects his Pyperheugh household directly to the Glasgow couple. The route depends on Jonet’s unproved place in the older Mathie network and on the probable identification of the 1618 husband with Ninian’s brother John represented by [[Glasgow-1030]]. John’s confident Robert [[Glasgow-1096]] father assessment remains conditional on that adult identification and is not newly upgraded. Other Stevenston fathers, collateral descent from the 1559–60 Matthew, and an unrelated same-name family remain possible. The 106-year interval rules out identity with that earlier adult; no missing generation is invented and no direct father edge to him is selected. The Saltcoats sailor John, spouse of Isobell Gray, remains a separate record-defined household; this model does not merge them. The 1619 baptismal witness Mathew Will is not Matthew Mathie or proof of a Glasgow relative.

Assess Jonet [[Mathie-159]] separately as Matthew’s uncertain working mother. Her recorded 1618 marriage and maternity in the following decade provide an actual couple and a compatible childbearing generation; her circa-1597 birth is an estimate. The Mathie associations in Stevenston are the proposed route for this household’s connection to Pyperheugh. Selecting her is a conditional couple hypothesis, not an automatic consequence of attaching John. It has very low confidence: no record calls Matthew her son, no Mathie ancestor is newly attached, and another mother remains possible. The paternal and maternal selections share the same network argument and are correlated, not two independent confirmations. === Distinct from the Ardeer Matthew ===
A Matthew Glasgow appears at Ardeer Nether, parish of Stevenston, in the 1559–60 teind lists.<ref>Margaret H. B. Sanderson, ''People of Sixteenth Century Ayrshire'' (1987), p. 331.</ref> That tenant or householder was active about 106 years before the record naming Jonet Cowan's spouse and was a different man. Their shared uncommon name and parish make possible descent or collateral kinship worth investigating, but no relationship is proved.

No source reviewed establishes this Matthew's birth or parents.
