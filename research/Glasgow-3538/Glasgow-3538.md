== Biography ==

An entry in the Commissariot of Glasgow testament index dated 24 April 1665 names [[Cowan-8376|Jonet Cowan]] as the spouse of Mathew Glasgow in Pyperheugh, parish of Stevenston, Ayrshire.<ref>Francis J. Grant, ed., ''The Commissariot Record of Glasgow: Register of Testaments, 1547–1800'' (Scottish Record Society; Edinburgh: James Skinner & Co., 1901), [https://electricscotland.com/history/records/scottishrecordso07scotuoft.pdf#page=120 p. 110], entry for Jonet Cowan, 24 April 1665.</ref>

=== Research Notes ===

The testament index supplies no birth date for Matthew. The profile's date of "before 1650" is an estimate, not a recorded birth. Its location should be normalised from "Stevenson" to Stevenston, Ayrshire, Scotland. The cited date is 24 April 1665, not 1664, and belongs to Jonet Cowan's testament index entry rather than a marriage or event of Matthew's.

A published transcription of the Cunninghame teind lists of 1559–60 names an earlier Matthew Glasgow at Ardeer Nether in Stevenston parish.<ref>Margaret H. B. Sanderson, ''The People of Sixteenth Century Ayrshire'', ''Ayrshire Collections'', vol. 14, no. 8 (Ayrshire Archaeological and Natural History Society, 1987), appendix "The Cunninghame Teind Lists of 1559–60," [https://aanhs.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/people-of-ayrshire.pdf#page=36 p. 331].</ref> He is not this Matthew: he was already a tenant/head of household about 106 years before the 1665 entry. The same name and parish suggest possible family continuity, but no reviewed record establishes a relationship.
