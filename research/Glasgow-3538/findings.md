# Findings: Glasgow-3538

## Current conclusion

Glasgow-3538 is documented indirectly by the 24 April 1665 testament-index
entry for his spouse, Jonet Cowan, which places “Mathew Glasgow” at Pyperheugh
in Stevenston parish. The profile has the year wrong as 1664. No source reviewed
establishes his birth or parents.

Matthew Glasgow at Ardeer Nether in the Cunninghame teind list of 1559–60 is a
different, much earlier man. He was already a tenant/head of household about 106
years before the later record. Their shared uncommon name and parish make possible descent or
collateral kinship worth investigating, but do not prove it.

## New-profile duplicate check

On 16 July 2026, `glasgow-one-tree.xlsx` and the live WikiTree `searchPerson`
API were searched for both Matthew Glasgow and Mathew Glasgow. The spreadsheet
contains no 1500s candidate. The live search returned seven viewable Matthews,
all born in 1650 or later, and two viewable Mathews, both born in the 1800s. It
also returned one privacy-restricted result (numeric person ID 15776853) with no
name, date, place, or WikiTree ID available for comparison. No existing public
profile matches the tenant recorded at Ardeer Nether in 1559–60.

Paste-ready draft for the new person is stored surname-wide at
`../Glasgow-3908/1542_Scotland_Ardeer_Nether_Matthew_Glasgow.md`.

## Suggested follow-up

Search Stevenston land, teind, kirk-session, and testament records between 1560
and 1665 for Glasgow households at Ardeer, Pyperheugh, Brakpleuch, Dubbs, and
Corsankell. A chain of relational records is required before linking the two
Matthews.
