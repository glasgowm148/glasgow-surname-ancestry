# Findings: [Thomas Glasgow (Glasgow-3556), born 1670, County Donegal, Ireland](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3556)

Last updated: 2026-08-09

Record every useful result and negative search here as it is found.
Do not treat an unsourced tree or naming pattern as proof.

## Current conclusion

Thomas is a genuine adult Donegal actor documented at Lifford in 1689 and in
the Raphoe/Ballindrait-Shannon property network from 1692 to 1695. These records
do not prove an Irish birth, exact age, spouse, children or relationship to
Archibald of Ramelton, Nathaniel of Keady/Fintona, or the later Killybegs James.

The 1693 Ballindrait material is also published with the surname **Glascow**.
Together with Archibald's `Glasgow/Glascoe` forms, the chronology, uncommon
surname and Donegal geography make a close Archibald-Thomas relationship a
reasonable working hypothesis. They still do not establish father, uncle,
brother or another degree.

   Ballindrait/Shannon deed sequence.
2. Use **born before 1689**, place unknown, rather than a certain 1670 County
   Donegal birth.
3. Describe him as a documented `Esq.` and legal/property actor, not as landed
   elite unless estate ownership is separately proved.
4. Do not attach him as son of Archibald-1101 or father of Nathaniel-1136.
5. Keep Thomas Glasgow-1103, the Scottish child baptised in 1612, separate from
   this Lifford man unless a cross-channel identity record is found.
6. Treat the Scottish Thomas baptised in 1612 as a competing family-structure
   lead: the Donegal Thomas could belong to his descendants rather than being
   Archibald's son. Do not identify the men on chronology alone.
