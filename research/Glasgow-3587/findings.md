# Findings: Glasgow-3587

## Current conclusion

William Glasgon married Margaret Scott at Hummelknows on 4 March 1708/9. His
parents and birthplace are not stated. The existing Cavers birthplace should be
retained as an uncertain estimate unless stronger evidence replaces it. A 1710
Isabel at Humble Knows is a strong candidate child but her mother is unnamed.

[Glasgow-4138](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4138), a William Glasgow documented as a Cavers/Ormistoun servant in 1679–84, is a possible earlier identity for this groom. Chronology and locality fit, but neither record supplies a shared relative, occupation or other identifying bridge. The attached Glasgow-1605 father is a separate unproved Polwarth baptism hypothesis. The two managed adult profiles were cross-linked with record citations on 26 September 2026 and remain unmerged.
