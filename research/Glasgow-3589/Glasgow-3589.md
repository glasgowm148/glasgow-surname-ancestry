== Biography ==

'''Marjorie Glasgow''' was a lawful child of [[Glasgow-3181|John Glasgow]] in
Hirmanscheillis. John's testament dative records Katherine and Marjorie as his
lawful children and executors-dative.<ref name="JohnTestament">National Records
of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/5, images 276–277, testament
dative and inventory of Johne Glasgw; ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register of
Testaments, 1514–1600'', [https://archive.org/details/commissariotreco16scot/page/n117/mode/2up?q=Glasgow p. 109], entry dated 4 May 1577; accepted human reading of the original register.</ref>

The inherited birth estimate of about 1575 and Hirmanscheillis location remain
uncertain profile fields, not facts established by the testament. No independent
record identifies Marjorie's later life, death, or birthplace.

== Research Notes ==

The accepted reading of the second child's name is ''m[ar]iory'', modernised as
Marjorie. The earlier model reading Androw/Andrew is superseded and is not a
competing current reading. John is therefore a proved father. Margaret Hoghame
is named as John's widow and is represented on WikiTree by
[[Hutcheson-1656|Margaret Hutcheson]], but the possessive before ''lauchfull
barnis'' is unresolved; retain the attached mother relationship as uncertain.

The record-defined Andrew must not be substituted into this Marjorie profile.

A Margaret Glasko was buried at Norwich, England, on 22 August 1588.<ref name="Norwich">“England, Deaths and Burials, 1538–1991,” FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:J87G-3P8 entry for Margaret Glasko], 22 August 1588.</ref> That entry has a different forename and no known connection to the Hirmenschelis household. It is retained as an excluded same-name lead, not as Marjorie's death record.
