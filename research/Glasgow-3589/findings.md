# Findings: [Marjorie Glasgow (Glasgow-3589)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3589)

## Current conclusion

The accepted human reading of John Glasgow's `CC8/8/5` testament names
Katherine and **Marjory** (`m[ar]iory`) as John's lawful children and
executors-dative. The earlier model reading Androw/Andrew is superseded and is
not a competing current reading. The testament therefore supports Marjorie as a
named lawful child of
[John Glasgow (Glasgow-3181)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3181).

The record does not establish a later adult identity, a birth or death date, or
a birth/death place for Marjorie, and the possessive before *lauchfull barnis*
is not securely distinguished as *his* or *thair*, so Margaret's maternity is
not securely proved.

Because no evidenced replacement parents are available, retain the attached
[John Glasgow (Glasgow-3181)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3181) and
[Margaret Hutcheson (Hutcheson-1656)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hutcheson-1656),
marking the father relationship proved and the mother relationship uncertain.

## Relationship and identity assessment

- John Glasgow-3181: **proved father** in the testament's lawful-children clause.
- Margaret/Hutcheson-1656: **documented widow** of John; retain as an **uncertain**
  mother because the possessive before *lauchfull barnis* is unresolved.
- Katherine Glasgow-3590: a proved child in the same source household and a
  probable sibling; the record names them together.
- The superseded Andrew reading does not establish another child or an
  identity lead to Glasgow-3163. Do not rename or merge Marjorie to Andrew.
