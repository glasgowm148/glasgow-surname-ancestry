== Biography ==

'''Katherine Glasgow''' was a lawful daughter of [[Glasgow-3181|John Glasgow]], the man indexed in Hirmenschelis. John's testament dative names Katherine and a second child, provisionally read Androw or Andrew, as his lawful children and executors-dative. Its confirmation repeats that John was their father.<ref name="JohnTestament">National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/5, images 276–277, testament dative and inventory of Johne Glasgw; ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments, 1514–1600'', [https://archive.org/details/commissariotreco16scot/page/n117/mode/2up?q=Glasgow p. 109], entry dated 4 May 1577.</ref>

Margaret, John's widow, submitted the inventory for the children. Her surname is only provisionally read as Cochrane, and the pronoun before “lawful bairns” is unresolved. The record is consistent with Margaret being their mother but does not independently prove maternity. The attached [[Hutcheson-1656|Margaret Hutcheson]] should therefore remain an uncertain mother.<ref name="JohnTestament" />

Katherine's birth about 1575 and at Hirmenschelis or Hirmanscheillis are estimates based on her father's record-defined household, not on a birth record. She was alive by the estate's court/index date, 4 May 1577.<ref>[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FWILLS%2F057444&tab=this Findmypast, Scotland, Will and Testament Index 1481–1807, record SCOT/WILLS/057444], John Glasgow in Hirmenschelis, 4 May 1577; derivative index.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

One debt owed by John's estate was wages to a '''Katherin Glasgow, servant'''. This may be the daughter Katherine, but the debt clause does not state that the servant and child were the same person.<ref name="JohnTestament" />

The high-resolution reading does not name [[Glasgow-3589|Marjorie Glasgow]] as Katherine's sibling. It instead names Androw/Andrew. [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow]] of the Calder Cleir area is a possible later identity for him, but no record yet connects the two households.

No inspected source establishes Katherine's later life, marriage or death.
