# Findings: [Katherine Glasgow (Glasgow-3590)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3590)

## Current conclusion

[Katherine Glasgow (Glasgow-3590)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3590)
is the unique compatible profile for the Katherine Glasgow named in John
Glasgow's `CC8/8/5` testament dative. The record directly identifies
[John Glasgow (Glasgow-3181)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3181) as
her father. Margaret, John's widow, presented the inventory for the children,
but the pronoun before “lawful bairns” is unresolved. The record therefore does
not independently prove maternity, and Margaret's identity with the attached
[Margaret Hutcheson (Hutcheson-1656)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hutcheson-1656)
is not proved.

Katherine's birth about 1575 and at Hirmenschelis/Hirmanscheillis are useful
identity estimates, not birth-record facts. She was alive by the administration
dated 4 May 1577. A wage debt to “Katherin Glasgow, servant” may refer to her,
but the clause does not say that the servant was John's daughter.

## Relationship assessment

- Father John Glasgow-3181: **proved**.
- Mother Margaret/Hutcheson-1656: **unproved**. Retain the attachment as
  uncertain; presenting the inventory for the children is consistent with but
  not proof of maternity.
- Sibling Androw/Andrew: **proved as a second child of John and Margaret, name
  provisionally read**. Glasgow-3163 is only a possible identity.
- Glasgow-3589: not the sibling named by this record.
