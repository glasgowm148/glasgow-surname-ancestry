# Thomas Glascow, Rathdowney 1777

Rathdowney Catholic parish register, NLI microfilm 05013/08, image 83, manuscript p.147, Thomas entry near the foot of the right-hand page. [Original page](https://registers.nli.ie/pages/vtls000631901_083). Inspected 22 October 2026.

The original Rathdowney Catholic register records Thoms, born 1 May 1777 and baptised 7 May, son of David Glascow and Anne Ford. The two May dates describe different events; the profile’s 1 May structured birth date and the indexed 7 May baptism are compatible. The existing biography’s description of 7 May as his birth needs correction.

The legible operative reading is “May 7th”, “Thoms”, “born 1st”, “son of David Glascow” and “Anne Ford”; this is a bounded field reading, not a complete diplomatic transcription. The year is supplied by the page heading 1777. The preceding and following rows were inspected to distinguish entries.

This assessment identifies the register child represented by Glasgow-3592 and his expressly named parents. It does not prove that this child became the Tipperary husband of Johanna Ryan or father of Michael Glasgow, and does not identify David with the Scottish David Glasgow-3456. No new parent is attached and no paternal family is joined. Margaret’s separate baptism and the 1773 marriage have not been inspected in this batch. The household townland and sponsors are not securely read from the available low-resolution scan and are not asserted here.

Read the 14 September 1773 Rathdowney marriage and Margaret’s July 1774 entry for the household place and witnesses, then test the later Thomas–Johanna–Michael identification against original Tipperary records. David’s own parents remain unknown; his before-1755 birth is an adult-marriage estimate, not a record of his birthplace or age.
