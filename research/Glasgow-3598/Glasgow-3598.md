== Research Notes ==
=== Selected uncertain working father — 15 October 2026 ===

'''Ninian Glasgow ([[Glasgow-3188]]) is selected as an uncertain working father.''' Select daughter from the existing daughter-or-niece comparison with Ninian Glasgow. Margaret married Edinburgh cordiner William Hardie in 1611. Barbara Glasgow married another Edinburgh cordiner in 1616, while Ninian’s documented Glasgow son Robert is probably the cordiner admitted in 1613. Ninian’s possible son William supplies a family route into South Leith. Margaret’s estimated adult generation fits Ninian’s approximate 1550 generation. Together the generation, adjoining Edinburgh–Leith setting and repeated family craft make Ninian’s household the leading existing placement; daughter supplies the simplest provisional degree.

No record names Margaret’s father or proves that she and Barbara were sisters. Niece, another close relative or another local Glasgow family remain alternatives. The cordiners worked in different burghs; a shared trade does not prove one incorporation or apprenticeship. The Robert-cordiner identity and William’s Ninian parentage are supporting inferences. Margaret’s before-1595 birth is an adult-status estimate, not a baptism. Repeated William Hardie marriages do not prove one serial husband or Margaret’s death. No mother is selected.

This is a revisable local inference from the existing family assessment, not newly documented parentage. The original biography and its competing identity and parentage explanations remain intact. No reviewed record identifies Margaret's parents or birthplace. Her structured birth before 1595 is only an uncertain adult-status estimate from the 1611 marriage.

The same published-register page records Barbara Glasgow marrying cordiner George Johnstoun in 1616 and Isobel Glasgow marrying tailor William Symountoun in 1619. This craft-marriage cluster is a useful kinship lead, not proof that the women were sisters.

=== William Hardie's identity ===
The Edinburgh marriage register lists a William Hardie, cordiner, with Eupham Wauchope on 1 September 1608, Margaret Glasgow on 16 July 1611, Catharine Leslie on 27 August 1641 and Catharine Angus on 17 November 1643.<ref>Henry Paton, ed., ''The Register of Marriages for the Parish of Edinburgh, 1595–1700'', [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/register_of_marriages_for_the_parish_of_edinburgh_1595-1700_~_1898.pdf#page=317 printed p. 307], entries under Hardie (Hardy).</ref> The repeated name and trade make these entries useful for tracing Margaret's husband, but they do not establish that all four marriages involved one man. The later entries could concern a younger namesake. Original parish and cordiner records are needed before inferring Margaret's death or additional relatives.

=== Possible family placement ===
Margaret is best placed as a possible daughter or niece of [[Glasgow-3188|Ninian Glasgow]]. Her husband William Hardie was a cordiner.<ref>Henry Paton, ed., ''The Register of Marriages for the Parish of Edinburgh, 1595-1700'', [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/register_of_marriages_for_the_parish_of_edinburgh_1595-1700_~_1898.pdf#page=277 printed p. 267], 16 July 1611.</ref> Ninian's proved son Robert was also a cordiner, while his probable son [[Glasgow-3075|William]] moved into Leith and William's son was apprenticed to a cordiner.

This direct craft and Edinburgh-Leith pattern makes Ninian's household or immediate collateral circle the leading branch, but the marriage register names no parent. The possible daughter-or-niece relationship remains uncertain.
