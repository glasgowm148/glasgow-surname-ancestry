# Findings: Thomas Glasgow

## Current conclusion

Current recorded identity and family fields retain their evidence limits.

## Eccles memorial identity comparison — 4 November 2026

The Eccles and Birgham churchyard memorial transcript names [Robert Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3706), joiner and feuar at Leitholm, his wife [Agnes Whitehead](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Whitehead-9630), daughter Euphans and two sons named Thomas. It gives one Thomas's death as April 1790, aged 8, and the other's as 27 April 1791, aged 3. Euphans died on 7 April 1791, aged 5. Robert's death is transcribed as 8 October 1800 or 1809, aged 55; Agnes's as 21 May 1840, aged 84. The two Thomas entries and Euphans's companion entry repeat one monument and are not independent witnesses.

Thomas is a candidate for the elder son in this memorial. His unverified March 1781 Eccles birth would imply about 9 years of age in April 1790, compared with the reported age of 8. This weak, unverified birth estimate does not exclude him, but the discrepancy prevents an established identification. His FamilySearch tree citation does not by itself establish the underlying birth entry. The younger son has not been assigned to an existing profile.

The ages imply that the two same-named sons' lifetimes overlapped. That unusual feature, the elder son's age discrepancy and Robert's ambiguous death year require checking against the original inscription or a photograph and the Eccles parish entries. The indexed birth years 1782 and 1788 are derived from ages at death, not recorded birth dates. The churchyard locality does not establish either son's birthplace or exact burial date. Neither Thomas's death nor his parents are established by this comparison.
