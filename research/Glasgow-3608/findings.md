# Findings: [John Glasgow (Glasgow-3608), Mid Calder](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3608)

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

John is not merely an unsourced name: derivative Mid Calder baptisms identify a
`Jhone Glasgow` as father of John in 1608 and William in 1609. Glasgow-3608 may
be retained as an uncertain record-defined placeholder, but the original pages
must be compared before concluding that both entries name the same adult.

They do **not** identify John's parents, wife, birth or death. The structured
attachments to [Stephen Glasgow (Glasgow-1029)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1029)
and [Margaret Graham (Graham-38221)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Graham-38221)
therefore remain unsupported. Mid Calder is a documented child-baptism
location, not a proved birth or death place for John.

[John Glasgow (Glasgow-1561)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1561) is a
credible duplicate candidate because another John fathered children at nearby
Linlithgow in 1613, 1617 and 1622. No inspected record connects the two parish
clusters, however. Keep both profiles separate unless an original record
bridges them. [Glasgow-3296](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3296), the
later Montfoid/Quothquan man, is a weaker identity candidate.

## Recommended WikiTree changes

1. Retain [John-1086](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1086) and
   [William-1087](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1087) only as uncertain
   children pending comparison of the original 1608 and 1609 entries; cite the
   derivative indexes and state their limits.
2. Mark Stephen-1029 and Graham-38221 **Uncertain** and state that no record
   connects their Glasgow-burgh marriage/property household to this Mid Calder
   father. If the profile is being kept strictly record-defined, detach both;
   do not replace them with another speculative couple.
3. Replace the apparent `before 1590, Mid Calder` birth with **before 1608,
   Scotland, uncertain**, or retain `before about 1590` only when clearly
   labelled as an estimate from parenthood.
4. Remove Mid Calder as a death place unless a separate death record is added.
5. Add Glasgow-1561 as an unresolved duplicate candidate, but **do not merge**
   on geography and chronology alone.

## Do not infer

- Stephen Glasgow and Margaret Graham as parents from chronology alone.
- A wife or mother of the two children.
- That the Mid Calder and Linlithgow fathers were one man without a bridge.
