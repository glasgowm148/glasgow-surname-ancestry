# Findings: John Glasgow (Glasgow-3610), reconstructed 1649 father

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

Glasgow-3610 is known from a derivative entry naming an adult Johne Glasgow as
father of a child Johne christened on 22 April 1649 under the combined
Kirknewton and East Calder catalogue label. That record says nothing about the
adult father's parents.

Disconnect Glasgow-3610 as a son of William Glasgow-1087. His structured 1626
birth and wider identity are not supplied by the child's baptism. Preserve him
only as a record-defined father unless a separate record proves those details.
