== Biography ==

'''John Estoun or Edstoun''' lived at Croftmalloch in the sixteenth century. [[Glasgow-3613|Margaret Glasgow]] is described in her testament record and its published index as his spouse or sometime wife. Margaret died intestate in December 1578.<ref name="MargaretTestament">National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/8, images 355–356, testament dative and inventory of Margaret Glasgow.</ref><ref name="MargaretIndex">[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FWILLS%2F057454&tab=this Findmypast, Scotland, Will and Testament Index 1481–1807, record SCOT/WILLS/057454], Margaret Glasgow, sometime spouse to John Edstoun in Croftmalloch, 8 November 1580; ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments, 1514–1600'', [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso01scotuoft/page/n117/mode/1up p. 108].</ref>

Margaret's son [[Edstoun-4|Cuthbert Kaddell or Keddell]] of Croftmalloch gave up her inventory as executor-dative. His surname appears as Kaddell in the opening and Keddell in the confirmation. The testament does not identify Cuthbert as John's son.<ref name="MargaretTestament" />

== Research Notes ==

John is a natural candidate for Cuthbert's father because he was Margaret's husband, but the relationship is not stated. The existing father attachment should remain uncertain pending a baptism, marriage, property or court record that names both men.

The record does not establish John's death date. The 8 November 1580 date belongs to Margaret's index entry, while the executor decree appears dated 16 October 1580. Neither date is evidence that John died before 1580.

James Estoun or Easton appears in Margaret's estate only as a debtor. No kinship with John is stated.
