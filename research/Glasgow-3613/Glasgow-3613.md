== Biography ==

'''Margaret Glasgow''' was the spouse or sometime wife of [[Edstoun-3|John Estoun or Edstoun]] in Croftmalloch. She died intestate in '''December 1578'''. Her testament dative was dealt with by the Edinburgh Commissary Court in 1580.<ref name="MargaretTestament">National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/8, images 355–356, testament dative and inventory of Margaret Glasgow.</ref><ref name="MargaretIndex">''Scotland, Will and Testament Index 1481–1807'', Margaret Glasgow, sometime spouse to John Edstoun in Croftmalloch, [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FWILLS%2F057454&tab=this record SCOT/WILLS/057454], 8 November 1580; see also ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments, 1514–1600'', [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso01scotuoft/page/n117/mode/1up p. 108].</ref>

Margaret's son '''[[Edstoun-4|Cuthbert Kaddell or Keddell]]''' of Croftmalloch gave up her inventory and was appointed executor-dative in place of the procurator fiscal. His surname appears as Kaddell in the opening and Keddell in the confirmation. The record repeatedly calls Cuthbert her son. The appointing decree appears to be dated '''16 October 1580'''.<ref name="MargaretTestament" />

The confirmation repeats the relationship in two independent phrases: Cuthbert
is “sone to the said umquhile Margaret” and Margaret is “his mother.” This is
direct evidence of maternity, not an inference from his surname or appointment
as executor.<ref name="MargaretTestament" />

=== Estate ===

Margaret's inventory describes a modest agricultural household. It includes
two cows, two one-year-old heifers, sheep including ''hoggis'' or young sheep,
six bolls of oats in the barn and barnyard, household utensils and furnishings,
and her clothing. Some individual stock quantities and valuations are unclear,
but the inventory total is legible as '''£47 14s 8d Scots'''.<ref name="MargaretTestament" />

The debts owed to Margaret include '''£8 from James Estoun in Wester
Whitburn'''. A second debtor, read provisionally as Michael Mertyn, owed 50
shillings. The recorded debts receivable total £10 10s, giving a gross estate
of £58 4s 8d.<ref name="MargaretTestament" /> James appears only as a debtor;
the record gives no kinship between him and Margaret, John or Cuthbert.

Margaret owed '''20 shillings to Roland Dundas''' in an entry concerning a
year's ''teind'', or tithe, on the ground. After that debt was deducted, the
free estate was £57 4s 8d. It was divided into two portions and the deceased's
part was recorded as £28 12s 4d.<ref name="MargaretTestament" />

The arithmetic reconciles: £47 14s 8d plus £10 10s is £58 4s 8d; after
the £1 debt, half of the £57 4s 8d balance is the recorded £28 12s 4d.
These are contemporary Scots amounts, not modern sterling values.<ref name="MargaretTestament" />

=== Court confirmation ===

The confirmation names the Edinburgh commissaries '''Edward Henryson''' and
'''Alexander Sym'''. It records the formal citation process, the procurator
fiscal's initial appointment, Cuthbert's substitution in his place and the
grant of authority to administer Margaret's estate. The marginal confirmation
date is '''8 November 1580''', matching the published index. The day in the
appointment decree is less certain and should remain '''[16?] October
1580'''.<ref name="MargaretTestament" /><ref name="MargaretIndex" />

== Research Notes ==

=== Dates and record boundaries ===

The index date, 8 November 1580, is not Margaret's death date. The manuscript
death clause reads December 1578. The older readings November 1578, a decree
dated 17 October, and a marginal date of the last day of November are not
supported by the higher-resolution reading.

Margaret's entry begins in the lower part of image 355 and continues on image
356. The upper portion of image 355 belongs to the preceding estate and its
names must not be incorporated into Margaret's family.

=== Family interpretation ===

The record proves Margaret's marriage to John and maternity of Cuthbert. It does not call John Cuthbert's father. John is a plausible father because he was Margaret's husband, but that relationship should remain uncertain unless another record states it.

Cuthbert's Kaddell/Keddell surname could indicate that he was a son of an
earlier Kaddell marriage, but the testament does not identify his father or an
earlier husband. Conversely, the phrase “sometime spouse” does not by itself
establish when or how Margaret's marriage to John ended.

The attached [[Glasgow-1096|Robert Glasgow]] and [[Rolland-239|Margaret Rolland]] are not named as Margaret's parents in this testament. They are retained only as uncertain working attachments.

No birth date or place for Margaret is stated. Croftmalloch is her documented
married residence and place at death, not direct evidence of her birthplace.

The readings of the core relationships are stronger than several personal
names and legal details. Kaddell/Keddell is supported by its two occurrences;
Michael Mertyn remains provisional. A possible cautioner's name in the closing
formula has not been used because it is not secure and would not establish kinship.
