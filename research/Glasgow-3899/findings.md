# Findings: Glasgow-3899

Last updated: 2026-07-17

## Current answer

David Glasgow's father is now directly proved. Daniel Glasgow of Kilrea called
David of the City of London `my son` in his original 1871 will. The will does not
name David's mother.

## Relationship effect

Daniel `Glasgow-3896` may be marked as David's certain father. Margaret Irwin's
relationship remains uncertain because the will does not name David's mother and
the only located record naming Margaret as a mother concerns Robert in 1827.
David's probate supplies no evidence that Daniel was a son of Adam Glasgow.
