# Findings: Glasgow-3903

Last updated: 2026-08-19

## Current answer

James Glasgow is directly identified as Adam and Rose Glasgow's son by the family
gravestone. He is no longer the best identity for the James in the 1847
Barkly-child report. An original 1851 marriage instead proves that the younger
James `Glasgow-1623`, also of Inishrush, married Rosanna Barkley, farm servant of
Tyanee and daughter of Henry Barkley—the exact people and relationship described
in the report. Do not attach the unnamed child to this older James.
