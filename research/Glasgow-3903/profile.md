== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' was born about 1794, calculated from the age of eighty-four
reported at his death. The Glasgow family monument at Tamlaght O'Crilly calls
him a son of [[Glasgow-3902|Adam Glasgow]] and Rose.<ref name="Grave">[https://www.oneirishrover.com/tamlaght-graveyard/ ''Tamlaght O'Crilly Lower Graveyard''], transcription of the Adam and Rose Glasgow family monument.</ref>

The leading civil record identifies James as a married farmer of Inishrush who
died there on 12 December 1878, reportedly aged eighty-four. Henry Glasgow was
present at the death; the register does not state Henry's relationship.<ref name="Death1878">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/deaths_returns/deaths_1878/020497/7192875.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil death register], James Glasgow, Inishrush, 12 December 1878, entry 138.</ref>

The family monument transcribes the death as 11 December, one day earlier than
the civil register.<ref name="Grave" /> The two sources concern the same death;
the original civil-register date is used for the structured field and the
one-day conflict is retained here.

== Research Notes ==

=== The two James identities ===

An original 1851 marriage records another—or possibly the same—James Glasgow as
a 38-year-old bachelor farmer of Inishrush, son of Adam Glasgow. He married
Rosanna Barkley, daughter of Henry Barkley.<ref name="Marriage1851">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/view/?record_id=cima-3208068 IrishGenealogy.ie], marriage of James Glasgow and Rosanna Barkley, 17 March 1851.</ref> A man aged 38 in 1851 would have been about 65 or 66 in 1878, not 84. The
records agree on name, father's name, occupation and locality but conflict by
about nineteen years in age.

This profile represents the monument-defined son of Adam and Rose and the
age-84 death. [[Glasgow-1623|James Glasgow]], the 1851 groom, remains a separate
working profile while the one-man and two-man explanations are tested. The
existing records do not justify merging them or creating two independently
proved fathers named Adam.

=== Unproved associates ===

Henry Glasgow must not be attached as James's son merely because he informed
the death. The later valuation sequence from James to Rose Anne and then Adam
shows occupancy succession at Inishrush plot 29, not inheritance or kinship.

A 20 March 1847 family letter reported that a James Glasgow had a child by a
servant who was a daughter of Henry Barkly.<ref name="Letter1847">[https://www.hiltner.com/mckeown/documents/John%20Glasgow%20letter%20to%20Jane%20Glasgow%20Gray%203-20-1847.pdf John Glasgow to Jane Glasgow Gray], 20 March 1847.</ref> The 1851 James-and-Rosanna marriage is the closer documentary match. The
letter does not name or sex the child, and the child should not be attached to
this older James.
