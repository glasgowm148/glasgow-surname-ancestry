== Biography ==

{{One Name Study|name=Glasgow}}

'''Alexander Glasgow''' is the proposed head of an early Bann Valley family.
His existence and family are reconstructed from later descendant testimony; no
contemporary record yet identifies him with certainty.

If [[Glasgow-3896|Daniel Glasgow]], born about 1795 or 1796, was his son,
Alexander must have been born before about 1778. The former estimate of about
1790 is incompatible with that relationship.

Alexander is associated with Rosanna, surname unknown. A 2007 descendant account
names Alexander and Rosanna as parents of [[Glasgow-987|Thomas Glasgow]] of
Lisnagaver and reports that Rosanna died about 23 October 1822.<ref name="DescendantAccount">[https://www.genesreunited.co.uk/boards/board/general_topics/thread/465937 “Trying to find Glasgow,” ''Genes Reunited''], descendant account posted 25 January 2007.</ref> No marriage, baptism or will has been found to verify the account. Rosanna's reported surname Shaw is unsupported and should remain a research lead only.

Daniel and Thomas are therefore proved brothers. Their common attachment to
Alexander and Rosanna remains an uncertain reconstruction. A half-sibling
relationship or an error in the later tradition has not been excluded.

=== Parentage ===

[[Glasgow-3902|Adam Glasgow]] and Rose were previously attached as Alexander's
parents. No inspected record names Adam, and Rose's age chronologically excludes
her from being the mother of a man old enough to father Daniel about 1795/96.
Adam and Rose were removed. [[Glasgow-3905|Unknown Glasgow]] is retained as
Alexander's uncertain structural father placeholder until this branch can be
placed beneath a source-identified ancestor. The placeholder is not a documented
person and must not be treated as an identified ancestor.

An Alexander and Robert Glasgow were tithe occupiers at Lisnagaver in 1834.<ref name="Tithe1834">[https://www.billmacafee.com/182030stithe/tithenorthantrimrevisionorder.pdf Bill MacAfee, ''North Antrim Tithe Applotment Books arranged in document order''], records 7493–7494, Lisnagaver, 1834.</ref> The entry may include this Alexander or his younger namesake, but it supplies no age, spouse or relationship.

=== Y-DNA and cluster placement ===

A direct paternal descendant of younger Alexander has a Big Y-700 result at
R-FT20271. His closest displayed project pairing is the tested James J Glasgow
line at genetic distance three over Y-111.<ref name="FTDNA">[https://www.familytreedna.com/public/Glasgow?iframe=ydna-results-overview FamilyTreeDNA Glasgow Surname Project, public Y-DNA results], kits B580327 and 1002232.</ref> This proves that the two tested male lines share a paternal Glasgow ancestor, but it cannot name that ancestor or prove any of Alexander's three working child links.

The family should remain a Bann Valley–Kilrea–Rasharkin research cluster. It
should not be forced beneath Adam of Inishrush, John of Glenleslie or a
Kilwaughter founder without a relationship record.

=== Highest-priority surviving family register ===

PRONI's Ballymoney Reformed Presbyterian register, references
'''CR5/32/1/1/1–2''', covers 1804–1844 and includes Dervock and Kilraughts
society lists, baptisms, marriages, deaths and removals. It spans younger
Alexander's probable birth, Thomas's probable birth and Rosanna's reported
death.<ref name="PRONICR5Guide">[https://www.proni.gov.uk/files/proni/2026-01/PRONI%20Guide%20to%20Church%20Records.pdf Public Record Office of Northern Ireland, ''Guide to Church Records'']; [https://apps.proni.gov.uk/eCatNI_IE/SearchPage.aspx PRONI eCatalogue], references CR5/32/1/1/1–2.</ref>

A targeted surname-variant and family-group request was sent to PRONI on 19
August 2026. Until the register is inspected, it does not prove this older
Alexander's existence, spouse or children.
