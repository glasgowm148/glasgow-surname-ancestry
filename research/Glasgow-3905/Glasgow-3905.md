## Suggested corrections

- Remove Inishrush as Unknown Glasgow's birthplace. No record identifies Unknown,
  places Adam's father there, or proves that Adam was born there.
- A placeholder profile cannot have a meaningful birth estimate of "about 1740."
  If retained, use only "before 1758" or leave the field blank; the present date is
  not evidence of an event.
- The record-defined 1831 John now exists as `Glasgow-3927`, with a strong
  identity case for `Glasgow-822`. If retained beneath this placeholder, mark
  the relationship uncertain. Probable brotherhood with Adam belongs in Research
  Notes until a relational record is found; cousin remains possible.
- The two 1740 Hugh households support a cleaner structural possibility: entry
  1272 is the stronger Hugh-548 candidate because of the Ellis/McCullough
  cluster, while entry 1195 could head the collateral older-Adam/Maria line.
  This remains a hypothesis because neither entry names children or kin.
- `Glasgow-549` and `Glasgow-550` remain possible direct fathers, but the current
  evidence does not distinguish them. Do not put this placeholder beneath either
  man: that would add a chronologically impossible generation between men born
  in the mid-1730s and Adam born about 1758.
- The best internally consistent naming model gives Adam and John's possible
  shared parents the names older Adam and Maria. That does not identify this
  placeholder or justify creating those parents. Descent from the other 1740
  Hugh is structurally cleaner than making older Adam an omitted fourth son of
  Hugh-548, whose late account explicitly lists only three sons. Neither name nor
  relationship is proved.

## Do not add as fact

- Do not name Robert, John, Hugh, or an inferred Adam/Maria couple as proved parents. Preserve John-549 and Robert-550 as undistinguished alternatives and older Adam/Maria as the better naming-model hypothesis.
- Do not call younger Hugh conclusively excluded by Adam's omission from the will; the instrument does not claim to name every child and also omits two children in the later seven-child account.
- Do not describe the two 1740 Hugh householders as Inishrush residents.
- Do not cite a missing Glasgow in the 1766 Religious Census or 1775 Dissenters'
  Petitions as evidence of absence; neither surviving collection covers the parish.
- Do not infer that a Glasgow obtained an Inishrush lease in 1764 merely because the whole townland was advertised; the notice names no tenants.
- Do not treat the four surnames on the reported 1801 joint lease as a complete Inishrush census; it covered only part of a townland with layered and intermixed occupancy.
- Do not treat the 1780 Inishrush Volunteers or 1783 Protestant-meeting notices as rosters; ordinary members and attendees were not printed.
- Do not identify the 1799 signatory with Adam of Inishrush as fact. The original
  scan supplies no address; it also disproves the former Cloughole assignment.
- Do not use attached but unproved sons of Adam to reverse-engineer his father's name.
- Do not retain the Alexander-Daniel-Thomas attachments as a coherent certain tree:
  Daniel's original will makes him Thomas's brother, while those links make him
  Thomas's uncle.
- Do not create a child of James, identify adult Henry Glasgow as that child, or assign Roseanne the Barkly maiden name from the 1847 report; none is explicitly documented.
- Do not claim a negative Second Portglenone baptism search; the surviving target
  pages are onsite-only and have not been read.
- Do not attach Margaret Glasgow, wife of John Patton of Tyanee, as Adam's daughter or sister without a relational source.
- Do not interpret John Glasgow's 1847 reference to a letter from "Adam" as contact
  with Adam of Inishrush; the target died in 1844 and the writer had a son Adam.
- Do not apply the current Y-DNA comparison to candidate Robert `Glasgow-550` or
  target Adam; neither man has a documented strict male-line tester in the table.

  membership list or infer a kinship between John and Adam from John's attendance.

- Do not identify the married Killwaughter leaseholder as `Glasgow-550`; he was an
  adult leaseholder in 1738, not a child born about 1737.
