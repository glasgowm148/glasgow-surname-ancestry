# Ulster archives research log: Glasgow-3905

Last updated: 2026-07-16

## Scope

Pre-1800 primary and near-primary records capable of identifying the father of Adam Glasgow of Inishrush (born about 1758), with explicit testing of Robert, John, and the local Hugh Glasgow cluster.

### Audit conclusion

No `Glasgow`, `Glasgo`, `Glasco`, `Glascow`, or close `Glasg*` surname occurs in the searchable text of the four downloaded decade pages. This does not identify Adam's father and does not materially favor Robert over Hugh or John: the most relevant Inishrush notices are advertisements without tenant names, while the 1780 and 1783 notices omit ordinary members/attendees. The strongest new result is a sequence of estate transactions (1758 bleaching works, 1764 Inishrush land re-letting, 1792 mill re-letting) that sharpens the target date and record series for the Vintners/Conolly lease chain.
