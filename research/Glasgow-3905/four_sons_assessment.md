## Resolved tree-topology conflict

The former attachments could not all be correct:

1. WikiTree attaches Alexander and Daniel as Adam's sons.
2. WikiTree attaches Thomas as Alexander's son.
3. Daniel's original will calls Thomas his brother.

Under the WikiTree arrangement Daniel would be Thomas's uncle, not his brother.
The original will is stronger evidence than the late Alexander/Rosanna account.
The working tree now places Daniel and Thomas as uncertain children of the older
Alexander and Rosanna, matching their proved sibling relationship. Younger
Alexander `Glasgow-951` is their probable brother and is attached to the same
uncertain parents. This is a useful topology, not proof of the parents.

Do not retain Adam and Rose above the older Alexander. No source names Adam as
his father, and Rose, born about 1767, is chronologically excluded as mother of a
man who needed to be born by about 1778 to father Daniel about 1795/96.

## Evidence standard applied

- A stated relationship in an original will, census, or civil register is direct
  evidence for that relationship.
- Absence from a will is adverse evidence only; it is not proof of non-relationship.
- A same-name tithe occupier, compatible age, locality, or naming pattern is a lead,
  not relationship evidence.
- The correct WikiTree action is to leave these parent links uncertain or remove
  them, not to replace Adam with another speculative parent.
