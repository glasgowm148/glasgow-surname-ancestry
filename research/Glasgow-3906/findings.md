# Findings: [Eliza Glasgow (Glasgow-3906)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3906)

## Current conclusion

The [Tamlaght O'Crilly Lower Glasgow gravestone](https://www.oneirishrover.com/tamlaght-graveyard/)
places Eliza in Adam and Rose Glasgow's family inscription, calls her their
daughter, and records her age as 26. The surviving inscription does not preserve
a readable death date.

The live profile has no proper source citation. Its fixed birth about 1790 and
death about 1816 cannot be calculated from the stone: without a death year, age
26 establishes neither year. The stone establishes a burial or commemoration
place, not necessarily Eliza's place of death.

These conflicts should be explained and the dates marked uncertain, but the
currently populated birth and death locations must not be cleared unless
specific sourced replacement locations are ready to be entered in the same
edit.
