# Findings: [Matthew Glasgow (Glasgow-3908), Ardeer Nether](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3908)

## Conclusion

The 1559-60 Cunninghame teind list separately names **Matthew Glasgow** at
Ardeer Nether. He is not a transcription duplicate of
[Michael-3911](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3911), who is separately
named at Brakpleuch on the same source page. Keep both profiles.

The `before 1542` birth is an adult-status estimate; the source supplies no
parents, spouse, children, exact birth or death.

## Mathie–Glencairn connection - 30 August 2026

The 1559–60 Cunninghame teind list places Matthew Glasgow and John Mathie
together at Ardeer Nether. This was part of a wider local pattern: Margaret
Mathie and William Mathie appear elsewhere in the same Stevenston list, along
with Michael Glasgow and the two Corsankell James Glasgows, within the
Cunningham/Glencairn estate landscape.

The connection is no longer based on the surname recurrence alone. On 10 May
1554 David Mathy and Robert Glasgow were fellow Glasgow burgesses and witnesses
to the same sasine. David had a documented son John; a John Mathie later acted
as a writer and was admitted with the Earl of Glencairn's burgess group in
1583. In 1600 a John Mathie of Clerkland had both John senior and junior in his
family circle. The earl's servant John Mathie was killed in July 1606, while a
second John Mathie wrote an estate bond that September and was probably his
son.

The family can now be taken one step further. Tutory retours entered on 20
January 1607 identify the deceased John Mathie of Ibert's full brother as
Matthew Mathie, burgess of Glasgow, and name John's lawful children James,
David, Agnes and Margaret. Ibert was in Kilmaronock parish under Glencairn
lordship, so this deceased John is a strong candidate for the servant killed in
July 1606. His brother Matthew was probably the Glasgow burgh officer who
appears in 1606–08. These are direct family and city links, although they do
not identify Matthew Glasgow of Ardeer as a relative.

[Jonet Mathie (Mathie-159)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Mathie-159), who
married [John Glasgow (Glasgow-1030)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1030)
in 1618, may belong to that Clerkland–Glencairn household. This does not make
the Ardeer John her proved ancestor, but it supplies a credible route between
Matthew's Ayrshire group and Robert Glasgow's city family. Jonet is not among
John of Ibert's four named children, but an older child could fall outside the
tutory appointment and the younger writer's household remains another
possibility. Full evidence and
identity controls are in the
[Mathie–Glasgow–Glencairn analysis](mathie-glencairn-bridge-1554-1618.md).

## Parent-cluster placement - 31 August 2026

[James Glasgow elder of Corsankell (Glasgow-1168)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1168)
is Matthew's leading possible father or uncle. Both men were tenant/householder
figures in the same 1559-60 Stevenston teind list: Matthew at Ardeer Nether and
James elder at Corsankell. James had proved sons James and John in 1571, but
that writ does not purport to enumerate every child. Matthew's estimated adult
birth generation permits either a younger son or a younger brother/nephew.

Assessment: **possible son or nephew; moderate locality/generation case**. If a
working father is required for tree navigation, James elder is the best
candidate and the edge must remain uncertain. Michael-3911 is better treated as
James's brother/cousin because he was already a legal witness in 1549.

Live application, 31 August 2026: James elder was attached as Matthew's working
father with the explicit WikiTree status **uncertain**. Public family readback
and the checked status control were verified.
