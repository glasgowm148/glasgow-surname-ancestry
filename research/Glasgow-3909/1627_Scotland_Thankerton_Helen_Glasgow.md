== Biography ==

'''Helen Glasgow''' was the spouse of Peter Broun of Thankerton, Lanarkshire. Her testament was recorded in the Lanark Commissary Court on 8 January 1627.<ref name="LanarkIndex">Francis J. Grant, ed., ''The Commissariot Record of Lanark: Register of Testaments, 1595–1800'' (Edinburgh: Scottish Record Society, 1903), printed p. 38, entry “Glasgow, Helen, spouse to Peter Broun, in Thankertoun,” [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/the_commissariot_record_of_lanark__register_of_testaments__1595-1800_~_1903.pdf#page=50 digital copy].</ref> She had died by that date.

The published index does not name Helen's parents, children, age or place of death. The original testament has not yet been reviewed.
