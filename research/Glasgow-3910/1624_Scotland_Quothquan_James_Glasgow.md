== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' was a weaver at Quothquan (written “Quodquen”) in the parish of Libberton, Lanarkshire. His testament was recorded in the Lanark Commissary Court on 26 July 1624.<ref name="LanarkIndex">Francis J. Grant, ed., ''The Commissariot Record of Lanark: Register of Testaments, 1595–1800'' (Edinburgh: Scottish Record Society, 1903), printed p. 38, entry “Glasgow, James, wobster in Quodquen,” [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/the_commissariot_record_of_lanark__register_of_testaments__1595-1800_~_1903.pdf#page=50 digital copy].</ref> He had died by that date.

The same index refers to Margaret Threipland, widow of a James Glesgow at Quothquan, whose testament was recorded on 27 October 1626.<ref name="ThreiplandIndex">Grant, ''Commissariot Record of Lanark'', printed p. 99, entry “Threipland, Margaret, relict of James Glesgow, in Quodquen,” [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/the_commissariot_record_of_lanark__register_of_testaments__1595-1800_~_1903.pdf#page=111 digital copy].</ref> She may have been this James's widow, but the index does not uniquely identify the two same-name Quothquan references as one man.
