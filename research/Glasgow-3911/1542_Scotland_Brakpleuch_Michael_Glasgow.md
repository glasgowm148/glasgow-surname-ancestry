== Biography ==

Michael Glasgow was born before 1542, probably in Scotland. The year is an estimate based on his being at least 18 years old when he appeared as a tenant in a record dated 1559–60.

In the Cunninghame teind lists of 1559–60, Michael Glasgow was listed with John Sparling, John Wilson, and Robert Wilson under Brakpleuch Over and Nether in the parish of Stevenston, Ayrshire.<ref name="Teinds1559">Margaret H. B. Sanderson, ''The People of Sixteenth Century Ayrshire'', ''Ayrshire Collections'', vol. 14, no. 8 (Ayrshire Archaeological and Natural History Society, 1987), appendix "The Cunninghame Teind Lists of 1559–60," [https://aanhs.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/people-of-ayrshire.pdf#page=36 p. 331], Brakpleuch Over and Nether entry. Sanderson identifies the underlying record as Scottish Record Office, Register of Acts and Decreets, vol. 22, fols. 102v–167v.</ref>

=== Research Notes ===

Suggested creation data:
* Birth: before 1542, Scotland, uncertain.
* Death: after 1558, place unknown, uncertain.

The same page also lists a Michael Glasgow with Finlay Brown and James Morton under Dubbs.<ref name="Teinds1559"/> Sanderson warns that one tenant could hold more than one property. The two entries may therefore describe one Michael Glasgow or two contemporaries. This profile is based on the certain Brakpleuch occurrence; the Dubbs man should not be merged into it as established fact or given a separate profile unless further evidence distinguishes the men.

No reviewed source identifies Michael's parents, spouse, children, precise birthplace, residence, or death. Brakpleuch and Dubbs should not be entered as birth or death places.

This Michael is not [[Glasgow-1406|Michael Glasgow]], whose estimated birth was more than a century later. No evidence currently establishes a relationship between them.
