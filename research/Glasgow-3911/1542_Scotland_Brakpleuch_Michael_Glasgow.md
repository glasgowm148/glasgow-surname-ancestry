== Biography ==

Michael Glasgow was probably born about 1525, perhaps in Ayrshire. The date and place are uncertain estimates based on his documented appearance as a witness at Stevenston in 1549.

On 28 January 1549 Michael Glasgow witnessed an instrument of sasine in favour of Janet Cunningham, daughter of John Cunningham of Caprington, and the heirs to be born between her and her husband Alexander, Earl of Glencairn, in the lands and barony of Stevenston and other lands. William Cunningham of Legland and Adam Mure were among the other witnesses.<ref name="NRS1549">National Records of Scotland, Charter Chest of the Earls of Glencairn, [https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/details.aspx?reference=GD39%2F1%2F59 GD39/1/59], instrument of sasine in favour of Janet Cunningham and Alexander, Earl of Glencairn, 28 January 1549. The official catalogue entry was inspected; no manuscript image is available online.</ref>

In the Cunninghame teind lists of 1559–60, Michael Glasgow was listed with John Sparling, John Wilson, and Robert Wilson under Brakpleuch Over and Nether in the parish of Stevenston, Ayrshire.<ref name="Teinds1559">Margaret H. B. Sanderson, ''The People of Sixteenth Century Ayrshire'', ''Ayrshire Collections'', vol. 14, no. 8 (Ayrshire Archaeological and Natural History Society, 1987), appendix "The Cunninghame Teind Lists of 1559–60," [https://aanhs.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/people-of-ayrshire.pdf#page=36 p. 331], Brakpleuch Over and Nether entry. Sanderson identifies the underlying record as Scottish Record Office, Register of Acts and Decreets, vol. 22, fols. 102v–167v.</ref>

The uncommon name and the repeated Stevenston/Glencairn estate setting make it probable that the witness of 1549 and the tenant of 1559–60 were the same man. The records do not supply an occupation, patronymic, or exact property chain, so this identification remains an evidence-based inference.

=== Research Notes ===

The same teind-list page also names a Michael Glasgow with Finlay Brown and James Morton under Dubbs.<ref name="Teinds1559"/> Sanderson warns that one tenant could hold more than one property. The Brakpleuch and Dubbs entries may therefore describe one Michael Glasgow or two contemporaries.

No reviewed source identifies Michael's parents, spouse, children, precise birthplace, or death. [[Glasgow-1168|James Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-3908|Matthew Glasgow]], and the other Glasgow surname bearers in the 1559 Stevenston rental form a local record cluster, but co-tenancy and locality do not prove kinship. No parent has therefore been attached.

This Michael is not [[Glasgow-1406|Michael Glasgow]], whose estimated birth was more than a century later.
