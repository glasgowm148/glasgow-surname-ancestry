# Findings: [Michael Glasgow (Glasgow-3911), Brakpleuch](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3911)

## Conclusion

The 1559-60 Cunninghame teind list separately names **Michael Glasgow** at
Brakpleuch Over and Nether. This is distinct from Matthew-3908 at Ardeer Nether;
similar dates and consecutive profile creation do not justify a merge.

Michael may also be the same-name tenant at Dubbs on that page, but the source
does not establish whether one or two Michaels held those tenancies.

See the [graph-claim audit](../../surname-research/pre-1700/graph-claim-audit-2026-07-27.md).

## Birth-estimate audit - 21 August 2026

The live structured birth was corrected to **about 1540, uncertain**. The 1559-60 teind list identifies Michael as an adult tenant but supplies no birth year. The earlier `before` value remains only a strict chronological boundary, not the most useful estimated birth. No biography, relationship, source or other structured field was changed in this audit.
