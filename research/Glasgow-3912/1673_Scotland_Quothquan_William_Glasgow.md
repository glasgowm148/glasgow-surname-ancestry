== Biography ==

'''William Glasgow''' lived at Quothquan (written “Quodquen”) in the parish of Libberton, Lanarkshire. His testament was recorded in the Lanark Commissary Court on 9 April 1673, in register CC14/5/9.<ref name="LanarkIndex">Francis J. Grant, ed., ''The Commissariot Record of Lanark: Register of Testaments, 1595–1800'' (Edinburgh: Scottish Record Society, 1903), printed p. 38, entry “Glasgow, William, in Quodquen,” [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/the_commissariot_record_of_lanark__register_of_testaments__1595-1800_~_1903.pdf#page=50 digital copy].</ref> He had died by that date.

The published index does not state his age, occupation, spouse, children or parents. The original testament has not yet been reviewed and may contain additional relationships.
