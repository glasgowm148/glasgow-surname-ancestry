== Biography ==

'''William Glasgow''' lived at Quothquan (written “Quodquen”) in the parish of
Libberton, Lanarkshire. His testament dative was confirmed in the Lanark
Commissary Court on 9 April 1673, in register CC14/5/9; he had died by that
date.<ref name="LanarkIndex">Francis J. Grant, ed., ''The Commissariot Record
of Lanark: Register of Testaments, 1595–1800'' (Edinburgh: Scottish Record
Society, 1903), printed p. 38, entry “Glasgow, William, in Quodquen,”
[https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/the_commissariot_record_of_lanark__register_of_testaments__1595-1800_~_1903.pdf#page=50 digital copy].</ref>

The original testament has been inspected. William's entry occupies the lower
part of image 24 through the space above the next testament on image 26. It
records livestock, crops, household possessions and clothing. The heading says
that no debts were owed to him and the following account lists debts owed by
him. George Brown and John Neilson occur in the executor-dative and inventory
procedure, while William Neilson in Biggar is named as cautioner.<ref
name="Original">National Records of Scotland, Lanark Commissary Court,
testament dative of William Glasgow, CC14/5/9, images 24–26; original images
inspected 10 September 2026, accessed through the
[https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/search-records/legal-records/wills Scotland's
People wills collection].</ref>

Those named men appear in administrative roles. The inspected passages do not
securely call any of them William's relative. Nor has this selective reading
recovered a securely read spouse, parent, child or sibling clause for William.
That is not proof that he had no family: the opening locality and death-date
wording, along with parts of the appointment and accounts, remain obscured or
unresolved.<ref name="Original" />

Quothquan is retained from the printed index identification; the manuscript
locality has not been independently resolved in this review. The original does
not establish an identification with [[Glasgow-1521|William Glasgow]], the
William Glasgow connected with Cavers or Ulster, or [[Glasgow-591|William
Glasgow]]. No relationship should be added or profile merged on name and
chronology alone.

== Research Notes ==

William was probably born before 1655 if he was an adult at the 1673 court
proceeding; this is an estimate, not a recorded birth date. His death is
bounded before 9 April 1673. Quothquan was his indexed residence, not an
expressly stated birth or death place.

The source remains a testament dative and inventory, not a personal will. A
full reading of the opening, executor appointment, crop quantities, debts and
estate totals is still needed. The current original-image working transcription
is maintained separately and is not a complete diplomatic edition.<ref
