## Superseded conclusion — before merge

William Glasgow of Quothquan (indexed as Quodquen), parish of Libberton,
Lanarkshire, died before the Lanark Commissary Court confirmation of 9 April
1673. The original NRS testament dative, CC14/5/9 images 24–26, has now been
reviewed. It establishes an estate containing livestock, crops, household goods
and clothing, with no debts owed to the deceased recorded and debts owed by him
listed. It does not securely identify a spouse, parent, child or sibling.

This is an incomplete selective reading, not proof that William had no family.
The opening locality and death-date wording remain partly obscured, so Quothquan
is retained from the printed index and the death boundary remains before 9 April
1673. The review supplies no identity bridge to William Glasgow (Glasgow-1521),
the Cavers/Ulster William, or Glasgow-591; do not merge or attach any of them.

## Recommended correction — the first executor in the 1673 testament is Alexander Brown, 10 September 2026

A fresh reread of William Glasgow's testament dative from the original images (National Records of Scotland, Lanark Commissary Court, CC14/5/9, images 24–26; 9 April 1673), made on 10 September 2026, corrects the first executor's name. The original reads `Alexr Broun` repeatedly on images 25 and 26, so he is **Alexander Brown**, not George Brown as stated in the source table and identity assessment above. Alexander Brown and John Neilson (`Jon Neilsone`) were co-executors dative and both appear personally in the proceeding; William Neilson in Biggar was cautioner. None of these roles states kinship to William Glasgow or to one another. The reread keeps Quothquan as an index-based identification, since the manuscript locality is not legible.

The maintained reading is now `research/transcriptions/originals-2026-09-10-reread/william-1673.json`, which supersedes the 9 September bundle file cited above. This profile has since been merged into [Glasgow-1521](../Glasgow-1521/findings.md), whose findings do not name the executors.
