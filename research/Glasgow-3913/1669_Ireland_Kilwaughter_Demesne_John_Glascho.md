== Biography ==

'''John Glasgow''', recorded as ''Jo Glascho'', was a taxed householder at
Kilwaughter Demesne in Kilwaughter parish, upper Glenarm barony, County Antrim,
in the 1669 Hearth Money Roll.<ref name="Antrim1669">Public Record Office of
Northern Ireland, 1669 County Antrim Hearth Money Roll, T/307/A; W. Macafee,
[https://www.billmacafee.com/1660shearthmoneyrolls/1669hearthmoneyrollsantrim.pdf
surname transcription], record 5347, PDF p. 36 (accessed 17 July 2026).</ref>

James ''Glascho'' appears in the immediately adjacent entry at the same
demesne.<ref name="Antrim1669" /> Their adjacency makes a close household or
kinship connection plausible, but the tax roll does not state a relationship.
