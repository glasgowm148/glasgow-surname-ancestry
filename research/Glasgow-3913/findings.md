# Findings: John Glasgow (Glasgow-3913), Kilwaughter

Last updated: 2026-08-19

## Current conclusion

John `Glascho` is directly recorded beside James `Glascho` at Kilwaughter Demesne
in 1669. The association is repeated about thirty years later: John represented
Kilwaughter while James was appointed its overseer in the same congregational
record. Two independent record groups therefore make close kinship probable,
but neither gives ages or a kin term. Brother, father-son and cousin remain open.

He is probably the John Glasgow called **senior** while representing Kilwaughter
in congregational business in 1699-1701. Repeated place, name and associates support
continuity, but no record explicitly links the appearances. `Senior` establishes a
younger namesake, not a father-son relationship.

The best candidate for that younger namesake is the unlinked John Glasgow recorded
at Killroot/Kilroot in a 1733 probate index. John senior would have been at least
about 85 in 1733 under a conservative estimate, so a younger man fits better. John
`Glasgow-3959` of Ballyboggy, documented in 1740, is a second candidate. Neither
man should be attached until the 1733 event type and 1740 lease lives are known.

Robert Glaskow of Larne (`Glasgow-3970`) is only a possible older relative. The
Scottish Robert `Glasgow-1078` remains separate absent a cross-channel identity record.
