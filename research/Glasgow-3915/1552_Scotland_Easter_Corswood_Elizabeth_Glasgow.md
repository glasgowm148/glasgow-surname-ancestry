# Profile draft: Elizabeth Glasgow, widow of Alexander Kaine

== Biography ==

Elizabeth Glasgow was the widow of [[Kaine-91|Alexander Kaine]] by 19 December 1552. On that date a Great Seal confirmation described the lands of Easter Corswood in Edinburghshire as occupied by Elizabeth Glasgow, relict of Alexander Kaine.<ref name="RMS739">John Maitland Thomson, ed., ''Registrum Magni Sigilli Regum Scotorum'', vol. 4, A.D. 1546-1580 (Edinburgh, 1886), no. 739, [https://archive.org/details/registrummagnisi04scot/page/164/mode/1up p. 164], 19 December 1552: ''per Elesabeth Glasgow relictam Alesandri Kaine occupatas''.</ref>

Alexander is confidently identified with the Alexander Kano named immediately after [[Glasgow-1098|William Glasgow]] in a royal remission to Sir James Sandilands of Calder's group on 2 July 1526.<ref name="Remission1526">National Records of Scotland, Papers of Lord Torphichen, [https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/browseDetails.aspx?reference=GD119%2F193 GD119/193], remission, 2 July 1526. The official catalogue reads ''Alexander Kano''; ''Registrum Secreti Sigilli'', vol. 1, no. 3412, p. 516, prints ''Alexandri Kame''.</ref> James V dined with Sandilands at Calder that day, placing the remission in an immediate estate-household setting.<ref name="Treasurer1526">Thomas Dickson and James Balfour Paul, eds., [https://electricscotland.com/history/records/accountslordhig05offigoog.pdf ''Accounts of the Lord High Treasurer of Scotland'', vol. 5, A.D. 1515–1531], preface p. xxvii, citing account p. 275.</ref> The identification rests on the uncommon name and on Elizabeth's later occupation of Easter Corswood within the same Sandilands barony of Calder.<ref name="Corswood1625">National Records of Scotland, Papers of Lord Torphichen, [https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/browseTreeview.aspx?reference=GD119 GD119/299], 1 July 1625, describing Corswoodburne alias Easter Corswood in the barony of Calder.</ref>

=== Research Notes ===

=== Possible relationship to William Glasgow ===

William is presently attached as Elizabeth's father, but the relationship must remain uncertain. The 1526 association between William and her husband, followed by Elizabeth's 1552 occupation in their patron's Calder estate, strongly suggests close kinship. It does not distinguish daughter from sister, niece or another female relative. No source names Elizabeth's parent.

Elizabeth's birth is estimated about 1525 from her marriage and widowhood; no record gives her age. The 1552 act proves that she was alive on 19 December 1552, so her structured death should be recorded as after that date, not on it. Easter Corswood is a residence, not a proved birth or death place. No reviewed record identifies a child.
