== Biography ==

Elizabeth Glasgow was the widow of Alexander Kaine by 19 December 1552. On that date a Great Seal confirmation described the lands of Easter Corswood, also called Corswood-burn, in Edinburghshire as occupied by Elizabeth Glasgow, relict of Alexander Kaine.<ref>John Maitland Thomson, ed., ''Registrum Magni Sigilli Regum Scotorum'', vol. 4, A.D. 1546-1580 (Edinburgh, 1886), no. 739, [https://archive.org/details/registrummagnisi04scot/page/164/mode/1up p. 164], 19 December 1552: "per Elesabeth Glasgow relictam Alesandri Kaine occupatas."</ref>
