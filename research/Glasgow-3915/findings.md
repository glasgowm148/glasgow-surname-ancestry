# Findings: [Elizabeth Glasgow (Glasgow-3915), widow of Alexander Kaine](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3915)

## Current conclusion

[William Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1098) is now the
strongest **possible father or close older kinsman**. The 1526 Sandilands of
Calder remission places William immediately beside Alexander Kano; the official
catalogue spelling and the later `Kaine` are plausible variants of the same
uncommon man. In 1552 Elizabeth Glasgow was Alexander Kaine's widow and occupied
Easter Corswood, itself a holding in the Sandilands barony of Calder. This
husband-to-proposed-father and widow-to-estate recurrence is strong evidence for
a close Glasgow-Kaine relationship, but it does not distinguish William's
daughter from sister, niece or another female kinswoman. If a structured father
link to William is retained, it must remain uncertain.

## Parentage assessment

The repeated Elizabeth/Bessie name in two branches descending from Ninian is now
secondary. The Sandilands-Kano evidence supplies a much earlier and more direct
network: Elizabeth's husband is probably the Alexander grouped immediately with
William Glasgow in 1526, and Elizabeth later remained on their patron's estate.

There are substantial cautions:

- The official 1526 catalogue reads `Kano`, the published register appears to
  read `Kame`, and the 1552 Great Seal abstract reads `Kaine`. The same-person
  identification is strong but not explicit across the records.
- Elizabeth was a common given name and could derive from either granddaughter's
  maternal family.
- William could have been Elizabeth's father, brother, uncle or other close
  kinsman; the evidence proves the social/estate connection, not the degree.
- No record calls Elizabeth Robert's daughter or Ninian's sister.

## Recommended next records

- Inspect NRS GD119/193 to settle `Kano/Kame/Kaine`, then search GD119/194-205
  and the underlying writ for Great Seal no. 739 for Alexander's death, the
  source of Elizabeth's Easter Corswood tenancy, or an express kinship term.
- Search Robert Glasgow's burgh property transactions and testamentary material
  for daughters, heirs or payments involving the Kaine surname.
- Do not attach Robert as Elizabeth's father from the naming pattern. William is
  the leading working father only if the edge stays uncertain and the equally
  plausible sister/niece alternatives are stated.
