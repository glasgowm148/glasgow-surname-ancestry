# Findings: [Elizabeth Glasgow (Glasgow-3915), widow of Alexander Kaine](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3915)

## Current conclusion

[Robert Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1096),
fisher and burgess of Glasgow, is the strongest presently indexed **possible
father**, but the relationship is not proved and should not be attached.

The hypothesis would make Elizabeth an older sister of
[Ninian Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3188).
The collateral naming pattern is worth investigating, but the current dates
only work if Robert's approximate 1520 birth is materially too late.

## Parentage assessment

The repeated Elizabeth/Bessie name in the descendant families of two sons of
Ninian supports testing whether an earlier Elizabeth was commemorated in the
family. If [Elizabeth](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3915) was Ninian's
sister, their proved father [Robert](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1096)
would also be her father.

There are substantial cautions:

- Elizabeth was at Easter Corswood in Edinburghshire in 1552; Robert and
  Ninian are documented in Glasgow. No record joins the two places or families.
- Elizabeth was a common given name and could derive from either granddaughter's
  maternal family.
- Elizabeth's `before 1530` birth and Robert's `about 1520` birth give a nominal
  gap of at most ten years. The relationship is biologically viable only if
  Robert's estimate is early by at least six years, and preferably more.
- No record calls Elizabeth Robert's daughter or Ninian's sister.

## Recommended next records

- Search the protocol books, Great Seal material and property records for
  Alexander Kaine, Elizabeth Glasgow and Easter Corswood for a marriage
  contract, sasine, kinship designation or tutor.
- Search Robert Glasgow's burgh property transactions and testamentary material
  for daughters, heirs or payments involving the Kaine surname.
- Do not attach Robert as Elizabeth's father solely from the naming pattern.

## Birth-estimate audit - 21 August 2026

The live structured birth was corrected to **about 1525, uncertain**. Elizabeth was already a widow in the 1552 record; the source supplies no birth year. The earlier `before` value remains only a strict chronological boundary, not the most useful estimated birth. No biography, relationship, source or other structured field was changed in this audit.
