== Biography ==

Isobella Glasgow was married to Archibald Herbisoune, a fisher. On 27 January 1562/3 the spouses sold a Glasgow tenement on the street leading from the South Port to the bridge over the Clyde. Isobella appeared before a bailie and judicially ratified the transaction.<ref>Robert Renwick, ed., ''Abstracts of Protocols of the Town Clerks of Glasgow'', vol. 3, 1561-1568 (Glasgow, 1897), protocol 719, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc06scogoog/page/n41/mode/2up p. 29], 27 January 1562-3.</ref>
