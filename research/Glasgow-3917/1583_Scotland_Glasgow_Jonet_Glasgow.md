# New-profile draft: Jonet Glasgow, wife of William Pady

Created as [[Glasgow-3917]] on 17 July 2026.

== Biography ==

Jonet Glasgow was married to William Pady. On 19 March 1582/3 the spouses acquired eight ells of land in Bridgegait, Glasgow.<ref>Robert Renwick, ed., ''Abstracts of Protocols of the Town Clerks of Glasgow'', vol. 8, 1581-1585 (Glasgow, 1899), protocol 2478, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsofproto08glas/page/78/mode/1up p. 78], 19 March 1582-3.</ref>

The same couple remained together on 20 June 1598, when William Pady and Jonet Glasgow received an equal pro indiviso feu portion in St Teneu Street.<ref>Robert Renwick, ed., ''Abstracts of Protocols of the Town Clerks of Glasgow'', vol. 11, 1597-1600 (Glasgow, 1900), protocol 3459, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc04renwgoog/page/74/mode/1up pp. 74-75], 20 June 1598.</ref>
