# Findings: [Jonet (Glasgow) Pady (Glasgow-3917)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3917)

## Current conclusion

Jonet Glasgow was the wife of William Pady by 19 March 1582/3 and was still his wife on 20 June 1598. William was admitted a Glasgow burgess and freeman on 17 July 1599 as the husband of a burgess's daughter. This is consistent with Jonet being the daughter of a Glasgow burgess, but the published abstract names neither the wife nor her father and therefore does not prove her parentage.

On 5 November 1600 William Pady conveyed property to himself and Margaret Playfair, his future spouse. Jonet's marriage to William had therefore ended between 20 June 1598 and 5 November 1600, whether by her death or otherwise. The record does not call William a widower, so Jonet's death should not be asserted.

The corrected reading of [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow of [Corsoun?]]]'s
1597/8 testament directly names a daughter Jonet. This profile is excluded as
the present match: her documented marriage and property life is in Glasgow
burgh, and no record connects her to Corsoun, Elizat Stevinsoun, Andrew's land
or the other children. [[Glasgow-3183|Jonet Glasgow who married Martin Ramsy at
Mid Calder in 1624]] is geographically closer but likewise lacks an identifying
bridge. A distinct creation draft now represents Andrew's daughter; later
direct evidence could justify a merge.

The currently attached [[Glasgow-3522|William Glasgow]] is also unproved as
Jonet's father. William Pady's 1599 admission as husband of a burgess's daughter
supports only that his wife was a burgess's daughter; the abstract names
neither the wife nor her father and cannot identify William Glasgow.
