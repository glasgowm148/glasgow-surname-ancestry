# New-profile draft: Robert Glasgow, occupier at Kilbride

Created as [[Glasgow-3919]] on 17 July 2026.

== Biography ==

Robert Glasgow occupied part of the church lands of the vicarage of Kilbride in Cunninghame, Ayrshire, by 9 February 1580. A charter enumerated the occupiers as Robert Andro, Robert Boyd, Thomas Young, "Robertum Glasgw," Robert Dunlop, Jonet Hunter, Robert Andro younger, John Wilson and John Young. The charter was confirmed under the Great Seal on 15 February 1581/82.<ref>John Maitland Thomson, ed., ''Registrum Magni Sigilli Regum Scotorum'', vol. 5, A.D. 1580-1593 (Edinburgh, 1888), no. 324, [https://archive.org/details/registrummagnisi05scot/page/104/mode/2up pp. 104-105], charter dated at Glasgow 9 February 1580 and confirmed 15 February 1581/82.</ref>

=== Research Notes ===

Suggested creation data:
* Birth: before 1580, Scotland, uncertain.
* Death: after 9 February 1580, place unknown, uncertain.
* Residence: parish of Kilbride, Cunninghame, Ayrshire.

The charter proves an occurrence, occupation of land and locality, but no family relationship. [[Glasgow-1096|Robert Glasgow]], fisher and burgess of Glasgow, was active through 1572/3 and is the only serious existing-profile identity candidate. The rural Kilbride description and later date justify a separate working profile, but a future record could show they were the same man; flag that possibility rather than merging by name alone.
