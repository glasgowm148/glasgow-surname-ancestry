# Findings: [Robert Glasgow of Kilbride (Glasgow-3919)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3919)

## Current conclusion

The leading identity is [Robert Glasgow, fisher and burgess of Glasgow
(Glasgow-1096)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1096). This is now more
than a same-name comparison but remains short of a merge-grade proof.

The city Robert was alive on 7 February 1575. Five years later `Robertum
Glasgw` occupied Kilbride church land in Cunninghame. Robert's 1554 city
co-witness David Mathy supplies a real route into the Glencairn network: the
Mathies later belonged to the same estate landscape in which the Stevenston
Glasgows occur. The Kilbride list also contains Dunlop, Wilson and Young
occupiers, surnames recurring in Robert's burgh/property circle. These details
make a move or additional rural holding plausible. A second Robert remains a
viable explanation because the 1580 charter gives no occupation, spouse or
former residence.

## Identity assessment

**Probable same man, moderate-to-strong circumstantial.** Keep the profiles
separate and interlinked until a charter, tenancy or later succession carries
an occupation, spouse, son or city designation into Kilbride.
