# New-profile draft: Patrick Glasco of Kilclone, County Meath

Created as [[Glasgow-3920]] on 17 July 2026.

- **Preferred name:** Patrick Glasco
- **LNAB:** Glasco
- **Born:** about 1694, uncertain, place unknown
- **Died:** May 1754, Kilclone, County Meath, Ireland
- **Children:** James, Richard and William Glasco

## Biography

Patrick Glasco died in May 1754 aged 60 and was commemorated on a Kilclone
memorial erected by his son James. The same inscription calls Richard and
William James's brothers, thereby identifying all three as Patrick's sons.

## Source

Association for the Preservation of the Memorials of the Dead in Ireland,
Kilclone inscription reported in 1892; [searchable modern transcription](https://meathhistoryhub.ie/memorials-of-the-dead-journal/),
accessed 17 July 2026. The published text gives no spouse or parents.
