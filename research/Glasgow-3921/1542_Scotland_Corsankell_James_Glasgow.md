# New-profile draft: James Glasgow younger of Corsankell

Created as [[Glasgow-3921]] on 18 July 2026.

== Biography ==

James Glasgow was born before 1542, probably in Scotland. The year is an estimate based on his being at least 18 years old when he appeared as a tenant in a record dated 1559–60.

In the Cunninghame teind lists of 1559–60, Katherine Campbell, James Glasgow younger, and James Glasgow elder were listed under Corsankell in the parish of Stevenston, Ayrshire.<ref>Margaret H. B. Sanderson, ''The People of Sixteenth Century Ayrshire'', ''Ayrshire Collections'', vol. 14, no. 8 (Ayrshire Archaeological and Natural History Society, 1987), appendix "The Cunninghame Teind Lists of 1559–60," [https://aanhs.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/people-of-ayrshire.pdf#page=36 p. 331], Corsankell entry. Sanderson identifies the underlying record as Scottish Record Office, Register of Acts and Decreets, vol. 22, fols. 102v–167v.</ref> The qualifiers younger and elder distinguish two contemporary men named James Glasgow but do not, by themselves, establish their relationship. A later record resolves it: on 9 February 1571 Corsankell was described as occupied by James Glasgow elder and by James and John Glasgow, his sons.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Charter Chest of the Earls of Glencairn, [https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/details.aspx?reference=GD39%2F5%2F59 GD39/5/59], letters of reversion concerning the 40-shilling land of Corsankell, 9 February 1571.</ref>

=== Research Notes ===

Suggested creation data:
* Birth: before 1542, Scotland, uncertain.
* Death: after 9 February 1571, place unknown, uncertain.
* Father: [[Glasgow-1168|James Glasgow elder]], confirmed.
* Mother: unknown.
* Brother: John Glasgow of Corsankell, confirmed.

This profile represents James Glasgow younger. [[Glasgow-1168|James Glasgow]] represents the man designated elder at Corsankell.

The former profile Glasgow-2429 now redirects to [[Glasgow-991|James Glasgow]], a Midlothian man estimated to have been born about 1585 from records of children born between 1605 and 1618. He could not have been the adult tenant designated younger in 1559–60. The Corsankell man therefore requires a separate profile.

A live WikiTree name-and-date search on 18 July 2026 found James elder but no
separate compatible profile for this younger James.

The 1571 record directly identifies James elder as the younger James's father.
It also identifies John Glasgow as another son of James elder, directly proving
that John and James younger were brothers.
No reviewed source identifies his mother, spouse, children, precise birthplace
or death. Corsankell is a residence and should not be entered as his birth or
death place.
