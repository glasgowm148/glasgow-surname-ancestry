# James Glasgow in Brekpleughe

== Biography ==

James Glasgow lived at Brekpleughe in the parish of Stevenston, Ayrshire, and was the husband of Margaret Smith. The Glasgow Commissary Court testament index records "Margaret, spouse to James Glasgow, in Brekpleughe, par. of Steinstoune" on 21 February 1632.<ref>Francis J. Grant, ed., ''Commissariot Record of Glasgow: Register of Testaments, 1547-1800'' (Edinburgh: Scottish Record Society, 1901), [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/page/460/mode/1up p. 460], Smith, Margaret, 21 February 1632; cross-reference under Glasgow, James, [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/page/195/mode/1up p. 195].</ref>

=== Research Notes ===

Profile implications:
* Birth: before about 1614, probably Scotland, uncertain; a pre-1600 birth is plausible but not proved.
* Death: after 21 February 1632, place unknown, uncertain.
* Residence: Brekpleughe, Stevenston parish, Ayrshire.
* Spouse: Margaret Smith.

The record establishes a later occurrence in the same place cluster as [[Glasgow-3911|Michael Glasgow]], documented at Brakpleuch Over and Nether in 1559-60. [[Glasgow-3921]] has been identified as the existing profile to receive this evidence. Because the 1632 calendar entry gives no parent, age or earlier residence, the equation with the younger Corsankell James should remain explicitly uncertain unless a holding or family record bridges the long interval.
