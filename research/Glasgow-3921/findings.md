- James Glasgow was born before 1542, probably in Scotland. The year is an estimate based on his being at least 18 years old when he appeared as a tenant in a record dated 1559–60.
- In the Cunninghame teind lists of 1559–60, Katherine Campbell, James Glasgow younger, and James Glasgow elder were listed under Corsankell in the parish of Stevenston, Ayrshire.[1] The qualifiers younger and elder distinguish two contemporary men named James Glasgow but do not, by themselves, establish their relationship. A later record resolves it: on 9 February 1571 Corsankell was described as occupied by James Glasgow elder and by James and John Glasgow, his sons.[2]
- James Glasgow lived at Brekpleughe in the parish of Stevenston, Ayrshire, and was the husband of Margaret Smith. The Glasgow Commissary Court testament index records "Margaret, spouse to James Glasgow, in Brekpleughe, par. of Steinstoune" on 21 February 1632.[3]
- Birth: before 1542, Scotland, uncertain. Death: after 9 February 1571, place unknown, uncertain. Father: James Glasgow elder.

He could not have been the adult tenant designated younger in 1559–60. The Corsankell man therefore requires a separate profile.
