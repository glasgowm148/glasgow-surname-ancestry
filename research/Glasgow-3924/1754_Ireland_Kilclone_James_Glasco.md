# James Glasco of Kilclone, County Meath

- **Preferred name:** James Glasco
- **LNAB:** Glasco
- **Born:** before about 1733, uncertain, place unknown
- **Died:** after May 1754, place unknown
- **Father:** Patrick Glasco
- **Brothers:** Richard and William Glasco

## Biography

James Glasco erected a memorial at Kilclone for his brothers Richard and
William, who died on 1 November 1738, and for Patrick Glasco, expressly called
James's father, who died in May 1754 aged 60. James was therefore alive after
his father's death; his estimated birth merely allows adulthood when the stone
was erected and is not stated by the inscription.
