# Richard Glasco of Kilclone, County Meath

- **Preferred name:** Richard Glasco
- **LNAB:** Glasco
- **Born:** about 1728, uncertain, place unknown
- **Died:** 1 November 1738, Kilclone, County Meath, Ireland
- **Father:** Patrick Glasco
- **Brothers:** James and William Glasco

## Biography

Richard Glasco died on 1 November 1738 aged 10. His brother James later
erected the Kilclone memorial, which also names their brother William and their
father Patrick.
