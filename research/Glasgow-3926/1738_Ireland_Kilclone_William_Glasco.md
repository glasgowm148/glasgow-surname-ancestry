# New-profile draft: William Glasco of Kilclone, County Meath

- **Preferred name:** William Glasco
- **LNAB:** Glasco
- **Born:** about 1731, uncertain, place unknown
- **Died:** 1 November 1738, Kilclone, County Meath, Ireland
- **Father:** Patrick Glasco
- **Brothers:** James and Richard Glasco

## Biography

William Glasco died on 1 November 1738. The 1892 transcription reads his age
as about seven but marks the numeral uncertain. His brother James erected the
Kilclone memorial, which also names their brother Richard and father Patrick.
