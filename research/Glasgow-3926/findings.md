## Source findings — Kilclone memorial inscription, transcribed 1892

The Kilclone memorial erected by James Glasco names his brothers Richard and William, who died on 1 November 1738, and their father Patrick (died May 1754 aged 60). William's age is printed as about seven with a query mark, so his calculated birth about 1731 is uncertain. Source: Association for the Preservation of the Memorials of the Dead in Ireland, Kilclone inscription reported in 1892 ([Meath History Hub transcription](https://meathhistoryhub.ie/memorials-of-the-dead-journal/)).
