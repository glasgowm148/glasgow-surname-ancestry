== Biography ==

'''Maria Glasgow''' was born on 22 September 1800, the daughter of
[[Glasgow-822|John Glasgow]] and [[Arthur-2480|Mary Arthur]]. She appears as the
second daughter in the family's Bible register.<ref name="Bible">Glasgow family
Bible, family-register page, image published by the Hiltner/McKeown family
database, [https://hiltner.com/mckeown/histories/8479.jpeg original page image]
and [https://hiltner.com/mckeown/showmedia.php?mediaID=532 Glasgow Bible media
page], accessed 8 September 2026. The volume was printed in 1797, but later
entries show that the register was continued by more than one hand.</ref>

Maria was probably the Maria Glasgow who married [[Wallace-21165|Alexander
Wallace]], a wheelwright and undertaker. The Wallace family lived at Rasharkin,
County Antrim, where daughters [[Wallace-19547|Mary]] and
[[Wallace-20650|Lavinia]] were born in 1840 and 1842.<ref name="WallaceFamily">Hiltner/McKeown
family database, [https://hiltner.com/mckeown/getperson.php?personID=I370&tree=tree1&sitever=standard
“Maria Glasgow”] and [https://hiltner.com/mckeown/familygroup.php?familyID=F782&sitever=standard&tree=tree1
“Alexander Wallace / Maria Glasgow” family group], accessed 8 September 2026.
The database identifies one person, I370, both as John and Mary Arthur's
daughter and as Alexander's wife. It cites Robert Glasgow's 2018 descendant
report and the family Bible, but the report itself is not publicly attached.</ref>
Published biographies of Mary and Lavinia also name Alexander Wallace and
Maria Glasgow as their parents and place the daughters' births at
Rasharkin.<ref name="MaryNZ">New Zealand Ministry for Culture and Heritage,
[https://nzhistory.govt.nz/suffragist/mary-wallace-rainey “Mary Wallace
Rainey”], accessed 8 September 2026.</ref><ref name="LaviniaNZ">New Zealand
Ministry for Culture and Heritage,
[https://nzhistory.govt.nz/suffragist/lavinia-rapley “Lavinia Rapley”],
accessed 8 September 2026.</ref>

A descendant account gives Maria's death as 5 October 1856, but no original
death record or death place has yet been recovered.<ref name="WallaceFamily" />

== Research Notes ==

=== Wallace identity ===

The identification of John and Mary's daughter with Alexander Wallace's wife
is probable rather than directly proved. The Bible establishes Maria's birth
and natal family but does not mention a Wallace marriage. The public descendant
database explicitly treats them as one woman. Its conclusion is supported by
compatible chronology and locality: Maria was aged 39 and 41 at the known
daughters' births, and the Wallace family was in the same Bann Valley setting
as Maria's Inishrush family.

The daughters' names add supporting context. Mary repeated Maria's mother's
name, while Lavinia repeated the uncommon name of Maria's sister. Naming alone
does not prove identity, but the two-name pattern is more informative than a
shared common surname. A live WikiTree exact-name and date audit on 8 September
2026 found no third compatible Irish Maria Glasgow.

[[Glasgow-2137]] represented Alexander's wife separately with an estimated 1822
birth. That estimate had no cited birth source and appears to have been inferred
from Mary's 1840 birth. It is not evidence for a second woman. The profiles are
therefore best merged into this Bible-supported profile while this evidential
qualification remains visible.

An original marriage, baptism, death, probate or church record naming Maria's
parents would upgrade the Wallace identification to direct documentary proof.
Until then, the reported 5 October 1856 death and Ireland birthplace should be
marked uncertain; no death place should be inferred.
