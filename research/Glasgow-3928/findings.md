# Findings: [Maria Glasgow (Glasgow-3928)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3928)

Last updated: 2026-09-06

## Current conclusion

The family Bible directly places Maria, born 22 September 1800, in the family
of [John Glasgow (Glasgow-822)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-822) and
Mary Arthur. A separate existing profile, [Maria (Glasgow) Wallace
(Glasgow-2137)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2137), represents the wife
of Alexander Wallace and currently carries an estimated 1822 birth.

The Hiltner descendant tree uses one Maria, 22 September 1800–5 October 1856,
for both families. The match is plausible because Alexander and Maria Wallace's
daughters Mary and Lavinia were born at Rasharkin in 1840 and 1842, but no
original record inspected in the supplied audit names Maria Wallace's parents.
Keep the profiles separate and place a merge/identity review on **HOLD**.

## Identity assessment

**Possible duplicate/identity:** Glasgow-3928 = Glasgow-2137.

The decisive evidence would be an original marriage, death, church, probate or
child record identifying Maria Wallace as John Glasgow and Mary Arthur's
daughter, or independently supplying the 22 September 1800 birth. Do not merge,
change the 1822 estimate, or attach the Wallace family to this profile before
that bridge is found.
