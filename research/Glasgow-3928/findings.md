# Findings: [Maria Glasgow (Glasgow-3928)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3928)

Last updated: 2026-09-08

## Current conclusion

The family Bible directly places Maria, born 22 September 1800, in the family
of [John Glasgow (Glasgow-822)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-822) and
Mary Arthur. A separate existing profile, [Maria (Glasgow) Wallace
(Glasgow-2137)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2137), represents the wife
of Alexander Wallace and currently carries an estimated 1822 birth.

The Hiltner descendant database uses one Maria, 22 September 1800–5 October
1856, for both families. The identification is not based on a merely similar
name: it assigns one database person (`I370`) both to John and Mary Arthur's
family and to Alexander Wallace, and cites Robert Glasgow's 2018 descendant
report and the Glasgow family Bible. The public source page does not expose the
2018 report, and the Bible itself records Maria's birth but not her Wallace
marriage.

The cumulative identity case is nevertheless now sufficient for a **probable
merge**. The unsupported 1822 estimate on [[Glasgow-2137]] is not conflicting
evidence; Maria born in 1800 was 39–41 at the known daughters' births. The
Wallace household was at Rasharkin in the same Bann Valley setting as Maria's
documented Inishrush family. The daughters were Mary and Lavinia: Mary was the
name of Maria's mother, and Lavinia was the uncommon name of Maria's sister.
The live WikiTree exact-name audit found no third Maria Glasgow of compatible
date and Irish setting. Merge [[Glasgow-2137]] into [[Glasgow-3928]], retain the
Bible-backed birth and parents, and describe the Wallace identification as
probable rather than as direct original-record proof.

## Identity assessment

**Probable duplicate/identity:** Glasgow-3928 = Glasgow-2137.

Proceed with a merge into [[Glasgow-3928]], whose Bible-supported identity is
better evidenced. Retain Ireland as uncertain, add [[Wallace-21165|Alexander
Wallace]] as the probable husband and preserve [[Wallace-20650|Lavinia]] as a
daughter. [[Wallace-19547|Mary Wallace Rainey]] is the other named daughter, but
her own original baptism or a record directly naming her parents should be
checked before a new structured attachment is made. Use the complete merged
profile draft in `Glasgow-3928.md`.

Glasgow-3928 is managed by [[Glasgow-933]], but Glasgow-2137 is managed by
[[McMeekan-55]]. The evidence supports submitting the merge; final completion
may require the other manager's approval under WikiTree's merge workflow.

An original marriage, death, church, probate or child record naming Maria's
parents would upgrade this from probable identity to direct documentary proof.
The reported death date, 5 October 1856, remains derivative and its place is
unknown.
