# Hugh Glasgow, first Tamlaght O'Crilly householder, 1740

== Biography ==

'''Hugh Glasgow''' was one of two distinct men of that name returned as
Protestant householders in Tamlaght O'Crilly parish, County Londonderry, in
1740. In the surviving collector-order transcription this Hugh is entry 1195,
between Tole O'Cain and Patrick O'Quin. The second Hugh is entry 1272, between
Henry Lennox and Hugh Ellis and immediately followed by James McCullough.<ref>
PRONI T808/15258, ''Names of 14,400 Protestant householders'', Tamlaght O'Crilly,
1740, pp. 108-109; Richard John Torrens,
[https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/books/Kilrea/Kilrea07.html
collector-order transcription], entries 1195 and 1272 (accessed 21 July 2026);
W. Macafee,
[https://www.billmacafee.com/1740returns/1740religiousreturnsulstername.pdf
surname transcription], both entries recorded with townland "Not specified".
</ref>

The return proves only that this was a separate adult householder in the parish.
It gives no age, occupation, townland, wife, children or relationship to the
other Hugh.

=== Research Notes ===

Suggested creation data:

* Birth: before about 1722, uncertain; place unknown.
* Residence: Tamlaght O'Crilly parish, County Londonderry, Ireland, 1740;
  townland unknown.
* Death: after 1740, uncertain; place unknown.
* Parents, spouse and children: unknown.

The other 1740 Hugh, entry 1272, is the stronger occurrence candidate for
[[Glasgow-548|Hugh Glasgow]] because a late family account says Hugh worked for
and married into a McCullough family after moving to County Londonderry, while
entry 1272 immediately precedes James McCullough. That remains an identity
hypothesis, not proof.<ref>William Melanchthon Glasgow,
[https://www.wikitree.com/photo.php/0/0a/Glasgow-590.pdf ''History of the Glasgow
Family''], pp. 2-3. The narrative was written about 160 years after the reported
1733 marriage and contains a demonstrated error in the younger Hugh's death
date.</ref>

This first Hugh is a possible earlier member of the same parish surname cluster
as [[Glasgow-3902|Adam Glasgow]] and [[Glasgow-822|John Glasgow]] of Inishrush.
Do not attach [[Glasgow-3905|the former unknown-father placeholder]] as his son:
the placeholder is not a documented person, and the 1740 return names no child.
An older Adam-and-Maria parent couple remains a naming hypothesis only.

Do not merge this man with [[Glasgow-548|Hugh Glasgow]] or
[[Glasgow-1503|Hugh Glasgow of the east-Antrim cluster]]. The two Tamlaght
entries prove that at least two separate Hugh households existed, and the Antrim
profile belongs to a different documentary cluster.
