### Record-bearing passages to audit

- Hugh Glasgow was one of two distinct men of that name returned as Protestant householders in Tamlaght O'Crilly parish, County Londonderry, in 1740. In the surviving collector-order transcription this Hugh is entry 1195, between Tole O'Cain and Patrick O'Quin. The second Hugh is entry 1272, between Henry Lennox and Hugh Ellis and immediately followed by James McCullough.[1]
- The return proves only that this was a separate adult householder in the parish. It gives no age, occupation, townland, wife, children or relationship to the other Hugh.
- Birth: before about 1722, uncertain; place unknown. Residence: Tamlaght O'Crilly parish, County Londonderry, Ireland, 1740;
- Death: after 1740, uncertain; place unknown. Parents, spouse and children: unknown.
- The other 1740 Hugh, entry 1272, is the stronger occurrence candidate for Hugh Glasgow because a late family account says Hugh worked for and married into a McCullough family after moving to County Londonderry, while entry 1272 immediately precedes James McCullough. That remains an identity hypothesis, not proof.[2]
