== Biography ==

'''Philip Glasco junior''' was a watchmaker of Christchurch Yard, Dublin.

A death column published on 28 September 1765 reports Mrs. Glasco, wife of Mr. Philip Glasco, junior, watchmaker, in Christ-church-lane. The designation, trade and address make this a probable earlier marriage of the younger Philip; the wife’s given and maiden names are unrecorded.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1050029120/ ''The Public Register, or, Freeman’s Journal'', 28 September 1765, p.3], original Deaths paragraph inspected 4 October 2026. It does not supply the exact death day.</ref>

He married Teresa Plunkett of Church Street on 17 September 1768.<ref name="Marriage 1768">[https://eddiesextracts.com/extracts 1700/pg 1758_74.html ''Public Gazetteer'' extraction], marriage of Philip Glasco junior and Teresa Plunkett, 17 September 1768.</ref>

A marriage column published on 20 September 1768 independently names Mr. Phil. Glasco the younger, watchmaker of Christ-church-lane, and Miss Teresa Plunkett of Church-street.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1050148115/ ''The Public Register, or, Freeman’s Journal'', 20 September 1768, p.3], original inspected 4 October 2026. This report corroborates the marriage but does not itself give the exact ceremony date.</ref>

Philip died at Christ Church Lane shortly before his death was reported on 22 December 1770.<ref name="Death 1770">[https://eddiesextracts.com/extracts 1700/dm 1723_73.html ''Dublin Mercury'' extraction], death notice for Philip Glasco, 22 December 1770.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The “junior” designation, shared rare surname, trade and address make him a probable son of the older Philip, but no inspected record explicitly states the relationship. He is not [[Glasgow-1241]], the profile being assessed as a possible identity for Philip senior.

If the 1765 identity is correct, the unnamed wife died before the 1768 Teresa Plunkett marriage. She must not be called Teresa on this evidence.

This profile represents the record-defined younger watchmaker. Duplicate reconstructions are not treated as separate people.
