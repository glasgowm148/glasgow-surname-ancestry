# Philip Glasco the younger — 1765 and 1768 original notices

## Current conclusion

An original death column of 28 September 1765 names the unnamed wife of Mr. Philip Glasco, junior, watchmaker, in Christ-church-lane. The junior designation, trade and address make Glasgow-3932 a probable match. If this is the same man, it indicates an earlier wife who died before his 1768 marriage; her given and maiden names are not printed. Do not identify her as Teresa Plunkett.

The original Freeman’s Journal marriage column published on 20 September 1768 names **Mr. Phil. Glasco the younger**, watchmaker of Christ-church-lane, and **Miss Teresa Plunkett of Church-street**. This agrees with the existing Philip junior evidence draft and is compatible with Glasgow-3932. The original is now preserved as a checked inline excerpt in [Sources](/sources/newspapers/#newspaper-1050148115).

## Identity and relationship assessments

The 1765 notice supplies a probable earlier wife and compatible identity evidence, not a proved link to any named spouse.

The rare name, younger designation, watchmaking trade, Dublin address and Teresa Plunkett support the same documentary person across the marriage sources. Glasgow-3932 is the compatible existing profile. The younger man is distinct from the elder Philip discussed under [Glasgow-1241](/people/glasgow-1241.html), and must not acquire the elder’s Scottish baptism or 1772 death.

The uncertain father connection to Glasgow-1241 remains uncertain. The original marriage notice does not name his parents. “Younger” and shared trade/address support the existing generation hypothesis without proving the elder’s Scottish identity or a father attachment. Neither Philip should be substituted into the surname-only 1779–1780 watchmaker notices: the younger man’s separately reported 1770 death is earlier than those adverts.
