# Findings: James Glasgow (Glasgow-3939)

Last updated: 2026-09-12

## Current conclusion

James is the child born 17 January 1837 and baptised 9 February 1837 in
Rasharkin Presbyterian entry 75, son of Alex and Mary McAughin at Gortereagh.
The exact birth date identifies the early James profile. He is not the James
born in October 1843, whose baptism is a separate entry.

Both parent–child relationships are documented in the maintained family
reconstruction: the names, maternal surname variants and 1837, 1838 and 1844
baptism sequence identify the couple as Alexander Glasgow and Mary/Molly
McCaughan. Earlier unassessed website labels reflected unmapped tree flags,
not absent baptism evidence. The transcription is derivative; this review
has not newly inspected an original register image.

No death or burial record has been located. The retained before-1841 boundary
is inferred from the 1851 household and its deaths-since-1841 information;
reuse of the name in 1843 supports an early death but not its exact date.
