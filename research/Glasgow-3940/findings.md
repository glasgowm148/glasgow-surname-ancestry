# Findings: [Unknown Glasgow (Glasgow-3940)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3940)

Last updated: 2026-10-06

## Current conclusion

This profile already represents the unnamed `Glascow` baptised in 1838 to
Alex and Molly McAughern of Gortereagh. The baptism is not a new finding and no
forename is supplied by the transcription. The profile's `before 1841` death
is an inference, not a recorded death date, and the 6 October 2026 audit below
withdraws its main support.

The named parents are documented relationships in the maintained family
reconstruction. The 12 September editorial review corrects the website's
unassessed tree flags for both parents using entry 106 and the existing
1837–1844 baptism sequence; it does not supply a forename or turn the inferred
death boundary into a recorded event.

The assessment of 12 September was **probably died before the 1841 census date**,
with an explicit uncertainty marker; the 6 October 2026 audit below supersedes it. The child was not listed anywhere in the
family's surviving 1851 return: not among the residents, not among absent
family, and not among deaths since 1841. An unrecorded move, permanent removal
from the household, or enumerator omission cannot be excluded absolutely.

## Rejected identity

Samuel Glasgow of Franklin/Blissfield, Michigan, is **not a credible match on
current evidence**. The theory arose from approximate age, Irish birth and his
presence in the same Michigan county as the possible later James. Those are
weak cluster clues. They do not overcome the 1851 family return, the absence of
any American parent or County Antrim statement, or the impossible cemetery
birth transcription `29 February 1838`.

Do not rename `Glasgow-3940` as Samuel, attach the Michigan wife, or add a
migration claim. The Michigan records are retained under Alexander's sources
as a rejected-candidate audit.

## Current conclusion — source audit, 6 October 2026

The child's absence from the 1851 return no longer supports an early death. The same return omits Alexander's daughter Mary (Glasgow-3167), who was certainly living: she married in 1871 and appears in the 1901 and 1911 censuses. A [search of the surviving 1851 returns](https://nationalarchives.ie/collections/search-the-census/search-results/#census_year=1851&surname__icontains=Glasgow&limit=100) on 6 October 2026 found no child Mary Glasgow of suitable age elsewhere in Rasharkin parish, and she is not among house 35's absent members or deaths. The return was therefore not a complete list of the couple's living children.

**"Probably died before 1841" is withdrawn. The child's fate is unknown.**

## Identity assessment — is the 1838 child Mary?

Mary's reported ages (28 at marriage in 1871, 60 in 1901, 72 in 1911) place her birth between about 1838 and 1843. The baptism sequence leaves little room after 1840: Robert was born about 1840–41 and the second James on 9 October 1843. The 1838 entry is the open place in the family, and Mary is its leading candidate. This is not proved. The published transcription gives no forename or sex. Do not merge Glasgow-3940 with Glasgow-3167 until the original register entry (PRONI MIC1P/292, entry 106) has been read.
