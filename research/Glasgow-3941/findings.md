documented at Coltrim from 1820 through 1831. Nothing presently identifies his
parents, wife or children, or proves that he was the Glenleslie Robert
`Glasgow-1367`, the 1827 Dunnabraggy occupier or the 1834 Lisnagaver occupier.

### Record-bearing passages to audit

- Robert Glasgow was an adult land occupier at Coltrim in the civil parish of Lissan by November 1820, when the Drapers' Company granted him a lease of a holding there.[1]
- No surviving record inspected so far identifies Robert's wife, children, parents, precise age or death. He may have been the Robert later recorded at Glenleslie in 1851 (Glasgow-1367), but no document directly connects the two locations. They should not be merged without additional evidence. A different Robert Glasgow held 35 acres at Lisnagaver in the 1834 Rasharkin tithe book. Nothing presently identifies that man with either the Coltrim occupier or the Glenleslie farmer.
