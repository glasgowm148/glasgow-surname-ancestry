== Biography ==

'''Katherin Glasgow''' was buried in the Church of Ireland parish of St
Audoen, Dublin, on 3 November 1713. Her age, address, occupation and relatives
are not supplied in the cited entry.<ref name="DublinBurial">IrishGenealogy.ie,
Church of Ireland burial register, St Audoen, Dublin, p. 96, Katherin Glasgow,
3 November 1713, record identifier DU-CI-BU-25141,
[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/church/d-116-1-1-098.pdf register image].</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Possible Saltcoats identity ===

A testament dative for John Glasgow, sailor in Saltcoats within Ardrossan
parish, names '''Katherine and Agnes Glasgow''' as his lawful children. John
died in December 1636, and the estate proceedings followed in April 1637. The
record also names Isobell Gray as John's widow but does not expressly call her
the girls' mother.<ref name="Saltcoats1637">National Records of Scotland,
Glasgow Commissary Court, CC9/7/27, register pp. 478–480, testament dative and
inventory of John Glasgow, sailor in Saltcoats; original register working
transcription. Locate by reference through the
[https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/search-records/legal-records/wills
Scotland's People wills search].</ref>

The Dublin woman was compared with John's daughter Katherine because her birth
and parents are unknown and a person alive in 1637 could have been buried in
1713. No inspected record carries the
Saltcoats residence, sailor household, Isobell Gray, Agnes Glasgow or a
Scotland-to-Dublin migration into the St Audoen burial. The two documentary
identities are not presently matched; name and theoretical longevity are not
enough. A separate Saltcoats profile is appropriate, with a later merge only if
positive evidence bridges the records.

The Dublin profile should not be assigned John as father or an estimated
Saltcoats birth without a bridging record.

The Dublin date is a burial date. It should not be described more precisely as
the date of death unless the register wording establishes that distinction.
