# Findings: [Katherine Glasgow (Glasgow-3942)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3942)

## Current conclusion

Glasgow-3942 represents Katherin Glasgow, buried in the Church of Ireland
parish of St Audoen, Dublin, on 3 November 1713. The source supplies no age,
parents, spouse, address or occupation.

She was compared with Katherine Glasgow, lawful child of John Glasgow, sailor
in Saltcoats, named in his 1637 testament dative. No record bridges the Scottish
child to Dublin. A same-name burial 76 years later, without age, parents,
spouse, address or origin, is insufficient for identity. Glasgow-3942 is
excluded as the present match; a distinct Saltcoats creation draft is ready.
Do not attach Saltcoats parents or a Saltcoats birth place to Glasgow-3942
unless later positive evidence supports a merge.
