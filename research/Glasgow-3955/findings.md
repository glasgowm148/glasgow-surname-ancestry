# Findings: Margaret Glasgow (Glasgow-3955)

Last updated: 2026-08-25

## Current conclusion

Margaret Glasgow, born about 1801 in County Antrim, was the unmarried daughter
of Robert Glasgow `Glasgow-1367`. The 1851 Glenleslie census gives her age and
relationship. Robert's will directly calls her “my daughter Margaret Glasgow”
and names James Young and Mary Young as her son and daughter.

The father of the Young children is not named, and the will does not state that
Margaret married. Her current surname is therefore Glasgow. Catherine was
Robert's wife in 1851 but is only an uncertain mother of Margaret because the
return does not separately state maternity and the will does not name her.

this Margaret as unmarried in Ireland.

Mary is now `Young-78755`. `Young-23682`, born about 1837 in Ireland, is a
possible later identity for James, but no source connects that profile to
Glenleslie. Do not create or attach another James until the candidate is
resolved.
