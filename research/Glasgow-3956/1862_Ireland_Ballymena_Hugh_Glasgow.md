'''Hugh Glasgow''' was a weaver and the father of
[[Glasgow-3957|Mary Glasgow]]. His wife, parents, residence and dates have not
been identified.

Mary's civil marriage register names her father as '''Hugh Glasgow, weaver'''.
She married Thomas Montgomery in the Ballymena registration district on 13
September 1862.<ref name="MaryMarriage1862">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1862/09623/5489617.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Ballymena district, marriage of Thomas Montgomery and [[Glasgow-3957|Mary Glasgow]], 13 September 1862; bride's father Hugh Glasgow, weaver.</ref>

Mary's death registration gives an age of seventy-nine in March 1898, implying
birth about 1818 or 1819.<ref name="MaryDeath1898">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/deaths_returns/deaths_1898/05842/4654852.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil death register], Mary Montgomery, 22 March 1898, Crankill, aged 79.</ref> Hugh was therefore probably born before about 1799, but this is only a cautious working estimate.

== Research Notes ==

=== Possible brother of Robert Glasgow ===

The 1851 census records an unmarried Mary Glasgow, aged thirty-four, as the
niece of [[Glasgow-1367|Robert Glasgow]] at Glenleslie.<ref>[https://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Dunaghy/Glenleslie/23/ 1851 Census of Ireland], Glenleslie household 23.</ref>

The census woman is a strong match to [[Glasgow-3957|Mary (Glasgow)
Montgomery]] in name, age, County Antrim birth, occupation and district. If they
are the same woman, Hugh was probably Robert's brother. The identity and sibling
relationship remain uncertain until a second record links Hugh or Mary to
Glenleslie.

No compatible WikiTree profile was found in the local One Tree export on 10
August 2026. The existing Irish Hugh profiles are either much earlier men or
belong to documented emigrant families.
