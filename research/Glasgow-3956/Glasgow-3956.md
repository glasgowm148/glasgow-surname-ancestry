# WikiTree correction: [[Glasgow-3956|Joseph Glasgow]]

The 1862 marriage used for this profile names [[Glasgow-3957|Mary Glasgow]]'s
father as **Hugh Glasgow, weaver**, not Joseph Glasgow.<ref>[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1862/09623/5489617.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Ballymena district, Thomas Montgomery and Mary Glasgow, 13 September 1862.</ref>

Remove Mary as Joseph's daughter and [[Glasgow-1367|Robert Glasgow]] as Joseph's
father. If no separate source proves that Joseph existed, this profile has no
remaining evidentiary basis and should not be retained as a family member.
