== Biography ==
'''Hugh Glasgow''' was a weaver in County Antrim. His birth date is unknown; he was born before about 1802 based on the known ages of his children.

Three original civil marriage registers name children of a father called Hugh Glasgow, weaver:

* [[Glasgow-3957|Mary Glasgow]], who married [[Montgomery-14069|Thomas Montgomery]] on 13 September 1862, expressly named her father as Hugh Glasgow, weaver.<ref name="MaryMarriage1862">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1862/09623/5489617.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Thomas Montgomery and Mary Glasgow, 13 September 1862.</ref> This parent-child relationship is direct and confident.
* [[Glasgow-4048|Hugh Glasgow]], aged 23 and a weaver of Liminary, married Ann Wilson on 24 December 1852 and named his father as Hugh Glasgow, weaver.<ref name="HughMarriage1852">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1852/09422/5413278.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Hugh Glasgow and Ann Wilson, 24 December 1852.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-4047|Eliza Glasgow]], aged 21 and of Liminary, married William Montgomery on 21 August 1854 and named her father as Hugh Glasgow, labourer and weaver.<ref name="ElizaMarriage1854">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1854/09463/5428881.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], William Montgomery and Eliza Glasgow, 21 August 1854.</ref>

The matching father name and occupation prove each child's relationship to a Hugh Glasgow. Hugh junior and Eliza also shared the Liminary residence, strongly indicating that they were siblings. Mary may have been their older sister, but her register does not supply a definite named residence (apparently “unknown”) and the common name does not by itself prove that all three records identify one father.

=== Relationship to Robert Glasgow ===

Hugh is currently attached as an uncertain son of [[Glasgow-1367|Robert Glasgow]]. That relationship is not proved. Robert's 1851 Glenleslie household contained an unmarried niece Mary Glasgow, aged 34 and a linen weaver.<ref name="Census1851">[https://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Dunaghy/Glenleslie/23/ 1851 Census of Ireland], Glenleslie household 23.</ref> She is a strong candidate for the Mary who later named Hugh as her father. If they were the same woman, Hugh was more probably Robert's brother or another collateral relative than his son. No record inspected names Hugh's parents or explicitly calls Hugh and Robert brothers.
