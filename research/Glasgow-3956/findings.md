# Findings: [Joseph Glasgow (Glasgow-3956)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3956)

Last updated: 2026-08-10

## Current conclusion

The 1862 marriage formerly used to create or support this profile was
misread. [[Glasgow-3957|Mary Glasgow]]'s father is **Hugh Glasgow, weaver**, not
Joseph Glasgow. The record therefore supplies no evidence that this Joseph
existed, was Mary's father, or was a son of [[Glasgow-1367|Robert Glasgow]].

## Relationship correction

- Detach [[Glasgow-3957|Mary Glasgow]] as Joseph's daughter.
- Detach [[Glasgow-1367|Robert Glasgow]] as Joseph's father.
- If no independent source for Joseph exists on the live profile, treat
  [[Glasgow-3956]] as an unsupported record-created profile requiring removal
  or repurposing, not as a member of the family.
