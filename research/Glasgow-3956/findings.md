# Findings: [Hugh Glasgow (Glasgow-3956)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3956)

Last updated: 2026-08-25

## Current conclusion

Hugh Glasgow was a labourer and weaver in County Antrim. The original civil
marriage register for [[Glasgow-4047|Eliza Glasgow]] directly names **Hugh
Glasgow, labourer and weaver**, as her father. Eliza was aged 21 and lived at
Liminary when she married William Montgomery on 21 August 1854.

The father-child relationship between Hugh and Eliza is therefore **confident**.
Hugh's own parents remain unproved; his link to [[Glasgow-1367|Robert Glasgow]]
remains uncertain.

## Remaining question

Robert's 1851 Glenleslie household contained an unmarried niece Mary Glasgow,
aged 34 and a linen weaver. If she was the Mary who married Thomas Montgomery
in 1862, Hugh was more likely Robert's brother or collateral relative than his
son. No inspected record names Hugh's parents or expressly connects him to
Robert.
