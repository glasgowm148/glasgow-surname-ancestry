'''Mary Glasgow''' was born about 1818, probably in County Antrim, Ireland. She was a
linen weaver (if the1851 census identification is correct) and the daughter of [[Glasgow-3956|Hugh Glasgow]], also a weaver, already represented on WikiTree.

Mary married Thomas Montgomery on 13 September 1862. The civil register
describes her as of full age and unmarried and names her father as Hugh Glasgow,
weaver.<ref name="Marriage1862">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1862/09623/5489617.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Ballymena district, Thomas Montgomery and Mary Glasgow, 13 September 1862.</ref>

The Mary Montgomery who died at Crankill on 22 March 1898, aged seventy-nine, is her probable death identity. Her son John
Montgomery was present at the death.<ref name="Death1898">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/deaths_returns/deaths_1898/05842/4654852.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil death register], Ballymena district, Mary Montgomery, 22 March 1898.</ref>
