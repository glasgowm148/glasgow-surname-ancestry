'''Mary Glasgow''' was born about 1818 in County Antrim, Ireland. She was a
linen weaver and the daughter of '''Hugh Glasgow''', also a weaver. No compatible
WikiTree profile has yet been identified for Hugh.

Mary married Thomas Montgomery on 13 September 1862. The civil register
describes her as of full age and unmarried and names her father as Hugh Glasgow,
weaver.<ref name="Marriage1862">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1862/09623/5489617.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Ballymena district, Thomas Montgomery and Mary Glasgow, 13 September 1862.</ref>

Mary died at Crankill on 22 March 1898, aged seventy-nine. Her son John
Montgomery was present at the death.<ref name="Death1898">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/deaths_returns/deaths_1898/05842/4654852.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil death register], Ballymena district, Mary Montgomery, 22 March 1898.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

Mary is probably the unmarried thirty-four-year-old linen weaver recorded as
the niece of [[Glasgow-1367|Robert Glasgow]] at Glenleslie in 1851.<ref>[https://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Dunaghy/Glenleslie/23/ 1851 Census of Ireland], Glenleslie household 23.</ref> If the identification is correct, Mary's father Hugh was probably Robert's brother. The match is strong but still indirect.

[[Glasgow-3956|Joseph Glasgow]] was previously attached as Mary's father through
a misreading of the marriage register. The original certificate clearly names
her father as Hugh Glasgow, weaver, so Joseph was detached.
