# Findings: [Mary (Glasgow) Montgomery (Glasgow-3957)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3957)

Last updated: 2026-08-13

## Current conclusion

Mary married Thomas Montgomery in 1862 and named her father as **Hugh
Glasgow, weaver**. Her age and locality strongly fit the Mary Glasgow described
as [[Glasgow-1367|Robert Glasgow]]'s niece in 1851. If the identification is
correct, Hugh was Robert's brother. No compatible WikiTree profile for this
Hugh has been identified.

Hugh Glasgow, 23, weaver of Liminary, married Ann Wilson in 1852, and Eliza
Glasgow, 21, of Liminary, married William Montgomery in 1854. Both named a
father Hugh Glasgow, weaver. They are proved children of an elder Hugh and are
plausible siblings of Mary, but Mary's register supplies no residence and does
not prove that all three named the same man.

## Relationship correction

Detach [[Glasgow-3956|Joseph Glasgow]] as Mary's father. The register explicitly
names Hugh. If this Mary is Robert's census niece, Hugh is Robert's probable
brother. A second record linking Mary or Hugh specifically to Glenleslie is
still required before that sibling relationship is treated as proved.

Mary's son John is proved only by his presence as informant at her 1898 death.
No Ballymena birth, marriage, 1901 Crankill household, or age-compatible later
record has yet connected a John Montgomery to parents Thomas Montgomery and
Mary Glasgow. He may have been born before civil birth registration began in
1864. The inspected 1893 Ballymena death of a Thomas Montgomery describes a
widower and therefore cannot be Mary's husband, because Mary lived until 1898.
