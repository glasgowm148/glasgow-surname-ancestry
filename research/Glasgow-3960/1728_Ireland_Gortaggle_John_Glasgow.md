== Biography ==

'''John Glasgow''', gentleman of Gortigillagh, County Leitrim, appeared in Registry of Deeds memorial 38213 on 30 May 1728. The near-contemporary forms “Gortogill” and “Cortogill” recorded for modern Gortaggle make that Annaduff-parish townland the strong locality match.<ref>Registry of Deeds memorial 38213, indexed extract preserved in the project research files; [https://www.logainm.ie/en/29111/ Logainm: Gortaggle].</ref>
