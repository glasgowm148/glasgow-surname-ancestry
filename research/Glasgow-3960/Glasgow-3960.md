== Biography ==
'''John Glasgow''' was recorded as a gentleman of Gortigillagh, County Leitrim, when he witnessed an agreement dated 30 May 1728, registered 11 July 1728. The retained source is the Registry of Deeds Index Project’s abstract of memorial 38213, volume 57, page 235; the original has not been newly inspected.<ref name="Deed1728">[https://irishdeedsindex.net/mem.php?memorial=38213 Registry of Deeds Index Project, memorial 38213, agreement 30 May 1728, registered 11 July 1728.]</ref>

The parties were James Johnston of Cashellmenon, County Donegal, and Robert Johnston of Aghadunvane, County Leitrim. The witness network includes Bartley McGowan and John Gilhuly. The abstract gives no age, birthplace or family relationship. John's present about-1705 birth is an estimate; the older before-about-1707 wording came from assuming an adult witness aged at least 21. There is no 1707 occurrence.

The townland identification remains disputed in the project: the findings favour Gortaggle, Annaduff, with near-contemporary Gortogill/Cortogill forms; the older biography favours Gortnasillagh, Rossinver. Both arguments are retained below. Neither identifies John's birthplace.

[[Glasgow-551|John Glasgow of the Tyrone family tradition]] is selected locally as a very uncertain possible father, using their different estimated generations. They may instead represent the same John. No contemporary record supplies this parent link. His wife remains unknown. [[Glasgow-3124|James Glasgow of Aghadonvane]] is already attached as his son, without a certainty mark; no cited record proves that relationship. His death date and place remain unknown.

== Possible Relationship to James Glasgow ==

John may have been related to '''James Glasgow of Lareen and Aghadunvane''', County Leitrim.

James was probably the gentleman of Lareen named in a 1775 marriage settlement and was later identified in a published Montgomery pedigree as '''James Glasgow of Aughadenvarn, County Leitrim, Esquire'''.

The evidence supporting a possible relationship includes:

* the uncommon Glasgow surname;
* the description of both men as gentlemen;
* their association with the same part of north County Leitrim;
* John's connection with Robert Johnston of Aghadunvane in 1728; and
* James's later association with Aghadunvane or nearby Lareen.

The chronology would permit John to have been:

* James's father;
* James's grandfather;
* an uncle;
* an older cousin; or
* another member of the same local Glasgow family.

No record located so far identifies James as John's son. John should therefore '''not''' presently be attached as James's father.

== Estimated Birth ==

John's birth before about '''1707''' is an estimate.

He was described as a gentleman and acted as a witness to a legal agreement on 30 May 1728. Assuming that he was at least twenty-one years old, he would have been born no later than approximately 1707.

He may have been considerably older. A broad working range of approximately '''1680–1707''' is possible, but no baptismal, birth or age record has been located.

His estimated birthplace in County Leitrim is based only on his residence there in 1728. It is not proof that he was born in the county.

=== Identity of Gortigillagh ===

The Registry of Deeds index transcribes John's residence as '''Gortigillagh'''.<ref name="Deed1728" />

No modern townland of that exact spelling has been identified in County Leitrim. The strongest candidate is '''Gortnasillagh''' in Rossinver parish because:

* it has numerous historical spelling variants;
* it is situated in the relevant north Leitrim district;
* it appears in Registry of Deeds material by 1718; and
* it is geographically associated with Aghadunvane.

The identification should remain qualified as probable until the original memorial image or another contemporary record confirms the spelling.

=== Nature of the 1728 Record ===

The surviving online entry is an abstract or index of Registry of Deeds memorial 38213 rather than a full transcription of the original memorial.

It records:

* type of deed: agreement;
* date of deed: 30 May 1728;
* registration date: 11 July 1728;
* volume 57;
* page 235;
* memorial 38213; and
* John Glasgow of Gortigillagh as a gentleman and deed witness.<ref name="Deed1728" />

The abstract does not state:

* John's age;
* his parents;
* his wife;
* his children;
* his property;
* his relationship to the Johnston parties; or
* his relationship to the later James Glasgow.

The original memorial should be examined before drawing further conclusions.

=== Johnston Connection ===

John witnessed a transaction involving Robert Johnston of Aghadunvane. This indicates some form of social, professional or local connection with the Johnston family.

Possible explanations include:

* John was a neighbouring landholder or tenant;
* he was a legal or estate associate;
* he was related by marriage;
* he was a trusted local witness; or
* he had another business connection with the parties.

The deed abstract does not establish which explanation is correct.

== Research Conclusion ==

John Glasgow is documented as a gentleman of Gortigillagh, County Leitrim, in 1728.

Gortigillagh was probably modern Gortnasillagh in Rossinver parish, although the original memorial should be checked to confirm the placename.

John is the strongest currently identified candidate for an older relative of James Glasgow of Lareen and Aghadunvane. The chronology and geography permit a father-and-son relationship, but no record presently proves it.

John's parents, wife, children, exact birth, death and relationship to James Glasgow remain unknown.
