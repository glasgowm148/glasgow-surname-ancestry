# Findings: John Glasgow (Glasgow-3960), Gortigillagh/Gortaggle, 1728

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

Registry of Deeds memorial 38,213 records **John Glasgow, gentleman, of
Gortigillagh, County Leitrim**, as a witness on 30 May 1728. Gortigillagh is
strongly matched to modern Gortaggle in Annaduff parish. This is a genuine
same-generation Irish candidate for comparison with John-551, but no record
connects him to the traditional County Tyrone family.

Within the Registry of Deeds Index Project's currently indexed Glasgow entries,
he is the only named John before 1750. That is an index-bound observation, not
proof that no other John existed.
