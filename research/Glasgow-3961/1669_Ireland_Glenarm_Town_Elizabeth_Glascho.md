== Biography ==

'''Elizabeth Glasgow''', recorded as ''Eliz Glascho'', was a taxed householder
at Glenarm Town in Tickmacrevan parish, lower Glenarm barony, County Antrim, in
the 1669 Hearth Money Roll.<ref name="Antrim1669">Public Record Office of
Northern Ireland, 1669 County Antrim Hearth Money Roll, T/307/A; W. Macafee,
[https://www.billmacafee.com/1660shearthmoneyrolls/1669hearthmoneyrollsantrim.pdf
surname transcription], record 5730, PDF p. 36 (accessed 17 July 2026).</ref>

The same return records James Glascho and [[Glasgow-3913|Jo Glascho]] at Kilwaughter Demesne in the
neighbouring upper Glenarm barony.<ref name="Antrim1669" /> The close date and
east-Antrim geography make a shared extended family plausible, but the roll does
not state any relationship.
