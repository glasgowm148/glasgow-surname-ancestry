== Biography ==

'''William Glasgow''', farmer, witnessed the Cavankilgreen lease recorded in Registry of Deeds memorial 113381 on 22 January 1746.<ref>Registry of Deeds memorial 113381, indexed extract preserved in the project research files.</ref>

An unnamed Glasgow had occupied part of the same townland in 1725. William is a plausible identity for that occupier, but the later memorial does not prove it. No record links him to [[Glasgow-1025|James Glasgow of Drumearn]] or identifies him as James's son.
