## Source findings — Cavankilgreen lease witness, 22 January 1746

William Glasgow, farmer, witnessed the Cavankilgreen lease recorded in Registry of Deeds memorial 113381 on 22 January 1746 (an indexed extract preserved in the project research files; the memorial image is not cited). Cavankilgreen is in Carnteel parish, County Tyrone. A January date before 1752 may be 1745/6 in Old Style; the extract does not say which.

**Assessment:** a documentary occurrence known from an indexed extract. An unnamed Glasgow occupied part of the same townland in 1725; William is a plausible identity for that occupier, but the 1746 memorial does not prove it. No record links him to [James Glasgow of Drumearn (Glasgow-1025)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1025) or identifies him as James's son. The project snapshot and public WikiTree searches on 22 July 2026 found no earlier compatible profile; this profile was created from the draft in this folder.

## Original-image access test — 23 October 2026

The [Registry of Deeds Index Project's two abstracts of memorial 71498](https://irishdeedsindex.net/mem.php?memorial=71498) give a lease dated 18 May 1725 and registered 1 June 1741. The fuller abstract describes an unnamed Glasgow as the existing occupier of the land leased by Acheson Moore to John Falls. Its Glasgow forename field is explicitly blank; the shorter abstract omits the occupier. These are two abstracts of one deed, not independent records of two Glasgow people.

The index's original-image button specifies image 175. Its book/image resolver supplies the FamilySearch film link above. The image request returned HTTP 403, and both the in-app browser and the research Chrome session displayed a FamilySearch Incapsula access failure. **No manuscript page was read.** The abstract also mentions a 1736 term-of-lives notation, whose wording needs the original; no Glasgow child or relative can be inferred from it.

Do not identify the unnamed occupier as William solely from his later appearance in the same townland. [John Glasgow-551](/people/glasgow-551.html) remains a different possible comparison from the Tyrone family tradition, without a named-person bridge. No birth date, father, identity selection or confidence percentage changes from this test. The distinct 1746 memorial 113381 still needs its own original locator and reading.
