The original 1713 General Synod roll names **Geo: Glasgow** in the ruling-elder
column beside minister Thomas Miligan. The pair appears under Tyrone Presbytery;
Bolton's later congregation index identifies their congregation as Keady,
**County Armagh**. Presbytery jurisdiction must not be mistaken for residence in
County Tyrone.

No record identifies George's parents, spouse or children or connects him to
Thomas-3556 or Nathaniel-1136. Because he was already a ruling elder in 1713,
father, uncle or older cousin is at least as plausible as brother. Remove any
specific `grandson of George` suggestion unsupported by another source.
