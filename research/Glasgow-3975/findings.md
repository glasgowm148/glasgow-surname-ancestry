# Findings: Sarah Glasgow (Glasgow-3975), wife of Charles Taylor

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

Sarah's original 1885 marriage directly proves that she was a daughter of
Thomas Glasgow, farmer, and lived at Lisnagaver. Civil registrations prove her
marriage to Charles Taylor and at least four children there; a contemporary
notice adds John Glasgow Taylor as another probable son.

Mary Ann Glasgow's 1914 Lisnagaver death return names Sarah Taylor as informant
but describes her as Mary Ann's `Grand-daughter`. This conflicts with Sarah's
direct marriage record and the chronology, so it is retained as an unresolved
clerical error or same-place identity conflict rather than a reason to weaken
the proved Sarah-to-Thomas relationship.
