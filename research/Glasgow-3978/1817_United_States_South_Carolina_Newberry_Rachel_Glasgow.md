== Biography ==

'''Rachel Glasgow''' was a daughter of [[Glasgow-1144|James Glasgow]] and his wife Mary. James named Rachel among his children in his will dated 17 October 1775.<ref name="JamesWill">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184337id_/http://www.lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_JGlasgowWill.html "Will of James Glasgow"], transcription of Newberry County Will Book A, p. 194; will dated 17 October 1775 and proved 13 July 1793.</ref>

Rachel married '''Robert McCrackin'''. The 1817 Wilson-estate equity complaint identifies Rachel as James's daughter and Robert McCrackin's wife. Her brother Robert Glasgow admitted the listed sibling relationships and marriages in his sworn answer.<ref name="Complaint">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184247id_/http://www.lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_Complaint.html "Equity complaint concerning the estate of Archibald Wilson"], transcription of the 1817 South Carolina pleading.</ref><ref name="Answer">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184247id_/http://www.lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_Reply.html "Answer of Robert Glasgow"], transcription of his sworn answer dated 2 June 1817.</ref>

The King’s Creek cemetery compilation records Rachel's death on 16 August 1831 aged 57. In the same family plot, Robert died on 10 August 1817 aged 46. Stones explicitly call Ruth, who died aged 14 in 1816, and Pulauski, who died aged 8 in 1817, children of R. and R. McCrackin.<ref name="KingsCreek">[https://fairfieldgenealogysociety.org/Members_Only/Families/Chapman/Documents/Newberry%20County%20Cemeteries%20Vol%20I.pdf ''Newberry County, South Carolina: Cemeteries, Volume I''], p. 3, King’s Creek A.R.P. Church Cemetery.</ref>

Rachel's marriage was one of three McCracken marriages in the same Glasgow family. Her brother [[Glasgow-1982|Wilson Crossier Glasgow]] married [[McCracken-3711|Mary McCracken]], and their daughter Mariah married William McCracken. No record yet establishes how the three McCracken spouses were related.

== Research Notes ==

The records prove that Rachel's mother was James's wife Mary, but they do not prove Mary's birth surname. The existing attachment to [[Crosier-512]] should therefore be marked uncertain or corrected unless independent evidence is found.

Col. Cyrus McCrackin (1792–1852) is the leading candidate for another son: he is buried in the same plot and administered Robert's 1817 estate. Neither fact alone states a parent-child relationship, so do not attach him yet.
