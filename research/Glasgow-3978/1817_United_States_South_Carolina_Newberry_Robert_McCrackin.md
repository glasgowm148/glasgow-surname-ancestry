## Evidence note

The 1817 equity case directly identifies Robert as Rachel Glasgow's husband.
The King’s Creek cemetery compilation gives his dates and explicitly identifies
Ruth and Pulauski as children of R. and R. McCrackin. Cyrus McCrackin's
administration of Robert's 1817 estate and burial in the same plot make Cyrus a
probable child, but not one safe to attach without a kinship statement.

Revill's transcription of the 5 January 1768 *Chichester* immigrant list
directly groups Arthur and Ruth McCrachen with ten children, but Robert is not
listed and was apparently born in South Carolina about 1771. In 1790, after
Thomas, John and James had separate households, the younger Arthur's four-adult-
male household could accommodate Arthur, Samuel and Robert plus another man.
Matching cemetery entries indicate the immigrant Arthur and Ruth had already
died in 1779 and 1774, so the census is not an exact parental household.
This is substantial indirect evidence, not a `son` statement. See
`sources/mccrachen-1768-immigrant-household.md` and `findings.md`.
