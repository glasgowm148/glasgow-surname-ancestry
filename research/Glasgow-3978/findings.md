# Findings: Rachel (Glasgow) McCrackin (Glasgow-3978)

Last updated: 2026-08-13

## Current conclusion

Rachel was a proved daughter of James and Mary Glasgow and wife of Robert
McCrackin. Her King’s Creek stone gives death 16 August 1831 aged 57. The same
plot identifies Robert (died 10 August 1817 aged 46) and explicitly names Ruth
and Pulauski as children of R. and R. McCrackin. Robert is therefore the man
whose 1817 estate was administered by Cyrus McCrackin, not the separate Robert
estate filed in 1821.

Cyrus (1792–1852), buried in the same plot and administrator of Robert's estate,
is the strongest candidate for another son, but no inspected record calls him
Robert or Rachel's child. Do not attach him yet.

Robert's father is now most probably the immigrant [Arthur McCracken
(McCracken-2429)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/McCracken-2429), husband of
[Ruth Unknown (Unknown-395112)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Unknown-395112).
The evidence is substantially stronger than a tree assertion, but remains
circumstantial because Robert was born after the immigrant list and no inspected
record calls him Arthur's son. Keep Robert's parents blank pending an explicit
heir, son or distribution statement.

## Best next proof target

The direct-proof targets remain Newberry estate 317 (Arthur, 1812), estate 308
(William, 1805) and estate 758 (Robert, 1817). Thomas administered Arthur's
estate; Thomas and Robert jointly administered William's. The FamilySearch
loose-papers collection is public but image browsing redirected to sign-in on
13 August 2026. The South Carolina Archives online index was also temporarily
offline for its announced August 2026 migration. Neither limitation requires a
PRONI request. An heir list, distribution receipt or administrator's petition
from these packets could turn the present Robert–Arthur inference into proof.

The repeated Glasgow marriages do not yet identify one generation. Mary
McCracken, wife of Wilson Glasgow, was born about 1777 and therefore cannot be
the Mary aged 17 in the 1768 immigrant household. William McCracken, husband of
Mariah Glasgow by 1837, is likewise too late to identify automatically with the
William aged 14 in 1768.
