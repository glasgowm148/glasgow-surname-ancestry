# Findings: [Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-3982), about 1808–1813](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3982)

Last updated: 2026-08-12

## Current conclusion

The photographed Gilders Creek memorial directly identifies this child as the
son of Elizabeth Glasgow and her husband,
[Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-1983)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1983).
Both parent attachments are directly supported by the shared gravestone. This
child is not
[Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-2769)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2769),
who survived into 1815 and 1818 records.

## Recommended WikiTree status

Retain Elizabeth and Glasgow-1983 as the gravestone-proved parents. Detach the
different Tennessee Robert, Glasgow-2769, from this couple because both Roberts
were alive during overlapping periods.
