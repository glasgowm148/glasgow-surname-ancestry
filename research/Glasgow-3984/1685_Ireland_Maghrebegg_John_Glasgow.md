== Biography ==

'''John Glasgow''' was recorded in an Irish pre-1858 wills and administrations index in 1685. His residence was written '''Maghrebegg'''.<ref name="JohnProbate1685">Public Record Office of Northern Ireland, ''Pre-1858 Wills and Administrations'' index, entry for John Glasgow, index date 1685, residence "Maghrebegg"; transcription supplied to the project in July 2026. The public index can be searched through the [https://apps.proni.gov.uk/ProniNames_IE/SearchPage.aspx PRONI Name Search]. The available transcription supplies no occupation, relatives, type of instrument or surviving original reference.</ref>

The record proves a same-name Irish resident and an estate or probate-related index event. It does not establish what the year represents, identify the place of death, or name a parent, spouse, child, executor, beneficiary or heir.<ref name="JohnProbate1685" />

The project map provisionally identifies the historical residence with '''Magherabeg''' in the Arboe and Loughinsholin area of County Londonderry. That modernisation is a close spelling and locality match, not a relationship record or proof of John's birthplace.

== Research Notes ==

The repository's One Tree export dated 6 August 2026 was searched for early men named John Glasgow and for Maghrebegg or Magherabeg. No profile carried that residence.

[[Glasgow-3913|John Glasgow]] was already an adult householder at Kilwaughter Demesne, County Antrim, in 1669 and had no known death date. He could have been the man indexed at Maghrebegg sixteen years later, but no record links the two localities. [[Glasgow-1498|John Glasgow of Cavers]] was an adult of the right generation, but no reviewed record places him in Ireland. [[Glasgow-551|John Glasgow]], estimated born about 1681, and [[Glasgow-2475|John Glasgow]], estimated born in 1685, were too young to be ordinary adult probate subjects in 1685.

Keep [[Glasgow-3984|this profile]] unconnected and do not assign Magherabeg as a certain birth or death place. Do not merge him with [[Glasgow-3913|the Kilwaughter John]] unless a fuller probate entry, will abstract, administration bond, diocesan index or estate reference bridges the two records.
