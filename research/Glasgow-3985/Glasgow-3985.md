# WikiTree update notes: [Henry Glasgow (Glasgow-3985)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3985)

```text
'''Henry Glasgow''' was present at Inishrush when [[Glasgow-3903|James Glasgow]] died there on 11 December 1878. The civil return gives Henry's residence as Inishrush but does not state his relationship to James.<ref name="JamesDeath1878">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/view/?record_id=cide-5928386 IrishGenealogy.ie], civil death of James Glasgow, Inishrush, 11 December 1878; [https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/deaths_returns/deaths_1878/020497/7192875.pdf original register image].</ref>

Henry's estimated birth about 1850 and his attachment as James's son are uncertain working hypotheses. No record presently located names his parents. Being present at a death can indicate a relative, household member or neighbour and does not by itself prove sonship.
```
