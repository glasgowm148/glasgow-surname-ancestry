# WikiTree update notes: [James Glasgow (Glasgow-3986), born about 1773, Larne](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3986)

## Paste-ready addition

=== Possible early records ===

A release dated 16 April 1795 concerning garden ground in Newtown of Larne was witnessed by James Glasgow of Larne, merchant, and Charles McGarel, gentleman.<ref name="Merchant1795">Registry of Deeds transcript, release dated 16 April 1795, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSH3-Q9B8-H FamilySearch image].</ref>

An assignment recorded in 1797 concerning a Larne tenement near Charles Dawson Stewart's tanyard includes a witness signature for James Glasgow junior.<ref name="Junior1797">Registry of Deeds transcript, assignment recorded in 1797, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSH3-Q96R-C FamilySearch image].</ref>

The date, place, merchant designation and use of “junior” fit this James, born about 1773, better than the older Larne men. Neither deed names his parents. “Junior” distinguishes him from an older James but does not by itself prove that the older man was his father, so the present father relationship remains uncertain.
