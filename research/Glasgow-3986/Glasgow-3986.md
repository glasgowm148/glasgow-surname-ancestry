== Biography ==

James was reportedly born about 1773 and died in 1831. His parentage has not been proved. He is currently attached to [[Glasgow-1505|William Glasgow]], but the approximately sixty-five-year gap makes a later generation more likely; the relationship should remain uncertain until a baptism, will or property succession identifies his father.

=== Possible early records ===

A release dated 16 April 1795 concerning garden ground in Newtown of Larne was witnessed by '''James Glasgow of Larne, merchant''', and Charles McGarel, gentleman.<ref name="Merchant1795">Registry of Deeds transcript, release dated 16 April 1795, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSH3-Q9B8-H FamilySearch image].</ref>

An assignment recorded in 1797 concerning a Larne tenement near Charles Dawson Stewart's tanyard includes a witness signature for '''James Glasgow junior'''.<ref name="Junior1797">Registry of Deeds transcript, assignment recorded in 1797, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSH3-Q96R-C FamilySearch image].</ref>

The date, place, merchant designation and use of “junior” fit this James better than the older Larne men. Neither deed names his parents. “Junior” distinguishes him from an older James but does not by itself prove a father-son relationship.

== Research Notes ==

[[Glasgow-4024|James Glasgow of Cookstown (abt.1791-bef.1831)]] was buried at St Luran's on 13 March 1831 aged 40. He is not this Larne man: the reported ages, locality and burial context differ. The profiles should remain separate.

=== Current Larne reconstruction ===

The younger James of Larne remains a provisional identity assembled from the 1795 merchant and 1797 “junior” occurrences. Those records need not identify the same man, and neither names him as the father of [[Glasgow-951|Alexander Glasgow]]. He is nevertheless an age-compatible possible son of [[Glasgow-1509|James Glasgow]] and a possible route to Alexander's generation: an estimated birth about 1773 would allow ordinary fatherhood ages in the early nineteenth century. This is an alternative to the older-Alexander route, not a replacement for the Thomas-family testimony, and Thomas and Daniel must not be assigned to this James merely to make a pedigree. The ropemaker, this possible merchant/junior identity, and the Larne tanner are distinct record groups unless a document joins them. Preserve the existing record citations and state the identity and kinship as provisional.<ref name="YoungerJamesCurrent">The merchant and junior references cited above establish leads, not a father statement for Alexander.</ref>
