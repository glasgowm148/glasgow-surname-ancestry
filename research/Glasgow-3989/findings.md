## Possible duplicate: the 1843 bride — 21 September 2026

[Mary Glasgow-4181](/people/glasgow-4181.html), the bride in the [1843 settlement](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSJW-FQ3Q), is a possible duplicate of this Mary. Her probable census identification gives death in autumn 1843 aged 37, conflicting with this Mary’s monument death 18 October 1844 aged 37. Separate profiles remain provisional. The new bride’s uncertain attachment to Hugh Glasgow-4180 does not displace this Mary’s monument-proved father William Glasgow-3324; if the women are identical, the Hugh hypothesis must be withdrawn.

## Current conclusion

Mary, daughter of William Glasgow and Elizabeth, died on 18 October 1844 aged 37 according to the [Larne monument transcription](https://archive.org/details/ulsterjournalofa06ulstiala/page/47/mode/1up), supporting birth about 1807. The earlier after 1783 birth boundary is superseded. Mary Glasgow-4181, the intended bride in 1843, remains a possible duplicate: her probable census match died autumn 1843 aged 37. Resolve the identity and date conflict before merging; retain this Mary’s documented father William.
