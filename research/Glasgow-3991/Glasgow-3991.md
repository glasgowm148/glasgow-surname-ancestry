# WikiTree update notes: [James Glasgow (Glasgow-3991), born about 1747/48, Larne](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3991)

## Paste-ready addition

=== Tanner at Larne ===

James is probably the Larne tanner documented between 1768 and 1786. A 1768 assignment by a different James Glasgow, a ropemaker, was witnessed by James Glasgow, tanner. The witness swore to the deed's execution at Larne.<ref name="Tanner1768">Registry of Deeds transcript, assignment dated 28 March 1768, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSJW-DDXW FamilySearch image].</ref>

A mortgage dated 22 June 1778 concerning premises in Newtown of Larne was witnessed by James Glasgow of Larne, tanner, and John Weir, notary. James swore the supporting affidavit at Larne on 29 June 1778.<ref name="Tanner1778">Registry of Deeds transcript, mortgage dated 22 June 1778, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSJW-64YW FamilySearch image].</ref>

The occupation, place and chronology also agree with the 1786 Blackcave lease to James Glasgow, tanner of Larne. The sequence is a strong identity match, although the deeds do not name his wife or relatives. He should not be confused with the older Larne ropemaker represented by [[Glasgow-1509]].
