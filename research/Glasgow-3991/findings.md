# Findings: [James Glasgow (Glasgow-3991), about 1748-1812, Larne](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3991)

Last updated: 2026-08-11

## Current conclusion

The published Larne Parish Churchyard monument directly identifies James as a
son of Hugh and Ann Glasgow. He died 14 April 1812 aged 64, placing his birth
approximately between 15 April 1747 and 14 April 1748. The same inscription
names his wife Jane and daughter Ann `Glasgow-3992`.

This man cannot be the already-established ropemaker `Glasgow-1509`: he was only
about twelve or thirteen when the ropewalk was advertised in January 1761. The
new separate profile correctly prevents that chronological conflation.

## WikiTree treatment

Keep James-3991 under Hugh-1503 and Ann as supported by the monument. Do not
merge him with James-1509 or assign Janet Shutter as his wife. Jane's maiden
surname is unknown.
