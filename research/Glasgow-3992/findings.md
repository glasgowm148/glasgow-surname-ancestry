### Relationship to the 1832 lease and existing Larne accounts

The previously inspected [1874 indenture recital](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CS54-77Q4-H) traces James Glasgow’s 2 October 1779 Newtown of Larne lease to a **25 February 1832 renewal to Anne and Jane Glasgow**. The 1821 abstract independently places an adult Ann and a sister Jane together in Larne eleven years earlier. This supplies a household explanation for the same-name lease pair and makes the connection more specific than two unrelated surname occurrences. It does not prove that No. 22 Main Street was the recital’s 24-foot north-side tenement, or that the women remained the same leaseholders in 1832. The renewal or intervening title records remain the next test.

[Ann-3992](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3992) remains a possible identity for the abstract’s Ann. The retained monument transcription places her birth about 1767–1768 and death on 25 May 1832. Its age differs from the abstract by about three years, which remains a discrepancy rather than a decisive exclusion. No record yet connects the abstract’s sibling household to that monument family.

The abstract’s living sister Jane cannot be the monument’s **wife Jane**, who died on 21 July 1820, on the retained chronology. A younger sister could explain the 1832 Jane without treating the dead mother as a later leaseholder, but her identity has not been assigned. Likewise, the brother James alive in 1821 cannot be the monument father [James-3991](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3991), who died in 1812.

[James-3986](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3986), the provisionally reconstructed younger Larne James with an uncertain birth about 1773, is a compatible comparison for the 47-year-old brother. His own adult identity and proposed father [James-1509](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1509) remain uncertain; age and locality alone do not identify him or transfer that father to Ann and Jane. Keep this alternative distinct from the monument family. No new person, parent or existing-profile sibling attachment is selected here.

**Next questions:** inspect the 1832 renewal for relationships, residence and intervening assignments; compare the exact premises with No. 22 Main Street; seek a named sibling or parent bridge in the monument family and younger Larne James’s records. Do not resolve the abstract’s uncertain occupation or land note by inference.
