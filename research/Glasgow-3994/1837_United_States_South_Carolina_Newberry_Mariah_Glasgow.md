== Biography ==

'''Mariah Glasgow''' was a daughter of [[Glasgow-1982|Wilson Crossier Glasgow]] and [[McCracken-3711|Mary McCracken]]. She was born before her father's death on 11 September 1817.<ref name="Gilders">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/gilders_creek_cem.htm "Gilders Creek Cemetery"], Newberry County, South Carolina, reciprocal inscriptions for Wilson and Mary Glasgow.</ref>

Mariah married '''William McCracken'''. Her mother's will, dated 28 November 1837, explicitly calls her “my beloved daughter Mariah, wife of William McCracken.” Mary left Mariah a life interest in one-sixth of the estate remaining after Ruth's share and appointed Mariah's brother Hiram as trustee.<ref name="MaryWill">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184247id_/http://www.lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_WilsonGlasgowWife.html "Will of Mary, widow of Wilson Glasgow"], transcription of Newberry Will Book N, p. 1; will dated 28 November 1837 and proved 22 October 1838.</ref>

Mariah's marriage continued a repeated McCracken alliance. Her mother was born a McCracken, and her paternal aunt Rachel Glasgow married Robert McCrackin. No inspected record yet identifies how William, Mary and Robert McCracken were related.

== Research Notes ==

The Newberry real-estate index prints her name as Maria. Mary's will uses Mariah and directly supplies her married surname and husband. Do not infer that William was her maternal cousin without probate or other kinship evidence.
