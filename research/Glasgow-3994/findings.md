# Findings: Mariah (Glasgow) McCracken (Glasgow-3994)

Last updated: 2026-08-13

## Current conclusion

Mariah was a daughter of Wilson Crossier Glasgow and Mary McCracken. Mary's
1837 will directly calls her “my beloved daughter Mariah, wife of William
McCracken” and appoints Hiram trustee of her share. No inspected source yet
identifies William's parents or shows how he was related to Mariah's mother Mary
or to Robert McCrackin, husband of Mariah's aunt Rachel.
