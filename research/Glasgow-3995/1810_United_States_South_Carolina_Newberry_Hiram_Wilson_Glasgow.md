== Biography ==

[[Category:Glasgow Name Study]]

'''Hiram Wilson Glasgow''' was born about 1810 in South Carolina. His Gilders Creek gravestone records that he died on 20 February 1847 aged 37.<ref name="Gilders">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/gilders_creek_cem.htm "Gilders Creek Cemetery"], Newberry County, South Carolina, transcription for Hiram Glasgow (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

Hiram was a son of [[Glasgow-1982|Wilson Crossier Glasgow]] and Mary. Mary's will directly calls Hiram her “beloved son” and appointed him trustee for the share left for his sister Mariah McCracken.<ref name="MaryWill">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124215757id_/http://lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_WilsonGlasgowWife.html "Will of Mary, wife of Wilson Glasgow"], transcription of Newberry Will Book N, p. 1; will dated 28 November 1837 and proved 22 October 1838 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

Hiram administered the estate of [[Glasgow-1145|Absolom Glasgow]] in 1834. In 1837 he became guardian of “J. L. Glasgow and others,” apparently Absolom's minor children.<ref name="EstateIndex">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/estates1785-1949/22.htm "Probate court general index to estates, 1785–1949"], entries for Absolom Glasgow, estate 1069, and J. L. Glasgow and others, estate 1901 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

Hiram married '''Mary Ann Brandon Kennedy'''. A later account identifies their son as the Reverend '''Leonidas K. Glasgow''', born in Newberry in 1837.<ref>[https://newsofdavidson.org/2025/02/08/85148/davidson-history-the-reverend-leonidas-k-glasgow/ "Davidson History: The Reverend Leonidas K. Glasgow"], ''News of Davidson'', 8 February 2025 (accessed 12 August 2026).</ref>

Hiram died on 20 February 1847. Robert G. Gilliam administered his estate.<ref name="Gilders" /><ref name="EstateIndex" />

== Research Notes ==

Hiram's administration of Absolom's estate shows a close collateral relationship but does not make him a son of [[Glasgow-1146|John Glasgow]]. Mary's will directly places Hiram in Wilson's household.
