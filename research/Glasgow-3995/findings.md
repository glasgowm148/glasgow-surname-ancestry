# Findings: Hiram Wilson Glasgow (Glasgow-3995)

Last updated: 2026-08-13

## Current conclusion

Hiram was a son of Wilson Crossier Glasgow and Mary McCracken. His gravestone
supports birth about 1810 and death 20 February 1847. He married Mary Ann
Brandon Kennedy. A biographical account identifies their son Rev. Leonidas K.
Glasgow, and Cleora (Glasgow) Speers's obituary calls Leonidas her brother,
thereby placing Cleora in the same household.

The live WikiTree profile has no spouse or children and contains an internal
birth conflict: structured 1810 versus 1800 in the biography.
