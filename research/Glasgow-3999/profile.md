== Biography ==

'''Ruth Glasgow''' was born on 26 May 1802. She married [[Glasgow-1145|Absolom Glasgow]] and was widowed before her mother made her will in 1837.

[[McCracken-3711|Mary (McCracken) Glasgow]], widow of [[Glasgow-1982|Wilson Crossier Glasgow]], explicitly called Ruth her daughter and gave her one-seventh of her property in her will dated 28 November 1837.<ref name="MaryWill">Newberry District, South Carolina, Will Book N, p. 1, will of Mary Glasgow, widow of Wilson Glasgow, dated 28 November 1837 and proved 22 October 1838; [https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184247id_/http://www.lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_WilsonGlasgowWife.html archived transcription]. The displayed recording date of July 1837 precedes execution and is evidently a transcription error.</ref>

Ruth died on 25 April 1863 and was buried at Gilders Creek Cemetery in Newberry County, South Carolina.<ref name="GildersCreek">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/gilders_creek_cem.htm Gilders Creek Cemetery survey], Newberry County, South Carolina, entry for Ruth Glasgow.</ref><ref name="FindAGrave">{{FindAGrave|33531789}}, memorial for Ruth Glasgow.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Parentage and identity ===

Mary's will directly proves that she had a daughter Ruth. The index to Wilson's real-estate packet lists Mary with Robert, Ruth, Naomi, Matilda, Hiram, Almina and Maria, a group matching the seven child-shares created by Mary's will.<ref name="RealEstate">Newberry County, South Carolina, real-estate packet 1-13, estate of Wilson Glasgow; [https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/realestate.html index], entries for Wilson Glasgow and Mary, Robert, Ruth, Naomi, Matilda, Hiram, Almina and Maria.</ref>

The exact 1802–1863 dates, Gilders Creek burial and marriage to Absolom make this Ruth a strong match for Mary and Wilson's daughter. The original real-estate packet has not yet been inspected for explicit kin terms, so the attached parent relationships should remain marked uncertain rather than treated as proved.
