== Biography ==

'''John Garvin Glasgow''' was the infant son of [[Glasgow-2406|James Garvin Glasgow]] and [[Aiken-2081|Eliza Ann Aiken]]. His family gravestone, erected by his father, names “his infant son JOHN GARVIN” but gives no dates.<ref name="RasharkinStone">Pauline O'Keeffe and Helen Evans, [https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/RasharkinP/graveyard.html ''Rasharkin Second Presbyterian Church headstones''], transcription made August 2004, GLASGO memorial erected by James Garvin Glasgo; accessed 19 August 2026.</ref>

His parents married in 1886 and were the only people in their household in the 1901 census. His birth is therefore estimated as after 1886 and his death as before 1901; these are bounds, not exact years.<ref>[http://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1901/Antrim/Killoquin_Lower/Lisnagaver/946378/ 1901 Census of Ireland], Lisnagaver house 33, Killoquin Lower, County Antrim.</ref>
