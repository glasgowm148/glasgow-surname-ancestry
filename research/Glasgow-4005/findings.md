# Findings: [John Garvin Glasgow (Glasgow-4005)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4005)

Last updated: 2026-09-23

## Current conclusion

[John Garvin Glasgow (Glasgow-4005)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4005)
was born at Lisnagaver on 3 October 1887 and died on 18 October 1887, aged
under one. The death was registered in Ballymoney; its exact location is
unknown. His civil birth return names James Garvin Glasgow and Eliza Ann Aiken
as his parents. His father's family gravestone also names an infant son John
Garvin, and the parents' 1901 census household has no children. The GRONI
death entry matches the child's name, age, birth month and district. The live
profile now carries the exact death date and uncertain registration district.
