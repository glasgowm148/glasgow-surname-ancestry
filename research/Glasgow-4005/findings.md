# Findings: [John Garvin Glasgow (Glasgow-4005)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4005)

Last updated: 2026-08-19

## Conclusion

The Rasharkin Second Presbyterian family gravestone erected by
[James Garvin Glasgow (Glasgow-2406)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-2406)
names “his infant son John Garvin.” James and Eliza Ann Aiken married in 1886
and were alone in the 1901 census. The profile therefore uses birth after 1886
and death before 1901 as uncertain bounds; the stone supplies no exact dates.
