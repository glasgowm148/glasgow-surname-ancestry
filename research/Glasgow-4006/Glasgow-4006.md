== Biography ==
'''Preferred working family, 17 October 2026:''' Andrew Glasgow-3163 and Elizat Stevinsoun-2 are now the selected '''uncertain local parents''' of Robert Glasgow-1156 through his possible identity with their testament-proved son Robert Glasgow-4006. Archibald Glasgow-1097 and Isobell Newlands-647 remain unselected uncertain/possible alternatives. The two Robert profiles remain separate; the recorded son’s documented parentage does not make the adult identification certain.<ref name="CorsounFamilyReview" />
'''Robert Glasgow''' was the son of [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow of uncertain Corsoun]] and Andrew's wife '''Elizat Stevinsoun'''. Andrew's testament, made on 6 February 1597/8, expressly describes him as:

<blockquote>“Robart Glasgw his sone begottin be him vpoun the said Elizat his wyff”</blockquote>

Andrew directed that Elizat should “bruik his [word unclear] land quhill his said Robart cum to aige,” showing that Robert had not yet attained the relevant legal majority and was intended to succeed to the land after Elizat's interim possession.<ref name="Andrew1598">National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124, testament testamentar and inventory of Andro Glasgw, made 6 February 1597/8, death stated as 7 February, confirmed 10 July 1598; corrected working transcription of the original register images. Printed index: Scottish Record Society, ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments, 1514–1600'', [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso01scotuoft/page/n117/mode/1up p. 108].</ref>

The testament also names Andrew's daughters Marion, Janet and Isobel. It makes conditional provision “incais his said spous be with bairne to him”; that does not prove that an additional child was born.<ref name="Andrew1598" />

=== Identity ===

The profile previously represented an Andrew Glasgow created from a superseded reading of this testament; the corrected record names no such son. It has been repurposed for the directly documented son Robert. [[Glasgow-1156|Robert Glasgow of Duddingston]], estimated born in 1582 and buried there in 1647, is the preferred uncertain later identity at '''28%'''. The generation and broad Lothian setting fit; no holding, occupation, spouse or named-relative bridge identifies the men. Missing corroboration does not exclude the candidate. The profiles remain separate. [[Glasgow-1027|Robert Glasgow]] is the proved son of Ninian Glasgow and Jonet Meik, so he cannot be Andrew's testament-defined son; he remains a possible alternative identity for the Duddingston adult, whose burial names no parents. [[Glasgow-1096|Robert Glasgow, the earlier fisher]], died before Andrew's will.
