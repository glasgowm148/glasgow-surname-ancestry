# Findings: [Samuel Glasgow (Glasgow-4010)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4010)

## Assessment history — August and September 2026

[Samuel Glasgow (Glasgow-4010)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4010) is
the Irish-born storekeeper aged next birthday 51 in Prescott's 1851 census. No
compatible live profile existed. [Mary E. Unknown
(Unknown-764031)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Unknown-764031) and the four
created children—[Mary (Glasgow-4016)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4016),
[James E. (Glasgow-4017)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4017), [Orena
(Glasgow-4019)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4019) and [younger Samuel
(Glasgow-4021)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4021)—are retained as
uncertain household relationships because the census does not state kinship.
Alba and Lycania remain uncreated identity holds.

A direct reading of Robert Glasgow of Prescott's 1849 will, registered in
1856, gives six shares for brothers Daniel, Samuel, John and Thomas, brother James's children collectively (the will does not say James was dead; revised 7 October 2026, see below), and sister Mary. The same Prescott
property chain identifies the named Samuel as merchant Glasgow-4010. Robert was
therefore Samuel's brother, not his father; the later father–son account appears
to have mistaken six estate shares for six children. FamilySearch image
`3Q9M-CSLG-LWY9-S` was inspected on 8 September 2026; the other named siblings
still require their own identity and duplicate checks before profiles are created.

The most direct next Canadian target is the registered land chain for Robert's
**Centre Street** house: conveyances or releases by the children of James for their collective share may name them or their residences. The Centre/Dibble parcel is now identified in original deeds and the official abstract. It is separate from Samuel’s **251 Water Street West** property.

### Property and estate distinctions reviewed 13 September 2026

The 1843 purchase from Ebenezer Bacon for £75 was already recorded in the July source audit. The useful additional locator in the [Town of Prescott register](https://www.prescott.ca/recreation-culture/history-heritage/heritage-properties/) is **251 Water Street West, Plan 19, Block 2, part Lot 16, designation by-law 1985-54**. This is the Glasgow/Smyth Building; it is separate from Robert's Centre Street house and its six-share estate. No original conveyance has been inspected, and Samuel may have disposed of the Water Street interest before his death.

The more direct family-identification target is Samuel's reported purchase of the other interests in Robert's Centre Street property, following the already inspected 1849 will registered in 1856 (film 008200419, image 35). A [2017 property account](https://www.theresataylor.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/Auction-all-for-others-Brockville-Recorder.pdf) reports that transaction while misdescribing the kinship. Conveyances, releases or powers of attorney could identify James's children or their representatives; this is a retrieval inference, not a located instrument. Both the 2017 account and the inspected municipal report identify 388 Centre Street; 280 was a project citation error. Original deeds and OnLand abstract pp.71–72 now identify Lot 12 and the 3,400-square-foot part of Lot 11 south of Dibble Street.

The [LAC Samuel Glasgow fonds, item 98738](https://recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/home/record?IdNumber=98738&app=fonandcol), R6229-0-0-E / MG24-I39, belongs to a Niagara farmer who died about 1830 and is excluded as this merchant's personal archive.

The census has eight household members, **six of them probable children**, not eight children. Adding the two later sons reported by local history accounts for eight possible children; it does not independently reconcile the reported total of ten. Two remain unaccounted for by that particular evidence combination.

For the consequence for Alexander's parentage, see the [Canadian comparison on Alexander's page](/people/glasgow-951.html#family-network). No record here identifies him as one of James's children.

## Identity and relationship assessments — original Prescott conveyances, 13 September 2026

The original [30 July 1861 deed, B465](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSLG-LWB8-D) now identifies Daniel of Kilrea, watchmaker, and Thomas of Lisnagaver, farmer, with wives Margaret and Mary Anne, as the men selling interests under Robert’s will. Their identity with Daniel-3896 and Thomas-987 is established by the combined places, occupations, wives and earlier brotherhood evidence. The siblings’ parents remain unnamed. Robert, Samuel, John, Daniel and Thomas belong to the same will-defined sibling group, with sister Mary and brother James’s children also named. No contemporary parent names follow, and Alexander-951 is not proved to be another brother.

## Current conclusion — linked family hypothesis, 30 September 2026

Robert-4150 and Samuel-4010 are proved brothers of Daniel-3896 and Thomas-987. Their current public WikiTree biographies already contain the sibling will/deed evidence. Thomas and Daniel share the existing father attachment Alexander-3904. The descendant account naming Alexander as Thomas’s father, combined with proved brotherhood and compatible chronology, supports extending that same attachment to Robert and Samuel with **uncertain** status. The original deeds do not name a parent; this is an inherited family hypothesis, not a newly certain father. Assess Rosanna’s maternity separately.

## Recommended correction — brother James not stated to be dead, 30 September 2026

The assessment history above formerly described the will's collective share as for the children of a **deceased** brother James. [Robert's will of 9 October 1849](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSLG-LWY9-S) leaves a share to brother James's children but does not say James was dead, and a death before 1849 must not be inferred. James is probably, not provably, James Glasco, formerly of Prescott, carpenter, whose children Eliza Jane, Mary and Alexander conveyed Prescott interests to Samuel or his widow in 1860–68.

Recorded in `research/latest-findings-subjects/prescott-rochester-james-family/james-glasgow.md` (30 September 2026); carried into this file on 7 October 2026.
