# Findings: [Jane Glasgow (Glasgow-4013)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4013)

## Current conclusion

Glasgow-4013 represents Jane Glasgow of Larne in the 1844 probate index and is
the best match for the Jane Glasgow whose death was registered in Larne district
on 4 September 1873, aged 50. The name and locality agree, and the death-index
age implies birth about 1823, compatible with the profile's uncertain
"before 1826" birth bound. That earlier Larne reference documents probate
residence, not a proved birthplace; the death registration district likewise
does not establish her exact death place. The 1845 and 1846 Larne entries may
be later actions for the same estate but do not independently prove identity.
Jane Glasgow-3929 and other candidates have different documented family contexts.
