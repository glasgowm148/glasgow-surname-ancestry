# Created profile: [Margaret Glasgow (Glasgow-4015)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4015), mother of Mary Patton of Tyanee

Created unconnected on 19 August 2026 after a fresh duplicate check.

'''Margaret Glasgow''' was the mother of Mary Patton, who married Wilson Gordon
on 6 October 1825 in First Kilrea Presbyterian Church. The marriage register
describes Mary as the daughter of John Patton and Margaret Glasgow of Tyanee and
associates the family with the Portglenone congregation.

<ref>Richard John Torrens, [https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/KilreaP1/Marriages/1825.html "First Kilrea Presbyterian marriages, 1825"], entry for Wilson Gordon and Mary Patton, 6 October 1825.</ref>

The record directly establishes Margaret's name, co-parent, daughter and Tyanee
association. It does not identify Margaret's parents, age, birthplace, marriage
or relationship to the contemporary Inishrush Glasgows. Because Mary was old
enough to marry in 1825, Margaret was probably born before about 1787. She is a
plausible sister or cousin of Adam Glasgow of Inishrush and John Glasgow born in
1767, but no relationship should be attached without a further record.
