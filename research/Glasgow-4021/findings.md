# Findings: [Samuel Glasgow (Glasgow-4021)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4021)

## Current conclusion

Glasgow-4021 is the Canada-born younger Samuel aged next birthday 2 in merchant
Samuel Glasgow's 1851 Prescott household. He is distinct from the Irish-born
merchant. Both parent links and his birth estimate are uncertain.
