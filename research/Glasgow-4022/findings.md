# Findings: [William Glasgow (Glasgow-4022)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4022)

## Current conclusion

Glasgow-4022 is William Glasgow who died 21 November 1882 aged 27 and is named
on the St Nossonus family stone as Adam Glasgow's grandson. The stone does not
name William's parents. The live structured data nevertheless attaches
Glasgow-1623 as his father; that edge is unsupported and contradicts the
profile's own biography. He is not Glasgow-939, whose later life and death
differ.

## Live-profile changes

Add the civil return, set death place to the evidenced replacement Inishrush,
and record Lindsey as a proved brother. The former Glasgow-1623 father
attachment was unsupported, but no replacement father has been established;
do not make any further parent change until a source identifies the specific
replacement. Preserve the existing structured birth location until an
evidenced replacement location is available.
