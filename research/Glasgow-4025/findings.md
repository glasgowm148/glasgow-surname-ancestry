# Findings: [Eliza Glasgow (Glasgow-4025)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4025)

## Current conclusion

Eliza was born 28 August 1814 in County Tyrone and married [William Glasgow (Glasgow-1969)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1969) at Auburn, New York, on 4 August 1836. The 1888 Hillsdale biography proves that Glasgow was her birth surname. `Unknown-571955` now redirectss to the corrected profile.

## Source findings

The [1888 Hillsdale biography, p. 395](https://archive.org/details/portraitbiograph00chpm/page/395/mode/1up) says Eliza came to the United States with two brothers at about age eighteen and distinguishes her Glasgow family from William's.

## Parentage and sibling assessment

Eliza's parents remain unknown. [Robert Glasgow (Glasgow-1171)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1171), born about 1809 in County Tyrone and later living in Cayuga County, is a plausible brother candidate, but no source proves the relationship. Do not attach him without direct family evidence.
