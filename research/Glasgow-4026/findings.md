# Findings: Margaret (Glasgow) Harkness of Moneymore

Last updated: 2026-08-20

## Current conclusion

WikiTree profile [Glasgow-4026](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4026)
was created on 20 August 2026. Margaret is connected to husband
[Harkness-2200](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Harkness-2200) and son
[Harkness-2201](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Harkness-2201).

A published 1892 biographical register identifies Margaret as born at
Moneymore, County Londonderry; her husband James as born at Oritor, County
Tyrone; and their son William as born at Jacksonville, Illinois. Margaret's
displayed c.1820 birth is only a working estimate required by WikiTree. Her
exact dates and parents remain unknown.

## Evidence significance

This is positive FAN-network evidence that Glasgow and Harkness families in
the Cookstown-Moneymore district intermarried. It modestly strengthens the
plausibility of another Glasgow-Harkness marriage but does not identify Mary
Harkness or prove her proposed marriage to `Glasgow-1024`.
