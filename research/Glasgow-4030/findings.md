The association remains positive but does not establish her father. John of Peters Township remains a probable brother at 83%; Faris p.15 also reconstructs the four brothers from the 1779–1780 tax/probate cluster. These sibling arguments do not identify their father. The 1824 Samuel will has been read only through Faris’s excerpt, not its manuscript. The Irish Robert in that will/pedigree is not identified as the emigrant Robert born in 1749.
