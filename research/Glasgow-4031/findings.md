# Findings: [John Glasgow (Glasgow-4031)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4031)

## Current conclusion

Glasgow-4031 represents John Glasgow of Drumgreenagh, County Armagh, farmer,
who was married to Jane Murphy and transacted Lislea property in 1786. He is
distinct from Glasgow-3439, who was born about 1794. No relationship to that
younger John or to Nathaniel Glasgow has been proved.

| [Registry of Deeds memorial, deed dated 13 February 1786 and registered 22 December 1792](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSJW-VDTD) | John Glasgow of Drumgreenagh, farmer, was the husband of Jane Murphy, daughter of the late Thomas Murphy. A one-fifth share of Thomas's Lislea property had fallen to Jane. David Murphy assigned John 2 acres, 1 rood and 35 perches at Lislea for GBP25. William Henry and Nathaniel Glasgow of Drumgreenagh witnessed the transaction. | Direct evidence for John's residence, occupation, wife and land transaction; direct evidence for Jane's father. ## Identity assessment

- Not John Glasgow of Peters Township, who died in 1779.
- Not Glasgow-3439, who was born about 1794.
- Possibly the John Glasgow of Drumgreenagh in the 1828 probate index, but the
  shared name and residence do not yet prove continuity over forty-two years.
- Possibly an older relative of Glasgow-3439 and Nathaniel Glasgow-4014; no
  parent-child or sibling wording has been found.

## Recommended action

Keep John linked to wife Jane Murphy (Murphy-41989). Do not attach children or
parents until a record states the relationship.
