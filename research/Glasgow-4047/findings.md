# Findings: [Eliza (Glasgow) Montgomery (Glasgow-4047)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4047)

Last updated: 2026-08-25

## Current conclusion

Eliza Glasgow was born about 1833 in County Antrim. She was aged 21, unmarried
and living at Liminary when she married William Montgomery at Ballymena on
21 August 1854.

The original register names her father as [[Glasgow-3956|Hugh Glasgow]], a
labourer and weaver. This is a direct primary-source statement, and the
father-child relationship is marked **confident** on WikiTree. Her mother is
not named.
