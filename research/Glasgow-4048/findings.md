# Findings: [Hugh Glasgow (Glasgow-4048)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4048)

Last updated: 2026-08-25

## Current conclusion

Hugh Glasgow was born about 1829 in County Antrim. On 24 December 1852 he was
a 23-year-old bachelor, weaver and resident of Liminary when he married Anne
Cushnehan, widow Wilson.

The original marriage register directly names his father as
[[Glasgow-3956|Hugh Glasgow]], weaver. The father-child relationship is marked
**confident** on WikiTree.

## Family and residence

Anne died at Liminary on 14 March 1877. She was recorded as married and as a
weaver's wife, demonstrating that this couple remained associated with
Liminary. Nathaniel Glasgow was present at her death, but the register does not
state his relationship.

## Identity assessment

This man is distinct from [[Glasgow-2998]], who was unmarried in 1901, and from
[[Glasgow-2599]], who had formed a family in Nova Scotia by 1876 while Anne
remained a married weaver's wife at Liminary in 1877.

A widowed weaver named Hugh Glasgow died in the Ballymena district on 8 June
1889, aged 55. He is a strong candidate, but the death entry does not securely
bridge him to the 1852 marriage, so that death remains unproved for this profile.
