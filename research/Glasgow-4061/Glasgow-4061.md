## Correction — 22 September 2026

Re-reading the adjacent printed name/date rows on p. 196 showed that the
17 April 1643 date belongs to “John, in Rashie [transcript: Rashlic], par. of
Kilbride,” not “John, in Bog of Steivinstoun.” The Bog entry is dated
6 November 1685 and is already recorded as NRS `CC9/7/47`. The 1643 Rashie
entry is not identified with Glasgow-4061. Removed the unsupported Bog-source
match and pre-1643 death bound from the live profile; it now records death
after 9 February 1571, the last-known-alive bound established by GD39/5/59.
The correction and new unlinked record are documented in the [FamilySearch
source assessment](../../surname-research/pre-1700/familysearch-full-text-glasgow-scotland-1699-2026-09-22.md).
