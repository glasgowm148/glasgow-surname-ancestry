# Findings: [John Glasgow (Glasgow-4061), son of James elder of Corsinkell](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4061)

## Conclusion

This is the record-defined John Glasgow expressly called a son of
[[Glasgow-1168|James Glasgow elder]] in the 9 February 1571 Corsinkell record.
The relationship is **direct and confident**. The same wording makes
[[Glasgow-3921|James Glasgow younger]] his brother.

Identity with [[Glasgow-3193|the John recorded at Burrowland and Dalgarven in
1559-60]] remains possible, not proved. No reviewed estate deed bridges those
holdings to Corsinkell, so the profiles remain separate.

## Source

National Records of Scotland, Charter Chest of the Earls of Glencairn,
[GD39/5/59](https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/details.aspx?reference=GD39%2F5%2F59),
letters of reversion, 9 February 1571. The official catalogue describes the
40-shilling land of Corsinkell as occupied by “James Glasgow, elder James
Glasgow and John Glasgow, his sons, Christian Campbell.” Catalogue entry
inspected 30 August 2026; original open but off site, no online image.
