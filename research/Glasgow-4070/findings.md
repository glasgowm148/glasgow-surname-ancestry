# Findings: Glasgow-4070

Last updated: 2026-09-17

## Current conclusion

[Nancy Glasgow (Glasgow-4070)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4070),
unmarried, "about 68" when she made her will at Plattsburgh, New York, on
15 January 1889 (born c.1820–21), died on 31 October 1891 and her will was
proved on 9 May 1892. Irish-born, she was a long-term servant in the Hiram and
Delia Walworth household in Plattsburgh (1860, 1870, 1880 censuses); Hiram
Walworth acted as her attorney in 1885 and executor in 1892.

**Her family of origin is the Rasharkin-parish Glasgow family of Lisnagaver.**
Her will names Margaret (Dysart) Kennedy as her niece and Robert Anderson as her
nephew; the Rasharkin Presbyterian register records Margaret as the daughter of
James Dysart and Jane Glascaw (Granagh, 1846) and Robert as the son of James
Anderson and Betty Glascaw (Drumack, 1834). The 14 January 1892 probate citation
— which New York law required to be addressed to her heirs and next of kin —
names no sibling directly and lists only the children of named people: John
Glasgow late of "Baladonly" (Ballydonnelly), Alexander Glasgow formerly of
Rasharkin, Robert Glasgow, Jane (Glasgow) Dysart, Elizabeth (Glasgow) Anderson
(sons at Gortahar), Ann McClelland and Mary Thompson. Read as the legal document
it is, that is the list of nieces and nephews of a woman all of whose siblings
were dead, and the seven named people were her brothers and sisters.

Consequences:

- Nancy was the **sister** of [Alexander Glasgow (Glasgow-951)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-951)
  of Lisnagaver (c.1811–1888), whose children James, Mary and Ann are the three
  the citation names, and of [John Glasgow (Glasgow-867)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-867)
  (c.1801–1885), whose children James Garvin, Ann and Elizabeth Ann are the
  "James G., Ann and Lizzie" of the citation.
- Her father was therefore the man John-867 named in his 1846 marriage return:
  **John Glasgow, farmer**. Status: plausible (indirect, conflict-free); no
  record names Nancy's or Alexander's father directly. The 1796 Desertoghill
  flax-grower placeholder [Glasgow-4090](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4090)
  represents that father on WikiTree and is not an identified individual.
- Her current WikiTree attachment to [Alexander Glasgow (Glasgow-3904)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3904)
  and Rosanna is **contradicted**: that couple is known only as the traditional
  parents of [Thomas Glasgow (Glasgow-987)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-987),
  and Thomas's adult children at Lisnagaver are absent from the citation while
  nieces in New Zealand are named. Thomas (and his proved brother Daniel-3896)
  were not Nancy's siblings. Recommended structured change: replace the
  uncertain parents Glasgow-3904/Unknown-761034 with uncertain father
  Glasgow-4090 in one edit, for Nancy and for Elizabeth-4071; propose the same to
  the manager of Jane (Glasglow-11).

Full reasoning, the reconstructed sibling chronology (Samuel c.1790 …
Nancy c.1820–21, implying two marriages of the father) and the exclusions are
maintained in `research/Glasgow-951/findings.md`, section *Nancy Glasgow's
next-of-kin citation and Alexander's father*.
