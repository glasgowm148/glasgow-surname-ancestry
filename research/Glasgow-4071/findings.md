# Findings: Glasgow-4071

Last updated: 2026-10-06

## Current conclusion

[Elizabeth (Glasgow) Anderson (Glasgow-4071)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4071), wife of James Anderson, **died at Gortahar, Rasharkin parish, on 7 September 1868 aged 66 (born c.1802)**; the civil death entry names James Anderson ([FamilySearch index QPTW-QQXN](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QPTW-QQXN), "Ireland, Civil Registration, 1845–1913"; image not online — GRONI holds the register). The WikiTree birth estimate "before 1816" should become **about 1802**, and the death added. Her children by James Anderson: Robert (bapt. Rasharkin 28 July 1834, Drumack; d. Beekmantown NY 23 Aug 1883), Hugh (bapt. 24 Nov 1836, Drumack; d. 21 May 1864 Gortahar per FS Tree G313-JQQ, unsourced), David (c.1830–11 Apr 1914, Gortahar; unmarried), James, and Samuel (c.1845–7 Dec 1925, Gortahar; m. Mary Ann, nine children). The 1892 probate citation of her sister [Nancy Glasgow (Glasgow-4070)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4070) lists "David Anderson, James Anderson and Samuel Anderson living at Gortahar" among Nancy's heirs or next of kin; Nancy's will calls Robert her nephew.

Her parents are not named in any located record. Nancy's will explicitly calls
Robert her nephew, and his baptism identifies his mother as Betty Glascaw;
this supports Elizabeth's sister relationship to Nancy. The broader citation
network probably joins Alexander-951, John-867, Jane, Robert, Ann McClelland
and Mary Thompson, but does not state all those relationships or Elizabeth's
name. John-867's recorded father, John Glasgow, farmer (represented by
Glasgow-4090), is a candidate for Elizabeth's father only if the relevant
siblings shared that parent. The 1796 Desertoghill flax recipient is a separate
unproved identity. Alexander-3904 and Rosanna remain an unsupported tradition;
absence of Thomas's children from the citation does not disprove it.

**21 September 2026 — probate evidence audit.** Retained the explicit will and
baptism kin evidence; withdrew the automatic shared-father inference and
categorical exclusion. See the rechecked newspaper transcription in
`research/Glasgow-4070/sources/` and the Alexander dossier. No automatic

to test the extended network and full/half relationships.

## Source findings

### James Anderson's death — 6 October 2026

[Civil death register, Dirraw registrar's district, 1888, entry 333](https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/deaths_returns/deaths_1888/06179/4766465.pdf): **James Anderson**, widower, retired farmer, reported age 94, died at **Gortahar on 18 April 1888** of debility of three years' standing, with no medical attendant. His son James Anderson of Gortahar, who signed by mark, was present at death and registered it on 4 May 1888. The image was read on 6 October 2026.

The townland, widowed status, age, occupation and son's name identify him at high confidence as Elizabeth's husband (Anderson-104743). The entry does not name his late wife. The age implies birth about 1793–94. The informant is the son James (Anderson-104794), until now known only from the 1892 citation. Elizabeth's probable brother Alexander (Glasgow-951) died in the same townland on 16 May 1888.

## Identity assessment — "Charlotte Glascow", baptism entry 138

The [Rasharkin Presbyterian baptism transcription](https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/RasharkinP/Baptisms_1834.html), entry 138, between entries dated March 1839 and August 1842, reads: Jane Anderson, daughter of Jas Anderson and **Charlotte Glascow**, Drumack. Entries 04 and 63 name James Anderson's wife at Drumack as Betty or Elisabeth Glascaw, and Elizabeth lived until 1868. A slip for Elizabeth is the likeliest explanation; a second Anderson–Glasgow couple at Drumack is not excluded. Do not add Jane as Elizabeth's daughter until the original register (PRONI MIC1P/292) has been read.
