== Biography ==

'''Daniel Glasgow''' was a farmer of the Rasharkin area of County Antrim, born
about 1817. He died at Culmore, a townland in Rasharkin civil parish adjoining
Lisnagaver, on 14 December 1867, aged 50, and his death was registered in the
Ballymoney superintendent registrar's district.<ref name="DanielDeath1867">General
Register Office of Ireland, civil death register, Ballymoney district, 14
December 1867: Daniel Glasgow, aged 50, Culmore. "Ireland, Civil Registration,
1845–1913", index record
[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QP3S-P6DL QP3S-P6DL]
(register image not online; GRONI holds the original).</ref>

No marriage between 1845 and 1867 or any later family has been found for him,
and no Glasgow remained at Culmore in the 1901 or 1911 censuses.

He is the natural identity for the '''Daniel Glasgow''' occupier at Gortereghy,
Rasharkin, in Griffith's Valuation (c.1861), where a Daniel and a Robert
Glasgow held land adjoining the Lisnagaver holdings of Alexander, John and
Thomas Glasgow.<ref name="GriffithRasharkin">[https://www.failteromhat.com/griffiths/antrim/rasharkin.htm
Griffith's Valuation, Rasharkin parish], Glasgow occupiers: Alexander, John and
Thomas at Lisnagaver; Daniel and Robert at Gortereghy; James at Dromore.</ref>
This identification is probable, not proved: the valuation gives no age,
occupation or relatives, and the Kilrea watchmaker [[Glasgow-3896|Daniel
Glasgow]] (died 1871) was formerly proposed for the entry.

== Research Notes ==

Daniel's relationship to the other Rasharkin Glasgows is unresolved. His c.1817
birth and presence in the parish make him age-compatible with a brother or
cousin of [[Glasgow-951|Alexander Glasgow]] (c.1811) and [[Glasgow-987|Thomas
Glasgow]] (c.1815), but nothing yet names his parents or kin. Do not attach a
father.

It is not known whether he left children: if childless, his absence from the
1892 next-of-kin citation of [[Glasgow-4070|Nancy Glasgow]] is uninformative;
if he had children who were overlooked, he would fall outside her sibling
group. Keep the sibling status open.
