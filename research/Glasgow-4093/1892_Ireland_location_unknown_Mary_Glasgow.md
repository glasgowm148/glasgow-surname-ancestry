== Biography ==

'''Mary Glasgow''' married a man named '''Thompson''' and had children, but no
record of her own birth, marriage or death has been located and her husband's
forename is unknown.

She is identified solely by the probate citation in the estate of
[[Glasgow-4070|Nancy Glasgow]] of Plattsburgh, New York. The citation, dated
14 January 1892, lists "the children of Mary Thompson whose names and places of
residence are unknown" among Nancy's heirs and next of kin, in the same group
as the children of John, Alexander and Robert Glasgow, of Jane (Glasgow)
Dysart, of Elizabeth (Glasgow) Anderson (sons "living at Gortahar"), and of Ann
McClelland.<ref name="Citation1892">''The Plattsburgh Sentinel'', 26 February
1892, p. 6, legal notices (citation dated 14 January 1892, returnable 9 May
1892).
[https://www.nyshistoricnewspapers.org/?a=d&d=plts18920226-01.1.6 newspaper page].
A second printing appeared in ''The Plattsburgh Republican'', 23 and 30 January
and 13 February 1892.</ref>

A New York probate citation of this period had to be directed to the heirs and
next of kin of the testator. Nancy was unmarried, about 68 when she made her
will in 1889, and had no living parent, so her next of kin were her brothers and
sisters and the children of deceased brothers and sisters. The citation's own
wording — "…the children of Mary Thompson…; to all other heirs or next of kin
of Nancy Glasgow, deceased, if any; to [the legatees]" — places Mary in that
next-of-kin class. She is therefore understood to have been a deceased sister
of Nancy, and a daughter of the sibling group's father (see Research Notes).

== Research Notes ==

This is the thinnest of the sibling profiles. Mary is not named in any located
Irish record; her maiden name Glasgow, her parentage and even her Irish
residence are inferences from the structure of the probate citation and from
her relatedness to Nancy. No dates should be added without a record.

A civil death of a Mary Thompson, widow of William Thompson, farmer, at
Tannybrake, Connor parish, on 7 March 1888 aged 73 is an age-compatible
candidate but is NOT proved to be this Mary; it is noted only as a lead and
must not be attached.
