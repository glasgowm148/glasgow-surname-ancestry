# Findings: [Marion Glasgow of Corsoun (Glasgow-4095)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4095)

## Current conclusion

WikiTree parent-status audit, 27 September 2026: Andrew Glasgow (Glasgow-3163) is now marked confident as Marion’s father. His 1597/8 testament directly names Marion among his daughters; Elizat Stevinsoun’s maternity remains separately uncertain.

Andrew Glasgow's 1597/8 testament directly names a daughter Marion. This
profile represents that record-defined child. Her birth year and Corsoun birth
place are estimates, not facts supplied by the testament. Her later life is
unidentified. The mother link to Elizat Stevinsoun should remain uncertain:
the testament directly identifies Elizat as mother of son Robert, but does not
separately state maternity for the daughters.

[[Glasgow-3062|Marion Glasgow, sister of Ninian]] is a possible namesake, but
Ninian's 1613 testament identifies her as his sister and the 1610 baptism
identifies her Fultoun household. Neither record links her to Andrew or Corsoun.
**Possible identity only; do not merge without a connecting record.** The live
biography was cross-linked and verified 26 September 2026.
