# Findings: [Isobel Glasgow of Corsoun, merged into Glasgow-3336](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3336)

## Current conclusion

Andrew Glasgow's 1597/8 testament directly names a daughter Isobel. The
record-defined Glasgow-4097 was merged on 27 September 2026 into
[[Glasgow-3336|Issobell Glasgow, wife of William Symountoun]], the leading
compatible Edinburgh identity. The live redirect and merged biography were
verified. The identification is **probable**, since the 1619 marriage and 1620
baptism give no father or Corsoun residence. Both structured parent statuses
on the survivor are uncertain; the testament does not separately name Elizat
as this daughter's mother. The 1597 birth estimate and Edinburgh birthplace
are not birth-register facts.
