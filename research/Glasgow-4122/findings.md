## Relationship assessment — father profile corrected, 18 September 2026

The 1726 baptism names Sarah's parents only as John Glasgow and Agnes Ferguson. The 18 September 2026 batch log records that a wrong merge of Glasgow-4163 (the adult John) into [Glasgow-1139](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1139) had made Sarah's father the John baptised at Irvine on 27 June 1721, who would have been about five in 1726, and had made Agnes Ferguson both his mother and his wife. The log records that Sarah was then re-parented to [John Glasgow MA (Glasgow-1077)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1077), the Provost, born 1690, husband of [Agnes Ferguson (Ferguson-15168)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Ferguson-15168). The same couple are named as parents of Marion Glasgow, baptised at Irvine on 10 March 1724 ([Glasgow-3425](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3425)).

Source: Irvine OPR baptism, 3 July 1726, [Findmypast transcript SCOT/OPR/BAP/3574134](https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3574134&tab=this) (transcription; original image not inspected). The record does not say which John Glasgow was the father. The attachment to Glasgow-1077 rests on the parents' names, place and chronology, and on ruling out a father born in 1721. It is a reasoned identification, not direct proof, and the batch log gives it no grade.
