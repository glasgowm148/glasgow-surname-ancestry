== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' was probably born about 1674. This is an uncertain estimate from his entry into an apprenticeship in 1690; his age was not recorded. His birthplace is unknown. Lindsayland in Biggar parish is usable only as an uncertain location inferred from his father's stated residence.

On 7 May 1690 James, son of the deceased James Glasgow, weaver in Lindsayland, was entered as apprentice to William Brown, a cordiner and tanner in Portsburgh and an Edinburgh burgess. The entry was scored out.<ref name="Apprentice1690">Henry Paton, ed., [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/edinburgh_apprentices_~_1666_to_1700_~_1929.pdf ''Register of Apprentices of the City of Edinburgh, 1666–1700''] (Scottish Record Society, 1929), printed p. 37, entry dated 7 May 1690; see also Findmypast, [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FEDINBURGH%2FAPPRENTICES%2F0005159%2F1&tab=this transcript SCOT/EDINBURGH/APPRENTICES/0005159/1], captured 3 September 2026.</ref>

The scoring-out shows that this apprenticeship was cancelled or did not proceed in its entered form. It does not show that James died.

== Research Notes ==

The father is probably the James Glasgow, weaver at Back of Lindsaylands, whose testament was registered in the Lanark Commissary Court on 10 November 1680.<ref>Francis J. Grant, ed., [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/the_commissariot_record_of_lanark__register_of_testaments_1595-1800_~_1903.pdf ''The Commissariot Record of Lanark: Register of Testaments, 1595–1800''] (Scottish Record Society, 1903), printed p. 38.</ref> The exact name, occupation, distinctive locality and prior death make that documentary identity strong, but the testament must be inspected before attaching a profile or naming the apprentice's mother.

This James is not the apprentice entered in 1696: the 1690 father was already dead, whereas the 1696 father was a living weaver and burgess. [[Glasgow-3076|James Glasgow]], born at Edinburgh in 1661, is excluded because his profile supplies no Lindsayland or correct-father bridge and he would have been unusually old for this entry.
