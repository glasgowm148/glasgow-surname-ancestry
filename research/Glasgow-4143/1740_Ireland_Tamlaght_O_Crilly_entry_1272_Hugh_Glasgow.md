== Biography ==

'''Hugh Glasgow''' was one of two distinct men of that name recorded as
Protestant householders in Tamlaght O'Crilly parish, County Londonderry, in
1740. In the surviving collector-order transcription this Hugh is entry
'''1272''', between Henry Lennox and Hugh Ellis and immediately followed by
James McCullough.<ref name="Householders1740">PRONI T808/15258, ''Names of
14,400 Protestant householders'', Tamlaght O'Crilly, 1740, pp. 108-109;
Richard John Torrens,
[https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/books/Kilrea/Kilrea07.html
collector-order transcription], entry 1272; W. Macafee,
[https://www.billmacafee.com/1740returns/1740religiousreturnsulstername.pdf
surname transcription], recording the townland as "Not specified".</ref>

The other householder, entry 1195, is represented by
[[Glasgow-3931|Hugh Glasgow]]. The two entries establish separate households;
they do not state that the men were related.<ref name="Householders1740" />

== Research Notes ==

=== Possible existing profile ===

[[Glasgow-548|Hugh Glasgow]] is the leading possible identity. A much later
family history says that he moved from County Tyrone to County Londonderry,
worked for a McCullough and married Margaret McCullough. The position of entry
1272 immediately before Hugh Ellis and James McCullough is compatible with that
account.<ref name="FamilyHistory">[[Glasgow-590|William Melanchthon Glasgow]],
[https://www.wikitree.com/photo.php/0/0a/Glasgow-590.pdf ''History of the Glasgow
Family''], pp. 2-3.</ref>

That is not proof of identity. Create entry 1272 as an unconnected,
record-defined person rather than assigning the event to [[Glasgow-548]]. The
1740 return gives no age, origin, occupation,
wife or child; collector order can cross localities; and the family account was
written roughly 160 years later and contains demonstrated chronological errors.
Do not merge the new profile into [[Glasgow-548]] or attach relatives without a lease, rental,
church record, will or other source that supplies identifying family details.

Hugh's actual birthplace is unknown. Tamlaght O'Crilly is suitable only as an
uncertain creation-field location inferred from the parish household in which
the 1740 return directly recorded him; the return does not name a townland.

=== Relationship to the Inishrush family ===

The entry is the strongest record-defined earlier-generation occurrence in the
local cluster alongside [[Glasgow-3931|the other 1740 Hugh]]. It is biologically
possible that either man was the father or grandfather of
[[Glasgow-3902|Adam Glasgow]] or a relative of [[Glasgow-822|John Glasgow]]. No
source states such a relationship. Do not attach [[Glasgow-3905|the former
unknown-father placeholder]] beneath either Hugh.
