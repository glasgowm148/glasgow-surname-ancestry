== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' was described as a gentleman of Oritor, County Tyrone, in an Armagh diocesan administration-bond index entry dated 1826.<ref name="Oritor1826">Public Record Office of Northern Ireland, [https://apps.proni.gov.uk/ProniNames_IE/SearchPage.aspx PRONI Names], ''Armagh Diocesan Administration Bonds'', search result for James Glasgow, gentleman, Oritor, 1826, accessed 7 September 2026. The result's item-detail URL is session-bound and the original administration bond does not survive.</ref>

An administration bond followed a death, but the index year is not necessarily James's exact death year. The entry proves his name, recorded status and Oritor association; it does not identify his age, birthplace, wife, children or parents.<ref name="Oritor1826" />

A later family statement preserved as PRONI T1959/3 supplies the family context missing from the probate index. Its unnamed writer, born five months after her father's death in July 1801, described her maternal grandparents as William Glasgow of Drumore and Anne Rutherford. She said that they had one surviving son, James, and three surviving daughters, including her mother Margaret. She later referred to “my uncle James of Orritor” and said that he died unmarried.<ref name="T1959">Public Record Office of Northern Ireland, T1959/3, photocopy of an undated and unsigned Glasgow–Rutherford family letter preserved with papers of the 1890s; online transcription by Albert Rutherford reproduced by Gary Harding, [https://www.genealogy.com/forum/surnames/topics/rutherford/5445/ “Rutherfords and Glasgows in Co. Tyrone, Ireland”], 9 January 2005.</ref>

The letter identifies the writer's father as John Glasgow, ordained at Coleraine and dead in July 1801. A Presbyterian-ministers compilation independently identifies John Glasgow of Orritor as the minister ordained at New Row, Coleraine, on 8 March 1796 and records his death on 13 July 1801.<ref name="RevJohn">[https://cotyrone.com/churchrecord/ministers-pres.html “Presbyterian Ministers in Co. Tyrone”], entry for John Glasgow of Orritor and New Row, Coleraine, citing his ordination on 8 March 1796 and death on 13 July 1801.</ref> This corroborates the letter's immediate setting. The matching rare locality, name, death and unmarried status make James the strongest and probable identity for the gentleman whose administration bond was indexed in 1826.<ref name="Oritor1826" /><ref name="T1959" />

== Research Notes ==

Oritor and neighbouring Killycurragh contained several men named James Glasgow. The probate index alone could not distinguish this man; the T1959/3 family letter does so by describing an unmarried maternal uncle James of Orritor in the correct generation. The identification remains probable because the letter is undated and unsigned and the surviving transcription has difficult words, while the original administration bond no longer survives.

The St Luran register records a different James Glasgow living at Orritor Craigs in 1829, when a child was baptised to him and Mary Anne of Drumcann.<ref name="Derryloran1829">Representative Church Body Library, [https://www.churchofireland.org/cmsfiles/pdf/AboutUs/library/AngRecord/DerryloranVol1.pdf ''Derryloran Parish Register, Volume 1''], baptism no. 466, 5 April 1829.</ref> That later householder cannot be the man whose administration bond was entered in 1826, although he may belong to the same local network.

=== Duplicate audit ===

The local One Tree export and live WikiTree data were searched again in September 2026. The automated name-and-county comparison returned several profiles, but none is an established identity match:

* [[Glasgow-1701|James Glasgow junior]] died at Killycurragh in 1875 and is therefore not the deceased man represented by the 1826 administration bond.
* [[Glasgow-1702|James Glasgow of Ballynasollus]] died in 1882 and is likewise excluded.
* [[Glasgow-3196|James Glasgow, farmer in County Tyrone]] is represented as the father of a son born about 1836, based on that son's 1859 marriage. If the attachment and estimated birth year are correct, he cannot be the man deceased by 1826; in any event, the profile supplies no Oritor residence or gentleman-status bridge.
* [[Glasgow-1970|James Glasgow near Cookstown]] and [[Glasgow-2989|James Glasgow of Derryloran]] are chronologically possible older Tyrone men with no proved death, but neither profile has a sourced Oritor residence or matching occupation. Locality and name alone do not justify attaching the estate.
* [[Glasgow-1165|James Glasgow]] belongs to the separately documented family of John Glasgow and Mary Arthur and has no Oritor connection. His 1823 family-Bible death alone would not exclude a delayed administration, but his incompatible relationship-defined family does.

The T1959/3 family structure does not match any of those profiles. No searched profile represents an unmarried son of William Glasgow of Drumore and Anne Rutherford or an uncle of the writer born in 1801. No compatible WikiTree profile remains, so a new unconnected profile is appropriate.
