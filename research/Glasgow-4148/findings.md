## Relationship assessment — daughter of Hiram Wilson Glasgow, 13 August 2026

Cleora's obituary abstract calls the Reverend Leonidas K. Glasgow her brother ([Newberry obituary abstracts](https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/observer/1906-7s.htm), a derivative abstract), and a biographical account names Leonidas as a son of Hiram Wilson Glasgow and Mary Ann Brandon Kennedy ([News of Davidson, 8 February 2025](https://newsofdavidson.org/2025/02/08/85148/davidson-history-the-reverend-leonidas-k-glasgow/), a secondary account). A marriage notice says she married Leland McNary Speers on 17 October 1867 at the residence of her mother, Mrs M. A. Glasgow ([early Newberry marriage notices](https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/marriages_early.html)). Together these **strongly support** [Hiram Wilson Glasgow (Glasgow-3995)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3995) as her father; no obituary, Bible or probate statement naming Hiram as her father has been found. A 1927 death notice gives her death on 12 July 1927 aged about 80, from which her birth about 1847 is estimated.
