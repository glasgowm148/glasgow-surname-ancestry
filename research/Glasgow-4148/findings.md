Her father was probably Hiram Wilson Glasgow (Glasgow-3995), an inference from three sources: her mother's initials fit Hiram's wife Mary Ann Brandon Kennedy, a Newberry Observer abstract of 1906–1907 calls the Reverend Leonidas K. Glasgow her brother, and a secondary account names Leonidas as Hiram's son.
