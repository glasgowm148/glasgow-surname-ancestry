== Biography ==

'''Agnes Glasgow''' was a lawful child of [[Glasgow-4152|John Glasgow]], a sailor in Saltcoats
within Ardrossan parish. She and [[Glasgow-4153|Katherine Glasgow]] were named in their
father's testament dative and inventory when his estate came before Glasgow
Commissary Court in April 1637. Their father had died in December 1636.<ref name="JohnTestament">National Records of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary Court,
testament dative and inventory of John Glasgow, sailor in Saltcoats, NRS
CC9/7/27, register pp. 478–480; death in December 1636 and court proceedings in
April 1637; original register re-read September 2026; locate by reference
through the [https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/search-records/legal-records/wills
Scotland's People wills search].</ref>

The proceeding calls Agnes and Katherine the lawful children of the deceased,
their father. It names '''Isobell Gray''' as John's widow and records that she
gave up the inventory on the children's behalf. This proves Agnes's father and
Isobell's marriage to him, but does not expressly call Isobell Agnes's
mother.<ref name="JohnTestament" />

== Research Notes ==

The testament does not state Agnes's age, birthplace, marriage or death.
Saltcoats is an uncertain creation-field birthplace inferred from her father's
residence, not a stated fact. The document's use of ''bairn'' proves a
parent-child relationship; it does not by itself establish that Agnes was a
young child in 1637.

[[Glasgow-1030]] has been excluded as the present identity of her father: his
documented Glasgow-burgh household has no Saltcoats, sailor, Isobell Gray,
Katherine or Agnes bridge. Create the distinct Saltcoats John first and attach
Agnes to that new profile with certain status. No mother should be attached
merely because Isobell Gray was John's widow and acted for the children.
