## Source findings — the 1637 testament, 28 September 2026

National Records of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary Court, testament dative and inventory of John Glasgow, sailor in Saltcoats, `CC9/7/27`, register pp. 478–480 (original register re-read September 2026): John died in December 1636; in April 1637 the court named Agnes and [Katherine](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4153) as his lawful children, and Isobell Gray, his widow, gave up the inventory on their behalf. The record proves Agnes's father and Isobell's marriage to John, not that Isobell was Agnes's mother; no mother should be attached merely because Isobell acted for the children. [Glasgow-1030](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1030) is excluded as the father: his Glasgow-burgh household has no Saltcoats, sailor, Isobell Gray, Katherine or Agnes bridge.
