== Biography ==

'''Alexander Glasgow''' was named as a defendant in a Cape Supreme Court motion in 1893. The executors of the estate of the late Gerhard Myburgh brought the motion.<ref name="MyburghMotion">National Archives and Records Service of South Africa, NAAIRS index, Cape Town Archives Repository (<code>KAB</code>), Cape Supreme Court (<code>CSC</code>) 2/6/1/146, reference 286, part 1, 1893, “Motion. Executors of estate late Gerhard Myburgh versus Alexander Glasgow”; searched through the [https://www.nationalarchives.gov.za/node/737 NARSSA guidance and NAAIRS access page], 13 August 2026.</ref>

Five years later, Maria Welch brought an illiquid action against a same-named Alexander Glasgow for recovery of money.<ref name="WelchAction">National Archives and Records Service of South Africa, NAAIRS index, Cape Town Archives Repository (<code>KAB</code>), Cape Supreme Court (<code>CSC</code>) 2/1/1/350, reference 123, part 1, 1898, “Illiquid case. Maria Welch versus Alexander Glasgow. Action for the recovery of money”; searched through the [https://www.nationalarchives.gov.za/node/737 NARSSA guidance and NAAIRS access page], 13 August 2026.</ref>

The exact name and same court make one recurring defendant probable, but the index does not prove that the two files concern the same man. The 1893 action alone establishes Alexander's existence and adult legal role; the 1898 matter is retained as a probable later occurrence. Neither index gives an age, birthplace, residence, occupation, parent, spouse or child.<ref name="MyburghMotion" /><ref name="WelchAction" />

== Research Notes ==

The live WikiTree <code>searchPerson</code> results for Alexander Glasgow were audited on 13 August 2026. No result stated a South African event or supplied a bridge to the Cape files. Chronologically possible profiles had documented lives elsewhere or no identifying South African evidence.

Alexander's actual birthplace is unknown. Cape Colony is suitable only as an
uncertain creation-field location inferred from his own first documented adult
appearance there. The repository's location in Cape Town does not establish
that Alexander lived or was born in Cape Town.

[[Glasgow-2702|Alexander Glasgow of Dundee]] is excluded by his documented Scottish life and 1894 death in Dundee. [[Glasgow-3797|Alexander McNutt Glasgow]] is excluded by his Virginia life and 1894 death in Rockbridge County. The Australian soldier described below did not reach Cape Town until 1899. With those candidates excluded, no compatible live profile remains.

A documented Australian namesake, born in Queensland on 19 November 1878,
first arrived at Cape Town with the 1st Queensland Mounted Infantry on
13 December 1899. His age and arrival date exclude him from both court files.<ref>Virtual War Memorial Australia, [https://vwma.org.au/explore/people/783892 “Alexander Glasgow”], citing the Australian War Memorial Boer War nominal roll and National Archives of Australia service dossier; accessed 13 August 2026.</ref>

A 1943 Transvaal estate-index entry for Eliza Glasgow says that her spouse Alexander Glasgow predeceased her (<code>TAB MHG 913/43</code>). Do not combine him with the Cape defendant unless Eliza's death notice or the court files supply matching identity details.

[[Glasgow-987|Thomas Glasgow of Lisnagaver]] reportedly had a son Alexander who emigrated to South Africa or the United States and was probably born about 1851-1863. The Cape defendant is the strongest destination candidate yet found, but exact name and country are insufficient to attach a father. Keep this profile unconnected even if the two case files prove one identity, unless an original record supplies an Irish origin or family bridge.

This profile is defined by the 1893 case. If the original 1898 file later proves a different man, remove only that probable occurrence; the 1893 profile remains valid.
