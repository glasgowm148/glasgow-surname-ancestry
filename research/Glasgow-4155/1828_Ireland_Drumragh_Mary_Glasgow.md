# New-profile draft: Mary Glasgow of Drumragh, mother of Samuel Speir

'''Mary Glasgow''' was the mother of Samuel Speir, who married Margaret Murphy
on 4 December 1828 in First Kilrea Presbyterian Church. The marriage register
describes Samuel as the son of William Speir and Mary Glasgow of Drumragh near
Ahoghill. Margaret was the daughter of Andrew Murphy and Mary Hastie of
Inishrush.

<ref>Richard John Torrens, [https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/KilreaP1/Marriages/1828.html "First Kilrea Presbyterian marriages, 1828"], entry for Samuel Speir and Margaret Murphy, 4 December 1828.</ref>

The record directly establishes Mary's name, son and Drumragh association. It
does not state whether Drumragh was her current residence, identify her parents,
give her age, or clarify whether William Speir was living. Her birth is estimated
as before about 1792 solely because she had a son old enough to marry in 1828.

== Research Notes ==

The record proves Mary's name, son and Drumragh association. It does not identify her parents, state whether Drumragh was her residence, give her age, or explicitly call William Speir her husband. Her birth is estimated before about 1792 solely because Samuel was old enough to marry in 1828.

Mary's actual birthplace is unknown. Drumragh near Ahoghill is suitable only as an uncertain creation-field location supplied by her documented immediate-family context; the marriage transcription does not establish that she was born there or even that she still lived there in 1828.

The live duplicate audit completed on 7 September 2026 initially retained [[Glasgow-3989|Mary Glasgow of Larne]] as a possible County Antrim match. The published transcription of her family monument resolves the comparison: she died on 18 October 1844 aged thirty-seven, so she was born about 1807.<ref name="LarneMonument">Francis Joseph Bigger and Herbert Hughes, [https://archive.org/stream/ulsterjournalofa06ulstiala/ulsterjournalofa06ulstiala_djvu.txt “The Parish Churchyard of Larne,”] ''Ulster Journal of Archaeology'', second series, vol. 6 (1900), p. 47, Glasgow monument.</ref> She could not have been the mother of a son old enough to marry in 1828. The other returned profiles have incompatible lives or no Drumragh, Ahoghill or Speir connection. No compatible WikiTree profile remains.
