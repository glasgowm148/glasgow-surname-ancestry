== Biography ==
James Glasgow is named as a father in two Irvine, Ayrshire, baptism entries. [[Glasgow-4129|James Glasgow]] was baptised on 11 April 1721 with parents James Glasgow and [[Rae-3513|Margaret Rae]]. [[Glasgow-4120|William Glasgow]] was baptised on 3 February 1723 with parents James Glasgow and [[Rae-3515|Marion Rae]].<ref name="James1721">Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564–1929'', Irvine Old Parish Register, [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3573667&tab=this James Glasgow baptism, 11 April 1721]. The individual child profile gives the full indexed entry.</ref><ref name="William1723">Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564–1929'', Irvine Old Parish Register, [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3573825&tab=this William Glasgow baptism, 3 February 1723]. The individual child profile gives the full indexed entry.</ref>

No birth, baptism or death record has been identified for this adult James. His birth before about 1702 is an adult-age estimate from the 1721 child; Irvine is his recorded household locality, not a proved birthplace.

== Research Notes ==
=== Selected uncertain family — 16 October 2026 ===

Select Hugh [[Glasgow-3109]], father with Jean Service at Irvine in 1696, as the very low-confidence working father of James [[Glasgow-4158]], father with Margaret/Marion Rae at Irvine in 1721–1723. This extends an actual local household across a compatible generation. A James born in the later 1690s could be a son of the 1696 household and an adult father by 1721; this is a compatibility example, not a new birth estimate. Hugh’s proposed identity with the 1676 Kilwinning child would make him a young father in the 1690s, which fits that example. The shared uncommon surname, same parish and two successive family generations favour this existing named parent model; no register entry names James’s father.

James could instead be a son of the Irvine John-2475/Isabell Kyle family, Michael-1406’s household, another Ayrshire family, or an unrecorded father. The named James child of John/Isabell indexed in 1711 would be only about ten at the 1721 fatherhood if baptised as an infant, so he is a weaker identity candidate unless an older-baptism explanation is established. James’s before 1702 boundary is an adult-status estimate, not an actual birth. If James were substantially older, Hugh’s proposed 1676 child identity would become less plausible; no date is changed to make either hypothesis fit. The older Hugh/Hew account, its existing uncertain parents, Jean Service/Mary Dean identity sequence and earlier alternatives remain as previously assessed. James’s merged Margaret/Marion Rae household remains a working combined father account; no remarriage or equivalence between the mothers is proved. The 1740 Prosperity of Irvine shipmaster is still a possible occupational identity only, with no transfer of occupation, voyage or trade to this father. Jean Service is not selected as James’s mother solely from Hugh’s co-parentage. The former father profile Glasgow-4164, based on the 1723 William entry, was merged into this profile on 27 September 2026. The same uncommon father name and Rae surname in the same Irvine parish within two years, with no separate identified James, support one adult identity. The maternal given names differ: Margaret and Marion may refer to distinct women or to a transcription/register variation. Both named mothers and both baptism entries are preserved; no marriage, death or remarriage between 1721 and 1723 is asserted without a record.

The birth estimates on the two former profiles were derived only from the children's dates. Compare the original 1721 and 1723 register pages and search Irvine marriage or burial entries for the two Rae names.
