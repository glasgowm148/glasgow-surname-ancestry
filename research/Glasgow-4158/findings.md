## Prior assessment — superseded by merge

A parish baptism at Irvine, Ayrshire names James Glasgow and Margaret Rae as parents of [James Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4129) on 11 April 1721. The indexed entry establishes the recorded parent names and child event, but gives no independently identifying birth or death for this adult. [Glasgow-4164](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4164) represents another James Glasgow named with Marion Rae in the same parish in a baptism dated 3 February 1723. These may represent one father across two households or two same-name men; the present evidence does not decide. The profiles remain cross-linked and unmerged.

## Source findings — newspaper review, 2 October 2026

James Glasgow is named in the Greenock arrival list headed “Greenock, August 12”: “The Prosperity of Irvine, James Glasgow, from Northcopen.” The item was published in The Caledonian Mercury on 18 August 1740, page 3. [Original newspaper scan](https://www.newspapers.com/image/394559372/). The ship–homeport–master–origin format supports reading James as the shipmaster. No age, relatives or personal residence are stated. Irvine is the ship’s homeport and Greenock the reporting locality; neither proves James’s birthplace. Northcopen is retained without an unverified modern-place identification.
