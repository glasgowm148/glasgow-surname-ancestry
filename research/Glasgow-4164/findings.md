## Prior assessment — superseded by merge

A parish baptism at Irvine, Ayrshire names James Glasgow and Marion Rae as parents of [William Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4120) on 3 February 1723. The indexed entry establishes the recorded parent names and child event, but gives no independently identifying birth or death for this adult. [Glasgow-4158](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4158) represents another James Glasgow named with Margaret Rae in the same parish in a baptism dated 11 April 1721. These may represent one father across two households or two same-name men; the present evidence does not decide. The profiles remain cross-linked and unmerged.
