== Biography ==
'''George Glasgow''' lived at Irvine, Ayrshire, where the parish register names him as the father of 3 children baptised there. Their mother was Margt, named in the same entries.

* [[Glasgow-4121|Isabel Glasgow]], baptised 26 Apr 1741
* [[Glasgow-4103|Magdalen Glasgow]], baptised 29 Mar 1743
* [[Glasgow-4126|Mary Glasgow]], baptised 21 Apr 1745

No birth or baptism record for George himself has been found. His birth is placed before 1722, the year by which he must have been born to be old enough to father Isabel in 1741; Irvine is inferred from his children's baptisms and is not independently recorded.<ref name="OPR">Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564–1929'', Irvine, Ayrshire, Old Parish Register; the individual baptism entries are cited on each child's profile, for example [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F3575036&tab=this Isabel Glasgow, 26 Apr 1741].</ref>

== Research Notes ==
=== Selected uncertain family — 16 October 2026 ===

Select John [[Glasgow-1057]] and Agnes [[Spark-330]] as the uncertain working parents of George [[Glasgow-4165]], father with Margt at Irvine in 1741–1745. The preferred model uses possible identity with their son George [[Glasgow-1058]], baptised at Kilwinning on 20 November 1687 and already associated with the George/Margaret Martain couple indexed marrying there on 11 June 1728. That couple’s son George-3312 is indexed at Kilwinning in 1729. The nearby Irvine household later repeats George and Margt, with no maternal surname or father’s age stated. This offers a specific existing couple and a compatible continuation across neighbouring parishes. On an infant-baptism assumption, the elder George would be about 54–58 at the Irvine children; late fatherhood remains possible. Margaret’s circa 1704 estimate would fit an adult mother in 1741–1745, but it is only a marriage-derived estimate. Neither calculation proves the identities or a move.

Agnes Spark’s proposed maternity is assessed independently from the same positive child evidence: the 1687 baptism index explicitly names John Glasgow and Agnes Spark as George’s parents. Their already confident parentage for the recorded child is not newly upgraded. Transferring either parent to the Irvine adult depends on the uncertain child/groom/Irvine identity chain, so both new adult links remain uncertain. They share that premise and are correlated, not independent confirmations. Margt in the 1741–1745 entries is George’s co-parent, not evidence for his own mother; Margaret Martin is not assigned a new mother or merged with any other Margaret.

The 1687 child, 1728 groom and Irvine father may be different men. The match of Margt to Margaret Martin is compatible, not a full recorded maternal surname match, and no age, occupation or property bridges the two parishes. The existing 1687-child/1728-adult family account also needs its identity assessment kept separate from the child’s explicit parent clause. A younger George from the Irvine Hugh/Service, James/Rae or another local family remains possible. The proposed father James-4158 would give a normal generation if George was born around 1720, but no record names him as George’s father; the specific older George/Margaret household is preferred provisionally. Keep the merchant son George-3312 distinct. If his 1729 entry represents infant baptism, he would be only about 12 at the 1741 fatherhood, a strong objection to assigning those children directly to that son. The index event type and adult-child identities still require their own review; an unsupported older birth estimate must not decide them. No merchant trade, Nethermains ownership, Manchester patent or artillery career is transferred to the Irvine father. No identity merge, duplicate removal or live relationship save is performed.
