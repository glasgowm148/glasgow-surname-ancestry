## Prior assessment — superseded by merge

A parish baptism at Kilmarnock, Ayrshire names Hugh Glasgow and Margaret McGechan as parents of [Mary Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4142) on 17 December 1732. The indexed entry establishes the recorded parent names and child event, but gives no independently identifying birth or death for this adult. [Glasgow-4167](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4167) represents another Hugh Glasgow named with Margaret Giffen in the same parish in a baptism dated 11 April 1744. These may represent one father across two households or two same-name men; the present evidence does not decide. The profiles remain cross-linked and unmerged.
