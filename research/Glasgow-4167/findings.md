## Prior assessment — superseded by merge

A parish baptism at Kilmarnock, Ayrshire names Hugh Glasgow and Margaret Giffen as parents of [Mary Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4108) on 11 April 1744. The indexed entry establishes the recorded parent names and child event, but gives no independently identifying birth or death for this adult. [Glasgow-4166](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4166) represents another Hugh Glasgow named with Margaret McGechan in the same parish in a baptism dated 17 December 1732. These may represent one father across two households or two same-name men; the present evidence does not decide. The profiles remain cross-linked and unmerged.
