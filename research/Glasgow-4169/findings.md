## Prior assessment — superseded by merge

A parish baptism at Libberton, Lanarkshire names Robert Glasgow and Katherin Gibson as parents of [Janet Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4130) on 22 April 1718. The indexed entry establishes the recorded parent names and child event, but gives no independently identifying birth or death for this adult. [Glasgow-4168](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4168) represents another Robert Glasgow named with Katherine Watson in the same parish in a baptism dated 21 August 1720. These may represent one father across two households or two same-name men; the present evidence does not decide. The profiles remain cross-linked and unmerged.
