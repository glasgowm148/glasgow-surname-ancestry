== Biography ==
'''James Glasgow''' lived at Stevenston, Ayrshire, where the parish register names him as the father of 2 children baptised there. Their mother was [[Auld-2246|Mary Auld]], named in the same entries.

* [[Glasgow-4111|Margaret Glasgow]], baptised 17 Feb 1717
* [[Glasgow-4106|Euphan Glasgow]], baptised 10 Aug 1718

No birth or baptism record for James himself has been found. His birth is placed before 1698, the year by which he must have been born to be old enough to father Margaret in 1717; Stevenston is inferred from his children's baptisms and is not independently recorded.<ref name="OPR">Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Births & Baptisms 1564–1929'', Stevenston, Ayrshire, Old Parish Register; the individual baptism entries are cited on each child's profile, for example [https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBAP%2F0369031&tab=this Margaret Glasgow, 17 Feb 1717].</ref>

== Research Notes ==
=== Selected uncertain family — 16 October 2026 ===

Select John [[Glasgow-3375]], the father associated with Barbara Alason/Allason at Kilwinning in 1679 and 1689, as the uncertain working father of James [[Glasgow-4170]], father with Mary Auld at Stevenston in 1717–1718. This is a low/very low-confidence regional household placement. The recorded couple supplies a compatible parent generation, and their son John [[Glasgow-1434]], indexed at Kilwinning in 1679, is already a possible identity for the John who had children with Margaret Cowan at Stevenston in 1701 and 1706. That existing route gives a specific neighbouring-parish family comparison for James’s later Stevenston household. The preferred provisional model places James alongside that John as a possible paternal brother. It does not identify a baptism for James or claim that the 1717–1718 entries name his parents.

James’s before-1698 birth is an existing estimated adult boundary from his 1717 fatherhood, not a baptism or an age. His actual birth could fit a child of the Kilwinning couple, but the route also depends on the unproved identification of their 1679 son with the Stevenston adult. The John-3375 and Rev. John-1073 profiles remain possible accounts of one man; no merge, duplicate resolution, ministry or new son of the minister is established here. The shared John-1604 parent and older family scaffold remain uncertain. The already selected uncertain son James-3105, father at Kilwinning in 1689 with Margaret Park, is a serious surviving identity alternative for James-4170: an earlier Park household followed by a later Auld household is possible, but no occupation, age or marriage bridge identifies them. Retain both accounts without counting the father selection as proof of two distinct James sons. Other local fathers, including the older Matthew of Pyperheugh as a possible father or earlier kinsman, remain alternatives; his 1665 spouse entry alone supplies no James child, and the long interval to 1717 requires caution. No mother is selected for James: Barbara’s known maternity of the 1679 child does not establish James’s mother, and no marriage to Mary Auld is invented beyond the recorded parent pair.
