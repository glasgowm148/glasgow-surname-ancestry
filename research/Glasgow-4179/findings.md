## Father candidate assessment

1. **Hugh Glasgow — first hypothesis, weak.** An established Larne household with children in John's estimated generation gives the most concrete family to test. Hugh would be about 42 at an estimated 1747 birth. This is compatibility, not direct kinship evidence; greater documentation must not be confused with proof.
2. **Older William Glasgow — viable alternative.** Adult in Larne by the 1730s and age-compatible. No identified record singles out John as his child. The evidence does not permit a decisive separation from Hugh.
3. **The earlier shipmaster John — open but less defined.** The 1739 occurrence permits the generation, but no inspected record gives the required son or household bridge. The chronology's numbering does not establish descent.

No mother's identity follows from this forced father ranking. Do not attach Hugh's wife Ann automatically. The Scottish parents remain on Glasgow-2846; no Scotland-to-Larne bridge has been established.
