# Mary Glasgow — 1843 intended bride

## Current conclusion

Mary Glasgow of Larne was the intended bride of John McGavock in the settlement of 10 February 1843. A payment from the estate of Hugh Glasgow connects them, but does not state fatherhood. Her uncertain father Hugh Glasgow-4180 and probable census identification remain hypotheses; Mary Glasgow-3989 is a possible duplicate, not an excluded namesake.

## Identity and relationship assessments

Hugh Glasgow-4180 is attached as uncertain father. Estate provision is compatible with a father’s marriage provision, but Hugh could be another relative or benefactor. Hugh’s proposed father James Glasgow-3991 is a separate low-confidence hypothesis. No mother is assigned. Larne is the bride’s recorded residence, not a proved birthplace; inferred birth/death places remain uncertain. McGavock and the estimated vital dates depend on the provisional census match.
