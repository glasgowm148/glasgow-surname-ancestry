# Findings: [Margaret Glasgow Walkinshaw (Glasgow-43)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-43)

## Current conclusion

The original [Glasgow Bible register image](https://hiltner.com/mckeown/histories/8479.jpeg)
records Margaret Glasgow, a child in John Glasgow and Mary Arthur's family
register, as born on **17 October 1810**.

The live structured date is 23 October 1810 and should be corrected. The live
biography is still a placeholder and does not cite the family register or state
the proved parent-family context.
