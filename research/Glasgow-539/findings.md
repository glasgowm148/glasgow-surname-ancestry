## Source findings — father's will, 29 March 1827

Hugh Glasgo of Deer Township, Allegheny County (`Glasgow-540`), in his will signed 29 March 1827 and proved 25 April 1831, says "I leave to my son Hugh one cow", to be delivered one year after the testator's death ("Hugh his one year after") ([Allegheny County Will Book 3, no. 252, pp. 492–493](https://www.ancestry.com/imageviewer/collections/8802/images/005537856_00276?pid=1733435); original loose packet FamilySearch DGS 5538049, no. 252). Seen as the recorded will-book image (the clerk's copy); the transcription at `../Glasgow-540/sources/hugh_glasgow_will_1827_transcription.md` was made from that image and checked against thumbnails of the original loose packet.

The will proves that Hugh senior had a son Hugh living in March 1827; it gives the son no age or residence. That this profile is that son is inference from the WikiTree family placement and W. M. Glasgow's late family history, which lists a son Hugh; no record here ties the will's Hugh to this profile's dates.
