== Biography ==
=== Early life and family ===

'''Hugh Glasgow''' was born in 1739 in County Londonderry, Ireland. He was the son of [[Glasgow-548|Hugh Glasgow]] and [[McCullough-1964|Margaret McCullough]], and brother of:

* [[Glasgow-549|John Glasgow]]
* [[Glasgow-550|Robert Glasgow]]

The family history written by [[Glasgow-590|William Melanchthon Glasgow]] says:

<blockquote>
''Hugh Glasgow was born in County Londonderry in 1739. He was reared in the strictest manner and taught the peculiar tenets of his fathers. While he followed to some extent the trade of his father, yet he turned his attention somewhat to farming. Thus passed away childhood and youth about the rural home, until he felt constrained to secure a home for himself.''

''In 1777, he married Sally Blair, who proved to be, in every respect, a worthy companion. As the fruit of this union were born to them five sons and two daughters, viz: John, Clatworthy (commonly called Tatty), Hugh and Nancy (twins), Alexander, Mary, and Robert. Hugh and Nancy, twins, lived to be eighty-eight years of age, and both died the same year.''

''The sons were all very large and corpulent, and were capable of enduring much hard labor. Hugh, however, gradually became so useless, by reason of his enormity, that he was derricked in and out of bed, and spent his leisure time reading his familiar Bible. Hugh died in 1824, aged 85.''
</blockquote>

<ref name="FamilyHistory">[[Glasgow-590|William Melanchthon Glasgow]], ''History of the Glasgow Family'', [https://www.wikitree.com/photo.php/0/0a/Glasgow-590.pdf digital copy], p. 2.</ref>

The identification fits the wider family evidence. The elder Hugh was recorded by family tradition as a weaver who moved from County Tyrone to County Londonderry and married Margaret McCullough in 1733. Two men named Hugh Glasgow were also recorded in Tamlaght O’Crilly Parish in the 1740 Protestant Householders’ Returns, one appearing near James McCullough.<ref name="Householders1740">Bill Macafee, compiler, ''Protestant Householders’ Returns (1740) for County Londonderry'', entries for Hugh Glasgow in Tamlaght O’Crilly Parish; [https://adamsfamilydna.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/1740-Protestant-Returns-Londonderry-County-Ireland.pdf PDF].</ref><ref name="HouseholderSequence">William Murphy, transcriber, “Census (Partial) of Protestant Householders in Tamlaght O’Crilly Parish in 1740,” ''Ireland Genealogy Projects Archives'', [https://www.igp-web.com/IGPArchives/ire/derry/census/cens-prot-tamlaght-1740.html online transcription].</ref>

=== Marriage and children ===

Hugh married [[Blair-3693|Sarah “Sally” Blair]] in 1777.<ref name="FamilyHistory" />

They had seven children:

* [[Glasgow-209|John Glasgow]]
* [[Glasgow-542|Clatworthy Glasgow]], called “Tatty”
* [[Glasgow-539|Hugh Glasgow]]
* [[Glasgow-543|Nancy Glasgow]], twin of Hugh
* [[Glasgow-544|Alexander Glasgow]]
* [[Glasgow-545|Mary Glasgow]]
* [[Glasgow-546|Robert Glasgow]]

The family history states that Hugh and Nancy were twins, lived to the age of 88, and died in the same year.<ref name="FamilyHistory" />

Hugh’s will names John, Hugh, Alexander, Nancy and Robert, together with his granddaughter Sarah, daughter of Alexander.<ref name="Will1827">[https://www.ancestry.com/imageviewer/collections/8802/images/005537856_00276?pid=1733435 Allegheny County Will Book 3, no. 252, pp. 492–493], will of Hugh Glasgo of Deer Township, signed 29 March 1827 and proved 25 April 1831.</ref>

Clatworthy and Mary were not named in the will.

=== Emigration to America ===

The family emigrated to America in August 1796 and landed at New Castle, Delaware.<ref name="FamilyHistory" />

They lived for about five years near Little York in York County, Pennsylvania. In 1802 they moved briefly to Washington County, then settled in Deer Creek Township, Allegheny County.<ref name="FamilyHistory" />

=== Will and death ===

By 1827 Hugh was living in Deer Township, Allegheny County, Pennsylvania.

He signed his will on '''29 March 1827'''. It named:

* sons [[Glasgow-209|John]], [[Glasgow-539|Hugh]], [[Glasgow-544|Alexander]] and [[Glasgow-546|Robert]]
* daughter [[Glasgow-543|Nancy]]
* granddaughter Sarah, daughter of Alexander
* an unnamed wife

John and Robert were appointed executors. Robert received Hugh’s land and the residue of the estate, with responsibility for supporting his mother.<ref name="Will1827" />

The will was proved on '''25 April 1831'''. A matching loose probate packet also survives.<ref name="WillPacket">FamilySearch DGS 5538049, Allegheny County probate packet no. 252: [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L991-GV6H?cc=1999196&wc=9PMK-MNG%3A268499101%2C281697201 cover], [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L991-GVS6?cc=1999196&wc=9PMK-MNG%3A268499101%2C281697201 will, p. 1], [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QSQ-G991-GV3K?cc=1999196&wc=9PMK-MNG%3A268499101%2C281697201 will, p. 2], and [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L991-GVD7?cc=1999196&wc=9PMK-MNG%3A268499101%2C281697201 probate].</ref>

The family history’s claim that Hugh died in 1824 is wrong, since he signed his will in 1827. He died between 29 March 1827 and 25 April 1831.

He is reported to have been buried in Pine Creek Cemetery, Allison Park, Hampton Township, Allegheny County, Pennsylvania.<ref name="FindAGrave">{{FindAGrave|94156705}}</ref>

=== Hugh–McCullough parentage assessment — 17 October 2026 ===

[[Glasgow-548|Hugh Glasgow, elder Irish weaver]] and [[McCullough-1964|Margaret McCullough]] are assessed as the probable parents of [[Glasgow-540|Hugh Glasgow, Pennsylvania emigrant]], the emigrant whose Pennsylvania will was made in 1827 and proved in 1831. William Melanchthon Glasgow’s family history, p.2, explicitly names the elder Hugh and Margaret as the married parents of John, Robert and their youngest son Hugh, born in County Londonderry in 1739. Page 3 follows this younger Hugh’s household through John, whom the author calls his grandfather, and its emigration in 1796. This is specific descendant testimony about a named family, rather than a parent invented from dates or a shared surname. The younger Hugh’s recorded will independently names the same four sons John, Hugh, Alexander and Robert, and daughter Nancy, within the corresponding Pennsylvania household. That agreement supports the family history’s identification of the emigrant and the reliability of its nearer family relationships. It does not independently name his parents. The explicit family account, consistent household identity and feasible generations together warrant probable parentage rather than leaving the father and mother ungraded.

Parentage rests on one retrospective family account; later online copies and family trees are derivatives, not independent witnesses. Its author gives no Irish baptism or marriage source, and its 1824 death claim is contradicted by Hugh’s signed 1827 will and 1831 probate. The 1733 marriage, 1739 birth, older Hugh’s circa-1706 birth and Margaret’s imported vital estimates retain their separate evidence limits. === Death date ===

The family history gives Hugh’s death as 1824.<ref name="FamilyHistory" />

His will proves he was alive on 29 March 1827. The earlier source list gave 26 March, but the recorded will and probate packet show the correct date was 29 March.

His death occurred before probate on 25 April 1831.

=== Earlier Hugh Glasgow records ===

Two men named Hugh Glasgow appear in the 1740 Protestant Householders’ Returns for Tamlaght O’Crilly Parish.<ref name="Householders1740" />

Neither can be this Hugh, who was born about 1739. One is likely to have been his father, [[Glasgow-548|Hugh Glasgow]].

A Hugh Glasgow also appears in a 1772 ''Belfast News-Letter'' notice concerning inhabitants of Tamlaght O’Crilly Parish who surrendered arms.<ref name="NewsLetter1772">[https://arborealis.ca/records/newspapers/kilrea-news/1770-1779/ ''Belfast News-Letter'', 14 April 1772], notice dated 6 April 1772 naming Hugh Glasgow among inhabitants of Tamlaght O’Crilly Parish.</ref>

That entry could refer to either father or son.
