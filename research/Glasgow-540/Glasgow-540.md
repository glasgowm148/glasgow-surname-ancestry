## Suggested correction: Hugh Glasgow (1739–abt. 1831) — Glasgow-540

Replace the current Research Notes paragraph beginning `The death date for Hugh`
with this exact text:

```text
=== Will and probate ===

William Melanchthon Glasgow's later family history says Hugh died in 1824. That date is incorrect: Hugh signed his will at Deer Township, Allegheny County, on 29 March 1827, and the will was proved on 25 April 1831. The profile's estimated death date of about 1831 is consistent with this evidence.<ref name="HughWill1827">Allegheny County, Pennsylvania, Register of Wills, ''Will Book 3'', no. 252, pp. 492–493, will of Hugh Glasgo, signed 29 March 1827 and proved 25 April 1831; [https://www.ancestry.com/imageviewer/collections/8802/images/005537856_00276?pid=1733435 Ancestry record image 005537856_00276], ''Pennsylvania, U.S., Wills and Probate Records, 1683–1993'', collection 8802, record 1733435 (accessed 17 July 2026).</ref>

The will names Hugh's sons John, Hugh, Alexander and Robert; his daughter Nancy; and Sarah, daughter of Alexander. Hugh appointed John and Robert as executors. Robert received Hugh's land and residual estate and was required to support Hugh's wife, whom the will does not name.<ref name="HughWill1827" />
```

Also replace `signed 26-Mar-1827` in the existing source-list entry with `signed
29-Mar-1827`. The inline citation above links to the actual will-book image; do not
substitute the local transcription for that source.

Confidence: high; the facts and relationships are stated in the original recorded
will.

## Do not add as fact

- Do not treat the will as an exhaustive child list. It never says `all my children` and omits Clatworthy/Tatty and Mary from the later seven-child account.
- Do not attach Adam Glasgow of Inishrush as Hugh's son. The will does not name Adam; his omission is adverse but not conclusive evidence.
- Do not attach either 1740 Tamlaght O'Crilly Hugh entry to this profile. Both men were householders before this Hugh's approximate birth.
- Do not claim the 1772 subscriber was this Hugh without a distinguishing record; the notice can also fit his father or another namesake.
