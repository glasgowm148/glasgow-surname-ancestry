- Alexander Glasgow was born in northern Ireland in 1786 (or possibly 1788?). His family was Scots-Irish. His parents were Hugh Glasgow and Sarah Blair. Alex's family immigrated to the United States after he was born, probably sometime in the 1790s. By 1810, the family of Hugh Glasgow was settled in Deer Township in Allegheny County, Pennsylvania. Alex was married at the time of the 1810 census and living on a farm of his own. The 1850 census of his household includes a woman name Sarah, one year younger than Alex in the household. Presumably, this was his wife. Alex died in 1855 and was buried in Pine Creek Cemetery in Allison Park, Pennsylvania.
