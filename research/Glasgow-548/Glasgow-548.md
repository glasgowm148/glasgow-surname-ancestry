== Biography ==
'''Earlier Tyrone parentage review, 17 October 2026:''' William Glasgow-591 is assessed as the probable father of John Glasgow-551, and John as the probable father of Hugh Glasgow-548, within the explicit family-history pedigree. These are tradition-based probable roles, not documented identifications of the Scottish candidates, the Leitrim witness or either 1740 Hugh.<ref name="TyronePedigreeReview" />
Hugh Glasgow, son of [[Glasgow-551|John Glasgow]], was reportedly born in County Tyrone, Ireland, about 1706. He later settled in County Londonderry, where he worked as a weaver and married [[McCullough-1964|Margaret McCullough]].<ref name="GlasgowHistory">[[Glasgow-590|William Melanchthon Glasgow]], ''History of the Glasgow Family'', [https://www.wikitree.com/photo/pdf/Glasgow-590 digital copy attached to Glasgow-590].</ref>

=== Family tradition ===

In the family history written by Hugh's second great-grandson, [[Glasgow-590|William Melanchthon Glasgow]], Hugh is described as follows:

<blockquote>
''was born in the County Tyrone about 1706. He was a weaver by trade, and followed this vocation. A little circumstance in his life is recorded to show how trifling transactions may determine our future place of residence. When a young man he had an unavoidable difficulty with a neighbor which resulted in personal combat. Being a strong and agile youth, he severely punished his dishonest debtor, in consequence of which he fled from the community, and found a resting place in the County Londonderry.''

''He was soon employed at his chosen vocation by a man by the name of McCullough. He immediately gained the confidence of his employer, for undoubtedly the young man was a good workman. The aged weaver, however little suspected that the young man was falling deparately in love with his charming daughter. But such was the case; and the sequel was that Hugh Glasgow and Margaret McCullough celebrated their marriage in 1733. To them were born in due time, three sons, John, Robert, and Hugh.''
</blockquote>

<ref name="GlasgowHistory"/>

Hugh and [[McCullough-1964|Margaret McCullough]] married in 1733 and had three known sons:

* [[Glasgow-549|John Glasgow]]
* [[Glasgow-550|Robert Glasgow]]
* [[Glasgow-540|Hugh Glasgow]]

=== Tamlaght O'Crilly ===

The 1740 Protestant Householders' Returns for County Londonderry contain two entries for men named Hugh Glasgow in Tamlaght O'Crilly Parish, Barony of Loughinsholin.<ref name="PHR1740">Bill Macafee, compiler, ''Protestant Householders' Returns (1740) for County Londonderry'', records 1195 and 1272, Hugh Glasgow, Loughinsholin Barony, Tamlaght O'Crilly Parish; [https://adamsfamilydna.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/1740-Protestant-Returns-Londonderry-County-Ireland.pdf PDF].</ref>

In a transcription preserving the parish sequence, one of the entries appears in the following order:

* Hugh Glasgow
* Hugh Allice
* James McCullough

<ref name="PHRSequence">William Murphy, transcriber, “Census (Partial) of Protestant Householders in Tamlaght O'Crilly Parish in 1740,” ''Ireland Genealogy Projects Archives'', [https://www.igp-web.com/IGPArchives/ire/derry/census/cens-prot-tamlaght-1740.html online transcription].</ref>

This fits the family account closely. Hugh is found in the correct county and parish only a few years after his reported marriage, and his name appears almost immediately beside that of James McCullough. Taken together, the date, location and McCullough connection make this the strongest candidate for the Hugh described in the family history.

The second Hugh Glasgow entry may represent another member of the wider Glasgow family living in the same parish.

=== 1772 record ===

A notice published in the ''Belfast News-Letter'' in April 1772 names a Hugh Glasgow among the inhabitants of Tamlaght O'Crilly Parish who surrendered their arms to Henry Ellis of Inishrush.<ref name="News1772">''Belfast News-Letter'', 14 April 1772, notice dated at Inishrush on 6 April 1772, naming Hugh Glasgow among inhabitants of Tamlaght O'Crilly Parish; transcript in “1770–1779: News transcripts for Kilrea & environs,” [https://arborealis.ca/records/newspapers/kilrea-news/1770-1779/ Arborealis].</ref>

The notice places a Hugh Glasgow in the same parish district more than thirty years after the 1740 return. This may have been Hugh himself, then about sixty-six years old, although his adult son [[Glasgow-540|Hugh Glasgow]] was also living by that time.

The 1740 and 1772 records together show a continuing Glasgow presence in Tamlaght O'Crilly and support the family's association with the district.

=== Later family in America ===

The family history later follows the household of Hugh's younger son, [[Glasgow-540|Hugh Glasgow]], who married Sarah or Sallie Blair.

That branch of the family emigrated to America in August 1796 and arrived at New Castle, Delaware. They lived for about five years near Little York in York County, Pennsylvania. In 1802 they moved briefly to Washington County and later settled in Deer Creek Township, Allegheny County, Pennsylvania.<ref name="GlasgowHistory"/>

=== Record limits ===

The family history preserves a valuable account of Hugh's origins, occupation, marriage and sons, but was written long after the reported events. The parentage and dates in that account remain family tradition rather than independently proved relationships.<ref name="GlasgowHistory"/>

The collector sequence beside James McCullough makes one of the 1740 householders a useful working candidate for this Hugh. However, the return records no wife, children, age or townland for either Hugh, so the association alone does not establish identity. The second entry remains a separate namesake to investigate.<ref name="PHR1740"/><ref name="PHRSequence"/>

The 1772 notice establishes a Hugh Glasgow's presence in Tamlaght O'Crilly Parish; it does not distinguish this man from his younger son or another namesake. The two records support continued Glasgow presence in the district without proving which Hugh appears in both.<ref name="News1772"/>
</blockquote>


=== Dates ===

The family history written by [[Glasgow-590|William Melanchthon Glasgow]] gives Hugh's birth as about 1706 and his marriage to [[McCullough-1964|Margaret McCullough]] as 1733.

The later online account gives Hugh's birth as 1724. That date does not fit comfortably with the reported 1733 marriage and may be a transcription error or a date belonging to another member of the family.

The accounts also differ over the birth year of the younger [[Glasgow-540|Hugh Glasgow]]. The later account gives 1753, while the family history gives an earlier date.

=== Identification ===

The entry beside James McCullough in the 1740 Tamlaght O'Crilly return is the strongest match for this Hugh because it agrees with the location, period and McCullough association recorded in the family history.

The presence of a second Hugh Glasgow in the same return suggests that there was another adult of the name in the parish, perhaps a relative.
