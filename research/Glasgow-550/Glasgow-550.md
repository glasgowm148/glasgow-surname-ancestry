== Biography ==

'''Robert Glasgow''' is named as a son of [[Glasgow-548|Hugh Glasgow]] and [[McCullough-1964|Margaret McCullough]] in William Melanchthon Glasgow's late family history.  The account gives no event, residence, wife, child, occupation, or death for Robert.  It records a family tradition, not proof of the profile's estimated birth, death, or relationships.<ref name="GlasgowHistory">[[Glasgow-590|William Melanchthon Glasgow]], ''History of the Glasgow Family'', [https://www.wikitree.com/photo/pdf/Glasgow-590 digital copy attached to Glasgow-590].</ref>

The 1831 Lissane return concerns a later Robert Glasgow and supplies no relationship to this profile.<ref>1831 Irish Census – National Archives of Ireland (http://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1831/Londonderry/Lissane/)</ref>

== Research Notes ==

Research has excluded several same-name men by dated place and circumstances, including the married Killwaughter leaseholder active in 1738, the St Vincent Army surgeon active after 1787, and later Robert Glasgows of County Tyrone.  No found source identifies this Robert at Inishrush, names [[Glasgow-3902|Adam Glasgow]] as his son, or distinguishes him from the traditional brother [[Glasgow-549|John Glasgow]].  He remains an uncertain research candidate only.
