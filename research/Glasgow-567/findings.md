# Findings: Barbara Glasgow (Glasgow-567), adult at Edinburgh in 1611

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

Barbara is directly documented as the mother of Catharine Little, born 9
September and baptised 29 September 1611 at Edinburgh. The index names Johne
Little as Catharine's father. It does **not** identify Barbara's parents or prove
that Barbara and Johne were married.

Detach William `Glasgow-3522` and Margaret Herbesoun `Herbison-439` as Barbara's
parents. Two 1586 protocols prove that couple as parents of George `Glasgow-1099`
only; neither names Barbara or calls her George's sister. `Son and heir` does not
make George an only child, but it supplies no bridge to Barbara. Barbara's birth,
death and parents remain unknown; remove the unsupported circa-1627 death.

Do not merge the adult 1611 Barbara with `Glasgow-3182` or `Glasgow-3302` without
continuity evidence. The woman `Glasgow-1102`, baptised in 1617, is conclusively
excluded by chronology.
