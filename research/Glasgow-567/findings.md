# Findings: Barbara Glasgow (Glasgow-567), adult at Edinburgh in 1611

Last updated: 2026-10-07

## Current conclusion

Barbara is directly documented as the mother of Catharine Little, born 9
September and baptised 29 September 1611 at Edinburgh. The index names Johne
Little as Catharine's father. It does **not** identify Barbara's parents or prove
that Barbara and Johne were married.

William `Glasgow-3522` and Margaret Herbesoun `Herbison-439` are not proved as
Barbara's parents. Two 1586 protocols prove that couple as parents of George
`Glasgow-1099` only; neither names Barbara or calls her George's sister. `Son
and heir` does not make George an only child, but it supplies no bridge to
Barbara. Preserve the populated links as uncertain while testing the positive
alternative below; do not leave the fields blank merely because the current
placement is weak. Barbara's birth, death and parents remain unproved.

Parent-status audit, 27 September 2026: Barbara's live father link to William
is marked confident despite this unresolved identity, while her mother link to
Margaret is unmarked. Barbara's profile is managed outside the project account;
the relationship fields were left intact. Both attached parent links need to be
treated as uncertain in research until a record bridges Barbara to this couple.

Do not merge the adult 1611 Barbara with `Glasgow-3182` or `Glasgow-3302` without
continuity evidence. The woman `Glasgow-1102`, baptised in 1617, is conclusively
excluded by chronology.

## Branch and identity placement - 31 August 2026

The leading alternative family is [Ninian Glasgow
(Glasgow-3188)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3188)'s household or
immediate collateral circle. Ninian's son Robert was a cordiner; his probable
son William moved into Leith; and William's son entered a cordiner
apprenticeship. The possible later Barbaras married at South Leith and to an
Edinburgh cordiner. This is more specific evidence than the unsupported
William-3522 attachment, but it depends partly on the unresolved identity of
the three adult Barbara profiles.

The chronology permits one woman: Catharine Little was born 9 September 1611;
the Wilson entry is 20 September or 26 November; and the Johnstoun marriage was
in 1616. The baptism proves Johne Little as co-parent, not husband. Assessment:
**moderate identity hypothesis and possible Ninian daughter/niece**. Do not
merge or replace parents until the original 1611 entries and John Wilson's fate
are resolved.
