```text
=== Possible Scottish identifications ===

The Scottish Covenanters Index entry for a William Glasgow at “Ormistone” derives from the 1684 ''Register of the Privy Council of Scotland''. The underlying record names “William Glasgow, his servant ther” immediately after William Leeden at Ormiston, among people from the Cavers area.<ref name="RPC1684">Henry Paton, ed., ''The Register of the Privy Council of Scotland'', 3rd series, vol. IX, A.D. 1684 (Edinburgh: H.M. General Register House, 1924), 473, 483, 673, [https://books.google.com/books?id=zDyZs3nDbR0C Google Books].</ref> Ormiston was a place in Cavers parish, Roxburghshire, not the parish of Ormiston in East Lothian.<ref name="OrmistonCavers">Ordnance Survey, ''Roxburghshire Ordnance Survey Name Books, 1858–1860'', vol. 8, Ormiston entry, [https://scotlandsplaces.gov.uk/digital-volumes/ordnance-survey-name-books/roxburghshire-os-name-books-1858-1860/roxburghshire-volume-08?display=transcription Scotland's Places].</ref> The record does not state William's age, birthplace or parents and does not identify him as this profile.

A separate prisoner list names John Glasgow and William Glasgow under Cavers parish and marks both as survivors of the December 1679 wreck.<ref name="CloudCavers">John H. Thomson, ed., ''A Cloud of Witnesses'', appendix, prisoner list under “Parish of Cavers”; the editor explains that survivors are marked with a star, [https://media.sabda.org/alkitab-8/LIBRARY/THO_CLWT.PDF digital copy].</ref> The list gives no age, parents, relationship between John and William, or later destination. No contemporary record has been found connecting the Cavers prisoner or the 1684 servant to the William remembered in the family's Irish tradition.

The family history says that a William Glasgow remained in northern Ireland after the 1689 Londonderry campaign, but it does not name his parents, birthplace, military unit, Cavers, the 1679 wreck, or the 1684 servant.<ref name="WMG">William Melanchthon Glasgow, ''History of the Glasgow Family'', [https://www.wikitree.com/photo/pdf/Glasgow-590 PDF attached to Glasgow-590].</ref> These Scottish men therefore remain possible candidates, not established identities. William's parents remain unknown.
```
