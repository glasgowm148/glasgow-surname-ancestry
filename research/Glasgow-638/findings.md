## Leadhills companion memorial: age conflict — 4 November 2026

It gives William’s death as 26 April 1853, aged 71; the existing Crawford rendering gives the same date but age 74. These imply approximate births around 1782 and 1779 respectively, not independently recorded birthdays. The matching occupation, spouse, death dates and children identify companion renderings of the same family memorial; they are not independent witnesses and do not resolve the age disagreement. Both readings are retained without changing vital fields.

The new text gives Janet’s death as 1 April 1845, aged 62; calls John, James and George three of her children; says John and James died in infancy; and gives George’s death as 21 July 1816, aged 7. It also commemorates Isabel Glasgow, died 27 November 1881, aged 73, without expressly calling her a daughter. Her daughter identification rests on the existing baptism and household context rather than that literal line alone. The reported surname Thomson does not independently establish maiden-name wording.
