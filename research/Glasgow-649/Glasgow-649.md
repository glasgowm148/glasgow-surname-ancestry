```text
=== Irish origin and possible collateral family ===

Arthur's approximate 1750 birth is inferred from his age at death. No original Irish record currently cited on this profile identifies his birthplace or parents. A later descendant account by Ellen Glasgow says that Arthur came to America in 1766, aged sixteen and a ward, with his mother and elder brothers, and places the family's earlier Irish residence broadly in Counties Down and Antrim.<ref>Ellen Glasgow, ''The Woman Within'' (1954), appendix “A Dull Note for Genealogists,” pp. 298-299; excerpt reproduced at FamilySearch Memories, https://www.familysearch.org/en/memories/memory/16905684 (accessed 21 July 2026).</ref>

David Faris's 1990 genealogy of Robert Glasgow describes Robert as a son of James Glasgow of Moneymore and says that Robert migrated in 1769 with three brothers and a sister. The public catalogue summary does not name those siblings or expose the evidence supporting the Moneymore parentage.<ref>David Faris, ''The Glasgow Family of Adams County, Ohio'' (Baltimore: Gateway Press, 1990), WorldCat catalogue record 22731567, https://search.worldcat.org/title/22731567 (accessed 21 July 2026).</ref>

Arthur, Robert and Joseph are independently described as three brothers in the later history of Glasgow, Virginia.<ref>Town of Glasgow, Virginia, “History,” https://glasgowvirginia.org/history (accessed 21 July 2026).</ref> These accounts make a northern-Irish sibling cluster plausible but do not prove Arthur's attached parents or a specific Moneymore birth. One research hypothesis places an otherwise unrecorded older brother Adam, married to Maria, in Ireland as father of Adam Glasgow (born about 1758) and John Glasgow (born 1767). John's sons were Adam, James, John and Arthur, a sequence which could preserve his father, grandfather, himself and paternal uncle. The hypothesis is weakened because John's wife was Mary Arthur and therefore independently explains the name Arthur. No record presently names Adam in Arthur's family. Faris's James of Moneymore must not automatically be equated with the internally conflicted [[Glasgow-1024]] or the derivative [[Glasgow-3305]].
```
