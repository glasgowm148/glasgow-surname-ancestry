## Possible New London childhood — 12 November 2026

Chester County Archives, RG4100.052, Negro Servant Returns 1788–1821, digital document 859766: image 86 is the docket dated 30 October 1795, naming John Menough junior and a child Robert. Image 87 records a male child Robert born 10 August 1795 in the family of John Menough junior of New London Township. No mother, father or Glasgow surname is stated in the inspected return. The county’s child index gives the birth date, place and registrant; its master index gives 30 October 1795 as the filing date. The search box returned no Robert hit even though the original is present, so that OCR miss is not a negative search result.

The date, county and reported racial classification are compatible with the later household, but do not identify this common forename with Robert Glasgow. A10 August 1795 birth would make him 84 on 11 June 1880, rather than the recorded 85; ordinary age variation is possible but must be acknowledged. Pennsylvania residence in 1880 does not establish a Chester County birth. A different Pennsylvania Robert remains a real alternative. The return describes a child registered under Pennsylvania’s gradual-abolition servitude provisions; that history is not transferred to Glasgow-752 unless identity is established.
