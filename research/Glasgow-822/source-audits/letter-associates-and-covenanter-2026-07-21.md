# 1847 letter associates and Covenanter search, 21 July 2026

## Question

Can any person named in John Glasgow's 20 March 1847 letter provide a record
explicitly connecting John, born 10 April 1767, with Adam Glasgow of
Inishrush?

## Original-letter check

The complete three-page manuscript was inspected, not merely the online
transcript. The relevant sequence says that:

- John had received a letter from `Adam` dated 27 December 1846;
- `R. Mulhollan` supplied news about Irish settlers in Western Canada and the
  potato failure;
- an uncertain `Bogg's` family had travelled with the Glasgows for part of
  their migration;
- a young man whose surname reads `McCaivey` brought news from across the
  Atlantic; and
- that news was the report about James Glasgow and an unnamed daughter of
  Henry Barkly.

There is no omitted word identifying Adam, James, Hugh, Mulhollan, McCaivey,
the Boggs family or Barkly as John's brother, father, uncle or cousin.

Source: [John Glasgow letter, 20 March
1847](https://www.hiltner.com/mckeown/documents/John%20Glasgow%20letter%20to%20Jane%20Glasgow%20Gray%203-20-1847.pdf).

a lead but is not independent proof of the voyage.

## First Kilrea test of the James-Barkly report

The publicly transcribed First Kilrea Presbyterian baptism register was read
entry by entry for 1845, 1846 and 1847, the immediate period surrounding the
news reported in John's March 1847 letter. It contains no baptism under
`Glasgow`, `Glasco`, `Barkly`, `Barkley`, `Barklie` or `Barclay`, and no
unidentified illegitimate child matching the report.

Sources:

- [First Kilrea baptisms,
  1845](https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/KilreaP1/Baptisms/1845.html);
- [First Kilrea baptisms,
  1846](https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/KilreaP1/Baptisms/1846.html);
- [First Kilrea baptisms,
  1847](https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/KilreaP1/Baptisms/1847.html).

The same site's transcription of communicant candidates drawn from First
Kilrea session book `MIC1P/87` for 1825-1838 contains no Glasgow or Barkly
entry. This list records people admitted or restored to communion, not the
congregation's complete membership, and it does not reproduce the later
discipline proceedings. It therefore cannot exclude either family from the
congregation.

Source: [First Kilrea communicant candidates,
1825-1838](https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/KilreaP1/Communion.html).

The baptism negative is significant but not conclusive. The child could have
been baptised in another congregation, baptised after 1847, omitted from the
register, or not baptised. The full 1845-1847 discipline pages of `MIC1P/87`
and Drimbolg book `MIC1C/15` are not publicly transcribed and remain the only
identified records likely to name the parties and any relationship.

Additional Barkly entries establish collateral households but supply no
Glasgow kinship: James Barkley of Ballyhay married Anne Smyth in 1834, and
their legitimate son Joseph was baptised at Maghera after a birth on 19 July
1845. They cannot be substituted for Henry Barkly's unnamed daughter or her
reported child without an explicit record.

Sources: [First Kilrea marriages,
1834](https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/KilreaP1/Marriages/1834.html);
[Maghera baptisms,
1843-1866](https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/MagheraP/Baptisms1843-1866.html).

## Reformed Presbyterian periodical search

John died at Londonderry, Guernsey County, Ohio, on 23 December 1851. Full
text extracted from the following digitised volumes was searched for John
Glasgow, the Glasgow surname, Londonderry, Guernsey and the death date:

- James McLeod Willson, [*The Covenanter*, volume 7,
  1851-1852](https://library.logcollegepress.com/Willson,+James+McLeod,+The+Covenanter,+Vol.+7+(1851-1852).pdf);
- James McLeod Willson, [*The Covenanter*, volume 8,
  1852-1853](https://library.logcollegepress.com/Willson,+James+McLeod,+The+Covenanter,+Vol.+8+(1852-1853).pdf).

No death notice, obituary, immigration account or kin statement for the John
born in 1767 was found. Glasgow matches concerned the Scottish city or
unrelated people. This is a bounded negative for these two volumes, not proof
that no notice appeared elsewhere.

## Result

This route produced no concrete record naming John and Adam as brothers or
naming shared parents. The Barkly/Tyanee report strengthens the conclusion
that John remained in close contact with Adam's immediate Inishrush family,
but it remains evidence of social familiarity rather than stated kinship.

The present evidence still supports `probable brother or close cousin`; it
does not justify changing that assessment to confirmed brother.

## Repository correction required

`research/Glasgow-3903/findings.md` currently says that `R. Mulhollan`
reported the James/Barkly news. The original manuscript instead attributes
that report to McCaivey. Every occurrence of the former attribution should be
corrected before the finding is used on WikiTree.
