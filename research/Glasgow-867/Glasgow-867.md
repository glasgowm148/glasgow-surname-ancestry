== Biography ==

'''John Glasgow''' was a farmer of Lisnagaver in the civil parish of Rasharkin,
County Antrim. His date of birth is uncertain. The 1851 census reported his age
as fifty, while the family gravestone says that he died in 1895 aged seventy-
eight, implying birth about 1816 or 1817.<ref name="Lisnagaver1851">[https://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Rasharkin/Lisnagaver/34/ 1851 Census of Ireland], Lisnagaver house 34, Rasharkin, County Antrim; [http://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/reels/c19/007246523/007246523_00173.pdf original census image].</ref><ref name="RasharkinStone">[https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/RasharkinP/graveyard.html Pauline O'Keeffe and Helen Evans, ''Rasharkin Second Presbyterian Church headstones''], transcription made August 2004, Glasgo memorial erected by James Garvin Glasgo.</ref>

John married [[Garvin-1831|Margaret Garvin]] on 16 November 1846. The original
register describes him as a bachelor farmer of Lisnagaver and names his father
as '''John Glasgow, farmer'''. Margaret was a spinster.<ref name="JohnMarriage1846">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/view/?record_id=cima-3156063 IrishGenealogy.ie], civil marriage registration of John Glasgow and Margaret Garvin, 16 November 1846, Ballymena registration district, group registration ID 3156063.</ref>
Her father was '''James Garvin, farmer'''. James Garvin and Ann Kelly witnessed
the marriage.<ref name="JohnMarriage1846" />

The 1851 household contained John, Margaret, their three-year-old daughter
[[Glasgow-1371|Ann]], and John's brother [[Glasgow-2908|Samuel Glasgow]]. John
Patterson, a fifteen-year-old linen weaver, was their servant. The return records
1846 as the marriage year and explicitly describes Samuel as John's brother.<ref name="Lisnagaver1851" />

Ann married [[Bellingham-700|Francis Bellingham]] on 18 February 1875. Her
marriage names her father as John Glasgow, farmer.<ref name="AnnMarriage1875">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1875/11190/8100682.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Francis Bellingham and Annie Glasgow, 18 February 1875, Finvoy parish church, entry 40.</ref>

John and Margaret also had a son, [[Glasgow-2406|James Garvin Glasgow]]. James's
1886 marriage records him as a Lisnagaver farmer and son of John Glasgow,
farmer. The family gravestone identifies Margaret Garvin as his mother.<ref name="JamesMarriage1886">[https://civilrecords.irishgenealogy.ie/churchrecords/images/marriage_returns/marriages_1886/10840/5957283.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], James Garvin Glasgow and Eliza Ann Aiken, 1 July 1886, Clough, entry 62.</ref><ref name="RasharkinStone" />

The gravestone records that Margaret died on 10 January 1892 aged seventy-four
and John died on 20 June 1895 aged seventy-eight. Their son James erected the
memorial at Rasharkin Second Presbyterian Church.<ref name="RasharkinStone" />

== Research Notes ==

=== Parentage ===

John's marriage proves that his father was another '''John Glasgow, farmer'''.
The father is currently represented by [[Glasgow-1022|John Glasgow]] as an
uncertain working identification. No record yet gives the older man's residence,
wife or age. If the gravestone age is correct, the older John would have been
about sixty-five when this John was born; possible, but not enough to identify
him uniquely.

The conflict between the 1851 age of fifty and the gravestone age of seventy-
eight at death remains unresolved. The marriage, residence, wife and 1846
marriage year securely identify the 1851 household, while the gravestone was
erected by the couple's son and names both parents. The birth year should
therefore remain estimated.

=== Alexander Glasgow of Glenleslie ===

[[Glasgow-2385|Alexander Glasgow]] was previously attached as John's son.
However, Alexander was already a full-age bachelor when he married in April
1847, only five months after John and Margaret's marriage. Alexander's original
marriage also names his father as '''John Glasgow, labourer'''. He was detached
from this household because he belongs to an older, separate father-John branch.<ref name="AlexanderMarriage1847">[https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/marriage_returns/marriages_1847/09322/5375567.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Alexander Glasgow and Mary Anne Martin, 6 April 1847, Clogh, entry 31; derivative [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QGVD-P9CS FamilySearch index entry].</ref>
