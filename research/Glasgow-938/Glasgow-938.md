## Suggested corrections

- Phrase the Griffith Gortereghy identification as **probable**, not proved.
  The valuation supplies only the occupier's name. Robert's marriage makes him
  the best candidate but does not explicitly refer to the valuation holding.

## Do not add as fact

- Do not describe Daniel, the other Gortereghy occupier, as Robert's proved
  uncle. That follows only if Daniel is `Glasgow-3896` and Robert's father
  Alexander was Daniel's brother; the latter link remains probable, not direct.
