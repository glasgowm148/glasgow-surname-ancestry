== Biography ==

'''Robert Glasgow''' was born about 1840 or 1841 in County Antrim, Ireland,
the son of [[Glasgow-951|Alexander Glasgow]] and Mary/Molly McCaughan.<ref
name="Census1851">[https://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Rasharkin/Lisnagaver/35/ National Archives of Ireland, 1851 census], Lisnagaver household 35; see also
[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QV9Y-WKKX FamilySearch index].</ref>
The often-repeated date 6 November 1840 has not been supported by a birth or
baptism record. The published Rasharkin baptism transcription has no entries
for 1840–1841.

In 1851 Robert was ten and living at Lisnagaver with his parents and siblings
[[Glasgow-973|James]] and [[Glasgow-438|Ann]]. His father was a farmer.<ref
name="Census1851" />

On 5 April 1866 Robert, aged twenty-five, married [[Graham-21720|Jane Graham]].
He was a bachelor farmer of Lisnagaver and named his father Alexander Glasgow,
farmer. Jane lived at Gortereghy.<ref name="Marriage1866">[https://civilrecords.irishgenealogy.ie/churchrecords/images/marriage_returns/marriages_1866/11531/8240963.pdf General Register Office of Ireland, civil marriage register], Robert Glasgow
and Jane Graham, 5 April 1866.</ref>

A Robert Glasgow occupied the separate house ''18c'' at Gortereghy in PRONI's
1864 revision book and continued in the next volume.<ref name="RevisionBooks">Public
Record Office of Northern Ireland, Valuation Revision Books,
''VAL/12/B/4/17A'', folio 21, and ''VAL/12/B/4/17B'', folio 22; [https://apps.proni.gov.uk/Val12B/Search.aspx database].</ref>
The age, residence, occupation and marriage make this Robert the best identity
candidate, but the valuation gives no age or relative, so the identification
remains probable rather than proved.

Robert and Jane later lived in Glasgow, Scotland. The retained census and
valuation citations place the family at 36 Graham Street in 1871, 59 McKechnie
Street in 1881 and 534 Gallowgate in 1891.<ref name="Census1871">[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QV9Y-71S6 FamilySearch, 1871 Scotland census index], Robert Glasgow household.</ref><ref
name="Census1891">Ancestry, 1891 Scotland Census, Robert Glasgow household,
534 Gallowgate.</ref><ref name="ValuationScotland">National Records of
Scotland, Glasgow valuation rolls, 1855–1895, entries attributed to Robert
Glasgow.</ref> His occupations were reported as labourer, mason's labourer and
later flesher or butcher.

The attached family comprises nine children: [[Glasgow-939|William]],
[[Glasgow-1108|Mary]], [[Glasgow-940|James Graham]],
[[Glasgow-937|Alexander]], [[Glasgow-944|Ellen Jane]],
[[Glasgow-941|Agnes]], [[Glasgow-945|Emily]], [[Glasgow-942|Robert]] and
[[Glasgow-943|Matilda]]. A supplied descendant chart reports ninety-nine known
descendants across four generations.<ref>Descendant chart supplied by
[[Glasgow-933|M Glasgow]].</ref>

Robert reportedly died on 27 November 1902 in Glasgow from chronic bronchitis.
The original Scottish death record remains a priority check.

== Research Notes ==

The exact birth date 6 November 1840 should be removed unless its underlying
record is recovered. The defensible estimate is about 1840–1841 from the 1851
census and 1866 marriage.

Daniel Glasgow, another Gortereghy occupier, is not proved to be Robert's
uncle. That depends on the unresolved hypothesis that Alexander was Daniel's
brother; cousinship or another close relationship remains possible.

The attached William's reported 1859 birth predates Robert and Jane's 1866
marriage and requires separate birth and parentage verification.

A different Robert Glasgow appears in a 1901 census entry with children
Matilda, John C., Archibald C. and Alexander; that household should not be
merged into this family merely from the name.<ref>[https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:7Y8M-W6MM FamilySearch, 1901 Scotland census index].</ref>
