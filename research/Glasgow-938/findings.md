# Findings: Glasgow-938

Last updated: 2026-09-05

## Current conclusion

The 5 September master review confirms Robert as Alexander's son from the 1851
household and his 1866 marriage, which names Alexander Glasgow, farmer. The
often-repeated exact birth date 6 November 1840 remains unsupported; retain an
estimated birth from the census and marriage ages.

Robert `Glasgow-938`, not older Robert `Glasgow-1367`, is the materially best
candidate for the Robert Glasgow listed at Gortereghy in Griffith's Valuation.
The identification is strong but still inferential because the valuation gives
no age or relative.

## Identity conclusion

The age, occupation, father's identity, Lisnagaver residence, and bride's
Gortereghy residence form a substantially better chain than the older Robert,
who was still at Glenleslie in 1851. The revision book now shows property
continuity at `18c`; treat the personal identification as probable rather than
certain because neither valuation record supplies an age or relative.
