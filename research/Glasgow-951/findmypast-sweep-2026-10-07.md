# Findmypast sweep for Glasgow-951 — 7 Oct 2026

Scope: a logged-in sweep of Findmypast (record search, transcripts and the British Newspaper Archive) for Alexander Glasgow (Glasgow-951), his wife Mary McCaughan, their children, the Lisnagaver/Gortereagh/Glenleslie Glasgows, and the overseas branches in the 1892 Nancy citation (Plattsburgh, West Troy, Glasgow). The session ran the site's own GraphQL search API from a browser session that the user signed into by hand.

Coverage: 83 record queries (all Glasgow-surname records keyed to 24 parishes and townlands, plus named-person searches), 30 newspaper queries, 312 transcripts and 8 newspaper OCR pages. Raw captures are in `tmp/fmp-951/` (ignored), in the `results/`, `news/`, `transcripts/` and `ocr/` folders.

Every candidate was checked against the existing research corpus. Most Irish hits duplicate records already held from NAI, irishgenealogy.ie, PRONI or FamilySearch: the 1851 Lisnagaver and Glenleslie households, Griffith's Valuation, the 1836 field book, the civil BMD indexes, the Jebb/Madden/Jamieson marriages, the 1861 M'Caughern gable collapse and the 1870 and 1880 Plattsburgh censuses. Only the items below are new.

## New, relevant

1. **Robert-938 in the 1901 Scotland census, at his death address.** Robert Glasgow, boarder, widower, 60, annuitant, born Ireland, was at 58 East Rose Street, Camlachie, Glasgow. The head of the household was Alexander Glasgow, 29. With him were Alexander's wife Mary, 35, their children Thomas 10, Jane G 8, John C 6, Archibald C 3 and Alexander 0, and Matilda Glasgow, boarder, 11, born Glasgow (Findmypast `GBC/1901/0036891791`, `/0036891792`).
   - This is the same address as Robert's death on 27 Nov 1902. Alexander matches Robert's son, born 1871 at 36 Graham Street, and Matilda matches the daughter listed in 1891.
   - **The profile note in Glasgow-938 and Glasgow-951 is wrong.** It says that "a different Robert Glasgow appears in the 1901 census with children Matilda, John C, Archibald C, and Alexander". Those are Robert's son and grandchildren, plus his daughter Matilda.
   - Together with 1871 (Graham St, "Glasco"), 1881 (59 McKechnie St), 1891 (534 Gallowgate), the 6 Jan 1892 "formerly of Rasharkin" notice and the 1902 death, Robert is now traced continuously from 1871 to his death. This closes the audit's open point that "Robert's alleged Scottish survival until 1902 itself needs original-record verification" at index level. The original 1901 return is still worth reading.
2. **Daniel Glasgow, West Troy/Watervliet, New York.** In the 1910 US census Daniel Glasgow, 50 (born c.1860, Ireland; both parents Irish), married, immigrated 1890, was a lodger with James Holmes, Watervliet Ward 1, Albany Co. (`USC/1910/004972898/00195/009`). He also appears in the Albany County Naturalization Index, Book 3, p.242 (`US/NY/ALBANYCO/NATIDX/019593`).
   - He is a candidate for **Daniel Neely Glasgow** (Glasgow-2413). Daniel Neely married in 1888 at Boveedy and later lived "somewhere in New York". The candidate is in the same town as the 1880 Lizzie Glasgow and the Dysart West Troy branch.
   - Unproved. Next step: the naturalization file (Book 3 p.242) for birth date, place and arrival.
3. **Elizabeth Glasgow, Cohoes, New York, 1940.** Head of household, 80, single, born Northern Ireland, in the same house in 1935; Cohoes Ward 3, Albany Co. (`USC/1940/1558009575`).
   - She is a candidate for the **Lizzie Glasgow** of John's cited branch: 1878 arrival aged 22, and in 1880 a 21-year-old Irish-born domestic in West Troy, which adjoins Cohoes.
   - Unproved. Next step: her death record (c.1940s, Cohoes) for parents' names.
4. **Robert Glascow, Ellenburgh, Clinton County, New York, 1860.** Robert Glascow, 35 (born c.1825, Ireland), with wife Martha, 26 (born Canada), and children Jane 8, John 6, Martha 4, Robert 2 and David under 1 (`USC/1860/0000669197382`).
   - This is the only Irish-born Glasgow man found in Clinton County in Nancy's lifetime. He is a lead for a Plattsburgh relative, but there is no link to Nancy yet.
5. **The 1889 "Mrs Smith (late Mrs Glasgow)" items: new details on a known event.**
   - *Coleraine Chronicle*, 23 Feb 1889, p.1: "Smith and Glasgow — At Ballymena, on the 18th inst., John Smith, formerly of Moneydie [Perthshire], but now of Rosdernat [Rosedermot], Cloughmills, to Mrs. Glasgow, Lisnagaver, Rasharkin" (BL/0001283/18890223/002). The civil index has Mary Anne Glasgow, Ballymena, Jan–Mar 1889, vol. 1 p.127; John Smith is on the same index page.
   - *Ballymena Observer*, 29 Mar 1889, p.3: Samuel Hood, auctioneer, was instructed by "Mrs. Smith (late Mrs. Glasgow), of Lisnagaver" to sell, on 1 Apr 1889, hay, corn, potatoes and the entire household furniture, including "3 Eight-day Clocks" (BL/0001426/18890329/067).
   - The corpus already has the 30 Mar 1889 Exchequer judgment against "Mary Ann Smith otherwise Glasgow, wife of John Smith". What is new is Smith's Perthshire origin, the exact wedding and notice, and the dispersal sale. These belong to the Thomas-987 file rather than 951.

## Minor or negative

- **Nancy's emigration:** a Nancy Glasgow, 46 (so born c.1803), arrived at New York on 2 Jul 1849 from Belfast on the *Marianne*. Other Glasgows on the voyage: Wm, Matilda, Jane, Henry, Adam, Isaac, Benjamin, Alexander and James (`US/MIGR/PASSENGERLIST/304240`). Her age does not fit Nancy-4070 (born c.1815–20), so this is a weak lead only.
- **Prerogative index entry:** Robert Glasgow, Dunaghy, Kilconway, 1854, appears in the *Prerogative* index, not only the Connor diocesan index (`IRE/DIOC/WILL/00186040`). This is the Glenleslie Robert (Glasgow-1367) already noted from PRONI. A Prerogative grant could survive in the Betham or prerogative abstracts.
- **1851 Rasharkin deaths table:** Findmypast indexes one Lisnagaver Glasgow death, Eliza, aged 1, of Thomas's family (family 37). It indexes none for Alexander's family (35). So the deaths section does not record the first James (born 1837) or the unnamed 1838 child as dying between 1841 and 1851. This is index-level; the original page should be checked.
- **Not found:** Plattsburgh Glasgows beyond Nancy (only her 1870 and 1880 census entries); Margaret Kennedy and Robert Anderson, Nancy's niece and nephew, in Plattsburgh. The Robert Anderson in 1850 was a 21-year-old born in New York, not the nephew. Also not found: any 1888 or 1892 BNA death notice for Alexander or Mary, a Findmypast index entry for Alexander's marriage, and any McCaughan record that names Mary's parents.
- **BNA newspaper hits** for Lisnagaver, Gortahar, Gortereagh, Glenleslie, Dirraw and Drumack, matched on personal names, were reviewed. No new items beyond those above.
