## Controlling conclusion

The ledger defines research units, not one pedigree. Alexander-951 and James
J-2738 still have no established parents. Adam's headcount, the Lisnagaver
acreage fit, William's chronology and the Y-DNA distances are useful tests but
cannot replace an explicit identity or relationship bridge. Claims not supported
by an inspected original remain labelled as transcription, catalogue lead,
earlier-audit recovery or descendant tradition.
