# Male candidate audit for Alexander Glasgow `Glasgow-951`

Date: 2026-08-05

## Result

No contemporary record found in this sweep names Alexander's parents. The best
specific hypothesis remains older Alexander `Glasgow-3904` and Rosanna Unknown,
because descendant testimony names them as Thomas's parents and Daniel is proved
to be Thomas's brother. Alexander's relationship to those brothers is still
probable rather than proved.

The strongest new collateral lead is Adam `Glasgow-1622`. His son James
`Glasgow-1623`, born about 1812/13, was a farmer of Inishrush and is almost
exactly Alexander's age. James and Alexander could be brothers or cousins, but
the original marriage names no mother and no Alexander.

## Evidence that changes the land hypothesis

The 1834 Lisnagaver holdings of Alexander (29 acres) and Robert (35 acres) total
64 acres. Later lots held by John, Alexander and Thomas total about 63 acres.
That is strong continuity of a Glasgow tenancy block, but not proof of one
inheritance. The original 1846 marriage of lot-24 John names his father as **John
Glasgow, farmer**. The three later occupiers therefore were not simply three
proved sons of the two 1834 men.

## New original record

[James Glasgow–Rosanna Barkley marriage, 17 March 1851](https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/view/?record_id=cima-3208068): James, 38, bachelor,
farmer of Inishrush, son of Adam Glasgow, married Rosanna Barkley, about 25, farm
servant of Tyanee, daughter of Henry Barkley. This exact match identifies them as
the compelling couple behind the 1847 James/Barkly report, formerly attributed
to older James `Glasgow-3903`. It does not name the reported child.
