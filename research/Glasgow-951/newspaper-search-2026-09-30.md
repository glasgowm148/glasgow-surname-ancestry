# Free newspaper searches — 30 September 2026

**Outcome:** no new parent or sibling statement for Alexander-951. John Glasgow,
farmer, remains his probable father; his mother remains unknown. These are
bounded searches of two resources linked by News4Clues, not exhaustive searches
of New York or Michigan newspapers.

  The collection identifies itself as Sandusky, **Sanilac County**, Michigan.
  Searches below cover its entire searchable collection, including cemetery
  entries, histories and obituaries, with no date restriction. This is not a
  comprehensive Tecumseh/Lenawee County newspaper collection.

The opened **Alex Glascow** cemetery entry gives Lakeside Cemetery, Port Huron,
St Clair County, section F; DOB **9/6/1842**, DOD **10/28/1915**. Those dates are
reported exactly as displayed. This man cannot be Alexander-951 or the
c.1807 Canadian Alexander. The repeated title in the results did not provide an
independent family identification. Record identifier:
`bfcde6e6-c692-4011-b87a-adc61846d131`, collection tote
`5524c4b6-602f-4732-8b31-6a3285eed301`.

The 1966 newspaper hit remains an **uninspected lead**, not a rejected match.
The viewer showed no readable page; direct image viewing and the browser's
asset export did not yield a usable image. Its issue identifier is
`bc793c16-f3ad-4a9c-b942-79fad8d8bc69`, tote
`897f97b8-e88e-4c4d-b452-a136042dfcad`. A late issue can contain historical
material, so its date alone does not exclude relevance. A `James Glascow`
exact-phrase attempt was also submitted, but its completed result was not
captured; it is not counted as a negative search.

## Consequence and remaining work

The new collections are useful supplementary routes, but their coverage does
not close the principal Plattsburgh, Rochester or Tecumseh searches. No identity
or parentage confidence changes. The existing 1891 Plattsburgh notice and 1896
Tecumseh report remain the relevant located newspaper evidence. Nancy's estate
and independent sibling parent statements remain stronger tests of Alexander's
parentage. No correspondence or live WikiTree edit was made.
