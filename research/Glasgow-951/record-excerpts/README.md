# Original record excerpts for Alexander Glasgow

Six source-reviewed excerpts from four originals, reviewed 13 September 2026. `manifest.json` is the maintained reading and annotation input. `file` paths are relative to this directory. `context_file` retains the wider row/table locator.

The PNGs contain original source pixels extracted with Poppler `pdfimages -png`, followed only by a 180° rotation for the census and rectangular crops. No resampling, contrast change, ink cleanup or reconstruction was applied. `crop_box` is `[left, top, right, bottom]` in the source image after the stated rotation; image dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are recorded. All six crops were checked pixel-for-pixel against those source regions.

Every highlight was visually reviewed on the exact exported image and in a separate proof under `proofs/`. These proof images contain orange guide rectangles and a separate numbered legend; they are QA artifacts, never the original shown by default. The renderer must display original pixels initially and show at most one selected outline where full-letterform regions overlap. Neighbouring strokes and column crossings remain visible.

Original references and specific interpretation limits accompany each entry. In particular, 1836 is the marriage year reported in the 1851 census, not a newly found marriage registration; the 1888 deceased's identity remains a strong circumstantial attribution. Nancy's relationship as daughter and residence Gortahar are explicit, while her identification with Ann is unresolved.
