== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' was born on 9 October 1843 at '''Lisnagaver''', County Antrim, Ireland, a son of [[Glasgow-951|Alexander Glasgow]] and Mary or Molly McCaughan. The published transcription of the Rasharkin Presbyterian register records his baptism on 7 January 1844 and names his parents as Alex Glasgow and Molly McAughern.<ref name="RasharkinBaptism">Richard John Torrens, [https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/RasharkinP/Baptisms_1834.html "Rasharkin Presbyterian: Baptisms 1834 onward"], entry for James Glasgow, born 9 October 1843 and baptised 7 January 1844, parents Alex and Molly McAughern, Lisnagaver. This is a transcription; the original register is PRONI MIC.1P/292.</ref>

In 1851 James was seven years old and attending school in his parents' household at Lisnagaver. The household also contained his mother Mary, brother [[Glasgow-938|Robert Glasgow]] and sister [[Glasgow-438|Ann Glasgow]].<ref name="Census1851">National Archives of Ireland, ''Census of Ireland, 1851'', Lisnagaver, Rasharkin, County Antrim, household 35, Alexander Glasgow household; [http://www.census.nationalarchives.ie/pages/1851/Antrim/Kilconway/Rasharkin/Lisnagaver/35/ household return]. See also [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QV9Y-WKKX FamilySearch record QV9Y-WKKX] and the [https://durrushistory.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/1851irishcensusreturnscommadelimitedtextfilejul24_071.pdf public comma-delimited transcription], p. 182.</ref>

No record inspected so far proves James's life after 1851.

== Research Notes ==

=== Birthplace ===

James's birthplace is '''Lisnagaver townland in Rasharkin civil parish, County Antrim'''. It is not Lisnagarvey, the historical name of Lisburn. The structured birth-location field should be corrected accordingly.<ref name="RasharkinBaptism" />

=== Possible later life in Michigan ===

A James Glasgow who lived at Tecumseh, Lenawee County, Michigan, is a strong but unproved later-life candidate. The evidence has not yet supplied his parents, exact birthday or townland, so the Michigan wife, children, death date and migration should not be attached to this profile as facts.

The existing [https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/127743880/james-glasgow Find a Grave memorial 127743880] proposes death on 29 August 1896. Both the Michigan historical index and the contemporary newspaper report establish 30 July 1896 for the Tecumseh candidate, so the memorial date should not be used.

The [https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/MQ57-BVR current FamilySearch tree profile MQ57-BVR] attaches the Lisnagaver 1851 census to these Michigan records. An [https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/MDBX-Y1G earlier FamilySearch identifier, MDBX-Y1G] was also recorded as a lead. FamilySearch tree links are derivative conclusions, not independent evidence that the child and adult were the same man. Several of the current profile's 20 source attachments are duplicate extractions of the same underlying event.

=== Dromore identification ===

A James Glasgow did occupy a house and small garden at plot 18b in Dromore, Rasharkin parish. The original PRONI valuation-revision books carry that name on the same holding from `VAL/12/B/3/13A` (1863-79) through `VAL/12/B/3/13C` (1891-1901). This continuing local valuation identity is unlikely to be the Tecumseh man, who was documented in Michigan by 1869 and reported residence there from about 1866. Revision-book names can lag an occupier's departure, however, and the Dromore man's identity has not been proved.

=== The unnamed 1838 sibling ===

[[Glasgow-3940|The unnamed child baptised in 1838]] should not be renamed Samuel or identified with Samuel Glasgow of Franklin and Blissfield, Michigan. Alexander's 1851 return lists no such child as resident, absent or dead since 1841, and no American record inspected names Samuel's parents or County Antrim.
