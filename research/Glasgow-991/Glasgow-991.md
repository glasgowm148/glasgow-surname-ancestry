== Biography ==

'''James Glasgow''' is the profile currently used for a father named in six
Midlothian baptism indexes between 1605 and 1618. Three children were recorded
at Mid Calder:

* [[Glasgow-1082|George Glasgow]], christened 17 February 1605.<ref
  name="George1605">“Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch,
  [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BK-1BW George Glasgow, 1605],
  father James Glasgow, Mid Calder.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-1083|Beatrix Glasgow]], christened 15 April 1610.<ref
  name="Beatrix1610">“Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch,
  [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BJ-L51 Beatrix Glasgow, 1610],
  father James Glasgow, Mid Calder.</ref>
* [[Glasgow-1084|John Glasgow]], christened 18 February 1616.<ref
  name="John1616">“Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch,
  [https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1BL-JXY John Glasgow, 1616], father
  James Glasgow, Mid Calder.</ref>

Three more children were recorded to a father of the same name at
Inveresk/Musselburgh:

  name="Eduard1609">“Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch,

== Research Notes ==

=== One father or two ===

The alternating Mid Calder and Inveresk/Musselburgh sequences may represent one
mobile family or two contemporaries named James Glasgow. The indexes name no
mother, occupation or residence precise enough to resolve that question. Keep
the present children together only as an explicit working reconstruction until
original entries, witnesses or another relational record distinguish the men.

=== Parentage correction ===

This James was formerly treated as the likely son of [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew
Glasgow of uncertain Corsoun]] because an earlier transcription of Andrew's testament
was believed to name a son James. Re-reading the original images instead names
Robert as Andrew and Elizat Stevinsoun's son and names daughters Marion, Janet
and Isobel.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court,
CC8/8/32, images 123–124, testament testamentar and inventory of Andro Glasgw,
made 6 February 1597/8 and confirmed 10 July 1598; corrected working
transcription of the original register images.</ref> The testament is therefore
not evidence that Andrew was James's father.

[[Glasgow-3183|Jonet Glasgow]], who married Martin Ramsy at Mid Calder in 1624,
is a possible daughter of this James based on locality and generation only.<ref>“Scotland
Marriages, 1561–1910,” FamilySearch,
[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XY9X-4PR Jonet Glasgow and Martin
Ramsy, 1624], Mid Calder.</ref> The corrected testament also makes Andrew a
competing father candidate for her; neither placement is proved.

An Inveresk birth for James and an attached wife have not been shown to belong
to this profile. A James Glas, son of Alexander Glas, was baptised at Perth on
19 January 1575, but no evidence carries that child into the later Midlothian
records.<ref>Church of Scotland Old Parish Register, Perth, parish 387, volume
10, p. 172, baptism of James Glas, son of Alexander Glas, 19 January 1575;
indexed on FamilySearch profile
[https://www.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/details/LZGY-NSY LZGY-NSY].</ref>
