# Findings: [William Glasgow (Glasgow-992), about 1615](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-992)

Last updated: 2026-09-10

## Current conclusion

William and Bessie Howie are indexed in Glasgow's banns and marriages on
5 December 1635 (OPR 644/1/230/126). This is earlier than the live tree's
1640 marriage date and consistent with children recorded from 1636. Their
existing marriage date remains uncertain pending the original: the combined
index does not distinguish proclamation from the ceremony. This is new
chronological evidence requiring a WikiTree note, not just a confirming citation.

[Margaret Stewart (Stewart-65226)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Stewart-65226)
is the only proposed missing mother from the graph audit with an exposed
transcription capable of direct proof. Her profile cites Glasgow High Kirk
register `1/754` for a **lawful son of George Glasgow and Margaret Steuart**,
with William Howie and David Scherrar as witnesses. The date range and William
Howie association strongly suggest that this is William-992's entry.

The transcription on Stewart-65226 omits the child's name and event date,
however, and William-992 currently cites no baptism at all. Do not attach the
mother from graph structure alone.

## Recommended update

1. Retrieve the complete Scotland's People entry `1/754` and record the child's
   name, exact date, parish and event type.
2. If it names William, attach Stewart-65226 as confirmed mother and add the
   full citation to both profiles.
3. Do not use sibling status or the single-wife assumption as substitute proof.
4. Separately source William's marriage and children; the current biography is
   largely reconstruction.
