== Biography ==

William Glasgow married [[Howie-1119|Bessie Howie (abt.1620-)]], and they had seven children between 1636 and 1648 in Glasgow. A "William Howie" appears as the guardian of [[Glasgow-1101|Archibald Glasgow (1614-)]]. It is likely that [[Glasgow-1099|George Glasgow (abt.1575-aft.1640)]] named a son William after the proposed family ancestor, [[Glasgow-1098|William Glasgow (abt.1504-bef.1585)]].

An Old Parish Register index records William Glasgow and Bessie Howie at Glasgow on 5 December 1635, parish 644/1, reference 230/126. This predates their children's recorded baptisms, but the available combined index does not establish whether its date records banns, proclamation, or the marriage ceremony. The existing structured 1640 marriage date is uncertain pending inspection of the original register.<ref>National Records of Scotland, ''Old Parish Registers, Banns and Marriages'', Glasgow, entry for William Glasgow and Bessie Howie, 5 December 1635, parish 644/1, reference 230/126; [https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/ Scotland's People], exact-surname Glasgow search through 1700, index extract supplied 10 September 2026; original images not inspected.</ref>
