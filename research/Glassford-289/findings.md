# Findings: [Rev. John Glassford (Glassford-289)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glassford-289)

## Current conclusion

Rev. John Glasford of Kilwinning is, with high confidence, the son of Patrick Glasfurd of Walltour, burgess and notary of Cupar, and Margaret Lucklaw or Lucklay. He married Jean Blair, not Jonet Murriell. His documented son John is [Glassfuird-1](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glassfuird-1). The Edinburgh slater and Murriell household has been split to [Glassfuird-2](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glassfuird-2).

John's exact birth is unresolved. The live profile records a birth before 1593, probably at Cupar; the biography explains this as an adult-age estimate from his 1614 admission to the ministry, rather than a baptismal date. Fasti gives his death as December 1636.

## Identity assessment — the slater's 1613 apprenticeship, 28 September 2026

The Edinburgh apprentice register (Francis J. Grant, ed., *Register of Apprentices of the City of Edinburgh, 1583–1666*, p. 71, [full text](https://archive.org/stream/registerofappren35edin/registerofappren35edin_djvu.txt); printed transcription) records on 28 April 1613 John Glassfuird, son of the late John Glassfuird, miller at Dalry mills, apprenticed to Walter Murriall, slater. The trade and the Murriall surname make him very probably the slater who married Janet Murriell on 15 July 1619, which gives the split of the slater ([Glassfuird-2](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glassfuird-2)) from this minister an independent footing; see Glassfuird-2's findings. The Edinburgh children attached to Jonet Morriel belong to the slater's household, including Euphame, whose 1634 FamilySearch index ([X1Y9-V87](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1Y9-V87)) gives the father as Johnne Glasgow; that spelling is an indexing or clerical variant within the slater's family, not evidence for the minister. The baptism originals have not been checked.

## Source findings — end of the ministry and the son's 1672 service, 28 September 2026

*Fasti Ecclesiae Scoticanae*, new ed., vol. III, [p. 116](https://archive.org/stream/fastiecclesiaesc03scot/fastiecclesiaesc03scot_djvu.txt) (compiled), reports that the minister's son John ([Glassfuird-1](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glassfuird-1)) was served heir to his father in lands in Kilwinning parish on 28 February 1672, citing Ayr retour no. 577; the retour has not been seen. *A Short History of Kilwinning Parish*, 2nd ed. ([Kilwinning Heritage PDF](https://www.kilwinningheritage.org.uk/office/resources/documents/a-short-history-of-kilwinning-parish-2nd-ed.pdf)), ends his incumbency by 1629, while *Fasti* gives Robert Baillie as successor in 1631, so he had left the charge by 1629–31.

A descent from this minister to the Tobacco Lord John Glassford of Dougalston (1715–1783) is unproved. Wikipedia, cited in the draft only as background, gives the Tobacco Lord's father as James Glassford, merchant in Paisley; no record connects that James to the minister's son John.
