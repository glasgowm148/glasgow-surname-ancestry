== Biography ==

'''John Glassfuird''' was the son of [[Glassford-289|Rev. John Glasford]], minister of Kilwinning, and [[Blair-16146|Jean Blair]]. He was born before August 1637, a conservative bound allowing for a possible posthumous birth after his father's reported death in December 1636; no baptism has been identified.

Edinburgh's apprentice register records that on 6 July 1642 John Glassfuird, son of the late John Glassfuird, minister at Kilwinning, was apprenticed to James Stevenson, merchant.<ref name="Apprentices">Francis J. Grant, ed., ''The Register of Apprentices of the City of Edinburgh, 1583-1666'' (Edinburgh, 1906), p. 71; [https://archive.org/stream/registerofappren35edin/registerofappren35edin_djvu.txt Internet Archive transcription].</ref>

''Fasti Ecclesiae Scoticanae'' reports that John was served heir to his father in certain lands in Kilwinning parish on 28 February 1672, citing Ayr retour no. 577.<ref name="Fasti">Hew Scott, ''Fasti Ecclesiae Scoticanae'', new ed., vol. III (Edinburgh, 1920), p. 116; [https://archive.org/stream/fastiecclesiaesc03scot/fastiecclesiaesc03scot_djvu.txt Internet Archive transcription].</ref>

== Research Notes ==
