## Source findings — service as heir, 1672, from the biography review, 28 September 2026

*Fasti Ecclesiae Scoticanae*, new ed., vol. III (1920), [p. 116](https://archive.org/stream/fastiecclesiaesc03scot/fastiecclesiaesc03scot_djvu.txt) (compiled), reports that John was served heir to his father, the minister of Kilwinning, in lands in Kilwinning parish on 28 February 1672, citing Ayr retour no. 577. The same page names the minister's wife as Jean Blair and John among their children. John's birth is bounded only as before August 1637, an estimate allowing for a posthumous birth after his father's reported death in December 1636; no baptism has been found. The retour, and John's wife and children, are needed before any descent to James Glassford, merchant in Paisley, or the Tobacco Lord family is tested.
