# Findings: [Peggy Hamilton (Hamilton-25516)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hamilton-25516)

Last updated: 2026-10-07

## Current conclusion

The surviving 1851 Dunaghy census directly records Peggy Glasgo, aged 40 and
born in County Antrim, as James Glasgo's wife at Ballycregagh. Both reported an
1848 marriage year. Peggy was alive in 1851, so the biography's statement that
she died about 1811 was a birth/death error.

The return identifies Nancy and Jane Hamilton as Peggy's sisters and Robert and
Catherine Hamilton as her parents. It calls the Glasgow children James's
children, not Peggy's. Mary Glasgow of the 1847 workhouse group is a stronger
mother candidate for the older children, but Peggy's earlier maternity is not
impossible and her links should remain uncertain until a better replacement is
proved.

## Source findings — original 1851 census images

The original 1851 return for James Glasgow's household at Ballycraigargh (Ballycregagh), Dunaghy, household 29, survives in two parts: the [living household, image 251](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QSQ-G94X-YD22) and the [mortality return, image 252](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L94X-Y621). These original images supersede the derivative transcription as the citation for Peggy's age (40), birthplace, 1848 marriage and housework occupation. The mortality return records her father [Robert Hamilton (Hamilton-44031)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hamilton-44031), linen weaver, aged 60, and her mother [Cathrin (Unknown-764457)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Unknown-764457), aged 58, both dying in spring 1848. Her unmarried sisters [Nancy (Hamilton-44032)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hamilton-44032) and [Jane (Hamilton-44033)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hamilton-44033) were in the living household.
