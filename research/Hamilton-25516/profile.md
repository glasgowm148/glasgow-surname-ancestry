== Biography ==

'''Peggy Hamilton''' was born about 1811 in County Antrim, Ireland, calculated from her reported age of 40 in the surviving 1851 census.<ref name="Census1851">1851 Census of Ireland, County Antrim, Dunaghy parish, Ballycraigargh, household 29, James Glasgow household: [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QSQ-G94X-YD22 living household, image 251] and [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L94X-Y621 mortality return, image 252].</ref>

Peggy married [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]] in 1848. In 1851 they lived at Ballycregagh, Dunaghy, County Antrim, where Peggy was recorded as James's wife and doing housework. Her unmarried sisters [[Hamilton-44032|Nancy Hamilton]] and [[Hamilton-44033|Jane Hamilton]] were in the household. The mortality portion of the same return records [[Hamilton-44031|Robert Hamilton]], a linen weaver, as James's father-in-law and [[Unknown-764457|Cathrin Hamilton]] as his mother-in-law. Because Peggy is named as James's wife, this directly identifies Robert and Cathrin as Peggy's parents. Both died in spring 1848.<ref name="Census1851" />

Peggy was alive at the census and therefore died after 1851. Her exact death date and place have not been identified.

== Research Notes ==

=== Children ===

The 1851 return directly calls several people James's children but does not explicitly state that Peggy was their mother. Most were born before the couple's reported 1848 marriage. That makes [[Unknown-764460|Mary Glasgow]] of the matching 1847 Glenleslie workhouse group a stronger mother candidate for the older children, but the marriage year alone does not make Peggy's earlier biological maternity impossible. Peggy should remain attached to those children with uncertain confidence until a better-supported mother can replace her.
