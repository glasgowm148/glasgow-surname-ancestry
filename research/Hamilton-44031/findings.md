# Robert Hamilton (Hamilton-44031)

## Conclusion

The original 1851 census mortality return proves Robert Hamilton as the father of Peggy Hamilton and husband of Cathrin. Robert was a linen weaver, aged 60 when he died in spring 1848.

## Evidence

| Record | Direct statement | Relationship status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1851 census, Ballycraigargh household 29, mortality return | Robert Hamilton was father-in-law of household head James Glasgow; Peggy Hamilton was James's wife; Cathrin was his mother-in-law | Robert and Cathrin are Peggy's certain parents |
