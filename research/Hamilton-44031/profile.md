== Biography ==

Robert Hamilton was born about 1788, estimated from his recorded age of 60 at death. He was a linen weaver in County Antrim, Ireland.

Robert died in spring 1848. The mortality portion of the surviving 1851 census recorded him in the household of [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]] as James's father-in-law. The same household names James's wife as [[Hamilton-25516|Peggy Hamilton]] and records [[Unknown-764457|Cathrin Hamilton]] as his mother-in-law. The living household also records unmarried [[Hamilton-44032|Nancy Hamilton]] and [[Hamilton-44033|Jane Hamilton]] as James's sisters-in-law. Together, the two portions directly identify Peggy, Nancy and Jane as daughters of Robert and Cathrin.<ref name="Census1851">1851 Census of Ireland, County Antrim, Dunaghy parish, Ballycraigargh, household 29, James Glasgow household: [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QSQ-G94X-YD22 living household, image 251] and [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L94X-Y621 mortality return, image 252].</ref>
