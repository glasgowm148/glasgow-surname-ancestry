Nancy Hamilton was an unmarried linen weaver who could read, aged 25 and born in County Antrim, living in 1851 in the household of James Glasgow at Ballycraigargh, Dunaghy, where the original census return calls her his sister-in-law. With her surname, that makes her very probably a sister of his wife Peggy Hamilton (Hamilton-25516), and so probably a daughter of Robert Hamilton (Hamilton-44031) and Cathrin (Unknown-764457), whom the household's mortality return names as James's parents-in-law; this is an inference, as no record names her own parents. Her birth about 1826 is calculated from her age, and her later life is not identified.
