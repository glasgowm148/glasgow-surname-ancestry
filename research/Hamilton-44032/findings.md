## Source findings — 1851 census particulars

In the [1851 living return for James Glasgow's household, Ballycraigargh, Dunaghy, household 29](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QSQ-G94X-YD22), Nancy Hamilton, his unmarried sister-in-law, was aged 25, a linen weaver who could read, born in County Antrim. Original image. Birth about 1826 is calculated from the age. She was alive in 1851; her later life is not identified.
