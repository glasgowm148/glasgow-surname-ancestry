== Biography ==

Jane Hamilton was born about 1835 in County Antrim, Ireland, estimated from her age of 16 in the surviving 1851 census. She was an unmarried linen weaver and could read.

Jane lived in the Ballycraigargh household of [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]], where she was expressly recorded as his unmarried sister-in-law. James's wife was [[Hamilton-25516|Peggy Hamilton]]. The mortality return records [[Hamilton-44031|Robert Hamilton]] and [[Unknown-764457|Cathrin Hamilton]] as James's father-in-law and mother-in-law. Jane's unmarried status, Hamilton surname and relationship to James therefore identify her as Peggy's sister and a daughter of Robert and Cathrin.<ref name="Census1851">1851 Census of Ireland, County Antrim, Dunaghy parish, Ballycraigargh, household 29, James Glasgow household: [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QSQ-G94X-YD22 living household, image 251] and [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L94X-Y621 mortality return, image 252].</ref>

Jane was alive in 1851. Her later life has not yet been identified.
