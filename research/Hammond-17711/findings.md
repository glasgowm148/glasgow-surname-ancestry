# Findings: [Sarah Hammond (Hammond-17711)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hammond-17711)

## Current conclusion

Sarah Hammond married Samuel Prime at Bickenhill, Warwickshire, on 15 June
1738. She is probably the mother of the younger Samuel Prime baptised there in
1746, but his baptism names only his father. No reviewed source establishes
Sarah's birth, death or parents.

## Source findings

- [Samuel Prime and Sarah Hammond marriage NV8V-3XT](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:NV8V-3XT): Samuel Prime married Sarah Hammond on 15 June 1738 at Bickenhill, Warwickshire. This directly supports Sarah's name, marriage and presence at Bickenhill.
- [Younger Samuel Prime baptism XXGD-R5G](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XXGD-R5G): Samuel was baptised in 1746 at Bickenhill, son of Samuel Prime. The index does not name the mother.
- FamilySearch tree profile: [Sarah MKM9-RMF](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/MKM9-RMF). This is an identity aid rather than independent evidence.

## Relationship assessment

Sarah's marriage to [Samuel Prime (Prime-955)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Prime-955)
is directly documented. Her maternity of
[Samuel Prime (Prime-954)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Prime-954) is an
evidence-based inference from the marriage, chronology and shared Bickenhill
setting, not a direct statement in the son's baptism. WikiTree correctly needs
that mother-child relationship marked uncertain.

Bickenhill may be used as an uncertain vital-event location because it is
Sarah's documented marriage place. It is not evidence that she was born or
died there.
