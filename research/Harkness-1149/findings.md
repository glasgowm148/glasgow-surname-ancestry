# Findings: Mary Harkness (Harkness-1149)

## Current conclusion

No contemporary record reviewed to date identifies Mary Harkness as the wife
of James Glasgow or the mother of Robert, Arthur and Joseph Glasgow.

## FamilySearch full-text deed pass - 21 August 2026

Exact-name searches for Mary Harkness and Mary Glasgow in eighteenth-century
Moneymore, Derryloran, Cookstown and Kilrea produced no matching record. The
same searches returned several James Glasgow deeds, but inspection identified
other men in Larne and Aghadonvane/Leitrim-Donegal. None names Mary or links a
Harkness woman to James Glasgow or to Robert, Arthur and Joseph Glasgow. This
pass supplies no evidence for Mary Harkness as the wife of Glasgow-1024 or the
mother of the emigrant siblings.
