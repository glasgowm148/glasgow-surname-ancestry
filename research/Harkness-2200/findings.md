# Findings: James Harkness of Oritor

Last updated: 2026-08-20

## Current conclusion

[Harkness-2200](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Harkness-2200) represents James
Harkness, born at Oritor, County Tyrone, husband of Margaret
`Glasgow-4026` and father of William Glasgow Harkness `Harkness-2201`.

The c.1820 birth is a working estimate only. Exact dates, parents, marriage,
immigration and death remain unknown.

James is not `Harkness-979`. That man married Annie Maria, had a different
documented family, remained in Ireland through the 1901 census and died in
County Donegal in 1911.
