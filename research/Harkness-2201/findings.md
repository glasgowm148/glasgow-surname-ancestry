# Findings: William Glasgow Harkness

Last updated: 2026-08-20

## Current conclusion

[Harkness-2201](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Harkness-2201) represents William
Glasgow Harkness, born at Jacksonville, Illinois, son of James Harkness
`Harkness-2200` and Margaret Glasgow `Glasgow-4026`.

In the 1892 biographical register he lived at 18 North Compton Avenue, St
Louis, Missouri, and was secretary of the Arkansas Land Company. The c.1850
birth is a working estimate only and should be replaced by a contemporary
record.
