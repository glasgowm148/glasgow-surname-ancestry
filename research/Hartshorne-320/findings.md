## Current conclusion

Ann Hartshorne was recorded as the wife of James Glasgow (Glasgow-2868) of Cecil County, Maryland, and as having died in 1784, the year a Find a Grave memorial gives for her husband's death; the couple's recorded children are Samuel, Hannah, James, Elizabeth, Adam, Ezekiel and William. A 1786 notice in which Robert Glasgow and Joshua Hartshorn were to give possession of 260 acres in North Susquehanna Hundred, Cecil County, is compatible with the settlement of the couple's estate, but it states no kinship, and no Robert appears among their recorded children.
