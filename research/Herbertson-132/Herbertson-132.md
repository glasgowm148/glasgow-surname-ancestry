== Biography ==
'''Helen Herbertson''' was born in Scotland, likely around 1520–1530. She was the sister of [[Herbison-440|Sir William Herbesoun]], a Roman priest who connected the Herbertson family with the Herbesoun or Herbert line.

Helen married [[Hutcheson-1647|Thomas Hutcheson]], a substantial landowner near Glasgow. Through this marriage, the Hutcheson family line became linked to significant property holdings at Lambhill, Hutchesontoun, and Gairbraid.

Helen was the mother of at least five children: George Hutcheson, Thomas Hutcheson Jr., and three daughters. Her sons would go on to found Hutchesons’ Hospital and School, benefitting Glasgow’s poor and orphaned for centuries.
