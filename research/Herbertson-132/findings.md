## Source findings — Helen's marriage and her brother Sir William, from the biography review, 28 September 2026

*The History of Glasgow*, vol. 2, [ch. XX](https://www.electricscotland.com/history/glasgow/glasgow2_20.htm), says that the mother of George Hutcheson "was Helen, sister of Sir William Herbertson, a Roman priest, and probably a cousin of the Hutchesons" (compiled history, quoted in the captured biography of [Sir William (Herbison-440)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Herbison-440)). W. H. Hill, *Early Records of an Old Glasgow Family* (1902), [p. 78](https://books.google.com/books?id=D9MMAwAAQBAJ&pg=PA78), as cited in the captured biography of [Thomas Hutcheson (Hutcheson-1647)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hutcheson-1647), abstracts charters of 15 April 1579, 11 May 1583 and 6 January 1587 to Thomas Hutcheson and his wife Helen Herbertson. Neither work was re-read for this note. Before 1600 the Scottish year began on 25 March, so the January 1587 date may be 1587/88 unless Hill modernised it.

The sibling statement comes from a compiled history, not a contemporary record. Protocol 1618 of 18 February 1568/69, which calls Sir William and George Herbertsone brothers german, does not mention Helen. If that George is the man represented by Helen's attached father [Herbertson-133](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Herbertson-133), the same structural question raised on Sir William's page applies here (inference; not tested).
