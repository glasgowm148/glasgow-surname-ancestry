# Findings: Margaret Herbesoun (Herbison-439)

Last updated: 2026-08-30

## Live profile update — 30 August 2026

Saved and verified the correction from the wrong William `Glasgow-1098` to
Margaret's husband William `Glasgow-3522`, with direct citations to protocols
2903 and 2916. The biography now limits Sir William Herbesoun to a possible
relative because his curatorship does not prove that he was Margaret's father.
No attached person was removed or detached.

## Current conclusion

Protocol 2916 of 1 September 1586 directly calls Margaret Herbesoun the mother
of George `Glasgow-1099`, son and heir of William `Glasgow-3522`. No located
record names Barbara `Glasgow-567` as Margaret's daughter. Detach that maternity
edge; retain George.

The attached father, [Sir William Herbesoun
(Herbison-440)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Herbison-440), is not proved and
the surrounding live Herbesoun pedigree contains a direct structural error.
Protocol 1618 of 18 February 1568/69 calls Sir William and George Herbertsone
**brothers german**. Sir William therefore cannot simultaneously be the son of
the same George represented by
[Herbertson-133](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Herbertson-133). Sir William's
1586 curatorship of Margaret's minor son proves a close family connection but
does not identify him as Margaret's father. Margaret could instead have been a
sister, niece or other close kinswoman of Sir William and George.
