## Relationship assessment — protocol 1618 calls Sir William and George Herbertsone brothers german, 18 February 1568/69

Robert Renwick, ed., *Abstracts of Protocols of the Town Clerks of Glasgow*, vol. 6, protocol 1618, [18 February 1568/69, printed p. 11](https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc01renwgoog/page/n27/mode/2up) (printed abstract, as read in `research/Herbison-439/findings.md` for [Margaret Herbesoun (Herbison-439)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Herbison-439)), records Sir William Herbertsone resigning an annualrent in favour of George Herbertsone, "his brother german". The protocol proves that Sir William and this George were full brothers. It names neither man's parents.

Margaret Herbesoun's findings take this George to be the man represented by [George Herbertson (Herbertson-133)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Herbertson-133), who is attached as Sir William's father; if that identity holds, the father–son placement cannot stand. Herbertson-133 has no research folder here and his identity with the brother of 1568/69 has not been tested, so this file keeps the father link at **uncertain** and does not regrade it. The protocol does not supply a replacement father.

*The History of Glasgow*, vol. 2, [ch. XX](https://www.electricscotland.com/history/glasgow/glasgow2_20.htm), says that Helen, mother of George Hutcheson, was "sister of Sir William Herbertson, a Roman priest" (compiled history, quoted in the captured biography). On 1 September 1586 Sir William and Stephen Glasgow were curators of Margaret Herbesoun's minor son George Glasgow (protocol 2916); that shows close kinship, not that Margaret was Sir William's daughter.

Caution: the biography captured on 28 September 2026 still says Margaret married [Glasgow-1098](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1098) and that her children George and Barbara were under Sir William's curatorship in 1586. Margaret's husband was corrected to William Glasgow-3522 on her profile on 30 August 2026, and the 1586 protocols name George only, not Barbara. Those sentences should not be reused.
