== Biography ==

'''Archibald Herbesoun''' was a fisher in Glasgow and the husband of
[[Glasgow-3916|Isobella Glasgow]]. On 27 January 1562/63 the spouses sold a
Bridgegait tenement; the record directly names Archibald's occupation and their
marriage.<ref name="Herbesoun1563">Robert Renwick, ed., ''Abstracts of Protocols of the Town Clerks of Glasgow'', vol. 3, protocol 719, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc06scogoog/page/n41/mode/2up p. 29], 27 January 1562/3.</ref>

Archibald was still alive on 19 December 1573, when Robert Glasgow, Archibald
Herbertsone and John Glasgow were collectively described as fishers in a
Bridgegait conveyance. His death is therefore after that date, with no proven
place.<ref name="Herbesoun1573">Renwick, ''Abstracts of Protocols'', vol. 7, protocol 2002, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc00robegoog/page/n52/mode/2up p. 36], 19 December 1573.</ref> A 1575 conveyance refers to a tenement formerly belonging to Archibald Herbertsoun, fisher; that corroborates the property identity but does not prove that he had died.<ref name="Herbesoun1575">Renwick, ''Abstracts of Protocols'', vol. 7, protocol 2151, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc00robegoog/page/n114/mode/2up p. 98], 25 March 1575.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The 1573 association with Robert and John Glasgow supports a close network
around Isobella, but no record calls either man Archibald's brother-in-law. An
Archibald Herbesoun described as a barker in 1559 and an Archibald Herbertsoun,
baker, in 1574 remain distinct candidates. No parent should be attached until a
record bridges the fisher to an earlier Herbesoun household.
