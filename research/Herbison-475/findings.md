# Findings: [Archibald Herbesoun (Herbison-475)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Herbison-475)

## Current conclusion

This profile is securely identified as the **fisher** Archibald Herbesoun,
husband of [Isobella Glasgow
(Glasgow-3916)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3916). Their 27 January
1562/63 sale directly calls them spouses. Archibald was still alive on 19
December 1573, when he, Robert Glasgow and John Glasgow were collectively
described as fishers, and his former Bridgegait property was identified on 25
March 1575.

Do not automatically merge him with every contemporary Archibald Herbesoun.
The protocols also identify an Archibald `ly Barkar` in 1559 and an Archibald
Herbertsoun, baker, in 1574. The younger Archibald who received property from an
elder namesake in 1536 is a possible earlier occurrence, but the inspected acts
do not bridge that man to the fisher.

## Relationship assessment

Archibald's repeated association with John and Robert Glasgow materially
strengthens Isobella's placement as their sister and as a probable daughter of
William Glasgow and Esbell Brade. The three-person fisher entry is strong
circumstantial evidence, not an explicit brother-in-law statement.

## Recommended WikiTree changes

- Add the 1573 fisher entry and set death to **after 19 December 1573**, uncertain.
- Retain Isobella Glasgow as the record-proved spouse.
- Add no parents until the fisher is distinguished from the barker/baker and
  connected to an earlier Herbesoun household by property or a kin term.
