# Helen (Hewitt) Glasgow (Hewitt-8271)

Last updated: 2026-09-23

## Current conclusion

Helen Hewitt was the wife of [William Glasgow (Glasgow-3157)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3157). The Armagh birth indexes for [Henry](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4182), [Alice](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4183) and [Henrietta](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4184) all name Hewitt as their mother's maiden surname. Those three children are linked to Helen on WikiTree. The duplicate placeholder Hewitt-9718 was merged into this named profile.

## Source findings

- William's profile preserves a family statement naming his wife Helen Hewitt and placing the family at Derrynoose, County Armagh.
- The [GRONI marriage index](https://geni.nidirect.gov.uk/search/marriage) lists William Glasgow and a spouse surnamed Hewett in Armagh on 23 October 1873, `M/1873/Q1/86/2/45`.
- The [GRONI birth index](https://geni.nidirect.gov.uk/search/birth) gives mother's maiden surname Hewitt for Henry (`U/1876/11/1002/11/31`), Alice (`U/1884/11/1002/16/205`) and Henrietta (`U/1895/11/1002/22/55`).

## Identity and relationship assessment

The marriage, family statement and three births form one Armagh Glasgow–Hewitt household. Hewitt-8271 is the surviving mother profile; Hewitt-9718 is merge history, not another woman.
