# Margaret Ann (Hilley) Glasgow (Hilley-66)

## Current conclusion

[Margaret Ann (Hilley) Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hilley-66) and
[Mary Hilley](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hilley-135) are recorded as sisters,
daughters of [James Hilley](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hilley-70) and
[Elizabeth Gilmour](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Gilmour-843). A GEDmatch
comparison supplied by Mark Glasgow on 12 August 2026 supports this family as
the leading route for his 171.9 cM match to T Siddle, but does not by itself
prove the segment attribution.

- Mark Glasgow (`FA7172012`) and T Siddle (`QP2207364`) share 171.9 cM across
  six GEDmatch segments, the largest 64.8 cM. Details are preserved in

## Relationship assessment

If Margaret is Mark's grandmother and Mary is T's grandmother, Mark and T are
second cousins sharing James Hilley and Elizabeth Gilmour as great-grandparents.
The observed DNA amount is compatible with that relationship. Alternative
common ancestors have not yet been excluded.
