# Findings: John Howe (Howe-15840), Glasgow dyer

## Current conclusion

John Howe, dyer and husband of Isabella Glasgow in 1600, is **probably** the
John Howee whom Peter Howie, dyer, and Isobella Maxwell explicitly called their
son in 1582. The parent-child statement is direct for the 1582 John; the
same-person identification is strong circumstantial evidence based on exact
name, city, chronology and inherited occupation. Keep any structured parent
links uncertain until a record repeats the son with his wife or later property.

## Evidence

| Same series, vol. 5, protocols 1611 and 1724-1725, 1568/9-1570/1 | Another John Howe was a fisher married to Marion Park. | Identity control: distinguishes the southern-property fisher from the dyer who married Isabella Glasgow. |
