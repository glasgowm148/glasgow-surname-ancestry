## Source findings — Lambhill and Carmyle charters, from the biography review, 28 September 2026

The captured WikiTree biography gives the following title sequence, citing W. H. Hill, *Early Records of an Old Glasgow Family* (1902), [p. 78](https://books.google.com/books?id=D9MMAwAAQBAJ&pg=PA78) for the charters and the same work, without a page, for 1574. The charters themselves were not seen and Hill was not re-read for this note.

| Date | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 1574 | Thomas was one of the neighbours who witnessed the coutching (division) of Dumbreck Quarter of Meikle Govan |
| 15 April 1579 | James, Archbishop of Glasgow, with the Dean and Chapter, granted the two merkland of Lambhill to Thomas Hutcheson and his wife Helen Herbertson |
| May 1581 | Infeftment on that charter |
| 11 May 1583 | Crown charter of confirmation |
| 6 January 1587 | Novodamus from Walter, Commendator of Blantyre, adding the merkland of Nether Carmyle (Hutchesontoun) |
| 1595–1596 | After Thomas's death, his eldest son George Hutcheson took infeftment in Lambhill and Carmyle |

The charters name Helen Herbertson as Thomas's wife and show him alive in the 1580s. His death about 1594 is an inference from George's infeftment of April 1596, in which Thomas is described as deceased. Before 1600 the Scottish year began on 25 March, so the January 1587 novodamus may be 1587/88 unless Hill modernised the date.
