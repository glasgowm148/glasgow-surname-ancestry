## Source findings — notarial work, marriage and death, from the biography review, 28 September 2026

The captured WikiTree biography, which lists W. H. Hill, *Early Records of an Old Glasgow Family* (1902), pp. 36–65 ([NLS PDF](https://deriv.nls.uk/dcn23/9556/95565896.23.pdf)) and *The History of Glasgow*, vol. 2, [ch. XX](https://www.electricscotland.com/history/glasgow/glasgow2_20.htm) as general sources without tying them to particular statements, says that:

- in 1587 George, as notary, acted in the infeftment of Walter Stewart, Commendator of Blantyre;
- after the 1588 kirk-session case he married Elizabeth (Elspeth) Craig;
- ten days before his death he mortified a tenement and 20,000 merks to found an almshouse;
- he died on 26 December 1639 and was buried on the east side of Glasgow Cathedral.

These are compiled-history statements; the notarial instrument, the kirk-session minute and George's will of 1639 have not been seen. Caution: the captured biography of Janet Hutcheson (Hutcheson-1654) says that George and Thomas died unmarried and without heirs, which contradicts the Craig marriage; neither claim has been tested.
