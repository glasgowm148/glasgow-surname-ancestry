## Source findings — career and the founding of Hutchesons' Hospital, from the biography review, 28 September 2026

The captured WikiTree biography, which lists *The History of Glasgow*, vol. 2, [chs. XX–XXI](https://www.electricscotland.com/history/glasgow/glasgow2_20.htm) and W. H. Hill, *Early Records of an Old Glasgow Family* (1902), pp. 36–65 ([NLS PDF](https://deriv.nls.uk/dcn23/9556/95565896.23.pdf)) as general sources without tying them to particular statements, says that:

- Thomas was educated at the University of Glasgow under his brother George's care and became Registrar of Sasines for Glasgow;
- he gave the College 1,000 merks to enlarge the library and 2,000 merks for a bursary for the librarian;
- as George's heir and executor he added 10,500 merks to the hospital endowment and 20,200 merks for a school for twelve orphan sons of Glasgow burgesses;
- he laid the hospital's foundation stone on the north side of the Trongate in March 1641, and died unmarried and childless in 1641.

These are compiled-history statements; the deeds of mortification and his testament have not been seen.
