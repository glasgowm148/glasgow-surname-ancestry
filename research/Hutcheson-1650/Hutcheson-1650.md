== Biography ==
'''Janet Hutcheson''' was living in Holland in 1679. A Glasgow burgh order quoted in ''The History of Glasgow'', volume II, chapter XX, names her as the natural daughter of deceased [[Hutcheson-1648|George Hutcheson]]. The surrounding narrative identifies George as the founder of Hutchesons' Hospital.<ref name="History">''The History of Glasgow'', volume II, chapter XX, “George Hutcheson, Notary, Banker, and Philanthropist,” [https://www.electricscotland.com/history/glasgow/glasgow2_20.htm published text], quoting Glasgow burgh orders of 1679 and 1684 and cross-referencing 24 July 1680. Original entries not inspected for this review.</ref>

Her father died on 26 December 1639. Allowing a posthumous birth in 1640 gives a conservative birth bound before 1641; no exact birth date or birthplace is established.<ref name="History" />

On 24 July 1680 Janet was in Glasgow after coming from Holland. The council allowed £4 sterling annually if she stayed here or £5 if she returned to Holland; she undertook to return and remain there. This records her presence and intended return, not exact travel dates.<ref>''Extracts from the Records of the Burgh of Glasgow, 1663–1690'', printed pp. 281–283, council entry dated 24 July 1680, [https://archive.org/download/extractsfromrec05unkngoog/page/n336.jpg Janet paragraph p. 283]. Scanned printed pages and date continuity visually checked 15 November 2026; manuscript minutes uninspected.</ref>

She died in Holland on or before 17 July 1684, expressed as before 18 July 1684. The council's 17 July order provided £30 Scots through Jean Main toward the already deceased town pensioner's funeral expenses. It supplies no exact death day or Dutch municipality; Jean Main appears as the payment intermediary. The order leaves Janet's marital status, children and heirs unknown.<ref>Glasgow Corporation, ''Memorabilia of the City of Glasgow'' (Glasgow: J. Maclehose, 1868), [https://archive.org/download/memorabiliacity00scogoog/page/n255.jpg p. 245] (1684 year heading) and [https://archive.org/download/memorabiliacity00scogoog/page/n256.jpg p. 246] (17 July funeral-assistance order). Scanned printed pages visually checked 15 November 2026; manuscript minutes uninspected.</ref>
