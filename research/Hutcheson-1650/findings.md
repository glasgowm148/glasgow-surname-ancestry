## Unresolved — whether the Glasgow pensioner in Holland, 1679–1684, was this Janet, 28 September 2026

The captured biography of this Janet, citing *The History of Glasgow*, vol. 2, [ch. XX](https://www.electricscotland.com/history/glasgow/glasgow2_20.htm) and the Glasgow burgh records for 1679–1684 in general terms, says that she lived in Holland in later life on the charity of Glasgow town council, that in 1679 the burgh treasurer was ordered to send her yearly payments, and that she died there in 1684, the city paying for her funeral. It also suggests that her mother Elspeth Craig was a sister or daughter of John Craig, notary; that is the biography's suggestion, not a recorded relationship.

The captured biography of her father, [George Hutcheson (Hutcheson-1648)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hutcheson-1648), instead says that the Holland pensioner was a second natural daughter of George, also called Janet, born outside the marriage. A Janet born in 1587 or 1588 would have been about ninety-six in 1684. Until the burgh accounts for 1679–1684 are read, the Holland pensioner should not be treated as this profile's Janet.
