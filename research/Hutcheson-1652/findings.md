# Findings: [Helen Hutcheson](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hutcheson-1652)

## Current conclusion

The inspected Lanark retour no. 206 of 24 May 1642 calls Helen Hutcheson the widow of Ninian Hill, citizen of Glasgow. It names Helen and Janet Hutcheson as heirs-portioners of their brother Mr Thomas Hutcheson of Lambhill in the lands described in no. 205. Hill reports marriage in 1609 and prints a 1623 baptism calling Ninian deceased. His reproduction of George’s testament names four of their children, and his wider register-based reconstruction identifies ten. The 1641 ratification names the three full sisters as Thomas’s heirs-portioners. The father relationship with [Thomas Hutcheson](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hutcheson-1647) retains its existing **uncertain** assessment. The retour supplies direct sibling evidence and does not name the parents.

## Source findings — marriage, children and service of heirs

The [inspected printed Lanark no. 206, 24 May 1642](https://archive.org/details/inquisitionumadc00grea/page/n498/mode/1up), register xvi.233, uses *relicta Niniani Hill civis Glasguensis* for Helen and *haeredes portionariae ... fratris* for the sisters. It refers back to no. 205, register xvi.232, for the Auchingray and Caldercruikis lands with mill in Monkland. Elizabeth appears separately in no. 205. These were legal services in named lands. The original inquests are uninspected. The contemporary sibling clause outweighs the unsupported niece wording in the captured spouse biography. The legal record supports the deceased-by boundary for Ninian, with his precise death date and place outside its scope.

Hill, [printed p. 9](https://archive.org/download/historyofhospita00hutc/page/n30.jpg), reproduces the testament clause naming Ninian, Margaret, Elspeth and Helen as Helen’s children. Its separately listed grandchildren are a different generation. [Printed p. 13 and note 1](https://archive.org/details/historyofhospita00hutc/page/13/mode/1up) report marriage in 1609 and Helen’s death shortly before 1654, describe six further children, and reproduce the 8 December 1623 baptism which names deceased Ninian and Helen as parents of a lawful son George. These are published extracts and conclusions. The original documents have yet to be checked. Hill’s 1623 birth assertion for the surviving Ninian differs from the 1621 Glasgow baptism index cited on his profile.

The 15 October 1641 ratification, transcribed in Hill’s appendix no. VI, [printed p. 254](https://archive.org/download/historyofhospita00hutc/page/n305.jpg) and [p. 255](https://archive.org/download/historyofhospita00hutc/page/n306.jpg), describes Janet, Bessie and Helen as Thomas’s three lawful full sisters and heirs-portioners. The deed expressly ratifies Thomas’s earlier grants. The surviving sisters’ 1642 services in specified lands document a separate act of legal succession.

The [held Ninian Hill biography](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hill-17648) cites Glasgow Old Parish Register 644/1 10 378 for Ninian’s baptism on 10 July 1621 and gives the parents as Ninian Hill and Heleine Hutchesoune. The register image was not inspected. The [Barlanark account](http://gdl.cdlr.strath.ac.uk/smihou/smihou008.htm) names daughter Helen, wife of John Maxwell. [Hill’s institutional history, p. 14](https://archive.org/details/historyofhospita00hutc/page/14/mode/1up), as quoted in the held Ninian Hill biography, names Helen Hutcheson’s daughter Margaret and her two marriages. The other children listed below and the exact family count remain compiled claims. The baptism date, estimated chronology, and the two Ninians with reported early dates require separate evidence checks.

## Source findings — marriage to Ninian Hill and the Hill children, from the biography review, 28 September 2026

The captured WikiTree biography, which lists W. H. Hill, *Early Records of an Old Glasgow Family* (1902), pp. 36–65 ([NLS PDF](https://deriv.nls.uk/dcn23/9556/95565896.23.pdf)), George Hutcheson's will of 18 April 1639 and other compiled works as general sources, says that Helen married Ninian Hill of Garioch, merchant burgess of Glasgow, about 1609, and that she survived him; he died in 1623. It names their children as Janet; Margaret (Hill or Bryson, later Pollock); Elspeth (Hill or Pollock); Helen (Hill or Maxwell, wife of John Maxwell); Marion (Hill or Gray); James, Ninian and George, who died young; and Mr Ninian Hill of Lambhill (1621–1685). It says that several children and grandchildren were legatees in George Hutcheson's will. The will has not been seen. The biography itself calls Helen's descent from Thomas Hutcheson and Helen Herbertson an informed hypothesis.

Historical chronology caution (7 October 2026): *The History of Glasgow*, vol. 2, ch. XX, as quoted in Sir William Herbertson's captured biography, places Thomas Hutcheson's three daughters between George (born 1554) and the younger Thomas. A daughter born then would have been in her fifties by 1609 and over sixty when a son was born in 1621. The earlier review treated this as a possible identity conflict. The 1641 ratification and 1642 retour now identify the Hill wife as Thomas of Lambhill’s sister. The older estimated dates and the suggested birth interval need separate verification.
