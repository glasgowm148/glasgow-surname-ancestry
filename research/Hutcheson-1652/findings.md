## Source findings — marriage to Ninian Hill and the Hill children, from the biography review, 28 September 2026

The captured WikiTree biography, which lists W. H. Hill, *Early Records of an Old Glasgow Family* (1902), pp. 36–65 ([NLS PDF](https://deriv.nls.uk/dcn23/9556/95565896.23.pdf)), George Hutcheson's will of 18 April 1639 and other compiled works as general sources, says that Helen married Ninian Hill of Garioch, merchant burgess of Glasgow, about 1609, and that she survived him; he died in 1623. It names their children as Janet; Margaret (Hill or Bryson, later Pollock); Elspeth (Hill or Pollock); Helen (Hill or Maxwell, wife of John Maxwell); Marion (Hill or Gray); James, Ninian and George, who died young; and Mr Ninian Hill of Lambhill (1621–1685). It says that several children and grandchildren were legatees in George Hutcheson's will. The will has not been seen. The biography itself calls Helen's descent from Thomas Hutcheson and Helen Herbertson an informed hypothesis.

Chronology caution (inference): *The History of Glasgow*, vol. 2, ch. XX, as quoted in Sir William Herbertson's captured biography, places Thomas Hutcheson's three daughters between George (born 1554) and the younger Thomas. A daughter born then would have been in her fifties by 1609 and over sixty when a son was born in 1621. Either the Hill marriage and children belong to a younger Helen Hutcheson, or the dates need re-examination, before this profile's parentage is relied on.
