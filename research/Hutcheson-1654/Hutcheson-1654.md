== Biography ==
'''Janet Hutcheson''' was born about 1565–1570, probably at Gairbraid or Lambhill near Glasgow, Lanarkshire, Scotland.  

She was one of the five known children of [[Hutcheson-1647|Thomas Hutcheson (abt. 1520–1594)]] and [[Herbertson-132|Helen (Herbertson) Hutcheson (1520–)]].  

This made her the sister of [[Hutcheson-1648|George Hutcheson (1554–1639)]] and [[Hutcheson-1649|Thomas Hutcheson Jr. (c. 1558–1641)]], founders of Hutchesons’ Hospital and Grammar School.

Janet was co-heiress with her sisters Helen and Elizabeth. The Hutcheson lands and annualrents were divided among their lines after George and Thomas died unmarried and without heirs.

== Family ==
Janet married [[Duncan-XXXXX|David Duncan]] — details of his parentage are not fully documented, but he was a burgess of Glasgow.  
Their descendants continued to hold interests in the extended Hutcheson and Gairbraid properties into the 17th century.

== Research Notes ==
Janet’s marriage is mentioned in *Early Records of an Old Glasgow Family* and other property records as part of the sisters’ distribution of Lambhill and Carmyle lands.
