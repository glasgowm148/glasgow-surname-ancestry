== Biography ==
'''Janet Hutcheson''' was born about 1565–1570, probably at Gairbraid or Lambhill near Glasgow, Lanarkshire, Scotland.  

She was one of the five known children of [[Hutcheson-1647|Thomas Hutcheson (abt. 1520–1594)]] and [[Herbertson-132|Helen (Herbertson) Hutcheson (1520–)]].  

Her brothers [[Hutcheson-1648|George Hutcheson]] and [[Hutcheson-1649|Thomas Hutcheson of Lambhill]] founded Hutchesons’ Hospital in Glasgow.

== Family ==
Janet married [[Duncan-27082|David Duncan]]. William H. Hill calls her the founders’ eldest sister. He explains that the heirs’ division contract leaves Duncan’s given name blank, but that a Gairbraid precept dated 15 June 1642 names David Duncan and calls Janet his widow. Hill says she apparently left an only son, George Duncan of Barrowfield.<ref name="Hill1881p8">William H. Hill, ''The History of the Hospital and School in Glasgow Founded by George and Thomas Hutcheson'' (Glasgow, 1881), [https://archive.org/details/historyofhospita00hutc/page/8/mode/1up p. 8 and footnote 2].</ref>

A printed abstract of a Lanark inquest dated 24 May 1642 already calls Janet the widow of a Duncan, citizen of Glasgow. It identifies her through her brother Thomas of Lambhill and her sister Helen. Read together with Hill’s account, it places David’s death by 24 May 1642. The abstract omits his given name.<ref name="Retour206">''Inquisitionum ad Capellam Domini Regis Retornatarum Abbreviatio'', vol. I, [https://archive.org/details/inquisitionumadc00grea/page/n498/mode/1up Lanark no. 206, 24 May 1642], register xvi.233.</ref><ref name="Hill1881p8" />

== Inheritance ==
The two Lanark retours of 24 May 1642 identify the sisters who inherited shares of specified lands from their brother Thomas. No. 206 names Janet and Helen, widow of Ninian Hill, as his heirs-portioners. No. 205 names their sister Elizabeth, widow of James Pollok, as his heir-portioner in the Monkland lands described there.<ref name="Retour206" /><ref>''Inquisitionum ad Capellam Domini Regis Retornatarum Abbreviatio'', vol. I, [https://archive.org/details/inquisitionumadc00grea/page/n498/mode/1up Lanark no. 205, 24 May 1642], register xvi.232.</ref>

The brothers’ own families are documented separately. A Glasgow council order of 18 January 1679 identifies [[Hutcheson-1650|Janet Hutcheson in Holland]] as George’s natural daughter. She was the niece of the Janet who married David Duncan. A published account reports that in 1588 George was ordered to marry Elizabeth Craig after the birth of a girl. A later Glasgow history describes Craig as George’s wife and records that George appointed his brother Thomas his heir and executor. The original marriage evidence has not been checked, and the unnamed girl has not been identified as Janet.<ref>History of Glasgow, vol. II, [https://www.electricscotland.com/history/glasgow/glasgow2_20.htm chapter XX, “George Hutcheson, Notary, Banker, and Philanthropist”].</ref><ref>''Memorabilia of the City of Glasgow'' (1868), [https://archive.org/download/memorabiliacity00scogoog/page/n241.jpg p. 231, council order of 18 January 1679].</ref><ref>Stuart Handley, “Hutcheson, George (1550x60–1639), philanthropist,” [https://doi.org/10.1093/ref:odnb/14275 ''Oxford Dictionary of National Biography''].</ref> Hill names Marion Stewart as Thomas’s widow and reports that he died without issue. Hill cites an account of their marriage contract and Thomas’s testament; the original documents have yet to be checked.<ref>Hill, ''History of the Hospital and School'', [https://archive.org/details/historyofhospita00hutc/page/49/mode/1up p. 49].</ref>

== Research Notes ==
The earlier working draft called David a burgess of Glasgow without a source for that status; Hill’s inspected passage does not establish it or his parents; the separately inspected retour calls the unnamed Duncan a Glasgow citizen, conditionally attributable to David as explained above. His inherited about-1565 birth remains unreviewed and is not established by Hill. Janet’s existing birth/place estimates and current parent assessments are not revised by this source review. FamilySearch PJC8-CWG now represents David with the qualified source and spouse Janet PJHW-VZ7; no live WikiTree edit has been made.

Janet’s marriage is mentioned in *Early Records of an Old Glasgow Family* and other property records as part of the sisters’ distribution of Lambhill and Carmyle lands.
