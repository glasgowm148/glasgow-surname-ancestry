== Biography ==
'''Janet Hutcheson''' was born about 1565–1570, probably at Gairbraid or Lambhill near Glasgow, Lanarkshire, Scotland.  

She was one of the five known children of [[Hutcheson-1647|Thomas Hutcheson (abt. 1520–1594)]] and [[Herbertson-132|Helen (Herbertson) Hutcheson (1520–)]].  

This made her the sister of [[Hutcheson-1648|George Hutcheson (1554–1639)]] and [[Hutcheson-1649|Thomas Hutcheson Jr. (c. 1558–1641)]], founders of Hutchesons’ Hospital and Grammar School.

Janet was co-heiress with her sisters Helen and Elizabeth. The Hutcheson lands and annualrents were divided among their lines after George and Thomas died unmarried and without heirs.

== Family ==
Janet married [[Duncan-27082|David Duncan]]. Hill’s printed account identifies him by name and calls Janet the founders’ eldest sister. Its footnote explains that the heirs’ division contract leaves Duncan’s given name blank, but that a Gairbraid precept of clare constat dated 15 June 1642 names David Duncan and describes Janet as his relict.<ref name="Hill1881p8">William H. Hill, The History of the Hospital and School in Glasgow Founded by George and Thomas Hutcheson (Glasgow, 1881), [https://archive.org/details/historyofhospita00hutc/page/8/mode/1up printed p. 8 and footnote 2], visually inspected. Original precept, division contract and testament uninspected.</ref> This reported relict clause places David’s death by 15 June 1642; it does not give an exact death date or place. The original documents have not been inspected. The printed Lanark retour no.206, dated 24 May 1642, already calls Janet Hutcheson the widow of an unnamed Duncan, citizen of Glasgow, and names her with Helen, widow of Ninian Hill, as portionary heirs of their brother Mr Thomas Hutcheson of Lambhill.<ref name="Retour206">Inquisitionum ad Capellam Domini Regis Retornatarum Abbreviatio, [https://archive.org/details/inquisitionumadc00grea/page/n498/mode/1up Lanark no.206, 24 May 1642, printed public leaf498], visually inspected; underlying inquest uninspected.</ref> Matching this named sibling and estate group to Hill’s Janet supports the earlier deceased-by-24-May-1642 boundary for David. The retour does not give Duncan’s forename: David comes from Hill’s separate compiled account. The citizen description belongs to the unnamed Duncan in the retour, so its attribution to David depends on that identification. Hill says she apparently left an only son, George Duncan of Barrowfield; that qualified compiled statement is not a new child attachment.
Their descendants continued to hold interests in the extended Hutcheson and Gairbraid properties into the 17th century.

== Research Notes ==
The earlier working draft called David a burgess of Glasgow without a source for that status; Hill’s inspected passage does not establish it or his parents; the separately inspected retour calls the unnamed Duncan a Glasgow citizen, conditionally attributable to David as explained above. His inherited about-1565 birth remains unreviewed and is not established by Hill. Janet’s existing birth/place estimates and current parent assessments are not revised by this source review. FamilySearch PJC8-CWG now represents David with the qualified source and spouse Janet PJHW-VZ7; no live WikiTree edit has been made.

Janet’s marriage is mentioned in *Early Records of an Old Glasgow Family* and other property records as part of the sisters’ distribution of Lambhill and Carmyle lands.
