== Biography ==
'''Elizabeth Hutcheson''' was the wife of [[Pollok-99|James Pollok]], citizen of Glasgow, and sister of [[Hutcheson-1649|Mr Thomas Hutcheson of Lambhill]]. The printed abstract of a Lanark inquest dated 24 May 1642 names her as James’s widow and Thomas’s heir-portioner. James was living on 15 October 1641 according to the published transcription of the sisters’ ratification of Thomas’s charitable grant. The deed records Elizabeth acting with the consent of James, her spouse, whom it calls a cooper and burgess of Glasgow. He had died by 24 May 1642. Their marriage date and his exact death date and place remain unknown.<ref name="Ratification1641">W. H. Hill, ''The History of the Hospital and School in Glasgow Founded by George and Thomas Hutcheson'' (Glasgow, 1881), Appendix no. VI, [https://archive.org/download/historyofhospita00hutc/page/n305.jpg printed p. 254] and [https://archive.org/download/historyofhospita00hutc/page/n306.jpg printed p. 255], published transcription of the ratification dated 15 October 1641. Original deed uninspected.</ref><ref name="Retour205">Great Britain, Record Commission, ''Inquisitionum ad Capellam Domini Regis Retornatarum Abbreviatio'', vol. I (London, 1811), [https://archive.org/details/inquisitionumadc00grea/page/n498/mode/1up Lanark no. 205, 24 May 1642], register xvi.232. Printed abstract inspected. Original inquest uninspected.</ref>

Compiled family accounts place Elizabeth among the children of [[Hutcheson-1647|Thomas Hutcheson]] and [[Herbertson-132|Helen Herbertson]], and identify [[Hutcheson-1648|George Hutcheson]] and Thomas as her brothers. Her reported birth about 1565–1570 and proposed birthplace at Gairbraid or Lambhill remain estimates.<ref name="Hill1902">W. H. Hill, ''Early Records of an Old Glasgow Family'' (1902).</ref><ref>''The History of Glasgow'', vol. 2, [https://www.electricscotland.com/history/glasgow/glasgow2_20.htm chapter XX].</ref>

== Children ==
Hill’s published extract of George Hutcheson’s testament names Elizabeth’s six children as John, Robert, George, Margaret, Janet and Christian Pollok. It calls Elizabeth Bessie. The grandchildren are listed separately. Hill identifies Robert as the son who adopted the Hutcheson surname after succeeding to Auchingray and Caldercruikis, and gives John as Robert’s son and successor there.<ref name="Hill1881p9">Hill, ''History of the Hospital and School'', [https://archive.org/download/historyofhospita00hutc/page/n30.jpg printed p. 9], published extract of the testament. Original testament uninspected.</ref><ref>Hill, [https://archive.org/download/historyofhospita00hutc/page/n31.jpg printed p. 10], citing Lanark retour no. 304, 28 June 1667.</ref>

== Inheritance ==
On 15 October 1641 Elizabeth, Janet and Helen ratified grants for Thomas’s charitable foundation. The published deed describes them as his three lawful full sisters and heirs-portioners.<ref name="Ratification1641" /> Elizabeth was served heir-portioner of her brother Thomas in the four-pound lands of Auchingray and the forty-shilling lands of Caldercruikis with its mill, in the parish of Monkland. The adjacent Lanark entry names [[Hutcheson-1652|Helen Hutcheson]], widow of [[Hill-17647|Ninian Hill]], and [[Hutcheson-1654|Janet Hutcheson]], widow of a Glasgow citizen surnamed Duncan, as Thomas’s other heirs-portioners in the same lands.<ref name="Retour205" /><ref>''Inquisitionum ad Capellam Domini Regis Retornatarum Abbreviatio'', vol. I, [https://archive.org/details/inquisitionumadc00grea/page/n498/mode/1up Lanark no. 206, 24 May 1642], register xvi.233. Printed abstract inspected.</ref>

== Research Notes ==
Hill calls James a grandson of Pollok of that Ilk, citing a pedigree paper attributed to the historian Crawford. The exact parents and the descent remain unverified. Two 1644 testament confirmations for a cooper of the same name remain unassigned. The identification with any Flemington, Ardmillan or Polquhirter man is unresolved.<ref name="Retour205" /><ref name="Hill1881p9" />
