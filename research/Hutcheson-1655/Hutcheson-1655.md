== Biography ==
'''Elizabeth Hutcheson''' was born about 1565–1570, probably at Gairbraid or Lambhill near Glasgow, Lanarkshire, Scotland.  
She was one of the five known children of [[Hutcheson-1647|Thomas Hutcheson Sr. (abt. 1520–1594)]] and [[Herbertson-132|Helen (Herbertson) Hutcheson (1520–)]].  
Elizabeth was the sister of [[Hutcheson-1648|George Hutcheson (1554–1639)]] and [[Hutcheson-1649|Thomas Hutcheson Jr. (c. 1558–1641)]], who founded Hutchesons’ Hospital and Grammar School.

She was co-heiress of parts of the family lands in Lambhill, Gairbraid, and Nether Carmyle. These lands helped fund the Hutcheson charitable legacy through her children’s lines.

== Family ==
Elizabeth married [[Pollok-XXXXX|James Pollok]], a Cooper and Burgess of Glasgow, who was grandson of the Pollok of that Ilk family — a prominent Renfrewshire and Glasgow line.

== Research Notes ==
Elizabeth’s marriage to James Pollok is noted in *Early Records of an Old Glasgow Family* as part of the dispersal of Hutcheson properties after her brothers’ deaths.
