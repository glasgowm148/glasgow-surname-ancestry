== Biography ==
'''Elizabeth Hutcheson''' was born about 1565–1570, probably at Gairbraid or Lambhill near Glasgow, Lanarkshire, Scotland.  
She was one of the five known children of [[Hutcheson-1647|Thomas Hutcheson Sr. (abt. 1520–1594)]] and [[Herbertson-132|Helen (Herbertson) Hutcheson (1520–)]].  
Elizabeth was the sister of [[Hutcheson-1648|George Hutcheson (1554–1639)]] and [[Hutcheson-1649|Thomas Hutcheson Jr. (c. 1558–1641)]], who founded Hutchesons’ Hospital and Grammar School.

She was co-heiress of parts of the family lands in Lambhill, Gairbraid, and Nether Carmyle. These lands helped fund the Hutcheson charitable legacy through her children’s lines.

== Family ==
Elizabeth married [[Pollok-99|James Pollok]]. The inspected printed Lanark retour no.205, dated 24 May 1642, calls her Elizabeth Hutchesone, widow of James Pollok, citizen of Glasgow, and heir-portioner of her brother Mr Thomas Hutchesoun of Lambhill, in the named Monkland lands.<ref name="Retour205">Great Britain, Record Commission, Inquisitionum ad capellam domini regis retornatarum, quae in publicis archivis Scotiae adhuc servantur, abbreviatio, vol. I (London, 1811), [https://archive.org/details/inquisitionumadc00grea/page/n498/mode/1up Lanark no.205, 24 May 1642], register xvi,232. Printed abstract visually inspected; original retour/register uninspected.</ref> James was therefore deceased by 24 May 1642, not necessarily on that day. The lands are property context, not a birth or death place. The abstract calls him a citizen, not a cooper, and supplies no parentage or identity with the Flemington, Ardmillan or Polquhirter men.

== Research Notes ==
The earlier draft called James a cooper and grandson of the Pollok of that Ilk family. Those inherited claims are not established by retour205 and remain unreviewed here; the printed abstract supports only the named widow, citizen and sibling/estate clauses. Separate cooper testament confirmations dated 5 February and 12 August 1644 remain unassigned, not death dates. The existing FamilySearch candidate MXZX-ZY6 has received the qualified source; L414-4YJ remains a compatible alternative, and neither the exact wife/family bridge nor wider ancestry is proved. No merge, new person, relationship change or live WikiTree edit is made.

Elizabeth’s marriage to James Pollok is noted in *Early Records of an Old Glasgow Family* as part of the dispersal of Hutcheson properties after her brothers’ deaths.
