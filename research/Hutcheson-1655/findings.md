## Source findings — Elizabeth’s retour205, 24 May 1642

The inspected [printed Lanark retour no.205](https://archive.org/details/inquisitionumadc00grea/page/n498/mode/1up) calls Elizabeth Hutchesone the widow of James Pollok, citizen of Glasgow, and heir-portioner of her brother Mr Thomas Hutchesoun of Lambhill in the specified Monkland lands. James was deceased by 24 May 1642; this is not an exact death date or place, and the original inquest/register remains uninspected. ## Source findings — legal succession and issue scope

The property clause in [Lanark no. 205](https://archive.org/details/inquisitionumadc00grea/page/n498/mode/1up), register xvi.232, specifies four-pound lands of Auchingray and forty-shilling lands of Caldercruikis including a mill, all in the parish of Monkland. [No. 206](https://archive.org/details/inquisitionumadc00grea/page/n498/mode/1up), register xvi.233, refers to the same lands and names Helen Hill and Janet Duncan as heirs-portioners of their brother Thomas. These entries support the sisters’ legal succession to the named property. Their 15 October 1641 ratification, Hill’s appendix no. VI, [printed p. 254](https://archive.org/download/historyofhospita00hutc/page/n305.jpg) and [p. 255](https://archive.org/download/historyofhospita00hutc/page/n306.jpg), describes Janet, Bessie and Helen as Thomas’s three lawful full sisters and heirs-portioners. Bessie acts with the consent of her spouse James Pollok, described as a cooper and burgess of Glasgow. This published transcription supplies the occupation missing from the retour and a living-on 15 October 1641 boundary. The charitable deed ratifies Thomas’s grants. Later family property transfers require their own evidence.

[Hill’s printed p. 9](https://archive.org/download/historyofhospita00hutc/page/n30.jpg) transcribes George’s testament clause naming Bessie’s children John, Robert, George, Margaret, Janet and Christian Pollok. The grandchildren in that testament clause are listed separately. Hill also describes James as a grandson of Pollok of that Ilk and attributes that ancestry to Crawford’s pedigree paper. The original testament and pedigree paper remain uninspected.

A later Lanark entry is a focused research lead. The held OCR of vol. I, Lanark no. 304, 28 June 1667, register xxviii.302, appears to describe John Hutchesone as heir of his father Robert Hutchesone of Auchingray, formerly Pollok, in Auchingray and Caldercruiks. Hill’s [printed p. 9](https://archive.org/download/historyofhospita00hutc/page/n30.jpg) and [p. 10](https://archive.org/download/historyofhospita00hutc/page/n31.jpg) connect Robert to Elizabeth and identify the later John as Robert’s son. The later abstract names John’s father and the estate. The printed return and intervening conveyances still need to be checked. The held OCR of Renfrew nos. 114–115 also gives a property cross-reference for the 1642 services. The Lanark abstracts are the inspected basis used here.
