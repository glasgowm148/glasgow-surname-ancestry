# Findings: [Margaret Hutcheson (Hutcheson-1656)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hutcheson-1656)

Last updated: 18 November 2026

## Current conclusion

The 1577 testament dative names Margaret as the widow of [John Glasgow (Glasgow-3181)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3181). Her surname remains unresolved, so identification with this profile remains possible. Margaret submitted the inventory on behalf of [Katherine Glasgow (Glasgow-3590)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3590) and [Marjory Glasgow (Glasgow-3589)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3589), John's lawful children and executors-dative. Their father's identity is explicit. The possessive before the lawful-children clause is unclear, leaving their maternity unresolved. The appointment as executors-dative does not establish their inheritance shares. [Accepted reading of the widow and children](/research/transcriptions/john-1577/#passage-john-widow-children).

## The widow and children in the 1577 testament

The inventory and confirmation are parts of one testament, NRS, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/5, images 276–277. The accepted human reading, *m[ar]iory*, supersedes the earlier reading Androw or Andrew. The older draft therefore supplies no support for identifying a son with [Andrew Glasgow (Glasgow-3163)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3163). The confirmation repeats the executor and paternal relationships. [Published testament index, p. 109](https://archive.org/details/commissariotreco16scot/page/n117/mode/2up?q=Glasgow), [illustrated reading](/research/transcriptions/john-1577/).

Cochrane and Hochaine have been suggested for Margaret's surname. The manuscript reading and the relationship of the associated Hutcheson fragment to the testament remain open. The present evidence establishes the widow's forename and role while leaving her identity in the Lambhill or Gairbraid families unresolved. Preserve the existing spouse and mother attachments as uncertain. [Associated manuscript fragment](/research/transcriptions/john-1577/associated-fragment-reading.txt), [John’s catalogue account](/people/glasgow-3181.html).
