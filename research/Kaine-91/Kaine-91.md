# WikiTree profile draft: Alexander Kaine/Kano (Kaine-91)

== Biography ==

Alexander Kaine was an associate of Sir James Sandilands of Calder. On 2 July 1526 Alexander Kano was named immediately after [[Glasgow-1098|William Glasgow]] among the men receiving a royal remission for the premeditated killing of James Somervile and other past offences.<ref name="Remission1526">National Records of Scotland, Papers of Lord Torphichen, [https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/browseDetails.aspx?reference=GD119%2F193 GD119/193], remission sealed at Edinburgh, 2 July 1526. The official catalogue reads ''Alexander Kano''; ''Registrum Secreti Sigilli'', vol. 1, no. 3412, p. 516, prints ''Alexandri Kame''.</ref> James V dined with Sandilands at Calder that day, and the Calder household servants received gratuities, placing the remission in an immediate estate-household setting.<ref name="Treasurer1526">Thomas Dickson and James Balfour Paul, eds., [https://electricscotland.com/history/records/accountslordhig05offigoog.pdf ''Accounts of the Lord High Treasurer of Scotland'', vol. 5, A.D. 1515–1531], preface p. xxvii, citing account p. 275.</ref>

He is confidently identified with Alexander Kaine, husband of [[Glasgow-3915|Elizabeth Glasgow]]. A Great Seal confirmation dated 19 December 1552 described Easter Corswood as occupied by ''Elesabeth Glasgow relictam Alesandri Kaine''—Elizabeth Glasgow, widow of Alexander Kaine.<ref name="RMS739">John Maitland Thomson, ed., ''Registrum Magni Sigilli Regum Scotorum'', vol. 4, A.D. 1546-1580 (Edinburgh, 1886), no. 739, [https://archive.org/details/registrummagnisi04scot/page/164/mode/1up p. 164], 19 December 1552.</ref> Easter Corswood belonged to the Sandilands barony of Calder, independently linking the 1526 Kano and 1552 Kaine records to the same estate.<ref name="Corswood1625">National Records of Scotland, Papers of Lord Torphichen, [https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/browseTreeview.aspx?reference=GD119 GD119/299], 1 July 1625, describing Corswoodburne alias Easter Corswood in the barony of Calder.</ref>

The record proves that Alexander died before 19 December 1552. His birth year and parents are unknown; about 1520 is only an estimate.

== Research Notes ==

The forms ''Kano'', ''Kame'' and ''Kaine'' should be retained as spelling controls until GD119/193 is inspected. The rare name, William Glasgow association and continued Calder-estate context make this a confident same-person identification, but no record states Alexander's relationship to William. Elizabeth may have been William's daughter, sister, niece or another close kinswoman.
