# Findings: Alexander Kaine/Kano (Kaine-91)

## Current conclusion

The Alexander Kano named with William Glasgow in the 2 July 1526 Sandilands of
Calder remission is **confidently identified** as Alexander Kaine, husband of
Elizabeth Glasgow. The 1552 record directly calls Elizabeth his widow. The
cross-record identity rests on the rare exact forename and near-identical
surname, the immediate William Glasgow association, and Elizabeth's later
occupation of Easter Corswood in the same Calder estate. The spouse relationship
is direct; Alexander's parents and exact birth remain unknown.
