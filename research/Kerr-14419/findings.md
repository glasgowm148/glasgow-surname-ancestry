# Findings: [William Kerr (Kerr-14419)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Kerr-14419)

## Current conclusion

The earlier statement that William Kerr had no parents was incorrect. The
live WikiTree profile attaches [Alexander Kerr (Kerr-14420)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Kerr-14420)
and [Christian (Child) Kerr (Child-2372)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Child-2372)
as his parents. William's 5 September 1862 Stirling death registration names
his parents as Alex Kerr, borough officer, and Christina Child. His marriage
to Ann Eadie in Stirling on 30 May 1819 and the 1851 Stirling census provide
the expected identity continuity. Alexander and Christian are therefore the
concrete parents of William, with the parent relationship supported directly
by the death record and indirectly by the marriage/census identity trail.

The ancestor view does not attach grandparents to either parent profile. The
two lines are not genuinely parentless in the documentary sense, however:
Alexander's baptism biography cites Robert Kerr and Jean Murray as parents,
and Christian's baptism biography cites George Child as her father. These are
research leads, not attached WikiTree relationships.

## Parent-line endpoint audit

### Paternal line

The attached paternal line is **Alexander Kerr (Kerr-14420)**. His live
profile has no structured father or mother, so it is the first genuinely
parentless WikiTree profile on the attached line. His 11 April 1762 South
Leith baptism nevertheless names **Robert Kerr** and **Jean Murray**. No
compatible WikiTree IDs for Robert Kerr or Jean Murray were established in
this pass, and no FamilySearch Family Tree person profiles were confirmed.
Do not attach either name from the baptism alone without a duplicate audit.

### Maternal line

The attached maternal line is **Christian (Child) Kerr (Child-2372)**. Her
live profile has no structured father or mother, so it is the first genuinely
parentless WikiTree profile on the attached line. Her 21 April 1765 Cargill
baptism names **George Child** as father and gives no mother. No compatible
WikiTree ID for the specific George Child was established, and no
FamilySearch Family Tree person profile was confirmed. Do not attach a George
Child candidate without a location/date/occupation match.

The FamilySearch historical-record searches used for the named birth and
marriage collections are represented by the source URLs above; the available
results did not yield a verified FamilySearch Family Tree profile URL for
Robert Kerr, Jean Murray, or George Child.

The FamilySearch Tree search surface used for the endpoint duplicate check was
<https://www.familysearch.org/search/tree/results>. No result was accepted as
one of these people without a matching date, place, and family context.

## Recommended action

Retain [Kerr-14420](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Kerr-14420) and
[Child-2372](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Child-2372) as William's parents.
