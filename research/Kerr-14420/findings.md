# Findings: Kerr-14420

## Current conclusion

[Alexander Kerr (Kerr-14420)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Kerr-14420), born
11 April 1762 at South Leith, is explicitly recorded as a son of **Robert Kerr**
and **Jean Murray**. The strongest WikiTree identification for his father is
[Robert Kerr (Kerr-15792)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Kerr-15792). Robert's
attached son [James Kerr (Kerr-15790)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Kerr-15790)
has the exact 23 August 1765 South Leith birth found in the same indexed
Robert-Kerr/Jean-Murray household. This is strong identity evidence, not merely
a same-name resemblance.

No compatible prior WikiTree profile was established for Jean Murray. The
duplicate review was repeated and she was created as [Jean Murray
(Murray-36890)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Murray-36890). Robert and Jean
are now Alexander's confident parents, and Jean is connected to Robert as his
partner. The links and citations were verified publicly on 2 September 2026.

## Duplicate assessment

### Robert Kerr

A focused WikiTree search for Robert Kerr, born around 1730 in Scotland,
returned 16 results. [Kerr-15792](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Kerr-15792) is
the compatible match because his attached son Kerr-15790 is the exact James
born 23 August 1765 at South Leith to Robert Kerr and Jean Murray.

[Robert Kerr of Newfield (Kerr-5175)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Kerr-5175)
is not this man: he married Eleanora Nugent and has a separately documented
aristocratic family. The Ayrshire, Renfrewshire, Dumfriesshire and emigrant
Robert Kerr results likewise have different places or families. **Do not create
a new Robert Kerr profile; use Kerr-15792.**

### Jean Murray

A broad WikiTree search for Jean Murray around 1730 in Scotland returned 237
results, many of them first-name variants or married-name matches. No result
was found with husband Robert Kerr or the South Leith children. The nearest
geographical loose candidate, [Murray-6367](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Murray-6367),
was baptised in 1711 at Cockpen and would have been about 57 when Charles was
born in 1769; nothing on that profile connects her to Robert or South Leith.
The 1751 Edinburgh and 1756 Canonbie candidates are too young to be mother of
the child baptised in 1757, and other displayed profiles have different spouses
or distant parishes. **No compatible WikiTree profile found—create a new
profile**, after repeating the spouse-and-child duplicate check immediately
before creation.

## FamilySearch famous-relative discrepancy

FamilySearch's famous-relative paths display an Andrew Kerr christened in
Kelso on 22 April 1621, son of George Kerr and Katherin Gardiner, before the
1656 George Kerr and the Mark Kerr line: [VQHH-NY3](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQHH-NY3).
WikiTree's [Andrew Ker (Ker-10)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Ker-10) is a
different Andrew, christened 30 September 1624 in Edinburgh to Robert Ker and
Marione Lyel, with an Edinburgh marriage and child context matching George
Ker (Ker-14). No bridge from the Kelso 1621 bearer to the Edinburgh family was
found. The Mark Kerr ancestry must therefore remain unattached to this
managed branch.
