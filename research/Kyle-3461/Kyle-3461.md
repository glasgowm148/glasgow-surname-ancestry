== Biography ==

'''John Kyle''' was of Tyanee when he married [[Glasgow-3907|Nancy Glasgow]] on 14 January 1842. A contemporary newspaper notice describes Nancy as the sixth daughter of [[Glasgow-3902|Adam Glasgow]] of Inishrush.<ref name="Marriage1842">''Londonderry Standard'', notice republished in [https://cotyrone.com/~inthenews/marriages-notices-1840-42/ ''Marriage Notices 1840–42''], marriage of John Kyle of Tyanee and Nancy, sixth daughter of Adam Glasgow of Inishrush, 14 January 1842.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The marriage notice gives no age or birth year for John. His birth can be stated only as before 14 January 1842 and should remain uncertain. Tyanee was his residence at marriage, not proof of his birthplace.

No inspected record identifies John's parents, occupation, children or later life. He should remain distinct from other Portglenone-area men of the same name unless a record also identifies wife Nancy or the 1842 marriage.

The Tamlaght O'Crilly graveyard account documents Nancy's family of origin but does not name John or support the former estimated birth year.<ref>[https://www.oneirishrover.com/tamlaght-graveyard/ ''Tamlaght O'Crilly Lower Graveyard''], Glasgow family monument transcription.</ref>
