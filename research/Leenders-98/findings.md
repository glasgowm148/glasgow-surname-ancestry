# Maria Magdalena Leenders Glass (Leenders-98) — findings

## Current conclusion

Maria's best-supported birth date is **1 January 1801, uncertain**. The year is
supported independently by the photographed New London headstone and the
[Tristan da Cunha Government historical account](https://www.tristandc.com/po/stamps202005.php),
which identifies Maria Leenders as 1801–1858.

## Source findings

[Find a Grave memorial 32959338](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/32959338)
shows the headstone inscription stating that Maria died aged 57 in October
1858, consistent with birth in 1800–1801. A transcription of the archived
[Glass Family Bible page](https://researcharchives.wits.ac.za/uploads/r/historical-papers-research-archive-library-university-of-witwatersrand/e/6/2/e62291f8683a58abd90b3530d91d67508f39183c6c52e1d50ef18a2ad03f3375/AB1612-Af1-4-03-jpeg.pdf)
cited on WikiTree instead gives 1 January 1802 and baptism on 20 November
1802. The live profile now preserves and explains this conflict; the Bible
image should be rechecked before treating either year as certain.
