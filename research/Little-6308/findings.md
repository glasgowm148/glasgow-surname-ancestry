# Findings: Johne Little (Little-6308)

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

Catharine Little's 1611 Edinburgh baptism names Johne Little as father and
Barbara Glasgow `Glasgow-567` as mother. This proves co-parentage, not marriage.
The John Little, tailor, marriages of 1599 and 1610 have not been proved to refer
to Catharine's father. Keep Barbara as an uncertain spouse unless a marriage or
other relationship record is found.
