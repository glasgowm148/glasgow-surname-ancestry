# Findings: [Jonet Mathie (Mathie-159)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Mathie-159)

## Current conclusion

Jonet Mathie married
[John Glasgow (Glasgow-1030)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1030) in
Glasgow on 25 January 1618. She now has a plausible family corridor into the
Glencairn household. A two-generation John Mathie family at Clerkland in 1600
fits the older and younger Glencairn servitors documented in 1606; the younger
John is the best current candidate for Jonet's father or close older kinsman.
No inspected record yet names her father.

## Relationship assessment

The strongest reconstruction runs from David Mathy and his documented son John
through a writer of that name, the 1583 Glencairn burgess, and the John
senior/junior household at Clerkland. It is circumstantial but coherent in name,
occupation, generation, patronage and locality. John of Ibert is now the
leading identity for the older Glencairn servant killed in 1606. His proved
brother Matthew was a Glasgow burgess and was still serving the burgh in 1608.
Jonet could be an older daughter omitted from the tutory returns, or a daughter
or close relative of the younger Glencairn writer, but she is not among John's
four named children.

This matters to the Glasgow genealogy because Robert Glasgow was directly
associated with David Mathy in 1554, while the Glencairn estate network later
contained Matthew, Michael and the Corsankell Glasgow family. If Jonet's husband
was Robert's son John, the 1618 marriage may continue the earlier civic
association while also connecting the city family socially to the Ayrshire
cluster. This is a promising bridge, not yet proof of shared Glasgow ancestry.

Do not confuse these men with John Mathie the Glasgow cordiner, who was the son
of Allan Mathie and brother of David Mathie in 1607.
