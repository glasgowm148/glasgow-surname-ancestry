# Findings: Mary (McCaughan) Glasgow

Last updated: 2026-09-13

## Current conclusion

The Gortahar death of Mary Glasgow is a **high-confidence identification** with
[Mary McCaughan](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/McCaughan-222), as already assessed
in the 5 September master integration. The earlier page's request for a direct
marriage before identifying her did not reflect that maintained family argument.
This is a reconciliation of existing research. Separately, the repeated
Rasharkin baptisms and the 1851 household document Mary as Alexander's wife.
The records preserve the literal forms **Mary McAughin** (1837) and
**Molly McAughern** (1838 and 1844), maintained as given-name and surname
spelling variants of Mary McCaughan rather than silently normalized.

Her daughter Mary Elder was present at death. The 14 June 1871 marriage identifies
[Mary Glasgow](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3167), of Lisnagaver, as the
daughter of Alexander Glasgow, farmer, and wife of Robert Elder. Elder's later
succession to Alexander's lot 25 supports this family bridge. Combined with the
Mary/Molly baptism and household reconstruction, it strongly identifies the
older Mary; no particular marriage certificate is a blanket prerequisite.

## Death-date correction

The original death image was inspected on 12 September 2026. Entry 234 reads
**“Seventeenth February” 1892**, independently checked in the complete page and
the enlarged row. The earlier maintained **27 February 1892** value was a
transcription error and is now superseded by **17 February 1892**. This does not
weaken the family identification and must not be confused with an unidentified
deceased.

## Assessment provenance and remaining limits

The [5 September master integration](../Glasgow-951/glasgow_parentage_master_integration_2026-09-05.md),
“Alexander Glasgow-951 and Mary/Molly McCaughan”, explicitly assesses the later
deaths as high confidence through Mary Elder. The earlier
[24 August family analysis](../Glasgow-951/sources/gortahar_deaths_and_lisnagaver_revisions_2026-08-24.md)
supplies the marriage/death argument; its obsolete marriage-district and 1888
informant readings are corrected by the master integration and are not adopted
here. The proposed 1901–1911 Elder continuation is not needed to make this bridge.

The unresolved age discrepancy and absence of an explicit McCaughan name on the
death return remain real limitations. Neither changes the documented parents
of James (1837), the unnamed child (1838), or James (1843), changes the
documented spouse relationship, proves other child relationships, or determines
Alexander's ancestry. No live WikiTree edit is part of this pass.
