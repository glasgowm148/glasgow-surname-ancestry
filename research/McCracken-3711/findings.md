# Findings: [Mary McCracken Glasgow (McCracken-3711)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/McCracken-3711)

Last updated: 2026-10-07

## Current conclusion

Mary's gravestone and will strongly identify her as the widow of Wilson Crossier
Glasgow. Her will establishes seven children. Wilson's matching real-estate
index lists Robert, Ruth, Naomi, Matilda, Hiram, Almina and Maria; James J
Glasgow-2738 is absent and should not be added as their child.

Her gravestone-derived birth about 1777 also excludes an apparent but false
identification: she was not Mary McCrachen, aged 17, in Arthur and Ruth's 1768
*Chichester* immigrant household. She may still belong to the post-arrival
generation of that family, but no inspected record names her parents.
