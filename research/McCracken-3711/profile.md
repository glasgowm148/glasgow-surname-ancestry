== Biography ==

'''Mary McCracken''' was born about 1777, calculated from her gravestone age. Her parents and exact birthplace have not been proved.

Mary married [[Glasgow-1982|Wilson Crossier Glasgow]]. Their reciprocal gravestone inscriptions identify Wilson as Mary's husband and Mary as Wilson's wife.<ref name="GildersCreek">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/gilders_creek_cem.htm Gilders Creek Cemetery survey], Newberry County, South Carolina, entries for Wilson and Mary Glasgow.</ref> Wilson died on 11 September 1817.<ref name="GildersCreek" />

Mary headed a household in Newberry County in 1820.<ref name="Census1820">"United States Census, 1820," FamilySearch ([https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XHG6-MT8 record]), Mary Glassgow Junior, Newberry, South Carolina.</ref> She was again enumerated as a household head there in 1830.<ref name="Census1830">"United States Census, 1830," FamilySearch ([https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XHP2-M7Q record]), Mary Glasgow, Newberry, South Carolina.</ref>

Mary made her will on 28 November 1837. She gave her daughter Ruth one-seventh of her property. Daughter Mariah, wife of William McCracken, received one-sixth of the remaining estate for life, with the share then to pass to her brothers and sisters. Mary's son Hiram was appointed Mariah's trustee. The residue went equally to Mary's “other five children.”<ref name="MaryWill">Newberry District, South Carolina, Will Book N, p. 1, will of Mary Glasgow, widow of Wilson Glasgow, dated 28 November 1837 and proved 22 October 1838; [https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184247id_/http://www.lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_WilsonGlasgowWife.html transcription]. The displayed recording date of July 1837 precedes execution and is evidently a transcription error.</ref>

Mary died on 23 March 1838, aged about 61, and was buried at Gilders Creek Cemetery.<ref name="GildersCreek" /><ref name="MaryFindAGrave">{{FindAGrave|33531777}}, memorial for Mary McCracken Glasgow.</ref> Her will was proved on 22 October 1838, and William Glasgow administered her estate in 1839.<ref name="EstateIndex">[https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/estates1785-1949/22.htm Newberry County estate index], Mary Glasgow, 1839, estate 1040, box 43, package 96.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

Mary's will creates seven child-shares: Ruth, Mariah and five other children. The index to Wilson's real-estate packet lists Mary with Robert, Ruth, Naomi, Matilda, Hiram, Almina and Maria. The widow plus these seven apparent children matches the will count, although the original packet is still needed for explicit kin terms for every person.<ref name="RealEstate">Newberry County, South Carolina, real-estate packet 1-13, estate of Wilson Glasgow; [https://genealogytrails.com/scar/newberry/realestate.html index], entries for Wilson Glasgow and Mary, Robert, Ruth, Naomi, Matilda, Hiram, Almina and Maria.</ref>

Ruth, Mariah and Hiram are directly identified as Mary's children. Robert, Naomi, Matilda and Almina complete the seven-name group in Wilson's estate, exactly matching Mary's stated child count. The original real-estate packet should still be inspected before describing its wording as direct proof of each relationship.

[[Glasgow-2738|James J Glasgow]] is not among the seven names and was not attached as Mary's son. A FamilySearch child list also assigns Mary and Wilson a Fleming Bates Glasgow born in 1823, but Wilson had died in 1817. The online list therefore combines more than one family.
