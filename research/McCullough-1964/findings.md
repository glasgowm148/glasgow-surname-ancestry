# Findings: McCullough-1964

Last updated: 2026-07-16

## Current answer

Margaret McCullough's name and alleged 1733 marriage to Hugh Glasgow are present
in William Melanchthon Glasgow's own circa-1890s family history, rather than being
a later WikiTree interpolation. No contemporary source found corroborates the
marriage, identifies Margaret's father, or supports her estimated birth and death.

## Source findings

| Source | Finding | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| William Melanchthon Glasgow family history, original scan, p. 2 | Says Hugh worked for an aged weaver named McCullough, married the employer's daughter Margaret McCullough in 1733, and had sons John, Robert, and Hugh. | Genuine late family tradition written about 160 years after the alleged marriage. It cites no register, will, deed, Bible, letter, or informant and does not give Margaret's father's forename. [Source manifest](sources/mccullough_marriage_claim_manifest.md). |
| 1740 Protestant householders' return, Tamlaght O'Crilly | One Hugh Glasgow occurs two entries before James McCullough; a widow McCullough appears later. No townlands or relationships are stated. | Parish surname coexistence only. Neither James nor the widow can be assigned as Margaret's parent from list adjacency. |
| County Derry church-record inventories | Surviving Kilrea and Tamlaght O'Crilly-area baptism and marriage registers begin decades after 1733. | Coverage gap, not evidence that the marriage did not occur. |

## Unsupported profile fields
