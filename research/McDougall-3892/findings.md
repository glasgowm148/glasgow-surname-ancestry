# Findings: Margaret McDougall (McDougall-3892)

Last updated: 2026-09-01

## Current conclusion

Margaret's daughter recorded as Jane Kerr at birth and Jean Gilmour at death
can be identified confidently. Jane was born in Bothwell in 1884, married
William Gilmour there in 1902, and died at Dearborn, Michigan, on 10 December
1945. Her Michigan death record names George Kerr and Margaret McDougall as her
parents.

Jane/Jean and William had seven children whose Scottish birth-index entries
form a coherent Bothwell sequence. Five of them were still in their parents'
Dearborn household in 1930. This branch was published to live WikiTree profiles
on 1 September 2026 after duplicate checking; it does not resolve Margaret
McDougall's own parents.

## Duplicate assessment

Precise WikiTree person searches found no matching profile for Jane/Jean Kerr or
for any of the seven children. `Gilmour-947` and `Gilmour-1157` are different
men; neither matches the William Gilmour who married Jeanie Kerr at Bothwell in
1902.
