birth date agree, and the profile names [[Glasgow-821|Rose Glasgow]] and
[[McKeown-873|Alexander McKeown]] as his parents. The catalogue occurrence is
now mapped to that profile; no new profile should be created.

County Londonderry. Preserve that conflict until the Bible image or a stronger
place analysis is inspected. The profile and descendant compilation also
disagree between 1899 and 1889 for his death year; do not change it from the
derivative transcription alone.
