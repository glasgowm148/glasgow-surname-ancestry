== Research Notes ==

=== Possible Kirkliston son ===

[[Glasgow-159|John Glasgow]], who married Agnes Gray at Kirkliston in 1785, may be the same man as [[Glasgow-2980|John Glasgow]], represented as the son of Janet Mill and [[Glasgow-1023|Thomas Glasgow]] at Mid Calder in 1750. A man recorded in 1750 could marry in 1785 at about 35. The Kirkliston children Thomas (1786) and Janet (1790) give modest naming support for this couple. The present working assessment is possible, 42%, dependent on that one unresolved identity; the two John accounts may represent one man. Other local Johns remain compatible, so no merge or birth correction follows. Janet’s circa-1730 birth is an estimate, not a recorded age.
