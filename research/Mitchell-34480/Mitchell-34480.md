== Biography ==
'''Hillsdale family roles, 17 October 2026:''' The unnamed Mitchell wife is the '''probable spouse''' of [[Glasgow-1970|James]] and '''probable mother''' of the record-defined Hillsdale [[Glasgow-1968|John]]. Her given name and life dates remain unresolved; she is not assigned to the Gortin John through an unproved identity.<ref name="CookstownGortinReview" />
The <i>History of Hillsdale County, Michigan</i> describes a James Glasgow who's "wife was a Mitchell".  They emigrated from Scotland to northern Ireland in about 1750 and settled in County Tyrone near Cookstown.
