== Biography ==
'''Robert Montgomerie''' was a member of the Montgomery family of Ayrshire, Scotland. He was born about '''1625''', probably in '''Ayrshire, Scotland'''. His birth year is estimated from the birth of his known son, Robert Montgomery of Bonyglen, in 1660.

Robert was the third son of '''Hugh Montgomerie of Auchinhood and Bowhouse'''.<ref name="Montgomery117">[https://electricscotland.com/webclans/m/genealogicalmontgomery.pdf Thomas Harrison Montgomery, ''A Genealogical History of the Family of Montgomery'', Philadelphia, 1863, p. 117.]</ref>

His father held '''Auchinhood''', part of the barony of Eaglesham, and '''Bowhouse'''. Hugh reportedly died in '''1658''', aged ninety-six.<ref name="Montgomery117" />

Robert's mother is uncertain. Hugh Montgomerie married '''Margaret Calderwood''', daughter of Calderwood of Peacockbank, and apparently married secondly a woman surnamed '''Peebles''', possibly of the Broomlands family. The published pedigree does not state which wife was Robert's mother.<ref name="Montgomery117" />

Robert married a woman described in the pedigree as '''Miss Mackwatersa of Maybole'''.<ref name="Montgomery120">[https://electricscotland.com/webclans/m/genealogicalmontgomery.pdf Thomas Harrison Montgomery, ''A Genealogical History of the Family of Montgomery'', Philadelphia, 1863, p. 120.]</ref> Her given name is not recorded, and the spelling of her surname may be incorrect or corrupted.

Robert later moved from Scotland to '''Ireland'''.<ref name="Montgomery120" />

Robert and his wife had at least one known child:

* '''Robert Montgomerie of Bonyglen''', born at '''Rosemont''' in '''1660'''. He later resided at Bonyglen or Bonnylyn, County Donegal, and married an unnamed daughter of the Rev. Alexander Conyngham, Dean of Raphoe.<ref name="Montgomery120" />

Robert's date and place of death are currently unknown.

== Ancestry ==
The published Montgomery pedigree gives Robert's paternal ancestry as follows:<ref name="Montgomery117" /><ref name="Montgomery120" />

* '''William Montgomerie of Greenfield'''
** '''Arthur Montgomerie of Stane'''
*** '''Hugh Montgomerie of Stane and Auchinhood'''
**** '''Hugh Montgomerie of Auchinhood and Bowhouse'''
***** '''Robert Montgomerie'''

William Montgomerie of Greenfield is identified as the fourth son of '''Hugh Montgomerie, 1st Earl of Eglinton'''.<ref name="Montgomery117" /><ref name="Montgomery120" />

== Research Notes ==
Thomas Harrison Montgomery identifies Robert as the third son of Hugh Montgomerie of Auchinhood and Bowhouse and as the ancestor of the Montgomerys of Bonyglen and Sir Henry Cunningham Montgomery.<ref name="Montgomery117" />

The Bonyglen pedigree states that Robert married “Miss Mackwatersa, of Maybole” and moved to Ireland, where he had a son named Robert.<ref name="Montgomery120" />

The surname '''Mackwatersa''' has not been independently verified. It may represent a typographical error, an obsolete spelling or an inaccurate transcription from an earlier pedigree.

The source does not provide:

* Robert's birth date or birthplace;
* his wife's given name;
* a marriage date or place;
* a definite identification of his mother;
* his death date or place; or
* evidence of any children other than Robert of Bonyglen.

The information comes from a nineteenth-century compiled genealogy rather than a contemporary birth, marriage, probate or land record. The relationships should therefore be treated as a published pedigree pending primary-source confirmation.
