## Source findings — mother, marriage, removal to Ireland and the Bonyglen son, from the biography review, 28 September 2026

Thomas Harrison Montgomery, *A Genealogical History of the Family of Montgomery* (Philadelphia, 1863), [pp. 117 and 120](https://electricscotland.com/webclans/m/genealogicalmontgomery.pdf), says that Robert was the third son of Hugh Montgomerie of Auchinhood and Bowhouse, who reportedly died in 1658 aged ninety-six; that Hugh married Margaret Calderwood, daughter of Calderwood of Peacockbank, and apparently secondly a woman surnamed Peebles, without saying which was Robert's mother; that Robert married "Miss Mackwatersa, of Maybole" and moved to Ireland; and that their son Robert Montgomerie of Bonyglen (Bonnylyn, County Donegal) was born at Rosemont in 1660 and married an unnamed daughter of the Rev. Alexander Conyngham, Dean of Raphoe.

This is a nineteenth-century published pedigree, to be confirmed from primary records. The wife's surname is unverified and may be corrupt, and Robert's birth about 1625 is only an estimate from the son's birth in 1660.
