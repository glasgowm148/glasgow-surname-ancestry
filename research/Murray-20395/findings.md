# Findings: [Sarah Murray (Murray-20395), wife of James Glasgow-1504](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Murray-20395)

Last updated: 2026-10-29

## Current conclusion

Sarah or Sara Murray married [James Glasgow (Glasgow-1504)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1504) in August 1727, and four children were recorded to the couple from 1728 to 1740. Both rest on a derivative register chronology, not an inspected original. Her birth is best given as **about 1711 or earlier** (estimate), assuming she was at least 16 at the 1727 marriage. The profile's "about 1710" is not wrong, but it states a single year that no record supplies. Her parents are unknown. The full account is in [James Glasgow-1504's findings](../Glasgow-1504/findings.md).
