# John Patton (Patton-11260)

## Current conclusion

John Patton is directly identified as the father of [[Patton-11261|Mary Patton]] with [[Glasgow-4015|Margaret Glasgow]] in Mary's 6 October 1825 marriage entry. The parental household was associated with Tyanee and the Portglenone congregation. John's birth before about 1787 is an adult-status estimate; his birthplace, occupation, marriage details and death remain unknown.

## Source findings

- First Kilrea Presbyterian Church marriage register, Wilson Gordon and Mary Patton, 6 October 1825; Richard John Torrens transcription: https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/KilreaP1/Marriages/1825.html
