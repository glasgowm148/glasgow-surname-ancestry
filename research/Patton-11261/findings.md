# Mary (Patton) Gordon (Patton-11261)

## Current conclusion

Mary Patton, daughter of [[Patton-11260|John Patton]] and [[Glasgow-4015|Margaret Glasgow]] of Tyanee, married Wilson Gordon on 6 October 1825 at First Kilrea Presbyterian Church. The entry associates the family with the Portglenone congregation. Mary's birth before about 1807 is an adult-status estimate; no later-life record has been identified.
