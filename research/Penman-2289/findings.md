# Findings: [Janet Penman (Penman-2289)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Penman-2289)

## Current conclusion

Janet's 1772 Auchterderran baptism directly names Alexander Penman and Helen
McKenzies as her parents. Their 1769 Auchterderran marriage independently names
the couple as Alexr Penman and Hellan McKinniss. [Hellan McKinniss
(McKinniss-12)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/McKinniss-12) was created and
attached as Janet's confident mother on 2 September 2026.

Alexander Penman is now treated as a **probable but uncertain** identification
with [Alexander Penman (Penman-1898)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Penman-1898).
The candidate has the exact 1747 Auchterderran baptism and later children with
Helen Beveridge, while Janet's 1772 baptism and the 1769 marriage establish an
earlier Alexander Penman/Hellan McKinniss family in the same parish. A second
marriage before the 1781 Beveridge child is chronologically plausible. This
does not prove that the two family groups are the same man, so the father link
is retained as uncertain pending an inspected marriage or original register
image.

## Duplicate assessment

WikiTree searches for Helen/Hellan in Fife around 1746 under McKinniss,
McKenzie and McInnes, including surname variants, returned no compatible
profile. `Penman-1757` was a creation-form suggestion but is a different woman,
born Penman at Airth. McKinniss-12 is therefore a distinct profile.
