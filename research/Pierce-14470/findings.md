# Findings: Mary J. (Pierce) Glasgow (Pierce-14470)

## Current conclusion

Reviewed 2026-10-26. Mary J. Pierce is assessed at 93% as the biological mother of Robert Finley Glasgow (Glasgow-2356). The inspected family-history typescript attributed to William Melanchthon Glasgow states that Moses Thomas Glasgow married Mary on 5 November 1839 and identifies three children: Lois Ladd Glasgow (Glasgow-2357), Robert Finley Glasgow (Glasgow-2356), and an infant John Cameron Glasgow, whose identity is not resolved and who must not be conflated with John Cameron Glasgow-466 (born 1820). The same account records Mary’s death on 14 March 1846 and Moses’s marriage to Martha Thompson (Thompson-63146) in 1848. This supports Mary as Moses’s first wife and Robert’s biological mother, while Martha was Robert’s later stepmother. No original birth or death certificate was inspected. The typescript has minor date errors; its wording that Mary was “in her twenty-sixth year” means age 25 and is compatible with the retained birth before 1821. No vital fields are changed.

The prior Martha assignment on Robert’s profile was unmarked; this first maternal review does not invent a prior percentage or a 0% baseline. The 93% estimate is for the complete biological-mother proposition within the stated source scope.

See the cross-linked accounts for [Robert Finley Glasgow](../Glasgow-2356/findings.md) and [Martha Thompson](../Thompson-63146/findings.md); the source is the [William Melanchthon Glasgow family-history PDF](https://www.wikitree.com/photo.php/0/0a/Glasgow-590.pdf).

The separate review of [Lois Ladd Glasgow](../Glasgow-2357/findings.md) also assesses the complete Lois–Mary mother proposition at 93%. Mary was already attached to Lois, unmarked; this is an existing-mother review, not another Martha-to-Mary replacement. The source's June 1870 report concerns Lois's death, not Mary's.
