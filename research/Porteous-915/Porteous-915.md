== Biography ==
Alexander Porteous was christened on 14 July 1608 in Edinburgh Parish, according to the FamilySearch index, which names the father as Andrew Porteous and the mother as Margaret Porteous.<ref>“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564–1950,” FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4N-KQ2 indexed record]. Original image unavailable and not inspected for this review.</ref>

== Research Notes ==
The event is a christening, not an established birthday. Birth before 1609 is a conservative bound; the former circa 1612 birth is not supported by the cited record. Margaret’s maiden surname and ancestry are unknown. No later adult identity is inferred. The maternity assessment is strongly supported by this derivative index; the prior 35% unselected assessment remains in research history, with no invented new percentage.
