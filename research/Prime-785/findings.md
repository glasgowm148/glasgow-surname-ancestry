# Findings: [Esther Prime (Prime-785)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Prime-785)

## Current conclusion

Esther Prime was baptised in 1784 at Coleshill, daughter of
[Samuel Prime (Prime-954)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Prime-954) and
[Elizabeth Burr (Burr-5654)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Burr-5654). Her
mother was not Green. The record-supported line also extends through both
parents: Samuel's father was [Samuel Prime
(Prime-955)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Prime-955), whose wife was probably
[Sarah Hammond (Hammond-17711)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hammond-17711);
Elizabeth's parents were [John Burr
(Burr-5655)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Burr-5655) and [Sarah (Unknown)
Burr (Unknown-765121)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Unknown-765121), whose
maiden surname is unproved.
