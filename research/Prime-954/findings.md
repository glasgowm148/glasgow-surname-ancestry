# Findings: [Samuel Prime (Prime-954)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Prime-954)

## Current conclusion

Samuel Prime was baptised in 1746 at Bickenhill, Warwickshire, as a son of
Samuel Prime. He married Elizabeth Burr at Bickenhill on 13 November 1770.
Their identification as the parents of Esther Prime is supported by Esther's
1784 Coleshill baptism, which names her parents as Samuel and Elizabeth, read
together with the marriage that identifies Elizabeth's birth surname.

The elder Samuel is [Samuel Prime (Prime-955)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Prime-955).
[Sarah (Hammond) Prime (Hammond-17711)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hammond-17711)
is a probable rather than directly proved mother: she married the elder Samuel
at Bickenhill in 1738, but the younger Samuel's baptism names only his father.

## Parentage and duplicate assessment

The baptism directly supports [Prime-955](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Prime-955)
as Samuel's father. The 1738 marriage and Bickenhill family sequence support
[Hammond-17711](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hammond-17711) as his probable
mother, but no cited record directly names her in that role.

No compatible English Samuel Prime profile was found before `Prime-954` was
created; the dated WikiTree search results were Massachusetts men.

## WikiTree treatment

The live profile `Prime-954` was created and linked as husband of
[Elizabeth (Burr) Prime (Burr-5654)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Burr-5654),
father of [Esther Prime (Prime-785)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Prime-785),
and son of `Prime-955` and `Hammond-17711`. The father relationship is
explicitly confident. The mother relationship is explicitly uncertain, and
the rendered live profile was checked to confirm that status. Bickenhill
locations inferred from the marriage and baptism context remain uncertain.
