# Findings: [Samuel Prime (Prime-955)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Prime-955)

## Current conclusion

Samuel Prime was baptised at Bickenhill, Warwickshire, in 1708 as a son of
William Prime. He married Sarah Hammond at Bickenhill on 15 June 1738. A 1746
Bickenhill baptism names Samuel Prime as the father of a younger Samuel; the
marriage and local child sequence make Sarah Hammond the younger Samuel's
probable mother, although that baptism does not name her.

## Source findings

- [Samuel Prime baptism NL85-9P2](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:NL85-9P2): Samuel Prime was baptised in 1708 at Bickenhill, Warwickshire, son of William Prime. The index does not identify his mother.
- [Samuel Prime and Sarah Hammond marriage NV8V-3XT](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:NV8V-3XT): Samuel Prime married Sarah Hammond on 15 June 1738 at Bickenhill.
- [Younger Samuel Prime baptism XXGD-R5G](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XXGD-R5G): Samuel was baptised in 1746 at Bickenhill and the record names his father as Samuel Prime. It does not name his mother.
- FamilySearch tree profile: [Samuel LCZJ-XF8](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/LCZJ-XF8). This helps align the records but is not independent proof.

## Parentage and relationship assessment

William Prime is directly named as Samuel's father in the 1708 baptism. No
reviewed record identifies Samuel's mother. The 1738 marriage directly supports
Samuel's marriage to Sarah Hammond. Sarah's relationship to the younger Samuel
is probable rather than proved because the 1746 baptism names only the father;
that maternal relationship should remain uncertain on WikiTree.

## WikiTree duplicate audit

The live duplicate search found no compatible English Samuel Prime profile;
the dated results were Massachusetts men. `Prime-955` was therefore created for
the Bickenhill man represented by these records. No profile should be merged
with it without matching the Bickenhill baptism, marriage and family context.

## Recommended action

Keep [William Prime (Prime-956)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Prime-956) as
Samuel's father; the relationship is explicitly marked confident from the
1708 baptism. Do not add a mother without a source. Keep
[Sarah Hammond (Hammond-17711)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hammond-17711)
as Samuel's wife. Preserve the younger Samuel's maternal relationship as
uncertain unless a baptism image, will or another primary record names Sarah as
his mother.
