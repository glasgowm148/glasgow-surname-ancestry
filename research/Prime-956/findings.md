# Findings: [William Prime (Prime-956)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Prime-956)

## Current conclusion

William Prime is directly identified as the father of Samuel Prime in Samuel's
1708 Bickenhill, Warwickshire, baptism. The reviewed evidence does not identify
William's wife, parents, birth or death.

## Source findings

- [Samuel Prime baptism NL85-9P2](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:NL85-9P2): Samuel Prime was baptised in 1708 at Bickenhill, Warwickshire, son of William Prime. This is direct evidence for William's name and paternity.
- FamilySearch tree profile for William's son: [Samuel LCZJ-XF8](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/LCZJ-XF8). This helps align the baptism with the researched Prime line but is not independent proof of William's wider identity.

## Parentage and identity assessment

The baptism supports William as the father of
[Samuel Prime (Prime-955)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Prime-955), but it does
not establish any other relationship for William. Bickenhill is documented for
his son's baptism and may support an uncertain location for William; it does
not prove that William was born or died there.

No reviewed evidence names William's parents or Samuel's mother. This is a
one-parent extension, not a complete parent set.

unsupported vital dates. Search the original Bickenhill baptism register,
