# Richard Prime (Prime-957)

## Current conclusion

Richard Pryme was christened at Bickenhill on 1 January 1644 to Richard and
Alis Pryme. He is probably the Richard Prime who fathered children in the same
parish from 1680 to 1687, including William Prime, but no record explicitly
bridges the christened child to the later father. The link to William remains
uncertain.
