Unique designation 'of Montgreenan', described as 'the late' in Sept 1847. Of the Montgreenan owners: Dr Robert Glasgow-1076 died in 1827. Wikipedia says he had no offspring, but his own findings record natural children, including Anne, wife of Robertson-30564, so that is not a ground for exclusion; he remains a weaker possible alternative (corrected 7 October 2026; see `research/Glasgow-1076/findings.md`). His son Robert Robertson-Glasgow inherited in 1845 and lived to 1860 (thepeerage), so he cannot be 'the late' in 1847. Only Robertson-30564 survives. ## Source findings — UCL Legacies of British Slavery summary, copied 10 March 2025

The [UCL LBS entry for Robert Glasgow late Robertson](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/lbs/person/view/27300), as copied into the owner's earlier notes, gives his dates as 1775 to **27 January 1845** and reports, from an e-mail of Dr Anthony J. Cooke (9 February 2011), that he died at Pau, France, in 1845, his inventory being NRS **SC70/1/69/533**. It also records an Edinburgh MD (1797) and FRCS (1802), his marriage to Anne Glasgow on 2 August 1804, **four children: Robert the heir, Philadelphia Jane, Anne and Charlotte Marie Cecilia**, and an 1841 census household at Holly Walk, Leamington Priors, that includes Charlotte Robertson, aged 10.

Seen as: a copied catalogue summary, not inspected originals; the 1841 household is quoted from that summary. A death in January 1845 agrees with the son's succession in 1845 and with 'the late Robert Glasgow of Montgreenan' in September 1847. The named daughters Anne and Charlotte Marie Cecilia support his identification as father of the 1847 brides. Next record: NRS SC70/1/69/533.
