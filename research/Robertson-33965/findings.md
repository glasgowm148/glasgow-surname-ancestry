## Unresolved — Montfoid testament comparison, 10 September 2026

Grant's *Commissariot Record of Glasgow* ([printed p. 426](https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/page/426/mode/2up); cross-reference p. 196) calendars the testament of Bessie Robertson, spouse of John Glasgow in Montfoid, confirmed **17 August 1610**. James Paterson's *History of the Counties of Ayr and Wigtown*, Cuninghame, [pp. 53–54](https://archive.org/download/historyofcountie0003pt1pate/historyofcountie0003pt1pate.pdf), quotes an **unnamed** Montfoid tenant's testament with the same confirmation date: Jean Boyd, Lady Montfoid, is creditor for 'ane boll of ferme meill', and the tenant died in February 1606. Paterson names neither Bessie nor John; his account is a later printed quotation, not an inspected original.

The shared estate and date make this a useful target, not proof. Obtain the original testament (CC9/7; volume and image not yet identified) and compare the confirmation date, Montfoid tenancy, the Lady Montfoid creditor, the ferme-meal debt and the death wording. Until then do not enter February 1606 as Bessie's death or treat the tenant as her husband. If a 1606 death is proved, reconsider children attributed to her from later baptisms only after distinguishing birth from baptism dates; it would not exclude [John Glasgow (Glasgow-3296)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3296) from later fatherhood. The 1650 Quothquan testament's Bessie Glasgow is John's sister, not this Bessie.
