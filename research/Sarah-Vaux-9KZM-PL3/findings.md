# Sarah Vaux (FamilySearch 9KZM-PL3)

## Current conclusion

FamilySearch `9KZM-PL3` is the surviving Sarah Vaux profile. Her former profile
`MJ4Z-29K` was merged into it. The former Thomas Osbourn duplicate has also
already been merged into the surviving older Thomas Bowen profile `LWHR-LR4`.

Four original RG7 manuscript versions establish one clandestine marriage on
30 March 1738. The rough working notebook, TNA RG7/698, and two register copies,
RG7/165 p. 15 and RG7/145 p. 83, identify the groom as Thomas or Tho Bowen of
Hempstead/Hemel Hempstead, Hertfordshire. RG7/73 p. 52 alone gives the corrupted
form Thos Osbourn of Redbourn. The original-source balance resolves the groom as
Thomas Bowen; Osbourn/Redbourn is a contemporary copying error, not a second
person, alternate surname, residence, or marriage.

The RG7/698 page is headed 1738 and the clean Bowen copies give 30 March 1738.
Derivative 30 March 1737 entries are misdated copies of the same event. Fleet,
London, Middlesex, and Westminster labels likewise represent catalogue/index
treatments of the same clandestine marriage rather than separate ceremonies.

## Relationship assessment

Older Thomas Bowen `LWHR-LR4` and Sarah Vaux `9KZM-PL3` are the couple in the
30 March 1738 marriage. Their son Thomas Bowen `LCXX-Q1F` was christened at
Coventry on 6 March 1741 to Thos Bowen and Sarah and later married Sarah
Gardner/Gardiner. The baptism proves parents named Thos and Sarah; identifying
that Sarah as Sarah Vaux is the genealogical conclusion supplied by the 1738
marriage and subsequent Thomas-and-Sarah Bowen family chronology.

Sarah is explicitly a widow in the RG7 manuscripts. Vaux may therefore be a
previous married surname rather than her birth surname. `Hollings` remains an
unproved research lead only; no primary source currently establishes it as her
maiden name.
