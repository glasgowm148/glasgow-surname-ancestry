== Biography ==

'''Margaret Scott''' married [[Glasgow-3587|William Glasgon]] on 4 March 1708/9. The Cavers parish entry says they were “Married in Hummelknows.” The transcription gives no age, occupation, residence or father for either spouse.<ref name="GlasgonScott">FreeREG, “William GLASGON and Margaret SCOTT,” marriage, 4 March 1708/9, Cavers Parish Church, Roxburghshire, [https://www.freereg.org.uk/search_records/68618e91865574707dcbe5ff/william-glasgon-margaret-scott-marriage-roxburghshire-cavers-1709-03-04 transcription] (accessed 16 July 2026).</ref> A derivative FamilySearch index independently points to the same 1709 marriage.<ref>“Scotland, Marriages, 1561–1910,” FamilySearch ([https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYQG-3XD record XYQG-3XD]), William Glasgow and Margaret Scott, 1709.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

Margaret's birth date, birthplace and parents remain unknown. Her birth can be bounded only as before her marriage on 4 March 1708/9; a circa-1690 estimate is not supported by the cited entry.

An Isabel Glasgon was baptised at Cavers on 27 August 1710. Her father was William Glasgon and her abode was Humble Knows, making her a plausible child of this marriage, but the baptism does not name her mother. Isabel should therefore remain a candidate child rather than a proved daughter of Margaret.<ref name="Isabel1710">FreeREG, “Isabel GLASGON,” baptism, 27 August 1710, Cavers Parish Church, Roxburghshire, [https://www.freereg.org.uk/search_records/68618e34865574707dcbe3e5/isabel-glasgon-baptism-roxburghshire-cavers-1710-08-27 transcription] (accessed 16 July 2026).</ref>
