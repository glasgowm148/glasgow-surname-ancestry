== Biography ==

'''Hugh Shannon''' was the husband of [[Glasgow-1500|Ann Glasgow]]. The
1817–1818 Wilson inheritance litigation identifies Ann, daughter of Rachel
Wilson, as having married Hugh Shannon of Ireland.<ref name="Equity">[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184247id_/http://www.lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_Complaint.html John Glasgow et al. v. Robert Glasgow, complaint], 1817, archived transcription; [https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184247id_/http://www.lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Paper_Reply.html Robert Glasgow's sworn answer], 2 June 1817, archived transcription.</ref>

Hugh Shannon appears with Robert and James Glasgow among the Kilwaughter
petitioners on 1 April 1772. That association supports the Kilwaughter network,
but the petition states no relationship.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20101124184109/http://lifesweaving.com/Glasgow/Kilwaughter/sources.html Archived Kilwaughter source notes], citing PRONI T808/15082, petition dated 1 April 1772.</ref>

PRONI catalogues a copy will for Hugh Shannon of Ballykeel dated 5 April 1776.
Its clauses have not been inspected and cannot yet establish children or other
relationships.<ref>[https://apps.proni.gov.uk/eCatNI_IE/SearchPage.aspx Public Record Office of Northern Ireland eCatalogue], reference LPC/1037, copy will of Hugh Shannon of Ballykeel, dated 5 April 1776.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The former birth about 1710 and death about 1776 are unsupported legacy
estimates. The date on the uninspected copy will is not a proved death date.
An unsourced family tree handed down to [[Glasgow-933|M Glasgow]] remains a
research lead only and does not establish Hugh's vitals, children, or parents.<ref
name="Legacy">Unsourced family tree handed down to M Glasgow; cited on this profile before this revision.</ref>

Later Ballykeel leases naming Hugh and Robert Shannon are useful identity leads,
but do not state their relationship to this Hugh.<ref>PRONI, D300/2/1/21/1,
13 October 1798; D300/2/1/21/7, 1 November 1801.</ref>
