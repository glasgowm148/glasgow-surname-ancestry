# Findings: Hugh Shannon (Shannon-4518), husband of Ann Glasgow

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

The 1817-1818 Wilson inheritance litigation directly identifies Ann Glasgow,
daughter of Rachel Wilson, as the wife of **Hugh Shannon of Ireland**. The
1 April 1772 petition places Hugh consecutively with Robert and James Glasgow
under Kilwaughter Parish, strongly supporting the same family network.

The petition states no relationship. A surviving **copy** of Hugh's will has now
been located at PRONI as `LPC/1037`, dated 5 April 1776. The catalogue marks it
open but exposes no digital image or clauses, so reported children of Hugh and
Ann must remain search leads until that copy is read.

## Priority original

Request the complete copy of `LPC/1037`, including endorsements. Transcribe every
beneficiary, executor, witness, residence and land clause. A Glasgow described as
brother-in-law, uncle, cousin, co-heir or predecessor could prove a collateral
bridge; an unexplained witness or neighbour would establish association only.
