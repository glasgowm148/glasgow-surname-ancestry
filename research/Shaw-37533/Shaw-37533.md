# WikiTree update notes: [Rosanna Glasgow (Shaw-37533), birth currently about 1790, birthplace unknown](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Shaw-37533)

## Suggested corrections

- Change LNAB from `Shaw` to `Unknown`; no inspected record identifies her birth
  family.
- Change birth from about `1790` to **before about 1780** if Daniel Glasgow,
  born about 1795/96, remains her uncertain son.
- Mark the reported death about 23 October 1822 uncertain and cite it only as a
  2007 descendant account, not a contemporary record.
- Keep Daniel `Glasgow-3896`, Alexander `Glasgow-951` and Thomas `Glasgow-987`
  uncertain as children. Daniel and Thomas are proved brothers, but their mother
  is not identified by the proof records.

## Do not add as fact

- Do not call James Shaw of Kilrea her brother. Slater's directory establishes
  only that he and Daniel Glasco shared a trade and town.
- Do not use the similar names Rose, Rose Ann and Rosanna to merge her with Adam
  Glasgow's wife Rose or another local woman.
