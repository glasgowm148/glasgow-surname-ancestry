# Findings: [Rosanna Glasgow (Shaw-37533), birth currently about 1790, birthplace unknown](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Shaw-37533)

Last updated: 2026-07-17

## Current conclusion

Rosanna is a defensible **uncertain working mother** of Daniel, Alexander and
Thomas Glasgow in the Bann Valley-Killoquin cluster, but only a late descendant
account names her as Thomas's mother. Her maiden surname is not established.
Use **Rosanna Unknown**, born before about 1780 if she is retained as Daniel's
mother. Her reported death about 23 October 1822 is unconfirmed.
