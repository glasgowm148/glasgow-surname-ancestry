# Findings: [Margaret (Smith) Glasgow (Smith-374110)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Smith-374110)

## Current conclusion

The Glasgow Commissary testament calendar directly identifies Margaret Smith
as spouse of James Glasgow in Brekpleughe, Stevenston parish, in an entry dated
21 February 1632. The current identification of that James with
[Glasgow-3921](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3921) remains a working
identity rather than a relationship stated by the calendar.

No inspected record identifies [Agnes (Glasgow) Logan
(Glasgow-3178)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3178) as Margaret's
daughter. Agnes's 1628 testament-calendar entry names her husband John Logan
but no parent. Detach the unsupported mother link and leave Agnes's mother blank.

- Retain Margaret's spouse placement only as the explicitly qualified
  Glasgow-3921 working identity.
- Detach Smith-374110 as Glasgow-3178's mother pending direct evidence.
