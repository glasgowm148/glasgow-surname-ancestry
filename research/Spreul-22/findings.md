## Unresolved — how many children the town clerk had, 28 September 2026

Printed accounts conflict. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Spreul_(town_clerk)) gives the town clerk twelve or more children. John William Burns ([Preface, p. vi](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=nyp.33433023184579&view=2up&seq=16)) says that Ann Spreull was his only child, that she married James Spreull, surgeon in Paisley, and that the surgeon left one daughter, who married James Shortridge in 1700. Neither account has been tested against parish registers, testaments or burgh records.
