== Biography ==

John Sprewle was born around 1590 (estimated based on 1640 marriage of son).

His father is unknown, but he was the son of John Spreull, vicar of Cambuslang. <ref name=metcalfe>William Musham Metcalfe, ''A history of the county of Renfrew from the earliest times'', 1905, page 104, https://archive.org/details/historyofcountyo00metc/page/104<blockquote>John Spreull, the vicar of Cambuslang, was succeeded by his son and heir, whose son was Provost of Renfrew, and attended the Parliament of 1630 as one of the Commissioners of the Royal Burghs. The Provost was succeeded by his son, who was bred to the law, and was appointed Town Clerk of the city of Glasgow, and subsequently was one of the principal clerks of the Court of Session.</blockquote></ref>

He was Provost of Renfrew. <ref name=metcalfe/>

He was the father of John Spreull, Town Clerk of Glasgow. <ref name=metcalfe/>

In 1630 he was one of the Commissioners of the Royal Burghs. <ref name=metcalfe/>

In 1649 he caught some witches. <ref>''The new statistical account of Scotland'', 1845, volume 7, page 241, <blockquote>27 Sept. 1649. Reported by John Sprewle, proveist of Renfrew, that he had apprehendit some women in Renfrew for eminent presumptions of Witchcraft.</blockquote></ref>
