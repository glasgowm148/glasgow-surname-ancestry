== Biography ==

John Sprewle or Spreull is represented here as the Provost of Renfrew whose son became Glasgow Town Clerk. His birth date and birthplace are not established. The inherited circa-1590 birth is an estimate based on the son's reported marriage, not a recorded birth.

Metcalfe describes a succession from John Spreull, vicar of Cambuslang, to the vicar's son and heir, then to that heir's son, the Provost of Renfrew. The Provost was therefore the vicar's grandson, not his son. The intervening father is represented by [[Spreull-17|Unknown Spreull]]. Metcalfe does not give the Provost's forename.<ref name="Metcalfe104">William Musham Metcalfe, ''A History of the County of Renfrew from the Earliest Times'' (1905), [https://archive.org/details/historyofcountyo00metc/page/104 p. 104]. Published compiled succession; no original kinship instrument inspected for this account.</ref>

The same account says that the Provost attended the Parliament of 1630 as a commissioner of the royal burghs. It describes his son as trained in law, appointed Town Clerk of Glasgow and subsequently a principal clerk of the Court of Session. This is the compiled basis for the maintained father relationship with [[Spreul-22|John Spreul, Glasgow Town Clerk]].<ref name="Metcalfe104" />

A published extract dated 27 September 1649 names John Sprewle, provost of Renfrew, reporting that he had apprehended women on suspicions of witchcraft. The report records an allegation and apprehension, not proof of witchcraft. Identification of this named provost with Metcalfe's unnamed commissioner is an inference from the office, surname and chronology.<ref>''The New Statistical Account of Scotland'' (1845), vol. 7, p. 241, entry dated 27 September 1649. Printed extract; the underlying burgh minute has not been inspected.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The maintained father assessments remain unchanged: the vicar's son and heir is the Provost's father, and the Provost is father of the Glasgow town clerk. No new percentage, parent addition or live relationship change is proposed by this wording correction.

FamilySearch candidates PQ6D-BXP and PW7S-HWX remain unresolved alternatives for this record-defined provost. The identified town-clerk account KZYG-RDF supplies a positive family comparison, but its attached father is not automatically proved to be this Provost. Neither candidate is excluded merely for missing dates; no merge, replacement or duplicate creation is authorized by this account. Compare original office, household and kinship evidence before selecting between them.

The reported dates for the town clerk do not independently date his father. A birth before 1631 is only a conservative existence bound from the reported 1630 office, not a birthday or an age estimate.
