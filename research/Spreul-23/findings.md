## Source findings — Provost of Renfrew, 1630 and 1649, from the biography review, 28 September 2026

Metcalfe, *A History of the County of Renfrew* (1905), [p. 104](https://archive.org/details/historyofcountyo00metc/page/104), says that the son of the Cambuslang vicar's son and heir "was Provost of Renfrew, and attended the Parliament of 1630 as one of the Commissioners of the Royal Burghs"; Metcalfe does not give his forename. *The New Statistical Account of Scotland* (1845), vol. 7, p. 241, quotes a Renfrew entry of 27 September 1649: "Reported by John Sprewle, proveist of Renfrew, that he had apprehendit some women in Renfrew for eminent presumptions of Witchcraft." Both are printed secondary accounts and the burgh minute has not been seen. That the provost of 1649 is the commissioner of 1630 is an inference from the office and the surname.

Caution: the captured biography also says John was "the son of John Spreull, vicar of Cambuslang". Metcalfe makes the Provost the vicar's grandson, as this file does (father Spreull-17); that sentence should not be reused.
