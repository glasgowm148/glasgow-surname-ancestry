== Biography ==

John Spreull was born around 1510 (estimated based on estimated 1485 birth of uncle).

He was the son of Robert Spreull, a burgess of Glasgow. <ref name=metcalfe>William Musham Metcalfe, ''A history of the county of Renfrew from the earliest times'', 1905, page 104, https://archive.org/details/historyofcountyo00metc/page/104<blockquote>John Spreul, a younger son of the Cowdon family, was, in 1507, made vicar of Dundonald [...] In 1541 he was a canon of Glasgow [...] He died in the year 1555, leaving the whole of his property to John Spreull, his nephew, and son of his brother Robert, a burgess of Glasgow. At the Reformation, John is said to have become rector of Cambuslang. [...] John Spreull, the vicar of Cambuslang, was succeeded by his son and heir, whose son was Provost of Renfrew, and attended the Parliament of 1630 as one of the Commissioners of the Royal Burghs.</blockquote></ref>

He had a son. <ref name=metcalfe/>

During [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reformation the Reformation], John become Rector of Cambuslang, Glasgow. <ref name=metcalfe/>

In 1555 he inherited the property of his uncle, [[Sprewel-11|John (Sprewel) Spreule of Ladymuir (abt.1500-)]], Canon of Glasgow. <ref name=metcalfe/>
