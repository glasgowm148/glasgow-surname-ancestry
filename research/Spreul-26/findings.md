## Relationship assessment — reading Metcalfe's two Spreull statements, 28 September 2026

Metcalfe, *A History of the County of Renfrew* (1905), [p. 104](https://archive.org/details/historyofcountyo00metc/page/104), says that the Dundonald vicar died in 1555 leaving his property to his nephew John, son of his brother Robert, burgess of Glasgow, and that at the Reformation this John "is said to have become rector of Cambuslang". On the same page it says that "John Spreull, the vicar of Cambuslang, was succeeded by his son and heir, whose son was Provost of Renfrew". The captured biographies of Robert and of [John (Spreul-24)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Spreul-24) read the Cambuslang vicar as that nephew-heir, which makes Robert the link between the Cowden house and the later Provost ([Spreul-23](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Spreul-23)) and Town Clerk ([Spreul-22](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Spreul-22)). This is an editor's reading of one compiled page, resting on Metcalfe's "is said to"; no source names Robert's own father.
