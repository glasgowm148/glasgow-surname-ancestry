# Findings: [Jonet Spreull (Spreull-4)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Spreull-4)

## Live profile update — 30 August 2026

Saved and verified protocol 1196 (31 December 1534, printed p. 72) and protocol
847 (24 August 1565, printed p. 73). The biography states the exact limit of
each relationship clause, including that protocol 847 calls William John's son
but does not expressly call him Jonet's son. No relationships were detached.

The reciprocal link to [Brady Family of Sauchie, Stirling & Easter Kennet — Research Notes](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Space:Brady_Family_of_Sauchie%2C_Stirling_%26_Easter_Kennet_%E2%80%94_Research_Notes) is live and verified because Jonet appears there as Esbell Brade's directly documented daughter-in-law in the 1534 settlement.

## Current conclusion

Jonet Spreull was the wife of [John Glasgow (Glasgow-1093)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1093). The 1534 protocol names her as John's wife; a 1565 protocol reserves liferent to John and Jonet while naming William Glasgow as John's son. A cited 1575 abstract identifies Stephen Glasgow as the son of the late John and names Jonet as his mother, but the full citation still needs direct verification.
