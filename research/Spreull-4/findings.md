# Findings: [Jonet Spreull (Spreull-4)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Spreull-4)

## Live profile update — 30 August 2026

Saved and verified protocol 1196 (31 December 1534, printed p. 72) and protocol
847 (24 August 1565, printed p. 73). The biography states the exact limit of
each relationship clause, including that protocol 847 calls William John's son
but does not expressly call him Jonet's son. No relationships were detached.

The reciprocal link to [Brady Family of Sauchie, Stirling & Easter Kennet — Research Notes](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Space:Brady_Family_of_Sauchie%2C_Stirling_%26_Easter_Kennet_%E2%80%94_Research_Notes) is live and verified because Jonet appears there as Esbell Brade's directly documented daughter-in-law in the 1534 settlement.

## Current conclusion

WikiTree parent-status audit, 27 September 2026: the attached father Robert Spreul (Spreul-26) is now marked uncertain. The inspected 1534 and 1565 property protocols do not state Jonet’s parentage.

Jonet Spreull was the wife of [John Glasgow (Glasgow-1093)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1093). The 1534 protocol names her as John's wife; a 1565 protocol reserves liferent to John and Jonet while naming William Glasgow as John's son. Protocol 2189 of **17 June 1575** expressly identifies Jonet as mother of John's son [Stephen Glasgow (Glasgow-1029)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1029); its full citation and wording were verified against the digitized edition on 27 September 2026. Stephen later witnessed William's son's inheritance and served as his curator in 1586. The combined marriage, kinship, chronology and household evidence supports Jonet as William's **confident, inferred mother**, saved and read back on [William's profile](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3522) on 27 September 2026. Protocol 847 alone does not expressly call her William's mother, and the inference should be revisited if an earlier marriage is documented.
