== Biography == 
John Sprewel, also spelled Spreull or Sproul, was a younger son of the old Cowden family in Renfrewshire.<ref name=nisbet>Nisbet, Alexander. ''A System of Heraldry'', vol. i, p. 437.</ref> He is the first of the Sprewels of Ladymuir and Blackairne.

In 1507 John became '''Vicar of Dundonald'''. He served as a '''Professor of Philosophy''' at the University of Glasgow, then as '''Rector''' of the University. He held a prebend in the cathedral and, by virtue of that prebend, the '''Rectory of Ancrum'''.<ref name=electricscot>[https://electricscotland.com/history/nation/sprewel.htm Electric Scotland, "Sprewel Family History"]</ref>

Property records place him in several acquisitions.  
* In 1541 a charter styles him a canon of Glasgow and records his purchase from Lord Lyle of '''King’s Meadow''', '''King’s Orchard''', and '''Castlemilk''', all within the territory of the burgh of Renfrew.<ref>[https://archive.org/details/historyofcountyo00metc/page/104 William Metcalfe, ''A History of the County of Renfrew'' 1905, p. 104]</ref>  
* In 1543 he bought '''Ladymuir''' from '''Gabriel Semple of Ladymuir''', with '''Jonet Spreull''' his wife consenting. The deed names Jonet as the seller’s wife, so John the buyer remained unmarried.<ref>[https://archive.org/details/historyofcountyo00metc/page/104 Metcalfe, p. 104]</ref>  
* Other notices place him with '''Blackairne''' in the Lordship of Provan and with a lodging in the city of Glasgow.<ref name=electricscot />

John died by 1555. He left no direct heirs. His estates passed to his nephew '''[[Spreul-24|John (Spreul) Spreull (abt.1510-)]]''', son of his brother '''[[Spreul-26|Robert Spreul (abt.1500-1555)]]''', burgess of Glasgow.<ref name=electricscot />

== Legacy and Descendants ==
The nephew’s grandson, also named '''[[Spreul-23|John (Spreul) Spreull (abt.1590-)]]'', became '''Provost of Renfrew''' near the beginning of the reign of Charles I. He was father to '''[[Spreul-22|John Spreul (1616-)]]''', '''Town Clerk of Glasgow''' and later one of the '''Principal Clerks of Session'''.<ref name=electricscot />

The Town Clerk’s son, '''John Sprewel of Blackairne''', married Agnes Sprewel, daughter of Andrew Sprewel of Milton. Their son, '''Andrew Sprewel of Blackairne''', worked as a writer in Edinburgh.<ref name=electricscot />

== Clan Association ==
The Sprewel family, in its many spellings, has recognition as a '''Sept of Clan Lennox''' by the Chief and Council of Lennox.<ref name=clan>Proclamation Recognising Spreull Family as a Sept of Clan Lennox, 2020</ref> This bond begins with '''Walter Sprewel''', steward to the Lennox earls in the late thirteenth and early fourteenth centuries.

The original Sprewel lands in Dumbartonshire and Renfrewshire came as feudal grants from the Lennox lords. The association continued across the centuries and reached Ireland in the seventeenth century when Lennox interests expanded there.<ref name=petition>Petition to the Chief and Council of Clan Lennox by Spreull descendants worldwide</ref>
