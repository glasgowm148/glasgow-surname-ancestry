== Biography ==

Margaret was born about 1590.
== Research Notes ==

The currently attached father [[Stewart-65233|Alexander Stewart]] is marked uncertain. The cited baptism identifies Margaret as the mother of a Glasgow child, but does not name Margaret’s own father; the attachment remains a working hypothesis.
