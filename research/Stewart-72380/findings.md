# Elizabeth (Stewart) Glasgow — source audit, 21 September 2026

## Current conclusion

The Larne family monument directly establishes Elizabeth as William Glasgow-3324’s wife and records her death on **17 September 1832, aged 60**, supporting birth about **1772**. The former 1765 birth was an unsupported estimate. Stewart is retained as her existing maiden surname; the monument does not itself supply it or her parents.

A widow Elizabeth Glasgow of Larne, administratrix of Jane Stewart of Curran, is a strong identity candidate for this Elizabeth, but the original 1823 assignment does not name her husband or relationship to Jane. No new parent, sibling or child is attached from that deed.
