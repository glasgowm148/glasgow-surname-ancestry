## Record findings and identity limits — 17 October 2026

A possible identification concerns Peggy Stuart. A reproduced Second Portglenone Presbyterian marriage list records John Elder of Gortrechy marrying Peggy Stuart of Killycuggin on 7 April 1842. The witnesses were James Ray and James Stuart. (RootsChat, Elder, Murdoch & McMeekin Families; [record](https://www.rootschat.com/forum/index.php?topic=214109.0).) It establishes an earlier Elder household in the same local area and supplies names for future church and civil-record tracing, but it does not identify John as an ancestor of Robert Elder; Robert's 1871 marriage names his father as Nathaniel Elder. The source is a secondary transcription and the original register remains to be inspected. The identification remains uncertain.
