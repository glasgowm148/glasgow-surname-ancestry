## Parentage and duplicate assessment

The christening and the parents' earlier Birmingham marriage jointly support
Richard Swift and Jane White as Jane's parents. A WikiTree search for Jane
Swift, born 1777 in Birmingham, returned only Swift-7403; no duplicate was
found. This is an already-linked parent set, not a newly created sixth set in
the current ten-set extension register.

## FamilySearch conflation warning

The FamilySearch profiles currently linked to Jane's parents are conflated and
must not be used to extend this branch. On 25 July 2026, FamilySearch profile
`LHK5-9TN` for the Birmingham Richard Swift was merged with `PCRD-2L2`, Richard
Swanton Swift of Dublin. The resulting profile combines Birmingham parish
children with the Irish landed-family children.

The two couples are demonstrably different:

- The Birmingham marriage record names **Richard Swift and Jane White** on 5
  August 1771.
- The Irish Richard's 28 February 1771 marriage settlement names his wife as
  his first cousin **Jane Swift**, daughter of Alexander Swift of Lynn, County
  Westmeath.
- The Irish Richard's 1796 will names children Benjamin, John, Elizabeth,
  Susanna, Christiana, Frances and Jane. It does not establish the Birmingham
  children Mary, Thomas, Richard or William.
- The 1749 Lamberhurst baptism attached to `LHK5-9TN` names parents Richard
  Swift and Sarah but supplies no Swanton middle name and no bridge from Kent
  to the Birmingham married man.

FamilySearch `LHK5-9Y6` is likewise unsafe for ancestry. It identifies the
Birmingham wife as Jane White while retaining parents Alexander Swift and
Elizabeth Pratt from an earlier Irish Jane Swift identity. WikiTree
`Swift-6604` is Jane Sophia Swift of the Irish family, wife first of Goodwin
Swift and later of Louis Auguste Alexander le Pelletier; she is not the
Birmingham Jane Swift who married Thomas Chinery.

**HOLD:** no parent should be attached to either `Swift-7404` or `White-98526`
from these FamilySearch relationships without an independent record-to-person
identity bridge.

On 2 September 2026, the deleted FamilySearch profile `PCRD-VFJ` was restored
