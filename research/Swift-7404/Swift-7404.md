== Biography ==

'''Richard Swift''' married [[White-98526|Jane White]] at Birmingham,
Warwickshire, on 5 August 1771.<ref name="Marriage">[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QGDL-ZHZC England, Warwickshire parish registers, marriage of Richard Swift and Jane White, 5 August 1771, Birmingham], FamilySearch.</ref>
Their daughter [[Swift-7403|Jane Swift]] was baptised at St Martin,
Birmingham, on 27 December 1777, to Richard and Jane Swift.<ref
name="Baptism">[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:NB42-SMZ England, Warwickshire parish registers, baptism of Jane Swift, 27 December 1777, St Martin, Birmingham], FamilySearch.</ref>

No inspected record identifies Richard's parents, birth, or death. The present
Suffolk birth and 1796 death details derive from a conflated online tree and
should not be treated as established facts.<ref name="Legacy">[https://www.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/sources/LHK5-9TN FamilySearch source list for LHK5-9TN], accessed 10 September 2026; a research lead only, not evidence for this Birmingham man.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The 1749 Lamberhurst baptism of Richard Swift, son of Richard and Sarah, is a
same-name candidate only; no record connects that child to the Birmingham
groom.<ref>[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:J741-F83 England parish registers, baptism of Richard Swift, 1749, Lamberhurst, Kent], FamilySearch.</ref>
His parents remain unidentified without an independent Birmingham identity bridge. The
Irish Richard Swanton Swift material formerly merged into the online tree is a
different family and supplies no evidence for this profile.<ref name="Legacy" />
