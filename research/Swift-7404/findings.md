# Findings: [Richard Swift (Swift-7404)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Swift-7404)

## Current conclusion

Richard Swift married Jane White at Birmingham on 5 August 1771 and was the
father of Jane Swift, baptized there on 27 December 1777. His parents remain
unidentified.

## False FamilySearch ancestry

FamilySearch `LHK5-9TN` is a conflation. Its change history records a 25 July
2026 merge with deleted profile `PCRD-2L2`, Richard Swanton Swift of Dublin.
The Irish man had a 1771 marriage settlement with his cousin Jane Swift and a
1796 Irish will naming children Benjamin, John, Elizabeth, Susanna, Christiana,
Frances and Jane. Those records do not identify the Birmingham husband of Jane
White.

FamilySearch currently displays two incompatible parent sets for the merged
profile: Richard Swift and Sarah Holman from the Lamberhurst baptism, and John
L'Estrange Swift and Christine Swanton from the Irish family. Neither set is
proved for `Swift-7404`.

The deleted Irish profile `PCRD-2L2` was restored on 2 September 2026. Its
former wife profile `PCRD-VFJ` was also restored and corrected to Jane Swift.
The Birmingham spouse `White-98526` should remain separate from that Irish
couple.

## Candidate Birmingham chain

FamilySearch search on 2 September 2026 found a closer but still unproved
lead: Richard Swift was christened 27 August 1737 at St Philip's, Birmingham,
with father Richard (`JSJH-W9F`). A Richard Swift married Mary Harding at St
Phillips, Birmingham, on 2 December 1728 (`N236-B5Q`; tree profile
`MK76-G4S`). The dates and place make this a plausible father-and-son chain,
but no record identifies the 1737 child as the Richard who married Jane White
in 1771. The baptism supplies no mother, and the 1771 marriage supplies no
age, residence or parentage.

A second nearby candidate, Richd. Swift christened 15 September 1751 at Great
Alne to Jno. Swift and Sarah (`JQTN-YQ7`; tree profile `MJYW-4RN`), has no
spouse or children linking him to the Birmingham couple. Neither candidate
is sufficient for a WikiTree parent attachment.
