# Findings: Martha W. (Thompson) Glasgow (Thompson-63146)

## Current conclusion

Source review confirms that Martha W. Thompson was Moses Glasgow-589’s second wife, married in 1848, and the stepmother of Robert Finley Glasgow-2356. She is not the named birth mother of Robert. The reviewed family-history page names Mary J. Pierce as Moses’s first wife in 1839 and identifies their children as Lois Ladd, Robert Finley, and infant John Cameron; it records Mary’s death on 14 March 1846, followed by Moses’s marriage to Martha in 1848. The same page records Martha’s death on 1 December 1862, matching the existing Thompson-63146 profile.

The live WikiTree profile still names Martha as Robert’s mother, with the certainty flag unmarked. Local research identifies Mary Pierce-14470 as his biological mother at93%; the live correction remains pending. Martha’s stepmother role is strongly supported by the reviewed chronology, but no percentage is assigned to that qualitative household role. This review does not establish Martha’s birth surname beyond the source’s “Miss Martha W. Thompson” wording; the older FindAGrave/Pierce suggestion remains explicitly uncertain and is retained as a research note.
