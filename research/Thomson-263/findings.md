## Edinburgh Peirie household — 15 November 2026

The public WikiTree API for [William Thomson](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Thomson-263) and [Grissell Peirie](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Peirie-1), retrieved 15 November 2026, supplies their Edinburgh marriage on 27 November 1663 and five attached children: [William](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Thomson-264), christened 18 December 1664; [Thomas](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Thomson-265), 23 May 1669; [Thomas](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Thomson-266), 28 August 1670; [John](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Thomson-267), 14 July 1672; and [Grissell](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Thomson-268), 4 January 1674. These are inherited tree christening assertions, not newly inspected baptism entries. The two Thomas profiles remain separate.

Henry Paton, ed., *A Register of Marriages for the Parish of Edinburgh, 1595–1700* (1905), [p. 541, PDF p. 551](https://electricscotland.com/history/records/scottishrecordso16scotuoft.pdf#page=551), records Grizel Peirie and William Thomson on 27 November 1663, describing William as servant to Sir William Thomson, town clerk. The printed page was visually verified on 15 November 2026; the original parish manuscript remains uninspected. The groom is not thereby the town clerk himself or his relative. Grizel and Grissell are the source and imported-profile forms respectively.

Peirie-1 preserves an Ancestral File number, 1XNQ-PP1, and a source description identifying the June 1998 publication, with data as of 5 January 1998. The family profiles were imported through Savage.ged on 21 February 2011. This explains the inherited tree provenance; it does not prove the estimated birth dates or supply parents for either spouse.

The known Edinburgh family is compatible with a later marriage at Carstairs in 1681. No dated competing household, source-proved prior death or other concrete conflict has been established. An absent continuation record does not exclude the Carstairs groom, but the different earlier spouse alone does not prove him either. The exact cross-platform comparison now uses the 1664 child’s christening, described below.


## FamilySearch household correspondence — 15 November 2026

The existing FamilySearch [William Thomson account LR4G-4QS](https://www.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/details/LR4G-4QS) is matched to this intended Edinburgh household with **medium confidence**. The freshly inspected [William Thomson christening index, X1Y2-R5W](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1Y2-R5W), gives 18 December 1664 at Edinburgh, names father William and mother Grissell Peirie, and is attached to that father account. Wife Grissell’s account is [9796-FLG](https://www.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/details/9796-FLG); the five named children agree with the imported family. This is an index reading, not an inspection of the original baptism manuscript.

FamilySearch displays the couple’s marriage as **27 November 1662**, whereas the visually verified printed Paton entry gives **27 November 1663**. Neither date is silently changed to force agreement; inspect the original parish entry to resolve the year. The FamilySearch account’s displayed 1753 death, title “Father”, ancestry and additional children are not endorsed by this correspondence. It supplies no new birth date, parentage assessment or proof that William was the 1681 Carstairs groom.
