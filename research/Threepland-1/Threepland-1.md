== Biography ==

'''Jean Thriepland''' is probably the child born 24 April and baptised 28 April
1693 at Lasswade, Midlothian, daughter of Adam Threipland and Agnes Dykes.<ref
name="Baptism">[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYSM-Z89 Scotland parish registers, baptism of Jean Thriepland, 28 April 1693, Lasswade], FamilySearch.</ref>

Jean married [[Clerksone-2|James Clerksone]] on 3 June 1715 at Dalkeith, Midlothian.<ref name="Marriage">[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYM3-66R Scotland parish registers, marriage of Jean Thriepland and James Clerksone, 1715], FamilySearch.</ref>
Their son William Clerksone was baptised in 1716 to James and Jean.<ref>[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4F-NR8 Scotland parish registers, baptism of William Clerksone, 1716], FamilySearch.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

This profile formerly conflated Jean with a different Jean baptised at South
Leith in 1658. That woman would have been about 57 at the 1715 marriage and
about 58 at William's baptism; the 1658 event is not evidence for this Jean.
The 1693 baptism, the rare surname, chronology and Lasswade–Dalkeith setting
make the identification strong, but no adult record expressly repeats the
parents' names. The identification of her parents therefore remains uncertain.<ref
name="Baptism" />

The MyHeritage collection entries and the Bogle and Harper family trees below
are derivative sources. Their South Leith identification conflicts with the
Lasswade candidate and does not establish Jean's parents.
