# Findings: [Jean Threepland (Threepland-1)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Threepland-1)

## Current conclusion

`Threepland-1` conflates two women. It should represent Jean Thriepland,
born/baptised in 1693 at Lasswade, daughter of **Adam Threipland** and **Agnes
Dykes**, not the Jean baptised in 1658 at South Leith.

## Source findings

- [Jean baptism XYSM-Z89](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYSM-Z89): Jean Thriepland, born 24 and baptised 28 April 1693 at Lasswade; parents Adam Threipland and Agnes Dykes.
- [Marriage XYM3-66R](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYM3-66R): Jean and James Clerksone, 1715.
- [Son William's baptism VQ4F-NR8](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4F-NR8): William Clerksone, 1716, son of James and Jean.
- FamilySearch PIDs: [Jean L7BW-P89](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/L7BW-P89), [Adam LHSW-YQ8](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/LHSW-YQ8), and [Agnes L7BW-PZG](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/L7BW-PZG).
- The separate 1658 South Leith Jean is [LZLX-9LS](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/LZLX-9LS). She would have been about 57 at marriage and 58 at William's baptism.

## Duplicate and parentage assessment

The 1693 identity is **moderate-high confidence**, supported by chronology,
rare surname, and the Lasswade-Dalkeith locality, but no adult record explicitly
names her parents. Mark the links uncertain. No Adam Threipland profile was
found. [Agnes Dykes (Dykes-225)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Dykes-225) is a
possible empty-vitals candidate, not a proved match; resolve it before creating
Agnes.

## Recommended correction

Remove the 1658 narrative from this identity only while simultaneously setting
the sourced 1693 Lasswade details. Do not leave locations blank: Lasswade is
direct for Jean and is usable as uncertain immediate-family context for newly
created parents.
