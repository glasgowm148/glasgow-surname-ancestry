== Biography ==

'''Nancy''' is attached as the uncertain wife of [[Glasgow-2769|Robert Glasgow]]. Her maiden name and vital dates have not been established.

The 1840 household of Robert Glasgow in Lauderdale County, Alabama, included a female aged 50–59. She may have been Robert's wife and would have been born between 1780 and 1790, but the census states neither her name nor her relationship.<ref name="Census1840">1840 United States census, Lauderdale County, Alabama, p. 48, Robert ''Glasgow'' household; NARA microfilm publication M704, roll 7; [https://archive.org/details/populationsc18400007unit/page/n245/mode/1up original schedule, scan 245].</ref>

Nancy was not in Robert's 1850 Giles County household.<ref name="Census1850">1850 United States census, Giles County, Tennessee, district 11, p. 446, dwelling and family 558, Robert ''Glaseoe'' household; NARA microfilm publication M432, roll 879; [https://archive.org/details/populationschedu0879unix/page/n278/mode/1up original schedule, scan 278].</ref> This establishes neither when nor where she died. The current about-1850 Giles County death is retained only as an uncertain working estimate.

== Research Notes ==

The Delaware marriage of Robert Glasgow and Nancy Black on 19 January 1814 belongs to another couple.<ref name="DelawareMarriage">[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QPQN-BH57 Delaware Vital Records, 1650–1974], Robert Glasgow and Nancy Black, 19 January 1814; FamilySearch.</ref> A Robert Glasgow also appears in Delaware militia records, and FamilySearch's sourced reconstruction gives that couple a Delaware-born son about 1825.<ref name="DelawareMilitia">Delaware Public Archives, [https://archivesfiles.delaware.gov/ebooks/Military_Records_Vol_5.pdf ''Military Records'', vol. 5], return naming Robert Glasgow in the 7th Company, 5th Regiment, Delaware Militia.</ref><ref name="DelawareFamily">FamilySearch, [https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/L7KB-QB9/robert-glasgow-1825-1911 Robert Glasgow (about 1825–1911)], parents shown as Robert Glasgow and Nancy Black; a derivative family reconstruction with linked record sources.</ref> Meanwhile [[Glasgow-2769|Robert Glasgow]] served in Tennessee in 1814–1815, was established in Giles County by 1818 and was enumerated there in 1820.<ref name="Muster1814">University of Tennessee Libraries, [https://scout.lib.utk.edu/repositories/2/resources/4000 ''Muster Roll and Pay Roll of Captain William McKay's Company of Tennessee Militia'', MS-3419], entry for Robert Glasco.</ref><ref name="Deed1818">Giles County, Tennessee, deed dated 28 December 1818, Josiah Stovall to Robert Glasco; [https://tngenweb.org/giles/deed-josiah-stoval-to-robert-glasco-glasgow/ transcription], TNGenWeb.</ref><ref name="Census1820">1820 United States census, Giles County, Tennessee, Robert Glasgow household; NARA microfilm publication M33, roll 124; [https://archive.org/details/populationsc18200124unit/page/n21/mode/1up original schedule, scan 21].</ref> Nancy Black was therefore not this woman, and the last name at birth remains Unknown.

No inspected record directly names Nancy as Robert's wife or as the mother of his attached children. The relationship remains an uncertain working attachment.
