# Findings: [Jonet Kinnie? (Unknown-717333)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Unknown-717333)

## Current conclusion

Reuse Unknown-717333 for **Jonet**, the widow of
[John Glasgow of Quothquan (Glasgow-1086)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1086),
rather than creating a duplicate spouse. NRS CC14/5/12 directly calls her his
relict and executrix dative surrogate. Her surname is provisionally read
`Kinnie`; it is not secure enough to normalise as Kinnear, Kinney or another
modern spelling.

The record names no child or parent for Jonet. The former profile name Isobel
Unknown is not supported by this testament and should be replaced only with
the uncertainty of the surname made explicit.
