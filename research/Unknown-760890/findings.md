# Findings: Rose, wife of Adam Glasgow (Unknown-760890)

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

Adam's gravestone proves Rose as his wife and records her death on 31 July 1833,
aged 66, implying birth about 1766/67. It does not state her birth surname or name
every child.

Rose cannot credibly be retained as mother of older Alexander `Glasgow-3904` or
Robert `Glasgow-1367` under their current birth estimates: she would have been
about eleven or twelve. Detach those maternity edges. She is the proved mother
of gravestone-named James and Eliza only where the stone's wording supports the
joint parentage; Nancy's record names Adam but not Rose.
