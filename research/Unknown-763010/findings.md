# Findings: Ann, wife of Hugh Glasgow (Unknown-763010)

Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Current conclusion

The Larne Parish Churchyard memorial explicitly names **Ann Glasgow** as wife of
Hugh Glasgow-1503. She died **8 June 1786 aged 75**, placing her calculated birth
between 9 June 1710 and 8 June 1711. Her maiden surname, parents, marriage and
birthplace remain unknown.

Jane should not be attached as Hugh's second wife from this monument. The stone
uses Jane for a deceased child and for the wife of Hugh and Ann's son James.
