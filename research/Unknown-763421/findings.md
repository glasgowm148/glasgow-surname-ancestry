# Findings: [Jane Glasgow (Unknown-763421), wife of James Glasgow-3991](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Unknown-763421)

Last updated: 2026-10-29

## Current conclusion

Jane, wife of [James Glasgow (Glasgow-3991)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3991), died on **21 July 1820 aged 79**, as recorded on the Larne monument. She was therefore born between **22 July 1740 and 21 July 1741**. The profile's 1750 birth year has no stated basis and is about ten years too late. Her maiden name and parents are unknown. The stone calls Ann Glasgow (Glasgow-3992) her daughter. The full account is in [James Glasgow-3991's findings](../Glasgow-3991/findings.md).

## Source findings

- Larne monument, as published in 1900 ([*Ulster Journal of Archaeology*, 2nd series, vol. 6, p. 47](https://archive.org/details/ulsterjournalofa06ulstiala/page/47/mode/1up)), re-read on the saved page image: "his wife Jane Glasgow died 21st July 1820 aged 79 years".
