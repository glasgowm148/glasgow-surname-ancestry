== Biography ==

Cathrin, also rendered Catherine, was born about 1790, estimated from her recorded age of 58 at death. Her maiden surname is unknown; the surviving record gives only her married surname, Hamilton.

Cathrin died in spring 1848 in County Antrim, Ireland. The mortality portion of the surviving 1851 census recorded her in the household of [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]] as James's mother-in-law. The same household names James's wife as [[Hamilton-25516|Peggy Hamilton]] and records [[Hamilton-44031|Robert Hamilton]] as his father-in-law. The living household also records unmarried [[Hamilton-44032|Nancy Hamilton]] and [[Hamilton-44033|Jane Hamilton]] as James's sisters-in-law. Together, the two portions directly identify Peggy, Nancy and Jane as daughters of Robert and Cathrin.<ref name="Census1851">1851 Census of Ireland, County Antrim, Dunaghy parish, Ballycraigargh, household 29, James Glasgow household: [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QSQ-G94X-YD22 living household, image 251] and [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L94X-Y621 mortality return, image 252].</ref>
