== Biography ==

'''Mary Glasgow''' was born about 1813 in Ireland. On 3 June 1847 she entered Ballymena Workhouse from Glenleslie, aged 34. The register describes her as married and deserted and a member of the Established Church. Consecutive entries record Boyd, William James, Mary Jane and Margaret Glasgow of the same townland.<ref>[https://www.billmacafee.com/otherrecords/ballymenaworkhouse.pdf William Macafee, "Admissions to Ballymena Workhouse: 13th May to 8th July 1847"], registrations 4406–4410, Mary and the Glasgow children of Glenleslie (accessed 13 August 2026).</ref> Mary was discharged alive on 31 July 1847.

== Research Notes ==

The names and ages of the four children closely match children whom the 1851 Ballycregagh return calls children of [[Glasgow-2981|James Glasgow]]. James had remarried [[Hamilton-25516|Peggy Hamilton]] in 1848. Mary is therefore the strongest identified candidate for James's first wife and the older children's mother, but the workhouse entry does not name her husband and no marriage record has been found.
