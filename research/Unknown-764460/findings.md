# Mary (Unknown) Glasgow (Unknown-764460)

## Conclusion

Mary was a distinct married and deserted woman admitted to Ballymena Workhouse from Glenleslie on 3 June 1847. Four immediately consecutive Glasgow admissions closely match children of James Glasgow in 1851, but the available transcript does not state relationships. Mary remains an unconnected candidate wife and mother pending the original register.
