## Current conclusion

baptised at Bickenhill, Warwickshire, in 1751. The baptism identifies
Elizabeth's parents as John and Sarah Burr but does not state Sarah's maiden
surname. Her last name at birth must therefore remain **Unknown**; the

## Relationship assessment

The 1751 baptism directly identifies Sarah as Elizabeth's mother and John Burr
as Elizabeth's father. Bickenhill is documented for their daughter's baptism;
using it for Sarah's own vital-event location is an immediate-family inference
and must remain marked uncertain.

No reviewed source identifies Sarah's parents or maiden surname.
