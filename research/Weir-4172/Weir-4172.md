== Biography ==

'''Thomas Weir''' lived in Stevenston parish, Ayrshire, and was the husband of
[[Glasgow-1495|Katherine Glasgow]]. Katherine's testament states that she died
in January 1603 and was confirmed on 9 May 1605.<ref name="KatherineWill">National
Records of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary Court, testament of Katherine Glasgow,
CC9/7/4, images 6–7; working transcription supplied 6 September 2026.</ref>

Thomas acted in the names of William, James and Jonet Weir in the executor
appointment.<ref name="KatherineWill" />

== Research Notes ==

The record is not a marriage entry: 9 May 1605 is the testament confirmation
date, and Stevenston is the household parish. The securely read passage does
not explicitly call William, James and Jonet their children.

An older FamilySearch link formerly used on the profile should be retained as
a derivative lead but does not override the original testament.<ref>FamilySearch,
[https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XY6J-CQS indexed record].</ref>

Thomas's birth, parents and exact death are not established. His former
Aberdeen identity should not be restored.
