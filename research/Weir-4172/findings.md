## Current conclusion

Weir-4172 has been repurposed as Thomas Weir of Stevenston, the husband directly
named by Katherine Glasgow's testament calendar. Its former Aberdeen identity
has been removed. The live profile incorrectly treats 9 May 1605—the testament
confirmation date—as a marriage date and uses Stevenson, Lanarkshire; the
record supports spouse and Stevenston parish, Ayrshire, but no marriage date.
The original further states that Katherine died in January 1603. Its
confirmation date was first read as `[26?] May 1605`, but the fresh reading of
10 September 2026 gives “the nynt day of may 1605”, 9 May 1605, agreeing with
Findmypast and Grant (revised on 7 October 2026; see below).

William, James and probably Jonet Weir were three minor executors, with Thomas acting
in their names. Their surname, minority and his representation make them very
probably the children of Thomas and Katherine, although no explicit
parent-child wording survives. Complete local and fully paged live WikiTree
duplicate audits on 8 September 2026 found no compatible profile for any of
the three. They can be created as distinct people with uncertain parent links;
Jonet's forename and each inferred Stevenston birthplace also remain uncertain.

## Recommended correction — confirmation date of Katherine Glasgow's testament, 10 September 2026

A fresh full reading on 10 September 2026 of the original testament, NRS Glasgow Commissary Court CC9/7/4, image 7 ([Scotland's People wills search](https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/search-records/legal-records/wills)), reads the confirmation line as `the nynt day of may 1605`. The written ordinal gives **9 May 1605**, which agrees with Grant's printed *Commissariot Record of Glasgow* (p. 196) and Findmypast SCOT/WILLS/057453. The earlier `[26?]` (or `[xxvj?]`) reading of the 9 September selective review is superseded, and the conflict recorded above is resolved. Seen as original image (LLM-assisted visual review of the two supplied NRS images); the reread treats the date as secure.

9 May 1605 is the confirmation date only, not a marriage or death date; Katherine died in January 1603. William, James and probably Jonet Weir remain probable, not proved, children: the testament names them as minor executors without a child term.
