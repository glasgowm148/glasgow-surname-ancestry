# Findings: [Jane White (White-98526)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/White-98526)

## Current conclusion

Jane White married Richard Swift at Birmingham on 5 August 1771 and was the
mother of Jane Swift, baptized there on 27 December 1777. Her parents remain
unidentified.

## False FamilySearch ancestry

Swift. Its profile calls her Jane White but attaches Alexander Swift and
Elizabeth Pratt as parents and includes Irish landed-family children alongside
the Birmingham children. The Irish Richard Swift's 1771 marriage settlement
explicitly identifies his wife as his cousin Jane Swift, daughter of Alexander
Swift, not as Jane White.

WikiTree `Swift-6604` is Jane Sophia Swift of that Irish family. She married
Goodwin Swift in Dublin in 1803 and Louis Auguste Alexander le Pelletier in
Paris in 1818. She is not `White-98526` and not `Swift-7403`.
