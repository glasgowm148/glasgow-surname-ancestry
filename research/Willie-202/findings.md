# Findings: [Jannet Willie (Willie-202)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Willie-202)

## Current conclusion

Jannet Willie is named with George Glasgow as Marie Glasgow's parent in the
Kilwinning parish entry indexed 29 July 1688. [[Willie-296]] cited the same
FamilySearch entry, had the same estimated 1660 Kilwinning birth, and provided
no distinct relatives or event. **The two profiles represented the same
record-defined mother.** Willie-296 was merged into the older Willie-202 on 26
September 2026; live API redirect and survivor readback were verified.

The survivor's biography was corrected before the merge. The earlier statement
that Jannet was George Glasgow's daughter reversed the relationship in the
source: she was named as Marie's mother alongside George. The baptism does not
by itself establish that the parents married. Jannet's own birth, parents and
death remain unknown; circa 1660 and Kilwinning are estimates. The merged
biography retains the OPR locator and FamilySearch record once.
