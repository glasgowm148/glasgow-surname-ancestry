The survivor's biography was corrected before the merge. The earlier statement
that Jannet was George Glasgow's daughter reversed the relationship in the
source: she was named as Marie's mother alongside George. The baptism does not
by itself establish that the parents married. Jannet's own birth, parents and
death remain unknown; circa 1660 and Kilwinning are estimates. The merged
biography retains the OPR locator and FamilySearch record once.
