== Biography ==

'''James Wilson''' is named with Isobel and Bessie Wilson in the executor
passage of the 1603 testament of [[Glasgow-3179|Geillis Glasgow]], spouse of
[[Wilson-127012|Thomas Wilson]] at Chaplehill in Ardrossan parish.<ref>[https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/search-records/legal-records/wills National
Records of Scotland, wills and testaments], Glasgow Commissary Court, testament
dative and inventory of Geillis Glasgow, CC9/7/3, images 113–114; working
transcription reinspection supplied 6 September 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The words between James and Isobel remain unresolved. The earlier reading
which called James the lawful son of Geillis and Thomas is not secure. Retain
the attached parents as uncertain pending a clearer reading; do not present
the relationship as proved.
