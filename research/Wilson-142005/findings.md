(Glasgow-3179)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3179)'s 1603 testament,
with Isobel and Bessie Wilson. The wording between James and Isobel remains
unresolved, so the earlier reading of James as Geillis and Thomas Wilson's

pending a secure re-reading; do not detach them without an evidenced
