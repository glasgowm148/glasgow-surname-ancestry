# Findings: [Rachel Glasgow (Wilson-78088), born 1680, birthplace unknown](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Wilson-78088)

Last updated: 2026-08-09

Record every useful result and negative search here as it is found.
Do not treat an unsourced tree or naming pattern as proof.

## Current conclusion

Registry memorial 74015 identifies Rachel, formerly Wilson, as wife of Robert
Glasgow of Kilwaughter in 1738. The 1817 equity case identifies her as a sister
of Archibald Wilson, says she died intestate in 1742 leaving James and Ann, and
is corroborated by grandson Robert Glasgow-1983's sworn answer.

The pleadings name Archibald's six sisters as **Jane, Margaret, Rachel, Anne,
Elizabeth and Susanna**. They further say that Mary, wife of Rachel's son James,
was a daughter of another sister. On that account James and Mary were first
cousins. Mary's mother is not identified, and neither the litigation nor James's
will supplies the surname Crozier/Crosier.

The statement that Rachel left two children materially conflicts with attaching
later people to her as children. It does not identify Robert Glasgow-1143's father.

The litigation conflicts on Archibald's death year, about 1792 versus 1795, and
on whether Ann Glasgow Shannon predeceased or survived him. Preserve both
versions until the original equity file and Irish estate papers are inspected.
