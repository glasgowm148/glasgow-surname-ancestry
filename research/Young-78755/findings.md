# Findings: Mary Young (Young-78755)

Last updated: 2026-08-25

## Current conclusion

Mary Young was born about 1836 in County Antrim. The 1851 census records her as
a fifteen-year-old granddaughter in Robert Glasgow's Glenleslie household.
Robert's will directly names Mary and James Young as the daughter and son of his
daughter Margaret Glasgow `Glasgow-3955`.

Mary's mother is therefore certain. Her father and later life remain unknown.

## Unresolved identity

The Mary Young who married James Rush in 1859 remains a later-life candidate,
not an established match. Her marriage names father William Young, but no
record yet connects that family to Glenleslie or Margaret Glasgow.
