# West Calder: a possible Kirknewton childhood for John

Reviewed: 2026-10-16

Glasgow-3526 is selected as the uncertain working father of Glasgow-2847, husband of Margaret Anderson and father of the West Calder children recorded from 1745 to 1755. The route provisionally identifies that adult with Glasgow-3525, indexed at neighbouring Kirknewton and East Calder on 31 March 1717 with father James. The parish setting, exact forename and surname, and feasible generation favour this concrete local childhood over an unidentified origin: if baptised near birth in 1717, John would have been about 28 in 1745 and 38 in 1755. The later family also contains a James, although a common repeated forename does not independently establish a grandfather. No register entry yet joins the child to Margaret Anderson’s husband. This is a low-confidence identity-based parent model, not a proved father clause for the adult.
