## Kerr identity conflict

FamilySearch's famous paths display this sequence:

`Andrew Kerr, 1621` -> `George Kerr, 1589` -> `Mark Kerr, 1st Earl of Lothian`.

The indexed baptism [VQHH-NY3](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQHH-NY3)
records an Andro Ker christened 22 April 1621 at Kelso, son of Georg Ker and
Katherin Gardiner. FamilySearch also has a separate baptism [X1YW-GPJ](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1YW-GPJ)
for Andrew Ker christened 30 September 1624 in Edinburgh, son of Robert Ker
and Marione Lyel.

WikiTree's [Andrew Ker (Ker-10)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Ker-10) is the
1624 Edinburgh man, supported by the Edinburgh OPR reference FR684. He is
currently attached as the father of [George Ker (Ker-14)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Ker-14),
whose 6 January 1656 baptism names Andrew Ker and Marione Blythman; the same
couple's 1646 Edinburgh marriage and their child sequence are compatible with
the 1624 Andrew. This is a strong locality and family-context identification,
but the marriage entry has not been inspected in an original image here.

The 1621 Kelso baptism is a distinct record bearer. FamilySearch appears to
have attached the 1646 marriage and later children to that bearer, producing
the famous Mark Kerr path. No evidence currently bridges the Kelso Andrew to
the Edinburgh marriage or to George Ker's 1656 baptism. **Do not merge Ker-10
with the FamilySearch 1621 person, and do not import the Mark Kerr ancestry
into the managed Kerr branch.**

## Stevenson-branch father candidate

Janet Penman's baptism [XYV4-556](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYV4-556)
names Alexander Penman and Helen McKenzies at Auchterderran in 1772. The
existing [Alexander Penman (Penman-1898)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Penman-1898)
was baptised at Auchterderran on 12 April 1747, and his profile documents
children with Helen Beveridge in 1781, 1784 and 1787. The earlier marriage
record [XTGB-CL3](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XTGB-CL3) names
Alexr Penman and Hellan McKinniss at Auchterderran on 10 November 1769.

The two wives and the same-parish child sequence make a first-marriage/second-
marriage explanation plausible: Penman-1898 may be Janet's father before his
later marriage to Helen Beveridge. The identity is not directly stated in
Janet's baptism, so any link should be marked **uncertain** and documented as
an evidence-based identification, not treated as proved.
