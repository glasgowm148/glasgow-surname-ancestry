# Findmypast sweep: Cookstown and Moneymore (7 October 2026)

Studies covered: research-irish-moneymore (Glasgow-1026, 1492, 2425, 2426), research-irish-derryloran (Glasgow-2988, 2915, 1970) and research-irish-cross-derry (Glasgow-2917, 1732, 1654), plus the Cookstown mill family (Glasgow-2656, 2058, 2059) and the Killycurragh, Ballynasollus and Lissan families.

Records searched: the place_* result files (Moneymore, Cookstown, Derryloran, Desertlyn, Lissan, Kildress, Artrea, Killycurragh, Ballynasollus, Gorteen, Coagh, Stewartstown, Coltrim, Tulnacross, Drapersfield, Magherafelt), charles_glasgow_ireland_1680-1780, david_glasgow_indiana, and glasgow_tyrone/londonderry_1700-1860. News sources were the pre-captured glasgow_* BNA files plus eight new personal-name searches (A_*). In total I fetched 49 transcripts and 11 BNA page OCRs.

## New

1. **Robert Glasgow of Tullyveagh married Margaret George (Glasgow-2656).** Banner of Ulster, 9 December 1854, p.2 (BL/0001375/18541209/039). The notice reads: Margaret, relict of John George, formerly of Coagh, died on 8 December 1854 "at Tullyreagh, near Cookstown, the residence of her son-in-law, Mr. Robert Glasgow". This links the 1829 groom to the Tullyveagh Robert who died in 1856. It does not name his children.
2. **Glasgow & Wood partnership, 1850–51 (mill family).** Newry Telegraph, 20 July 1850 (BL/0000998/18500720/004), and the copies in the Whig, Banner and Standard, carry the dissolution and auction notice for "Drapersfield Flaxspinning Mill" and the Tullyveagh mills and farm. Drapersfield was held under the Drapers' Company from 1847, and Tullyveagh in perpetuity under the Lord Primate. Perry's Bankrupt Gazette, 8 February 1851 (BL/0000207/18510208/026), records the dissolution: "Glasgow and Wood, of Drapersfield, Cloghog, and at Tullyveagh … yarn spinners", dissolved 1 November [1850], "Debts by Glasgow". The partners' forenames are not given.
3. **1825 executors' notice.** "ROBERT GLASGOW, WILLIAM GLASGOW, Executors of R. GLASGOW, deceased. COOKSTOWN, 13th July, 1825." The notice offers Cookstown houses and land held under William Stewart of Killymoon (Belfast News-Letter, 22 July 1825, BL/0000038/18250722/088; Belfast Commercial Chronicle, 3 August 1825, BL/0001287/18250803/033). The deceased is a strong candidate for the "late Robert Glasgow of Cookstown", father of the surgeon Benjamin. The notice does not say how the executors were related to him.
4. **Landed Estates Court rental, Jan–Feb 1851** (DGS 4506849, volume 005, document 023). Robert and William Glasgow at Cookstown (tenancies dated 1839 and 1851) and Matthew S. Glasgow at Loy (1824, 1851) and Tullagh. Ids: IRE/LEC/4506849/00763/010, /00763/011, /00761/010–012, /00756/012–013, /00749/007. Only the index was read.
5. **Outrage Report, 5 July 1838.** William Glasgow, "Orator" (Oritor), Kildress (IRE/HO100/020002; HO 100/255, image 393). Index only; William's role is unknown. A candidate for Glasgow-2915 or the Oritor William.
6. **Will index, 1836.** William Glasgow, Armagh Diocese (IRE/ENEC001/26356; NAI IWR/1836/F/177). No residence is given.
7. **Valuation Office field books.** 1834: Mathew Glasgow at Loy; Robert and Wm Glasgow at Cookstown; Saml Glasgow at Killycurragh (IRE/CENSUS/VALUEBOOKS/183669, 183798, 183796, 183583). 1833: John Glasgow at Drummeen (Drapers) and Robert at Coltrim, Lissan (1848873, 1848860; the Coltrim household is already known from the 1831 census).
8. **Pre-civil marriage near Moneymore.** Esther Glasgow married John McCanny at Tamlaght by Moneymore on 12 March 1835 (R_795107790/2). Not linked to any profile.
9. **Samuel Glasgow, Esq., P.L.G.** He seconded the first resolution at the Moneymore Tenant League meeting of 14 March 1851 (Freeman's Journal, 27 March 1851, BL/0000056/18510327/014; also in the Tablet, 22 March 1851). Separately, Samuel Glasgow, merchant of Cookstown, married Fanny Stewart Cathcart at Moneymore on 20 February 1855 (FN-M-9C495E4E and others; Fanny's profile Cathcart-1169 already exists). According to BNA snippets not checked against OCR (Coleraine Chronicle, 12 February 1859; Belfast Morning News, 6 April 1859), their infant son Cathcart died in February 1859 and Samuel's wife died suddenly that April. Separately again, Samuel Glasgow of Tyrone died on 29 July 1857, with executors Andrew Thomas Ferguson and John Creighton (IRE/WILL-CALL/1639731942/1).
10. **Drapersfield deaths.** William Glasgow, jun., aged 28, died 17 November 1866 (BL/0007614/18661124/121). Ellen Glasgow died 17 May 1869 "at her brother's residence, Drapersfield House" (BL/0001318/18690522/053). Neither is assigned to a profile.

## Minor or negative

- 1740 Derryloran: Findmypast's index has three Glasgow households (James 1, James 2, William: IRE/PRONI/1740PH/G/00357, 00358, 00361). Macafee has four. Check the second William against the original return.
- 1766 and 1775 index entries confirm already-known occurrences: Drumearn James x2 and John; Gortin Sam; Killycurragh William; Whitehill Sam; Corvanaghan James; Edernagh (Artrea) John; 1775 James, Cookstown Congregation.
- Moneymore: no Glasgow record before 1835. Nothing for William Walter, Robert-2425 or William-2426.
- Charles Glasgow, Ireland 1680–1780: only Charles Glass records were found.
- David Glasgow, Indiana: no man born about 1803 was found.
- BNA "william glasgow" with oritor or orritor: no hits before 1871.
- Additional detail on the Killycurragh families: Silas, son of Samuel, married Isabella Loughlin in 1855; William, son of Samuel, died at Gortin in 1854 aged 8; Allen McFadden Glasgow married Esther Henry in 1857; James married Rachel Bell in 1857. These mostly concern profiles already known.
- Robert Glasgow married Anne Sloan at Ballyclog on 15 September 1857; his father was William (R_794825615). John Glascow, RIC no. 11332, a Protestant from Tyrone aged 19, was appointed on 10 January 1848 (IRE/RIC/HO184/0425153). Neither was checked against profiles.
