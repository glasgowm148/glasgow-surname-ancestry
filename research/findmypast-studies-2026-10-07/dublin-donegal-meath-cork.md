## New

### Dublin watchmakers

1. **Thomas Glasco names his father, 1773.** *Hibernian Journal* 11 Jan 1773 p.2 (`BL/0000817/17730111/005`), repeated 8, 10, 22 Feb, 3, 5, 22 Mar, 7, 9 Apr 1773; *Saunders's News-Letter* 12 Mar 1773 (`BL/0001057/17730312/016`). OCR of 5 Mar (`BL/0000817/17730305/010`): "THOMAS GLASCO, who served Apprenticeship to his Father, Philip Glasco, late [of] Christ-Church-lane, Watch-maker, and since had several Years Practice in London ... carries on the usual [business] at his said Father's House". On chronology the father is very probably the elder Philip (died 1772), not Philip junior (died 1770, born about 1740). This undercuts the attached chain Glasco-173 → Glasco-144 → Glasco-143 if any of those men is the watchmaker. Not in the corpus before.
2. **Miss Hynes = Eliza(beth) Hynes; two children.** St Michan's RC: Mary bapt. 5 Dec 1781, parents Thomas Glasco and Elizabeth (`IRE/PRS/BAP/3211466`); John bapt. 17 Sep 1784, parents Thomas Glasco and Eliza Hynes (`IRE/PRS/BAP/3212705`). Very probably the Ormond Quay watchmaker's family; no trade or address in the entries.
3. **1773 Duffy marriage is from the Catholic register.** `IRE/PRS/MAR/0623479/1` (St Audoen's RC, NLI microfilm 06778-06). Existing notes call it Church of Ireland.
4. **Court bill-book entries (index only, roles unknown):** Philip and Ellinr Glasco, Exchequer, 9 Mar 1743 (`IRE/COURT/EXCHEQUER/0001369387`, `/0001369388`); Philip, Chancery, 7 Dec 1749 (`IRE/COURT/CHANCERY/0000398412`); Philip, Exchequer, 20 Dec 1760 (`IRE/COURT/EXCHEQUER/0002199803`); Philip Glasco Jun and Mary Glasco, Chancery, 11 Feb 1765 (`IRE/COURT/CHANCERY/0000834604`, `/0000834605`); Philip and Thomas Glasco on the same page, Chancery, 3 Nov 1777 (`IRE/COURT/CHANCERY/0001223970`, `/0001223973`). None of these was in the corpus.
5. **Dublin diocesan wills and grants index:** Catherine Glasco 1760 (`IRE/IET0090/0424/212`), Philip 1772 ×2 (`/215`, `/216`), Margaret 1777 (`/214`), Philip 1777 ×2 (`/217`, `/218`), James 1793 (`/213`). Catherine 1760 and Margaret 1777 are candidates for the wives of the elder Philip / Glasco-173 and Glasco-144 respectively; none is assigned.
