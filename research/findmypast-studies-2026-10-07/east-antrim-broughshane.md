# Findmypast sweep: East Antrim (Larne to Kilwaughter) and Broughshane, Racavan and Clough — 7 October 2026

Studies: `research-irish-east-antrim` and `research-irish-broughshane` (agent C).
Profiles in scope: Glasgow-1078, 1081, 3913, 1143, 1144, 1499, 1504, 1505, 1022 and 1657, with Archibald (Glasgow-2304) and the Larne ropemaker (Glasgow-1509).

Findmypast's indexed record sets have almost nothing for eighteenth-century east Antrim. The place searches for Kilwaughter, Ballykeel, Raloo, Carncastle and Glenarm returned no Glasgow-surname records, and Larne returned only four entries after 1870. The useful material came from the British Newspaper Archive. Its *Belfast News-Letter* runs back to the 1760s, and searches restricted to dates before 1830 removed most of the noise from the city of Glasgow and Larne–Glasgow shipping.

## Minor or negative

- **Racavan stone (Glasgow-1657).** Findmypast's *Antrim Cemetery Records* index for Racavan Burying Ground, reference R17-10, lists John (1836, aged 70; IRE/BMD/D/ANT/202500373), Jane (1858, aged 85; …372) and William (**1829**, aged 22; …374). The stone was already known from the thebraid.com transcription, which gives William's death as **1823**. The index confirms John's and Jane's entries; the stone or a photograph must settle William's year. Jane Armstrong would be indexed under Armstrong, which was not searched.
- 1865 Racavan marriage of John Glasgow and Rachel Grey (R_794917151): already known from the original certificate.
- The 1761 rope-walk advertisement also ran on 13 and 20 January 1761 (BL/0006140/17610113/017, …17610120/022).
- Hugh Glasgow will entries of 1825–1833 (IRE/DIOC/WILL/00011507, 00334108; IRE/INREV/WILLS/0139010/1, 0148379/1; IRE/ENEC001/26354) belong to Hugh of Drumgreenagh, County Armagh. They are not the Larne Hugh.
- The 1798 "Brig William, William Glasgow, Master" (BL/0000038/17981019/070) was a Belfast–Bristol trader with no stated Larne connection, so it is not evidence for Glasgow-1505.
- A garbled 1860 *Belfast Morning News* marriage notice (BL/0000428/18600718/079) records a marriage at Melbourne of the "relict of the late Wm. James Glasgow (brother of Dr. … Street, London)", with Larne mentioned. The OCR is too damaged to assign it, so it is left unrecorded in the findings files.
- Searches that found nothing for the open questions: the founding household, Robert's father, Archibald's sons, Robert of Tennessee, the 1772 petitioner John and any named emigrant passenger. The BNA searches with nothing relevant were "hugh glasgow" Larne, "archibald glasgow", "james/william/robert/john glasgow" Larne (all periods, and 1738–1830), "glasgow" Kilwaughter (1738–1830), "glasgow" Larne 1765–1775, "glasgow" "larne carolina", "john glasgow" Broughshane, "glasgow" Broughshane 1800–1860, "glasgow" Ballyclug, and "john glasgow" 1836. No Findmypast US records were captured for this study.
- No new evidence was found for Glasgow-1078, 1081, 3913, 1504 or 1022.
