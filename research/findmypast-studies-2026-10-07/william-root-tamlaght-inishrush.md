# Findmypast study: the 1656 William root, the Tamlaght O'Crilly Hughs, Inishrush collateral leads and Desertoghill

Agent B, 7 October 2026. Profiles studied: Glasgow-591, Glasgow-548, Glasgow-3931, Glasgow-540, Glasgow-822, Glasgow-3902, Glasgow-1623, Glasgow-3927, Glasgow-1367, and the Desertoghill flax grower of 1796, who has no profile. Findings were also added to Glasgow-3124 (Leitrim) and Glasgow-1022 (Glenleslie) because records from this study bear on them.

Sources used: captured place and name searches (`results/place_*`, `hugh_glasgow_ireland_1730-1850`, `adam_glasgow_ireland_1740-1880`, `glasgow_tyrone_1700-1860`, `glasgow_londonderry_1700-1860`, `glasgow_leitrim`, `glasgow_pennsylvania_1730-1800`, `glasgow_antrim_1700-1840`, `results_951/glasgow_place_*`), 13 new transcripts (ids_B_1–3), 15 new BNA snippet searches (`news/B_*`) and the pre-captured `news/glasgow_<place>` files. Every finding was checked against the research corpus with `known.py`.

## Minor or negative

- **The 1740 Hughs** (`IRE/PRONI/1740PH/G/00359`, `/00360`): Findmypast's index adds nothing. It gives no townland or order, and cites "T808/1528 and T716/9", which looks like a typing error for T808/15258.
- **Hugh probate entries:** the 1825/1833 Irish Hugh Glasgow probate entries are all for Hugh of Drumgreenagh, Co. Armagh, who died on 22 Feb 1825 (`IRE/DIOC/WILL/00011507`, `/00334108`, `IRE/INREV/WILLS/0139010/1`, `/0148379/1`, `IRE/ENEC001/26354`). They are not the Tamlaght O'Crilly family. These transcripts had already been fetched by another agent, and their new details (will of 18 Jan 1825, executrix Anne) belong to Glasgow-4012, which I did not edit.
- **No other Inishrush occupier:** no John or other Glasgow appears in the Valuation Office Books for Inishrush or Tamlaght O'Crilly. Only Adam does.
- **Desertoghill and Garvagh:** there are no Glasgow-surname Findmypast records for either before 1900. BNA searches for "John Glasgow"/"John Glasco" with Desertoghill, Garvagh or Innisrush returned 0 results. The 1796 flax grower remains unidentified.
- **The Irish places tied to William:** Cavankilgreen, Augher, Gortigillagh and Ballybogey have no Findmypast Glasgow records. The 1740 Derryloran William (`IRE/PRONI/1740PH/G/00361`) and the 1775 Cookstown dissenter James (`IRE/PRONI/1775DP/G/00118`) are already in the Derryloran research.
- **Pennsylvania 1730–1800:** Chester County tax entries (from 1751), a 1743 indictment, and the 1790 census households. None gives an Irish origin or a father, so none can be tied to the Robert c.1710 or James William 1731 lines.
- **Esther Glasgow's 1835 marriage** (`R_795107790/2`) is at Tamlaght by Moneymore, not Tamlaght O'Crilly. It is not this family.
- **Other BNA searches** returned nothing about these families. Searches for Hugh/Adam/James Glasgow with Innisrush, Hugh with Tamlaght or Portglenone, Glasgow with Drimbolg or Innishrush, and Glasgow with "covenanter" found nothing. Before 1870, "Adam Glasgow" hits are the India missionary Rev. Adam Glasgow. The 1772 Belfast News-Letter subscriber notice was not recovered from the BNA OCR.
- **Glasgow-1623:** nothing new. The 1851 marriage and the 1859 Griffith entries are already known. The Castledawson directory James Glasgow (1843–64) is not linked.
