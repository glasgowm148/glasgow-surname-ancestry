# Concrete profile evidence recovered from the Free-Space archive

Last checked against live WikiTree profiles: 29 August 2026.

This audit treats the archived Free-Space pages as leads, then checks the cited
record itself and the current profile. Old theories and facts already present on
the live profile are not counted as missing evidence.

## Rejected apparent omissions

* The FamilySearch identifiers around Glasgow-2710 are companion index records
  for the same baptism, not evidence of an additional person or event.
* The 1728 Killybegs parliamentary account belongs with Glasgow-3001 and is
  already cited there; its proximity to Glasgow-3556 in a Free-Space page is not
  evidence about Glasgow-3556.
* The speculative parentage claims in older Free-Space prose for Glasgow-1022,
  Glasgow-1495 and the Inishrush branches are contradicted or explicitly left
  unresolved by the current record-led research. They should not be imported.
