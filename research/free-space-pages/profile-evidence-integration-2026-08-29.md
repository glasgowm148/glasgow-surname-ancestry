# Profile evidence integration — 29 August 2026

## Scope

The deep free-space audit extracted 122 ranked passages referring to 78 WikiTree profiles. Every referenced profile now has a `research/<WikiTree-ID>/findings.md` case file. This is a triage result, not a claim that all 122 passages are suitable for a live biography.

## Decision rule

1. Transfer a fact only when the passage identifies an underlying reliable source and the fact is missing from the biography.
2. Preserve a useful passage already represented by equal or stronger evidence as corroboration, not duplicate prose.
3. Put unsourced trees, blogs, surname-frequency arguments, naming patterns and unproved migration narratives on **HOLD**.
4. Keep fact, inference and hypothesis visibly separate.
5. Never clear or detach an attached person or structured location unless an evidenced replacement is applied in the same edit.

## Publication queue

The case file, original record and existing live biography must be compared before each public update. “Found on a free-space page” is not itself a source citation. The digital workbench is a discovery tool and must not be cited as historical evidence.
