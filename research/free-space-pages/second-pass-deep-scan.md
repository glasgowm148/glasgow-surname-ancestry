## Material free-space corrections

### Parentage of John Glasgow-1022

Saved page 029 still presented Robert-1143 as the strongest/probable father,
James-1144 as John's half-brother, and Y-DNA as confirmation. The cited 1747
discipline entry names no woman or child; the 1772 petition does not identify
its John signer as John-1022; project Y-DNA cannot name the common ancestor.

The saved master has been replaced with an evidence-led analysis. It preserves
the useful census, church-transcription, archived-timeline and Y-DNA links while
withdrawing the unsupported relationships.

### Identity of the 1851 nephew

Saved page 030 incorrectly moved the census household to Killycurragh, County
Tyrone. The household was at **Glenleslie, Dunaghy parish, County Antrim**. Its
favoured Killycurragh candidate, Glasgow-2056, named Henry Glasgow as father and
has no evidenced connection to John-1022.

The corrected master now distinguishes:

* Glasgow-3474 — strongest compatible later-life candidate, not proved;
* Glasgow-2056 — wrong name-and-age identification and wrong county framework;
* Glasgow-867 — in a separate Lisnagaver household in 1851;
* Glasgow-2407 — bachelor in 1865, incompatible with the nephew's 1839 marriage;
* Glasgow-3140 — not the resident nephew if its attachment represents the son
  whom household 23 recorded in America.

No parent or kinship link is recommended.
