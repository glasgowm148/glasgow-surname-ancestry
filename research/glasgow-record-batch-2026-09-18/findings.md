# Findings: Glasgow record-profile batch, 18 September 2026

## Current conclusion

Fifty-seven record-defined Glasgow-surname profiles (Glasgow-4098 and
Glasgow-4099–4155) were created from reviewed Findmypast and archival drafts,
together with their record-named parents and spouses. A follow-up quality pass
rewrote the biographies in natural prose, gave every estimated birth date an
explicit status and rationale, marked inferred locations uncertain, and linked
the named relatives and source transcripts.

The records themselves are baptisms, tithe and rate-book entries, apprenticeship
and testament records, and probate or emigration indexes. They define distinct
documentary people; most cannot be joined to the wider Glasgow pedigree on the
evidence found so far.

## Source findings

- The child profiles rest on individual Findmypast transcripts (Scotland,
  Parish Births & Baptisms 1564–1929; Scotland, Occupations & Professions;
  Westminster Rate Books; Scottish Covenanters; Emigrant Ministers to America)
  and on PRONI, NRS and Irish Genealogy records. Each source URL is retained in
  the profile's `== Sources ==`.
- Baptism-derived births are now recorded with the baptism date and status
  `before`; births recorded in the register use status `certain`; all other
  estimates use `about`/uncertain. Parish birthplaces inferred from baptisms or
  residences are marked uncertain.
- The parent estimates follow the convention "eldest recorded baptism year − 19"
  and the biography states that rationale.

## Resolution of holds — same day

- **Glasgow-4122 (Sarah Glasgow, 1726):** the erroneous merge had made the 1721
  son John Glasgow M.A (Glasgow-1139) her father and Agnes Ferguson both his
  mother and his wife. Sarah was re-parented to [[Glasgow-1077|John Glasgow MA]]
  (the Provost, born 1690, husband of Agnes Ferguson), the wrong spouse link was
  removed from Glasgow-1139, and both biographies were rewritten. Sarah now reads
  "Child of John Glasgow MA and Agnes (Ferguson) Glasgow", born before 3 July
  1726, Irvine uncertain. The 4163→1139 merge was therefore wrong; 4163 was the
  adult John, who is represented by Glasgow-1077.
- **Glasgow-4147 (Robert Glasgow, *John of Portrush*):** save conflated the
  "still living" death status with an empty death date. Corrected to born about
  1640 [uncertain], died after 1666; biography already clean. Verified live.
- **Glasgow-4168:** biography save retried successfully after a failed attempt;
  all 17 created parent/mother profiles now carry record-based biographies.
- **Saltcoats family:** John Glasgow-4152 is now attached (per the 1637
  testament) as the father of Agnes Glasgow-4151 and Katherine Glasgow-4153;
  the two daughters are shown as sisters.
- **Cleora Glasgow-4148 family:** attached to her documented father Hiram Wilson
  Glasgow-3995; her mother Mary Ann Brandon Kennedy (new Kennedy-35691) and her
  husband Leland McNary Speers (new Speers-583) were created from the 8 February
  2025 biographical account and the 1867 marriage notice, each with a sourced
  biography. Her brother Leonidas K. Glasgow still has no profile.
- **Mary Glasgow-4155 family:** William Speir, named alongside Mary as Samuel's father and therefore an inferred partner, was created
  (Speir-488) and attached from the 4 December 1828 First Kilrea marriage entry
  for their son Samuel.
