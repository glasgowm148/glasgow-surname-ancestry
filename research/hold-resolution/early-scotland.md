# Early-Scotland HOLD resolution

Reviewed 8 September 2026. These decisions apply an evidence threshold for
identity: a shared name, compatible date, or broad/exact parish overlap is a
search lead, not a profile match without a kin, property, occupation,
residence, associate, or succession bridge. “Excluded” below means excluded as
the **present catalogue match**; it does not claim that later positive evidence
could never justify a merge.

## Sources checked

### 1577 Hirmenschelis child

- National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, `CC8/8/5`, images
  276–277, testament dative of Johne Glasgw: Katherine and `[Androw?]` are his
  lawful children and John is repeated as “thair father”; Margaret
  `[Cochrane?]` is his widow, not explicitly their mother.
- Scottish Record Society, *Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments,
  1514–1600*, [p. 109](https://archive.org/details/commissariotreco16scot/page/n117/mode/2up?q=Glasgow),
  John at Hirmenschelis, 4 May 1577.
- Findmypast, *Scotland, Will and Testament Index 1481–1807*,
  [SCOT/WILLS/057444](https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FWILLS%2F057444&tab=this).
- The maintained early-profile corpus and exact web search for Hirmenschelis;
  no independent place or cross-testament identity bridge was found.

### 1598 Corsoun household

- National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, `CC8/8/32`, images
  123–124, Andrew Glasgow's testament: made 6 February 1597/8, death 7 February
  1597/8, confirmed 10 July 1598; Robert explicitly his son by Elizat
  Stevinsoun; daughters Marion, Jonet, and Isobel named; unborn child only
  conditional; Elizat to retain the land until Robert came of age.
- Scottish Record Society, *Commissariot of Edinburgh*,
  [p. 108](https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso01scotuoft/page/n117/mode/1up),
  and Findmypast
  [SCOT/WILLS/057425](https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FWILLS%2F057425&tab=this).
- Robert comparison: Duddingston OPR burial, parish 684, ref. 60/47, 17 January
  1647; *Register of Apprentices of Edinburgh*,
  [p. 71](https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso28scotuoft/page/71/mode/2up),
  for the separate 1641 Robert-at-Wester-Duddingston father lead.
- Marion comparison: NRS `CC9/7/9`, Ninian Glasgow's 1613 testament, naming
  Marion as his sister; Glasgow High Kirk baptism of James Fultoun,
  [7 August 1610](https://www.gwsfhs.org.uk/records/james-fultoun/).
- Jonet comparisons: Glasgow protocol abstracts for Jonet and William Pady,
  [1582/3](https://archive.org/details/abstractsofproto08glas/page/78/mode/1up)
  and [1598](https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc04renwgoog/page/74/mode/1up);
  FamilySearch [XY9X-4PR](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XY9X-4PR),
  Jonet Glasgow and Martin Ramsy, Mid Calder, 1624.
- Isobel comparison: Edinburgh OPR marriage, parish 685/1, ref. 430/120, frame
  721, 10 June 1619; baptism, parish 685/1, ref. 20/284, frame 527, 2 May 1620.
- Maintained profile findings, local One Tree/early-profile cache, and the
  completed September 2026 WikiTree candidate search.

### 1637 Saltcoats household

- National Records of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary Court, `CC9/7/27`, register
  pp. 478–480: John Glasgow, sailor in Saltcoats, died December 1636; Isobell
  Gray his widow; Katherine and Agnes his lawful children; Isobell's maternity
  not explicit; old fishing boat reading tentative.
- Francis J. Grant, ed., *The Commissariot Record of Glasgow*,
  [p. 196](https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/page/196/mode/2up?q=Glasgow%2C),
