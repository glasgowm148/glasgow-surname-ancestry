# Record dossier: Hugh Glasgow 1733/1751 index conflation

## Current conclusion

**Resolved as a non-person source-correction destination.** Findmypast record
`GBOR/DOBSON/013379` is not evidence of a Hugh Glasgow event on 3 February
1733. It conflates Hugh's wording with the date of the next Glasgow entry.

The valid evidence concerns a Hugh Glasgow, sometime sailor in Irvine and
later in London, whose testament was registered at Edinburgh on 12 March 1751.
That evidence is preserved here without selecting among several compatible
WikiTree profiles or creating a duplicate.

## Source findings

Francis J. Grant's *Commissariot Record of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments,
Part III, 1701–1800*, printed p. 106, places these entries consecutively:

- Hugh Glasgow, sometime sailor in Irvine, thereafter in London — 12 March
  1751;
- James Glasgow, Edinburgh weaver and burgess, and his son James, surgeon on
  H.M. sloop *Surf* — 3 February 1733.

The scanned volume is available as [searchable full text](https://archive.org/stream/scottishrecordso03scotuoft/scottishrecordso03scotuoft_djvu.txt).

Findmypast independently preserves the correct Hugh event twice:

- [Clyde Mariners, `GBOR/DOBSON/003411`](https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=GBOR%2FDOBSON%2F003411&tab=this):
  `sailor in Irvine, then in London, cnf 1751 Edinburgh`;
- [Will and Testament Index, `SCOT/WILLS/057434`](https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FWILLS%2F057434&tab=this):
  12 March 1751, London, Commissariot Edinburgh.

All were checked 8 September 2026. The malformed
[`GBOR/DOBSON/013379`](https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=GBOR%2FDOBSON%2F013379&tab=this)
instead reads `enf 3.2.1733`, demonstrating the row-level corruption.

## Recommended action

Use this file as the destination for catalogue record
`record-fmp-glasgow-a0b809464cc9`; do not describe it as a 1733 person. Treat
the two valid 1751 records as one source cluster held here until an executor,
relative, vessel, address or equivalent continuous identifier permits an
existing-profile assignment. No profile creation or amendment follows from
