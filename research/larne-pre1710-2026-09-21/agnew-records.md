# Agnew / Kilwaughter rental check

21 September 2026. Scope: whether Lauren Bell, *The Great Scottish Migration to Ulster in the 1690s* (Ulster University PhD, 2022), supplies a named Glasgow or a digitised pre-1710 Agnew rental entry beyond the maintained Larne evidence.

## Result

No new named Glasgow record was found. The public University record describes Bell's thesis as a study of 1690s migration and leases. Search-indexed text identifies the Agnews of Lochnaw as one of two case studies, but the retrieved passages discuss migration, estate economy and historiography rather than a Kilwaughter tenant list. Searches of the indexed thesis for Glasgow, Kilwaughter and GD154 returned no usable named tenant, quotation, page or transcription. It must not be cited as proof of any Glasgow residence, migration or relationship.

The downloadable thesis endpoints were tested at the supplied CORE URL and Ulster's linked repository URL; both returned Cloudflare 403/challenge responses, so no complete PDF, appendix or footnote reading was available in this pass. The open catalogue item is still the only accessible direct surface checked: [NRS GD154/532](https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/details.aspx?reference=GD154%2F532&st=1&tc=y&tl=n&tn=n&tp=n). Its item description is catalogue metadata, not a digitised rental text or named Glasgow occurrence.

## Existing precise targets retained

The relevant unexamined originals remain GD154/514–515 (c. 1653), /525 (c. 1690), /530 (November 1692), and /532 (1693–1708). They are on-site catalogue items. Request or inspect the original tenant/rent pages, recording folio/page, exact spelling, holding and any relationship language before changing the early Larne reconstruction.

Sources: [Bell thesis landing record](https://pure.ulster.ac.uk/en/studentTheses/the-great-scottish-migration-to-ulster-in-the-1690s/); [CORE indexed record](https://core.ac.uk/download/533891989.pdf); NRS catalogue link above.
