The collection description says it contains images of Betham research notes and abstracts from wills, grants, administrations, and related records; it specifically says the records may include deceased persons’ residences, probate dates, and heirs’ names and relationships. I could not inspect a James Glasgow hit or image in this session. The page is therefore a useful substitute-search route, not a record extract.

The catalogue view redirected to sign-in; no James Glasgow page or text was read.

## Source versus inference

The NLI reference and PRONI shelfmark are catalogue provenance. The FamilySearch collection descriptions establish that probate abstracts may contain relationship information, but they do not establish that this James appears there. Any bridge from the 1686 Belfast testator to Robert or later Larne people remains an inference until the extract or a complete transcription is inspected.
