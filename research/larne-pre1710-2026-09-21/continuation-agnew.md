Checked 21 September 2026. Purpose: bounded retrieval attempt for Lauren Bell's thesis and published Kilwaughter/Agnew material relevant to Robert Glaskow/Glasgow (3970), James (1081), John (3913), and Robert (1502).

## New evidence actually readable

- [Ulster University thesis landing record](https://pure.ulster.ac.uk/en/studentTheses/the-great-scottish-migration-to-ulster-in-the-1690s/) confirms Lauren Bell, *The great Scottish migration to Ulster in the 1690s*, doctoral thesis, awarded March 2022. Its abstract says the thesis studies 1690s Scottish migration, migrant demographics and the impact on Ulster leases. The page exposes a 1.21 MB PDF link, but the direct file URL (`https://pure.ulster.ac.uk/files/224457675/The_great_Scottish_migration_to_Ulster_in_the_1690s.pdf`) returned HTTP 403 Forbidden.
- [CORE indexed record](https://core.ac.uk/download/533891989.pdf) was readable only through search-indexed excerpts. Those excerpts concern general migration historiography (for example, discussion of the 1690s influx and cited works on Scottish/Ulster migration). Searches for `Kilwaughter`, `Glasgow`, `Glasgo`, `Glassgow`, `Glaskow`, `Agnew`, and `rental` yielded no usable named tenant, quotation, footnote, appendix, or page reference. The alternate `files.core.ac.uk` PDF surface was also only an indexed result; no complete document was available for page-level reading.
- [Landed Families, “Agnew of Kilwaughter”](https://landedfamilies.blogspot.com/2013/07/56-agnew-of-kilwaughter.html) is a published secondary account that can be read in full. It states that the Lochnaw Agnews held the Larne/Kilwaughter lease until 1708, when they sold their interest to resident rent-collector Patrick Agnew; its family notes identify Patrick Agnew (d. 1686) and Patrick Agnew (d. 1724) as successive sub-tenants/rent collectors. It names no Glasgow/Glasgo/Glassgow/Glaskow tenant and supplies no Glasgow kinship evidence. Its archive list points to PRONI D282/2-160 (estate papers c.1647-c.1800), but this is a research lead, not a read record.
- [Andrew Agnew, *The hereditary sheriffs of Galloway*, vol. 2 (1893), pp. 58-60](https://fairfieldgenealogysociety.org/Members_Only/World_Research/Scotland/Documents/Hereditary_sheriffs_of_Galloway%2C_Volume%202%20%281893%29.pdf) contains a printed Kilwaughter/Larne estate narrative. The readable passage gives a 22 May 1645 lease to William M'Cachie and a 23 June 1656 rent order to John Turnbull, but no Glasgow-family name or relationship. It is contextual published evidence only.
