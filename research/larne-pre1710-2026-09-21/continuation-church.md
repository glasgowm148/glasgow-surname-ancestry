# Larne/Kilwaughter church evidence — continuation

**Research date:** 21 September 2026  
**Scope:** printed/local church histories and public descriptions relevant to Larne/Kilwaughter, 1650–1730. James’s 1698/1703/1705 synod entries and the misread 1706 John Gordon lead were excluded as directed.

## Exact new data

- [Eric V. Stewart, *A Short History of First Larne Presbyterian Church* (PDF)](https://www.firstlarne.org.uk/sites/default/files/A%20Short%20History.pdf), pp. 2–3 (PDF pp. 1–2), gives a concise chronology: Rev Thomas Hall ministered at Larne **1646–1695**; the first Presbyterian meeting house was built about **1668**; William Ogilvie served the newly formed congregation **1700–1712**; the prolonged vacancy after Ogilvie’s death led to a division in **1715**, with the Bridge congregation’s meeting house erected in **1716**. This is useful institutional context for the 1699–1722 Glasgow elder/overseer entries, but the inspected early pages name no Glasgow/Glascho household or relationship.
- The same history states that it consulted available Synod, Presbytery and Congregational Minutes and relies on Classon Porter among earlier writers (PDF p. 1), but it is a modern narrative history, not an original minute book. Its text search found no `Glascho`; the only `Glasgow` hits are later references to Glasgow University and a **1829** sexton, Mary Glasgow (PDF p. 9), outside this target.
- The public [NLI catalogue record for Porter’s *Congregational memoirs*](https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000818390) confirms the work’s scope as the old Presbyterian congregation of Larne and Kilwaughter, with the original edition dated **1929** and a **1975** updated edition. The accessible catalogue metadata supplies no Glasgow names or family clauses; the Internet Archive copies linked from the project notes were not text-fetchable in this session.

## Original MIC/1B/6 availability

The accessible [Larne/Inver/Kilwaughter research guide](https://studylib.net/doc/7083819/general-information-series) identifies **MIC/1B/6** as the PRONI holding for Non-Subscribing Presbyterian Larne and Kilwaughter: baptisms **1720–1769** (plus later gaps), marriages **1721–1769** (plus later coverage), session minutes **1720–1748**, discipline cases **1721–1749**, and poor accounts **1720–1757**. It is a useful exact scope check, but it is a catalogue/guide entry, not a digitised surrogate. I found no accessible public scan or transcription of the original MIC/1B/6 volume. Its later minutes could still name older members or kin of people born before 1710, so the date range alone does not exclude a useful parentage clue; no such entry was inspected here.

## Negative result and limits

No new Glasgow/Glascho person, date, parent, child, spouse, sibling, origin, or kinship statement was recovered for 1650–1730. The church-history PDF confirms that the relevant congregation existed through the period and provides ministerial/institutional chronology, but it does not extend the Glasgow evidence. No relationship status should change for Glasgow-1081, Glasgow-3913, Glasgow-3970, or the later Larne cluster.
