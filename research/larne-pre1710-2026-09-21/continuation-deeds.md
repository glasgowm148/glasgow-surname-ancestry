# Larne joint leases: manuscript continuation, 21 September 2026

## Current conclusion

The original 1736 joint lease is now inspected and confirms its earlier catalogue date: **10 March 1736**, not the 12 March date formerly quoted in the profile. Its memorial number **63221** is correct. The named lives are royal lives, not Glasgow relatives. A newly located 1757 assignment independently refers to William and Hugh Glasgow of Larne as joint lessors, but gives no kinship or date for their underlying lease. Neither record proves the uncertain parentage of [Hugh Glasgow-1499](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1499) or [William Glasgow-1505](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1505), or proves that they were brothers.

## Source findings

### Memorial 63221: known lease, newly inspected original

[FamilySearch image 542, group 008088380](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSN1-FXR2), Registry of Deeds, group labelled volumes 87–88 (1736–1738). Original downloaded and visually inspected, including the number, date, lives clause and affidavit; the AI transcript is not the authority.

- Lease dated **10 March 1736**, Earl of Antrim to **William and Hugh Glasgow of Larne**, for two tenements already in their possession on the north side of Newtown's street.
- One frontage: 23 feet, bounded east by Mr Agnew's tenement and west by Robert Robinson's. Other frontage: 32 feet, east by John Boyd's tenement and west by John White's. Both extend 150 feet backwards.
- Annual rent £1 16s sterling, with receiver's fees of two pence per pound. This corrects Hugh-1499's former £1 15s and 65-foot frontage readings; the first frontage is 23 feet.
- Term from the preceding 1 November, for George II, Frederick Prince of Wales and Prince William Augustus Duke of Cumberland, with renewal provisions. These are not the lessees' sons. This does not determine whom later 1751/1767 endorsements inserted.
- Lease witnesses James Stenhouse of Larne and William Harrison, clerk to Alexander Stewart of Ballylough. Memorial witnesses Stenhouse and Robert Wilson of Larne, merchant.
- Hugh-1499's former Stephenson/Morrison/Ballygally readings are corrected by the inspected manuscript. Alexander Stewart executed the lease in the Earl's name; the text does not list him as a third witnessing party.
- Stenhouse's proof of execution at Carrickfergus is dated **28 March 1738**; registration **10 April 1738 at 11 a.m.** The next memorial is 63224, not another part of 63221.
- The deed names neither man's father, spouse, child or relationship to the other lessee. Identification with the existing Larne profiles is strongly supported by the established joint-lease provenance and locality, but the lease is not evidence for their assigned birth years.

Private original/crops: `sources/memorial63221-image542.jpg`, `memorial63221-lease.jpg`, `memorial63221-reading.jpg`; catalogue panel: `memorial63221-information.txt`.

### Memorial 159181: new 1757 assignment citing the joint lessors

[Opening, image 257](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSJM-MJX4) and [continuation, image 258](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSJM-MJP6), manuscript pp.473–474. Both original images downloaded and visually inspected.

- Deed poll dated **4 June 1757**, Matthew Stewart (abbreviated Mathw.; surname also rendered Steward) of Carncastle, County Antrim, farmer, to **Robert Rainey of Larne, weaver**.
- Conditional assignment for £20, concerning Stewart's interest for the remaining term in a head lease granted by **William Glasgow and Hugh Glasgow of Larne**. The memorial does not date that head lease or supply a sufficient property description to identify it as the 1736 Newtown holding.
- Deed witnesses **Alexander Weir/Wyer**, Larne merchant, and **James Baggs**; memorial witnesses Weir and **John Lyle**, Larne merchant. These are associates, not proved relatives.
- Registered **26 May 1761 at noon**. This is a registration date, not evidence that either Glasgow personally appeared or was living then.
- This occurrence was absent from the maintained case findings. It independently documents the joint-lessor association by 1757, but neither man executes this assignment and no Glasgow parent, child, spouse, age or relationship is stated.

Private originals: `sources/memorial159181-image257.jpg`, `memorial159181-image258.jpg`; working crops and DOM transcripts are alongside them.
