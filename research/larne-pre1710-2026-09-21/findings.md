## Search limits and next records

The Synod/Bolton original-page check confirms that the apparent 1706 Larne John Glasgow search hit is **John Gordon**. The known James Glasgow entries for 1698, 1703 and 1705 name no relatives. See `synod-evidence.md` for exact original-page citations. Public VRTI searches recovered no new extract of the 1686 Belfast James will; `T828/15` remains the specific original target. The Agnew thesis download was blocked and its indexed passages supplied no Glasgow evidence; that is not a negative search of the estate originals.

FamilySearch full-text searches were checked by their actual results and record pages, not catalogue date labels: one result labelled 1734–1736 was the already-known 1790 James-tanner memorial. `+"Glasgow" +"Larne" +"father"`, filtered to the 1700s, returned 17 images, including the known 1764 deed and the newly logged 1772 William occurrence. `+Glasg* +Kilwa*` returned 25 images in the 1700s and three in the 1600s; the accessible relevant deeds were the known 1738 and 1764 leases. Many newspaper, probate and church results require an affiliate library or FamilySearch centre; their contents were not inspected. These are bounded searches, not proof of absence.

The strongest remaining original targets are the Kilwaughter rentals **NRS GD154/514–515, /525, /530 and /532**, **PRONI T828/15**, congregation registers/minutes **MIC1B/6**, and the named lives/endorsements in **D2977/3A/4/81/72A** and **D2223/15/56**. Independent Kilroot estate or church material is needed to identify the 1733 decedent; the lost index bond is no longer an outstanding retrieval target.

Original downloads, page crops, browser transcriptions and API captures remain private research working files under `sources/`. Public synthesis belongs in the affected person and Larne family accounts; no separate public audit page is needed.
