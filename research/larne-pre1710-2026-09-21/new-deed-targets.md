# Early Larne Registry of Deeds index check

21 September 2026. Public Registry of Deeds Index Project exact-surname searches for **Glasgow**, **Glascho**, **Glasco** and **Glaskow**, with current-deed dates 1708–1750, were checked against the stated known memorials. The volunteer index is incomplete, so a zero result is not an absence finding.

## Existing provenance target: memorial 62131

**Memorial 62131** — lease dated **10 March 1736**, registered **10 August 1737**, vol. 84 p. 481 — is an already maintained Larne occurrence whose original-image provenance can be upgraded. Alexander MacDonnell, Earl of Antrim, leased John and James Moore two Newtown-to-mill-street tenements and gardens in Larne. The index calls **William Glasgow of Larne** the **west adjoining occupier** of the north-side tenement; it gives no kin term. This is a location/property-neighbourhood occurrence only, but it is independently useful beside the contemporaneous Hugh-and-William lease.

- Index abstract: [memorial 62131](https://www.irishdeedsindex.net/mem.php?memorial=62131)
- Direct FamilySearch film navigation target: [film 008093187, image 481](https://www.familysearch.org/search/film/008093187?cat=185720&i=480) (vol. 84 p. 481; inspect register image before quoting or assigning identity).

## Checked results and limit

The same query returned the already maintained memorial 74015 (Robert, James junior and Rachel Glasgow of Kilwaughter, 1738) and 76724 (Robert Glasgow, farmer, of Kilwaughter, witness in 1741; the original shows registration in 1743), plus unrelated Tyrone/Dublin namesakes. No indexed **Glascho** or **Glaskow** hit was returned for the period. The `Glasco` hits were Dublin watchmaker Philip and do not concern the Larne study.

No new kinship, parentage or migration evidence was found. Read memorial 62131's original image for full parties, boundaries and any recital; do not infer that its William is William-1505 or the William in memorial 63221 from proximity alone.
