## Update — 6 October 2026: Larne's elders year by year, and other Glasgows in the three volumes

All three volumes were searched as OCR text and the entries below checked on the page images unless marked otherwise.

### Larne's row

In the printed General Synod rolls the ruling elder on the Larne minister's row is: 1694 "from Larne, Rob: Neilson" ([p. 12](https://archive.org/details/recordsofgeneral01pres/page/12/mode/1up)); 1703 James Caldwell (beside Ogilvie, [p. 64](https://archive.org/details/recordsofgeneral01pres/page/64/mode/1up)); 1704 Sam: Smily (p. 75); 1706 John Gordon (p. 106); 1707 Robert Neilson (p. 124); 1708 Robt Ker (p. 141); 1709 none printed (p. 168); 1710 Robt Kerr (p. 188); 1711 William Murray (p. 218); 1712 Ja: Lyle (p. 248); 1715 Ja: Lyle (beside Hud, p. 346); 1716 Rob: Ker (p. 385); 1718 James Lyle (beside Clugston, p. 449); 1719 Rob: Ker (p. 482); 1720 John Tarbett (p. 513). There is no Larne row in 1697 and 1698 (the congregation was vacant; the 1698 row "Wm Leeck - Wm Adair" is probably Ballymena's, Leech being "of Belly-manogh" in 1712), in 1705 (Ogilvie is not on the roll) or in 1713, 1714 and 1717 (vacant). Getty's "Enver" rows, 1715-1720, print Ja: Whyte, Ja: Boyd, James Jackson, John McCullogh, Ja: Jackson and Samuel Smely. The 1691 and 1692 lists name no Larne elder, and the Synods of 1699 to 1702 print no names.

**No Glasgow of any forename is printed as Larne's elder in any of these years**, or anywhere in the Antrim presbytery lists from 1691 to 1720 (every year checked on the page images) or the Belfast presbytery lists (checked on the images except 1703, part of 1706, 1710, 1719 and 1720, which are OCR only). From 1721 the rolls print ministers and elders in separate columns, so no elder can be tied to Larne; no Glasgow appears in the Antrim lists of 1721-1732 (1721 on the image, later years OCR only). Each congregation sent one elder, so this shows only that no Glasgow represented Larne at Synod in the years printed. It does not show that no Glasgow sat on the Larne session: Porter's session list has James Glasgow an elder by 1721.

### The 1697 and 1698 rows

The Synod text does not name William Adair's congregation; Killen (*History of Congregations*, p. 17) makes him minister of Antrim from November 1690 until his death on 14 February 1699. The 1691, 1692 and 1694 rolls list elders by congregation; the 1697 and 1698 rolls print each minister with one elder and no congregation. Robert Neilson, Larne's elder in 1694, is printed beside Adair in 1697 and 1698, while Larne had no minister (Thomas Hall was dead by June 1697; William Ogilvie was ordained on 5 November 1700), and beside Ogilvie in 1707. The rows do not show that Neilson sat for Antrim; one possible reading is that the vacant congregation's elder was printed beside a neighbouring minister. Where a minister is in post the rows pair him with his own congregation's elder: Larne's rows of 1703-1712 name men who are on the Larne session list of November 1700.

### Larne's ministers

Thomas Hall (dead by June 1697, Synod p. 17; Killen: died 1695 aged 75); vacant 1697-1700 (Killen has William Leech called in 1697, "his career here was short"; the Synod places Leech at Ballymena); William Ogilvie, ordained 5 November 1700 (Killen), died 12 September 1712 (p. 293); vacant 1712-15, the congregation divided between parties for James Hud and Samuel Getty (1714, pp. 330-331); on 15 June 1715 Hud was ordained "at Larne" and Getty "at Enver" (p. 350), giving two congregations; Hud died 12 October 1716 (p. 418); Josias Clugston ordained at Larne 19 March 1717/18 (p. 455).

### Other Glasgows (none linked to the Larne branch)

- **George Glasgow, ruling elder, 1713:** General Synod at Antrim, 23 June 1713 ([vol. 1 p. 290](https://archive.org/details/recordsofgeneral01pres/page/290/mode/1up)), Tyrone presbytery list: "Thomas Milligan ... Geo: Glasgow". The roll gives no congregation; Bolton's "Keady" is his index label.
- **Nathaniel Glasgow, minister:** entered on first trials by Monaghan presbytery (reported June 1715, vol. 1 p. 350); on the Augher presbytery ministers' list in 1719 (p. 482); reported by Monaghan presbytery in June 1743 as having "dyed in Apr 1743" ([vol. 2 p. 284](https://archive.org/details/recordsofgeneral02pres/page/284/mode/1up)). Leads, OCR only: "design'd for America" 1718; installed at Golen 1732; "children of Mr Glasgow, deceased" 1749.
- **John Glasgow, minister of Coleraine:** reported by Route presbytery to the Synod of June 1802, "Mr Glasgo of Coleraine died on 13th July" 1801 ([vol. 3 p. 252](https://archive.org/details/recordsofgeneral03pres/page/252/mode/1up)). Leads, OCR only: first trials 1793; a 1799 row "Francis Dill - John Glascow".

Not checked: the Belfast presbytery lists for 1703, part of 1706, 1710, 1719 and 1720 and the rolls of 1722-1730 are OCR only; the *Fasti of the Irish Presbyterian Church* was not consulted.
