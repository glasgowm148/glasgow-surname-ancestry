## Bolton comparison: no disagreement between the books

Charles Knowles Bolton, *Scotch Irish Pioneers in Ulster and America*, [p.351](https://archive.org/details/scotchirishpione00boltuoft/page/351/mode/1up), [direct image inspected](https://archive.org/download/scotchirishpione00boltuoft/page/n368.jpg):

- **GLASGOW**: George, ruling elder 1713, Keady; James, 1698/1703, Randalstown; James, 1705, Dunean.
- Then a separate **GLEN** heading.
- Then **GORDON**, whose indented **John, R E 1706 — Larne, Antrim** line is the supposed lead.

Bolton did not call the 1706 Larne elder Glasgow. The proposed occurrence arose by carrying the Glasgow surname across intervening headings. His geographical assignments are a secondary index; the Synod attendance pages themselves provide minister/elder pairings rather than those locality labels. James-3967 remains distinct from the Larne/Kilwaughter James-1081 research; nothing here links the families.
