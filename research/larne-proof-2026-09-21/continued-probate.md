Names wife Jane; daughters Letty, wife of Thomas Beggs, and Matty/Martha; property in Larne, Killiglen and Ballyhackett; sister Margaret Stewart and half-sisters Rose and Jannet Magill. Names daughter Jane, daughter Mary, son Alexander, wife Jane, stepdaughter Ann Kirk and sister Agnes Leech. The printed editor's question mark must be preserved when using this abstract. It is a separate source route for the known Holmes property context, not proof of James's ancestry.
