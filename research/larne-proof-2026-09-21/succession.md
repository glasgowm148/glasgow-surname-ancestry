# Larne lease succession: bounded source check, 21 September 2026

## Result

No accessible primary image or full archival transcription was located for the
decisive clauses. The 1751/1767 inserted lives, the 1824 renewal recital and
William Glasgow's 1815 copy will remain **unread**. Consequently there is still
no proof that the 1824 widow Elizabeth was William-3324's widow, that either
1736 lessee was her husband's ancestor, or that William and Hugh were brothers.

This check does, however, add a useful identity boundary for the later Larne
ropemaker: a 1799 will abstract calls an occupant **James Glasgow, roper**. It
supports a continuing Larne ropemaker sequence, but gives neither wife nor
child and cannot turn that occupation sequence into descent.

## Descendancy assessment

* **William Glasgow-1505 (older Larne adult, not William Glasgow-3326 born 1730):** adult in the 1730s; joint 1736 tenancy with Hugh is direct. No inspected source names a child or makes Hugh a brother. Confidence: **high** for separate older adult occurrence; **none** for a proved descendant chain.
* **William Glasgow-3326 (born 1730):** must remain distinct from the older William-1505. The requested property records do not identify him, despite older notes sometimes conflating the two. Confidence: **high** for the distinction; no new succession evidence.
* **James Glasgow-1504's household:** `D2977/3A/4/81/116` is only a same-name 1744 property occurrence until its original is read. It proves neither the derivative children nor a continuation to a later James. Confidence: **low** for identity assignment.
* **James Glasgow-1509 / the ropemaker:** the 1799 “James Glasgow, Roper” occupation is compatible with the established 1761, 1766 and 1773 ropemaker sequence. It does not resolve the competing 1737 parent reading under Hugh, nor prove a father of William-3324. Confidence: **moderate** for occupational continuity, **low** for descent.
* **William Glasgow-3324 and Elizabeth:** the published monument proves William, Elizabeth, and daughters Jane Shutter, Eliza and Mary within that household; the 1815 will and 1824 renewal are still required to identify the renewal widow. Confidence: **high** for the monument household; **unproved** for Elizabeth = 1824 widow and for descent from either 1736 lessee.
