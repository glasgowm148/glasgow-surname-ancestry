== Biography ==
The published headstone transcription for Rasharkin Second Presbyterian Church records a memorial erected by Alexander Elder of Gortrickey to his father Robert Elder, his mother Jane, and his sisters and brothers. It also records Alexander Elder's death on 5 January 1943.<ref>Rasharkin Second Presbyterian Church headstones; https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/RasharkinP/graveyard.html. This is a new direct memorial transcription for the catalogue's parentage study, not a Glasgow record and not proof that the Elder family was related to Alexander Glasgow. Its value is locality context: Gortrickey/Gortereghy is the townland where the Glasgow family appears in the 1837–1838 Rasharkin Presbyterian baptism entries. The memorial supplies a named Elder household for future comparison with the later Robert Elder who occupied Alexander Glasgow's former Lisnagaver lot; identity between those Elder references remains unproved.</ref>

The same reproduced Second Portglenone Presbyterian marriage list records Neal Anderson, full-age bachelor farmer of Gortreaghy, marrying Sarah Elder, full-age spinster of Gortreaghy, on 28 September 1893. Sarah's father is recorded as Robert Elder, farmer; witnesses were Robert Anderson and Nancy Elder.<ref>RootsChat, Elder, Murdoch & McMeekin Families; https://www.rootschat.com/forum/index.php?topic=214109.0. This is a new parent-bearing record for the Gortreaghy Elder household and is consistent with the 1908 entry naming Alexander Elder as a son of a Robert Elder, but the shared father's identity and any connection to Robert Elder, husband of Mary Glasgow, remain unproved until the original register is inspected.</ref>

The reproduced Second Portglenone Presbyterian marriage list records Isaac Elder, full-age bachelor farmer of Gohorney, marrying Jane Elliott on 19 February 1903. Isaac's father is recorded as Robert Elder, farmer.<ref>RootsChat, Elder, Murdoch & McMeekin Families; https://www.rootschat.com/forum/index.php?topic=214109.0. This is another catalogue-absent parent-bearing record for a Robert Elder household in the Rasharkin area. It supports the existence of a substantial local Elder family network, but does not by itself identify Isaac's Robert with Robert Elder, husband of Mary Glasgow, or establish any Glasgow relationship.</ref>

A 2007 RootsChat research post reproduces a 18 June 1908 Second Portglenone Presbyterian marriage entry for Alexander Elder, full-age bachelor farmer of Gortreaghy, whose father is recorded as Robert Elder, farmer. Alexander married Mary Anderson, full-age spinster of Gortreaghy, daughter of Neil Anderson; witnesses were Robert Anderson and Margaret Elder.<ref>RootsChat, Elder, Murdoch & McMeekin Families; https://www.rootschat.com/forum/index.php?topic=214109.0. The entry is not yet an inspected original, so it is a secondary-source lead rather than final evidence. It is nevertheless a new, specific parent-bearing record for the Gortreaghy Elder household and gives a testable route for determining whether the Alexander Elder on the Rasharkin Second Presbyterian memorial was a son of the Robert Elder who later occupied Alexander Glasgow's Lisnagaver lot. That identity chain remains unproved.</ref>
