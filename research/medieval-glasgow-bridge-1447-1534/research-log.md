## False-positive controls

- `Glasguensis`, bishop/archbishop/canon/official “of Glasgow,” the cathedral,
  diocese, city, burgh and document location were excluded unless the person had
  Glasgow as the actual recorded name/byname.
- John de Glasgow alias Smith was retained as a distinct candidate because the
  alias is evidentially important.
- Andrew Brady, Alexander Brade, Gilbert Brady, Robert Brade and Esbell Brade
  were not merged.
- Master John Spreull and Janet Spreull were not treated as related without a
  relationship statement.
- Boundary names were distinguished from transaction parties and witnesses.
