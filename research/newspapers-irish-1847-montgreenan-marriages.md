## Reading and date

The notice reads: “On the 25th ult, at Paisley, George Sherbrooke Airey, Esq., Lieutenant in the Royal Navy, son of the late Lieutenant-General Sir George Airey, to Anne, second daughter of the late Robert Glasgow, Esq. Also, at the same time, the Rev. George Drake, son of the Rev. John Drake, rector of Stourton, to Charlotte Maria Cecilia, youngest daughter of the late Robert Glasgow, Esq., of Montgreenan, in the county of Ayr.” The wedding date 25 August 1847 is inferred from “25th ult” relative to the 2 September issue.
