C W notice that there is a deeds past betwit , Mr Bradbourne & my sd touching such debts as should be assigned unto him and me any other for his and my be which I desire may be formed on my she and expect it shall be formed on his Item , I and desire that that Lease which Lindsey and I did take of the Duke of Burlingham of the Manor of Villias in the Queens County in the kingdom of Ireland and afterwards did purchase of the said Hendrey his Magee of the said Lease he that the who purchase of my one I say my will and press order is , that the said Lease may be sold one convemently it may after my decease , and the money to bee said upon the sale thereof to be played toward the paymentof my debts and forth other uses as by my will is direct Item I give and bequeath to my Son Martin Rock the Moretie of all my Interest of and in the farm of the Customes and imported use of all Ireland to common the next Quarter after my decease he paying unto my dear wife Dame Elizabeth Noell fifty pounds Anned out of the said Magee thus bequeathed unto him , during the continuance of the said farm the other Moyle of the profitts of the said farm or will order and appoint to goe towards the payment of my debts and other such let as by this my said Will appointed And I doe hereby nominate & appoint my said Son Martine well to bee my Deputy manage my Juterest of and in the said farm , and to receive the Salary due thereupon Item I give and bequeath unto my said Son Martin Holt the one - Morate of all my Interest of and in the farme of the Ewise of been and be and Lioner for Alehouses in Ireland he paying unto my dear wife Dame Elizabeth Noel Fifty pounds And out of the said Moretiethus bequeathed unto him , during the continuance of the said farm the other Moyoke of the said farm I say the profitts of the said farme I doe will order & appoint to go towards the payment of my debts and such other the 152 uses as by my will is appointed And in case the private Albert betwixt the Partnership of the said farme , bee inperferted in my name with the rest before my decease , then I doe desd and earnestly request the partnership of that farme be inserted in the name of my son Martine well wishead of my own , he my said Son Martine Swell Declaring before hand , under his hand , that he will form in all parts my will the intent and meaning as is by declared Item I give and bequeath to my Sonne Thomas Now one sixth part of all the profit arising by my Intery of and from the farme of the Addison all duty in the bind and of England And I doe nominate and appoint him my sd 00 Jonne Thomas Hill to bee my Deputy for ye mang of the part that I have in the said farm , of the Addison Dutger And to receive the Salary thereof and for the residue of the profitts arring from the said farme , do will ordain and appoint shall be disposed towards the payment of my debts and other uses as is appointed in this my will . Item I give and bequeath unto my dear wife Dame Elizabeth Will one hundred pounds of Anne to paid unto her yearly from the time of my decease out of my Interest in the farme of Timber Dates Glasses gone and stone ware or during the continuance of the said farm , the remainder of my Interest of the profitts of the said farm I do order and appoint to go towards the payment of my debts and others my said will expressed and as touching all my personal State which shall arise by ready money plate Jewell Cloushold stuffe Merchandize and other comodities and for the sale of all the Lands and Reversion and other things by this my will ordered and appointed to be sold I doe hereby order will and dispose the same as following first I do will and desire that all such debts , as I shall justly and truly owe to any person or persons whatsoever shall be justly paid and satisfied by my Executor hereafter named the for the remainder of the Said sonall Estate which is all that the Citty of London sun take notice of , I doe order will and devise
