King to pounds Item I give and bequeath unto Thomas Carew and his wife to by each of them a hint five pounds . Item I doe give and bequeath unto my Godhills Mr England Sonne five pounds . Item I doe give and bequeath unto Robert Breretono wife his daughters child any other God will five pounds . Item I doe give and bequeath unto M. Phillip andey one short Ravier a Spanish leather Bolt , a dosenan half of small coller bands , and a black Standar & Item & doe give and bequeath unto Mr Maule the Elder one table with drawer , one Curry Carpett , and one Rusha leather Cangeth , Item I doe give and bequeath unto man the younger the Male thounton the hanging in my chamber over the Custom house Item I doe give and bequeath one Mr Michael and one large trunk with a sealein , covering standing in my possett Item , I give andbequeath unto Thomas Hale one great bible Item I do give and bequeath unto M Alcocke dutch Amanace ten do give and bequeath unto George Rutland Blacksmith two old on Carrell of Guns which are in my possett Item I doe give and bequeath unto Mr Moore wife to John Moore one large looking glass already in her Custody , Item & doe give and bequeath unto Mr Stephen Palmer daughter C one Lane Looking glass in the Chamber where I now Lodge , Item I doe give and bequeath unto my Laundresse her husband two Trustees of Coather , and two pair of storing And Lastly 1 constute , ordaine doe nominate and at my dear and we beloved frent & Mr Michael Hare and Mr Phillip Candey to bee the Executor of this my last Will and Testament and to disposed of the rest of my goods , debts Shatter , and credit not herein mentioned according to their joint will and pleasure In Witness whereof I have hereunto sett my hand and Seale the day and year first above written Henry Wright Simed and sealed in the presence of the undernamed Witnesses Wm Jones . Wm Courtney S Brinsmead Samuel Probate of approbat in co Junis Lorna Aisq ( or Rood on Cransie Earle or insinuation first rumor testament in Sentine Wright my de Civitate Dublin Gend of labord or nona E is at Administrat Coner on and left his feet to at and and Jacoburn Cuman onemon Credit of each Have at Philip Tandey on Executor Testam to norat prices and seal des Evangelia & Souliter weat salvo June or at subsignate and and after our Surrogation Datum In the name of God Amen I Sannell Brinsmead of Dublin being sick in body , but of perfect sence and memory praised be Almighty God doe make and ordain this my last Will and Testament in manner and forme following Imprimis I bequeath my Sould God into the hands of God my Saviour in hope of a full Assurance of a resurrection and my body to be decently buried in the Parish of St Katherine in Thomas Street Dublin Item there to here pounds a yeare at Waterford to be from Mr William Ellis One or over all and for the same of Seven years to be disposed of for the good of my children as my Executors shall seem meet and expedient and my Willis that my house in Waterford be left to the disposal of my friends M Richard Barrett for him to keeper , or to dispose of or deliver up to the forty nine men or other the shall think fitt Itein that whereas I have a stone of the act Dower and keep in the hands of Lies Thomas Inman of Murrayhleigh in the County of Kilkenny valued by him the said Cuman at sixstore pounds store for which he the said Liel Inman was to give Twenty foure pounds for eight yeares , and afterwards the said Store to be his own by Bond to that purpose may more appear And whereas there in Twenty four pounds due from the said Juman at May day 1664 for one years rent for the said Cattle I doe also leave the disposal thereof to my Executors for the use and benefit of my children
