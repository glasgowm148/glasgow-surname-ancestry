as privately as may be defireing my Enrs not to exceed the sum of ten pounds in order to my Interment And as touching that temporall Estate which the Lord in much mercy hathbestowed on me , I give , bequeath , and dispose of the same in manner and form following That is to say Imprimis I give and bequeath unto my son John Potter the Sume offour hundred pounds of Lawfull money of England to be paid unto him when he shall accomplish the full age of one and twenty yeares and in the mean time my will and mind is that my Executors hereafter named shall allow and pay unto my said son John Boteler out of the interest of the said four hundred pounds the sum of tenn pounds per annum for and towards the better support and maintenance , the same to be paid half yearly by even and equall portions , and the rest of the interest of that money to be continued and reserved for his own proper use and benefitt - Item I give devise and bequeath unto my Daughter Susan Hubbard the Sume of twenty pounds per ann of the Like money to be instly and truly paid unto her or her assignes out of my Estate and the revenon thereof for and During 466 during the terme of her naturall life at fourequall quarterly payments Item I give and beque ath unto my two sons in Law Anthony Wright and Francis Hutchinson the sume of fiftypounds a piece Lawfully English money Jan I give and bequeath unto my master Andrews Daughter Elizabeth the late wife of Henry West brook late of Lendon Goldsmith deceased the same of Thirty pounds of the Like money Item I give and bequeath unto my deare frind sd George Wharton , and Mr John Brewer Glasier the sume of ten pounds a piece to buy them Mourning Item my will and mind is , and I doe hereby give , order and appoint that my son William Boteler and my Son John Botter Shall have each of them affeather bed well prepared with Curtaines , Vallene Sheets Rugg , Pillowers and all other furniture thereunto belonging as shall be necessary and convenient . And further my will and mind is that if my shop goods shall exceed in value over and above Six hundred pounds Then the overplus to be disposed of as my said Sons William Boteler shall think fit and my desire also is that what money I have now abroad
