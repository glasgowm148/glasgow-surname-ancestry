my Frank Booker God bestow upon my Dear beloved Brother Isaac Jeane the paying , and or to be due to the Cheif and Lord as the said house four his also give unto aforesaid wife all any their the Glass house in the County of Cavan the fourth part to Dispose of enjoy as her own tell Igited also to my Dearly beloved Brother Isaac Jean all that my his house messuage or very appurtinances in S. James Thos Dublin Comonly called the litle Remick upon the Land of the Lord Dungan to have and to hold unto my said Brother his heirs and assigned for and During my whole Interest therein her paying the Cheife Rent unto the said Lord one I give and Devise unto lord 1114 two she can often wife of in lands by ten of London Gold Smith & Mary Blanchard wife of Isaac Blanchard of London aforesaid Taylor threescore pounds set to be equally divided between them not my will and was that the Legatee of three pose pounds bequeathed to my said Sister shall be paid unto them out of the many unto me from Mr Henley and others for with hand the Bonds of Seventy two pounds Sted . And the other halfe found Remaind of the said Bond of Seventy two pounds I Doegued and - Demise unto my near Robe Vergantafore desire that my said wife and Brother be made a full and Sole Executor of this my last will and testament In witness Whereof hereunto set my hand and all and Published this my last will and stament the day and year first above with Davis and Delivered in the present In Maud Nick at fullen Probation approbation in ordered for a high Regd Prerogative & after firt stamentum David Jean De Civilah Dublingen Defund her & nomon and exquisiters of Admr one & Defendant for a fund P Riser Lambur Armasham & Jud & Joane videlin In defent 21 Pacio Jean for State for Simon the not paid ad arisen at ( Sheper & of Doodie mensis January A D 16 name of God Amen I Maried Michal Gillen being sick & weake in body but of pfect mind and memory doe make this my last will and Testament In manner following my soule I bequeath unto Almighty God hoping to be saved throu ye merits of John Christ my Savior ; my bond to bee buried as my Executor shall appoint give and bequeath unto my Cowen Theobald Cole all my worldly Estate of what Kindon nature soever Lastly I make noial and accomb my said Colson Theobald Poole Executor of this my last will and testament In witness whereof I have hereunto set my hand and sealed and published the same Farm this 11th Day of November by and Wycombat Cullen Herman Signed sealed and published my sence of us while Garrett Byrne Peter Took Terren Daughter Tole her mark Probate
