God A 27 X the name of God Amen Alice Godwin of the City of Dublin Widdow being she body , but in feet some and memory do make my last will and Testament in manner following first I bequeath my soul to God that reated me and my body to theearth from whence it same desiring to be buried at as may bee my deceased husband John Godwin in St Owen Church is a decent manner And for what X worldly goods the Lord late been pleased to bestow on in I desire it may be disposed of do followeth first bequeath that out of the money due to me from Mr Holgate and the Executors of S William Parsons there be fifty pounds paid to Mr Balentine Savage he is to have with my Sonne George Item I will that my goods be Sold and out of the profitt of them and on of into my son Arthur fee maintained but he be put to bee an Apprentice Item my will is that after my funerall Charged and paid and my said some Nothing maintenance deducted that forty or fifty pound at the discretion of my Executors be paid out of the first of my rent to bind my Sonne Arthur Aprenton And whereas Mornall is to repay forty pounds he had with my found William within three years --- I desire my said Son William may have the same when he comes to the age of twenty one years and whereas owe my uncle Bennett 27th and which I assigned him to receive the sale of my brother out of my person which is not yet paid I desire the same may to be satisfied by my brother and of my person onely defaulting the value of a Sale of Damas be my much Bennett late of nine Item I desire that each of my Children may have a bodd and a little of my him which I desire may not bee sould . He owe my Coron to Corn John Bennett foure pounds which I desire brother may sell the Cow he hath of mine , and pay the said foure pounds . And as for the remainder of my said person I leave and bequeath the same to my Sonne Arthur Hom I leave my wedding king , and my husband seale with his Case of Armas to my sound William . Item I leave my diamond King to my soune Arthur and my two small gold ring to posinger to Arthur , my trunk with water to in Some William , and my other trunk to my son George Item bequeath to Ringo of Twenty shillings a posse to Mr Thomas Worsopp and Mr Valentine Savage , and a full morning King to Mr Worsopp , another to Mr Elizabeth Brookes , and another to M. Sabage I desire my Children be put in mourning , but we globe , or Ribbons to be disposed of at my funerall Item owe Jam Reeber five pounds which an Sisson was to pay who owed and seaven pounds & desire my Executors to see the five pounds paid and to call on Jan Joston for the leaven pounds Item I desire my neere Nice may have the gowner ( weare every day and my now stufe gown some linnen and forty shillings in money . Item my Lake and to Mantler to be equally devided amongst my three Sound as for any other debts Rents that shall be made out of the land in the Country or house in town untill the mortgage shall be redeemed I leave and bequeath to be equally divided amongst my three Sound as alsoe the mortgage money wheresoever the same shall be redeemed Item I leave to my sound William my looking glasse appoint Thomas Morson Mr Valentine Sabage and my Sonne William Executor of this my last Will and Testa In Wittnes whereof I have hereunto put my hand and Seale this Eleventh day of March 1662 . Ales Godwin
