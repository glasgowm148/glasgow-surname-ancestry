# Betham: Archibald Glasgow's eldest son Thomas

Accessed and inspected 30 September 2026. **New direct father–son evidence at the record level; the exact match of the son to Glasgow-3556 remains probable. Do not upgrade that profile's Father status solely from this abstract.**

## Inspected source

National Library of Ireland, Genealogical Office MS 225, William Betham's genealogical abstracts/pedigrees from Prerogative wills, Old Series vol. 3, D–O, written p. 90, digital viewer image 97. [Catalogue and viewer](https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000540633); microfilm POS 1219. Catalogue dates the work 1808. This is Betham's pre-destruction derivative abstract/pedigree, **not the original will or registered copy**.

Literal key text, line breaks retained:

> Archibald Glasgow
> of Rathmalton in
> Co Donegal Clk
> will dat — April
> 1673

The pedigree connects Archibald and Elizabeth M'Auley [surname provisional] by a marriage line. Their shared descendant line leads to **“Thomas Glasgow / eldest Son”** and **“Margaret / only daur.”** The diagram directly expresses the father–son relationship. Margaret appears connected to an unnamed Cairns husband and Thomas Cairns child; details near the right page edge are incomplete and should not be used without another reading. There is no Nathaniel in this entry.

“April 1673” is the **will date**, not probate or death. No day is supplied. It can coexist with the 1677 probate index. “Clk” should be expanded cautiously as clerk; the pedigree does not give an ecclesiastical appointment.
