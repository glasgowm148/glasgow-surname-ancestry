## Current conclusion

The visible right page contains two indentures naming Daniel Glasco/Glascow, Richard Linton, and **Mary, Daniel’s wife**. Daniel is described as a **Norfolk County planter**. These are positive residence/spouse records. **They do not presently prove Daniel’s father.** The earlier-title clause in the lower deed is too small to read reliably. A provisional “by William …” reading must not become a named parent or kinship claim.

The lower deed continues beyond this page. Do not describe the whole transaction as fully transcribed, or automatically label the pair lease-and-release: both visible texts include a one-year possession arrangement.
