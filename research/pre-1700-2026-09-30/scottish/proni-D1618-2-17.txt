Title :
Lease from Richard Barry, Gent., of Dublin ...
Dates :
25 March 1682
Description :
Lease from Richard Barry, Gent., of Dublin to Hugh Hamill of Strabane, reciting a deed of mortgage of 1 May 1673 whereby Edward Edwards of Londonderry borrowed £1,500 from Sir James Langham of Cottesbrooke, Northamptonshire, and Sir Robert Clayton of London, on the security of Edwards's manor of Hastings, Co. Tyrone, '... and all the proportions of land by the General Survey of all lands within the said county called the small proportion of Clonaghmore, lying and being within the barony of Omagh ...', the castle of Castlederg, Donnerea, Trimoglaght, Naffarageine, etc, etc. some 250 place-names are itemised.
