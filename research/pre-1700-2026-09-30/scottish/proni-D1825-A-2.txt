Title :
Documents of title, etc, relating to Murrroe, Magheremenagh, Millbrook and Greenhill, Portin Island, Knocknafore [Knocknafaugher], Sessiogh/Carrowcessie, Sandhill, Rannarrow [Rinaraw], Woodhill, Rockhill, Breaghy, Fore [Faugher], Ballymore, Clonmessa [Clonmass], Corcreggan, etc
Dates :
[1628]-1814
Description :
Documents of title relating to Murrroe, Magheremenagh, Millbrook and Greenhill, Portin Island, Knocknafore [Knocknafaugher], Sessiogh/Carrowcessie, Sandhill, Rannarrow [Rinaraw], Woodhill, Rockhill, Breaghy, Fore [Faugher], Ballymore, Clonmessa [Clonmass], Corcreggan (including water corn mill), etc., in parish of Clondahorky. This estate was built up by the Wray family of Ards and sold to Samuel Hadfield, merchant of Londonderry, in 1770, and by George Hadfield to Alexander Stewart in 1814. These deeds fall into several distinct groups, as follows:
Copy grant, Charles II to Sir Mulmurray McSwyne, [1628]; mortgage, power of attorney, conveyances, grant of fee farm rent, agreement, etc., relating to transfer of property from Sir Mulmurray McSwyne, Donough McSwyne and Seely Ni Swyne, to Sir Paul Davis of Dublin, 1633-1635; copy and translation of letters patent, Charles I to Sir Paul Davis, Manor of Portin Island, [1640]. [12 items].
Copy grant, Charles I to Thurlagh Boyle, of Manor of Castle Boyle, [1639]; certificate of Sir John Stephens under Act of Settlement, 1667; copy grant, Charles II to Sir John Stephens, property including Foare als Duore [Faugher], [1667]; bond, Sir John Stephens of Dublin to Michael Harrison of Marlea [Magheralave, Lisburn], 1673. [Four items].
Copies of grant, Charles II to Sir Hans Hamilton, of property including Carnegarrow, Clonmessa [Clonmass], Ballymore, and part of Foare [Faugher], [1668], also including grant to Alexander Woodhall of Island Derry, Co. Down; lease and release, Sir Hans Hamilton of Hamiltonsbawn to Hugh Hamill of Dublin, 1673. [Four items]
Bond and defeazance, Sir John Davis to Hugh Hamill, 1673; exemplification of recovery suffered by Hugh Hamill of Manor of Portin Island, 1674; mortgage, Hugh Hamill to Samuel Norman and William Smith, merchants, Londonderry, 1675; bond, William Dutton of Greenforte to Archibald Glasgow of Ramelton, 1675/6; conveyance of equity of redemption and grant of rent charge, Hugh Hamill to Michael Sampson and William Wray, 1682. [1673-1685]. [Eight items].
Wray and Hadfield title deeds, including fee farm grant, William Wray to Robert Paterson, of part of Dunfanaghy, 1690; marriage settlements of Humphrey Wray of Ards and Ann Brooke, 1707, and William Wray and Ann, daughter of Andrew Hamilton, Archdeacon of Raphoe, 1731; deeds concerning woods, 1770. [12 items].
