Title :
One of two copies of release. Consid. £200....
Dates :
13 March 1700
Description :
One of two copies of release. Consid. £200. Schedule attached: "The Schedule or acct unto wch the annexed deed doth referr being an acct of debts wch Coll. Abraham Creighton has paid for Hugh Hamill for which he had no Countor Security save only the deeds made by the said Hugh Hamill bearing date the fifth and sixth of October one thousand six hundred eighty and five". Abraham Creichton of Crom, Co. Fermanagh, Robt. King of Dublin and John Hamilton of Murvagh, Co. Donegal to David Creichton of Dublin. Lifford, Co. Donegal.
