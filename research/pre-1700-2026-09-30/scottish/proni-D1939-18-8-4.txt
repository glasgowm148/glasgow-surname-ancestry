Title :
Fee farm grant for ever. Rent 10sh. p.a....
Dates :
21 December 1680
Description :
Fee farm grant for ever. Rent 10sh. p.a. Suit of mill on Hugh Hamill's Estate. Hugh Hamill of Lifford, Co. Donegal to John Wigtown of Raphoe, Co. Donegal. Ballindrate, Co. Donegal.
"This Indenture made the one and twentieth day of December one thousand six hundred and eighty between Hugh Hamill of Lifford in the County of Donegal Esquire of the one part and John Wigtoune of Raphoe in the said County Gent. of the other part witnesseth that the said Hugh Hamill for and in consideration of the rents services covenants and other agreements hereafter in these presents expressed and reserved hath given granted enfeoffed and to fee farm let and by these presents doth give grant enfeoff and to fee farm let unto the said John Wigtoune and his heirs for ever all that one house tenement backside and garden in the town of 'Ballendreate' which was heretofore in the possession of Richard Robison and is now in the possession of the said John Wigtoune with the equal halfs of that part of the land of Dobson's Sessiogh that lies under the high way on the right hand as you go from Ballindrait aforesaid to John Kea's house in Cavanacorr and which lies next the house and tenement that is laid out to David Smith which was heretofore Dobson's tenement in Ballindrait aforesaid with all the rights members and appurtenances thereunto belonging or in any wise appertaining all 'scituate' lying and being in the quarterland of Monyne in the County of Donegal excepting ... all mines minerals quarries fishings and royalties of what kind soever with liberty to and for the said Hugh Hamill his heirs and assigns and servants to fish fowl hunt and hawk on the premises or any part thereof and to draw and dry their nets thereon and also free liberty with horses cars carts and other engines to dig take and carry away the said mines minerals quarry stones fish wood underwood nets and other engines of fishing without any let hindrance ...yielding and paying ... ten shillings sterling ...over and above all subsidies taxes cuts and county charges whatsoever that are ...charged on the premises ... and also doing and performing suit and service to the court leet and courts baron of the manor of Castlefin in the County of Donegal... and that the said John Wigtoune his heirs and assigns, undertenants and cottars on the premises shall from time to time and at all times during the continuance of this ... grind all his and their grindable grain that shall grow or be expended on the premises at the mill or mills of the said Hugh Hamill his heirs or assigns that are or shall be erected on the south side of the river of Deale in the manor of Lifford in the said county and shall pay for the toll and moulter of all grain except malt the sixteenth grain and for malt and twentieth grain and if he the said John Wigtoune ... shall grind any of their grains ... at any other mill or on 'quernes' that for every time he or they shall do the same he the said John Wigtoune shall forfeit and pay unto the said Hugh Hamill... the sum often shillings sterling and if it shall fortune the said yearly rent or any part thereof to be behind or unpaid by the space of ten days ... it shall ... be lawful ... for the said Hugh Hamill ... into the premises ... to enter and distrain ... provided always that if it shall fortune the said yearly rent to be behind or unpaid by the space of three hundred and sixty five days after the days and times whereon the same ought to be paid and no sufficient distress can or may be had or found on the premises, unless it be in time of open rebellion and so proclaimed ... it shall ... be lawful ... to re-enter ... and the said John Wigtoune for himself his heirs ... doth covenant ... that he ... his heirs and assigns undertenants and cottars shall and will from time to time and as often as occasion shall require during the continuance of this present grant give their proportionable assistance to the thatching 'skrewing' and 'watleing' the said mill and to the keeping up her dams watercourses and water wheels and to the bringing home timber and stones for the rebuilding repairing and upkeep of the said mill in good and serviceable condition for the best advantage of the said Hugh Hamill his heirs and assigns and the inhabitants of the 'succen' belonging to the said mill and others that shall have occasion to grind at her and the said John Wigtoune for himself his heirs ... doth covenant ... that he ... shall ... yearly ... sow 'flex' and hemseed upon the premises according to the direction of the Act of Parliament in that case made and provided ...'
