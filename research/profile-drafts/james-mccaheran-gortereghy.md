== Biography ==

'''James McCaheran''' was probably born about '''1785''' [uncertain], with a broad working range of approximately '''1775–1795'''. He was an adult occupier or tenant at Gortereghy, in Rasharkin parish, County Antrim, in 1834. His exact birth date, death, wife, children and residence history are not yet known. The estimate is based only on his adult tenancy and the possibility that he fathered Mary (McCaughan) Glasgow, probably born about 1811; it is not a recorded age.

The 1834 Rasharkin tithe material records James McCaheran at Gortereghy alongside William McCaheran.<ref name="Tithe1834">Public Record Office of Northern Ireland, Rasharkin parish tithe applotment material, 1834, PRONI FIN/5/A/236; [https://www.billmacafee.com/182030stithe/tithenorthantrimrevisionorder.pdf North Antrim tithe material]. The relevant transcription/index identifies William and James McCaheran at Gortereghy; the original page should be checked for the exact holding details.</ref>

A newly located FamilySearch/Find a Grave record identifies a '''James McCaughern''', born about '''1790''', who died '''13 September 1858''' and was buried in Rasharkin Presbyterian Church Graveyard. The memorial names '''Nancy McCaughern (1794–1859)''' as his spouse and '''Thomas McCaughern (1829–1857)''' as a child.<ref name="Burial1858">[https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:CVDP-S2MM FamilySearch Find a Grave Index record]; [https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/205517378/james-mccaughern Find a Grave memorial 205517378]. The linked family details are memorial data and require original-record confirmation.</ref>

This James is now the best newly located chronological and local burial candidate for the unidentified 1834 tenant. The match is '''not proved''': the memorial does not state Gortereghy, William McCaheran, Mary Glasgow or parentage, and the surviving 1851 Rasharkin return does not show a James-and-Nancy household in the extracted Lisnagavor sequence. It must remain a candidate pending an inscription, marriage, probate, church or valuation bridge.

The spelling is relevant to the nearby family of Alexander Glasgow and Mary/Molly McCaughan. Their first located children were recorded at Gortereagh in 1837–1838, with the mother's surname written McAughin or McAughern, forms compatible with McCaheran and McCaughan in this locality.<ref name="RasharkinBaptisms">Rasharkin Presbyterian baptism transcription, entries 75 and 106, including James Glascaw and the incomplete Glascow entry at Gortereagh; [https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/RasharkinP/Baptisms_1834.html transcription].</ref>

== Research Notes ==

=== Possible connection to Mary (McCaughan) Glasgow ===

James is a '''working candidate only''' for the father of [[McCaughan-222|Mary (McCaughan) Glasgow]], who was probably born about 1811 and married [[Glasgow-951|Alexander Glasgow]] about 1836. The hypothesis rests on three weak-to-moderate clues taken together:

* James McCaheran is documented in Gortereghy in 1834.
* Alexander and Mary Glasgow's first recorded children were associated with Gortereagh immediately after their reported 1836 marriage.
* The first known son was named James, although the naming clue is not diagnostic and could refer to either side of the family.

No record currently states that James McCaheran was Mary's father, and no record identifies William McCaheran as her father or brother. The 1851 McCaheren households at Lisnagaver, the Finvoy Alexr. McCaughan holding and the Drumadarragh McCaughey/McCahan family are separate comparison clusters, not proved relatives.

=== Identity warning ===

Do '''not''' merge this unidentified Gortereghy tenant with [[McCaughan-68|James McCaughan]], who is recorded on WikiTree as the husband of Mary Larny and father of [[McCaughan-67|John McCaughan]]. McCaughan-68 is chronologically possible but has no demonstrated Gortereghy or Rasharkin connection. The two men remain separate until a source links them.

=== Evidence needed ===

The most valuable unresolved sources are the Kilraughts–Dervock Reformed Presbyterian society and family material, PRONI CR5/32A/1 and related references CR5/32/1/1/1–2, covering approximately 1804–1831; a marriage or baptism naming Mary; a lease, probate record or valuation sequence; or a witness/kinship statement connecting James to Mary's household. Until such a record is found, this profile should remain an unidentified local candidate and should not be attached as Mary's father on WikiTree.
