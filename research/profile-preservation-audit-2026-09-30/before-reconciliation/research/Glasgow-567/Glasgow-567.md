== Biography ==

'''Barbara Glasgow''' is documented as the mother of [[Little-6307|Catharine Little]], born 9 September and baptised 29 September 1611 in Edinburgh parish, Midlothian.<ref name="Catharine1611">“Scotland Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950”, database, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQ4N-CDX : 16 September 2021), Johne Little in entry for Catharine Little, 1611; citing Edinburgh Parish, Edinburgh, Midlothian, Scotland. The entry is also transcribed by Findmypast.</ref> The index names Catharine's father as [[Little-6308|Johne Little]], but does not establish whether the parents were married, nor name Barbara's parents, birthplace, or death.

Known child:
* [[Little-6307|Catharine Little]], born 9 September and baptised 29 September 1611.<ref name="Catharine1611" />

== Research Notes ==

=== Parentage and relationships ===

The current links to [[Glasgow-3522|William Glasgow]] and [[Herbison-439|Margaret Herbesoun]] remain unproved. Glasgow town-clerk protocols 2903 and 2916 (1586) prove that couple as parents of George Glasgow only; they do not name Barbara or call her George's sister. Preserve those links, and any currently populated date or location, as uncertain pending a supported replacement; do not clear them solely because the evidence is weak.

Johne Little is Catharine's proved co-parent, but a spouse relationship is unproved. The published Edinburgh marriage register has no Barbara Glasgow/Johne Little marriage; same-name John Little identities remain unresolved.

=== Distinguishing same-name women ===

Do not merge this adult Barbara with [[Glasgow-3182|Barbara (Glasgow) Wilson]] or [[Glasgow-3302|Barbara (Glasgow) Johnson]] without continuity evidence. The latter is associated with George Johnstoun through a 21 November 1616 Edinburgh marriage<ref name="JohnstounMarriage1616">“Scotland, Marriages, 1561-1910”, database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYMH-HZJ : 11 February 2020), Barbara Glasgow in entry for George Johnstoun, 1616.</ref> and children including Daniell in 1617<ref name="Daniell1617">“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950”, database, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1YQ-BX4 : 16 September 2021), Barbara Glasgow in entry for Daniell Johnestoun, 1617.</ref> and Archebald in 1618.<ref name="Archebald1618">“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950”, database, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X1Y3-2XL : 16 September 2021), Barbara Glasgow in entry for Archebald Johnstoun, 1618.</ref> A Barbara Glasgow married John Wilson in South Leith on 26 November 1611.<ref name="WilsonMarriage1611">“Scotland, Marriages, 1561-1910”, database, FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XYML-ZBB : 11 February 2020), Barbara Glasgow in entry for John Wilson, 1611.</ref> These may concern the same woman or different contemporaries, but neither record establishes the mother of Catharine's identity. [[Glasgow-1102|Barbara Glasgow (1617-)]] is excluded by chronology.<ref name="Barbara1617">“Scotland, Births and Baptisms, 1564-1950”, database, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VQC9-JYX : 12 February 2020), Barbara Glasgow, 1617.</ref>

The Ninian Glasgow household or immediate collateral circle is a research hypothesis, not a basis for a parent attachment or merge.
