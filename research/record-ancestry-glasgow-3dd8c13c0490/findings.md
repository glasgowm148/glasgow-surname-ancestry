# Jane Glasgow source attribution review

## Current conclusion

Reviewed: 2026-11-22

The Anderston index gives a birth on 16 October 1863 to Samuel Glasgow and Jane Horn. Glasgow-2526 has a documented Belfast child, Jane Trelford Glasgow (Glasgow-2530), born on 26 May 1877 to Samuel Glasgow and Jane Woods. These are different children and couple attributions.

A remarriage or surname variant is possible, but no dated evidence connects the Horn household to Glasgow-2526. The comparison uses held derivative indexes; original register images remain uninspected.
