## Current conclusion

Reviewed: 2026-11-22

The parallel Symington and Covington and Thankerton entries name William Glasgow and Margaret Henderson as parents of Marion, born 25 March 1838. Glasgow-2876’s wife Margaret Graham is documented on the memorial as dying in 1814 and cannot be Marion’s mother. The paired entries support the existing documentary father William Glasgow.

Glasgow-2876 could have remarried. No dated link establishes that he and the documentary father are the same man. The comparison uses held derivative indexes; original register images remain uninspected.
