# Margaret Calder Glasgow source attribution review

## Current conclusion

Reviewed: 2026-11-22

The New Monkland index gives a birth on 27 June 1859 to John Glasgow and Ann Little. Glasgow-1699 is recorded with Ann Jane Ferguson and a daughter named Margaret (Glasgow-1798) in County Tyrone; the Tyrone account does not independently identify the child in New Monkland.

No record bridges the Little and Ferguson households or establishes that the two Margaret entries name one child. The comparison uses held derivative indexes; original register images remain uninspected.
