# James Glasgow and Mary Ann Sutton: Kirknewton family

## Current conclusion

Reviewed: 2026-11-15. Held derivative indexes correlate James Glasgow and Mary Ann Sutton across the 1867 Edinburgh marriage, five children born during 1868–1881 at Kirknewton and East Calder, and the shared Kirknewton inscription naming wife Mary Ann Sutton and parents James Glasgow and Agnes Hunter. These are probable documentary adult identities, not a proved existing WikiTree match. Original registers and stone remain uninspected; repeated indexes are not independent evidence. Fred in the inscription is not automatically equated with Frederick Dunford. The 1841 child and older father remain different generations. The Limekilns probate candidate remains separate: an exact 1903 death date alone does not establish identity. The original adult source accounts remain available within the consolidated complete draft.



## Kirknewton family source comparison —15 November 2026

The [marriage index](https://www.ancestry.co.uk/search/collections/60144/records/2075836) names Jas. Glasgow and Mary Ann Sutton on 28 June 1867 at Edinburgh Parish. The [shared monument transcript](https://www.ancestry.co.uk/search/collections/62497/records/348011) calls Mary Ann Sutton James’s wife and gives her death as 5 November 1898; it names his parents James Glasgow and Agnes Hunter and records the younger James’s death on 14 November 1903, aged 62. Sutton is the supported maiden surname, not an alternative married name. The [wife’s indexed monument row](https://www.ancestry.co.uk/search/collections/62497/records/348010) is another index representation of that shared inscription, not an independent source.

The child indexes name the same father and mother at Kirknewton and East Calder:

- [Edward Sutton Glasgow](/people/record-ancestry-glasgow-7b9b5531c6ac.html), birth 13 Feb 1870, [held index](https://www.ancestry.co.uk/search/collections/60143/records/21528416). This is an indexed birth date, not a supplied baptism date.
- [Marion Glasgow](/people/record-ancestry-glasgow-d7da0b1bd039.html), birth 16 Sep 1872, [held index](https://www.ancestry.co.uk/search/collections/60143/records/3648596). This is an indexed birth date, not a supplied baptism date.
- [Frederick Dunford Glasgow](/people/record-ancestry-glasgow-ffe390a110bc.html), birth 5 Aug 1874, [held index](https://www.ancestry.co.uk/search/collections/60143/records/17848021). This is an indexed birth date, not a supplied baptism date.

The [1859 Beith child index](https://www.ancestry.co.uk/search/collections/60143/records/2959477) names Mary Glasgow, daughter of William Glasgow and Mary Boyd. It does not name Mary Ann Sutton. Equating that child with the 1867 bride would make her eight; the former mixed attribution is withdrawn as a selected personal event and retained only as an unselected comparison. The child source itself is preserved.

The [1841 birth/baptism index](https://www.ancestry.co.uk/search/collections/60143/records/25485493) names parents James Glasgow and Agnes Hunter, matching the monument’s parental names. This is a strong possible childhood correlation for the younger adult, not a reason to merge the [older father account](/people/record-ancestry-glasgow-30c156d061c5.html) into his son or to select new parent hypotheses. The [Limekilns probate candidate](/people/record-anc-james-glasgow-1904-28658.html) remains separate because matching death dates alone do not resolve identity. Fred’s infancy death in the inscription is not automatically assigned to Frederick Dunford.

The surviving [James account](/people/record-ancestry-glasgow-98605ded7d74.html) and [Mary Ann account](/people/record-ancestry-glasgow-0b71482626a0.html) remain on hold for original-source and existing-profile identity checks. Seven redundant documentary adult accounts have been consolidated reversibly; no WikiTree person was created or edited, and no parent assessment was upgraded.
