## Officer comparisons — 12 November 2026

Its text refers to a prior Gazette notice dated 25 October 1834; the publication of that earlier notice has not been independently established. The original Gazette page was not read. Name, Bengal and a compatible period alone do not identify this officer as the children’s father.

This is catalogue wording, not an inspected service file; no full death date is adopted. The two officer records may concern different men.
