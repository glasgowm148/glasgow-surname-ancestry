# Findings: Henry Glads, Duneane, 1740

Last updated: 2026-09-13

## Current conclusion

The 1740 County Antrim Protestant Householders' Return records **Henry Glads**
in Duneane civil parish. This is a distinct documentary occurrence whose surname
identity remains unresolved. It is not evidence for Hugh Glasgow
([Glasgow-1503](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1503)), who is documented
at Larne with wife Ann and died there in 1767.

Do not normalise Glads to Glasgow, attach this occurrence to Hugh, or create a
person profile until a closer record or corroborating household evidence
establishes the surname and identity.
