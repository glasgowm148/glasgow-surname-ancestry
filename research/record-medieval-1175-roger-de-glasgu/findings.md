# Roger de Glasgu

## Current conclusion

Roger witnessed a charter granting William Gley a toft in Glasgow and fishing rights in the Clyde. The charter is dated to Bishop Jocelin’s episcopate, c.1175–1199. Its witness list names Roger de Glasgu separately from Brother Roger the almoner.

## Sourced draft

[[Category:Glasgow Name Study]]
== Biography ==
Roger witnessed a charter granting William Gley a toft in Glasgow and fishing rights in the Clyde. The charter is dated to Bishop Jocelin’s episcopate, c.1175–1199. Its witness list names Roger de Glasgu separately from Brother Roger the almoner.<ref>Cosmo Innes, ed., ''Registrum Episcopatus Glasguensis'', vol. I (Edinburgh, 1843), no. 41, pp. 37–38; vol. II, index under “Glasgu, Rogerus de”; [https://archive.org/details/registrumepiscv175glasuoft Internet Archive].</ref>
