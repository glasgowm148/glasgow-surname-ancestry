== Biography ==
Richard was admitted to the Dublin merchant guild, probably in the late twelfth century. Membrane 2d/B of the roll records his payment of eight shillings. The next entry names Robert de Glasgu, but gives no relationship between them.<ref>Philomena Connolly and Geoffrey Martin, eds., ''The Dublin Guild Merchant Roll, c.1190–1265'' (Dublin: Dublin Corporation, 1992), membrane 2d/B; Dublin City Library and Archive, transcript by Philomena Connolly; [https://www.johngrenham.com/dcla/dmgr/browse.php?image_order=8 online transcript].</ref><ref>Patricia Becker, “An Analysis of the Dublin Guild Merchant Roll c.1190–1265” (M.Phil. thesis, Trinity College Dublin, 1996), appendix and improved place-name index; [https://www.tara.tcd.ie/items/7e36bba9-c81a-4964-8eba-58e1c682a323 Trinity College Dublin repository] and [https://www.tara.tcd.ie/bitstreams/005632d1-a582-4fb1-9278-99a49552ea92/download appendix PDF].</ref>
