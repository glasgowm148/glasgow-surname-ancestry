# Robert de Glascu

## Current conclusion

An English royal plea roll for Michaelmas 1200 names Robert among men said to have been present at a robbery at Dadinton. The place has not been securely identified. The entry does not establish that Robert committed the robbery.

== Biography ==
An English royal plea roll for Michaelmas 1200 names Robert among men said to have been present at a robbery at Dadinton. The place has not been securely identified. The entry does not establish that Robert committed the robbery.<ref>C. T. Flower, ed., ''Curia Regis Rolls of the Reigns of Richard I and John'', vol. I, Richard I–2 John (London: HMSO, 1922), p. 354, TNA KB 26/22, m. 1, Michaelmas term, 2 John (1200), naming ''Rob<sup>us</sup> de Glascu''; [https://books.google.com/books?id=SWRnAAAAMAAJ Google Books]. See also Paul Hyams, “Orality and Literacy in the Age of the Angevin Law Reforms,” in Richard W. Kaeuper, Paul Dingman and Peter Sposato, eds., ''Law, Governance, and Justice: New Views on Medieval Constitutionalism'' (Leiden: Brill, 2013), pp. 27–71, at p. 43 n. 48, doi:10.1163/9789004236424_004; [https://brill.com/display/book/edcoll/9789004236424/B9789004236424_004.xml Brill].</ref>

== Research Notes ==
Secure printed occurrence; identity with the Dublin Robert unproved. The Dublin guild member, the man in the English plea roll and Alexander’s father may overlap. The records do not establish their identity.
