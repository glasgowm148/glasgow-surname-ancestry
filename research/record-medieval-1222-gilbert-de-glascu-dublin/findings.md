== Biography ==
Gilbert, also rendered Gilibertus, paid eight shillings on admission to the Dublin merchant guild. His entry on membrane 11d/B falls before 1222/23. The roll gives no relationship to Richard or Robert de Glasgu.<ref>Connolly and Martin, ''Dublin Guild Merchant Roll'', membrane 11d/B; [https://www.johngrenham.com/dcla/dmgr/browse.php?image_order=22 online transcript].</ref><ref>Patricia Becker, “An Analysis of the Dublin Guild Merchant Roll c.1190–1265” (M.Phil. thesis, Trinity College Dublin, 1996), appendix and improved place-name index; [https://www.tara.tcd.ie/items/7e36bba9-c81a-4964-8eba-58e1c682a323 Trinity College Dublin repository] and [https://www.tara.tcd.ie/bitstreams/005632d1-a582-4fb1-9278-99a49552ea92/download appendix PDF].</ref>
