== Biography ==
A charter of Gilbert, bishop of Dunkeld, names Robert among his clerks. The act dates between 1229 and 1236. The fuller witness list survives in a copy made in 1423.<ref>D. E. Easson and Angus Macdonald, eds., ''Charters of the Abbey of Inchcolm'' (Edinburgh: Scottish History Society, 1938), no. XIV, pp. 12–13 and notes pp. 119–21. The expanded witness list containing ''Roberto de Glasgu'' survives in the 1423 transumpt; the editors note “Not identified”; [https://deriv.nls.uk/dcn23/1283/0085/128300859.23.pdf National Library of Scotland].</ref>

== Research Notes ==
Secure in a fifteenth-century transumpt; identity with other Roberts unproved. The Dunkeld clerk and the Kippax witness could be the same man. No shared office or associate identifies them.
