# Robert de Glascu, father of Alexander

## Current conclusion

Alexander’s admission to the Dublin merchant guild in 1238/39 identifies him as the son of Robert de Glascu. The byname belongs to Robert. The entry does not say that Alexander used it.

## Sourced draft

[[Category:Glasgow Name Study]]
== Biography ==
Alexander’s admission to the Dublin merchant guild in 1238/39 identifies him as the son of Robert de Glascu. The byname belongs to Robert. The entry does not say that Alexander used it.<ref>Connolly and Martin, ''Dublin Guild Merchant Roll'', membrane 27/B, admissions for 1238/39; [https://www.johngrenham.com/dcla/dmgr/browse.php?image_order=41 online transcript].</ref>

== Research Notes ==
Secure family-identifying use; not an additional direct working identity. The Dublin guild member, the man in the English plea roll and Alexander’s father may overlap. The records do not establish their identity.
