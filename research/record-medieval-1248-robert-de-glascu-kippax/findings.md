## Sourced draft

[[Category:Glasgow Name Study]]
== Biography ==
Robert witnessed an episcopal ordinance issued at Kippax on 30 January 1248/49. The witness list supplies his byname but gives him no office.<ref>DEEDS, charter 03320015, ''Durham 1241–1283'', vol. 29, transcribing Durham Cathedral Archive, Register II, fol. 321v.; [https://deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca/charters/03320015 DEEDS 03320015].</ref>

== Research Notes ==
Secure occurrence; office not stated. The Dunkeld clerk and the Kippax witness could be the same man. No shared office or associate identifies them.
