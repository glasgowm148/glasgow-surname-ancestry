# John de Glasgu

## Current conclusion

John appears as chaplain to Gamelin, bishop of St Andrews, on 2 February 1259. The register study also identifies him as a probable witness at Dunipace.

== Biography ==
John appears as chaplain to Gamelin, bishop of St Andrews, on 2 February 1259. The register study also identifies him as a probable witness at Dunipace.<ref>''Registrum Prioratus Sancti Andree'' (Bannatyne Club, Edinburgh, 1841), p. 173; [https://archive.org/details/libercartarump6900edinuoft Internet Archive]. See also People of Medieval Scotland, person no. 3404 and transaction factoid no. 15755, dating the renewal of Lathrisk and Kettle to 2 February 1259; [https://poms.ac.uk/record/person/3404/ PoMS].</ref><ref>''Registrum Monasterii S. Marie de Cambuskenneth'', no. 81; [https://deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca/charters/07280081 DEEDS 07280081]. See also People of Medieval Scotland, source no. 5639, suggesting a probable date in the 1250s or 1260s; [https://poms.ac.uk/record/source/5639/ PoMS].</ref>
