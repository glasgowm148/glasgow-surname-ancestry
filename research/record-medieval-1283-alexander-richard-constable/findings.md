# Alexander, son of Richard the former constable of Glasgow — 1283

## Current conclusion

The companion Paisley-register agreement defines a distinct neighbouring landholder and directly proves Richard, former constable of Glasgow, as his father. Pre-1500: maintain the [individual free-space draft](../../surname-research/free-space-pages/Alexander_son_of_Richard_constable_of_Glasgow.md); do not create a person profile.

## Source findings

* *Registrum Monasterii de Passelet*, pp. 384–385; [DEEDS 06190340](https://deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca/charters/06190340/). His property was the western boundary of the exchanged burgage. The exchanged property had formerly belonged to the deceased Cristiana Briane.

## Identity and relationship assessment

Richard → Alexander is **certain from the text**. Keep this record identity separate from Richard the messenger's son: the acts distinguish the fathers by office and the sons by role, but do not prove that the two families were unrelated. Glasgow may describe the father's office or location rather than hereditary surname use.

## Recommended next records

Trace the adjoining property and seek any act that equates Richard the former constable with Richard the messenger or distinguishes their sons further.
