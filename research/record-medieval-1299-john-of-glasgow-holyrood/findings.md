## Sourced draft

== Biography ==
John was one of the Holyrood religious who swore fealty to Edward I on 7 January 1299. The record does not identify him with Bishop Gamelin’s chaplain of forty years earlier.<ref>Joseph Bain, ed., ''Calendar of Documents Relating to Scotland'', vol. II (Edinburgh, 1884), no. 1052; [https://poms.ac.uk/record/person/14528/ PoMS].</ref>

== Research Notes ==
Secure single occurrence; identity with Gamelin's chaplain unproved.
