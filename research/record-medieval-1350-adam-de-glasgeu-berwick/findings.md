== Biography ==
Adam, a burgess of Berwick, granted land at Hiddeslaw in the tenement of Bondington. The grant is undated and has been assigned broadly to the fourteenth century.<ref>Cosmo Innes, ed., ''Liber Sancte Marie de Melros: Munimenta Vetustiora Monasterii Cisterciensis de Melros'', vol. 2 (Edinburgh, 1837), Appendix no. 25, p. 688; [https://saints.hca.ed.ac.uk/entry/EN/JD/3830 Adam de Glasgeu] and [https://saints.hca.ed.ac.uk/document/DO/JD/2961 document record].</ref>
