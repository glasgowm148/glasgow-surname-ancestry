# John de Glasgow alias Smith — 1506

## Current conclusion

The catalogue securely identifies John by both Glasgow and Smith in a joint four-shilling-rent acquisition. It proves no occupation and no relationship to Sir Allan Smith. The only explicit descent is Robert Watson → Agnes. Maintain this pre-1500-born subject on the [canonical free-space draft](../../surname-research/free-space-pages/John_Glasgow_of_Saltmarket.md), not a person profile.

## Source findings

* [Glasgow University Archives GUA BL/204 catalogue description](https://yourscottisharchives.com/catalogues/565b7ef3-fc87-3f6f-aaac-149925faa39d?component-id=7cdbf375-dc6d-3de3-b53a-86376e2106da&terms=%22Theses%22), instrument dated 28 April 1506. Agnes, daughter and heir of Robert Watson, disposed the rent to Sir Allan Smith and John de Glasgow alias Smith; Allan's property was the northern boundary. The catalogue's legacy wording includes the malformed `Watsonm`, which must be checked against the instrument. The complete instrument was not retrieved, so the terms of joint acquisition, survivorship and any omitted associates or relationships remain unknown.

## Identity and relationship assessment

John's dual designation is **secure at catalogue level**. Sir Allan Smith → John is **unsupported**. Identity with the 1527–1553 Saltmarket holder is **plausible but unproved**.
