# Michael Glasgow — 1756 documentary account

## Current conclusion

The National Archives catalogue describes an order sent by Thomas Frankland at English Harbour to the Naval Officer at Antigua on 3 January 1756, directing the discharge of Michael Glasgow and three other men for incompetence and preventing their entry to the yard. This describes the order; it does not prove when the discharge was carried out. The catalogue gives Michael no age, birthplace, family or specific trade.

Michael Glasgow ([Glasgow-1410](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1410)), baptised at Irvine in 1712, remains chronologically compatible. Michael Glasgow ([Glasgow-1416](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1416)), born 19 November 1741, would have been fourteen; the catalogue gives neither age nor occupation, so this is not an exclusion. Michael Glasgow ([Glasgow-1406](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1406)), recorded as an Irvine skinner in 1679, would be very old in 1756 but is not excluded merely from a before-birth bound. No record bridges any of these Irvine men to Antigua. [Glasgow-3911](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3911) belongs to the sixteenth century and [Glasgow-3595](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3595) was born in 1815, concrete chronological conflicts.
