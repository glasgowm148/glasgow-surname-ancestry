# Michael Glasgow — 1797 documentary account

## Current conclusion

The National Archives catalogue indexes Michael Glasgow, aged 40, a supernumerary seaman, on the sick list from 16 March 1797 with venereal gonorrhoea, subsequently discharged to his ship. No discharge date or name of that ship is supplied for Michael. The parent description identifies the journal as that of HMS Gladiator, kept by surgeon H Maybee from 5 January 1797 to 25 February 1798 while Gladiator served as a convalescent ship in Portsmouth Harbour. This identifies the treatment setting, not Michael’s normal ship.

The recorded age 40 implies birth approximately 1756–1757, if accurate. [Glasgow-1416](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1416), born 19 November 1741, would be 55 on 16 March 1797, a concrete age conflict; do not assign this record to him without evidence correcting the reported age. [Glasgow-1410](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1410) was baptised in 1712 and [Glasgow-1406](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1406) was already recorded in 1679, also incompatible with an accurate age 40. [Glasgow-3911](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3911) belongs to the sixteenth century; [Glasgow-3595](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3595) was born in 1815 and [Glasgow-4252](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-4252) in 1889. No compatible maintained Michael profile was established. Wider live duplicate search and the original journal remain outstanding, so this is a source-verification hold, not an authorized live creation.
