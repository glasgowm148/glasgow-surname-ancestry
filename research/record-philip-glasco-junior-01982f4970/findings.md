# Philip Glasco junior — catalogue identity review

Updated: 2026-08-11

## Current conclusion

The 1768 marriage and 1770 death define the younger Dublin watchmaker and map directly to [Glasgow-3932](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3932). The catalogue now uses that profile as the canonical record identity. [Glasco-147](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasco-147) is a duplicate candidate for the younger man; [Glasco-146](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasco-146) is dated as the older generation but contains younger Philip's events and is conflated.

## Profile assessment

- `Glasgow-3932` contains both newspaper events and represents the younger man.
- `Glasco-147` represents Philip the younger as a son of `Glasco-146`, relying on the probate index.
- `Glasco-146` is dated as the older generation but its biography also includes the younger man's 1768 marriage and 1770 death, so it is conflated.

## Recommended action

Keep the catalogue linked to `Glasgow-3932`. Review `Glasco-147` for merger into the canonical younger profile and remove the younger man's 1768 marriage and 1770 death from the conflated elder profile `Glasco-146`.
