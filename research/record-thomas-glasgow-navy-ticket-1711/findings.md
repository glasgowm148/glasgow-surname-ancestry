# Thomas Glasgow, ticket sent after death (1711)

## Current conclusion

The official catalogue for ADM 106/663/238 says Captain Hemington sent the ticket of Thomas Glasgow, who had died, on 9 February 1711. Thomas was therefore deceased by that date. No precise death day, ship, occupation or personal locality is supplied.

The catalogue gives no age, ship, rank, trade, birthplace, family or death place. Tower Hill identifies Captain Hemington’s writing location, not Thomas’s residence or death. None of the checked Thomas accounts is positively identified. Retain chronologically compatible candidates; missing naval details or locality alone do not exclude them. Glasgow-3943 has a later concrete death date 6 October 1725 and cannot be this deceased 1711 subject if that date belongs to him. Glasgow-1103’s circa 1675 death remains an estimated conflict, not a firm exclusion.
