## Leith access result

NRS identifies South Leith as OPR 692/2, with early register coverage beginning
in 1599. The four 1654-1660 child-record dates therefore lie within the broad
surviving coverage; this does not prove that every particular entry survives
or that each belongs to this parish. Confirm the parish on every target.

A secondary film catalogue points to GS film 1067770 for an early South Leith
register and films 1067770-1067781 for the broader parish-register/index run.
