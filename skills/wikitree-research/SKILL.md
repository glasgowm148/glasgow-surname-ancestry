# WikiTree Research

- Catalogue synchronization (owner correction, 15 October 2026): after a targeted research sync, rebuild the browser's compact search index, detail shards and worker with `tools.catalogue_search_index.refresh`, and refresh their content-versioned URLs. Verify the actual `needsUpdate` filter membership/count against the full index; a current profile page or full JSON index alone does not prove that the catalogue UI is current.

Check documentary claims against the underlying record. Distinguish documented facts, logic-based working inferences, and open alternatives; direct proof is not a prerequisite for an explicitly uncertain working connection.

- Glasgow ancestry programme: prioritise connecting the oldest surname branches into one evidence-led working family tree. Work from the earliest surviving records, especially before 1650, and keep post-1850 work subordinate unless it can resolve an earlier branch connection. Extend defensible tentative connections with their actual uncertainty rather than leaving compatible branches isolated. Maintain the reproducible paternal-connectivity baseline, sizeable unattached family heads and early connection queue in `research/ancestry-connections/`; refresh `/onetree/progress/` after a connection batch. Track confidently marked and working links separately. The owner's reported common Y-DNA origin and circa 1400–1450 (possibly earlier) founder model guide the research; they do not supply missing generations, identify every untested branch, or establish a named father. A conceptual common-origin link must never lower the count of unresolved genealogical connections.

- Working connections (owner correction, 15 October 2026): when asked to connect branches, use the existing site evidence to select and materialise the most plausible compatible parent or identity hypothesis. Assess generations, actual versus estimated dates, locality, households, occupations and naming together; do not demand a literal parent clause or seek further sources before making a useful uncertain model. Explain why the selected option is preferred, retain competing options and counterevidence, and leave mothers independently assessed. Put selected hypotheses in durable structured assessments and the actual local tree/network/working metrics, not only prose or a research queue. Keep confidently marked measurements unchanged and live WikiTree captures separate. Missing corroboration or omission from a testament is not a concrete exclusion. Never invent generations, ignore a real date conflict, or choose a link solely to improve the score. For this scoped working-link overlay, use targeted affected builders and verification; a full catalogue rebuild is unnecessary unless the change actually affects the wider catalogue pipeline.

- For newspaper surname research, verify that the inspected passage names a person with the surname before retaining it as a surname record. “Of Glasgow”, city datelines, destinations and vessel names are search exclusions, not Glasgow people. A quoted forename/surname search or OCR match alone is insufficient. Keep temporary reading captures and exclusion logs separate from retained surname sources; report inspected hits, retained surname records and unresolved readings separately. Do not exclude an entire page merely because it also contains a Glasgow place reference.
- Sources publication: organise collected evidence under `/sources/` by record type, jurisdiction and supported record year, with original-provider links and specific coverage/loss limits. When the user explicitly requests inline historical newspaper excerpts, publish only visually checked bounded clippings listed in the hash-pinned `research/source-library/newspaper-clippings.json`; keep full browser captures and OCR private. Do not infer permission to publish other restricted captures. Preserve family conclusions, profile assessments and old deep-link routes when moving source tables.
  When asked to save all newspaper excerpts, audit every retained record and complete the full set rather than a sample. Verify each published crop contains its claimed passage and is legible at full size. Report actual source-access blockers explicitly; do not count unresolved search hits or rejected city/vessel matches as retained records, or give those logs the same missing-excerpt message as accepted evidence. Check the complete retained set for missing images before declaring delivery.
  For repeated newspaper texts, group only exact readings after case and whitespace normalization unless a maintained source explicitly defines a duplicate group; preserve meaningful punctuation, every issue date, original link, image anchor and source-page count, and show one default crop with alternative issue crops in expandable details. Keep distinct-notice counts separate from source-page counts. Profile links require curated per-person confidence, role and evidence basis; prose, namesake similarity or a research-draft link never upgrades identity, and unresolved candidates remain labelled possible or unknown.
  Newspaper cards lead with the documentary subject's name, supported place and record year, followed by useful occupation and stated kin. Maintain `research/source-library/newspaper-targets.json` alongside the reviewed readings; a changed reading invalidates its old target hash. Show a sourced birth date or clearly explained estimated cutoff separately from candidate-profile dates; an adult-role estimate must state its age assumption and never become a sourced birth fact. Do not derive an occupation from an honorific such as “Master”. Keep original clippings, full readings, identity reasons and issue alternatives reachable. The Clippings dropdown includes relationship and kin-type filters driven by curated positive family statements in the record, never negated wording, candidate biographies or keyword matches.
  Person findings: keep one overall `## Current conclusion` current (add `Reviewed: YYYY-MM-DD`); a dated or topic `## Current conclusion — <topic>, <date>` is an addition to it, never a second overall conclusion. Every other `## ` section is published unless its heading marks an editing log (patterns in `FINDINGS_INTERNAL_HEADINGS`, `tools/build_research_catalog.py`); text above the first `## ` is never published, so cite sources in published sections, not only under WikiTree instructions. Record a finding in the file of the person it is about and cross-reference it from the relatives it concerns. After any findings, register or queue change, run `make check-research` (or `tools/check_research_publication.py --id <IDs>`) and fix or consciously baseline what it reports before saying the work is published; also check that the new citation appears in the person's rendered HTML and JSON. When findings correct a reading that may sit in a record row, correct `research/canonical_records.json` too and list the old reading in `data/site/superseded-readings.json`. WikiTree register entries use the forms the parser reads: `| [Name (ID)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/ID) | OPEN | change |` or `- [[ID]] — change` under a dated section. Check links with `make check-links`.
  Rebuilding the local site: after changing a builder or many research files, run `make rebuild-local` (`tools/rebuild_local_site.sh`, about an hour, local only, never deploys). It fixes the order: section pages, then the research sync and every person page, then the shared shell, then `finalize`, then the research, release, link, site-experience, asset and privacy checks. Do not run `tools/publish_clean_urls.py`, `tools/repair_site_experience.py` or the full catalogue build on their own to refresh one thing: each rewrites pages other tools have since improved. Refresh place pages with `tools/refresh_place_pages.py`; the records index is written only by the full catalogue build, so a corrected record shows in research text at once and in record lists only after that build. A queued WikiTree update whose captured profile text names a living person or a DNA tester is kept off the public page automatically and stays in the register for the owner.
  Census collections must group by the enumeration year and jurisdiction, excluding citation access/review dates, archive numbers and subjects' birthplaces. Retain all explicitly supported enumeration years in a shared reference and a visible route to unresolved years. Show the linked public people as well as reference counts; neither count establishes unique households, enumerated surname entries or a complete archive search.
  Search thumbnails are leads: before retaining a person, inspect an original-page passage wide enough to show the complete name and punctuation within one notice or column. A forename in a neighbouring advertisement and Glasgow as a city are not a surname occurrence, even when the search thumbnail highlights both. Keep cropped or cross-column readings pending until that context is visible.
  When saving a provider JPG through the native browser, verify the actual newly downloaded file, including numeric filename suffixes. A reused base filename can point to an older cropped download. Confirm complete page dimensions/context and source metadata before using the file; filenames alone do not prove source or completeness.
  During parallel newspaper research, use as many working tabs as the active jobs need, then close each agent-created search, viewer, download-error and preview tab as soon as its job is done. Share saved captures for offline work and avoid duplicate browsing. Check the task tab count after cleanup; do not accumulate retained tabs across turns.
  For early Irish newspaper coverage, first extract the catalogue's people documented or plausibly active in Ireland in the requested period, with their dates, places and recorded forename variants. Prioritize the earliest requested period (pre-1800 before later material). Search quoted full names and scan the words immediately around Glasgow in result previews to triage likely surname occurrences quickly. Open the original only for promising or ambiguous hits; log obvious city/vessel previews separately without treating them as fully verified exclusions. Record each query's date/geographic bounds and retrieval/review progress. Use broader surname/place searches to fill identified gaps after the catalogue-led quoted-name pass, not as the initial exhaustive method.

- Profile update comparisons must keep the complete captured and replacement biographies intact and show them in an accessible, GitHub-style side-by-side diff: unchanged lines aligned in both columns, removed lines red on the current-profile side, added lines green on the replacement side, with line numbers, escaped text, and horizontal scrolling with visible side labels on narrow screens.
  Audit complete current and effective replacement texts passage by passage before marking a draft reviewed. Source URLs surviving does not prove preservation: account for facts, dates, places, occupations, quoted narratives, every named relative/associate and profile link, research hypotheses, contradictions and source limitations. Check pending-work descriptions against the current biography. Document supported replacements and retain conflicting attributed evidence in Research Notes; invalidate a preservation review when either reviewed text changes.
  Collapse the Paste-ready revised profile section and full-copy biography by default. Filtering must never shorten either stored biography or the copied replacement; show identical biographies explicitly rather than an empty changes view.
  Manual/structured amendments and drafts blocked by profile-manager scope must retain that comparison whenever both texts exist. Those labels describe the action required, not biography equality; never claim the biography is already updated from the label alone. If the capture is unavailable, state that explicitly instead of treating an empty capture as the current profile.

- When verifying search filters, open every panel on desktop and mobile and check that its lower controls remain visible or reachable by scrolling within the panel. Check actual pointer visibility and operation, including Gender; DOM presence alone does not detect menus clipped by an ancestor.

- For a documented sibling pair sharing the paternal surname, a proved father of one sibling can support a confident inferred father for the other when chronology fits and no concrete alternative parentage or conflicting evidence is known. Do not keep that father link uncertain solely because the record omits “full” before “sister” or “brother”. Explain the combined evidence as an inference; assess maternity separately and do not turn an unsupported half-sibling possibility into an active competing hypothesis.

- Favour supported family connections over orphaned profiles. When proved siblings already have a plausible shared father attached to one of them, assess extending that attachment to the others rather than leaving them disconnected solely because no record names their parent directly. Preserve the father's actual evidence grade: an uncertain father remains uncertain across the group; a proved father may support the inference described above. Check chronology and concrete conflicts, explain the inherited evidence, and assess each mother separately. Never invent an anonymous parent solely to force a link.

- The Free-space library is a full local second edition: publish public-safe study bodies and original citations, browse by category and importance, retain WikiTree links and dated synchronization comparisons. Preserve local corrections when pulling remote changes; prepare outgoing full replacements and diffs without claiming an unsaved WikiTree change is live. Represent blank source pages honestly and omit only specific private/living-person or personal DNA-match material.

- Historical relationship audits must reconcile the findings ledger with dated profile findings, structured assessments and saved before/after live correction logs. A ledger-only count is not a history audit. Preserve actual prior grades: uncertain/unknown and explicitly unmarked are distinct. Documented unmarked → Confident edits can appear as newly marked certain, labelled previously ungraded; an absent earlier record alone does not establish a change. Keep supported inferences and later identity limits visible, rather than requiring a literal parent clause for every confident conclusion. Show both finding-batch and individual-relationship counts.
- Keep branch maps immediately after the page introduction, ahead of long audit/lead panels. Clicking a map background dismisses its preview; preserve area links, zoom controls and drag gestures.
- Maintain the People catalogue's central held/pending research queue from the older draft register, documentary-subject handoffs, pending profile amendments and current research questions. Reconcile completed/retired drafts before listing them and link every item to its full evidence. Rebuild it after each coherent batch; do not make readers search individual branch pages for all holds.

- A held-research matching batch must review every baseline queue item and record its disposition: matched profile, surviving compatible candidate, no compatible profile found with actual search scope, unnamed record, superseded draft, or an already-linked amendment/study. Keep record-specific matches separate within shared-name groups. Replace rejected name-score leads with checked candidates, retire drafts already represented by profiles, and show a dated profile-match assessment in the central queue. A relationship or source question may stay open after the person is matched; do not label that person unmatched because the wider research remains unfinished.
