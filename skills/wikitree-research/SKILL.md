# WikiTree Research

Verify each claimed identity, relationship, date, and place against the underlying record. Distinguish documented facts, evidence-based inferences, and hypotheses.

- For newspaper surname research, verify that the inspected passage names a person with the surname before retaining it as a surname record. “Of Glasgow”, city datelines, destinations and vessel names are search exclusions, not Glasgow people. A quoted forename/surname search or OCR match alone is insufficient. Keep temporary reading captures and exclusion logs separate from retained surname sources; report inspected hits, retained surname records and unresolved readings separately. Do not exclude an entire page merely because it also contains a Glasgow place reference.

- Before ending a turn that changes project files, verify and commit the completed changes with a concise, descriptive Conventional Commit message. Commit coherent batches separately when useful; never push, create empty commits, or include unrelated pre-existing changes. The orchestrator stages and commits delegated work. Report verification or commit blockers explicitly.

- Complete the active research/profile/site-integration batch before starting separately queued follow-up work. If the owner says later requests come afterwards, save them in the project queue and finish the current evidence, drafts, synchronization, verification and package first.

- Keep `/latest-findings/` current during research: after every coherent batch, update `research/latest-findings.json` and the affected person/study accounts, then rebuild the findings page before moving on or handing off. Every named research subject needs a checked existing profile, explicitly linked compatible candidate, or complete sourced public draft. Check names, chronology, places and relatives properly; reject a plausible match only for concrete conflicting evidence, not missing fields or an unproved additional bridge. Consolidate compatible records and reuse drafts. Record genuine relationship upgrades with their evidenced previous uncertain/unknown status and new certain status; already-certain links and newly found supporting sources are not upgrades. Follow the routed references for exact ledger/draft fields and publication.

- Write public pages in plain genealogy and WikiTree language. Prefer Biography, Research Notes, family tree, records, source references, possible parents and next records to check over dossiers, atlas, canopy, workspace, provenance and abstract process labels. Lead with the person, family or research question and give clear routes to the supporting records. Simplify presentation without deleting citations, historical detail, uncertainty, earlier assessments or specialist tools; explain necessary technical terms where readers first meet them. Maintain wording in the builders or rendering inputs so rebuilds preserve it.

- Profile update comparisons must keep the complete captured and replacement biographies intact and show them in an accessible, GitHub-style side-by-side diff: unchanged lines aligned in both columns, removed lines red on the current-profile side, added lines green on the replacement side, with line numbers, escaped text, and horizontal scrolling with visible side labels on narrow screens.
  Audit complete current and effective replacement texts passage by passage before marking a draft reviewed. Source URLs surviving does not prove preservation: account for facts, dates, places, occupations, quoted narratives, every named relative/associate and profile link, research hypotheses, contradictions and source limitations. Check pending-work descriptions against the current biography. Document supported replacements and retain conflicting attributed evidence in Research Notes; invalidate a preservation review when either reviewed text changes.
  Collapse the Paste-ready revised profile section and full-copy biography by default. When expanded, default the comparison to Changes only, hiding unchanged rows with a reversible accessible control. Filtering must never shorten either stored biography or the copied replacement; show identical biographies explicitly rather than an empty changes view.
  Manual/structured amendments and drafts blocked by profile-manager scope must retain that comparison whenever both texts exist. Those labels describe the action required, not biography equality; never claim the biography is already updated from the label alone. If the capture is unavailable, state that explicitly instead of treating an empty capture as the current profile.

- Preserve the agreed homepage order: reading guide, study map, then Alexander's research example. Label the full-tree control "Whole tree" and keep local tree tabs and the section submenu ordered Whole tree, Branch cards, Timeline, Y-DNA (Map follows in the submenu). Apply these rules after restoring older research markup as well as during presentation repairs.

- Complete website presentation fixes with a fresh verified `www/glasgow-site-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.tar.gz` for the owner's server upload. Follow `docs/public-catalogue-deployment.md`, report the archive path and exact byte size plus decimal MB, and distinguish packaging from live deployment.
- When verifying search filters, open every panel on desktop and mobile and check that its lower controls remain visible or reachable by scrolling within the panel. Check actual pointer visibility and operation, including Gender; DOM presence alone does not detect menus clipped by an ancestor.

- For a documented sibling pair sharing the paternal surname, a proved father of one sibling can support a confident inferred father for the other when chronology fits and no concrete alternative parentage or conflicting evidence is known. Do not keep that father link uncertain solely because the record omits “full” before “sister” or “brother”. Explain the combined evidence as an inference; assess maternity separately and do not turn an unsupported half-sibling possibility into an active competing hypothesis.

- Favour supported family connections over orphaned profiles. When proved siblings already have a plausible shared father attached to one of them, assess extending that attachment to the others rather than leaving them disconnected solely because no record names their parent directly. Preserve the father's actual evidence grade: an uncertain father remains uncertain across the group; a proved father may support the inference described above. Check chronology and concrete conflicts, explain the inherited evidence, and assess each mother separately. Never invent an anonymous parent solely to force a link.

- When a user directs an audit to be additive, prioritize newly found records and sourced conclusions. Preserve existing profile text, citations, structured dates and family links; add a clearly qualified research note for conflicts. Do not turn the audit into removals, detachment or citation pruning unless the user later asks for a specific change.

- Keep the transcription index compact: short display names, record year with a concise supported place beneath it, populated kin fields only, and a link to the full reading. Put detailed dates, court/image references, source-availability notes and interpretation on case pages, not preview cards. Place the illustrated-reading guide after the catalogue, and do not add an index-wide original-pages availability panel. Maintain display labels separately from full research titles and evidence.

- Coloured teaching-font overlays must share the base word's text origin, padding, font metrics and wrapping. Check actual glyph registration with colour groups enabled on desktop and mobile; overlapping outer boxes do not demonstrate aligned lettering.
- Teaching-font colours attach to explicit character/glyph mappings, never horizontal-width masks of the whole word. Manuscript region masks remain separate and must not be represented as literal glyph tracing; keep neutral and coloured typography consistent.

- When a supplementary manuscript concerns a distinct estate but its family connection is untested, classify it as associated research material, not a rejected match or an unrelated family. Keep record boundaries separate from genealogical conclusions. Synchronize revised classifications and accepted names across case pages, family-summary cards, audit labels and downloadable data; correcting explanatory prose alone does not retire a stale status.

- After an approved public research batch, use the targeted `tools/sync_catalogue_research.py --write` workflow or completed `tools/publish_research_release.py`; these refresh local QMD/PDF search automatically. A daily 03:00 local fallback catches missed updates. QMD covers locally known Open, non-living profiles through public summaries/basic fields, with bounded lexical excerpts and embedded original JSON pointers. Raw case notes and PDFs still require reviewed hashes. Never index living/private captures, raw DNA or restricted source content; verify original evidence before citing search/OCR/compressed text. See `docs/genealogy-tooling.md` for status verification and manual refresh.

## Routed references

Before substantive research or identity/relationship judgments, read Identity and evidence. Also load Profile workflows before preparing/editing profiles, Catalogue and site before findings/site synchronization, and Transcription before source-reading work. Load only the applicable references:

- [Identity and evidence](references/identity-and-evidence.md): research batches, platform boundaries, attached people and locations, identity/duplicate/relationship analysis, group descriptions, provenance, Glasgow scope, dates, chronology and WT+ location suggestions.
- [Profile workflows](references/profile-workflows.md): preparing WikiTree material, profile markup and links, new-person drafts, draft retirement, and live WikiTree browser edits.
- [Catalogue and site](references/catalogue-and-site.md): findings, catalogue pages, transcription publication, website synchronization, rendering, accessibility and local site verification.
- [Transcription](references/transcription.md): manuscript teaching, corrections, testament coverage, structured kin previews, missing originals, manuscript fragments and archival confirmations.

The references preserve the original heading sections and their substantive instructions verbatim. Do not infer that a moved section is optional: load the relevant reference before acting.

- The Free-space library is a full local second edition: publish public-safe study bodies and original citations, browse by category and importance, retain WikiTree links and dated synchronization comparisons. Preserve local corrections when pulling remote changes; prepare outgoing full replacements and diffs without claiming an unsaved WikiTree change is live. Represent blank source pages honestly and omit only specific private/living-person or personal DNA-match material.

- Historical relationship audits must reconcile the findings ledger with dated profile findings, structured assessments and saved before/after live correction logs. A ledger-only count is not a history audit. Preserve actual prior grades: uncertain/unknown and explicitly unmarked are distinct. Documented unmarked → Confident edits can appear as newly marked certain, labelled previously ungraded; an absent earlier record alone does not establish a change. Keep supported inferences and later identity limits visible, rather than requiring a literal parent clause for every confident conclusion. Show both finding-batch and individual-relationship counts.
- Keep branch maps immediately after the page introduction, ahead of long audit/lead panels. Clicking a map background dismisses its preview; preserve area links, zoom controls and drag gestures.
- Maintain the People catalogue's central held/pending research queue from the older draft register, documentary-subject handoffs, pending profile amendments and current research questions. Reconcile completed/retired drafts before listing them and link every item to its full evidence. Rebuild it after each coherent batch; do not make readers search individual branch pages for all holds.

- A held-research matching batch must review every baseline queue item and record its disposition: matched profile, surviving compatible candidate, no compatible profile found with actual search scope, unnamed record, superseded draft, or an already-linked amendment/study. Keep record-specific matches separate within shared-name groups. Replace rejected name-score leads with checked candidates, retire drafts already represented by profiles, and show a dated profile-match assessment in the central queue. A relationship or source question may stay open after the person is matched; do not label that person unmatched because the wider research remains unfinished.
