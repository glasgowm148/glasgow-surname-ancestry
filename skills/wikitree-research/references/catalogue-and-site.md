# WikiTree Research — Catalogue And Site

This reference preserves the original project skill sections for tasks in this area. Read it when the request matches the section headings below.

## Maintain findings and catalogue person pages

- After each coherent research batch, record new concrete discoveries and
  supported corrections as findings recorded with `tools/research_entry.py upsert finding` (they appear in `research/latest-findings.json`), with the actual
  discovery/review date, inspected source links, affected public people and
  explicit evidence limits. A confirmed record fact does not make an uncertain
  identity or relationship certain. Deduplicate a shared finding across
  profiles; exclude negative searches, speculative rankings, routine source
  tidying and website/build changes. Do not manufacture a discovery date from
  a file timestamp. Synchronize the relevant dossiers/studies first, then run
  `tools/build_latest_findings.py` so readers can see the new facts in the
  newest-first Latest findings table.

- Every named subject in a finding must link to a checked existing profile, a
  compatible candidate with its assessed role, or a complete sourced public
  draft. Reuse the consolidated new-person/evidence draft; synchronize its
  public page and creation/update queue before linking it. Never use an empty
  People cell or “No matched profile” as a substitute for duplicate checking
  and drafting. Keep genuinely unnamed record subjects identified as such;
  do not invent a named person from an anonymous index entry.
- For findings that resolve a relationship, maintain `relationship_changes`
  with `relationship`, `subject`, `relative`, `before` (`unknown`,
  `uncertain`, or documented `unmarked`), `after` (`certain`), `explanation`, and internal
  `previous_status_provenance`. Capture the previous assessment at research
  time; do not infer it merely from a missing ledger field. Assess each
  relationship separately from unrelated identity/date uncertainty in the
  row. Already-certain WikiTree links, source confirmations, and explicit kin
  clauses whose proposed profile identity remains unresolved do not count as
  a newly certain profile relationship. The table offers both relationship
  and father-only upgrade filters; a father-only research goal counts only
  genuine father upgrades. Review these fields and rebuild the page after
  each coherent batch, including subagent work.
  Preserve unmarked as a previously ungraded status, not a fabricated uncertain
  assessment. Saved live before/after edits provide its provenance; a missing
  earlier note alone does not. Display individual relationship counts as well
  as batches, and retain the separately filterable previously ungraded group.
- Assess the claim made by the finding itself. Unknown parents or a separate
  unresolved question do not downgrade a proved sibling, spouse or vital fact.
  Keep unrelated limits visible without making the proved claim uncertain.
  When a new candidate or match supersedes a “no compatible profile” assessment,
  update the maintained record review and its public study as well as this table;
  preserve the earlier assessment explicitly as dated history.

- Manuscript boxes must enclose complete letterforms, including ascenders, descenders, abbreviation strokes and trailing flourishes, with a small visible margin. Never fit only the word's body or mechanically expand every box by the same percentage. Where lines overlap, allow contextual strokes and explain them; do not truncate the target word to avoid overlap. Keep numbers and controls outside the handwriting and show one selected outline at a time when full-stroke boxes overlap.
- For illustrated testament readings, provide magnified, unaltered word samples and image-specific letterform explanations that a secretary-hand novice can compare with the source. Separate literal reading, expanded/modern form, observed strokes, same-clerk comparisons and unresolved alternatives. Do not manufacture visual features or use an expected legal formula as proof of unreadable letters. Organise the transcription index by record-keeping court area and ascending record year, showing the year prominently and distinguishing it from death dates.
- A letter guide must identify its word, letter position and exact source-pixel region, with a wider passage locator and an original-only default. Align any coloured path to visible ink, not to an expected letter shape; do not trace overlapping or unreadable strokes as certain. Withhold unaligned guides rather than presenting decorative curves as evidence. Prefer source-specific comparisons from supplied examples and the same document; label teaching alphabets separately and never use them as proof of the reading.
- When the user supplies a saved full-text/machine transcription of a record, continue with it in the same task: assess and record its content, clearly labelled as a transcription with its provenance and OCR-error risks. Do not block on obtaining the original image, and do not spawn image-verification agents when no supplied image path exists. Schedule a source-image visual check as a follow-up, and treat any image-only conclusion as unverified until that check.
- Follow the project AGENTS.md cost-aware model routing for manuscript vision, letterform alignment and visual review of transcription walkthroughs; do not use Astra. Make important kin names independently inspectable through numbered source-crop reading steps, comparisons and explicit alternatives, not generic alphabet descriptions. Reproduce requested transcription UI problems in a real browser and inspect the rebuilt desktop/mobile views and reading controls before handing back; a successful local build alone does not satisfy the visual check.
- Letterform teaching must pair the actual manuscript with toggleable, clearly labelled coloured stroke guides and sourced comparative letterforms. Keep original pixels unchanged, locate traces on the exact displayed image, preserve full selected strokes and explain untraced intersections or damage. Trace only visible diagnostic ink; never reconstruct missing strokes, imply a proven pen-stroke order, or present a guide as the original ink.
- There is no single universal normal secretary hand. Label published alphabet forms as teaching variants, identify their source, and explain the manuscript's specific matches and differences. Distinguish a same-clerk comparison from another writer's exemplar; any assembled model word must be labelled a teaching reconstruction, never documentary evidence. Retain separate literal reading, expansion, confidence, alternative readings and genealogical consequences.
- On the transcription index, allow year labels their intrinsic width with explicit spacing before a divider. Do not place four-digit years inside a fixed narrow track whose padding or border overlaps the text.
- Website delivery is local: regenerate the site's local HTML and assets after changes, leaving upload to the user unless separately requested. Describe this as a local rebuild, not publishing or deployment; never imply that rebuilding uploaded the site.

- Anchor manuscript highlights to the exact exported image displayed by the page, using its actual pixel dimensions and crop origin. Locate every labelled word visually before converting pixel bounds to percentages; never estimate positions from a different crop, a page thumbnail or remembered line order. Split phrases that wrap across lines into separately labelled boxes. If a crop omits the quoted words, replace the excerpt or remove the unsupported callout. Inspect a rendered overlay proof for every passage against the actual handwriting before publishing; valid numeric ranges alone are not alignment evidence. Recheck annotation placement whenever the image crop changes.
- Publish saved wills/testament transcription work through `/research/transcriptions/`, using the maintained transcription builder. Include document reference, associated profiles, latest saved reading, revision provenance and unresolved passages. Keep LLM-assisted transcription distinct from interpretation and source-verified conclusions; preserve uncertainty marks, do not invent missing full text, and attribute a particular model only where its provenance is recorded. New or corrected readings must update this section in the same research task, not remain only in chat or a free-space draft.
- Make public testament transcriptions image-led guided readings, not text-only reports. Lead with focused original-manuscript crops and named-person/relationship signposts; pair each key passage with its literal reading, modern rendering, explanation of the visible spelling or kinship wording, and evidential limits. Highlight names separately from relationship words and uncertain text using removable overlays; preserve unaltered crops, image references and archive attribution. Never invent letterform comparisons, missing-page screenshots or manuscript wording. Keep full transcripts, revision logs and technical provenance accessible beneath the walkthrough. Where original images are unavailable, say so rather than imply the reading is illustrated.
- Publication audits must preserve authorized historical transcription detail, manuscript previews, reading tools and source links. Record explicit asset exceptions in the maintained publication approval manifest and apply them during generation and release checks; do not let a generic licensed-source export setting silently replace approved illustrated readings with summaries or withheld notices. Verify the restored images and reading controls in the browser. This exception does not authorize unrelated private material.
- Treat the research catalogue as the canonical current research account and shared model; other pages are linked views of its people, records, relationships and conclusions. Follow `docs/website-research-sync.md` for the maintained inputs and existing relationship/confidence fields. Research done anywhere in this repository must enter that model and the affected public-safe narrative, data, full drafts and open questions within the same task. Reconcile existing accounts and reuse their IDs before adding a person, study or register; do not leave substantive discoveries only in findings files, free-space drafts, chat or agent handoffs.
- Synchronize after each coherent batch, not only when asked for a rebuild. Track affected people, records, research groups, regional summaries, open questions and any relevant DNA/timeline claims. Update every affected maintained source and rendered surface; resolving a question must also update the pages that still present it as open.
- Free-space indexing publishes metadata and links, not manuscript bodies. Integrate material conclusions into the site's relevant research account as well as linking the canonical free-space source. Research groups and open questions currently require explicit curated-source updates; do not assume the builders discover them automatically.
- For scans of recovered files or archive dumps, integrate useful findings directly into the relevant person, family, locality or subject accounts. Keep inventory and audit handoffs internal; do not create a standalone public research-audit page or audit discovery panels unless the user explicitly requests one.
- After research materially affects a person, update `research/<WikiTree-ID>/findings.md`; do not leave the substantive result only in chat, an agent note, or another file.
- Keep one overall `## Current conclusion` current; dated or topic conclusions (`## Current conclusion — <topic>, <date>`) are additions to it. Every other `## ` section is published, whatever its heading, unless the heading marks an editing log (`FINDINGS_INTERNAL_HEADINGS` in `tools/build_research_catalog.py`); text above the first `## ` heading is never published. Run `make check-research` (or `tools/check_research_publication.py --id <IDs>`) after editing findings and fix or baseline what it reports.
- Keep the handoff self-contained and source every transferable claim. Put an external record or catalogue URL first when one exists. Treat relative `sources/` links as local research artifacts, not published evidence.
- When integrating a historical-record batch into the research catalogue, list every unresolved new person in the catalogue's new-profile section and every sourced amendment to a matched existing person in the existing-profile update section. Do not treat record rows alone as catalogue integration, and do not mix the two action classes.
- Create a complete paste-ready replacement-profile draft for every substantive amendment to an existing profile, including a newly proved relationship, an evidence-supported relationship-confidence change, an identity correction, or correction of a material factual error. A newly found source that only confirms facts already represented on the profile is not a substantive amendment and must not by itself create an amendment draft or update-queue item; retain it as supporting evidence in `findings.md` and the evidence register.
- Before listing an amendment or new person as catalogue-ready, inspect the draft content rather than testing only that its file exists. Verify that it identifies the documentary person, contains meaningful paste-ready WikiTree markup and citations, records the proposed change or unresolved identity test, and is exposed in the corresponding generated catalogue page/JSON. An empty, generic, unrelated, or legacy draft does not satisfy catalogue integration.
- Publish proved collateral kinship as reciprocal structured research relationships and network edges even when WikiTree has no parent-derived link. Keep imported tree relationships intact underneath, cite canonical original records, and distinguish proved siblings from uncertain proposed parents. Do not leave a proved family connection only in narrative prose or the findings filter.
- Preserve the individual profile comparison through layout repairs: expose the captured current WikiTree biography with its revision/date, the complete prepared replacement, and a readable diff. Keep pending amendments visible with the catalogue update flag; retain completed comparisons without a false pending badge. Honour reopened completion records, and retire an outstanding biography badge when a checked capture already contains the amendment. A relationship finding must update the relevant biographies/drafts, assertions and actual pending queue, not merely the Latest findings table.
- For an existing-profile amendment, verify all three outputs before reporting completion: the updated person dossier, its complete replacement draft, and the catalogue's **Needs updated on WikiTree** flag/filter entry. Register the pending work in the maintained update queue and compare against the live biography so the queue describes an actual outstanding change, not an error confined to the local catalogue.
- In a catalogue integration audit, check partially ingested cases as well as missing pages: a rendered conclusion does not prove that its source table or later correction was included. Normalize reviewed source headings to `## Source findings`, reconcile superseded instructions against the canonical case, and run `tools/sync_catalogue_research.py` to check visible content, drafts and flags. A completed or non-managed research case still needs its public-safe catalogue account; only an actual pending WikiTree amendment needs the update flag.
- Keep verified outstanding amendments visible in the catalogue even when live work is blocked by profile management or an earlier task is only partially complete. State the manager requirement or exact remaining action, retain completion history, and supply the full current replacement draft; do not hide pending work because the register status is `BLOCKED` or `PARTIAL`.
- The user's website-first instruction authorizes necessary local synchronization, including `.venv/bin/python tools/build_family_map.py` when a full catalogue build is required. After routine live profile edits, refresh only the affected live-profile overrides, evidence, and person/branch pages with targeted tools. Do not rebuild the full catalogue after each edit. Reserve the full builder for changes that actually require regenerated catalogue-wide structure, indexes, or graph data; batch those changes and preserve current public content in maintained sources before regenerating. Do not run the full builder merely for an unrelated styling change.
- If the user explicitly asks to defer building, continue maintaining source inputs and record which website updates remain pending. Otherwise synchronize completed research batches without another permission question; more research still to come is not by itself a reason to leave completed findings hidden indefinitely.
- After catalogue synchronization, check the affected `www/people/<catalogue-slug>.html` and `.json`, ensure the expected material is visible and the JSON includes `research_findings`. Use focused browser checks for changed public views; run `.venv/bin/python -m unittest tests.test_research_catalog -q` when tests are authorized or expressly requested. - Preserve direct local use of generated catalogue pages. Clean-route publishing must not replace `www/catalogue.html` or other legacy `.html` entry points with redirects, or rewrite their relative links; gate every browser redirect so it never runs under `file://`. A clean-route page opened locally must use a relative site-root `<base>` rather than `<base href="/...">`. After route or catalogue build changes, verify under `file://` that `www/catalogue.html` loads its assets and data, its Glasgow Surname Project brand reaches the sibling `www/index.html` from both legacy and clean catalogue pages, and `www/ydna.html` and `www/timeline.html` reach their sibling local pages without resolving to `file:///ydna`, `file:///timeline`, or a directory listing.
- When replacing an evidence-rich public research page with a state/data-driven rebuild, do not count material as preserved merely because it remains in JSON, an archive, or a catch-all evidence page. Inventory the previous page's visible tables, qualifications, links and interactions; integrate each retained evidence unit into the relevant narrative or research-tool section so the site reads as one cohesive account. A standalone full-evidence dossier alone is not sufficient. Keep superseded rankings visibly archival, preserve an unchanged prior-page snapshot, and add tests for contextual rendered coverage as well as data parity.
- For long person dossiers, lead with the question being tested and the current conclusion, then group the evidence by its role in the argument. Use summary-led disclosure to keep detailed sections compact by default: each closed section must state its finding and what opening it adds. Preserve every evidence table, qualification, citation, figure and control in its relevant section; keep deep links opening the required panels, provide a way to read all sections together, and retain keyboard, no-JavaScript and complete-print access.
- For heavily researched person pages, synthesize the important research into the default view: one current answer, the family, the strongest evidence, competing explanations and next useful checks. Use visible diagrams, maps and compact comparisons to explain relationships and uncertainty; do not substitute a wall of closed summaries for the account. Fold long transcriptions, source-by-source analysis and audit material into a source notebook after their important findings are represented in context. Avoid nested accordions and repeated assessments. Keep navigation short; new research must update the visible synthesis rather than expanding the whole notebook.
- Before completing that synthesis, inventory every source study for distinct material findings, rival identities, conflicting records and discriminating tests. Give each a meaningful visible counterpart in the relevant account; a notebook link or an index entry alone does not constitute coverage. Audit the whole remaining notebook when the owner says important material is still folded away, rather than adding only another isolated illustration.
- Walk readers through the evidence in a deliberate order, with brief transitions explaining how each step leads to the next. Place short, accurate source excerpts beside the relevant diagram or conclusion; label original wording, transcribed fields and expanded abbreviations distinctly, retain direct provenance, and explain what the excerpt establishes and what remains inferred. Use approved original record pixels where they help the reader inspect the claim. More decorative graphics or additional summary cards alone do not satisfy this requirement.
- When the owner asks for a stronger visual redesign, reassess the whole composition, hierarchy and duplicated navigation rather than making another colour-and-spacing pass. Use the family structure and evidence trail to guide the design, and reduce administrative clutter in the opening; decorative restyling alone is insufficient. Give family information and original records deliberate visual roles, retain access to research controls, and inspect the complete desktop and mobile result.
- For every catalogue statistics link intended to filter people, verify the click-through from both `www/catalogue.html` and the clean `/catalogue` route: it must land at the results table, visibly restore the selected filters, and restrict results by the exact intended field. In particular, a spouse birth-surname statistic must not reuse a combined birth/current-surname match.
- Keep `/catalogue/` results as a clean table with full birth and death locations visible directly in their cells, including on narrow screens with table scrolling. Preserve field-specific status colours when simplifying the layout. Keep the colour legend out of the catalogue results; use cell tooltips and accessible status text without an extra explanatory block.

- Catalogue marriage-surname statistics must include Glasgow-at-birth people marrying into the named surname as well as people of that birth surname marrying a Glasgow. Derive the associated surname from the structured birth surname or WikiTree ID, group recognised spelling variants under the canonical label, deduplicate the Glasgow-side people or relationships being counted, and exclude middle-name text matches. - In the catalogue One Tree view, show a married-in non-Glasgow spouse inline with the Glasgow-line partner rather than repeating that person as a standalone tree node. Keep the spouse's catalogue link and fold any children below the couple's displayed line so no person or descendant branch is lost.
- In catalogue or standalone One Tree views, make recorded family branch or pedigree structure the primary organisation. Present geography only as a secondary visual cue such as colour, badges, labels, or filters; never partition the main tree primarily by location. Keep large-tree rendering progressive and culling-based so branch navigation remains responsive.
- For large One Tree overviews, collapse descendants into size-scaled branch aggregates at low zoom and progressively reveal recorded generations as the user zooms. When geography must be legible at a glance, prefer branch-local location blooms or petals (area by country count, ordered by the evidenced route) over a single composition ring; let these split into smaller real-pedigree aggregates as zoom increases. Never merge unrelated pedigrees into global country clusters. Show colour transitions along revealed parent lines so migration offshoots remain traceable. Keep the branch landing view selective and readable rather than rendering every anchor and lineage card at once.
- When subdivisions of one large pedigree become navigation anchors, never present those descendant anchors as independent peer roots: keep their shared recorded ancestor structurally above every child offshoot at every level of detail. Represent multi-stage migration with compact ordered country trails on the existing aggregates and parent lines (for example Scotland → Ireland → United States), not with geography-based layout partitions or extra pseudo-ancestor nodes.
- Treat the generated `similar_people` table as a research lead, never as proof. Inspect both subjects and their sources, relatives, locations, and witnesses before proposing a merge or relationship.
- When a branch audit says the tree continues, recursively follow every attached father and mother until the first profile(s) with no attached ancestry; do not label the requested branch subject parentless merely because its own parents are unresolved. Research and report those true endpoints separately.
- When evidence confirms or rules out a possible duplicate or relationship, record the conclusion in each affected existing profile's `findings.md` and update the relevant project register.
