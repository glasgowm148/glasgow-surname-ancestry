# WikiTree Research — Transcription

This reference preserves the original project skill sections for tasks in this area. Read it when the request matches the section headings below.

### Word-level transcription teaching

- Show a compact, text-labelled confidence badge on collapsed word-study rows, grounded in the maintained reading assessment. Distinguish mixed or qualified readings and word-reading confidence from family identity; do not invent numerical probabilities. Keep name-specific contributor discussions within the relevant word study rather than above the whole reading desk.
- Do not present a short diagnostic stem as a word tracing. For public reading desks, compare the untouched complete word with a clearly labelled source-pixel ink view; retain crossing ink and expose any threshold adjustment. Never manufacture missing strokes or claim pixel colouring reconstructs pen order.
- When a user requests an exact-word secretary-hand comparison, use a genuine licensed teaching font with the same proposed diplomatic spelling, source attribution and an explicit different-hand caveat. Unresolved signs must not be silently expanded into model words.
- Keep name studies collapsed by default, preserve direct evidence anchors and reveal the reasoning progressively. Transcription-index cards should list maintained extracted people, their documentary roles and reading uncertainty, not turn candidate profile associations into established kinship.
- In word-comparison desks, suppress separable neighbouring-line ink only with source-reviewed focus envelopes; keep the original unchanged and retain inseparable crossings neutrally. Do not use an expected font shape to erase manuscript evidence.
- Colour correspondences must use explicit source-group-to-spelling mappings and identical colours in the derived ink and teaching-font views. Preserve the font's whole-word shaping, keep unsupported groups neutral, and show a visible key. Extend name-specific reading steps and source comparisons across testament cases rather than giving non-featured records only a generic diagnostic letter.


### Accepted human transcription corrections and owner attribution

- When the project owner explicitly accepts a supplied human transcription as a correction to an earlier model reading, make it the current reading and retire the superseded model-specific labels and letter analysis. Do not retain an unsupported model guess as an equal live dispute merely because some shapes look similar. Preserve attribution and distinguish acceptance of the reading from proof of profile identity or kinship.
- Do not display the project owner's personal account username in public transcription discussions. Use "project owner" where attribution is needed; retain requested attribution to the contributing transcriber.


### Public testament coverage notes

- Keep duplicate-download and duplicate-register-image bookkeeping in internal provenance, not reader-facing coverage summaries. Public notes should describe available record spans and genuinely unresolved reading work.
- Before claiming an opening or continuation is missing, inspect the actual pages of nested PDFs and supplied page-image variants, not only the flat image list or filename. Check record continuity across the page boundary; a continuation's accounts must not be excluded merely because the confirmation begins lower down. Correct availability claims across the maintained case, research summaries and public exports together. A supplied but not fully transcribed page is not a missing original.


### Structured kin on transcription previews

- Transcription components must explicitly load `/assets/research/transcription-kin-cards.css` on individual case pages as well as transcription indexes. UI checks must inspect the rendered styling, not only source/class presence.

- Every transcription preview card must visibly show Spouse, Children and Other kin rows, sourced from explicit maintained `card_kin` relationship groups. Keep the testator and unrelated officials, witnesses, creditors and executors out of these groups; an estate office alone does not establish kinship. Label probable relationships and uncertain name readings separately. Use a reviewed-passage absence label rather than claiming a person had no spouse or children. Link listed names to their supporting reading steps or word studies.


### Indexed testaments awaiting originals

- Include supplied archival-index testament entries in the public catalogue even before an original image or full transcription is linked. Preserve the exact indexed description, date, court and reference; provide an index-detail destination and visibly separate index-derived kin from manuscript-confirmed readings. Do not label an unmatched original as absent without checking supplied files. Keep distinct records with the same name/year separate by place, court and reference, and retain existing variant-spelling records when adding an exact-surname search result set.


### Associated manuscript fragments

- A fragment identifying a distinct estate is not thereby an accidental inclusion or genealogically unrelated. Inspect shared references, pagination, supplemental views and visible physical boundaries before explaining why a fragment accompanies a purchased record. Distinguish delivery/archival association, textual record boundaries and family relationships; do not invent a flap, attachment or indexing rationale. Retain relevant associated fragments as explicit research leads even when excluded from the main testament transcription.


### Archival-index confirmations

- When the maintained research identifies a recorded child and the named parents, map that assessment into the shared parent–child relationships in both directions. Do not leave imported “unassessed” flags as the public verdict merely because presentation mapping was missing. An unnamed child can have documented parents; an inferred death boundary is a separate claim. Confirm each named parent from the record, not by propagating the other parent's relationship. Display stored candidate scores separately from reviewed parent hypotheses, with individual scores distinguished from proposed couples and exclusions preserved.
- Recompute parent-candidate scores only from the rich source model that still contains relationship status, dated-record provenance and date qualifiers; never rescore the stripped public people index. Cap branch-derived clues below a substantive lead when any descent path is unproved or merely possible, and state that pedigree structure is not independent evidence.

- When the user supplies an archival-index description and accepts its details, mark the indexed name, historical place spelling, occupation, expressly stated kin, date and record type as confirmed by that index. Remove obsolete general uncertainty labels for those facts while keeping ambiguity in individual manuscript strokes, unindexed details and later-profile identities separate. An indexed testament date is not automatically a death date. Reconcile result rows by name, date, court and reference together: several distinct entries can share one volume reference. Preserve supplemental records not present in the supplied result set.
