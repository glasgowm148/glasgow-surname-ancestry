## Working structure

```text
Philip Glasco senior (adult by 1750; died 1772), watchmaker
  spouse: widow Mangan of County Kildare (married by 8 May 1750; died 1760)
  -> Philip Glasco junior (probable son; died 1770), watchmaker
       spouse: Teresa Plunkett (married 1768)
  -> Thomas Glasco (possible son or successor; active 1783), watchmaker
```

The senior/junior designation, same rare surname, same trade and adjacent
Christ Church addresses make a father-son relationship highly plausible. It
is not yet an explicit parent statement. Thomas belongs to the same commercial
cluster, but his precise relationship remains open.
