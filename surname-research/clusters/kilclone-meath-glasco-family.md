# Kilclone, County Meath Glasco family

Last updated: **17 July 2026**

## Direct family record

An inscription transcribed at Kilclone in 1892 records that James Glasco
erected the memorial for his brothers Richard and William, who died on
1 November 1738, and for Patrick Glasco, expressly called the father of James,
who died in May 1754 aged 60.

| Person | Record-backed facts | Relationship |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Patrick Glasco | Died May 1754 aged 60; therefore born about 1694 | Father of James; also father of Richard and William through James's `brothers` wording |
| James Glasco | Alive when memorial erected | Son of Patrick; brother of Richard and William |
| Richard Glasco | Died 1 Nov 1738 aged 10; born about 1728 | Brother of James and William; son of Patrick |
| William Glasco | Died 1 Nov 1738 aged about 7, reading uncertain; born about 1731 | Brother of James and Richard; son of Patrick |

## Source and limits

- Association for the Preservation of the Memorials of the Dead in Ireland,
  Kilclone inscription reported in 1892; searchable transcription at
  [Meath History Hub, *Memorials of the Dead Journal*](https://meathhistoryhub.ie/memorials-of-the-dead-journal/).
- The modern page reproduces a nineteenth-century transcription, not a current
  photograph. William's age is printed with a query mark and should remain uncertain.
- [NLI's Kilcloon, Batterstown and Kilcock register guide](https://registers.nli.ie/parishes/0945)
  shows surviving Catholic baptisms and marriages begin only in 1836.
