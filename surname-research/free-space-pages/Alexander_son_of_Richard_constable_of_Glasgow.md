'''Alexander, son of Richard, formerly constable of Glasgow''', owned property adjoining the western boundary of a burgage exchanged in 1283. The exchanged property had formerly belonged to the deceased Cristiana Briane. The agreement identifies the western neighbour as `Alexandri filii Ricardi quondam constabularii de Glasgw`.<ref>''Registrum Monasterii de Passelet'', pp. 384–385; [https://deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca/charters/06190340/ DEEDS 06190340], burgage exchange, 1283.</ref>

The wording directly proves Richard as Alexander's father and identifies the father's former office. It does not prove that “Glasgow” was either man's hereditary surname.

== Research Notes ==
This man is the neighbouring landholder, not the principal exchanger described as son of the late Richard the messenger. A search result reduced to “Alexander son of Richard, Glasgow, 1283” cannot distinguish them. Keep the two identities separate unless another record equates Richard the messenger with Richard the former constable or joins their sons by property, office or an explicit alias.
