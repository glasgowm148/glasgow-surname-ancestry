== Biography ==
'''Gilbert or Gilibertus de Glascu''' entered Dublin's merchant guild before 1222/23 and paid eight shillings.<ref>Philomena Connolly and Geoffrey Martin, eds., ''The Dublin Guild Merchant Roll, c.1190–1265'', membrane 11d/B; [https://www.johngrenham.com/dcla/dmgr/browse.php?image_order=22 Dublin City Library and Archive transcript].</ref>

The roll's place index identifies ''Glascu'' with Glasgow in Scotland and lists Gloucester separately. The entry gives no family relationship, occupation beyond guild context or later history.

== Research Notes ==
No record states that Gilbert was related to the earlier Dublin entrants Richard and Robert de Glasgu. The repeated admission payment is not kinship evidence.
