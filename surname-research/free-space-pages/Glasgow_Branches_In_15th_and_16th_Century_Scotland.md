=== Key Observations & Data Issues ===

* '''Core Structure:''' The JSON data confirms the lineage back to [[Glasgow-1095|John Glasgow]] and the primary splits through his sons [[Glasgow-1098|William]], [[Glasgow-1096|Robert]], and [[Glasgow-1097|Archibald]]. The line of [[Glasgow-1093|John Glasgow]] (son of William) is particularly expansive.
* '''Geographic Spread:''' Movement from Lanarkshire (A) into Midlothian (B), Ayrshire (C), Linlithgowshire (D), and Berwickshire (F) is documented within the main connected lineages.
* '''Outliers:''' Presence in Roxburghshire (E), Stirlingshire (G), and Argyll (H) suggests wider dispersal or potentially separate Glasgow lines.
* '''Early Migration:''' Branches I and J demonstrate established migration patterns out of Scotland from the early-mid 17th century onwards.
* '''Data Quality & Identifiers:'''
** Links use the `Name` identifier from the JSON (e.g., [[Glasgow-1095|John Glasgow]]). '''Users must verify these IDs on the live WikiTree site''' as they can change.
** Widespread use of estimated birth/death dates limits accuracy.
** Numerous missing profiles for crucial connecting individuals (spouses, mothers) persist.
** Specific profile issues noted previously (e.g., [[Glasgow-1168|James Glasgow]] 'Conflated' flag; likely incorrect mother link for [[Glasgow-1093|John Glasgow]]) require verification on WikiTree.

* On 31 December 1534 '''William Glasgow''' and '''Esbell Brade''' transferred property to "John Glasgow, their son," and John's wife '''Jonet Spreull'''. Ninian's 1613 testament directly names [[Glasgow-1027|Robert]] as his son, [[Glasgow-3062|Marion]] as his sister and a living brother John; William remains only a possible son because the damaged relationship wording is insecure. The testament does not make Ninian's then spouse Jonet Hutchesoun Robert's mother, distinguish full from half-siblings or identify John's birth order. John cannot be [[Glasgow-3181|John of Hirdmanscheillis]], who died in 1576.<ref>James R. Anderson, ed., ''Burgesses & Guild Brethren of Glasgow, 1573-1750'' (Edinburgh, 1925), [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso43scotuoft/page/24/mode/2up p. 24], 23 October 1596.</ref><ref>National Records of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary Court, CC9/7/9, testament of Niniane Glasgw, fisher and burgess of Glasgow, 1613, images 142-145; working transcription reinspection supplied 6 September 2026.</ref>

* [[Glasgow-1168|James Glasgow elder]] was already listed with '''James Glasgow younger''' at Corsankell in the 1559-60 teind material. A 1571 deed directly calls James younger and John Glasgow his sons. James elder therefore belongs around 1510-20, not about 1540, and cannot credibly be a son of John Glasgow born about 1510. William Glasgow is chronologically possible as James elder's father, but no record joins the burgh and Corsankell households.<ref>Margaret H. B. * [[Glasgow-3179|Geillis Glasgow]], wife of Thomas Wilson at Chaplehill in Ardrossan parish, and [[Glasgow-1495|Katherine Glasgow]], wife of Thomas Weir in Stevenston parish, both died in January 1603. Geillis's estate owed James Glasgow in Stevenston. The geography and shared month support one local kin-network investigation, but the testaments do not call the women sisters or identify James as kin. The Wilson and Weir executor groups also lack securely read child wording.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary Court, CC9/7/3, images 113–114, testament dative of Geillis Glasgow, confirmed 11 May 1603; CC9/7/4, images 6–7, testament of Katherine Glasgow, confirmed 9 May 1605; working transcription reinspection supplied 6 September 2026.</ref>

John died in December, provisionally 1636, and the testament was confirmed 11 April 1637. The record does not identify him with Ninian's brother John; [[Glasgow-3539|Mary Glasgow]] is the high-confidence profile candidate for Marie, while identification with the Mary Glasgow whose 1677 testament index calls her wife of sailor James Montgomerie remains unproved.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary Court, CC9/7/27, images 478–480, testament dative of John Glasgow, sailor in Saltcoats; working transcription supplied 6 September 2026.</ref>

* [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow of Corstoun]] and Jonet Anderson had four testament-named children: Marioune, James, Andrew and William. His testament names no parent.
