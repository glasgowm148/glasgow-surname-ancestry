= Glasgow Families of County Londonderry =
''A record-led research page for the Glasgow households of County Londonderry, especially the unresolved connection between Hugh, Adam and John Glasgow in the Tamlaght O'Crilly–Inishrush district.''

This page uses the historic name '''County Londonderry''' because that is the form used by many cited records and repositories. Derry and Londonderry refer to the same county here. '''Londonderry, Guernsey County, Ohio''' is a different place and is identified explicitly whenever it occurs.

The purpose is to collect evidence and identify records capable of resolving relationships. A person appearing in the same parish, church network or surname cluster is not automatically a relative.

== Evidence key ==
* ✅ '''Documented''' — a contemporary or near-contemporary record establishes the person, event, place or stated relationship.
* ⚠️ '''Probable or working''' — several records support the interpretation, but no record states the exact identity or relationship.
* ❓ '''Unresolved''' — evidence does not yet distinguish competing people or family structures.
* ⛔ '''Contradicted or excluded''' — chronology or a direct record rules out the proposed interpretation.

== Current conclusion ==

The strongest current reconstruction is:

<pre>
Unknown earlier Glasgow family in the Tamlaght O'Crilly district
│
├── Hugh Glasgow household, 1740, entry 1195     relationship unknown
└── Hugh Glasgow household, 1740, entry 1272     probable Hugh-548 candidate

Possible later collateral household:
Older Adam Glasgow + Maria Unknown
├── Adam Glasgow (about 1758–1844), Inishrush
└── John Glasgow (1767–1851), probably the 1831 Inishrush John
</pre>

[[Glasgow-3902|Adam Glasgow]] and [[Glasgow-3927|John Glasco]], probably the same man as [[Glasgow-822|John Glasgow born in 1767]], are best treated as '''probable brothers or close cousins'''. No inspected record calls them brothers or names their shared parents. Adam cannot have been John's father because Adam was only about nine years older.

An older Adam and Maria are the best working names for their parents because John's sons were Adam, James, John and Arthur, while Maria was his second daughter. The order fits the traditional Scottish pattern of paternal grandfather, maternal grandfather and father for the first three sons, with Maria in the paternal-grandmother position.<ref name="NamingPattern">[https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/help-and-support/guides/forenames Scotland's People, "Forenames: traditional naming patterns"], accessed 20 July 2026.</ref> Naming patterns are clues, not proof.

The wider evidence makes a connection with [[Glasgow-548|Hugh Glasgow's]] extended local family highly probable, but no source identifies the intervening father or exact degree of kinship.

== The Hugh and McCullough tradition ==

William Melanchthon Glasgow's late-nineteenth-century narrative says [[Glasgow-548|Hugh Glasgow]] married Margaret McCullough in 1733 and had three sons: [[Glasgow-549|John]], [[Glasgow-550|Robert]] and [[Glasgow-540|Hugh]]. It says the youngest Hugh's eldest son John was born on 28 April 1780 on the family farm in County Londonderry, "not far from" Portglenone, and that this younger Hugh's household emigrated in August 1796, leaving many relatives and acquaintances in Ireland.<ref name="Melanchthon">William Melanchthon Glasgow, [https://www.wikitree.com/photo.php/0/0a/Glasgow-590.pdf ''History of the Glasgow Family''], original scan, pp. 2–3, accessed 20 July 2026.</ref>

This is useful geographic testimony but was written about 160 years after the reported marriage and contains errors. It does not name Adam-3902, John-822 or either man's parents.

The younger Hugh's original 1827 Pennsylvania will names sons John, Hugh, Alexander and Robert, daughter Nancy, granddaughter Sarah and an unnamed wife. It does not name Adam or John born in 1767.<ref name="HughWill">Allegheny County, Pennsylvania, Will Book 3, no. 252, pp. 492–493, Hugh Glasgo of Deer Township, will signed 29 March 1827 and proved 25 April 1831; [https://www.ancestry.com/imageviewer/collections/8802/images/005537856_00276?pid=1733435 indexed image], accessed 20 July 2026.</ref> This materially weakens the younger Hugh as Adam's father while leaving a collateral connection possible.

== John of Inishrush and John born in 1767 ==

[[Glasgow-822|John Glasgow]] and Mary Arthur's family register gives nine children born from 1799 to 1815.<ref name="JohnRegister">[https://hiltner.com/mckeown/getperson.php?personID=I368&tree=tree1 John Glasgow family register and descendant page], accessed 20 July 2026.</ref>

After married daughter Rose and deceased son James are removed, the expected males in 1831 are John and sons Adam, John and Arthur: exactly four. Mary and daughters Maria, Lavina and Margaret give four females if Jane was already with John Gray at Boveedy. This is a strong household fit, but the census names only household heads.

== Records already checked without direct proof ==

No inspected family Bible, gravestone, 1847 letter, census, tithe book, flax list, deed index, freeholder index, will index, church-history extract, public Y-DNA table or accessible family compilation explicitly calls Adam and John brothers or names their shared parent.

Important bounded negatives include:
* The complete Adam gravestone names Rose, Eliza, James and grandson William, but no John.
* The original 1847 letter contains no omitted kinship term for Adam, James or Hugh.
* Hugh-540's 1827 will names four sons and no Adam or John born in 1767.
* The volunteer Registry of Deeds index contains no transaction naming Adam or John as parties at Inishrush.
* Public Y-DNA data contain John-822's attributed kit but no documented strict male-line comparator from Adam-3902 or the relevant earlier Hugh household.

These results limit what can be claimed; they do not prove that no relationship existed.
