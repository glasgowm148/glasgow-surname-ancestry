== Agnes Glasgow burial-index anomaly ==

Findmypast record `SCOT/OPR/BUR/1625561` displays Agnes Glasgow, `07 Apr ?`, a death year of 1642, the place Kirknewton and East Calder, the range 1642–1737 and archive reference OPR 689/20.<ref name="AgnesFMP">[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FOPR%2FBUR%2F1625561&amp;tab=this Findmypast, ''Scotland, Parish Deaths & Burials 1564-2017'', Agnes Glasgow, SCOT/OPR/BUR/1625561], accessed 8 September 2026.</ref>

Those fields cannot describe one register. Parish 689 is Inveresk with Musselburgh, and its volume 20 contains deaths from 1820 to 1854.<ref name="InvereskInventory">[https://www.plaidwerx.com/scotland-parishes/edinburgh/OPR689.htm Inveresk parish 689 register inventory], accessed 8 September 2026.</ref> Kirknewton and East Calder is parish 690; its volume 1 contains deaths from 1642 to 1739.<ref name="KirknewtonInventory">[https://www.plaidwerx.com/scotland-parishes/edinburgh/OPR690.htm Kirknewton and East Calder parish 690 register inventory], accessed 8 September 2026.</ref> The displayed 1642 repeats the opening year of that broad register range, while the transcript supplies no year beside 7 April.

The row may derive from a genuine entry, but it does not establish its year, parish, register or relatives. It cannot be assigned to [[Glasgow-993|Agnes Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-2460|Agnes Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-1312|Agnes Glasgow]] or a new person. A corrected transcript or original entry with coherent metadata is required.

== Hugh Glasgow: 1733/1751 index conflation ==

The Findmypast row `GBOR/DOBSON/013379` combines Hugh Glasgow's descriptive clause with the date of the next Glasgow entry in its printed source.<ref name="MalformedHugh">[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=GBOR%2FDOBSON%2F013379&amp;tab=this Findmypast, malformed Hugh Glasgow row, GBOR/DOBSON/013379], accessed 8 September 2026.</ref> Francis J. Grant's printed Edinburgh testament index places the entries consecutively: Hugh Glasgow, sometime sailor in Irvine and thereafter in London, was confirmed on '''12 March 1751'''; James Glasgow, weaver and burgess of Edinburgh, follows with the date '''3 February 1733'''.<ref name="GrantIndex">Francis J. Grant, ed., ''The Commissariot Record of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments, Part III, Volumes 81 to 131—1701–1800'' (Scottish Record Society, 1899), [https://archive.org/stream/scottishrecordso03scotuoft/scottishrecordso03scotuoft_djvu.txt printed p. 106], accessed 8 September 2026.</ref>

Two independent Findmypast rows give Hugh's correct year and date: the Clyde Mariners entry says he was a sailor in Irvine, later in London, confirmed in 1751; the testament index gives London and 12 March 1751.<ref name="Hugh1751Mariner">[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=GBOR%2FDOBSON%2F003411&amp;tab=this Findmypast, ''Clyde Mariners, 1700–1800, Part 1'', Hugh Glasgow, GBOR/DOBSON/003411], accessed 8 September 2026.</ref><ref name="Hugh1751Will">[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FWILLS%2F057434&amp;tab=this Findmypast, ''Scotland, Will and Testament Index 1481–1807'', Hugh Glasgow, 12 March 1751, SCOT/WILLS/057434], accessed 8 September 2026.</ref>

The corrected evidence defines a deceased sailor who had moved from Irvine to London, but supplies no age, parents, spouse, vessel or address. [[Glasgow-3108|Hugh Glasgow]], [[Glasgow-1426|Hugh Glasgow]] and [[Glasgow-3109|Hugh Glasgow]] are strong locality candidates; several Kilwinning Hughs are also chronologically possible. None has a sailor-and-London identity bridge. Do not create a 1733 Hugh or amend an existing profile from the malformed row.
