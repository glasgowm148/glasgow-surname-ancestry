== Biography ==
'''Hugh de Glasgw''' witnessed a lay quitclaim concerning three perticates of land in Glasgow's Rottenrow. The act is assigned to about 1283 rather than internally dated.<ref>''Registrum Monasterii de Passelet'', no. 341; [https://deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca/charters/06190339 DEEDS 06190339].</ref>

Because the instrument already identifies Glasgow as the venue yet still distinguishes Hugh personally as `de Glasgw`, the phrase has strong personal-byname function. The source does not call him a burgess, landholder, neighbour or officeholder.

== Research Notes ==
The text survives through the later Paisley cartulary, not an autograph thirteenth-century instrument. No family relationship to another bearer is stated.
