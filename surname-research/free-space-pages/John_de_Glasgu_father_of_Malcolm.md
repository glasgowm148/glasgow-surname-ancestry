== Biography ==
'''John de Glasgu''' was the father of Malcolm, who received payment for a royal debt in a Scottish Exchequer account rendered at Perth on 26 March 1394. The entry reads `Malcolmo filio Johannis de Glasgu`.<ref>George Burnett, ed., [https://archive.org/details/rotuliscaccariir03grea ''The Exchequer Rolls of Scotland''], vol. 3 (Edinburgh, 1880), p. 342.</ref>

The grammar directly identifies John as Malcolm's father and applies `de Glasgu` to John. It does not style Malcolm `de Glasgu`.

== Research Notes ==
No office, property or associate identifies this John with the 1343 payee or Master John, chancellor of Dunkeld.
