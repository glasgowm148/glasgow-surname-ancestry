== Biography ==
'''John de Glasgu''' received 7s 6d in a Scottish Exchequer account in 1343 concerning the board or table of certain royal servants.<ref>[https://archive.org/details/rotuliscaccariir01scot ''The Exchequer Rolls of Scotland''], vol. 1, p. 531.</ref>

The entry gives no office. Descriptions such as caterer or steward would be conjectural.

== Research Notes ==
Thirty-four years separate this payment from the first secure record of Master John, chancellor of Dunkeld. No repeated office, associate, benefice or patron joins them, so they remain separate identities.
