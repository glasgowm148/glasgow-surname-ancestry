== Biography ==
'''John of Glasgow''' was a member of the Holyrood Abbey community that swore fealty to Edward I on 7 January 1299.<ref>Joseph Bain, ed., ''Calendar of Documents Relating to Scotland'', vol. 2 (Edinburgh, 1884), no. 1052; [https://poms.ac.uk/record/person/14528/ People of Medieval Scotland].</ref>

The record identifies John through Glasgow but gives no parentage, office or earlier history.

== Research Notes ==
John de Glasgu, chaplain of Bishop Gamelin in 1259, could theoretically have entered the Augustinian community in old age. Forty years and the absence of any repeated office, patron, associate or alias make that identity unproved. Keep the men separate.
