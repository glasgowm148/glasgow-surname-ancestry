== Biography ==
'''Robert de Glascu''' witnessed an episcopal ordinance issued at Kippax on 30 January 1248/49. The act styles him ''dominus'' but does not state his office.<ref>DEEDS, charter 03320015, transcribing Durham Cathedral Archive, Register II, fol. 321v.; [https://deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca/charters/03320015 DEEDS 03320015].</ref>

The ecclesiastical context makes clerical status plausible, but the record does not justify calling him a priest, canon or episcopal clerk.

== Research Notes ==
Identity with the Dunkeld clerk recorded in 1229–1236 is chronologically possible but unproved. Keep the records separate pending an explicit bridge.
