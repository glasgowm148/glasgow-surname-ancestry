== Biography ==
'''Robert de Glascu''' was named in an English Curia Regis roll for Michaelmas term 1200 among men said to have been present at a robbery at Dadinton. The notice says that Robert had poor eyesight and could not open his eyes well.<ref>C. T. Flower, ed., ''Curia Regis Rolls of the Reigns of Richard I and John'', vol. 1 (London: HMSO, 1922), p. 354, TNA KB 26/22, m. 1; [https://books.google.com/books?id=SWRnAAAAMAAJ Google Books].</ref>

The entry does not establish Robert's occupation, residence or precise procedural status. It does not justify calling him a convicted robber, merchant or formal witness.

== Research Notes ==
Identity with the late-twelfth-century Dublin guild member is possible but unproved. Keep the records separate until a shared associate, occupation, property or explicit alias is found.
