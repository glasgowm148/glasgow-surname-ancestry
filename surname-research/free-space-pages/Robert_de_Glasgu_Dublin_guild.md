== Biography ==
'''Robert de Glasgu''' entered Dublin's merchant guild in the undated earliest portion of the surviving roll and paid eight shillings. He appears immediately after Richard de Glasgu, who paid the same amount.<ref name="Guild">Philomena Connolly and Geoffrey Martin, eds., ''The Dublin Guild Merchant Roll, c.1190–1265'', membrane 2d/B; Dublin City Library and Archive, [https://www.johngrenham.com/dcla/dmgr/browse.php?image_order=8 digital transcript].</ref>

The early membranes were copied from an earlier source and are conventionally assigned to the later twelfth century. Adjacency does not prove that Robert and Richard were relatives, partners or simultaneous entrants.

== Research Notes ==
Robert may have been the father of Alexander admitted in 1238/39, but the later entry does not call the father the earlier guild member. Keep that identity uncertain and keep this man separate from the 1200 plea-roll Robert, the Dunkeld clerk and the Durham witness.
