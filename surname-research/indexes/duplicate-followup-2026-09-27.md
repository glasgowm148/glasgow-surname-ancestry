records and original-event references in the locally captured profiles, then
checked current WikiTree profiles and management before live action. A common
citation was not by itself counted as a duplicate: parents and children often
cite the same baptism or census. Seven new identity pairs were found; five
managed or orphan-to-managed pairs were merged and verified live.
