# Early Glasgow duplicate follow-up — 26 September 2026

Scope: follow-up to the pre-1600 and recent-profile audits, screening early
profile pairs by shared FamilySearch record links, same-name parents and
children, birth-year proximity, places and live biography wording. Shared
citations were checked for parent-child use before treating them as duplicate
events. The screen is not proof that every spelling variant or undated profile
has been found.
