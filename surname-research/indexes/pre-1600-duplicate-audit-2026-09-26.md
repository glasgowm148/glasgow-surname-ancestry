# Pre-1600 Glasgow duplicate audit — 26 September 2026

Scope: WikiTree searchPerson queries for 24 given-name forms under Glasgow,
including paginated results for John, James, William, Robert and Margaret;
comparison with 47 locally captured pre-1600 Glasgow/Glasko/Glascoe profiles
and the maintained testament and protocol findings. The search is a candidate
screen, not proof that all variant spellings or profiles without dates were
found.

## Original 26 September result

**No further merge is proved in this batch.** The live search uncovered the
three 18 September 2026 Corstoun daughter profiles that were absent from the
local evidence capture. Each has a same-name later profile, but Andrew's
testament supplies no spouse or later identifier for the daughter. Merging now
would erase an unresolved identity question.

**27 September updates:** Glasgow-4096 was merged into Glasgow-3183 as the
leading district and generation match. Glasgow-4097 was merged into
Glasgow-3336 as the leading compatible Isobel of the same generation and
Midlothian setting. Both adult identities remain probable, not directly
identified by Andrew's testament. Andrew is now marked confident as father of the testament-defined daughter
(verified 28 September); Elizat’s maternity remains uncertain. The later bride
identity remains probable, since the marriage and baptism do not name either
parent or Corsoun. The original audit verdict above is retained as
the decision history, not the current profile state.

The controlling source is Andrew Glasgow's testament, NRS Edinburgh Commissary
Court CC8/8/32, images 123–124. The later events are separately cited in each
profile's `findings.md`. The new profiles and the corrected Jonet profile were
saved live and read back on 26 September 2026. The affected local person
dossiers were synchronized; no production website deployment was done.

The same testament directly identifies [[Glasgow-4006|Robert]] as Andrew's son
by [[Stevinsoun-2|Elizat]]. The live Robert profile had no structured mother;
Elizat was connected and verified 26 September. Her unsupported circa-1540
birth estimate was changed to uncertain circa 1560 and clearly described as an
estimate. Local findings for Robert and the merged Glasgow-4007 redirect were
reconciled with the live profiles.

The Robert of Kilbride profile Glasgow-3919 has since merged into
[Robert Glasgow of Glasgow (Glasgow-1096)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1096),
and the Corsinkell John Glasgow-4061 has merged into
[John of Kilwinning (Glasgow-3193)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3193).
The 1580 Kilbride charter still lacks Robert's spouse, occupation or burgh
identity; the 1571 Corsinkell writ still lacks John's Kilwinning residence or
spouse. Both survivors retain the identity qualifications in their findings.

Two apparent duplicate pairs are distinct generations. [James elder
(Glasgow-1168)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1168) and [James younger
(Glasgow-3921)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3921) are explicitly
father and son in [NRS GD39/5/59](https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/details.aspx?reference=GD39%2F5%2F59).
[William Glasgow (Glasgow-1098)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1098)
was dead by March 1535/36, whereas [William Glasgow
(Glasgow-3522)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-3522) was named as
[John-1093](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Glasgow-1093)'s son in Glasgow town
protocol 847 of 1565 (see the two Williams' findings for the printed source).

Next decisive records: Corsoun estate succession or marriage portions for the
three daughters; an occupation/spouse bridge between Glasgow and Kilbride for
Robert; a residence or spouse bridge between Kilwinning and Corsinkell for John.
