This manual register records useful proposed connections without turning them
into facts. A working attachment may be useful for navigation, but its uncertainty
must remain visible on WikiTree and in both affected case folders.

The early Scottish and Irish records do not yet form a proved pedigree. Robert-1027
and Jonet Cunningham were parents of the Scottish Robert-1078; another Scottish
record defines James-3971. Robert-3970 at Larne, James-1081 at Kilwaughter and
John-3913 at Kilwaughter are separate Irish identities. Cross-channel identity and
parentage remain hypotheses, not profile merges or proved structural relationships.
John-3913 was probably a close collateral of James-1081 because the two entries are
adjacent, but even their exact relationship is unstated.
Rev. John-1073 should be placed in the wider Glasgow-1098 descendant project,
most probably through the Corsankell-Stevenston-Kilwinning branch. John-1604 is
the leading existing-profile father candidate, while John-3375 is a priority
duplicate test. See the [five-generation map](../clusters/early-scotland-glasgow-1098-map.md).
