== Biography ==

'''[Androw?] Glasgow''' was a lawful child of [[Glasgow-3181|John Glasgow]],
the man identified in the derivative testament index as living at
Hirmenschelis. John's testament dative names Katherine and a second child whose
forename is provisionally read ''Androw'' or Andrew as his lawful children and
executors-dative. The confirmation repeats that they were executors to John,
“thair father.”<ref name="John1577">National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh
Commissary Court, CC8/8/5, images 276–277, testament dative and inventory of
Johne Glasgw; working transcription of the original manuscript. Published
index: ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register of Testaments, 1514–1600'',
[https://archive.org/details/commissariotreco16scot/page/n117/mode/2up?q=Glasgow
p. 109], entry dated 4 May 1577.</ref>

Margaret, John's widow, submitted the inventory for the children. Her surname
is provisionally read Cochrane. The pronoun immediately before “lawful bairns”
has not been securely resolved, so this record proves Margaret's marriage to
John but does not independently establish that she was the child's
mother.<ref name="John1577" />

The court or index date is 4 May 1577. The death clause says that John died in
March, but the year remains unresolved. Hirmenschelis comes from the derivative
index rather than the present manuscript reading and is used only as an
uncertain household-derived location for the child.<ref name="John1577Index">[https://www.findmypast.co.uk/transcript?id=SCOT%2FWILLS%2F057444&tab=this
Findmypast, ''Scotland, Will and Testament Index 1481–1807'', record
SCOT/WILLS/057444], John Glasgow in Hirmenschelis, 4 May 1577; derivative
index.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The child's relationship to John is direct; the uncertainty concerns the
forename and later identity. [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow of [Corsoun?]]] was
compared because his name, generation and broad rural Lothian setting fit. His
own testament of 1597/8 names his wife and children but does not name his
father, and John's testament does not carry the child from Hirmenschelis into
Corsoun. Those similarities are insufficient for a profile match or parent
attachment without a rental, property, kirk or court bridge.<ref>National
Records of Scotland, Edinburgh Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124,
testament testamentar and inventory of Andro Glasgw, made 6 February 1597/8,
death stated as 7 February and confirmed 10 July 1598.</ref>

[[Glasgow-3589|Marjorie Glasgow]] is not this second child: the corrected
manuscript reading does not name Marjorie. [[Glasgow-3590|Katherine Glasgow]]
is the other lawful child explicitly named in the same estate.
