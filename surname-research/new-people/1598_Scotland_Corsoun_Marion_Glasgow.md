== Biography ==

'''Marion Glasgow''' was a daughter of [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow of
uncertain Corsoun]]. Andrew's testament, made on 6 February 1597/8, provides that if
his wife Elizat Stevinsoun was not pregnant, his third was to be divided among
“his said sone and his said [dochter… struck through] Marione, Jonet,
Isobell.”<ref name="Andrew1598">National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh
Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124, testament testamentar and inventory
of Andro Glasgw, made 6 February 1597/8, death stated as 7 February, confirmed
10 July 1598; corrected working transcription of the original register images.
Printed index: Scottish Record Society, ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register
of Testaments, 1514–1600'', [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso01scotuoft/page/n117/mode/1up
p. 108].</ref>

Her brother Robert is expressly called Andrew's son by Elizat. The testament
does not separately state that Elizat was Marion's mother, so maternity should
not be inferred as certain from this clause alone.<ref name="Andrew1598" />
