== Biography ==

'''Robert Glasgow''' was the son of [[Glasgow-3163|Andrew Glasgow of uncertain
Corsoun]] and Andrew's wife '''Elizat Stevinsoun'''. Andrew's testament,
made on 6 February 1597/8, expressly describes him as:

<blockquote>“Robart Glasgw his sone begottin be him vpoun the said Elizat his
wyff”</blockquote>

Andrew directed that Elizat should “bruik his [word unclear] land quhill his
said Robart cum to aige,” showing that Robert had not yet attained the relevant
legal majority and was intended to succeed to the land after Elizat's interim
possession.<ref name="Andrew1598">National Records of Scotland, Edinburgh
Commissary Court, CC8/8/32, images 123–124, testament testamentar and inventory
of Andro Glasgw, made 6 February 1597/8, death stated as 7 February, confirmed
10 July 1598; corrected working transcription of the original register images.
Printed index: Scottish Record Society, ''Commissariot of Edinburgh: Register
of Testaments, 1514–1600'', [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso01scotuoft/page/n117/mode/1up
p. 108].</ref>

The testament also names Andrew's daughters Marion, Janet and Isobel. It makes
conditional provision “incais his said spous be with bairne to him”; that does
not prove that an additional child was born.<ref name="Andrew1598" />

does not name Andrew, Elizat, Corsoun, siblings, or the land. Name and regional
