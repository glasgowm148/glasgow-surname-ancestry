== Biography ==
'''John Glasgw''' was a burgess of Glasgow. He made his will on 7 April 1637 and died that day. The Glasgow Commissary Court confirmed his testament on 27 April 1637.<ref name="Testament">National Records of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary Court, testament testamentar and inventory of John Glasgw, burgess of Glasgow, CC9/7/27, images 478–480, made and death dated 7 April 1637, confirmed 27 April 1637; digital images at ScotlandsPeople.</ref>

The inventory provided £60 Scots for the maintenance of John's unnamed children from his death until the testament was processed. He owed surgeon James Anderson £10 for curing a hurt leg. John nominated Robert Stewart, merchant burgess of Glasgow, as sole executor; John Bell elder and Robert Stewart witnessed the will.<ref name="Testament" />

== Research Notes ==
The division of the free estate into a defunct's part and children's part may indicate that John's wife had predeceased him, but the testament does not name a wife or explicitly state her death. No spouse should be created or attached from that inference.

The injured leg is documented; it is not stated to have caused his death.
