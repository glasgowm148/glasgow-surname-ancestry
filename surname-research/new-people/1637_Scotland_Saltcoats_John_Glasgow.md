== Biography ==

'''John Glasgow''' was a sailor in Saltcoats, Ayrshire. He died in December,
provisionally read as 1636. His testament dative was confirmed on 11 April
1637.<ref name="Testament">National Records of Scotland, Glasgow Commissary
Court, testament dative and inventory of John Glasgow, sailor in Saltcoats,
NRS CC9/7/27, images 478–480; death month December, year provisionally 1636;
confirmed 11 April 1637. Working transcription of the original images supplied
6 September 2026.</ref>

The testament names Isobel Young as his widow and calls Katherine and
[[Glasgow-3539|Marie or Mary Glasgow]] their lawful children.<ref
name="Testament" /> John Browne, sailor in Saltcoats, was cautioner; the
document does not state that he was related to the family.

== Research Notes ==

[[Glasgow-1030|John Glasgow of Glasgow]] is a possible identity match, not a
proved one. He was an adult by 1618/19 and has no proved death, but no record
connects his wife Jonet Mathie or Glasgow household to Isobel Young and this
Saltcoats family.

The indexed Mary Glasgow, wife of James Montgomerie, sailor in Saltcoats,
whose testament was recorded on 24 October 1677, is a promising identity for
daughter Marie. Her original testament must identify this family first.
