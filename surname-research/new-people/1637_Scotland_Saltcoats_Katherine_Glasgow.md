== Biography ==

'''Katherine Glasgow''' was a lawful child of John Glasgow, sailor in
Saltcoats, and his widow Isobel Young. She and her sister
[[Glasgow-3539|Marie or Mary Glasgow]] were named in their father's testament
dative, confirmed on 11 April 1637.<ref>National Records of Scotland, Glasgow
Commissary Court, testament dative and inventory of John Glasgow, sailor in
Saltcoats, NRS CC9/7/27, images 478–480; confirmed 11 April 1637. Working
transcription of the original images supplied 6 September 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==

The testament supplies Katherine's parentage but not her age, birth, marriage
or death. Saltcoats is an uncertain birth location inferred from her father's
household, not a stated birthplace. Her parents should be linked only after
John's possible identity with [[Glasgow-1030]] and an appropriate profile for
Isobel Young are resolved.
