== Biography ==
'''Bessie Glasgow''' lived at Quodquen, now Quothquan in Lanarkshire, in 1650. She was the lawful sister of [[Glasgow-3296|John Glasgow]], whose testament dative and inventory were confirmed at Lanark on 27 May 1650. The opening calls Bessie his lawful sister; the confirmation calls the deceased John her brother and identifies her as his executrix dative.<ref name="Quothquan1650">National Records of Scotland, Lanark Commissary Court, testament dative and inventory of John Glasgow in Quodquen, confirmed 27 May 1650, CC14/5/5, register images 65–66; [https://www.scotlandspeople.gov.uk/search-records/legal-records/wills Scotland's People wills search]. The sibling clauses and continuity of both images were visually checked in the maintained original-image review of 10 September 2026; the full opening and accounts are not yet completely transcribed.</ref><ref>Francis J. The calendar confirms John's estate entry; the original supplies Bessie's relationship.</ref>

Bessie was alive at that confirmation and was born before 1650. No age, birthplace, death date, spouse or child has been established. Quothquan is her recorded residence, not an established birthplace or death place.<ref name="Quothquan1650" />

== Research Notes ==
=== Family and identity ===
The two sibling clauses support the record-defined John–Bessie relationship at '''99% subjective confidence'''; they belong to one testament, not two independent sources. Bessie's identity among existing profiles remains unresolved. Compatible candidates include [[Glasgow-3361|Bessie Glasgow of South Leith, indexed in 1616]], [[Glasgow-1091|Elizabeth Glasgow of Glasgow, baptised in 1616]], [[Glasgow-1092|Bessie Glasgow of Glasgow, indexed in 1623]] and [[Glasgow-1517|Elizabeth Glasgow of Glasgow, christened in 1625]]. No baptism or later household is assigned to this woman merely from the shared name.

[[Glasgow-1029|Stephen Glasgow]] is the selected uncertain father of Bessie's brother John, assessed at '''22% subjective probability'''. Extending that same paternal hypothesis to the documented sister gives Bessie a '''conditional 22%''' placement. Stephen's adult Glasgow life in 1575 and marriage to [[Graham-38221|Margaret Grahame]] in 1582 permit the proposed generation, but do not connect that household to the Quothquan siblings.<ref>Robert Renwick, ed., ''Abstracts of Protocols of the Town Clerks of Glasgow'', vol. 7, protocol 2189, printed p. 110, 17 June 1575, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsprotoc00robegoog/page/n126/mode/2up digital copy]; vol. 8, protocol 2431, 25 June 1582, [https://archive.org/details/abstractsofproto08glas/page/56/mode/2up digital copy]. These name Stephen's parents and spouse, respectively, not Bessie or the Quothquan John as his children.</ref> This placement is dependent on John's hypothesis and adds no independent evidence for Stephen's paternity. Bessie's mother remains unknown; Margaret Grahame is not thereby her mother. No candidate baptism profile's documented parentage is changed by this conditional model.

Bessie Glasgow must be distinguished from [[Robertson-33965|Bessie Robertson]], the deceased spouse of a John Glasgow in Montfoid whose testament was confirmed in 1610.<ref>Francis J. Grant, ed., ''The Commissariot Record of Glasgow: Register of Testaments, 1547–1800'' (Edinburgh: Scottish Record Society, 1901), [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/page/n435/mode/1up p. 426], Robertson, Bessie, 17 August 1610; cross-reference under Glasgow, John, [https://archive.org/details/scottishrecordso07scotuoft/page/196/mode/1up p. 196].</ref> The Montfoid husband's identity with the Quothquan deceased is itself uncertain.
