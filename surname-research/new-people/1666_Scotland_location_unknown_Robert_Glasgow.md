## Biography draft

Robert Glasgow was master of the *John of Portrush* in 1666. The Argyll Estates Archives catalogue describes his receipt for transporting barrels of coal for the earl of Argyll.<ref>Argyll Estates Archives, Campbell family, dukes of Argyll papers, “Various accounts and receipts,” 1666–1678, GB 3452 ARG-04-03-67-78, former reference PFV 67, catalogue p. 149, [https://www.argyll-papers.com/downloads/campbell-family-dukes-of-argyll-papers.pdf catalogue description]. The original receipt has not yet been inspected.</ref>

The catalogue gives no age, residence, birthplace, wife, child or other relative. The vessel name supplies a relevant Scottish–Ulster maritime context but does not prove that Robert lived at Portrush.

An instrument of resignation and sasine at Ayr on 6 August 1688 was written by a Robert Glasgow, servitor to the notary Alexander McDermeit.<ref>National Records of Scotland, GD86/741, instrument of resignation and sasine, 6 August 1688, [https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/browseDetails.aspx?reference=GD86%2F741 catalogue record].</ref> The shipmaster and notarial servitor could be one literate west-coast man, but neither record identifies the other. Their handwriting, signatures and associates must be compared.
