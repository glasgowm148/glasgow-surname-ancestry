The printed Edinburgh register records the marriage of Eupham Glasgow and Alexander Hutcheson, tailor, on 1671-12-08.<ref>Henry Paton, ed., ''Register of Marriages for the Parish of Edinburgh, 1595–1700'' (Scottish Record Society, 1905), printed pp. 267 and 346, PDF pp. 277 and 356, [https://electricscotland.com/history/records/scottishrecordso16scotuoft.pdf#page=356 scanned edition]. The unnumbered Prefatory Note, PDF p. 9, describes the dates in register volumes I–II (1595–1694) as marriage dates.</ref>

An Edinburgh tailor named Alexander Hutcheson became a burgess on 22 June 1670 as apprentice to the late Alexander Thomsone, tailor; his apprenticeship date was 31 January 1655. This is a probable continuation of the groom's occupation, while identification remains unproved. Thomsone was his master, not a recorded father.<ref>Charles B. Boog Watson, ed., ''Roll of Edinburgh Burgesses and Guild-Brethren, 1406–1700'' (Scottish Record Society, 1929), printed p. 265, PDF p. 275, [https://www.tradeshouselibrary.org/uploads/4/7/7/2/47723681/roll_of_edinburgh_burgesses_1406_to_1700.pdf#page=275 scanned edition]. Printed abstract inspected 30 September 2026; original burgess record unchecked.</ref>
