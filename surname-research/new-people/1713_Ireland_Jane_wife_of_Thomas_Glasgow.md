== Biography ==
'''Jane''' was named as the wife of Thomas Glasgow among defendants in a case brought by David Rynd, Esq., in the Irish Court of Exchequer. The bill-book entry is dated 8 March 1713 as written. Her maiden surname, age, birthplace, parents, marriage date and death have not been established. Dublin is the court venue, not an established residence.<ref name="Exch1713">National Archives of Ireland, Equity Exchequer Bill Books 1712–1714, volume 18, facing left page opposite p.231, 8 March 1713 as written, image 583; [ https://search.findmypast.co.uk/record?id=IRE%2FCOURT%2FEXCHEQUER%2F007635140%2F00583&parentid=IRE%2FCOURT%2FEXCHEQUER%2F0001416171 Findmypast original image]. == Research Notes ==
The 1713 plaintiff is David Rynd, not the formerly published Lloyd reading. The clearly written David and John Rynd names in the 27 January 1712 entry allow the 1713 surname to be reread as Rynd. Jane’s wife clause is unchanged. The 1712 and 1718 Rynd entries consolidate the Thomas litigation sequence but add no maiden name or maternity evidence.

Prefer [[Glasgow-3556|Thomas Glasgow of Lifford and Donegal]] as her husband, probable at 88% subjective confidence for the complete spouse attachment. The 1713 husband identification is separately assessed at 89%. William Humphrys also appears with the established Thomas in the 1701 Exchequer case; the Moffett network adds context. No residence or estate is stated in the new entry. Another Thomas or a succeeding generation remains possible. The entry's literal “Jane his wife” wording is documented (99%); that is not 99% confidence in the Lifford identification.

No child is assigned to Jane merely because she was Thomas’s wife in 1713.
