== Biography ==
Nicholas Glasco is named in a 20 June 1746 report in the ''Weekly Worcester Journal'' among officers in the rebel army committed to New Gaol, Southwark. The notice lists him between John Duncan and Alexander M'Laughlan. It gives no rank for Nicholas individually and does not state a conviction, birthplace, residence, relatives, occupation, or dates of birth or death.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1214317653/ ''Weekly Worcester Journal'', 20 June 1746, p.4], original roster inspected 4 October 2026. Date follows provider metadata; masthead date has not been separately checked.</ref>

== Research Notes ==
The record preserves the spelling Glasco. It is not automatically a Glasgow family identification. The phrase “rebel army” describes the newspaper's group label; this entry does not establish Nicholas's individual rank or guilt. A bounded local lookup of Nicholas Glasgow, Nicholas Glasco and Nicholas Glascow found no compatible historical profile. Later North American profiles were not treated as candidates.

No parents, spouse, children, birthplace, Irish origin, or Glasgow connection is established. Further work would require the underlying gaol, legal, military, or related 1746 records.
