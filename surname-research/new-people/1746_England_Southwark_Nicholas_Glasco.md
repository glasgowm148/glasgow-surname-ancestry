# Nicholas Glasco / Glascoe, Southwark proceedings

== Biography ==
Nicholas Glasco is named in a 20 June 1746 report in the ''Weekly Worcester Journal'' among officers in the rebel army committed to New Gaol, Southwark. The notice lists him between John Duncan and Alexander M'Laughlan. It gives no rank for Nicholas individually and does not state a conviction, birthplace, residence, relatives, occupation, or dates of birth or death.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1214317653/ ''Weekly Worcester Journal'', 20 June 1746, p.4], original roster inspected 4 October 2026. Date follows provider metadata; masthead date has not been separately checked.</ref>


The 27 and 28 June reports describe Nicholas Glascoe as a self-styled major and allege a role in the attack on the Hazard sloop. These are allegations, not a finding of guilt.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/394536822/ The Derby Mercury, 1746-06-27, p.1], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/404059808/ The Newcastle Weekly Courant, 1746-06-28, p.1], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref>

An August report lists Nicholas among persons indicted for high treason, while the 7 November paper announces the pending trial.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/404060798/ The Newcastle Weekly Courant, 1746-08-23, p.2], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/394542827/ The Derby Mercury, 1746-11-07, p.3], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref>

The 8 and 17 November reports say he was acquitted, with defence evidence identifying him as a French native born at Saint-Germain-en-Laye and a lieutenant in Dillon’s Regiment. They report an order to detain him as a French prisoner. The Caledonian account also mentions a Not Guilty verdict and removal of his bolts; the saved excerpt stops before the full cartel articles. These reports require comparison with the underlying legal and military records.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/404061824/ The Newcastle Weekly Courant, 1746-11-08, p.3], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/394462695/ The Caledonian Mercury, 1746-11-17, p.2], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref>

In May 1747 a notice names Mr Glascoe as major in Lord Ogilvy’s new regiment. Nicholas is a probable contextual candidate, but the missing forename and unproved continuity prevent a firm identification.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/404076485/ The Newcastle Weekly Courant, 1747-05-09, p.1], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref>

No parents, spouse, children, birth date, death date or Irish origin is established.

== Research Notes ==
The rare full name, Southwark proceedings and Hazard episode support a probable association across the 1746 reports. French birth and service are reported defence evidence. They do not establish an Irish origin, a Glasgow-family connection or relatives. The May 1747 appointment lacks a forename.
