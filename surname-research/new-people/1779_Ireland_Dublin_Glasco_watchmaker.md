## Identity assessment

Surname Glasco as printed; first name unknown; male from his in the advertisement; adult watch and clock maker trading in Dublin in 1779–1780. Birth, death, origin and family unknown.

- [[Glasco-143]]: Existing Thomas candidate for the 1779–1783 trade cluster. Birth c.1766 is an unsupported estimate and conflicts with adult trade in 1779; it cannot securely exclude identity.
- [[Glasco-144]]: Thomas, 1773 Dublin marriage groom, remains compatible by chronology and place. No cited record identifies him as the watchmaker.
- [[Glasco-173]]: Thomas, father in a 1760 Dublin baptism, remains compatible by chronology and place; no trade/address bridge has been established.

## Duplicate and chronology review

- Reviewed the public historical catalogue and QMD notes for Dublin watchmakers and the two addresses. Reuse the existing trade cluster; this is not a new person per advertisement.
- The known Philip junior died in 1770 and elder Philip died in 1772, so neither deceased watchmaker can be this living 1779 advertiser. The Scottish Philip profile identity remains separately unproved.
