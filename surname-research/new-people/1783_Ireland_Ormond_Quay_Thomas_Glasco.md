## Paste-ready biography

Thomas Glasco was listed as a watchmaker at 20 Ormond Quay, Dublin, in Watson's 1783 directory.<ref>[https://www.swilson.info/wat1783tr.php?brw=occ&amp;brwkey=Watch+maker Watson's Almanack directory transcription], 1783.</ref>

His name, trade and city make him a plausible successor or relative of the two Philip Glasco watchmakers at Christ Church, but no record inspected states a relationship. Do not attach him to either Philip without further evidence.
