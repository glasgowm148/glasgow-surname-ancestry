== Biography ==
At Kildress church on 16 January 1793, the meeting appointed Hugh Faulkner and William Glasgow as delegates to the Dungannon parliamentary-reform meeting. William subsequently took the chair and signed its thanks resolution. <ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/72019090/ Northern Star, 23 January 1793, p.2].</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/72019250/ Northern Star, 26 January 1793, p.4, repeated Kildress resolutions].</ref>

William Glasgow, Kildress delegate and subsequent chairman; meeting 16 January 1793. Repeated Kildress resolutions; meeting date is 16 January 1793 from the earlier complete headed original, not the 30 January issue date. This bounded passage names Hugh Faulkner and William Glasgow as delegates and Glasgow taking the chair. No spouse, parent or birthplace is given.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/72019314/ Northern Star, 1793-01-30, p.4].</ref>

== Research Notes ==
William Glasgow (Glasgow-2915) is a compatible candidate by period and district. The notice does not establish that profile’s father, wife or birthplace.

Checked the captured Cookstown William biography and its unproved father/spouse links.

Glasgow-585’s estimated c.1804 birth is substantially later than an adult delegate in1793; that estimate is a strong mismatch, not an exact birth-date exclusion.

The delegate could also be another William, including the shipmaster; no source joins or separates those roles.

Look for delegate correspondence, Kildress leases and signatures that identify his residence or family.
