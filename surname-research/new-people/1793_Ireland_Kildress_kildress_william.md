== Biography ==
At Kildress church on 16 January 1793, the meeting appointed Hugh Faulkner and William Glasgow as delegates to the Dungannon parliamentary-reform meeting. William subsequently took the chair and signed its thanks resolution. <ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/72019090/ Northern Star, 23 January 1793, p.2].</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/72019250/ Northern Star, 26 January 1793, p.4, repeated Kildress resolutions].</ref>
