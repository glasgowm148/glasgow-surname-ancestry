# New-profile draft: Maria Glasgow, wife of Alexander Wallace

'''Maria Glasgow''' was born on 22 September 1800, one of the nine children
recorded for [[Glasgow-822|John Glasgow]] and Mary Arthur in their family
register. She married Alexander Wallace.

The descendant family page records children including Mary Wallace, born at
Rasharkin in 1840, and Lavinia Wallace, born at Rasharkin on 14 February 1842.
This establishes a branch of John and Mary Arthur's Inishrush family living in
Rasharkin parish during the same period as the Lisnagaver Glasgow households.
It does not prove that Maria was related to Alexander or Thomas Glasgow of
Lisnagaver beyond their broader shared surname community.
