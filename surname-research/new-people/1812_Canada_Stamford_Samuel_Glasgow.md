== Biography ==

Samuel Glasgow was described as the paternal grandfather of [[Glasgow-959|Dr Sinclair Holden Glasgow]] in the 1887 Welland county history. Sinclair’s father was [[Glasgow-958|William Glasgow]], a Stamford farmer and justice of the peace, then aged 61, whose wife was Mary Elizabeth Lundy. This identifies Samuel as William’s father.<ref name="Welland 1887">''The History of the County of Welland, Ontario'' (Welland: Welland Tribune, 1887), p.559, Sinclair Holden Glasgow family account; [https://archive.org/details/historyofcountyo00murp/page/n573/mode/1up original scan, IA leaf 573].</ref>

The same account calls Samuel a native of Scotland, says he moved at an early age to County Tyrone, was educated for the ministry at Belfast and ordained by that presbytery, and settled in Stamford before the War of 1812.<ref name="Welland 1887" /> Those details remain the county history’s retrospective account; no ordination or migration record has yet been inspected. His exact birth and death dates are unknown.
