## Paste-ready biography

'''James Glasgow junior''' was a tithe occupier at Ballybogy, Dunaghy parish, County Antrim, in 1825. The original Tithe Applotment Book lists him separately from an unsuffixed '''James Glasgow'''. James junior was assessed 10 shillings and 11 pence; the other James was assessed 8 shillings.<ref name="BallybogyTithe1825">Public Record Office of Northern Ireland, ''Tithe applotment for the parish of Dunaghy'', 1825, PRONI FIN/5/A/128, manuscript p. 25, Ballybogy entries for James Glasgow and James Glasgow junior; indexed transcription: W. Macafee, [https://www.billmacafee.com/182030stithe/tithenorthantrimrevisionorder.pdf ''Data from the Tithe Applotment Books covering North Antrim, arranged in document order''], entries 6001–6002.</ref>

The assessments are monetary amounts rather than acreages. ''Junior'' distinguishes this man from the other contemporary James; it does not by itself establish that the older man was his father. No parent, wife, child, age or occupation beyond tithe occupancy is stated.

The surrounding Alexander and Wier entries establish a shared Ballybogy tenant grouping. They do not prove a family relationship or continuous descent from the similarly named families documented there in 1740.
