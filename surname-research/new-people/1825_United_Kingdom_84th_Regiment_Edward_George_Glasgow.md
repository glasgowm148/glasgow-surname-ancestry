The full name is rare, and a single army career can be traced. He appears in the 84th Regiment officer list c.1813-20 (Raikes, York and Lancaster roll) and at an insolvent debtors' hearing in London in Nov 1823 (Times). He was made Lt 84th from half-pay of the 49th on 7 Apr 1825 (London Gazette 16 Apr 1825, matching this notice). He retired on half-pay of the 18th Light Dragoons in June 1826 (London Gazette 17 June 1826) and became Lt 41st Foot from that half-pay on 26 July 1827 (London Gazette 14 Aug 1827). 'Capt. Glasgow, 41st foot' was a passenger from Madras in 1829 (Oriental Herald). The Sunday Times of 16 Dec 1849 names 'Emily Caroline, widow of the late Captain Edward George Glasgow, 41st Regiment'.
