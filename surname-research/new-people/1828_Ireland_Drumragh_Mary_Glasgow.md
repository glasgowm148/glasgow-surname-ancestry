# New-profile draft: Mary Glasgow of Drumragh, mother of Samuel Speir

'''Mary Glasgow''' was the mother of Samuel Speir, who married Margaret Murphy
on 4 December 1828 in First Kilrea Presbyterian Church. The marriage register
describes Samuel as the son of William Speir and Mary Glasgow of Drumragh near
Ahoghill. Margaret was the daughter of Andrew Murphy and Mary Hastie of
Inishrush.

<ref>Richard John Torrens, [https://genealogy.torrens.org/BannValley/church/KilreaP1/Marriages/1828.html "First Kilrea Presbyterian marriages, 1828"], entry for Samuel Speir and Margaret Murphy, 4 December 1828.</ref>

The record directly establishes Mary's name, son and Drumragh association. It
does not state whether Drumragh was her current residence, identify her parents,
give her age, or clarify whether William Speir was living. Her birth is estimated
as before about 1792 solely because she had a son old enough to marry in 1828.

## Suggested data

* Name: Mary Glasgow
* Birth: before about 1792, uncertain; place unknown
* Residence/association: Drumragh near Ahoghill, County Antrim, by 1828
* Spouse or co-parent: William Speir; relationship is implied by the son's named
  parents but no marriage has been found
* Son: Samuel Speir, adult by 4 December 1828
* Death: unknown
