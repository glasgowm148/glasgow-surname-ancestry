== Biography ==
An auction notice describes the large shop and stores at No.32 Rosemary Street, Belfast, as formerly occupied by Mr James Glasgow. The lease interest was offered for sale on 1 April 1828; the notice dated 21 March gives 34 years unexpired from 1 November next and £30 late Irish currency yearly rent.<ref>''Belfast News-Letter'', 1828-04-01, p. 3 ([https://www.newspapers.com/image/390544933/ Newspapers.com]).</ref>
