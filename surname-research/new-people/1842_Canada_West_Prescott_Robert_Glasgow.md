== Biography ==

'''Robert Glasgow''' was an early shipbuilder at Prescott, Canada West. Prescott's municipal heritage material identifies him as the builder of the Centre Street house now called Glasgow House.<ref>[https://www.prescott.ca/media/3dtiztjk/council-agenda-june-16-2025.pdf Town of Prescott, Council Agenda, 16 June 2025], heritage designation material for 280 Centre Street, Glasgow House.</ref>

A later account based on local historian and descendant Fraser Laschinger's research says that Robert built the house about 1835, died in 1856 and left it to six children. It calls merchant Samuel Glasgow his son and says Samuel bought the shares of his siblings.<ref name="Recorder">[https://www.theresataylor.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/Auction-all-for-others-Brockville-Recorder.pdf Theresa Taylor, “Auction all for others,” ''Brockville Recorder & Times'', 22 February 2017].</ref>

== Research Notes ==

A modern census index reports a Robert Glasgow in Augusta in the 1842 Canada West census. The image previously cited for that entry, frame `004569584_00050`, was inspected at full resolution on 13 August 2026 and does not contain a readable Robert Glasgow row.<ref>[https://central.bac-lac.gc.ca/.item/?app=Census1842&op=img&id=004569584_00050 Library and Archives Canada, 1842 Census of Canada West, image 004569584_00050], inspected 13 August 2026.</ref> It should not be cited as Robert's census record unless the correct frame or an indexing bridge is supplied.

A derivative Leeds and Grenville estate index lists “Glasgow Robert 1849.” That year may identify another man, the start of a proceeding, or an error; it conflicts with the reported 1856 death. The corresponding Brockville estate-file film is restricted behind FamilySearch sign-in. The original file and the property deed chain are the decisive evidence for Robert's death date and children.

The local One Tree export and public WikiTree indexing were searched again on 13 August 2026. No Prescott shipbuilder profile was found. [[Glasgow-1367|Robert Glasgow of Glenleslie]], [[Glasgow-1171|Robert Glasgow of New York]], and [[Glasgow-1983|Robert Glasgow of South Carolina]] are incompatible identities. His origin in Kilrea or Ireland must not be inferred from Samuel's later notice.

The six-share account is a promising kinship source, not permission to attach
the named brothers to the similarly named Kilrea/Lisnagaver profiles. Retrieve
and inspect the original image and registration before using its relationships
in structured data.
