== Biography ==
John Glasgow, explicitly called a merchant at Cookstown, appears among John Cassell coffee agents on 30 September 1848, and again in February 1849. A separate 2 February 1849 advertisement appoints him there as agent for the Russian Company’s Caravan Tea and Arabian Coffee. This extends the inspected Cookstown agency sequence to 1848–1850. The Russian Company agent list is repeated on 13 April 1849, again explicitly calling John a merchant at Cookstown. Glasgow-2058 is compatible by name, Cookstown district and the merchant occupation independently recorded in Benjamin-2059’s 1856 marriage. None of these advertisements names relatives, age or street address. Repeated agent lists are advertisements for one trading role, not independent family proofs. John’s parents remain unknown. Glasgow-1968 remains a weaker comparison: his family histories give migration to Michigan in 1844 or 1848; the Drummeen tithe and 1850/1851 probate leads are not securely assigned to him, and probate year is not a death date.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1256974148/ The Sligo Champion, 30 September 1848, p.3, John Cassell coffee agents].</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/390559166/ Belfast News-Letter, 2 February 1849, p.3, Russian Company tea and coffee agents].</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/390559823/ Belfast News-Letter, 20 February 1849, p.3, John Cassell coffee agents].</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1257358494/ Impartial Reporter and Farmers’ Journal, 22 February 1849, p.3, John Cassell coffee agents].</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/390562652/ Belfast News-Letter, 1849-04-13, p.3].</ref>
