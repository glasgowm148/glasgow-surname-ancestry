Reviewed: 2026-10-18. The documents establish a John Glasgow/Glasco of Prescott, watchmaker, as the brother-beneficiary named in Robert Glasgow's will and the grantor in B13. His identification with any existing John Glasgow profile is unresolved. Retain all six checked comparisons; none is selected or excluded by migration alone.

== Profile fields and identity hold ==

* First name: John; last name at birth: Glasgow (record spelling varies Glasgow/Glasco); male.
* Birth and death: unknown. The first known dated appearance is the will of 9 October 1849; this is not a birth-year estimate. Alive and conveying land on 30 October 1856.
* Birthplace and death place: unknown. Recorded residence: Prescott, Canada West (now Ontario), in 1856. Occupation in B13: watchmaker.
* Wife: Jane, releasing dower in B13; surname and further identity unknown.
* Brother: [[Glasgow-4150|Robert Glasgow of Prescott]], identified by the will and matched deed share.

=== Biography ==

'''John Glasgow''', also recorded as John Glasco, was a brother of [[Glasgow-4150|Robert Glasgow of Prescott]], Canada West. Robert's will, dated 9 October 1849, gives one sixth of his estate to his brother John. The same will names brothers Daniel, Samuel and Thomas, sister Mary, and the children of brother James as beneficiaries.<ref name="will">Robert Glasgow of Prescott, will dated 9 October 1849, registered 13 November 1856, Prescott land registry, volume B, instrument B12, printed pp. 9–10, images 35–36; [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSLG-LWY9-S FamilySearch image 35], [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSLG-LWYM-6 image 36]. The original clauses and date line were visually inspected 18 October 2026.</ref>

On 30 October 1856, John Glasgow/Glasco of Prescott, described as a watchmaker, and his wife Jane conveyed their one-sixth interest to Samuel Glasgow, Prescott merchant, for £125. Jane released her dower interest. The conveyance covers Town Lot 12 on the south side of Dibble Street and part of Lot 11, a 3,400-square-foot strip. The deed was registered 13 November 1856.<ref name="b13">Indenture B13, dated 30 October 1856, registered 13 November 1856, Prescott land registry, volume B, printed pp. 10–11, image 36; [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSLG-LWYM-6 FamilySearch image 36]. Original instrument visually inspected 18 October 2026.</ref>

The records establish his Prescott residence and watchmaking occupation by 1856. They do not establish his birth date or place, death, parents, or Jane's surname.

== Research Notes ==

The identification of the will's brother-beneficiary with B13's grantor is '''strongly supported (98% subjective confidence)''': the deed transfers exactly the one-sixth share allocated to John in the will, in the same Prescott property, seven years later. The will's registration request was witnessed by Alexander Glasgow; his role was witness, not beneficiary.<ref name="will" /><ref name="b13" />

[[Glasgow-3904|Alexander Glasgow-3904]] is the same tentative father hypothesis used for the related Prescott brothers Thomas, Daniel, Samuel and Robert. Extending that existing hypothesis to John is assessed at '''27%''', uncertain. It is not independent support for Alexander's paternity and must not raise the existing estimate. No mother is inferred. The will names no parents.

No existing John profile is selected. All six comparisons above remain open because an earlier or later residence elsewhere does not establish incompatible identity for a possible migrant. Further linking evidence could come from Canadian vital, religious, occupational or property records connecting a candidate's spouse/household to Jane and the Prescott share.

The 2007 online discussion supplies the derivative tradition naming Alexander and Rosanna as Thomas Glasgow’s parents, not a newly inspected record; it is retained as context only and does not establish this identity.<ref>“Glasgow Family,” Genes Reunited discussion board, 2007, https://www.genesreunited.co.uk/boards/board/general_topics/thread/465937 (prior review reused; not freshly inspected for this draft).</ref>
