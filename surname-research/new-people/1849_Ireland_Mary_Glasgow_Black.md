== Biography ==

'''Mary Glasgow''' was the sister of [[Glasgow-4150|Robert Glasgow of Prescott]], Canada West. In his will dated 9 October 1849, Robert gives one sixth of his estate to his sister Mary and a separate one sixth collectively to the children of his brother James.<ref name="will">Robert Glasgow of Prescott, will dated 9 October 1849, registered 13 November 1856, Prescott land registry, volume B, instrument B12, printed pp. 9–10, images 35–36; [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSLG-LWY9-S FamilySearch image 35], [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSLG-LWYM-6 image 36]. Original clauses and date line visually inspected 18 October 2026.</ref>

A deed dated 15 September 1869 identifies her as “Mary Black, formerly Mary Glasgow,” sister of the late Robert Glasgow of Prescott. Registered on 1 July 1870, C298 conveys the shares of her six children to Mary Eno Glasgow, widow of Samuel Glasgow of Prescott, for $800. The deed expressly traces the children's interests to Robert's will: each had one sixth of Mary's one-sixth share. The property was Town Lot 12 and part of Lot 11 on the south side of Dibble Street in Prescott.<ref name="c298">Grant and quitclaim C298, dated 15 September 1869, registered 1 July 1870, Prescott land registry, volume C, pp. 477–482, images 277–280; [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSLG-LQTY-K FamilySearch image 277], [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSLG-LQT5-R image 278], [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSLG-LQTP-M image 279], [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSLG-LQT2-C image 280]. The relationship clause on printed p. 477 was visually reread 18 October 2026; later execution details reuse the maintained original inspection of 13 September 2026.</ref>

The six children named in C298 are:

* John Black, yeoman of Drumoolish, County Londonderry, Ireland; his wife Martha Black also joined the conveyance to release dower.
* Rose Ann Black, spinster of Drumoolish.
* Jane Miller of Newburgh, Orange County, New York; her husband Robert Miller, yeoman, joined.
* Agnes Black, spinster of Newburgh.
* Thomasina Montgomery of Legrand, Marshall County, Iowa; her husband Mathew Montgomery, yeoman, joined.
* Elizabeth Black, formerly of Newburgh, spinster, deceased unmarried and intestate. The deed calls the five living children her lawful brother, sisters and heirs. They conveyed her inherited share along with their own.

The deed records execution in stages: John, Rose Ann and Martha at Coleraine on 27 November 1869; Jane Miller and Thomasina Montgomery at Newburgh on 29 April 1870; and Agnes's separate attestation at Coleraine, sworn 4 June 1870. These are execution places and dates, not Mary's residence. Rose Ann signed by mark in the registered copy.<ref name="c298" />

No source cited here establishes Mary's birth or death, her husband's forename, or the identity of her parents beyond the documented sibling relationship to Robert.

== Research Notes ==

Mary's relationship as sister of Robert is '''proved (99%)''' by the direct statement in C298; Robert's 1849 will independently names a sister Mary, and C298 also traces the children's six shares to that will. The documents establish one consistent record-defined relationship, not a proven match to any existing Mary Glasgow profile.<ref name="will" /><ref name="c298" />

[[Glasgow-3904|Alexander Glasgow-3904]] is the tentative father hypothesis already applied to the related Prescott brothers Thomas, Daniel, Samuel and Robert. Extending that same hypothesis to their documented sister Mary is assessed at '''27%''', uncertain. This adds no independent support for the father's identity and does not change the existing estimate. Her mother remains unknown; the records do not name either parent.

The local historical catalogue check did not identify an established Mary Black/Glasgow profile. It was not a comprehensive search of all WikiTree profiles or records. Further work could compare Irish and North American records for the named children, while keeping each child's stated residence distinct from Mary's unknown residence.

The 2007 online discussion supplies the derivative tradition naming Alexander and Rosanna as Thomas Glasgow’s parents, not a newly inspected original; it is retained only as context and does not establish Mary's identity.<ref>“Glasgow Family,” Genes Reunited discussion board, 2007, https://www.genesreunited.co.uk/boards/board/general_topics/thread/465937 (prior review reused; not freshly inspected for this draft).</ref>
