== Biography ==

Thomas is indexed as Thomas Glasgo, father of Joseph Glasgo, in a Campbell County, Virginia death entry dated 1 September 1877. Rose is indexed as the deceased’s mother. The deceased was described as a colored farmer aged 80, with birthplace Campbell. The original register image has been inspected; it writes the parents as Thomas & Rose Glasgo and informant J. Glasgo, Son.<ref name="Campbell 1877">"Virginia, Deaths and Burials, 1853-1912", FamilySearch, [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X5Y6-54C Joseph Glasgo, 1 September 1877], film 2056976, image 45; original Campbell death register, James River District,1877, film2056976, image 45, inspected 23October2026; [https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:S3HT-6LS7-3D7 original image].</ref>

The deceased is the preferred, probable identification of [[Glasgow-2573|Joseph Glasgow]], the Richmond-born Roanoke man whose family with Lucy Gilbert is described in the 1866 cohabitation account.<ref name="Roanoke 1866">CSSR, Roanoke College, [https://cssr.roanoke.edu/s/genarchives/item/4703 Joe Glasgow], derived from Roanoke County 1866 cohabitation register, p. 2 line 37; online account inspected 23 October 2026, original not inspected.</ref> The complete Thomas-to-Roanoke-Joseph relationship is assessed as 74% probable. The younger Joseph’s later Campbell household with named siblings and mother Lucy supplies family context; it does not independently name Thomas.

== Research Notes ==

The death index’s age 80 implies about 1797, whereas the 1870 census age 80 suggests about 1790 for the Roanoke man. Campbell and Richmond are different stated birthplaces. The consort column also repeats Unmarried rather than naming Lucy. These unresolved differences prevent a confident identification. Companion indexes of the same death are not additional witnesses.

Thomas’s own dates, birthplace, residence and parents are unknown. The original writes the parents as Thomas & Rose Glasgo; this surname is not proof of Thomas’s birth surname. The naming of Thomas and Rose as parents does not establish a marriage or Rose’s birth surname. No connection to an existing numbered Thomas, an enslaver’s family or a common Y-DNA founder is established.
