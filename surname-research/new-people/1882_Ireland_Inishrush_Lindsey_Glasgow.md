== Biography ==
'''Lindsey Glasgow''' lived at Inishrush, Tamlaght O'Crilly, County Londonderry, on 21 November 1882. On that date he registered the death of his brother, [[Glasgow-4022|William Glasgow]], a 27-year-old bachelor farmer who died at Inishrush. The return identifies Lindsey as William's brother and as present at the death.<ref>Irish General Register Office, civil death return, Ballymena registration district, Portglenone sub-district, 1882, entry for William Glasgow; [https://www.irishgenealogy.ie/files/civil/deaths_returns/deaths_1882/06388/4834124.pdf image 4834124], accessed 29 August 2026.</ref>

No record yet found names Lindsey and William's parents. The Tamlaght O'Crilly Lower family gravestone calls William a grandson of [[Glasgow-3902|Adam Glasgow]], but does not name William's parents or Lindsey.<ref>[https://www.oneirishrover.com/tamlaght-graveyard/ “Tamlaght O'Crilly Lower Graveyard”], Glasgow gravestone transcription naming William as Adam's grandson, accessed 29 August 2026.</ref>
