== Biography ==
Andrew Glasgow is a named signatory in a Leith and St Anthony’s journeymen-society notice dated 3 February 1779, under the grouped Preses to annotation. Notice dated Leith, 3 February 1779, printed 9 February. The grouped signatures are annotated Preses to; the brace does not independently assign Andrew a particular trade/society. The notice opposes proposed repeal of penal statutes; no individual religion, birth, household or family is inferred. No existing Andrew is identified. Three local candidates and other compatible chronological namesakes remain unresolved; missing records and uncertain bounds do not establish exclusions.
