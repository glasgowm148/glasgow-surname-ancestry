== Research Notes ==
One or more surname-only shipmasters, with the two passenger reports clearly one reprinted notice. The January inward and February clearance entries are compatible but unproved continuity. Glasgow-3324, Glasgow-2474 and Glasgow-4124 remain possible comparisons; no forename or family identity is established.

Bounded local public dossier and documentary-subject comparison; no external ship register or exhaustive duplicate search.

The 1768 Lady Forbes William, 1754 Robert master, 1777 James master and 1792/1796 William-vessel records supply comparisons, not an identity bridge. Missing occupations or death dates are not exclusions.

Glasgow-4120 and Glasgow-4101 remain weak chronological namesakes. Glasgow-1505’s c.1783 death is an unresolved estimate: poor fit for 1788 if accurate, not a secure exclusion.

Voyage origins and destinations are not birthplaces or proof of the captain’s residence.

[[Glasgow-3324]]: Uncertain 1763 Larne birth and 1816 death permit 1788. Prior research compares this profile with the independently named 1792 William master; neither the Ann notices nor that later role identifies the 1788 captain.
[[Glasgow-2474]]: Robert born 1716 would be about 72. Prior public research reports a 1738 Irvine shipmaster, not re-inspected here; no bridge to Ann or Larne.
[[Glasgow-4124]]: Prior research reports a 1733 mariners-list surgeon; no later life, brig Ann or Larne bridge. No James forename in the 1788 notices.

Inspect brig Ann ownership, port entry/clearance, passenger and shipping registers around 1788; seek the captain’s forename and signatures before linking him to the Larne family or another master.
