== Biography ==
A newspaper shipping paragraph published on 17 June 1766 names Mr. Glasco among those who sailed in the King of Prussia, Barry, for Parkgate.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1050032001/ Freeman’s Journal, 17 June 1766, p.2], original inspected 5 October 2026.</ref>

A paragraph headed 22 in the 23 August 1766 issue lists Glasco among passengers who arrived in the Fortescue-packet from Holyhead.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1050032672/ Freeman’s Journal, 23 August 1766, p.3], original inspected 5 October 2026.</ref> Neither notice gives his forename or family. They may describe different people.

== Research Notes ==
Two surname-only passenger notices are recorded together without assuming they describe one person or a return journey. No parent or existing-profile relationship is changed.
