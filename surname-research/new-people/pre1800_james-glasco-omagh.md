== Biography ==
James Glasco is reported in the marriage column published on 5 September 1786 as a publican of Omagh, aged 61, marrying Jane Murray of the same place, aged 16.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1050075355/ Freeman’s Journal, 5 September 1786, p.3], original inspected 5 October 2026.</ref> If accurate, the reported ages imply approximate births in 1724–1725 and 1769–1770 respectively. The exact ceremony date is unstated.

Advertisements in the Northern Star on 11, 18 and 25 May 1793 name Mr. James Glasco, Omagh, as a freight or passage contact for the Hope, sailing to Newcastle and Philadelphia. The 25 May copy postpones sailing to the 25th instant.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/72021067/ Northern Star, 11 May 1793, p.1]; [https://www.newspapers.com/image/72021086/ Northern Star, 18 May 1793, p.4]; [https://www.newspapers.com/image/72021100/ Northern Star, 25 May 1793, p.1], originals inspected 5 October 2026.</ref> These probably concern the same Omagh man. They do not establish that he was a passenger or emigrated.

== Research Notes ==
The 1786 groom and 1793 Omagh contact are probably the same documentary person. Glasgow-3944 is the leading compatible candidate; all listed alternatives remain possible. No record bridges the Omagh notices to an existing profile. No parent or existing-profile relationship is changed.
