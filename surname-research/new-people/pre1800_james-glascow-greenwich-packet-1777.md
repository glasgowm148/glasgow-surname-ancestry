# James Glascow, master of Greenwich Packet

HOLD — documented surname; identity unresolved

== Biography ==
Greenwich Packet: James Glascow, master; James Young, owner; from Philadelphia to Winyew, Carolina; with rum, bread, flour and porter. Three issues preserve the same vessel-list passage. The visible heading is “By the Orpheus and Daphne”; no overall heading or explicit genre label was found in the inspected area. The purpose of the list remains unresolved. The destination spelling is uncertain and not normalized. The owner is James Young, not James Glascow.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/34421452/ The Public Advertiser, 1777-05-14, p.2], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/396765804/ The Ipswich Journal, 1777-05-17, p.1], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/971522063/ The Gloucester Journal, 1777-05-19, p.1], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref>
