== Biography ==
James Glasgow and Ezekiel Glasgow appear in the list of letters remaining in Charlestown’s General Post Office, dated 21 August 1775. Alphabetical entries printed surname first: Glasgow James, followed by a small 2 marker, and Glasgow Ezekiel. The marker is not read as an age. Letter-list appearance alone proves no kinship, occupation, continuous residence or identity with later James records.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/605068969/ The South-Carolina and American General Gazette, 1775-08-25, p.1], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026.</ref>

[[Glasgow-804]]: Born 11 Nov 1735, Ayr, exact; died 19 May 1788, Newberry, South Carolina, exact. A James Glasgow documented in South Carolina and alive during 1775 is geographically and chronologically compatible with a Charleston addressee. No record connects him to this post-office entry. His exact 1788 death is a concrete conflict with identifying him as the James Glasgow listed in Edinburgh in December 1789.
[[Glasgow-1144]]: Born 24 May 1731, Kilwaughter, County Antrim, exact; death c.1793, Newberry, South Carolina, estimated. A James Glasgow with a South Carolina location and a 1775 will is chronologically compatible with the Charleston addressee, but the will does not bridge him to the letter list. The estimated c.1793 death is not evidence he lived to December 1789; it also is not a precise death boundary excluding the 1789 entry. The profile's exact birth and Newberry family are distinct from Glasgow-804's; the catalogue calls them similar, which does not establish identity or justify combining their family evidence.
