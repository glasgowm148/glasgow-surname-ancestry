== Biography ==
James Glasgow, Esq., Edinburgh, appears in the Mid-Lothian game-duty list of certificates issued since 8 September 1789. Entry reads Glasgow, James, Esq; Edinburgh. The list concerns issued game certificates, not a dated shooting event or a family relationship. Date scope is the list heading, not an exact certificate date. The clipping has a disclosed contrast adjustment, without reconstruction.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/601822968/ The Edinburgh Advertiser, 1789-12-11, p.8], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026.</ref>
