# James Glasgow, Wilmington tannery sale advertiser,1782

HOLD — documented record; profile assignment qualified

[[Category:Glasgow Name Study]]

== Biography ==
James Glasgow, at Christiana Bridge in Newcastle county, advertises for private sale the former Charles Whitlock tanyard in the borough of Wilmington; notice dated 5 August 1782. Five issues repeat one private-sale advertisement dated 5 August 1782. James is the signed advertiser/contact; ownership, tanning occupation, completed sale, age and kinship are not proved. Printed places are borough of Wilmington and Christiana Bridge, Newcastle county; Delaware identification is a geographical interpretation, not the Philadelphia paper’s locality. The signed Wilmington/Christiana Bridge advertiser remains unidentified. Two Cecil County chronological/regional James profiles are compatible, without a property or individual bridge. == Research Notes ==
The signed Wilmington/Christiana Bridge advertiser remains unidentified. Two Cecil County chronological/regional James profiles are compatible, without a property or individual bridge. The North Carolina secretary is another adult namesake but has a different documentary region; do not merge him solely because Wilmington is also a North Carolina place.

Approved bounded QMD queries for Christiana/Glasgow and tannery/Glasgow returned no relevant reviewed identity note. Catalogue James profiles with Cecil/Delaware places were checked; this is not an exhaustive external duplicate search.

Glasgow-2868 has exact 1720 birth and estimated c.1784 Cecil Marylanddeath; Glasgow-3290 has uncertain 1758 Cecil Marylandbirth/1789 death. Neither has an inspected Christiana Bridge or tanyard source.

Missing property or occupation fields do not exclude either candidate. Advertiser need not be the owner or a tanner. The future possession date 1 November is an offered sale term, not completed transfer.

[[Glasgow-2868]]: Adult chronology and later Cecil Maryland regional context are compatible with nearby New Castle, without a Wilmington/Christiana Bridge or property bridge.
[[Glasgow-3290]]: Uncertain 1758 Cecil Marylandbirth permits an adult advertiser in 1782; no tanyard, Whitlock or Christiana Bridge identity bridge.
[[Glasgow-634]]: Adult chronology fits, but its North Carolina office context is a different regional lead. No connection to the Delaware tannery; missing corroboration alone is not an exclusion.

Inspect Wilmington/New Castle deeds, tannery title and Charles Whitlock estate records, Christiana Bridge tax lists, and the candidates’ own family evidence for an individual bridge.
