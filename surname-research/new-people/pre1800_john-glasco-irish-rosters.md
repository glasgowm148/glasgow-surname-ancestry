== Biography ==
An association report published on 29 May 1798 names John Glasco as PRESENT at the Black-Rock Association, Half Barony of Rathdown, County Dublin, meeting on 8 May 1798.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1214061848/ Freeman’s Journal, 29 May 1798, p.4], original inspected 5 October 2026.</ref>

A roster published on 24 September 1799, page 2, names John Glasco; its previous page contains the heading COUNTY OF LONDONDERRY.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1214179229/ Freeman’s Journal, 24 September 1799, p.2]; [https://www.newspapers.com/image/1214179139/ preceding page, p.1], originals inspected 5 October 2026.</ref> Whether these refer to one person is unresolved. The category of the 1799 roster has not been established.

== Research Notes ==
Two occurrences are consolidated for comparison; their shared identity is unresolved. Neither should be assigned to a profile solely by surname or county. No parent or existing-profile relationship is changed.
