== Biography ==
John Glascow is an adjoining landholder in a West-Nottingham Township, Chester County execution-sale notice for 22½ acres, late Michael Dintz’s estate, bounded by Abraham Bunting, John Glascow and others. Planned sale 11 May at one o’clock on the premises; Henry Hayes, sheriff; notice dated 28 April 1773. John is the neighbouring landholder, not the deceased estate owner, buyer or executor. Sale completion is unproved. Two issues print the same notice.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/39401494/ The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1773-04-28, p.1], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/39401765/ The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1773-05-05, p.5], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref>

John Glascow is appointed to provide for distressed families of Associators in actual service by the Chester County Committee of Inspection and Observation, in a resolution dated 13 August 1776. Full heading and roster inspected. William Evans is chairman. This establishes an appointment, not that John was a soldier, Associator, recipient or relative of anyone listed. Same-name/county context with the 1773 land notice is compatible, but personal identity across the two events is unproved.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/39398635/ The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1776-08-28, p.3], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref>
