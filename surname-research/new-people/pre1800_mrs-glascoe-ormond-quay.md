== Biography ==
Mrs Glascoe’s shop on Ormond Quay, Dublin, is reported with shutters taken down and several panes broken. Passers-by alarmed the house; she requested police protection, which the paper says was refused. Reported attempted robbery on the preceding Saturday, around one to two in the morning.
