# Mrs Glascoe, Ormond Quay shopkeeper

HOLD — documentary person; identity unresolved

[[Category:Glasgow Name Study]]

== Biography ==
Mrs Glascoe’s shop on Ormond Quay, Dublin, is reported with shutters taken down and several panes broken. Passers-by alarmed the house; she requested police protection, which the paper says was refused. Reported attempted robbery on the preceding Saturday, around one to two in the morning. No given name or husband is supplied; neither Dublin candidate is identified. <ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1050062764/ Freeman’s Journal, 1787-10-09, p.4], original record inspected 5 October 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==
An unnamed Dublin woman is directly reported. Ann (Duffy) Glasco and Mary (King) Glasco remain possible compatible candidates; neither has a shop or household bridge.

Check Dublin directories, property leases, police records and advertisements for an Ormond Quay Glasco household. Do not infer a particular husband from Mrs alone.

[[Duffy-6026]]: possible candidate. Ann (Duffy) Glasco has an uncertain 1756 Dublin birth and a recorded Thomas Glasco spouse. No Ormond Quay shop, given-name or household bridge.
[[King-67628]]: possible candidate. Mary (King) Glasco has an uncertain 1755 County Dublin birth and a recorded Thomas Glasco spouse. No Ormond Quay shop, given-name or household bridge.
