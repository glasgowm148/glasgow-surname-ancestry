# Robert Glasgow and father Andrew, miller at Calder, 1772

HOLD — documented record; profile assignment qualified

== Biography ==
Robert Glasgow is listed as son of Andrew Glasgow, miller at Calder, in an Edinburgh magistrates’ notice dated 28 November 1772. The notice offers one guinea for discovery within the city/liberties of listed persons previously banished or bound to leave. The printed father–son statement is documentary family evidence; it does not prove a particular robbery conviction, apprehension, reward payment or identity with the 1767 prisoner. Neither existing Wiki Tree profile is identified. The notice explicitly names Robert’s father Andrew. Compatible existing Robert and Andrew profiles remain possible assignments only. Robert-1445’s tree-only father James and sibling Andrew-3052 are unverified leads; no parent replacement follows from identifying only the documentary family. Calder alone does not distinguish East from West Calder.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/395731868/ The Caledonian Mercury, 1772-11-28, p.3].</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/395732022/ The Caledonian Mercury, 1772-11-30, p.3].</ref>
