## Identity review — 6 October 2026

Record bridge between the two Edinburgh notices: the 21 Dec 1767 sentence banishes 'Robert Glasgow, George Garnock, Niel M'Kinnon and Donald M'Donald' (clip OCR), and the magistrates' list of banished persons of 28 Nov 1772 names, among others, 'Neil Mackinnon, son of Normand Mackinnon chairbearer', 'George Garnoch, son of John Garnoch serjeant in the 44th regiment' and 'Robert Glasgow, son of Andrew Glasgow miller at Calder' (clip OCR). Three of the four 1767 co-convicts reappear together as banished persons, so the 1767 prisoner is (probably) Robert son of Andrew Glasgow, miller at Calder. WikiTree: Glasgow-1445 (bapt.
