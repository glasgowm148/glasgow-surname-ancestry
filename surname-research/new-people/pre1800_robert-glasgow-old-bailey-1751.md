# Robert Glasgow, Old Bailey and Newgate reports, 1751

HOLD — documented surname; identity unresolved

[[Category:Glasgow Name Study]]

== Biography ==
Robert Glasgow is reported among nine Old Bailey convicts receiving judgment of death, for highway robbery of Joseph Weedon’s gold ring, silver shoe buckles and other things. A conviction/sentence report. The Last Saturday trial reference has no saved report dateline; no exact trial date inferred. This is not proof of actual execution, death or family identity.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/971486582/ The Gloucester Journal, 1751-07-16, p.2], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026.</ref>

Robert Glasgow, condemned for robbing Joseph Weedon on the highway, is included among Newgate prisoners ordered for execution on Monday next. Same named case as the 16 July sentence report, but a different reported action. John Young is the prisoner reprieved for life. This execution order does not establish that Robert was executed. Relative Yesterday and Monday next are not converted to issue-based dates.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/979343886/ The Western Flying Post; or, Sherborne and Yeovil Mercury, 1751-07-29, p.2], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026.</ref>
