== Biography ==
Samuel Glasgow is listed with Capt. Murray’s company among soldiers of the first Maryland regiment ordered to rendezvous in a Baltimore notice. Notice dated Baltimore, 19 October 1777, signed Joseph Ford, captain in the first Maryland regiment, orders listed soldiers to rendezvous on the 10th of next month. It threatens deserter punishment for failing to attend and offers to seek pardons for those returning after absence without leave. It does not establish an existing desertion conviction; William Davis is a separate following person. Glasgow-1134 is a possible Maryland/age candidate.
