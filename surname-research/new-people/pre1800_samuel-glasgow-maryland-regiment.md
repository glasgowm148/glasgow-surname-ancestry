# Samuel Glasgow, First Maryland Regiment

HOLD — documentary person; identity unresolved

[[Category:Glasgow Name Study]]

== Biography ==
A notice dated Baltimore, 19 October 1777, signed Joseph Ford, orders absent soldiers of the First Maryland Regiment, including Samuel Glasgow, to return by 10 November. It threatens apprehension and punishment as deserters and offers influence toward pardons. Samuel is in the final group without an assigned company. Captain Murray’s heading applies to a preceding group. This is a recall and allegation, not proof of adjudicated desertion, punishment or return. <ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/590968568/ Maryland Gazette, 1777-10-23, p.2], original record inspected 5 October 2026.</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/41048973/ Maryland Gazette, 1777-11-06, p.2], original record inspected 5 October 2026.</ref>
