# Samuel Glasgow, Whitpain boundary landowner

HOLD — documentary person; identity unresolved

[[Category:Glasgow Name Study]]

== Biography ==
Samuel Glasgow is an adjoining landowner in a Whitpain Township execution-sale notice for about fifty acres, late Isaac Williams’s estate, bounded by John Saunders, Samuel Glasgow, John Eldridge and Thomas Blackledge. February planned sale 17 February; August planned sale 28 August. Reprints are issue appearances, not independent land transactions. <ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/39398014/ The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1770-02-01, p.3], original record inspected 5 October 2026.</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/39398170/ The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1770-02-15, p.2], original record inspected 5 October 2026.</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/39401453/ The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1770-08-16, p.3], original record inspected 5 October 2026.</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/39401610/ The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1770-08-23, p.6], original record inspected 5 October 2026.</ref>

Samuel Glasgow is a boundary landowner for thirty acres, part of a fifty-acre Whitpain Township tract, late Isaac Williams’s estate, also bounded by John Saunders and John Eldridge. A sale is advertised for 9 August at William Lawrence’s house in Plymouth Township. Three issue appearances of one notice. The separate Limerick property’s 7 August sale does not apply here. No completed sale, occupation or kinship is established. <ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/39400791/ The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1770-07-12, p.3], original record inspected 5 October 2026.</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/39400983/ The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1770-07-19, p.5], original record inspected 5 October 2026.</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/39401146/ The Pennsylvania Gazette, 1770-07-26, p.6], original record inspected 5 October 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==
The repeated Isaac Williams estate notices probably refer to one adjoining landowner. None identifies an American family or connects him to the soldier or justice.

Trace Whitpain deeds and tax returns beside the named neighbours; distinguish boundary ownership from residence and completed sale. The three U.S. Samuel contexts could connect, but evidence does not make that link probable.

[[Glasgow-3175]]: possible candidate. Uncertain 1740 Ulster birth and 1801 Hanover Township, Washington County death are chronologically compatible. Strongest contextual candidate for the 1791 county office; no land or service identity bridge.
[[Glasgow-1134]]: possible candidate. Estimated c.1749 Cecil County, Maryland birth and c.1834 Ohio death. Strongest contextual candidate for the Maryland regiment; no service, land or Washington office bridge.
[[Glasgow-1492]]: possible candidate. Estimated c.1726 Moneymore birth and c.1824 United States death allow these dates. The separate 1766 Gorteen occurrence does not establish American migration or identity.
