# Thomas Glascoe of Tinicum Island

HOLD — documentary person; profile identity unresolved

[[Category:Glasgow Name Study]]

== Biography ==
Thomas Glascoe signed a STOP THIEF notice dated 9 April 1799, reporting $115 stolen from his house on Tinicum Island, within four miles of old Chester on the Delaware, on 31 March. He accused a man calling himself William Steel and offered $20 for recovery of the money, or $15 for the thief only. The notice appeared on 9 April and was reprinted on 11 April.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/586542475/ Aurora General Advertiser, 1799-04-09, p.3], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/586542512/ Aurora General Advertiser, 1799-04-11, p.1], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref>

== Research Notes ==
Thomas is the advertiser and reported victim, not the alleged thief. The notice states his residence but no age, birth, death, spouse, parents or children. Chronologically compatible Thomas Glasco profiles in Dublin remain possible namesakes; no migration or Delaware identity bridge has been found. Reprints represent one reported event. No inheritance or family relationship is established.
