== Biography ==
Gentleman Cadet William Allen Glasgow is announced as second lieutenant in the Corps of Royal Engineers, vice Lefebvre, promoted, dated 1 October 1795. The notice is under Office of Ordnance, October 9; its preceding all dated 1 October 1795 applies through Dated as above. Both William and Allen are printed personal forenames across the line break. This is an announced commission, not family proof, an age statement or independently checked service history. A separately documented William Allen Glasgow is announced as an engineer officer.
