# William Glascoe, Maryland notice

HOLD — documentary person; identity unresolved

[[Category:Glasgow Name Study]]

== Biography ==
A Lancaster Goal notice dated 13 October says John M’Connel, about 35, imprisoned as a suspected runaway servant, claims to belong to William Glascoe in Maryland, about forty miles from Baltimore. William is the attributed master, not the jailed man. John Carson belongs to a separate prisoner’s account. No link to the 1760 defendant, a shipmaster, or a family is proved. <ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/39392109/ Pennsylvania Gazette, 1775-10-25, p.1], original record inspected 5 October 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==
Known through a jailed man’s attributed statement; no connection to the 1760 London defendant is established.
