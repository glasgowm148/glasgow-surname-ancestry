# William Glascoe / Glascow, 1760 defendant

HOLD — documentary person; identity unresolved

[[Category:Glasgow Name Study]]

== Biography ==
William Glascoe is among prisoners reported sentenced to transportation after Old Bailey trials, for stealing a sack of malt from a warehouse, valued in the newspaper at £4 10s. Strong candidate case match to William Glascow, Old Bailey session 16 April 1760, t17600416-27. The newspaper’s “Yesterday” and values differ from the proceedings; the session date is not asserted as the exact individual trial day. Sentence does not prove an actual voyage or Maryland arrival. <ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/35690357/ Public Ledger or Daily Register of Commerce and Intelligence, 1760-04-18, p.2], original record inspected 5 October 2026.</ref>

== Research Notes ==
The newspaper and the primary Old Bailey case are strongly associated; no origin, family or later Maryland identity is proved.

The matching proceedings t17600416-27 name William Glascow, theft from Benjamin Smith’s warehouse on 15 March: a sack valued 6d and malt 10s; verdict Guilty, 4s 10d, followed by Transportation. Preserve the newspaper’s different £4 10s value and relative trial date. Locate actual transportation and subsequent records before linking the 1775 Maryland notice.
