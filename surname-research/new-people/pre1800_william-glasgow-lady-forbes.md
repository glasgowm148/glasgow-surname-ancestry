== Biography ==
William Glasgow is named as master of the Lady Forbes in the Sutton shipping casualty report. The paper reports the crew saved. A Scottish vessel designation does not establish the master’s residence or origin.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1050136699/ The Public Register, or, Freeman's Journal, 1768-01-23, p.2].</ref><ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/395700744/ The Caledonian Mercury, 1768-02-06, p.2].</ref>
