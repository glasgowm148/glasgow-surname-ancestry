# William Glasgow, master of the Lady Forbes

HOLD — documentary person; profile identity unresolved

[[Category:Glasgow Name Study]]

== Biography ==
William Glasgow is named as master of the Lady Forbes in a newspaper report published 23 January 1768. The vessel is described separately as “of Glasgow,” carrying rum for Waterford and driven ashore near Sutton; the crew were reported saved.<ref>[https://www.newspapers.com/image/1050136699/ The Public Register, or, Freeman's Journal, 1768-01-23, p.2], original passage visually inspected 5 October 2026; issue metadata supplied by Newspapers.com.</ref>

== Research Notes ==
The notice supplies no age, residence, birthplace, parents, spouse or children. Chronologically compatible William profiles remain candidates only. No evidence connects the master to [[Glasgow-4120]], [[Glasgow-4101]] or [[Glasgow-1505]]. The 1796 brig William advertisement does not name its captain William: that is the ship’s name.
