## The Ninian-Leith-Edinburgh craft model

Ninian's 1613 testament directly names son Robert. William occurs, but his
formerly asserted sonship is not securely readable; the later Leith mealmaker
is only a possible identity. That William's son Archibald was apprenticed
to an Edinburgh cordiner in 1639. Against that documented family background:

- Margaret Glasgow married a cordiner in Edinburgh in 1611;
- Barbara Glasgow married a cordiner there in 1616;
- Isobel Glasgow married a tailor there in 1619; and
- another Barbara Glasgow had a South Leith marriage/proclamation entry in 1611.

The repeated trade and Leith/Edinburgh geography makes Ninian's household or
its immediate collateral circle relevant. The corrected Andrew-of-Corsoun
testament independently names daughters Jonet and Isobel. Their documentary
profiles have since been merged into the leading Mid Calder and Edinburgh
adult identities. The later records do not carry Andrew's name or Corsoun
residence, so both identity joins remain probable and the parent edges
uncertain rather than record-proved continuities.

## Structural recommendation

The strongest older uncertain parent edges for John-1095 to William-1098,
William-1098 to Elizabeth-3915 and James-1168 to Matthew-3908 have already
been applied. Jonet-3183 now carries Andrew-3163 as an uncertain father after
the Glasgow-4096 merge; James-991 is a superseded candidate. Keep
Jonat-3262's parentage and identity with Jonet-3183 unstructured pending the
1609/1624 household test. Michael remains an unstructured brother/cousin
hypothesis, while Robert-3919 has merged into Robert-1096. The three adult
Barbara profiles remain unmerged pending the original 1611 entries. Do not
connect Nancy, Margaret Glasko or George Glascoe merely to eliminate an island.
