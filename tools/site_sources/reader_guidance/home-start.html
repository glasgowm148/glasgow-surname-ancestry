How to use this site

  1. Search for a name, or choose the family or place you know.
  2. On a person’s page, read the summary first: dates, family, and the label on each relationship. Each fact lists its sources.
  3. A label such as “Imported / unassessed” means the detail comes from a family tree and has not yet been checked against a record. How to read the evidence explains every label.
  4. Have a record, a photograph or a correction?

The people index includes WikiTree profiles and people found in historical records. Some have detailed research; others are starting points for further work.
