    How to read the evidence

    DocumentedA cited record states this directly, and the research has checked that the record belongs to this person.

    Strongly supportedSeveral independent, consistent records point to this, but no single record states it outright.

    ProbableThe evidence favours this over the alternatives, but it is not proved and could still change.

    PossibleConsistent with the evidence so far. A lead to test, not a conclusion.

    Unassessed tree linkCopied from the family tree with no certainty mark. It has not been assessed against records.

    Profile leadReported on a tree profile; inspect its sources before relying on it.

    EstimatedA date or place inferred from other evidence, such as an age at death or a later census.

    People are grouped by household, place or related records to help investigate possible family connections. Being in the same group does not prove that people were related.
