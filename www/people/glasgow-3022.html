Reviewed 24 October 2026. Jane Glasgow-3022 → Robert Robertson, later Glasgow is a probable father relationship, assessed 78%, with profile identity still provisional. Robertson-30564’s captured biography lists a daughter called Philadelphia Jane, and the copied 1841 household account places Jane Robertson, age 20, in Robert’s Holly Walk household. Together these are credible evidence for the relationship, but neither item independently identifies WikiTree profile Glasgow-3022 as that daughter, and the census page has not been independently inspected. Identity and family

Father assessment — 24 October 2026

The captured WikiTree biography records Jane as born in 1804 in France and as a daughter of Robertson-30564 and Anne Glasgow-2874. Its family source note transcribes an 1841 household at Holly Walk, Leamington Priors: Robert Glasgow, age 65, of independent means, born in Scotland; Ann Glasgow, age 40; Mary Glasgow, age 19; Ann Robertson, age 18; Jane Robertson, age 20; Charlotte Robertson, age 10; and Wilhelmina Robertson, age 1, plus one male and four female servants. The note says Wilhelmina was the daughter of Robert’s eldest son, also Robert, and says the younger Anne Glasgow or Robertson appears in the household. It does not label Jane’s relationship to Robert.

The Robertson-30564 captured biography names four children: Robert (1811), Philadelphia Jane, Anne, and Charlotte Marie Cecilia. The “Jane Robertson” entry in the same household is a plausible match to Philadelphia Jane, but this is a profile-summary comparison, not a direct name-to-profile bridge. The unverified 1804-France profile field and copied age 20 cannot both be treated as established; neither source settles the discrepancy. Do not change the birth fields or infer the mother’s confidence from this father review.

The 78% assessment is subjective and reflects a credible family and naming match with unresolved identity mapping. The existing father claim is C-5dc8ca93d3bc, unmarked and without an individual previous percentage; citation evidence E-47788b946002 is the copied household note.

Located census transcript and daughter-in-law comparison — 31 October 2026

The Holly Walk household now has a precise census locator: HO107/1135, enumeration district 10, folio 11, page 15, Leamington Priors (FreeCEN district Kenilworth). FreeCEN transcribes Mary Glasgow 19 and Wilhelmina Robertson 1, both born Scotland, with Robert Glasgow 65. It gives no relationship labels. This may be the source of the old copied WikiTree paragraph, so it is one census record, not independent family testimony.

A specific daughter-in-law alternative can now be tested. Paterson’s 1866 county history, printed pp. 513–514 (PDF 249–250), says the elder Robert’s eldest son and heir, born 22 May 1811, married Mary-Wilhelmina, daughter of John Campbell of Stonefield, on 11 July 1839; their children include Wilhelmina-Colquhoun. The checked existing bride is Campbell-38211 and groom Robertson-Glasgow-11. A married Mary beside the infant of this same family could be that daughter-in-law rather than another daughter of the household head. This is a new comparison, not an established identification.

The age problem remains real: FreeCEN gives Mary 19 in 1841, while Campbell-38211’s public profile gives 1816 and copies an 1851 age 34. The latter original is also unread. The infant’s full name and birth particulars are not established by FreeCEN. Paterson is a compiled family history, not an original marriage entry, and its adjacent Montgreenan descent narrative has known conflicts with the separately held court evidence. Do not transfer the book’s explicit John Campbell parentage to Glasgow-3021 before identifying the two Marys. Retain the selected Robert Robertson father at 45%, Jane’s father at 78%, all mother/vital fields and separate profiles; no father replacement, merge or confidence improvement is recorded.
