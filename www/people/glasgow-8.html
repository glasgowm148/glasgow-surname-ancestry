About Robert

Harvey Glasgow-13 is now a confidently identified son at 94%, superseding 58%. Other child and upstream grades remain unchanged. Faris’s inspected printed page 35 lists Harvey, born 22 September 1816, among the children of Robert B. and Rosannah (Finley) Glasgow. Entry 39 on page 62 independently supplies the full named son clause within the same book and agrees with Harvey-13’s exact birth date and 1874 death year. The father’s entry on page 35 identifies Robert B. as Robert Barclay Glasgow, born 20 January 1783 in Rockbridge County, married Rosannah Finley in 1809 and dead near Hanna City on 15 May 1861, matching Glasgow-8. This explicit, identity-matched compiled family account materially strengthens the selected father beyond the former 2011 tree-import-only basis. The complete Harvey-to-Robert profile pair is assessed at 94% subjective confidence. Faris (1990) is a retrospective compiled genealogy, not an original birth, marriage or probate record. Its family list and individual entry are one source, not independent witnesses; the imported pedigree may share that source. The underlying family papers and contemporary probate were not inspected. This is a reassessment of the same attached father, not a new father or merger. The mother, all vital fields, sibling grades and upstream father grades remain separately assessed and unchanged.

Peoria family evidence — 23 October 2026

Samuel’s reassessment: 58% → 92%, confident. Martha’s reassessment: 78% → 94%, confident.

The distinctive name and age fit Samuel Steele Glasgow-9, born in September 1814. Stewart’s established Peoria family and independently reviewed 94% paternal identification with Robert Barclay Glasgow-8 support extending the same father to this documented brother. This is a confident combined paternal inference, not a literal statement naming Samuel’s father in the obituary. 92% is a subjective assessment of the complete Samuel-9 to Robert-8 father identification. The 1902 county-history account of William Steele McCullough identifies his mother as Martha Glasgow, born in Adams County, Ohio, in 1810, and his maternal grandfather as Robert Glasgow. Martha’s husband John, their son’s 26 September 1835 birth, the Adams County origin and later Logan Township family identify the maintained Martha Steele Glasgow-11 account. This combined family identification supports Robert Barclay Glasgow-8 as her father beyond the earlier inherited placement. 94% is a subjective assessment of the complete Martha-11 to Robert-8 father identification. The original printed page has not been inspected; page 749 is a search lead, not a verified page citation. The source does not establish Rosanna’s maternity, Martha’s exact birthday or any older Glasgow ancestry.
