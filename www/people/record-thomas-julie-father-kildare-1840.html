Other research notes

Original family record and scope

The family roster gives Jno (John), 18; Thomas, 15; Daniel, 11; James, 5; Anne, 13, in that source order. The ship arrived on 5 October 1840. Charles’s paper records County Kildare as his native place and Julie’s paper says the same place as her husband. The native-place wording does not prove the children’s or ancestors’ birthplaces. Charles’s November 1800 statement concerns his baptism, not an exact birth. Julie’s age 38 in December 1840 conflicts with her sworn birth in December 1801; her approximate year remains qualified. Julie’s maiden surname Burns remains unverified. Charles’s mother is Anne Du[?]e, not his wife; the surname is not expanded. The two fathers called Thos are separate source actors: Charles’s father is a labourer, Julie’s a farmer. No marriage of either grandparent couple is inferred. Certification-column dates are not birthdays, and Daniel’s faint 20 October 1829 annotation is not independent birth proof. No exact Daniel birthday or existing vital field is corrected.

DERIVED conservative upper bound from the child’s reported baptism/birth, not an actual parental birth record. Charles reported baptism November 1800; Julie’s December 1801 sworn birth conflicts with age 38 in December 1840.

Sources: Charles original paper · Julie and family original paper · Official arrival guide.
