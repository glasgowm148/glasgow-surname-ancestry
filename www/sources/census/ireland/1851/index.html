Coverage, survival & limitations

1851 survives in fragments. Antrim, one Belfast ward and parts of Fermanagh retain material; Dublin city has a heads-of-household index. These are not complete county or national returns. Some returns record people absent and deaths since 1841, making the original page particularly useful for family research.

Irish census survivals and substitutes, 1821–1851

Most 1821, 1831, 1841 and 1851 returns were destroyed in the 1922 fire. Surviving original fragments include Antrim 1851; Belfast one ward 1851; Dublin city heads-of-household index 1851; Fermanagh 1821/1841/1851; Cavan 1821/1841; Cork 1841; Galway 1821; Offaly 1821; Meath 1821; Waterford 1841; and Londonderry/Derry 1831 with 1834 revisions.

The Derry 1831 material includes names of householders and counts by religion and sex, with 1834 revisions. It is not a person-by-person list of every household member. Search transcripts are finding aids; verify the scanned return and record omissions.
