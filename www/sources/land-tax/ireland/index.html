Ireland: Land, tax & valuation

Tenure, ownership, occupiers, leases, taxation and valuations. An occupier list is not a complete household census.

Coverage, survival & limitations

Irish Tithe Applotment Books

Compiled 1823–1837, generally listing occupiers of productive/tithable agricultural holdings, acreage/value and tithe. They usually identify occupiers rather than all household members and do not systematically cover urban land.

  • NAI Tithe Applotment Books

Irish Primary Valuation (Griffith’s Valuation)

Published 1847–1864, this property valuation lists occupiers, lessors and property amount/value. It is an occupancy/property source and should not be treated as a household census.

  • NAI family-history sources guide

Reading the evidence: an index, transcript, provider search page and original image are different sources. A recorded image link does not mean its reading or identity match is confirmed.
