What the notice says

Hugh Allan v. Trustees of Robert Glasgow: the report calls Mrs Robertson Glasgow the late Robert’s natural daughter and first heir under his 1818 entail. It reports his death in 1827 and a supplementary entail and trust dated 23 June 1821, involving Seafield near Ayr.

Evidence and identity limits

Contemporary account of the 25 August hearing, independently supporting Anne’s daughter/heir description and the 1827 death year. It does not verify 19 April. Hugh Allan-8034, the profile-defined nephew, is a compatible appellant candidate; older Allan-8028 has no death recorded but would be 109. Neither identity is proved. These later deeds are distinct from the uninspected 1803 trust. Identity review (6 October 2026): Aug 1835 report of the Lords appeal Hugh Allan v. Trustees of Robert Glasgow. The late Robert is Glasgow of Montgreenan (1076, strong) and 'Mrs Robertson Glasgow', his natural daughter and heir, is Anne (2874, strong), so Anne's father link can be made confident. Hugh Allan is probably the nephew Allan-8034 (heir-at-law).

Record-level bridge. The report's late Robert Glasgow died in 1827, made an 1818 entail and supplementary deeds dated 23 June 1821, and held Seafield near Ayr. Anne Glasgow (Glasgow-2874)strong
[b. 1788-03-11; d. 1867-07-29] · natural daughter and first heir of entail of Robert Glasgow
Why this candidate?

The report calls Mrs Robertson Glasgow the late Robert's natural daughter and first heir under the 1818 entail. The 1846 report quotes Robert's 1802 deed: 'Miss Anne Glasgow, my reputed natural daughter by Mrs. Anne Swan', later the wife of Mr Robertson Glasgow, with the estate settled on her and her issue. LBS (claim 466 and person 27300) has Robert Robertson, later Glasgow, marrying Anne Glasgow on 2 Aug 1804, both claiming Mt Greenan. Paterson says the estate was left to a daughter 'married to Mr Robertson of Prenderguest' and that 'Mrs Robertson Glasgow, now proprietor ... resides at Pau'.

Published 1835-09-03 · The Morning Chronicle · p. 4

Additional excerpts from this issue — 2

What the notice says

House of Lords appeals: the report reproduces clauses of Glasgow’s settlement and says Lord Brougham recommended reversing the judgment below; judgment reversed accordingly. The heading appears to read Henry Allen v. Trustees of R. Glasgow.

Evidence and identity limits

Reports the Wednesday judgment, 2 September 1835. The estate, Seafield dispute and counsel identify the same litigation as the 26 August report, whose appellant is Hugh Allan. Preserve the discrepant heading; compare the original court papers before resolving the name. Reported deed extracts are not an inspection of the original deed. Identity review (6 October 2026): Sept 1835 Morning Chronicle report of the House of Lords reversing the judgment in favour of the appellant against 'R. Glasgow's' trustees. R. Glasgow is Robert of Montgreenan (1076, strong). The heading's 'Henry Allen' is the same appellant as Hugh Allan.
