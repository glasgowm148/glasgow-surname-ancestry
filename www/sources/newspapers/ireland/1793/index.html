How to read these notices

Cards lead with the person named in the notice; profile candidates are assessed separately. Birth cutoffs labelled “by c.” are estimates, usually assuming an adult professional was at least 18; open the birth note for the basis. Event groups are broad navigation aids built from each notice’s record type. A relationship filter identifies family wording in the notice, not a proved match to a WikiTree family.

Published 1793-02-20 · Northern Star · p. 3

What the notice says

Shipping intelligence says the William, Captain Glasgow, belonging to Belfast, sailed from Bordeaux on the 3rd instant; an embargo order from Paris arrived there the following day, detaining four British and thirty-three Dutch vessels.

Evidence and identity limits

The original reports an embargo, not capture of the William. The account does not explicitly say whether this vessel was among those detained after departure. The 3rd instant is relative to the report; no new exact voyage day is assigned. Captain forename unstated; continuity with the named 1792 master remains probable. Identity review (6 October 2026): Northern Star 20 Feb 1793: the William, Captain Glasgow, belonging to Belfast, sailed from Bordeaux on the 3rd just before the French embargo. This confirms a Belfast-owned vessel, not the captain's residence. Same captain cluster.

Published 1793-09-21 · Northern Star · p. 3

What the notice says

Shipping intelligence names William Glasgow as master of the Jean, from Belfast, arrived safe at Oporto on the 5th instant and expected ready to sail in twenty-one days.

Evidence and identity limits

Jean is a different vessel from the brig William. Same named master, Belfast and Oporto support compatible continuity, not a proved identity or vessel renaming. Expected departure is not a completed voyage. Identity review (6 October 2026): Northern Star 21 Sep 1793: William Glasgow, master of the Jean, from Belfast, arrived at Oporto. Same name, port and trade as the brig William master. Continuity is probable but the vessel is different; no profile proved.

Who this may be

Why this candidate?
