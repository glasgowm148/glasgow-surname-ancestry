What the notice says

The Belfast News-Letter of 4 March 1831 reports a Belfast Juvenile Society meeting at Knockbracken Meeting-house on “Tuesday evening last” to form an auxiliary society. Rev. Thomas Houston chaired the meeting; Rev. Walter Moffat and Mr James Glasgow attended as the Society’s deputation.
