Published 1834-05-20 · Belfast News-Letter · p. 3

What the notice says

Benjamin Glasgow, surgeon, formerly of Vera Cruz, Mexico, died at New Orleans on 26 November last, according to a May 1834 notice. He was son of the late Robert Glasgow of Cookstown, County Tyrone. A Dublin notice repeats the surgeon, New Orleans and father details.

Evidence and identity limits

1833 is inferred from “last” in the 1834 publication; no age or mother is stated. Both notices concern one death, not independent witnesses. Glasgow-3412 is a strong compatible surgery-graduate candidate, not a proved identity. The father was dead by 20 May 1834, distinct from the mill father who died in 1856. Robert-3941 remains compatible by chronology only. No existing-profile father confidence is upgraded. Identity review (6 October 2026): Belfast News-Letter, 20 May 1834: Benjamin Glasgow, surgeon, formerly of Vera Cruz, died at New Orleans on 26 November 1833, son of the late Robert Glasgow of Cookstown. Benjamin is probably Glasgow-3412 (Glasgow University surgeon, 1825, 'Hibernus'); his father is not securely on WikiTree, so the parent link stays a research note.
