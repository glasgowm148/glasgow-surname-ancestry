Rev. ministerial role documented by 1836; estimated age 18+ at that role, not stated by the source and not a sourced birth date.

Occupation / trade

Published 1836-04-05 · Belfast News-Letter · p. 4

Additional excerpts from this issue — 1

What the notice says

Under ‘ORDINATION AT BELLAGHY,’ the report says the Magherafelt Presbytery ordained Rev. Gilbert Kirker Wilson to Bellaghy on ‘the 15th ult.’ Rev. James Glasgow, of Castledawson, commenced the service with a sermon. After the report of the service, the same account describes the toasts: the chairman gave ‘The Gentlemen who officiated this day,’ and Rev. James Glasgow returned thanks. With the 5 April issue date, ‘15th ult.’ means 15 March 1836.

Evidence and identity limits

James Glasgow, Institution student / assistant and Castledawson minister, 1828–1848: The Institution mathematics/student/assistant sequence, Castledawson call and ordination, and Indian missionary family notices converge strongly on the checked career of James McClure Glasgow-1780. The early notices lack his middle name and parents; the Society secretary remains a separate qualified comparison. The current biography’s 6 October 1835 call is corrected locally to ordination; the call had been reported by 10 July. No parent or sibling relationship is upgraded.
