What the notice says

House occupied by Joseph M’Cormick, formerly residence of late Robert Glasgow; apply to Benjamin and William Glasgow, Drapersfield Mills, Cookstown; notice dated 16 January.

Evidence and identity limits

Partners named. The former occupant Robert died before 16 January 1864, but identity with 1842 partner still requires a bridge. Identity review (6 October 2026): Notice (Belfast News-Letter, 19 Jan 1864): the house occupied by Joseph M'Cormick, formerly the residence of the late Robert Glasgow, is offered; apply to Benjamin and William Glasgow, Drapersfield Mills, Cookstown. Benjamin is the Drapersfield Benjamin Glasgow-4340, and the late Robert is probably Glasgow-2656. No kinship is stated.

Published 1864-02-18 · Belfast News-Letter · p. 2

What the notice says

Benjamin Glasgow of Drapersfield, Cookstown, married Wilhelmina, eldest daughter of Richard Pike, Esq., of Beechgrove, Dungannon, at Dungannon on 16 February 1864.

Evidence and identity limits

The original notice gives his wife and her father but no groom age or father. It belongs to the consolidated Drapersfield account; do not assign Wilhelmina as mother of every later child without further evidence. Identity review (6 October 2026): Marriage notice (Belfast News-Letter, 18 Feb 1864): Benjamin Glasgow of Drapersfield, Cookstown, married Wilhelmina, eldest daughter of Richard Pike Esq. of Beechgrove, Dungannon, at Dungannon on 16 Feb 1864. The groom is Glasgow-4340. The bride is Wilhelmina Pike-9903 (Quaker birth 1842, eldest child of Richard Pike-9902). The 'Unknown Pike' Pike-17078 now married to 4340 is the same woman and should be merged into Pike-9903.

Who this may be

Glasgow-4340 (Glasgow-4340)strong
· groom
Why this candidate?

Glasgow-4340 rests on the GRONI index 'Benjamin Glasgow and Pike, 16 February 1864, Dungannon district' (M/1864/T1/1447/2/35). Date, district and bride's surname are exact. His Cookstown children with mother's maiden name Pike include Richard (b. 10 Apr 1865, named for Richard Pike) and Margaret (b. 7 Nov 1867, the 'eldest daughter' of 1891). No other Benjamin Glasgow/Glascow/Glasco is married to a Pike. 2059 (married Jane Little 1856) and 2566 (bachelor in 1876) are excluded.
