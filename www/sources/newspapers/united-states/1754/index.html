How to read these notices

Cards lead with the person named in the notice; profile candidates are assessed separately. Birth cutoffs labelled “by c.” are estimates, usually assuming an adult professional was at least 18; open the birth note for the basis. Event groups are broad navigation aids built from each notice’s record type. A relationship filter identifies family wording in the notice, not a proved match to a WikiTree family.

What the notice says

Robert Glasgow, master of the snow John and Archibald, of Glasgow, is reported arriving in distress during a Virginia-to-Glasgow passage.

Evidence and identity limits

Robert Glasgow, master of John and Archibald, 1754: One distress report preserved in three issues, not three masters. Later reprints give St John’s, Antigua, 15 February. The passage describes gale damage, leakage and lightning; Glasgow is also the vessel’s designation/port, not the master’s birthplace. The preceding Swanton vessel’s provisions and urine story in the Pennsylvania page belongs to a different vessel. No family or link to the 1751 defendant established.
