Daniel senior and Mary of Moyock; junior witness; recited 1692 title

15–17 September 1714 (recites 16 May 1692) · Moyock, North Carolina; land in Norfolk County, Virginia · United States · Original inspected; same-family chronology and witnesses

A second original transaction identifies Daniel Glasgow as a planter of Moyock, Albemarle County, North Carolina. By a lease dated 15 September 1714 and release dated 16 September, he conveyed 100 acres to Thomas Miller. The release names Daniel senior; Mary joined it and acknowledged her consent in court on 17 September. Daniel Glasgow junior witnessed both instruments with John Nicholls and G. Halliday. The deeds recite Thomas Miller’s 800-acre patent of 20 April 1689 and his conveyance of this 100-acre parcel to Daniel on 16 May 1692, acknowledged on 17 May. Thus this 1714 original supplies a specific 1692 acquisition to follow; the original 1692 deed was located and inspected on 30 October 2026, completing this acquisition test. The 100-acre Miller parcel is distinct from the 80 acres inherited through John Gamon and from the 100-acre Henry Slade parcel sold in 1729. Shared acreage alone does not join those titles. The same court day, Moyock residence, Mary, Daniel junior and John Nicholls strongly identify the senior as the father in the separately proved Gamon deed. This adds chronology and a witness network, without naming William Glasgow-428 as a son or resolving junior’s later identity. Father ratings remain junior→Glasgow-3322 98%, William-428→Daniel-1158 42%, Daniel-1158→Robert-1157 12%, and Daniel-3322→Robert-1157 34%. Correction30October2026: both originals read Thomas Miller. The exact1692acquisition is now inspected; the1692Glascock and1714Glasgow are linked by the specific title recital. Father ratings unchanged.
