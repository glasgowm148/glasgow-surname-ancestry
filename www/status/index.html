Sources for each record entry

A record entry links a person to a record. 13 entries cite their source directly. 1,189 have a possible match in the source library that still needs checking. 3,675 still need their source added. This is a measure of how far the linking work has gone, not of how many records are missing.

What these numbers do not show

They describe what the catalogue contains, not whether every family relationship is correct. A possible source match is a lead until someone has checked it.
