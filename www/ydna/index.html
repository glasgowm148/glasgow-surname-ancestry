Method: the matrix reports a transparent allele-step sum from the public Glasgow Project Y-STR table and two administrator-supplied Y-111 rows; multi-copy values are paired in sorted order. It should not be assumed to reproduce every company's GD algorithm, because FTDNA applies special rules to some multi-copy markers. The new B580327 relational export independently reproduces every available B580327 value shown here as its GDist. Its RDist is a separate panel-normalized grouping score, not genetic distance or generations. For ancestral-state inference, the four closely related Glasgow-12 kits were collapsed to one reconstructed family branch so they could not outvote the three independent basal lines. What causes SAPP's adjusted GD 7 for B580327–B696189? The observed endpoints differ by one repeat at DYS576, but SAPP selects ancestral 16 and models two separate paths: 16→17 for B696189 and 16→18 for B580327. That marker therefore contributes three inferred path mutations instead of one observed endpoint step, raising total GD from 5 to 7. The same inferred parallelism raises comparisons involving 333002. This is a useful convergence model, not a directly observed mutation history.

TMRCA calibration warning: the group estimate around 1500 broadly overlaps the present FT25406 and FT20271 age ranges, but the known Glasgow-12 control shows that an uncalibrated STR date can be materially too recent. The c.1750 and c.1600 nodes are search windows, not birth dates or relationship proof. A stronger rerun would add the confirmed SNP states and a genealogy group for the independently documented Glasgow-12 descendants.

Reading distance

Measurements that must not be mixed

Definitions, interpretation boundaries and full source notes.

Allele-step distance

This page's transparent sum of allele differences on the smaller shared panel. It reproduces the new B580327 export's available GDist values, but is labelled separately because provider rules for multi-copy markers can differ.

GroupingApp GDist / RDist

GDist is the app's pairwise genetic distance. RDist is its panel-normalized grouping score. Neither is a generation count, TMRCA, SNP branch or proof of relationship.

SAPP original / adjusted GD

Original GD is calculated from the observed STR endpoints. Adjusted GD is recalculated along SAPP's chosen tree to model convergence and back-mutation. It can change when SNPs, genealogies or the input kit set change.

Named SNP branches

A downstream SNP such as FTE32242 gives a reliable branch order. A block may contain several equivalent named SNPs, so one displayed branch step is not necessarily one mutation.

Private Big Y variants

These usually provide the most useful recent separation. They require kit-owner or administrator downloads and coverage checks; public tables do not provide the exact pairwise count.
